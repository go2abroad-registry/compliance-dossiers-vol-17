Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Under the Korean Commercial Act and National Tax Service interpretation, a pure virtual office or commercial mail-forwarding address is rejected as the sole registered office for an air cargo charter broker requiring an air transport business registration under the Aviation Business Act. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None: South Korean law no longer imposes a statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Chusik Hoesa. The incorporators may set the initial issued share capital at their discretion, but the subscribed amount must be fully paid and held in a blocked capital-deposit account in the name of the company at a Korean commercial bank until the court registers the incorporation. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

National Tax Service of the Republic of Korea (NTS); online electronic portal: HomeTax electronic taxation service (www.hometax.go.kr). Export customs declarations are processed by Korea Customs Service through the UNI-PASS electronic clearance portal.

E-government filing portal

For a Korean Chusik Hoesa with a domestic place of business: (1) complete corporate incorporation at the commercial registry; (2) file the standard business registration application with the competent NTS district tax office within 20 days of opening the business; (3) NTS will issue the Business Registration Certificate containing the 10-digit Business Registration Number, which is the unified tax identification number for VAT, corporate income tax, withholding tax, and e-Tax Invoice purposes. Required evidence includes certified articles of incorporation, corporate registry excerpt, proof of registered office and actual place of business, business opening details, and the representative director's identification. For a non-resident foreign entity with no Korean place of business: (1) use the English-language HomeTax non-resident simplified VAT registration service; (2) upload the foreign incorporation certificate, tax residence certificate, bank proof, electronic contact details, and a power of attorney appointing a Korean tax representative or certified tax accountant where required; (3) receive the assigned simplified VAT ID / business number for filing Korean VAT on Korean-sourced supplies; (4) if exporting goods or operating specialized air-cargo brokerage, also obtain the necessary Korea Customs Service entity registration or customs ID and register the export-related business activity. All TIN issuance is subject to NTS verification; no fictional or provisional TIN is recognized.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

KRW 0 for domestic taxable persons; registration is mandatory from business commencement. For non-resident electronic-service or offshore suppliers, nexus is based on Korean consumer location; simplified VAT registration is mandatory when supplying taxed electronic services to Korean recipients, and actual returns report only Korean-source taxable turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under the Korean VAT Act applies to exports of goods physically removed from Korea and to services, including brokerage and freight-forwarding services, that are directly ancillary to such exports, supplied to non-resident parties, and paid for in foreign currency. An air-cargo charter broker must demonstrate that the commission or brokerage fee arises from an international air-transport/cargo-charter transaction for goods exported from Korea, that the service recipient is a foreign enterprise or a designated non-resident party, and that the service is actually performed in connection with the export movement. Certain qualifying services supplied electronically to non-resident customers may also be zero-rated where the place of supply rules place the service outside Korea.

  • Customs Export Declaration transmitted through UNI-PASS and certified by Korea Customs Service (export clearance confirmation for physical outbound air cargo)
  • Commercial invoice and the cargo charter agreement or international freight forwarding contract naming the Korean exporter, foreign consignee, and the international flight/cargo service
  • Bill of Lading (for sea legs) or Air Waybill (Master and/or House) showing pickup in Korea and delivery outside Korea, including flight numbers, cargo description, and consignee details
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or Korean Foreign Exchange Settlement Certificate issued by a Korean foreign exchange bank evidencing receipt of the foreign-currency payment, supported by SWIFT wire or Fedwire remittance advices
  • Service completion or delivery confirmation proving the cross-border service was actually performed, such as destination customs entry, delivery receipt, or release instruction
  • Corporate and VAT identity evidence, including the Business Registration Certificate and the e-Tax Invoice, confirming the legal supplier and that no separate domestic VAT was charged

Every VAT-registered Korean business supplier, including an export-oriented air-cargo charter broker, must issue an electronic tax invoice (e-Tax Invoice) through the NTS HomeTax platform or an NTS-integrated accounting/ERP API and transmit it to NTS by the day after the supply. Paper invoices are not acceptable for input VAT credit. Korea does not use physical fiscal stamping; anti-fraud digital control is implemented through centralized NTS real-time invoice registration, mandatory periodic VAT return reconciliation, and the Korea Customs Service UNI-PASS export declaration interface.

NTS may cross-check export e-Tax Invoices with UNI-PASS customs declarations, signed waybills, and bank foreign-exchange records during post-clearance VAT audits. If the zero-rated export cannot be substantiated, the 10% VAT is assessed and collected with statutory interest and penalty surcharges under the Framework Act on National Taxes and the VAT Act. Willfully false zero-rating or fabricated customs and foreign-exchange documentation triggers criminal sanctions under the Punishment of Tax Evaders Act and the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, including fines, imprisonment, loss of tax advantages, full retrospective clawback of any refunded VAT, and debarment from export-related tax relief. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days without an approved extension similarly attracts foreign-exchange penalties unless the amount is lawfully exempted or re-imported.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Korean won is generally convertible on the current account for bona fide trade, service fees, and dividend remittances, but capital account transactions remain strictly controlled under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act. Non-resident equity investments, cross-border loans, and repatriation of principal investment proceeds require prior reporting or approval through a designated foreign exchange bank. Export proceeds must be received and surrendered into a domestic foreign currency account or converted into won within prescribed deadlines; a substantial portion of export earnings is subject to mandatory conversion into KRW under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act relevant subordinate regulations. For a Korea inbound foreign-owned Chusik Hoesa engaged in air cargo charter broking, profit repatriation and capital redemptions are permissible after corporate tax clearance, external audit where applicable, and submission of the required Foreign Exchange Transactions Report to the Bank of Korea via the designated bank. Practical hurdles include documentary verification of the underlying commercial transactions, anti-money laundering checks on counterparties across multiple jurisdictions, and the requirement to prove that remitted funds originate from legitimate operating revenues rather than disguised capital flows. The Corporate body must maintain accurate foreign exchange transaction ledgers and file month-by-month BOK reports for each foreign exchange position above statutory thresholds. Because Korea applies a negative-list capital account regime, transactions not expressly restricted can proceed through reporting, but any deviation from the declared business purpose is treated as unauthorized foreign exchange trading and can trigger monetary penalties. Thus the treasury blueprint must embed transaction purpose codes, underlying invoices, and contemporaneous compliance documentation at every wire point.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts must be collected within a statutory period of 6 months from the export declaration date, subject to extension for justified trade terms, and the exporter must convert or surrender such receipts into won through a designated foreign exchange bank on or before the due date. The mandatory conversion ratio is effectively 100% of export proceeds, except where explicitly exempted under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, for example certain intercompany settlement flows or insured export receivables that qualify for retention in a foreign currency account after obtaining approval from the designated bank.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Korea (BOK) acting with the Ministry of Economy and Finance under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, supported by the Korea Customs Service for trade declarations and the Foreign Exchange Information Network (FEIN) for reporting

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and legally restricted. Offshore digital EMI providers are not permitted to act as the primary clearing or settlement bank for restructuring-oriented entities in Korea because corporate account registration must be held with an institution licensed under the Korean Banking Act and Foreign Exchange Transactions Act. A foreign fintech EMI may operate only as a compliant technology vendor intermediated by a domestic licensed bank, and it cannot open domestic won settlement accounts, issue Korean IBAN corporate facilities, or independently process BOK foreign exchange reporting. Any unlicensed solicitation or EMI wallet-based movement of incoming equity capital in KRW would be deemed unauthorized foreign exchange brokerage.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital injection must be routed via SWIFT wire to the designated domestic commercial bank under the foreign exchange bank's capital account registration. The bank will issue a certificate of receipt, and the non-resident shareholder must submit the Korean-styled report of acquisition of shares to the Bank of Korea within the statutory window. The treasurer must use the appropriate foreign exchange purpose code for equity investment in a Korean corporation, usually 004003 for acquisition of shares by a non-resident investor, and maintain the source-of-funds documentation. Upon receipt the funds are held in a capital escrow account until a certified capital verification report is issued by an external auditor confirming actual cash contribution. The Chusik Hoesa then transfers the funds into the operational KRW account, distinct from the foreign currency settlement account, and each subsequent transfer must be internally matched against the corporate ledger to prove the equity capital was not diverted into trade finance speculation.Outbound trade disbursements for aircraft charter payments must use SWIFT MT103 for unambiguous prime beneficiary wire settlement, with full documentary records attached to the instruction. Larger contractual periodic block-charter or ACMI payments are better executed through an irrevocable commercial letter of credit issued by the Korean bank and advised to the foreign airline or charter broker, ensuring reconciliation against an aircraft charter agreement that includes tail number identification, crew and maintenance operating scope, and delivery and redelivery dates. For lower-value but recurrent handling, fuel invoices, and ground handling costs, documentary collections can be used where the bank transmits transport documents through the correspondent banking network and releases documents against payment. Every outbound remittance must carry a precise foreign exchange bank purpose code, and the physical or electronic copy of the underlying invoice must be archived for five years to withstand BOK and tax audits.
