Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS) de Luxembourg, administered by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) under the judicial supervision of the District Court of Luxembourg (Tribunal d'arrondissement de Luxembourg).

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (Loi du 10 août 1915 concernant les sociétés commerciales), notably as modernized by the Law of 10 August 2016; Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL) provisions at Articles 710-1 to 710-25; complemented by the Law of 19 December 2002 on the Trade and Companies Register and the Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations (RESA).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarial Deed of Incorporation (Acte constitutif notarié) containing the Articles of Association (Statuts), filed electronically with the RCS and mandatorily published in the Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations (RESA).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 25 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum subscribed and fully paid-up capital of EUR 12,000 for a standard SARL; cash contributions must be deposited prior to notarization with a Luxembourg credit institution into a blocked escrow account (compte bloqué) opened in the future company's name, evidenced by a blocking certificate (attestation de blocage) delivered to the notary; contributions in kind require a valuation report by an approved réviseur d'entreprises; the SARL-S simplified variant allows EUR 1 capital but is outside the standard formation here.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification for commercial warehousing and office premises issued by the Corps grand-ducal d'incendie et de secours (CGDIS) after a structural load inspection and the commune's occupancy permit; workplace clearance from the Inspection du travail et des mines (ITM) covering occupational health and safety, fire extinguisher placement, emergency evacuation signage and the safe handling of residual hydraulic fluids in stored equipment.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum subscribed and fully paid-up capital of EUR 12,000 for a standard SARL; cash contributions must be deposited prior to notarization with a Luxembourg credit institution into a blocked escrow account (compte bloqué) opened in the future company's name, evidenced by a blocking certificate (attestation de blocage) delivered to the notary; contributions in kind require a valuation report by an approved réviseur d'entreprises; the SARL-S simplified variant allows EUR 1 capital but is outside the standard formation here.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Ministry of the Economy — Directorate General for SMEs (Direction générale des classes moyennes et PME) as the reference authority for the general business establishment permit under the Law of 2 September 2011; no sector-specific industrial license applies to ISIC 4659 wholesale of used construction machinery.

Category: Autorisation d'établissement (Business Establishment Permit); if the company operates exclusively as a cross-border wholesale trader without local premises, registration as a commercial merchant with the RCS suffices and the establishment permit is limited to occupational safety clearances.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; portal: myguichet.lu

E-government filing portal

Luxembourg SARL automatically receives its corporate tax number (matricule) at commercial registry incorporation via the RCS and ACD. For VAT, file Form 645 with the AEDT or register electronically through MyGuichet. A non-resident entity without Luxembourg establishment must register directly for VAT with the AEDT before making taxable supplies; if it creates a taxable permanent establishment, it must also file for corporate income tax registration with the ACD and obtain a Luxembourg tax file number.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (Luxembourg-resident SARL is taxed at entity level on worldwide income; no pass-through treatment) — Luxembourg SARL automatically receives its corporate tax number (matricule) at commercial registry incorporation via the RCS and ACD. For VAT, file Form 645 with the AEDT or register electronically through MyGuichet. A non-resident entity without Luxembourg establishment must register directly for VAT with the AEDT before making taxable supplies; if it creates a taxable permanent establishment, it must also file for corporate income tax registration with the ACD and obtain a Luxembourg tax file number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Luxembourg-resident SARL is taxed at entity level on worldwide income; no pass-through treatment)

Available election forms

Luxembourg has no check-the-box election; classification is statutory. Available elections include: (i) VAT group election (assujetti unique) under Article 60 of the VAT Law; (ii) fiscal integration for CIT among 95%-owned resident subsidiaries under Article 164bis CIR; (iii) option to tax real estate rentals under Article 44(1)(j) of the VAT Law; (iv) SICAR/SICAV transparent election where legally eligible.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for established small businesses (franchise d'affaires); EUR 0 for non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies in Luxembourg

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to exports of goods to third territories under Article 41(1) of the Luxembourg VAT Law when the seller, or a third party acting on its behalf, dispatches or transports the goods outside the EU and holds sufficient proof of physical export. Intra-Community supplies are zero-rated where the customer provides a valid EU VAT identification number and the goods are transported to another Member State. For used machinery, export zero-rating applies to the full sale price; alternatively the margin scheme may be used but cannot be combined with zero-rating on the same transaction.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / electronic exit declaration via Luxembourg customs systems)
  • Bill of Lading (or CMR or air waybill)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing FX repatriation
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of origin where required by the destination country

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime. Electronic filing of periodic VAT returns and VAT listings is mandatory via MyGuichet/e-TVA. B2G e-invoicing through the PEPPOL network is mandatory; B2B e-invoicing is optional and requires recipient acceptance; Intrastat declarations apply when dispatches exceed EUR 460,000.

Zero-rating claims are subject to strict audit review. If export proof is missing, incomplete, or contradictory, the tax authorities reassess VAT at 17% plus late-payment interest of 0.6% per month and impose administrative fines up to 100% of the evaded VAT. Falsified customs or export documents trigger criminal fraud sanctions under Article 78 VAT Law and Article 1101 General Tax Law, including imprisonment up to five years and substantial monetary penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility within the euro area. Luxembourg maintains no general foreign-exchange control, no prior central-bank approval for capital movement, and no separate profit-remittance permit for an SARL. Current account and capital account transactions between Luxembourg and non-EEA states are presumed free under EU free-movement-of-capital rules. Profit repatriation to non-resident shareholders is subject only to ordinary Luxembourg corporate-law distribution requirements, the net-assets/balance-sheet test, and any applicable withholding tax or EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive relief. No mandatory FX conversion or central-bank allocation applies to foreign-currency balances held by the company.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio in Luxembourg. Foreign currency received from used construction excavator exports may be retained, converted, or hedged freely. The only obligations are statistical: resident companies may need to provide the BCL with balance-of-payments or external-transaction data and banks must make reporting filings; these are informational and do not constrain retention of USD or other currencies.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), with the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) acting as the competent authority for AML/CFT supervision, licensing of banks and payment institutions, and enforcement of financial-sector obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility for EU-licensed fintech EMIs is High through the PSD2 EU passport, and Low for non-EU offshore EMIs. A regulated European Electronic Money Institution or Payment Institution may be used for lower-value operating disbursements while passported into Luxembourg under CSSF notification, but a full credit institution remains the recommended treasury rail for high-value secondhand excavator imports/exports, documentary credits, and trade-finance activity. Offshore digital EMIs without an EEA authorisation cannot legally provide payments or hold operational EUR accounts for a Luxembourg SARL and will typically be rejected as a compliance risk.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Root cause: UBO opacity or nominee shareholding where natural-person beneficial owners are not clearly identifiable.

Defense evidence: Luxembourg RCS extract, updated UBO declaration, shareholding chain diagrams, subscription documents, certified passports, source-of-wealth statement for each UBO, tax residency certificates, and bankers' reference letters from the UBOs' primary banks.
Over- or under-invoicing red flags typical of used excavator/earthmoving-machinery cross-border trading.

Root cause: Over- or under-invoicing red flags typical of used excavator/earthmoving-machinery cross-border trading.

Defense evidence: Comprehensive transaction dossier comprising purchase contracts, original commercial invoices, proof of payment instructions, transport documents (CMR, bill of lading, ocean/air waybill), customs declarations including HS codes, independent valuation or condition survey report for each secondhand excavator, and the full provenance chain from last owner to end buyer.
Counterparty concentration in high-risk jurisdictions or involvement of sanctioned/embargoed entities.

Root cause: Counterparty concentration in high-risk jurisdictions or involvement of sanctioned/embargoed entities.

Defense evidence: Export/import compliance policy, EU and UN sanctions screening results for all counterparties and vessels/vehicles, details of commodity classification, proof that the equipment is not dual-use or military-capable beyond standard earthmoving use, end-user certificate where required, and a regulatory counsel opinion on jurisdiction-specific embargoes.
Unusual payment pattern, including third-party settlement, back-to-back trades, rapid movement of funds between related accounts, or cash-advance requests from non-bank sources.

Root cause: Unusual payment pattern, including third-party settlement, back-to-back trades, rapid movement of funds between related accounts, or cash-advance requests from non-bank sources.

Defense evidence: Contractual explanation of the trade economics, a full funds-flow map, signed tripartite agreements where third-party payments are legally necessary, board-approved internal controls over payment instructions, account statements showing consistent relationship with the funding bank, and historical execution records of six to twelve months.
Directors or UBOs with prior adverse corporate insolvency, enforcement history, or adverse media exposure.

Root cause: Directors or UBOs with prior adverse corporate insolvency, enforcement history, or adverse media exposure.

Defense evidence: Criminal background certificates from country of residence, court extracts showing discharged or non-relevant insolvency, auditor's certificate of no undisclosed liabilities, detailed personal and corporate restoration narrative, and evidence of rehabilitation or compliance improvement, accompanied by legal counsel letter explaining any prior adverse history.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for the Lu SARL should be routed through TARGET2/SEPA or SWIFT MT103 from the ultimate shareholder's own bank account to the SARL settlement account at a Luxembourg credit institution. The payment instruction must clearly cite the purpose as 'Equity contribution' and include the SARL corporate reference in the remittance information; the bank's payment form must be completed with the EU Balance-of-Payments classification for 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' where applicable and any internal purpose code required by the receiving bank. The financing bank will require a notarised or privately executed capital-increase resolution, an updated share subscription statement, proof of the capital credit in local corporate books, updated articles/statutes if the registered capital is amended, RCS updating, and a source of funds narrative showing the original ownership of the cash. Wire transfers should not be split into artificial subpayments solely to avoid scrutiny.Outbound payments to non-EU sellers and buyers must use cleared institutional rails only: SWIFT MT103 corporate-to-corporate wires for supplier prepayments or settlement, and documentary letters of credit opened and confirmed by the Luxembourg bank for large lot shipments of used excavators/rollers. Commercial LC terms should be dominated by presentation documents (commercial invoice, full set of bills of lading/CMRs, packing list, insurance certificate, condition survey report, and customs export/import declarations) and should be handled by the bank's trade-finance desk.
