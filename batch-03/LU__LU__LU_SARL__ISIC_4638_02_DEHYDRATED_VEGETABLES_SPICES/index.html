Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A fully virtual or mailbox-type office is not permissible for a trading SARL: the Law of 10 August 1915 on commercial companies, as amended, and Luxembourg tax substance doctrine require a genuine registered office (siège social) and an effective management seat in Luxembourg with local premises, books, and decision-making. Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), operated by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR), located at 8-10, rue Léon Laval, L-3372 Leudelange, Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, accessible via the e-filing portal www.lbr.lu.

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended, Title V regarding the Société à Responsabilité Limitée (Articles 710-1 to 710-37), together with the Law of 19 December 2002 on the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) and the Law decorating the electronic compendium of companies and associations (RESA).

Formation / constitutional filings

Acte de constitution (Deed of Incorporation) comprising the Charte constitutive and Articles of Association, executed either as a notarial deed before a Luxembourg notary or, where legally permitted, as a private instrument (acte sous seing privé) signed by all incorporators, and filed in full with the RCS.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 27 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum share capital of EUR 12,000. At least 25% of each cash-subscribed share must be paid up at subscription and deposited in a non-interest-bearing blocked bank account ('attestation de blocage') opened in the company's name; the blocked funds are released only upon presentation of the RCS registration certificate. Contributions in kind must be fully paid up and supported by the statutory auditor's report where required.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification issued by the Luxembourg fire and rescue inspection authority covering emergency exits, extinguishers and smoke detection for the dry storage warehouse; structural load inspection certificate for racking and pallet systems prepared by a registered structural engineer; workplace clearance from the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) verifying electrical safety, ventilation and occupational health for the packing and warehouse areas.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum share capital of EUR 12,000. At least 25% of each cash-subscribed share must be paid up at subscription and deposited in a non-interest-bearing blocked bank account ('attestation de blocage') opened in the company's name; the blocked funds are released only upon presentation of the RCS registration certificate. Contributions in kind must be fully paid up and supported by the statutory auditor's report where required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (resident SARL: full Luxembourg corporate income tax on worldwide income; combined headline burden in Luxembourg City of 24.94% for fiscal year 2024, comprising 17% CIT, 7% solidarity surcharge and 6.75% communal business tax; no tax-exempt or free-zone status; territorial carve-outs apply only to income from foreign permanent establishments and foreign immovable property, exempt or credit-relieved under tax treaties).

Available election forms

Luxembourg does not operate a US-style check-the-box or entity classification election. Relevant statutory elections available to the trading SARL: (i) participation exemption election for qualifying dividends and capital gains (10% or EUR 1.2 million acquisition-cost threshold, continuous 12-month holding period, corporate income tax-subject subsidiary); (ii) IP regime election granting an 80% exemption of qualifying IP-derived income under the modified nexus approach; (iii) VAT grouping election under Article 61 of the VAT law, combining the SARL with financially, economically and organisationally linked Luxembourg entities into a single taxable person ('assujetti unique'); (iv) election for joint and several VAT liability on commissionaire arrangements; (v) option to file VAT returns quarterly instead of monthly where annual turnover is below EUR 2 million; (vi) hybrid mismatch alignment elections under the Law of 20 December 2019 (ATAD I/II) to neutralise double non-taxation. The SARL is opaque and separately taxable; no pass-through or transparency election exists.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is freely convertible for both current and capital transactions in Luxembourg; the jurisdiction does not operate exchange controls, dual exchange rates or conversion licences and applies Article 63 TFEU plus the OECD Code of Liberalisation of Capital Movements. Profit remittance by the SARL to non-resident shareholders is lawful without prior ministerial approval, subject to corporate law conditions (distributable reserves confirmed by audited annual accounts, board resolution) and to a nominal 15% withholding tax which can be reduced under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive or an applicable double-tax treaty. The only non-FX hurdles for remittances are banking AML/KYC source-of-funds checks, BCL balance-of-payments statistical declarations for flows in excess of the prevailing materiality threshold (approximately EUR 12,500 per counterparty per annum), and compliance with EU financial sanctions. USD or CNY receipts can be retained, assigned or converted at market price without quantitative limits.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender, forced conversion or repatriation timeline applies to Luxembourg resident exporters; proceeds may remain in USD/CNY accounts indefinitely. The SARL's only implementation duties are (i) respecting BCL balance-of-payments reporting on cumulative export receipts with a given non-resident counterparty above the reporting threshold (about EUR 12,500 per counterparty per year, as per BCL external statistics instructions), (ii) keeping complete transaction documentary evidence to ensure inward receipts are classified as exports of goods rather than equity-like or undefined inflows, and (iii) ensuring all invoices and LC presentations are consistent with the commercial reality of the dehydrated vegetables and spices transaction. Conversion of EUR, USD and CNY is free but will be priced and executed per the bank's spot books without regulatory constraint.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), acting within the Eurosystem and the European System of Central Banks; no separate Foreign Exchange Administration exists in Luxembourg, as exchange-control functions (statistical monitoring, external sector data, sanctions screening coordination and TARGET2 oversight) are absorbed by the BCL, the Ministry of Finance, and the CSSF for credit institution conduct.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as primary treasury rail, MODERATE only for low-value operational automation. A CSSF-licensed or EU/EEA-passported EMI may provide euro-denominated payment accounts, SEPA credit transfer initiation and KYC APIs for a Luxembourg SARL without requiring a full banking relationship; however, it cannot operate the notarial capital-blocage attestation required for incorporation, cannot issue documentary letters of credit or bank guarantees, cannot provide SWIFT MT103/CHIPS/Fedwire correspondent clearing equivalent, and cannot support a Luxembourg-law ISDA hedging framework. Non-EEA offshore EMIs have NO legal viability: they do not benefit from PSD2 passporting, would expose the SARL and its account bank to licensing and AML infringements, and cannot clear through TARGET2 or SEPA; use of such entities must be limited strictly to non-treasury, non-documentary pilot payments with fully screened counterparties.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Protocol for subscriber capital and shareholder loans: the remitter must be the exact legal shareholder named in the SARL deed and RBE; an MT103 wire is sent to the bank account opened in the SARL's name, using tag 70 to state clearly 'Capital subscription - [SARL name] - incorporation' or, for loans, 'Shareholder loan - [date]', accompanied by the BCL statistical classification for direct-investment equity (FDI equity capital) rather than a generic or ambiguous code. The Luxembourg receiving bank credits a blocked capital account and delivers an attestation de blocage to the notary, which is mandatory evidence for signing the incorporation deed and filing with the RCS; if the injection is in USD or another foreign currency, the bank may spot-convert to EUR and must evidence the conversion rate applied; funds are released only after RCS registration. EUR clearing uses TARGET2, USD clearing uses the CHIPS/Fedwire network through the Luxembourg bank's US correspondent; signatories must provide source-of-funds declarations and the bank-completed UBO/PEP interview, and all cross-border movements are captured automatically in BCL balance-of-payments statistics.Supplier and logistics payments are executed principally by direct SWIFT MT103 transfers for TT settlement and by irrevocable commercial letters of credit issued under ICC UCP 600 for significant Chinese, Indian, Egyptian or Spanish purchases, covering presentation of commercial invoice, bill of lading, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and independent inspection certificate; standby letters of credit under URDG 758 may secure performance obligations. EUR payment legs settle via SEPA credit transfers (ISO 20022 pain.001) and TARGET2; USD legs clear via CHIPS or Fedwire through the Luxembourg account bank's USD correspondent. Every disbursement must carry the appropriate BCL balance-of-payments classification for imports of goods, must be reconciled with underlying contracts and customs declarations, and must pass EU and OFAC sanctions screening before release; documentary collections on a DP/DA basis may be used for smaller quantities and established counterparties.Outright EUR/USD forward contracts documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement and Credit Support Annex, used to lock settlement margins on export receipts and import procurement without speculative intent.Foreign exchange swaps employed intra-company to bridge short-term USD liquidity against EUR treasury and to roll forward open positions without affecting the company's long-term FX risk profile.Cross-currency swaps (XCCY) for medium-term alignment of USD disbursement legs with EUR receipt flows, subject to ISDA documentation and EMIR margin and clearing obligations.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in restricted, non-convertible or high-volatility currencies such as offshore CNY, INR or EGP, permitted under EU law for hedging demonstrable physical import and export exposures and strictly disallowed for speculative flows.Vanilla purchased currency options to protect trade tender margins, subject to EMIR transaction reporting and prohibited under the SARL treasury policy when used for leveraged or speculative optionality.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required if related-party transactions exceed EUR 1 million; Master File required if group consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 50 million.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg applies Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) for certain activities (e.g., IP, financing). For a trading company, adequate substance (physical office, qualified staff, decision-making in Luxembourg) is required to avoid being classified as a shell company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; additional tax penalties of 10% to 50% of the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for unpaid taxes if they knowingly participated in evasion; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of tax benefits, reclassification of income, and potential administrative fines up to EUR 250,000.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held liable for unpaid taxes and penalties if they fail to ensure substance; potential criminal charges for fraud.
AML / Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Fines up to EUR 1 million for serious breaches; suspension of business license.

Director personal liability: Directors can face personal fines and imprisonment up to 5 years for willful AML violations; travel bans may be issued.
