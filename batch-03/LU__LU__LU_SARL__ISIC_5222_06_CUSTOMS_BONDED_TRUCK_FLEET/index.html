Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum share capital EUR 12,000 (approximately USD 13,000), fully subscribed and fully paid up before RCS registration; share capital must be deposited in a Luxembourg or EEA credit institution in a blocked account, with the notary obtaining a blocking certificate evidencing availability of funds.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification issued by the Corps grand-ducal d’incendie et de secours (CGDIS) for parking, loading bays, and any fuel storage; structural load inspection report by an approved ingenieur-conseil for warehouse and dock floors; workplace occupancy and occupational health clearance from the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM).

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum share capital EUR 12,000 (approximately USD 13,000), fully subscribed and fully paid up before RCS registration; share capital must be deposited in a Luxembourg or EEA credit institution in a blocked account, with the notary obtaining a blocking certificate evidencing availability of funds.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) competent for corporate income tax, net wealth tax and municipal business tax, and Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AED) competent for VAT and registration duties; joint online filing through Guichet.lu / MyGuichet.lu portal and eCDF electronic filing system

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must first obtain a Luxembourg business registration number (matricule) from the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) through MyGuichet.lu, then file the standard VAT identification form (Formulaire 640) with the AED; non-established taxable persons must appoint a Luxembourg VAT representative and are subject to mandatory VAT registration without a turnover threshold; the issued VAT identification number follows the format LU followed by 8 digits

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity must first obtain a Luxembourg business registration number (matricule) from the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) through MyGuichet.lu, then file the standard VAT identification form (Formulaire 640) with the AED; non-established taxable persons must appoint a Luxembourg VAT representative and are subject to mandatory VAT registration without a turnover threshold; the issued VAT identification number follows the format LU followed by 8 digits

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election is available for an SARL; statutory elections include (i) VAT grouping under Article 5(7) of the Luxembourg VAT Law to create a single taxable person, (ii) joint taxation of qualifying 75% group companies under Article 164bis LIR, and (iii) election of the EU OSS/IOSS simplification regimes; hybrid mismatch elections are constrained by the Luxembourg ATAD implementation

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual turnover threshold for the resident small-business exemption; non-established foreign suppliers making taxable supplies in Luxembourg must register mandatorily without any threshold unless the supply is subject to reverse charge or covered by the EU OSS return mechanism

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Article 56 of the Luxembourg VAT Law to goods physically exported outside the EU upon production of proof of dispatch and exit; ancillary transport services, including GPS-sealed bonded truck feeder services, qualify under Article 44(1)(h) where directly linked to international transport of goods or to an EU customs export/transit procedure (e.g., TIR or NCTS transit); the service must be rendered for the benefit of the importer/exporter or transport principal

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD) or transit declaration with proof of exit from the EU
  • CMR International Consignment Note (Convention on the Contract for the International Carriage of Goods by Road)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for the underlying international shipment
  • GPS sealing records and unbroken customs seal certificates evidencing bonded movement integrity
  • Customs bonded warehouse or authorized consignor/consignee authorization reference
  • Commercial invoice, transport contract and bank payment / inward remittance certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping or continuous transaction control mandate applies in Luxembourg; B2B e-invoicing is optional under standard EU VAT rules, while B2G e-invoicing for public contracts is mandatory through the national PEPPOL-based e-invoicing platform; SAF-T reporting is the accepted electronic audit standard

Unjustified export zero-rating is clawed back by the AED together with default interest calculated from the original return due date; fraudulent claims are prosecuted as VAT fraud under Article 14 of the Law of 10 August 1992, carrying imprisonment of 8 days to 5 years and fines of EUR 250 to EUR 25,000 per count, plus revocation of customs carrier and bonded warehouse authorizations and senior management criminal exposure

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg applies unrestricted convertibility for both current account and capital account operations pursuant to Articles 63-65 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. The euro is fully convertible, and no exchange control approval is required for profit remittance, dividend distributions, loan servicing, or capital repatriation by a Luxembourg SARL. Any procedural constraint arises solely from EU AML/tax transparency rules and the Luxembourg balance of payments statistical reporting regime, not from convertibility restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion requirement applies to export proceeds in Luxembourg. The SARL may retain export earnings in EUR or any permitted foreign currency. Export-related settlements are subject only to the BCL balance of payments monthly reporting obligations for transactions at or above EUR 50,000 in aggregate, and to standard commercial documentation retention under VAT and customs rules.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) under the Eurosystem framework of the European Central Bank (ECB); conduct supervision of banking and payment institutions is exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium. Only EEA-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) holding a passport into Luxembourg under the EU Payment Services Directive (PSD2) may provide EUR-denominated payment accounts and clearing services. Offshore digital EMIs based in third countries are materially constrained: they cannot provide Luxembourg IBANs, cannot access TARGET2 directly, and are not recognized as eligible settlement institutions by the BCL. EMI accounts are limited to payment services and are not suitable for custody, escrow, documentary credit, or material treasury liquidity. For primary operating treasury, a Tier-1 domestic bank remains mandatory.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Unexplained source of initial capital contribution from foreign parent

Root cause: Unexplained source of initial capital contribution from foreign parent

Defense evidence: Provide audited group financial statements, a consolidated organizational chart, the parent's board resolution approving the capital contribution, bank statements of the remitting entity, share subscription agreements, and clear evidence that the contribution is recorded in the SARL's statutory accounts as share capital or shareholder loan.
Insufficient economic substance for a Luxembourg road-freight services SARL

Root cause: Insufficient economic substance for a Luxembourg road-freight services SARL

Defense evidence: Submit a substance file containing lease agreement for registered office, local management mandates, minutes of annual general meetings, payroll evidence or payroll service provider contracts, vehicle parking and maintenance agreements, GPS fleet telematics summary, commercial contracts with airlines and customs agents, and a realistic staffing and revenue projection.
Customs-bonded logistics transactions raising ambiguity on beneficial ownership

Root cause: Customs-bonded logistics transactions raising ambiguity on beneficial ownership

Defense evidence: Produce the customs authorization or NCTS transit license, transport and freight forwarding contracts, CMR / air waybills, issued and received invoices, customs warehouse or bonded truck agreement, insurance coverage certificates, and a complete register of all ultimate beneficial owners (%25 or higher) filed with the Luxembourg Registre des bénéficiaires effectifs (LBR).
High-volume cross-border disbursements for fleet acquisition without supporting trade documentation

Root cause: High-volume cross-border disbursements for fleet acquisition without supporting trade documentation

Defense evidence: Assemble signed purchase agreements for trucks and trailers, pro-forma and commercial invoices, export licenses or safety certificates, financing agreements, transfer-pricing documentation if intra-group, bank-approved payment purpose codes, and an explanation of clearing and settlement timelines.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for inbound EUR wires: use SWIFT MT103 from the parent company via a correspondent or SEPA Credit Transfer for smaller amounts. The payment must reference the underlying purpose code under BCL balance of payments statistics, typically 'Direct investment – Subscription/contribution to equity' (ESS 310) or 'Loans between affiliated enterprises' (ESS 321). To pass bank compliance, provide the executed loan or subscription agreement, group resolution, an estimated treasury cash-flow plan, and professional source-of-funds evidence. All transfers above EUR 10,000 require accurate ordering information; EU Regulation 2023/1113 on information accompanying transfers must be complied with.For travel of trade settlements, the SARL should operate through Luxembourg SWIFT-connected banks using MT103 messages with ISO 20022 remittance details. Clearing rails available: SEPA Credit Transfer for intra-EUR payments, SWIFT MT103 for extra-EU supplier payments, and commercial letters of credit issued or confirmed by a Luxembourg bank for high-value fleet and fuel procurement. Each disbursement must be matched to underlying invoices, transport contracts, and customs documentation.
