Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

The SARL must have a minimum issued share capital of EUR 12,000. All shares must be fully subscribed and paid up at incorporation. Cash contributions must be remitted to a blocked escrow account opened in the name of the proposed company with a Luxembourg credit institution or to the notary's client account, with a bank confirmation certificate delivered to the notary. Non-cash contributions require a report by an approved statutory auditor (réviseur d'entreprises agréé).

Funding & unlock path: Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) workplace registration and health and safety inspection certificate for any non-seagoing employees; fire safety attestation from the Corps Grand-Ducal d'Incendie et de Secours for occupied premises; municipal structural load clearance for any workshop or storage facility; seagoing staff must hold Luxembourg maritime medical fitness certificates and STCW-compliant qualifications.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct corporate taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for Luxembourg VAT; central e-Government filing portal: myguichet.lu; direct tax e-filing: e-Tax (ACD); VAT e-filing: eTVA (AEDT).

E-government filing portal

For a Luxembourg SARL with foreign shareholders: (1) incorporate before a Luxembourg notary and register with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) to obtain the national business number (matricule). (2) If taxable supplies are made in Luxembourg, register for VAT with the AEDT via the standard VAT registration application filed through myguichet.lu; the AEDT issues the Luxembourg VAT ID (TVA). (3) A non-established foreign entity making taxable supplies in Luxembourg must appoint a Luxembourg VAT representative, jointly and severally liable for VAT, pursuant to Art. 61 of the Luxembourg VAT Law. (4) Any permanent establishment created in Luxembourg also requires separate RCS registration and the corresponding ACD tax number.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — For a Luxembourg SARL with foreign shareholders: (1) incorporate before a Luxembourg notary and register with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) to obtain the national business number (matricule). (2) If taxable supplies are made in Luxembourg, register for VAT with the AEDT via the standard VAT registration application filed through myguichet.lu; the AEDT issues the Luxembourg VAT ID (TVA). (3) A non-established foreign entity making taxable supplies in Luxembourg must appoint a Luxembourg VAT representative, jointly and severally liable for VAT, pursuant to Art. 61 of the Luxembourg VAT Law. (4) Any permanent establishment created in Luxembourg also requires separate RCS registration and the corresponding ACD tax number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

An SARL is a resident capital company and may not elect partnership pass-through status; Luxembourg does not provide a check-the-box regime. Available elections include: VAT fiscal unity (Art. 60 VAT Law) for closely related persons; urban income tax / CIT fiscal integration under Art. 164bis LIR; exemption of small enterprises from VAT (Art. 57 VAT Law) where the annual turnover threshold is met; and the participation exemption regime (Art. 166 LIR) for qualifying shareholdings. Hybrid mismatch and ATAD rules apply by statutory operation rather than elective check-the-box.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold for non-established foreign entities; VAT registration is compulsory before the first taxable supply in Luxembourg. Domestic small enterprises may apply for a VAT exemption franchise under Art. 57 VAT Law where annual turnover does not exceed EUR 35,000 (VAT-exclusive).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% VAT applies to goods dispatched or transported to a destination outside the EU (Art. 41 VAT Law / Art. 146 EU VAT Directive), supported by documentary proof of removal. Services: offshore towage and salvage services qualify for 0% VAT under Art. 43 Luxembourg VAT Law / Art. 148 EU VAT Directive where such services are supplied directly for the needs of qualifying sea-going vessels used in international or high-seas navigation, or for their equipment, cargo or crew. The direct-needs requirement and the vessel's international-navigation status must be contractually evidenced; zero-rating is not automatic for services supplied to non-vessel owners or for shore-side services later time-chartered.

  • Customs export declaration (SAD) accepted by Luxembourg or relevant EU customs authorities
  • Bill of lading / sea-waybill or equivalent transport document showing departure from EU customs territory
  • Commercial invoice and the underlying towage, salvage, or affreightment contract (e.g., Lloyd's Open Form)
  • Proof of payment and inward remittance certificate (bank SWIFT MT103 or equivalent evidence of hard currency repatriation)
  • Ship registry certificate or declaration by the vessel owner confirming international navigation (e.g., trading outside EU waters)
  • Vessel logbook, port call itinerary, or GPS/route documentation evidencing actual deep-sea use

Luxembourg does not currently impose mandatory B2B e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, SAF-T continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping. B2G e-invoicing is mandatory for invoices to Luxembourg central public-sector bodies through the PEPPOL BIS 3.0 network. Standard VAT invoice rules continue to apply and no API-based clearance or digital stamping is in force.

The AEDT and ACD may jointly audit export zero-rating claims and require full documentary substantiation within the statutory retention period. If zero-rating is denied or unsupported, VAT is reassessed at the standard rate of 17% together with delay interest; administrative fines of 50% up to 200% of the evaded tax may be imposed, with higher penalties for intentional fraud; Luxembourg criminal sanctions may include imprisonment. Input VAT already claimed or refunded in respect of the zero-rated export is clawed back with statutory interest, fresh export refunds are suspended, and the limitation period is extended to 10 years in fraudulent cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. Luxembourg applies EU capital movement liberalisation under Articles 63–66 TFEU with no general exchange control restrictions. Profit remittance is unrestricted for non-resident shareholders, subject only to standard corporate income tax (IRC), municipal business tax (ICB), and net wealth tax (IFI) compliance, plus withholding tax on dividends (15% standard, reduced under tax treaties or the parent-subsidiary directive). No prior BCL authorisation is required for dividend distributions where the SARL's net assets remain above the minimum share capital threshold (€12,370 for a standard SARL).

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender obligation exists in Luxembourg. Invoices billed in EUR or foreign currency for offshore towing and salvage services may be held freely onshore or offshore. However, the BCL requires statistical reporting of cross-border payments in excess of €12,500 per transaction under Regulation BCE/2013/33 and BCL Circular 2011/240, for balance-of-payments statistical purposes only; this does not constitute a repatriation or conversion mandate.

Central bank supervision: Banque Centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) – Luxembourg Monetary Institute and member of the Eurosystem; foreign exchange policy competence resides with the European Central Bank (ECB) within the euro area framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low for primary treasury use; acceptable only as secondary payment aggregation rails. Luxembourg-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) under CSSF supervision (e.g., emerchantpay, Satispay-style operators) may hold e-money and process disbursements, but the CSSF requires such institutions to escrow client funds in a segregated account at a credit institution. Offshore EMIs (UK FCA or Lithuania LB regulated) are legally usable for ancillary EUR collections under EU passporting, but their credit-rating and weak custody protections make them unsuitable as primary clearing rails for salvage contract deposits and LOF security. Due to the Medium_Risk_Enhanced AML classification of ISIC 5222, the CSSF-regulated banks will likely reject offshore EMI-sourced funds for the first 12 months of the banking relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injection from a non-EU parent must be paid by SWIFT MT103 to the SARL's BCL-licensed bank account with the purpose code under EU BOP Statistics classification 'S.12' (other investment) or Code 22 (equity capital contribution), with the free-text field stating 'Apport en capital – SARL Luxembourg, augmentation de capital social'. Verification requires: attribution of the funds to the SARL's notarised articles of association and the BCL statistical survey declaration (collecte BCL) where the transaction exceeds €12,500. Where the capital injection exceeds €20 million, ECB notification is required under Regulation (EU) No 1333/2014 on balance-of-payments statistics, but no prior approval is needed. Routing must avoid so-called 'shell-company banking' red flags: the remitter must be a bank-account holder, not an anonymous intermediary, and the SARL must receive the funds in the same name as reflected in its registration certificate (extrait du RCS).Salvage and towage trade settlements should be cleared via SWIFT MT103 on a standard 'our cost' basis for sub-threshold disbursements, and via irrevocable documentary credit (Commercial LC, UCP 600) for vessel purchase, bunkering, or sub-contract salvage payments above €250,000. For emergency salvage operations, the target rails are Lloyds Standard Form Wire Credits via the Lloyd's Salvage Branch and P&I club security providers, using ISO 20022 pain.001/camt.103 messages for corporate-to-bank initiation. The FX leg (where subcontractors invoice in USD, GBP, or NOK) must use covered SPOT FX executed via the bank's electronic FX portal with deal ticket capture to satisfy CSSF best-execution under MiFID II. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% combined Luxembourg City marginal rate (base CIT 17% + unemployment fund surcharge 1.19% + municipal business tax 6.75%)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% domestic Luxembourg dividend withholding tax; 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for qualifying corporate shareholders holding at least 10% for an uninterrupted 12-month period (refund available if the 12-month condition is fulfilled after distribution); reduced rates from 0% to 15% under applicable double tax treaties

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000,000 consolidated annual group turnover threshold for mandatory Master File and Country-by-Country Report; Local File and supporting TP documentation must be prepared where cross-border related-party transactions (towage charter hire, salvage fees, management fees, intercompany financing) exist, irrespective of size, and produced to the ACD within 45 days of audit request

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not enact a standalone Economic Substance Regulations statute (as in BVI/Cayman), but the domestic GAAR (Article 6 LIR), ATAD rules and CJEU jurisprudence impose a substantive economic substance test: the SARL must have a physical office, operative employees, locally taken management decisions and genuine offshore towage/salvage activity to benefit from treaty relief, the participation exemption and the 0% parent-subsidiary dividend WHT. Artificial letterbox arrangements are disregarded and recharacterized

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax Returns

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of all evaded taxes plus 0.8% monthly late interest, administrative penalty up to EUR 25,000, criminal fine up to EUR 25,000 and/or imprisonment up to five years under the General Tax Law (AO sections 396-399), publication of conviction, and loss of professional licenses for maritime towage and salvage

Director personal liability: Directors and managers who order or knowingly accept the false filing are jointly and severally liable for the tax deficiency; criminal courts may order restitution of personal assets, travel bans, prohibition from managing commercial entities, and imprisonment up to five years
Artificial Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The ACD disallows the participation exemption and treaty benefits, recharacterizes intercompany cash flows as constructive dividends, imposes the full 24.94% combined CIT plus 0.8% monthly interest and an administrative penalty up to EUR 25,000; in cases of intentional abuse, criminal tax fraud sanctions may be added

Director personal liability: Directors face civil liability for the unpaid tax under the unlawful distribution doctrine of Luxembourg Company Law, travel bans in cross-border investigations, and criminal sanctions if the artificial arrangement was knowingly structured
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Ex officio upward TP adjustment of the Luxembourg taxable profit, recharacterization of undisclosed intra-group transactions, 0.8% monthly late interest, administrative fine up to EUR 25,000 for missing documentation, and double taxation exposure without MAP relief

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for the unpaid tax and interest if the omission resulted from fraud or gross negligence; deliberate TP fraud escalates to criminal tax evasion liability with potential imprisonment
AML / Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Violation

Statutory sanction: CSSF and ACD administrative fines up to EUR 1,000,000 or 10% of annual turnover; criminal AML penalties of imprisonment up to five years and fines up to EUR 5,000,000; RBE registration violations carry criminal fines up to EUR 250,000; escrow accounts may be frozen by the Luxembourg Financial Intelligence Unit (CRF)

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly facilitate maritime salvage money laundering are criminally liable, with seizure of personal assets, travel bans, withdrawal of the towage operating license, and imprisonment up to five years
Unlawful Dividend Repatriation and WHT Evasion

Statutory sanction: The full 15% dividend WHT becomes immediately recoverable from the withholding agent, plus 0.8% monthly late interest and a 10% additional tax penalty; criminal fine up to EUR 25,000 or imprisonment if intent is proven

Director personal liability: Withholding agent liability is extended personally to directors who authorized the unlawful distribution; they face civil recovery, travel bans, personal bankruptcy exposure and imprisonment for deliberate WHT evasion
