Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) – Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS) via the eJustice online portal.

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (Loi du 10 août 1915 concernant les sociétés commerciales), notably Articles 180 to 205 governing the Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL).

Formation / constitutional filings

Acte constitutif (notarised deed of incorporation) together with the coordinated articles of association, submitted electronically by the Luxembourg notary to the RCS.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 26 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

EUR 12,000 minimum issued and fully paid-up share capital, fully subscribed at incorporation; the capital must be deposited in a blocked bank account opened in the name of the company in formation, with the bank's blocking certificate (attestation de blocage) produced to the notary.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance evidenced by a certificate from the Corps Grand-Ducal d'Incendie et de Secours (CGDIS) or communal fire service; structural and building safety approval from the municipal building authority; workplace health-and-safety clearance by the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) covering electrical safety, emergency exits, ventilation, and occupational risk assessment.

Paid-in capital rule: EUR 12,000 minimum issued and fully paid-up share capital, fully subscribed at incorporation; the capital must be deposited in a blocked bank account opened in the name of the company in formation, with the bank's blocking certificate (attestation de blocage) produced to the notary.

All resolutions are effective immediately.

Category: Autorisation d'établissement (Business Establishment Licence) for food and beverage serving activities under the Law of 2 September 2011 on the access to professions and the exercise of services, supplemented by the communal commercial operating permit.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) — Luxembourg direct tax administration — and Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) — VAT, registration and excise duties. Unified portal: MyGuichet.lu (https://guichet.public.lu/en.html).

E-government filing portal

For foreign founders establishing an SARL in Luxembourg: (1) complete incorporation with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) through MyGuichet, obtaining the 8-digit national matricule; (2) apply for VAT using AEDT Form 604 or eTVA online application, with non-established foreign suppliers appointing a Luxembourg tax representative when no permanent establishment exists; (3) the VAT ID is issued in the format 'LU' followed by 8 digits; the same national number serves as the statutory TIN for corporate income, municipal business and net wealth tax purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — For foreign founders establishing an SARL in Luxembourg: (1) complete incorporation with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) through MyGuichet, obtaining the 8-digit national matricule; (2) apply for VAT using AEDT Form 604 or eTVA online application, with non-established foreign suppliers appointing a Luxembourg tax representative when no permanent establishment exists; (3) the VAT ID is issued in the format 'LU' followed by 8 digits; the same national number serves as the statutory TIN for corporate income, municipal business and net wealth tax purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Luxembourg domestic tax law does not provide a check-the-box election for SARLs: an SARL is a per se taxable corporation resulting in CIT/MBT/NWT exposure with no pass-through option. Available elections include VAT grouping (assujetti unique) for economically/financially linked persons and fiscal unity (intégration fiscale) for 95%+ resident group losses/income. Hybrid mismatch rules (ATAD) apply; only specific structured arrangement timing elections are possible and no elective classification for legal form is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover (excluding VAT) under the Luxembourg small-business exemption; EUR 10,000 EU-wide threshold for distance sales of goods/e-services; non-established suppliers generally must register for Luxembourg VAT when making taxable supplies unless reverse charge or customer self-assessment applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAT) is available for physical exports of goods to destinations outside the EU where the supplier retains evidence of dispatch/transportation within the applicable deadline (generally one month after supply). Intra-EU dispatches to a valid EU VAT number may be zero-rated/exempt subject to correct reporting. Services supplied to non-EU customers are normally outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT under place-of-supply rules. On-premises tea salon and lounge services delivered in Luxembourg are taxable at the reduced sit-down rate and are not eligible for export zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/NCTS export declaration through Luxembourg Customs)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List where applicable
  • Transport Document (CMR, Bill of Lading or Air Waybill) evidencing movement from Luxembourg to the non-EU destination
  • Proof of physical exit from the EU (customs validation or destination customs entry stamp)
  • Proof of payment for the export supply (e.g. bank credit advice, SWIFT/MT103)
  • Inward remittance/foreign currency receipt evidence to substantiate payment repatriation

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or continuous transaction control (CTC) mandate exists in Luxembourg for B2B/B2C retail. E-invoicing is permitted where authenticity, integrity and legibility are guaranteed; B2G invoices must be transmitted via a PEPPOL-compliant platform under EU Directive 2014/55/EU. Luxembourg VAT returns are submitted periodically through the eTVA/MyGuichet portal with no government pre-clearance of invoices.

Luxembourg tax and customs authorities may conduct retrospective audits of export substantiation. Failure to provide complete statutory evidence invalidates zero-rating, and VAT becomes due with default interest (monthly late-payment interest), administrative fines up to EUR 25,000 per infringement, and, in fraud cases, criminal penalties including additional fines and imprisonment. Wrongly claimed input VAT or zero-rated export treatment is clawed back with statutory interest from the original filing date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg maintains full capital and current account convertibility as a member of the EU and the Eurozone, consistent with OECD and IMF Article VIII obligations. No foreign exchange controls restrict currency conversion or transfers for the SARL's capital contribution, profit repatriation, dividend distribution, or operational settlements. Dividend distributions are subject to Luxembourg withholding tax at 15% under domestic law, but may be reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive or 5% / 0% under applicable double tax treaties, provided corporate substance, beneficial ownership, and compliance with anti-abuse provisions are satisfied. Cross-border payments above EUR 12,500 must be reported for balance-of-payments statistics by the ordering bank or the reporting institution to the BCL, but this is a statistical and monitoring mechanism, not a restrictive control.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory obligation exists to surrender, repatriate, or mandatorily convert export proceeds into EUR within a specified timeline. Luxembourg law adheres to EU free movement of capital principles, allowing the Specialty Tea Salon Lounge to hold foreign currency proceeds offshore or in domestic multi-currency accounts, provided underlying transactions are bona fide and anti-money laundering record-keeping requirements are met. Banks may require commercial invoices and shipping documents to validate export flows, but there is no legal conversion ratio or surrender deadline.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) together with the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) as the competent prudential and foreign exchange operations supervisor within the Eurosystem.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility for offshore, non-EU digital EMI (Electronic Money Institution) was assessed as LOW due to absence of a Luxembourg or EU authorisation and the failure to qualify for passporting. EU/EEA-licensed EMIs operating under PSD2 (e.g., established in Lithuania, Ireland, or the Netherlands) may provide ancillary payment accounts and card issuance through EEA passport notification to the CSSF; however, they cannot act as the SARL's primary settlement bank for commercial deposit-taking, trade finance, or escrow of statutory share capital. The thematic cash management account must ultimately be held with a fully-fledged credit institution established or passported in Luxembourg to ensure regulatory protection, insolvency ring-fencing, and integration with SEPA / TARGET2.

Defense evidence: Comprehensive dossier including formal PEP risk assessment by the Luxembourg registered agent; source of wealth and source of funds documentation with authenticated bank references; valid passport, tax residence certificate, and proof of the owner's legitimate business history; notarised explanation of the underlying investment rationale; commitment to transactional limits; enhanced ongoing monitoring acceptance letter.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and shareholder loan injections should be routed via SWIFT MT103 (single payment) or MT202 (bank-to-bank cover) in EUR to the SARL's EUR account at a Luxembourg credit institution, with a purpose code aligned to BPM6 / ECB statistics. For equity capital subscription, use the purpose code 'S0102' (Investment in capital of non-financial corporations) or equivalent BCL statistical purpose code; for shareholder loans use 'S0101' (Investment in debt of non-financial corporations). The sending bank must reference the beneficiary account name exactly as per RCS, include the ISIN or RCS number, and provide underlying evidence (signed subscription agreements, notarised deed, bank reference) to the receiving Luxembourg bank. For amounts above EUR 50,000, the Luxembourg bank will file the mandatory balance-of-payments report with the BCL within the statutory deadline, but no exchange-control approval is required. A bank confirmation of receipt can also serve as the statutory capital certificate (attestation bancaire) required to register the SARL with the RCS and before a notary.Outbound settlement for specialty tea imports and ceramic salon inventories shall primarily use SWIFT MT103 direct transfers in EUR or major currencies, and for high-value or first-time vendor relationships, commercial letters of credit (LC) issued by the Luxembourg bank (SWIFT MT700) will cover documentary risk. Within the EEA, SEPA Credit Transfers and SEPA Instant Credit Transfers are the lowest-cost standard rails. For imports from tea-producing jurisdictions (e.g., Japan, China, India, Taiwan), the bank may require invoices, packing lists, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificates, and transport documents; for LC settlements, full set of clean on board bills of lading, commercial invoice, insurance certificate, and beneficiary drafts must be presented within the LC expiry.
