Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers — Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (LBR-RCS), operated under the authority of the Luxembourg Ministry of Justice.

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended, Articles 1-10, 50-55 and 57-68 (Société à Responsabilité Limitée); Law of 19 December 2002 on the Register of Commerce and Companies and the accounting duties of undertakings; Grand-Ducal Regulation of 23 January 2003 on the RCS electronic filing procedure.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarial deed of incorporation (acte constitutif de la Société à Responsabilité Limitée) submitted electronically through the e-RCS portal using RCS Form E1.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 24 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum share capital of EUR 12,000.00 fully subscribed; at incorporation each share must be paid up to at least 25% of its nominal value, and any share premium must be fully paid; the paid-up capital must be deposited in a blocked company escrow account (compte bloqué) opened at a Luxembourg credit institution, and the bank's blocked-funds certificate is annexed to the notarial deed before registration with the RCS.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: the leased premises must obtain a favourable fire-prevention review by the Corps Grand-Ducal d'Incendie et de Secours (CGDIS) and the municipal safety commission, including the evacuation plan, fire alarm and extinguishing systems; Structural: an occupancy certificate or building licence (certificat d'occupation) confirming structural load capacity and safety conformity; Occupational: registration with the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM), a documented workplace risk assessment, verification of electrical installations, adequate welfare facilities, and first-aid arrangements as required by the Labour Code.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum share capital of EUR 12,000.00 fully subscribed; at incorporation each share must be paid up to at least 25% of its nominal value, and any share premium must be fully paid; the paid-up capital must be deposited in a blocked company escrow account (compte bloqué) opened at a Luxembourg credit institution, and the bank's blocked-funds certificate is annexed to the notarial deed before registration with the RCS.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; unified digital portal MyGuichet (myguichet.lu).

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities registering for VAT must complete form 640/TVA, submit proof of incorporation, copy of statutes, and a power of attorney. Non-EU companies must appoint a Luxembourg fiscal representative accepted by AEDT. For corporate income tax, registration with ACD follows incorporation with the Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS), including filing of annual accounts, director identification, and appointment of an approved auditor when applicable.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign entities registering for VAT must complete form 640/TVA, submit proof of incorporation, copy of statutes, and a power of attorney. Non-EU companies must appoint a Luxembourg fiscal representative accepted by AEDT. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

VAT grouping (assujetti unique) election under Article 62 of the VAT Law; optional small undertaking exemption election under Article 57 (annual turnover below EUR 35,000); tax consolidation election for resident subsidiaries under the CIT Law. No check-the-box election exists; hybrid mismatch notifications apply under ATAD.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for existing small undertakings (exemption); new undertakings must not project first-year turnover above EUR 25,000. Distance sales into Luxembourg exceeding EUR 100,000 trigger registration; no threshold applies to non-established suppliers making taxable B2B supplies in Luxembourg.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to exports of goods to non-EU countries where the exporter possesses a customs export declaration and evidenced dispatch from EU territory within three months. B2B services supplied to a customer established outside the EU are outside Luxembourg VAT when the customer possesses a non-EU VAT identification number and actual use and enjoyment occurs outside the EU. Intra-Community supplies are also zero-rated with the customer's EU VAT ID and Intrastat filing where applicable.

  • Customs Export Declaration (EX-E)
  • Commercial Invoice (three originals)
  • Transport documents (CMR / Bill of Lading / Air Waybill)
  • Proof of payment (SWIFT / Bank transfer certificate)
  • Dispatch register and transport order
  • Contract or purchase order

Mandatory B2G e-invoicing via PEPPOL (UBL/CII); no compulsory domestic B2B real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime in force as of 2025. E-invoices are acceptable provided the authenticity of origin and integrity of content are guaranteed.

Luxembourg VAT authorities may retroactively adjust zero-rating for up to four years when export substantiation is incomplete. Reassessment carries interest at 0.8% per month, a civil fine of 10% of the VAT amount, and monetary penalties up to EUR 4,000. Fraudulent export refund claims trigger clawback of refunded VAT, joint liability under Article 73 VAT Law, and criminal prosecution with potential imprisonment in cases of serious VAT fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a full euro-area member with complete current account and capital account convertibility under the EC Treaties and the EU Capital Movement Directive (2015/2361). There are no exchange control restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distribution, shareholder loans, or capital repatriation. A SARL may freely maintain accounts in EUR or any foreign currency, although statutory accounts must be presented in EUR for filing. Withholding tax leakage on dividends is reduced by tax treaties or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive. No prior central bank approval is required for inbound or outbound transfers, subject only to reporting thresholds for balance-of-payments statistics and anti-money laundering verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement in Luxembourg. All export receipts in convertible currencies may be retained, sold, or hedged at the exporter's discretion. Settlement of export invoices is subject to the general AML/CFT transaction monitoring rules of the CSSF and requires accurate economic purpose and underlying commercial documentation.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) and, with delegated prudential supervision, Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for banking and financial instruments

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-to-High for EU-authorized Electronic Money Institutions passported into Luxembourg under PSD2 (e.g., Wise or Revolut Business) for operational EUR transfers. For non-EU offshore digital EMIs, the legal constraint is significant: they may not hold a bank license in Luxembourg, cannot provide lending or safeguarded investment products, and are normally rejected for high-value treasury clearing because they lack a Luxembourg branch or CSSF passport. If the SaaS company needs commercial bank-grade counterparty risk, an EU EMI is complements a T1 bank account rather than replacing one.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions (share capital or shareholder loans) are wired via SWIFT MT103 to the SARL's euro-denominated account at a Luxembourg Bank. The wire must cite the bank's required reference fields: Beneficiary Legal Name, VAT/EIN if applicable, and the payment purpose code covering 'Inbound capital injection' (under the ECS/BCL external statistics classification). For share capital, the type code 'S030' (equity securities) or the ECB Balance-of-Payments event code for cross-border equity injection should be used; a copy of the notarised share subscription deed and the RCS registration excerpt must be retained. The sending financial institution should include the full corporate identifier and the specific mention 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' in the field 70 (Remittance Information). The BCL requires the receiving bank to report the transaction in the balance-of-payments statistic when the amount exceeds EUR 12,500; however, no prior regulatory authorization is required.Outbound trade settlements for B2B SaaS (licensing, intercompany royalties, cloud infrastructure charges) are cleared primarily via SWIFT MT103 (for non-SEPA / non-EUR currencies) or SEPA Instant / SEPA Credit Transfer for euro payments within the EEA. Where a commercial invoice is backed by a bank payment guarantee, the SARL may use a Documentary Collection or an Irrevocable Confirmed Commercial Letter of Credit. The MT103 must include the proper remittance information, the underlying invoice numbers, and the contractual references.
