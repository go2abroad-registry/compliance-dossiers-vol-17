Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is not permissible as the sole statutory registered office (siège social) of a Luxembourg SARL. The company must maintain a genuine physical office presence in Luxembourg where the statutory books, accounting records, shareholders' register and board minutes are kept and where mail can be received by duly authorized persons. A serviced office or administrative agent arrangement may be acceptable only if supported by a genuine lease or service agreement, display of the company name, and substantive indicators such as the availability of premises, personnel, local decision-taking and engine lease management functions; these elements are decisive for Luxembourg tax residence and for the OECD and Luxembourg economic substance requirements applicable to an aviation dry-leasing vehicle.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) - Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), Ministry of Justice; e-filing via the RCS Online portal at www.lbr.lu and the State portal www.guichet.lu; notarial oversight by the Chambre des Notaires du Luxembourg.

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (Loi modifiée du 10 août 1915 concernant les sociétés commerciales), Title V, Articles 710-1 to 710-27 (S.à r.l. regime); Law of 19 December 2002 on the Register of Commerce and Companies and the Annual Accounts (RCS Law), Articles 1-8; Law of 31 May 1999 on the fire and rescue service; Law of 2 September 2011 on access to the professions of craft, commerce and industry (optional establishment authorization); Law of 12 November 2004 on the fight against money laundering (AML risk tier: Medium-Risk Enhanced).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarial deed of incorporation (acte constitutif) containing the articles of association (statuts), executed before a Luxembourg notary in French, filed electronically with the RCS by the notary within one month of execution, and published in the official electronic gazette RESA (Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations). Regulated notarial tariffs (approx. EUR 1,500-3,000) and bank charges are payable separately from the state fee.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum share capital EUR 12,000, fully subscribed; each part (share) must be paid up to at least 20% of its nominal value at incorporation (aggregate minimum EUR 2,400), deposited into a blocked escrow account (compte bloqué) in the name of the S.à r.l. en formation at a Luxembourg credit institution; the bank blocking certificate is provided to the notary and funds released only upon RCS registration. For aircraft-engine dry-leasing SPEs, full payment of the stated capital is market practice. The former EUR 1 simplified S.à r.l.-S regime has been abolished by the Law of 23 September 2020.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD) - Luxembourg direct tax administration, corporate income tax and net wealth tax; Administration de l'Enregistrement, des Domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) - VAT, registration duties, excise and customs (including EORI issuance); Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS) - business register; unified e-government portal: MyGuichet.lu (Guichet Unique) for company registration, VAT registration and annual declarations.

E-government filing portal

For a Luxembourg SARL: (1) incorporation is completed before a Luxembourg notary and the coordinated articles of association are filed with the RCS through the Guichet Unique within one month; (2) the RCS issues the registration extract (extrait) confirming the RCS number (format B-xxxxxx); the national identification number (matricule) is automatically issued by the State IT Centre (CTIE); (3) the AEDT automatically allocates a Luxembourg VAT identification number (format LU + 8 digits) where a taxable activity is declared; in the absence of automatic allocation, the entity files the standard VAT registration application (form n° 400 for EU/foreign entities or standard RCS-linked notification) via MyGuichet.lu; (4) the ACD activates the corporate income-tax and net-wealth-tax file upon RCS registration; all annual CIT, NWT and VAT returns are e-filed through the ACD/AEDT Extranet and MyGuichet.lu; (5) for customs and export operations, the SARL obtains an EORI number (LU + 8 digits) from the AEDT customs division before lodging any import or export declarations.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope - The SARL is a resident, opaque taxable company subject to Luxembourg corporate income tax (17% federal rate), the 7% solidarity surcharge on CIT, municipal business tax (6.75% in Luxembourg City, aggregate headline burden approx. 24.94%), and net wealth tax (0.5%); worldwide income is taxable for residents with credit and treaty relief; no pass-through or transparent election is available for a SARL (it is also a per-se corporation for US check-the-box classification); no tax-exempt zone applies because Luxembourg is a unitary national jurisdiction without sub-national tax-distinction regimes. — For a Luxembourg SARL: (1) incorporation is completed before a Luxembourg notary and the coordinated articles of association are filed with the RCS through the Guichet Unique within one month; (2) the RCS issues the registration extract (extrait) confirming the RCS number (format B-xxxxxx); the national identification number (matricule) is automatically issued by the State IT Centre (CTIE); (3) the AEDT automatically allocates a Luxembourg VAT identification number (format LU + 8 digits) where a taxable activity is declared; in the absence of automatic allocation, the entity files the standard VAT registration application (form n° 400 for EU/foreign entities or standard RCS-linked notification) via MyGuichet.lu; (4) the ACD activates the corporate income-tax and net-wealth-tax file upon RCS registration; all annual CIT, NWT and VAT returns are e-filed through the ACD/AEDT Extranet and MyGuichet.lu; (5) for customs and export operations, the SARL obtains an EORI number (LU + 8 digits) from the AEDT customs division before lodging any import or export declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope - The SARL is a resident, opaque taxable company subject to Luxembourg corporate income tax (17% federal rate), the 7% solidarity surcharge on CIT, municipal business tax (6.75% in Luxembourg City, aggregate headline burden approx. 24.94%), and net wealth tax (0.5%); worldwide income is taxable for residents with credit and treaty relief; no pass-through or transparent election is available for a SARL (it is also a per-se corporation for US check-the-box classification); no tax-exempt zone applies because Luxembourg is a unitary national jurisdiction without sub-national tax-distinction regimes.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or transparent status is available for a SARL; the relevant statutory elections and regimes are: (i) the participation-exemption election for qualifying dividends and capital gains (10% holding or EUR 1.2 million acquisition price for dividends; 10% or EUR 6 million for capital gains plus a 12-month holding period, subject to the subject-to-tax test); (ii) the notional interest deduction (NID) on qualifying new equity; (iii) the IP box (80% partial exemption on net qualifying IP income, subject to the nexus ratio); (iv) VAT grouping election (assujetti unique) between related Luxembourg entities under Article 61 LVA; (v) ATAD 2 hybrid-mismatch rules are mandatory with no elective carve-outs except statutory matching adjustments; (vi) revaluation and depreciation elections under the Luxembourg Income Tax Law (LIR) available within the annual CIT return.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

18%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover ceiling for the small-business franchise exemption (Article 57 LVA; goods and services); EUR 10,000 threshold for intra-Community distance sales and cross-border e-services; VAT registration is compulsory from the first euro of taxable activity for B2B intra-Community services (reverse-charge supplies), for non-established taxable persons, and for any entity seeking input VAT recovery; a dry-leasing SARL is registered from inception in practice.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods outside the EU are zero-rated at 0% (Article 45 LVA) if the physical export takes place within 90 days, is evidenced from Luxembourg/EU customs records, and is documented before the VAT return deadline; for aircraft-engine dry leasing the transaction is treated as a supply of services (hiring of moveable tangible property other than means of transport), so the place-of-supply rule (Article 58 LVA for B2B) applies: an EU lessee outside Luxembourg triggers reverse charge in the lessee's Member State (no Luxembourg VAT), a non-EU lessee results in no Luxembourg VAT (outside scope), and a Luxembourg-resident lessee is subject to 18% Luxembourg VAT; where the transaction is re-characterised as a supply of goods (engine sale or finance-lease transferring title), full 0% zero-rating requires a customs export declaration, physical exit proof, and invoice issued within the statutory export evidence window.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/ENS lodged via the UCC Export Control System)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing dispatch from Luxembourg/EU territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT payment evidence evidencing the contractual consideration
  • Lease agreement (operating dry lease, ISIC 5223) identifying engine serial numbers and lessee
  • Engine logbooks, handover and redelivery certificates
  • VIES/valid VAT registration proof of the lessee (EU B2B reverse-charge substantiation)
  • Customs exit confirmation (UCC Article 335 electronic exit messages)
  • EORI-LU number evidence for the exporting SARL

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or continuous-transaction-control API regime exists in Luxembourg (no SdI/VeriFactu-style mandate); B2G e-invoicing is mandatory via the PEPPOL network (EN 16931 format) since 23 April 2022; B2B and B2C e-invoicing remain voluntary and interoperable; the EU VAT-in-the-Digital-Age (ViDA) framework will introduce structured e-invoicing and digital reporting requirements by 2030; all customs export declarations must be lodged electronically through the Union Customs Code systems via the AEDT customs e-portal (including the Export Control System - ECS with exit confirmation).

The AEDT may reassess zero-rating or outside-scope treatment within the 3-year VAT limitation period (extended to 5 years where no return has been filed and up to 10 years in cases of fraud); retrospective clawback reinstates Luxembourg VAT at 18% plus default interest of 0.6% per month on arrears; administrative fines generally range from EUR 250 to EUR 12,500 per infringement, and fraudulent export zero-rating claims carry penal tax of up to triple the evaded amount, risk of criminal prosecution (fines and imprisonment), and suspension of the offender's fiscal good standing, invoicing and EORI/customs privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg has no foreign exchange law restricting convertibility; capital and current account transactions are fully convertible for EUR and all convertible currencies. Profit remittance, dividends, loan repayments, and lease payments are unrestricted under EU free movement of capital (TFEU Articles 63-65), subject to tax compliance and AML/KYC verification. BCL statistical reporting obligations apply for cross-border payments to aid eurosystem balance-of-payments compilation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Luxembourg; dry-lease receivables may be held in contract currency or EUR. However, the SARL must report cross-border trade payments to its Luxembourg bank under BCL external-statistics rules, and any receipt from a sanctioned or high-risk jurisdiction may require documentation proving the underlying aircraft engine transaction before crediting.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) as eurosystem member; foreign exchange regulatory oversight is exercised at EU level through ECB monetary policy and EU capital-movement guarantees, while BCL/CSSF administer bank and AML supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as primary treasury rail. Luxembourg-licensed EMIs and PIs can support pre-funded accounts and payment services for operating expenses, but most do not provide SWIFT direct membership, escrow-grade settlement, aircraft title-money handling, or large FX lines; non-EU digital EMI-only accounts are not adequate for an aviation asset-holding SARL and carry enhanced AML refusal risk under CSSF circulars.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity subscription proceeds and shareholder loans (subordinated or ordinary) must be routed through SWIFT MT103 wire transfers to the SARL's dedicated account in a Luxembourg Tier-1 bank; the funds reference field must show the transaction purpose, operating agreements or board resolution number, and the ECB/BCL balance-of-payments category for direct-investment capital (e.g., 'equity capital injection' or 'intercompany financing'). The bank requires a notarised contribution/corporate resolution, proof of identity/source of assets, and confirmation that incoming funds do not breach sanctions/embargoes. For amounts exceeding prescribed BCL thresholds, the bank will report to BCL on behalf of the SARL; an incoming capital payment may be placed in a blocked escrow-capital sub-account pending completion of registration/requisite legal formalities.Dry-lease distributions and trade payments (engine maintenance reserves, OEM purchase invoices, redelivery costs, lessee refunds, dividends and lender repayments) should be settled via SWIFT MT103 for single beneficiary payments and MT202 covers for bank-to-bank nostro transfers. For large repossession/reserve movements, an irrevocable documentary credit or standby letter of credit issued by an OECD-country bank and financed against the engine lease assignment can be used, but ordinary operating leases settle on clean T/T terms.
