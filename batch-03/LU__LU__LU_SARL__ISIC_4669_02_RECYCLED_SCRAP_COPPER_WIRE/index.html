Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — Luxembourg resident SARL is subject to corporate income tax (IRC), municipal business tax and net wealth tax on worldwide income, with territorial sourcing relief under participation exemption.

Available election forms

Luxembourg does not offer check-the-box or US-style hybrid classification. Elective regimes include: (i) VAT fiscal unity (assujetti unique) under Article 60 of the Luxembourg VAT law; (ii) fiscal integration / group relief under Article 164bis IRG for companies held directly/indirectly at least 95%; (iii) participation exemption election under Article 166 IRG; (iv) risk-capital deduction under Article 168ter. Hybrid mismatch adjustments apply per EU ATAD.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for resident small enterprises (Article 57 VAT law); no threshold for non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies in Luxembourg; EUR 35,000 for distance-selling of goods to Luxembourg consumers (Article 15a VAT law)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated export of recycled scrap copper wire within the meaning of Article 41 of Luxembourg VAT law requires actual physical departure of goods from the EU within 90 days from the supply date; zero rating also applies to transport and ancillary services directly linked to the exported goods and to qualifying triangular supplies under Article 46quater.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Déclaration d'exportation) lodged in Luxembourg or EU customs system
  • Transport document (Bill of Lading, CMR or Air Waybill) proving receipt outside the EU
  • Commercial invoice and SWIFT/LC payment receipt (inward remittance evidence) matching the export value

No real-time clearance mandate for B2B. B2G invoices must be e-invoiced in PEPPOL BIS format. All invoices must be issued within 15 days of supply and preserved 10 years; telecommunications/ILI systems or EDI with electronic signature satisfy authenticity and integrity; no digital stamping imposed.

Export zero-rating is scrutinized by the AEDT through periodic audits. Missing substantive export evidence triggers reversal to the 17% standard rate plus late interest of 0.6% per month and fines of up to 100% of the evaded VAT for negligence; criminal penalties for fraudulent claims include imprisonment up to 5 years and/or fines up to EUR 500,000. Retroactive reassessments may extend to six years for fraud, and exporters share joint and several liability with their clients under Article 83 of the VAT law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Legal-tender currency is the euro (EUR). Full convertibility applies to both current and capital account under Article 63 TFEU; no exchange-control approval, licensing, or quantitative restriction blocks profit remittance, dividend distribution, shareholder loan repayment, or liquidation proceeds. Withholding tax (15% standard dividend WHT, reduced to 5% under the Luxembourg parent-subsidiary regime with tax-residence and holding-period conditions) remains the only material friction on outward profit flows. Banks apply their own enhanced due diligence to remittances involving sanctioned or high-risk third countries, but the Luxembourg statutory regime imposes no conversion obligation and no redemption restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable — Luxembourg imposes no statutory surrender, no mandatory repatriation window, and no forced EUR conversion ratio on export receipts. Exporters may freely retain, denominate, or redeploy foreign currency balances. The only administrative requirement is declarative: transactions equal to or exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported monthly to the BCL for balance-of-payments statistics (implementing Regulation (EC) No 184/2005 and BCL circulars), using the harmonised EU coding taxonomy.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) within the Eurosystem; monetary policy is delegated to the European Central Bank (ECB); banking conduct and AML/CFT supervision are exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF), with the BCL supervising payment and settlement systems (TARGET2/T2, ELIPS national component) and carrying out EU balance-of-payments statistical reporting duties.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility as an auxiliary payment rail only. Luxembourg-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs, e.g., PayPocket) and EU-passported EMIs can open payment accounts, execute SEPA Credit Transfers and MT103 pass-throughs for open-account collections. They are legally prohibited from granting credit, opening irrevocable documentary credits, issuing guarantees, or maintaining nostro liquidity buffers for LC reimbursement; CSSF requires safeguarding of client funds and compliance with Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 (wire transfer travel rule) and the EBA outsourcing guidelines. Consequently, an offshore EMI cannot replace a tier-1 credit institution for the documentary LC or bulk cross-border settlement layers of this copper-scrap trading mandate, but it may serve as a low-cost operational float account in parallel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Statutory capital must be blocked in a dedicated capital escrow account (compte de dépôt de capital) at a Luxembourg credit institution until the notary certifies the subscription; after incorporation the escrow converts into an unrestricted operational account. Wire format uses SWIFT MT103 with ISO 20022 CategoryPurpose codes 'EQUI' (equity capital) or 'CAPH' (capital injection) stated in field 70 / Remittance Information; for euro legs up to EUR 500,000 on the Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA), an equivalent SEPA Credit Transfer with XML 'CtgyPurp' code is accepted. The receiving bank verifies that the remitter is the sole beneficial owner of the funds; remittances above EUR 12,500 trigger a mandatory BCL balance-of-payments declaration to be lodged by the bank within the statutory monthly report and, for capital inflow above the EUR 5 million direct investment threshold, the CSSF/BCL may request supporting evidence of the notarial certificate and immediate source of funds.For the settled copper-scrap trade against suppliers in non-EU jurisdictions, the principal clearing rail is an irrevocable Documentary Letter of Credit (UCP 600) issued in SWIFT MT700 via the Luxembourg branch, advised in MT710 and, when required, confirmed by a corridor bank in the seller's country; settlement occurs either by SWIFT MT754/MT202 against presentation of compliant transport and quality documents. For open-account or pre-shipment disbursements, SWIFT MT103 (with 'TRAD' category purpose) is used, converted to TARGET2/T2 for the euro leg and to correspondent banking rails for USD/CNY/MYR legs; all EUR payments within SEPA can alternatively clear through the BCL-operated ELIPS component and EBA CLEARING STEP2. The letter of credit bears the inherent advantage of balancing the cash-to-document cycle against the seller's possession of goods; for this medium-risk commodity the bank imposes standard enhanced documentary requirements: original Bill of Lading/CMR, weightbridge certificate, spectrographic analysis, EU Waste Shipment Regulation Annex VII shipment document, and packing list.Plain-vanilla deliverable FX forwards (EUR/USD, EUR/CNY) covering specified trade invoices and purchase orders, booked under ISDA 2002 or the bank's master agreement with bilateral CSA collateral.FX swaps (swap de change) managing intra-trade- cycle funding mismatches between the inbound capital leg and the outbound disbursement leg.Cross-currency swaps (fixed/floating) permitted where the long-term shareholder loan or commercial finance extended to the Luxembourg SARL is denominated in USD or CNY against an EUR balance sheet.Protective FX options (vanilla calls/puts on EURUSD or EURCNY) as zero-premium or traded for the exclusive mitigation of physical commodity price-to-FX correlation risks.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are legally permissible only where the non-EUR currency leg is non-convertible or restricted (e.g., certain emerging-market counterparties); for freely convertible EUR/USD and EUR/CNY pairs, the bank requires fully deliverable forwards settled through continuous linked settlement (CLS) where applicable.All derivative transactions are subject to EMIR (Regulation (EU) No 648/2012) trade reporting, clearing thresholds, and risk-mitigation rules, with the Luxembourg entity required to identify its derivative category (financial or non-financial counterparty above the clearing threshold).

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

17% (bracketed: 15% on first EUR 175,000; 16% on EUR 175,001–200,000; 17% above EUR 200,000). Aggregate marginal rate including 7% unemployment solidarity surcharge and Luxembourg City communal business tax of 6.75% equals approximately 24.94%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% standard Luxembourg withholding tax on SARL dividend distributions. Exemptions: 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for a corporate EU shareholder with a 10% holding held 12 months; 0% under Art. 147 LIR for any non-resident parent company holding 10% (or an acquisition cost of at least EUR 1.2 million) for 12 months; DTA rates generally 5% for qualifying corporate holdings and 15% for portfolio investors.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 500,000 of annual intra-group ordinary revenue triggers the mandatory simplified transfer pricing statement annexed to the Model 202; a full Local File and Master File are required for Luxembourg entities belonging to groups with consolidated turnover of at least EUR 750 million; Country-by-Country Reporting applies to groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million.
