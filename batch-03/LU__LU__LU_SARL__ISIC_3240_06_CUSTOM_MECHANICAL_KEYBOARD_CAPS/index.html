Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Luxembourg Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT and registration. Main portal: MyGuichet.lu (https://guichet.public.lu) and ACD/AEDT e-Space.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity not established in Luxembourg must file an advance VAT registration application with the AEDT using the official national form, submitted electronically via MyGuichet.lu or by post. If the foreign entity has no fixed establishment, the AEDT may require appointment of a Luxembourg VAT fiscal representative. Upon approval, the AEDT assigns a Luxembourg VAT number and a national identification number (matricule). A direct tax registration is additionally required only where a Luxembourg permanent establishment is created; the ACD then registers the entity for corporate income tax and municipal business tax via the matricule system.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity not established in Luxembourg must file an advance VAT registration application with the AEDT using the official national form, submitted electronically via MyGuichet.lu or by post. If the foreign entity has no fixed establishment, the AEDT may require appointment of a Luxembourg VAT fiscal representative. Upon approval, the AEDT assigns a Luxembourg VAT number and a national identification number (matricule). A direct tax registration is additionally required only where a Luxembourg permanent establishment is created; the ACD then registers the entity for corporate income tax and municipal business tax via the matricule system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Not applicable as a general pass-through election for a Luxembourg SARL, which is a capital company and tax-opaque. Available statutory elections include: VAT grouping (assujetti unique) under Article 62 of the Luxembourg VAT Law; fiscal unity for corporate income tax purposes under Article 164bis of the Income Tax Law (99% qualifying shareholding and five-year commitment); and specific hybrid mismatch consequential adjustment elections under the 2018 ATAD implementation law. No US-style check-the-box election exists under Luxembourg domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general minimum turnover threshold for established VAT registrants. A small-business exemption applies to undertakings with annual turnover not exceeding EUR 35,000 (excluding VAT), which may be waived at the taxpayer's option.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Luxembourg to destinations outside the European Union are zero-rated under Article 62 of the Luxembourg VAT Law where the goods are physically dispatched or transported out of the EU by the supplier or on the supplier's behalf, evidenced by the customs export declaration and transport documents. Intra-Community supplies to customers holding a valid VAT identification number of another Member State are also zero-rated, provided the goods are dispatched to that Member State and the summary recapitulative statement is filed. Certain offshore services supplied to third-country entrepreneurs fall outside Luxembourg VAT pursuant to place-of-supply rules, thereby being outside the scope rather than expressly zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / Export Accompanying Document EAD)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill or other transport evidence proving dispatch to a third country
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT proof evidencing receipt of export proceeds in Luxembourg)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List and destination customs import evidence where obtainable

Luxembourg does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-based transaction reporting for B2B sales. Electronic invoices are accepted provided authenticity and integrity of origin and content are guaranteed using EDI or advanced electronic signatures. B2G invoicing on public contracts must use the national e-Procurement platform and PEPPOL/EN16931 formats. Luxembourg is monitoring and preparing for the EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) reforms, which may introduce digital reporting requirements after 2025.

The VAT Administration (AEDT) may retrospectively verify export substantiation within the statutory record-keeping and assessment windows. If the taxpayer fails to produce the mandatory export documentation, the zero-rating is reversed and VAT is assessed at 17%, together with late-payment interest and administrative fines under Articles 59 to 64 of the amended VAT Law. Intentional falsification of customs declarations, bills of lading, or remittance evidence constitutes criminal tax fraud under the Luxembourg Criminal Code, punishable by imprisonment and substantial fines. Refund claims based on insufficient export proof are subject to clawback, including interest from the date the refund was paid.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg maintains a fully liberalised external financial regime. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible with no residual exchange controls on the euro or on the repatriation of dividends, profits, or invested capital by non-resident shareholders. Profit remittance hurdles are limited to corporate-law solvency tests (net assets must exceed paid-up capital plus non-distributable reserves) and, where applicable, withholding tax obligations under the law of 4 December 1967 on income tax. No administrative foreign-exchange approval is required for cross-border payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratios apply to export proceeds. Exporters are free to maintain foreign-currency accounts inside or outside Luxembourg. Under European Central Bank balance-of-payments statistical requirements, resident corporates must report individual transactions equal to or exceeding EUR 12,500 through their reporting bank; there is no economic obligation to repatriate or convert export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), acting alongside the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for banking supervision and the Ministry of Finance for prudential and anti-money-laundering policy; monetary policy authority is effectively delegated to the European Central Bank within the euro area.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Fintech electronic money institutions (EMIs) holding a valid Luxembourg or EEA passport may be used for ancillary collections and disbursements, but are not a substitute for a tier-1 credit institution as the primary treasury rail. Non-EEA digital EMIs are generally not viable without authorisation from the CSSF or a recognised passport. In practice, the SARL should rely on a domestic bank for trade finance, liquidity products, and documentary services; fintech EMIs can handle niche flows such as ShopPay or cross-border wallet settlement. Overall viability rating: Medium for ancillary flows, Low as sole treasury clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder equity is routed through the notarised subscription phase. The founder or investor transfers the subscribed capital into a blocking account (compte de dépôt de capital) held with a Luxembourg credit institution opened in the name of the SARL in formation. SWIFT transfers must include the exact beneficiary account reference and the notarial deed identification; the purpose code used by the sending bank should identify a direct equity investment (BOP code for inward direct investment) when reporting is required. After incorporation, the released funds are converted into the operational account. No exchange-control approval is required; for non-EEA transfers, the bank will perform standard AML verification of the source of funds and apply the applicable EU sanctions screening. All inbound capital increases are treated similarly, with share premium reported as equity capital.Operational payments to foreign suppliers of PBT resin, moulds, keycap stems, and packaging are executed through SWIFT MT103 for transactions below documentary-letter-of-credit thresholds. For high-value mould purchases or recurring procurement from Asia, irrevocable commercial letters of credit (L/C at sight) issued in EUR, USD, or CNY provide settlement security. Intra-EEA suppliers are paid using SEPA Credit Transfer with standard XML payment formats. The SARL’s bank may require underlying invoices and transport evidence for each cross-border disbursement, but does not impose statutory currency conversion restrictions.
