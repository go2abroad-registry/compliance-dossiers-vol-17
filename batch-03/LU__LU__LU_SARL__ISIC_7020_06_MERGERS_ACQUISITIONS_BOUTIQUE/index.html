Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum issued share capital: EUR 12,000. The capital must be fully subscribed; at least 25% of each cash share must be paid up on incorporation and deposited in a blocked pre-incorporation bank account with a Luxembourg credit institution, evidenced by a bank blocked-funds certificate (attestation de consignation) addressed to the notary. In-kind contributions must be fully paid up at signing and specifically described in the deed. No escrow agent is required beyond the notary's control over the blocked account.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification by the Corps grand-ducal d'incendie et de secours (CGDIS) for the leased office premises, including accessible exits, alarm systems, and extinguisher placement; structural load and electrical safety certificate from an approved bureau de contrôle (e.g., Secolux, APAVE, DNV) confirming floor load capacity and electrical conformity; occupational health and safety registration with the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM); mandatory employee health-surveillance contract with a licensed occupational health service (Service de Santé au Travail) when staff are engaged.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum issued share capital: EUR 12,000. The capital must be fully subscribed; at least 25% of each cash share must be paid up on incorporation and deposited in a blocked pre-incorporation bank account with a Luxembourg credit institution, evidenced by a bank blocked-funds certificate (attestation de consignation) addressed to the notary. In-kind contributions must be fully paid up at signing and specifically described in the deed. No escrow agent is required beyond the notary's control over the blocked account.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direct taxes: Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD), www.acd.public.lu. Indirect taxes: Administration de l'Enregistrement, des Domaines et de la TVA (AEDT), VAT Department, https://aedt.public.lu. Single digital entry point: www.guichet.lu / www.myguichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the SARL with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) within 15 days of formation; the RCS national registration number is the statutory general TIN under Luxembourg law. 2) File VAT registration Form 645 (Demande d'immatriculation TVA) with the AEDT via myGuichet.lu prior to commencing taxable activity; the AEDT issues a VAT identification number (LU + 8 digits). 3) Registration is compulsory when expected annual taxable turnover exceeds EUR 35,000, and may be voluntary below this threshold; non-resident companies operating a Luxembourg branch or fixed establishment use the same Form 645 and must appoint a Luxembourg tax representative if established outside the EU. 4) Foreign entities claiming treaty relief or fiscal regimes must attach a certificate of fiscal domicile issued by their home tax authority.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (opaque capital company; the Luxembourg SARL is a societe de capitaux, not eligible for pass-through; subject to corporate income tax, municipal business tax and the solidarity surcharge on worldwide profits for resident companies). — 1) Incorporate the SARL with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) within 15 days of formation; the RCS national registration number is the statutory general TIN under Luxembourg law. 2) File VAT registration Form 645 (Demande d'immatriculation TVA) with the AEDT via myGuichet.lu prior to commencing taxable activity; the AEDT issues a VAT identification number (LU + 8 digits). 3) Registration is compulsory when expected annual taxable turnover exceeds EUR 35,000, and may be voluntary below this threshold; non-resident companies operating a Luxembourg branch or fixed establishment use the same Form 645 and must appoint a Luxembourg tax representative if established outside the EU. 4) Foreign entities claiming treaty relief or fiscal regimes must attach a certificate of fiscal domicile issued by their home tax authority.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (opaque capital company; the Luxembourg SARL is a societe de capitaux, not eligible for pass-through; subject to corporate income tax, municipal business tax and the solidarity surcharge on worldwide profits for resident companies).

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid classification election exists under Luxembourg law. Available elections: 1) Tax consolidation (fiscal unity) under Art. 164bis LIR for qualifying 95% Luxembourg/EU/Norwegian/Icelandic subsidiaries, binding for at least five fiscal years; 2) VAT grouping (assujetti unique) under Art. 60 of the Luxembourg VAT Law for financially, economically and organizationally integrated persons; 3) Small-business exemption waiver (Art. 57a VAT Law), permitting the taxable person to opt into normal VAT liability below EUR 35,000; 4) Participation exemption election per Art. 166 LIR for qualifying dividend and capital-gain relief subject to holding-period and substance conditions. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are automatically neutralized under ATAD 2 (Arts. 168a-168f LIR), and DAC6 disclosure obligations apply to reportable cross-border arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

16%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover (small-enterprise exemption, Art. 57a VAT Law). Mandatory registration for a Luxembourg-established supplier once turnover exceeds or is expected to exceed this threshold; non-established suppliers must register for the supply of goods in Luxembourg without threshold (intra-Community distance sales exceeding EUR 10,000 may use the EU OSS regime instead of local registration).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% VAT applies to exports of goods to non-EU destinations (Art. 54 VAT Law) and to intra-Community supplies when the EU customer has a valid VAT ID validated in VIES (Art. 53 VAT Law), subject to EC Sales List and recapitulative statement filing. Services: cross-border B2B advisory services, including M&A success fees, are outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT under the place-of-supply rule (Art. 57 VAT Law) because the services are deemed rendered where the business customer is established; hence no Luxembourg VAT is charged and the foreign recipient accounts for reverse charge or local VAT. The 0% rate may also apply to services directly connected with exported goods (Art. 54(1) VAT Law). Zero-rating or non-application claims must be evidenced by written contracts, engagement letters, customer VAT identification and payment flows.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document - SAD / IE515 via Luxembourg LUCAS single window) or Intrastat declaration (DEB) for intra-Community dispatches
  • Transport evidence of physical departure: Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR consignment note (or warehouse certificate for customs-warehoused goods)
  • Commercial invoice and M&A advisory engagement letter / success-fee agreement evidencing the nature, place and beneficiary of the services
  • Proof of payment and foreign-exchange repatriation: SWIFT MT103/MT202 credit advice or bank statement confirming receipt from the foreign client within 180 days
  • Validation of customer B2B status: VIES-valid EU VAT ID for intra-Community services/supplies, or certificate of foreign tax residence for extra-EU service recipients

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime is in force as of 2025. Mandatory B2B e-invoicing with structured formats (EN 16931) and VAT digital reporting to the AEDT via the national OsiLo platform is announced with effect from 1 January 2026. E-invoicing via the PEPPOL network has been obligatory for suppliers to Luxembourg public entities since 18 April 2019. Until the B2B mandate becomes effective, paper or PDF invoices remain valid provided they satisfy the Fields of Arts. 57-60 VAT Law (sequential numbering, VAT ID, date, supply description, and tax amount). The M&A boutique must issue compliant VAT invoices for all Luxembourg-taxable fees; success fees and fixed fees charged under reverse-charge arrangements require a clear mention that VAT is shifted to the recipient.

VAT reassessment periods are three years ordinarily, extended to five years for transactions giving rise to cross-border supplies or information exchange with foreign competent authorities, and ten years for VAT fraud (fraude fiscale) under the Abgabenordnung. Unwarranted zero-rating is clawed back with monthly interest of 0.60% (7.2% p.a.) from the due date. Administrative fines range from EUR 250 to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment up to five years for aggravated fraudulent conduct, imposed under the VAT Enforcement Law of 1 December 2009; directors and the beneficial client may be held jointly and severally liable. The AEDT conducts focused audits on advisory boutiques claiming service export treatment, scrutinizing economic substance, board-managed documentation, and actual performance versus invoice narrative; failure to substantiate the non-resident customer and value of consideration triggers immediate reassessment and penalty exposure.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility under Eurozone membership and EU free movement of capital (TFEU Article 63; Directive 88/361/EEC). Current-account flows, including success fees, advisor commissions, and cross-border reimbursements, and capital-account flows, including equity injections, shareholder loans, and deal-escrow settlements, are unrestricted for residents and non-residents. Profit-repatriation hurdles are purely fiscal: domestic dividend withholding tax is 15%, reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive when a 10% shareholding is held for 12 months, or under most double-tax treaties to 5% or less; no central-bank clearance, foreign-exchange permit, or repatriation license is required. One prudential limit applies: a non-bank SARL may not accept deposits or intermediate client deal monies outside a credit-institution escrow structure, by reason of Article 2-1 of the Law of 5 April 1993.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no obligation to remit export receipts or cross-border advisory success-fee proceeds to the central bank. Receipts may be retained in EUR or any freely convertible currency (USD, CHF, GBP) and may be reinvested offshore without restriction. The sole compliance duty is statistical: resident banks attach BCL balance-of-payments purpose codes to each inbound and outbound wire for macro-financial data collection, with no exchange-control effect.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) and Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF). Luxembourg operates under the Eurosystem single-currency framework, with no separate foreign-exchange control authority. Resident banks carry statutory responsibility for BCL balance-of-payments statistical reporting and CSSF AML/CFT supervision under the Law of 12 November 2004, as amended, and CSSF Regulation 12-02.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium for the SARL's own operating funds; Low for client and deal funds. A PSD2-licensed electronic-money institution (Luxembourg-authorised or EU/EEA-passported, such as Airwallex, Payoneer, or a Luxembourg e-money issuer) can hold the boutique's own balance, execute SWIFT and SEPA/instant transfers, and issue virtual IBANs settled through TARGET2. Legal constraint: under the Luxembourg Law of 1 April 2019 implementing PSD2 safeguarding rules, an EMI may not hold M&A client monies or third-party escrow funds in the manner of a credit institution. Client success-fee collections and deal escrows must settle at a licensed credit institution; therefore the EMI is a viable secondary rail for own-account professional fees and expenses only, never the primary treasury anchor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for a SARL: (1) equity injection, where the investor wires via SWIFT MT103 from its own account to the SARL's EUR IBAN, with the bank instructed to classify the transfer as BCL balance-of-payments code for equity capital; the wire must be accompanied by a notarised increase-of-capital deed or contemporaneous shareholder resolution and UBO passport; (2) shareholder loan, coded as intercompany loan, non-resident, with an executed loan agreement, arm's-length interest indication, and repayment schedule; (3) third-party deposits are blocked. No BCL or CSSF prior clearance applies to EU/EEA or OECD-origin inbound capital, but the bank independently verifies source of funds and ultimate beneficial ownership; any inbound wire lacking the underlying corporate deed is held in suspense pending documentation.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 single-customer credit transfer via TARGET2, carrying full structured remittance information and the deal reference to pass bank sanctions and OFAC screening. For EUR payments below EUR 100,000, SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) is the efficient second rail; for USD, GBP, and CHF beneficiaries, MT103 with nostro routing is used.
