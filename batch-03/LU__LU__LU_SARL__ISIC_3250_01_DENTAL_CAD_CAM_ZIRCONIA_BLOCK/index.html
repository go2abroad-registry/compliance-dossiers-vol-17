Competent corporate registry

Registre de Commerce et des Societes (RCS), operated by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR), attached to the Greffe du Tribunal d'arrondissement de Luxembourg; the incorporation deed is executed before a Luxembourg notary, who carries out the electronic eRCS filing on behalf of the founders.

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (Title V - Societe a Responsabilite Limitee, Articles 710-1 to 710-24), as modernized by the Law of 23 June 2016; Law of 19 December 2002 on the Luxembourg Business Registers; Grand-Ducal Regulation of 23 January 2003 on RCS tariffs; Law of 2 September 2011 on the regulation of establishments (autorisation d'etablissement).

Formation / constitutional filings

Authentic notarial deed of incorporation (acte constitutif) containing the articles of association (statuts), registered in the RCS via the LBR eRCS e-filing portal and published in the Recueil electronique des societes et associations (RESA).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 19 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum share capital EUR 12,000, fully subscribed at execution of the deed; cash shares must be paid up to at least 25% of nominal value at constitution (minimum EUR 3,000), with the balance due within five years; the paid-up funds must be deposited on a blocked escrow account (attestation de blocage) with a Luxembourg credit institution and released to the company upon RCS registration; contributions in kind require an independent statutory auditor's report; no capital duty (droit d'apport) is levied, it having been abolished with effect from 1 January 2013.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Corps grand-ducal d'incendie et de secours (CGDIS); building permit and structural acceptance from the municipal building authority of the commune where the production site is located; occupational health and safety clearance from the Inspection du travail et des mines (ITM) and the Division de la sante au travail covering workplace dust, noise and thermal stress; electrical conformity certificate, pressure vessel inspection, and employer accident insurance registration with the CCSS.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum share capital EUR 12,000, fully subscribed at execution of the deed; cash shares must be paid up to at least 25% of nominal value at constitution (minimum EUR 3,000), with the balance due within five years; the paid-up funds must be deposited on a blocked escrow account (attestation de blocage) with a Luxembourg credit institution and released to the company upon RCS registration; contributions in kind require an independent statutory auditor's report; no capital duty (droit d'apport) is levied, it having been abolished with effect from 1 January 2013.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (Luxembourg Direct Tax Administration) and Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT / Luxembourg Registration, Duties and VAT Administration); central access via guichet.lu and MyGuichet e-portal

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a Luxembourg SARL must file articles of incorporation with the Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS), obtain a business registration number (matricule), and then apply through the AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT identification number (TVA). Non-resident suppliers not established in Luxembourg must obtain a direct VAT identification number if they conduct taxable supplies in Luxembourg, acquire goods subject to VAT, or exceed the distance-selling registration threshold; a local VAT representative is mandatory for non-EU suppliers.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity establishing a Luxembourg SARL must file articles of incorporation with the Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS), obtain a business registration number (matricule), and then apply through the AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT identification number (TVA). Non-resident suppliers not established in Luxembourg must obtain a direct VAT identification number if they conduct taxable supplies in Luxembourg, acquire goods subject to VAT, or exceed the distance-selling registration threshold; a local VAT representative is mandatory for non-EU suppliers.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Luxembourg SARL is a resident corporate entity taxed at entity level on worldwide income, with no US-style check-the-box regime. Elections available under Luxembourg law include group tax consolidation (régime d'intégration fiscale) for a qualifying resident parent and its resident subsidiaries, and the option to apply the EU ATAD hybrid mismatch rules. No check-the-box or pass-through election is available for a SARL.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover (the Luxembourg VAT small business exemption threshold); VAT registration becomes mandatory once annual taxable turnover exceeds EUR 35,000, or immediately upon importation of goods, intra-Community acquisition of goods, or engagement in cross-border distance sales subject to the EU distance-selling threshold of EUR 35,000 for Luxembourg.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of dental zirconia ceramic blocks physically exported from Luxembourg to a destination outside the European Union are eligible for VAT zero-rating under Luxembourg VAT law (Article 42 of the Luxembourg VAT code). Intra-Community supplies to a taxable person registered for VAT in another EU Member State are likewise zero-rated provided the goods are dispatched or transported out of Luxembourg and adequate transport evidence is retained. Services directly linked to the exported goods and performed for the non-EU customer may also qualify for zero-rating, subject to documentary proof.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic EAD submitted via Luxembourg Customs)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or CMR road consignment note (irrefutable transport evidence)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/TT confirmation evidencing payment in foreign currency
  • Proof of the foreign customer's VAT registration or business status (for intra-Community supplies)

Luxembourg currently does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls for standard B2B supplies. Taxable persons must issue valid VAT invoices, and electronic invoicing is permitted subject to authenticity and integrity controls. The Luxembourg authorities accept structured e-invoicing standards (e.g. Peppol), and future obligations under the EU ViDA framework are anticipated but not yet in force.

Luxembourg tax authorities may retrospectively disallow zero-rating if export documentation is incomplete, contradictory, or forged, resulting in recharacterization as a domestic taxable supply at 17% VAT. Late assessments carry statutory interest, and deliberate fraud exposes the taxpayer to VAT penalties up to 100% of the evaded tax, criminal fines, and personal criminal liability for directors and managing officers. Export substantiation files must be retained for at least six years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a eurozone member with fully liberalized capital and current account convertibility under Articles 63-65 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. No exchange control or approval regime applies to the operations of a resident SARL. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted, subject only to Luxembourg withholding tax on dividends (standard 15%, treaty-reduced rates under double tax treaties, with certain participation-exemption reliefs), corporate formalities (audited annual accounts, notarized or registered shareholder resolutions), and standard AML/KYC substantiation. Foreign-currency bank accounts and invoicing in USD, CHF, GBP or CNY are permissible without BCL authorization; only balance-of-payments statistical reporting obligations apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratios exist in Luxembourg. Export receipts may be retained in the invoiced foreign currency, converted at freely negotiated commercial bank rates, or offset against trade payables. The euro is the SARL functional currency; settlement risk is managed via standard treasury policy rather than regulatory prescription. Export and import transactions are captured for statistical purposes through the BCL Balance of Payments / International Transactions Reporting System, with permitted thresholds and automatic bank reporting.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), in coordination with the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for banking conduct and prudential supervision, and the European Central Bank (ECB) for euro-area monetary policy

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

High feasibility as an ancillary cash-collection and vendor-disbursement rail through CSSF-authorized electronic money institutions (EMIs) or EU passported payment institutions under PSD2, notably for low-value SEPA EUR collections from dental distributors and for fast reconciliation with e-commerce or dealer portals. Legal constraint: EMIs and payment institutions are not licensed credit institutions, cannot accept deposits, extend working-capital credit, issue letters of credit, or offer FX hedging/derivative products, and their client-money safeguarding frameworks are ring-fenced payment accounts rather than treasury balances. Accordingly, a Tier-1 Luxembourg commercial bank must remain the primary treasury concentration bank, FX execution counterparty, and trade-finance provider; the EMI is supplementary only.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound share-capital and shareholder-loan wires must be executed by SWIFT MT103 / TT with full beneficiary details: the bank BIC of the Luxembourg vault institution, the SARL IBAN in the LU-format (LU + 3-digit bank code + 5-digit branch/key + 8-digit account), and remittance-information fields clearly stating the purpose as share-capital subscription, capital increase, or shareholder loan. For intra-euro-area EUR inflows, the SEPA Credit Transfer rail is used without SWIFT MT103. Verification protocol prior to crediting: notarized incorporation deed or notarial capital-increase minutes; updated RCS extract; validity of IBAN and the annual percentage rate of the statutory minimum capital of EUR 12,500 (or higher where the articles of association so provide); FATCA/CRS tax self-certifications; sanctions and PEP screening; source-of-funds substantiation; and BCL balance-of-payments statistical classification. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No monetary threshold for Local File/Master File under Luxembourg domestic law. Transfer pricing documentation must be contemporaneous and available on request for all related-party transactions. Mandatory Country-by-Country Reporting threshold: EUR 750,000,000 consolidated group revenue.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg has no separate Economic Substance Regulations filing regime comparable to offshore centres, but economic substance is a binding legal condition under Luxembourg tax jurisprudence, ATAD, and general anti-abuse rules. A SARL manufacturing dental ceramic blocks must maintain real local presence, management, staff, premises, and economic activity proportionate to its profits. Artificial or shell arrangements are exposed to denial of tax treaty benefits, tax residence challenge, and recharacterisation of transactions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Ex-officio reassessment, denial of deductions, late-payment interest, administrative fines up to EUR 25,000, criminal fines, and imprisonment for tax fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly sign or cause a false tax return are criminally liable; the corporate veil can be pierced where the company is a facade, exposing personal assets; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed during criminal proceedings.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits under principal purpose test, recapture of Luxembourg tax residence benefits, recharacterisation of payments, ATAD CFC and GAAR adjustments, administrative fines, and interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who tolerate or orchestrate artificial substance arrangements may be personally liable for the resulting corporate tax debts; management agreements can be disregarded; travel restrictions and director disqualification may follow in aggravated cases.
Transfer Pricing / Thin Capitalisation Disregard

Statutory sanction: Primary transfer pricing adjustment, secondary recharacterisation as constructive dividend or royalty subject to 15% withholding tax, disallowance of interest deductions, penal interest, fines, and potential criminal referral for reporting false documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false or inadequate transfer pricing documentation may be held jointly liable for adjusted corporate taxes; civil claims for mismanagement and restitution can reach personal assets.
AML / Money Laundering Predicate

Statutory sanction: Criminal money laundering prosecution, asset confiscation, monetary penalties, revocation of corporate authorisations, and enhanced tax fraud penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners engaged in laundering the proceeds of tax evasion face imprisonment and personal confiscation orders; professional disqualification and travel bans may apply.
