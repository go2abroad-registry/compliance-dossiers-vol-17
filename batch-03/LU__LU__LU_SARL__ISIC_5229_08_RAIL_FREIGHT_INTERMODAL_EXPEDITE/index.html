Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) — Luxembourg Inland Revenue, responsible for corporate income tax, net wealth tax and municipal business tax; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT), responsible for VAT registration, VAT returns and all indirect tax audits; Administration des douanes et accises (ADA) for customs and export-clearance supervision. All statutory filings and registrations are channelled through the official MyGuichet.lu e-government portal and the eTVA electronic filing channel.

E-government filing portal

A Luxembourg-resident SARL obtains its statutory tax identity through a two-step process: (1) incorporation before a notary and registration with the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR/RCS), which automatically allocates the national business identification number (numerus matricule / NIF) and communicates it ex officio to the ACD and AEDT; (2) VAT registration by filing the standard VAT registration declaration (form 102 / MIF) with the AEDT via MyGuichet.lu, after which the entity receives a LU-prefixed 8-digit Luxembourg VAT identification number. A foreign undertaking without a corporate seat that performs taxable rail-freight activities in Luxembourg must register directly if it is established in the EU or EEA; a non-EU undertaking must appoint an AEDT-approved VAT fiscal representative having joint and several liability for Luxembourg VAT, and must file the home-country VAT certificate, constitutional documents and LBR extract. Freight forwarding under ISIC 5229 does not require a sectoral licence, so no additional authorisation is prerequisite for tax registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: the SARL is a fully opaque Luxembourg-resident corporation taxed at entity level on its worldwide income. The applicable tax burden comprises corporate income tax (CIT) at 15% on taxable income up to EUR 175,000 and 17% above that threshold, a 7% solidarity surcharge on the CIT, municipal business tax at the communal rate (6.75% in Luxembourg City), and net wealth tax at 0.5% on taxable net assets above the EUR 500,000 exemption, producing an aggregate top effective rate of 24.94% for the Luxembourg City SARL. No pass-through or check-the-box treatment is available under Luxembourg law; the SARL is within the territorial scope of full residence-based taxation, while foreign PE profits may be relieved under tax treaties and the Luxembourg participation exemption where applicable. — A Luxembourg-resident SARL obtains its statutory tax identity through a two-step process: (1) incorporation before a notary and registration with the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR/RCS), which automatically allocates the national business identification number (numerus matricule / NIF) and communicates it ex officio to the ACD and AEDT; (2) VAT registration by filing the standard VAT registration declaration (form 102 / MIF) with the AEDT via MyGuichet.lu, after which the entity receives a LU-prefixed 8-digit Luxembourg VAT identification number. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the SARL is a fully opaque Luxembourg-resident corporation taxed at entity level on its worldwide income. The applicable tax burden comprises corporate income tax (CIT) at 15% on taxable income up to EUR 175,000 and 17% above that threshold, a 7% solidarity surcharge on the CIT, municipal business tax at the communal rate (6.75% in Luxembourg City), and net wealth tax at 0.5% on taxable net assets above the EUR 500,000 exemption, producing an aggregate top effective rate of 24.94% for the Luxembourg City SARL. No pass-through or check-the-box treatment is available under Luxembourg law; the SARL is within the territorial scope of full residence-based taxation, while foreign PE profits may be relieved under tax treaties and the Luxembourg participation exemption where applicable.

Available election forms

Luxembourg does not provide check-the-box elections for a SARL. The available statutory election and classification mechanisms relevant to a rail-freight SARL comprise: (i) tax unity (integration fiscale) allowing a resident parent company to elect consolidation with 75%+ subsidiaries; (ii) VAT grouping (assujetti unique) available since 1 January 2020 for affiliated entities with fixed establishments in Luxembourg; (iii) the substantial-participation capital-gains exemption election requiring a 12-month holding period and at least 10% shareholding or EUR 6 million acquisition cost; (iv) the participation exemption election for dividend income, applicable where the SARL holds at least 10% or a EUR 1.2 million participation; (v) ATAD anti-hybrid conformity elections, which are limited to eliminating hybrid mismatches and cannot confer pass-through character on a resident SARL; and (vi) fiscal-neutral reorganisation elections under the 2009 merger and reorganisation law for restructuring intermodal container-pool assets without exit taxation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Total convertibility is guaranteed. Luxembourg is a euro-area founder member and imposes no exchange control regulation. Both current-account (services, freight, operational expenses) and capital-account (share capital, loans, dividends) movements are fully liberalised under Articles 63-66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, subject only to (i) EU financial sanctions and asset-freeze measures, (ii) anti-money-laundering controls under the Law of 12 November 2004 and CSSF Regulation 12-02, and (iii) balance-of-payments statistical declarations to the BCL. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted at the treasury level; the sole fiscal obstacle is the domestic 15% withholding tax on dividend distributions, reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for qualifying EU parents or under the relevant double taxation treaty.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation exists. Luxembourg has no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio and no central-bank repatriation requirement for export or service receipts. Freight-forwarding proceeds may be received and held in EUR, USD, CNY or any convertible currency and retained indefinitely in multicurrency accounts. Only generic BCL balance-of-payments threshold reporting applies to cross-border flows, and all transactions remain subject to the EU sanctions and anti-money-laundering framework.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) in conjunction with the European Central Bank (ECB) for euro monetary policy and payment system integrity; prudential conduct of banking and payment institutions is supervised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM as an ancillary collections channel; LOW as a treasury, escrow or trade-finance keeper. Legal constraints: Under Directive (EU) 2015/2366 (PSD2), an electronic money institution servicing a Luxembourg-established corporate must hold a CSSF licence or a valid EU passport; an offshore EMI without passporting may not lawfully hold operational funds. EMIs can neither accept deposits nor issue letters of credit or guarantees, and customer funds must be safeguarded, not banked. Recommended usage is limited to small ticket cross-border collections from shippers; the primary clearing, LC and FX lines must remain at a fully licensed Luxembourg credit institution.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% aggregate in Luxembourg City for taxable profits above EUR 175,000; 15% CIT on the first EUR 175,000, 17% CIT above that amount, plus 7% solidarity surcharge and municipal business tax at the applicable communal rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory Luxembourg withholding tax on dividends; 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for qualifying corporate shareholders holding at least 10% for 12 months; reduced DTA rates commonly 5%, 10% or 15% depending on the treaty.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 1,000,000 per related-party transaction category or in aggregate as specified by Luxembourg reporting practice; Form 1001 TP transfer-pricing disclosure is required with the annual CIT return when thresholds are met. Local File and Master File must be produced upon tax authority request; Country-by-Country Reporting applies at consolidated group revenue over EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not maintain UAE-style ESR categories, but substance is enforced through GAAR, ATAD, tax ruling practice and the 2022 substance guidance. A SARL must demonstrate physical presence, employees, active management decisions and real economic risk in Luxembourg; letterbox structures or conduit arrangements will be recharacterized and denied treaty/domestic exemption benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Declaration

Statutory sanction: Reassessment at the aggregate 24.94% rate, plus monthly late-payment interest of 0.6%; administrative penalties up to 20% of evaded tax; criminal tax fraud conviction can lead to significant fines and imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false returns or direct tax evasion may be held jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax; civil restitution, travel bans, asset freezes and imprisonment can result from criminal conviction.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure and Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of deductions, recharacterization of transactions, tax reassessment at up to 24.94%, late-payment interest, and administrative penalties for incomplete or inaccurate transfer pricing disclosure.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false or unsupported transfer-pricing statements can be personally liable for additional tax when the SARL is unable to pay; criminal sanctions apply in cases of intentional concealment.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Application of GAAR/ATAD to disregard the entity or recharacterize payments as hidden dividends subject to 15% withholding tax; denial of DTA benefits, participation exemption and IP regime; possible net wealth tax and exit tax consequences.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for unremitted withholding tax on recharacterized dividend distributions; continuing artificial arrangements can trigger criminal tax fraud exposure and travel bans.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Breach of the Luxembourg AML/CFT law of 12 November 2004: administrative fines up to EUR 1,250,000; money laundering convictions carry imprisonment and confiscation of assets.

Director personal liability: AML compliance officers and directors can be personally fined or imprisoned; serious violations may result in removal from office, travel bans and personal restitution of illicitly transferred proceeds.
