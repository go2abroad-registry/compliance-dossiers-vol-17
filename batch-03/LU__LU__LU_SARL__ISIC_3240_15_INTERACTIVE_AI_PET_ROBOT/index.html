Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual or domiciled registered office is permissible if supplied by a licensed Luxembourg domiciliation and management company governed by the Law of 31 May 1999 on domiciliation companies. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) – Luxembourg indirect tax authority; portal: MyGuichet.lu (single access point) and AEDT e-filing systems.

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must (1) register with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) to obtain a corporate identification number (Numéro d'immatriculation); (2) file standard VAT registration request (Form 645) with AEDT, either directly or through a fiscal representative if no establishment in Luxembourg; (3) provide constitutive documents, proof of economic presence or intended activity, and valid board resolution; (4) receive the Luxembourg VAT identification number (Numéro de TVA LU). The number is issued within 7–15 business days on submission of a complete dossier.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Luxembourg SARL is fully tax resident and subject to corporate income tax, municipal business tax, and net wealth tax on worldwide income subject to specific territorial exemptions (e.g., participation exemption). — Foreign entities must (1) register with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) to obtain a corporate identification number (Numéro d'immatriculation); (2) file standard VAT registration request (Form 645) with AEDT, either directly or through a fiscal representative if no establishment in Luxembourg; (3) provide constitutive documents, proof of economic presence or intended activity, and valid board resolution; (4) receive the Luxembourg VAT identification number (Numéro de TVA LU). The number is issued within 7–15 business days on submission of a complete dossier.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Luxembourg SARL is fully tax resident and subject to corporate income tax, municipal business tax, and net wealth tax on worldwide income subject to specific territorial exemptions (e.g., participation exemption).

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid entity election exists in Luxembourg. Available elections include VAT grouping (assujetti unique) for VAT consolidation; option to apply standard VAT to otherwise exempt real-estate transactions; simplified small-business VAT scheme for turnover below EUR 30,000; and the option to apply the Luxembourg corporate income tax group contribution regime (subject to statutory form and economic substance conditions).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 30,000 annual turnover exemption for small enterprises established in Luxembourg; non-resident suppliers have no threshold and must register for VAT upon the first taxable supply in Luxembourg, unless covered by an EU VAT One-Stop-Shop special regime.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods dispatched or transported to a destination outside the European Union are zero-rated, provided the exporter retains documentary evidence of physical departure within a reasonable period. Related services to the exported goods (where the services directly benefit the goods being exported) and certain offshore B2B services supplied to non-EU customers are also zero-rated if effectively used and enjoyed outside the EU.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Discharge EAD with customs stamp)
  • Bill of Lading (or CMR, Airway Bill, or other international transport document)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing foreign currency proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice and Intrastat/Extrastat declaration if applicable

No real-time fiscal clearance, SAP, or digital stamping mandate for standard B2B/B2C invoices. Mandatory B2G e-invoicing applies through PEPPOL (factur-x/UBL). B2B e-invoicing is permitted and legally equivalent to paper, requiring only authenticity of origin and integrity of content; no prior government authorisation or API clearance is required.

Luxembourg VAT law imposes export substantiation requirements that are actively audited. Where a zero-rating claim is disallowed, the entire VAT becomes due from the date of supply; interest at the statutory rate (currently 3% per annum above EURIBOR) applies. Intentional fraud is punishable by fines of up to EUR 50,000 or imprisonment and, in serious cases, aggravated penalties. Retrospective clawbacks may cover a 10-year limitation period in the case of fraud, and the exporter loses the right to deduct input VAT on the exported goods; criminal prosecution may follow.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg applies full capital- and current-account convertibility under EU law and the eurosystem framework; no exchange-control authorisation is required for inflows, outflows, dividends, or liquidation proceeds. The euro is legal tender and unrestricted in cross-border payments. Profit remittance through dividend distributions is legally unrestricted but is generally subject to Luxembourg dividend withholding tax at the domestic rate of 15%, reducible or exempt under tax treaties, the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive, or the Luxembourg participation exemption regime. Foreign-currency bank accounts are permissible, and no prior central-bank approval is required for capital injections or repatriations, provided the underlying corporate, substance, AML/KYC, and tax reporting obligations are satisfied.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Luxembourg imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement and no statutory mandatory conversion timeline or ratio. Exporters may retain receivables in EUR or foreign currency, convert freely, and settle trade payables as commercially required. Cross-border receipts are reported to the BCL/eurosystem for balance-of-payments and international-transactions statistics, normally by the resident bank rather than the exporter. Exporters should retain underlying sale contracts, invoices, transport documents, and payment confirmations to support AML source-of-funds reviews and any CSSF-required transparency in banking relationships.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Luxembourg (Banque centrale du Luxembourg – BCL), operating within the Eurosystem; banking and AML/CFT conduct supervision for credit institutions and electronic money institutions is exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium (supplementary rail only). Luxembourg permits payment services and e-money under the CSSF licensing regime implementing PSD2; an EU/EEA-licensed EMI may passport into Luxembourg, while a non-EU offshore EMI cannot operate a payment account or provide regulated cross-border payment services without a Luxembourg/EU licence or an authorised branch. A regulated EMI or global payment facilitator such as Stripe may be used for merchant credit-card collection, marketplace payouts, and small-value international disbursements, but the primary corporate treasury account should be held with a Luxembourg-licensed credit institution to ensure eurosystem clearing access, deposit protection, stable correspondent banking, and direct BCL statistical reporting.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Opacity in ultimate beneficial ownership or multi-tier cross-border group structure

Root cause: Opacity in ultimate beneficial ownership or multi-tier cross-border group structure

Defense evidence: Complete UBO declaration filed in the Luxembourg Register of Beneficial Owners (RBE), current Luxembourg trade register excerpt (RCS), notarised articles of association, full group organisation chart, shareholder registry, share-transfer chain documentation, sworn English translations, and a legal cover letter explaining the 100% ownership path from each UBO to LU_SARL.
Source-of-funds or source-of-wealth mismatch in the inbound capital subscription

Root cause: Source-of-funds or source-of-wealth mismatch in the inbound capital subscription

Defense evidence: Signed board resolution approving the capital injection, share subscription agreement, 3-6 months bank statements of the contributing shareholder or parent, source-of-wealth statement, tax residence certificates, audited or management financial statements of the parent, evidence of the corporate commercial activity, and a reconciliation of the exact capital amount against bank MT103 payment instructions.
FATCA/CRS classification ambiguity or doubtful tax residence of shareholders/directors

Root cause: FATCA/CRS classification ambiguity or doubtful tax residence of shareholders/directors

Defense evidence: Form W-8BEN/W-8BEN-E or CRS self-certification for every reportable controlling person, Luxembourg tax-residence certificate for LU_SARL, proof of Luxembourg substance (office lease, local director or employee arrangements, bank account, operational contracts), and written confirmation that no treaty-shopping structure is present.
Positive sanctions, PEP, or adverse-media screening hit on a shareholder or counterparty

Root cause: Positive sanctions, PEP, or adverse-media screening hit on a shareholder or counterparty

Defense evidence: Valid passport copies, sanctions and PEP questionnaire, explanatory statement addressing any name matches, official public-registry extracts, independent third-party due-diligence report, negative-news search, legal opinion confirming no sanctioned connection, and evidence of the legitimate commercial purpose of the transaction or relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route the share-capital contribution from the offshore or EU parent by SWIFT MT103 in EUR to the Luxembourg operational bank account. Field 59 must state the exact LU_SARL legal name and IBAN; Field 70 should read 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / LU_SARL' and, where the receiving bank requires an ISO 20022 purpose code, use 'EQUI' (equity) or 'CAPH' (capital increase) as accepted by the beneficiary bank. Field 71A should be set to 'OUR' so that the full amount reaches the SARL without correspondent deduction. Deliver to the bank outside the remote system a complete equity-issuance dossier: signed shareholder resolution, subscription deed, updated RCS excerpt, UBO declaration, source-of-funds evidence, tax self-certifications, and an economic-substance summary. No exchange-control permit is required; the receiving Luxembourg bank will classify the inbound amount as direct investment and report it to the BCL/eurosystem for balance-of-payments statistical purposes. Do not route capital through personal accounts, third-party pass-through accounts, or unregulated fintech intermediaries.Use SEPA Credit Transfer for EUR payments within the SEPA zone, including component purchases and EU supplier settlements. For non-EUR payments outside the eurozone, use SWIFT MT103 with full beneficiary name, IBAN/account number, SWIFT/BIC, invoice references, and transfer-purpose details; set correspondent charges to 'OUR' or allocate charges under 'SHA' only when commercially agreed. For documentary trade settlements, use a Commercial Letter of Credit opened via MT700 and amendments via MT707, governed by ICC UCP600, with the bank's trade desk confirming documents against the credit. Maintain a complete trade file for each disbursement: purchase order, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading/CMR/air waybill, insurance certificate, EORI/customs declarations (where goods cross borders), and inspection certificates. Intrastat/EXTRASTAT declarations must be made where value or volume thresholds are exceeded for intra-EU or extra-EU goods flows. 24.94% aggregate in Luxembourg City (17% corporate income tax + 7% solidarity surcharge on CIT = 1.19% + 6.75% municipal business tax); municipal business tax varies by commune

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory rate; reduced to 0% under the Luxembourg participation exemption / EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for shareholders holding ≥10% for ≥12 months; treaty rates generally 5% (≥10% participation) or 15% (portfolio)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: related-party transactions in excess of EUR 1,000,000 per transaction category (per ACD Circular L.I.R. 164/2); Master File: groups with consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750,000,000; CbCR: groups with consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750,000,000; documentation must be produced within 30 days of ACD request.

Economic substance scope: No separate ESR statute; substance is enforced through Art. 56 LIR (sham transactions), Art. 164 LIR (arm's length), EU ATAD GAAR and CFC rules, EU Anti-Tax-Avoidance Directive rules, and treaty beneficial-ownership requirements under the OECD MLI; a SARL manufacturing/packaging AI pet robots must maintain real premises, qualified staff, strategic decision-making in Luxembourg, and adequate capitalisation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Non-Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine of up to EUR 25,000 per offence, imprisonment of 8 days to 5 years, plus 100% surcharge on evaded tax and interest at 0.6% per month.

Director personal liability: Directors and de facto managers may be criminally prosecuted, subjected to travel bans, and held civilly liable for the company's unpaid taxes under the fraudulent-acts doctrine (action en responsabilité, Art. 1382 Civil Code).
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation under Art. 56 LIR, denial of treaty benefits and IP box/NID relief, re-pricing under Art. 164 LIR, and assessment of hidden profit distributions with 15% WHT.

Director personal liability: Liability to third parties and the tax administration for gross negligence; exclusion from tax relief and, in cross-border fraud, personal extradition and asset-freezing orders.
AML / Cash Transaction Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines up to EUR 1,250,000 or 10% of annual turnover for serious AML breaches; criminal sanctions for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors may face criminal prosecution, director disqualification, and personal civil liability for losses caused by AML breaches.
