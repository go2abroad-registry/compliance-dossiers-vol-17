UBO Ultimate Beneficial Owner Screening Rules Official Registry Only

>=25% ownership / control

Applicable AML statute: Law of 12 November 2004 on the fight against money laundering and terrorist financing, as consolidated and amended; Law of 13 January 2019 establishing the Register of Beneficial Owners (Registre des Bénéficiaires Effectifs) and its amendments; CSSF Regulations 12-02 and 16-07; Grand-Ducal Regulation of 1 February 2019 on RBE operational procedures; CSSF AML/CFT supervision requirements for investment and digital-economy intermediaries
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office addresses are accepted only as correspondence and marketing addresses; they do not satisfy the statutory requirement for a physical registered office in Luxembourg. A Sàrl must maintain a real, accessible seat within the Grand Duchy where statutory books, corporate records, and board minutes are held, and where process may be served. For a voice-streaming social-app company with payment rails through Apple and Google, a virtual-only office without local operational substance will not meet Luxembourg commercial substance expectations and may trigger tax, AML, and anti-abuse inquiry.

✓ Partner licensed company-secretary channels provide local registered address and government mail forwarding.

Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers, Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), operated by the Luxembourg Business Registers private-law company under delegated public authority.

Governing statute

Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as consolidated, notably Articles 710-1 to 710-16 for the Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL); Law of 19 December 2002 on the Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) and company accounts; Grand-Ducal Regulations on RCS filing and publication.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarial deed of incorporation (acte constitutif) containing the articles of association (statuts), authenticated by a Luxembourg notary and filed with the RCS for registration.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 21 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

EUR 12,000 minimum issued share capital, fully subscribed; at least 25% of the nominal value of each cash-created quota must be paid up at incorporation. Paid-in funds must be deposited in a Luxembourg bank blocked account in the company's name and remain blocked until full RCS registration is obtained.

Funding & unlock path:

Paid-in capital rule: EUR 12,000 minimum issued share capital, fully subscribed; at least 25% of the nominal value of each cash-created quota must be paid up at incorporation. Paid-in funds must be deposited in a Luxembourg bank blocked account in the company's name and remain blocked until full RCS registration is obtained.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED: (1) The first shareholders’ meeting approves the subscription of the entire share capital of EUR 12,000, consisting of 12,000 quotas of EUR 1.00 each, subscribed by the shareholders identified in the articles of association. (2) The registered office is fixed at [address], Luxembourg. The manager may elect additional administrative premises. (3) [Name] is appointed manager (gérant) with authority, acting alone or jointly as provided in the articles, to represent the company, open and maintain bank accounts, execute agreements, and complete regulatory filings. (4) The manager shall deposit the paid-up capital with a Luxembourg bank, instruct the Luxembourg notary to execute the deed of incorporation, and effect registration with the RCS and publication in the RESA.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Mandatory Environmental Clearance

Ministère de l'Économie – Direction générale des classes moyennes; Administration de l'environnement (AEV) for classified establishments under Luxembourg environmental law

Category: Autorisation d'établissement (business operating licence) under the Law of 30 May 2005 on commercial activities, together with, where applicable, the autorisation d'exploitation or notice de classement for classified establishments under the Law of 9 May 2014 on the prevention and management of environmental risks.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% (17% CIT + 7% solidarity surcharge + 6.75% municipal business tax)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% domestic; 0% under EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive; treaty relief often 0–5%.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due by 30 June of the year following the tax year for calendar-year taxpayers; automatic administrative extension to 31 December when filed electronically.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 Identification: company name, registered office, RCS number, tax ID. SARL with Luxembourg residence must provide local address and ultimate beneficial owner details. Late filing: administrative fine EUR 250–2,500; after formal notice, additional 10% of tax due; late interest 0.6% per month; intentional false declaration may lead to criminal penalties of imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 250,000.
Box 2 Accounting period and balance sheet data. State any taxable permanent establishment in Luxembourg separately. Late filing: administrative fine EUR 250–2,500; after formal notice, additional 10% of tax due; late interest 0.6% per month; intentional false declaration may lead to criminal penalties of imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 250,000.
Box 3 Net commercial profit before tax adjustments. Adjust for non-deductible expenses and other differences. Late filing: administrative fine EUR 250–2,500; after formal notice, additional 10% of tax due; late interest 0.6% per month; intentional false declaration may lead to criminal penalties of imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 250,000.
Box 4 Non-deductible charges (e.g. excess interest, non-arm's length payments, fines). Include in full; denial of deduction triggers additional tax. Late filing: administrative fine EUR 250–2,500; after formal notice, additional 10% of tax due; late interest 0.6% per month; intentional false declaration may lead to criminal penalties of imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 250,000.
Box 5 Deductions: interest, royalties, participation exemption, tax losses carried forward. Apply treaty and EU Directive relief only with proper documentary support. Late filing: administrative fine EUR 250–2,500; after formal notice, additional 10% of tax due; late interest 0.6% per month; intentional false declaration may lead to criminal penalties of imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 250,000.
Box 6 Taxable income after all adjustments. Must match transfer pricing documentation and substance evidence. Late filing: administrative fine EUR 250–2,500; after formal notice, additional 10% of tax due; late interest 0.6% per month; intentional false declaration may lead to criminal penalties of imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 250,000.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 50 million group consolidated turnover; local/master file mandatory for cross-border transactions exceeding EUR 1 million (goods/intangibles) or EUR 5 million (services/financing), if income tax benefit is derived.

Economic substance scope: No separate ESR code, but Luxembourg GAAR and tax administration require genuine substance: premises, employees, decision-making, and actual commercial activities. Absence triggers recharacterisation, denial of treaty/participation exemption, and penalty surcharges.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (fraude fiscale)

Statutory sanction: Tax surcharge of 10–50% of evaded tax; late interest 0.6% per month; criminal fine up to EUR 250,000; imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally liable for the tax debt, subject to seizing of personal assets, travel bans, and criminal imprisonment.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Disregard of legal personality for tax purposes, upward recharacterisation of income, loss of participation exemption, additional tax of up to 10% plus penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors may face joint liability for tax underpayments and fines; severe cases lead to prohibition from managing corporate entities and criminal imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Upward adjustment to taxable base, 10% penal surcharge, late interest, and in cases of flagrant fraud criminal sanctions up to EUR 250,000 fine.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or authorise mispriced transactions can be held personally responsible for resulting tax liability and fines.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine up to EUR 250,000 per individual and EUR 1 million per legal entity; imprisonment up to 5 years for deliberate concealment.

Director personal liability: Directors exposed to personal criminal liability, travel bans, and directors' disqualification.
