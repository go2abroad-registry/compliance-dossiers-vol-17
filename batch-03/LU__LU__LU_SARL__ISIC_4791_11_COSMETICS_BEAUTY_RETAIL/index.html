Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) – Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), with mandatory notarial intervention by a Luxembourg civil-law notary.

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (Title VI, Articles 710-1 to 710-24, governing the société à responsabilité limitée); Law of 19 December 2002 on the Register of Commerce and Companies and the Luxembourg Business Registers; Law of 2 September 2011 on access to the professions of craftsman, merchant and industrialist.

Formation / constitutional filings

Authentic notarial deed of incorporation (acte constitutif) containing the articles of association, followed by RCS registration through the Luxembourg Business Registers.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 40 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum share capital is EUR 12,000 and must be fully subscribed. Cash contributions must be deposited and blocked in a bank account opened with a Luxembourg credit institution in the name of the company being formed, or held in notarial escrow, and evidenced by a bank blocking certificate or notary receipt before the deed is signed; in-kind contributions must be fully vested and, when required, valued by an approved statutory auditor (réviseur d’entreprises). The blocked funds are released only after RCS registration.

Funding & unlock path: No fire safety clearance is required for a purely administrative registered office. Any warehouse or premises open to employees must pass a fire safety inspection by the Corps Grand-Ducal d’Incendie et de Secours, provide structural load certificates if racking is installed, and be declared to the Inspection du Travail et des Mines before the first employee is engaged.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum share capital is EUR 12,000 and must be fully subscribed. Cash contributions must be deposited and blocked in a bank account opened with a Luxembourg credit institution in the name of the company being formed, or held in notarial escrow, and evidenced by a bank blocking certificate or notary receipt before the deed is signed; in-kind contributions must be fully vested and, when required, valued by an approved statutory auditor (réviseur d’entreprises). The blocked funds are released only after RCS registration.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AED) for indirect taxes; official multichannel portal: Guichet.lu / MyGuichet.lu; company register interface: rcs.lbr.lu (Luxembourg Business Registers).

E-government filing portal

1) Notarized incorporation deed of the SARL (minimum share capital EUR 12,000; at least 25% paid up); 2) Registration with the Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS) within 1 month of deed execution to obtain the national business identification number (matricule, 11-digit); 3) The RCS online filing automatically notifies the ACD for corporate income tax, municipal business tax and net wealth tax purposes and the AED for VAT; the taxpayer receives the direct tax file number and the Luxembourg VAT number (format LU + 8 digits) through MyGuichet; 4) Filing of the beneficial ownership (RBE) declaration within 1 month; 5) Non-resident shareholders or directors must present certified identification and a local registered office address; no economic-substance filing is required at registration stage, but substance documentation is expected for future tax treatment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - A Luxembourg SARL is fully opaque and resident for tax purposes; taxable on worldwide income under Luxembourg CIT, municipal business tax and net wealth tax; no pass-through or check-the-box option is available.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Luxembourg. Available statutory elections: (1) fiscal integration (tax consolidation) under Art. 164bis LIR for Luxembourg-resident subsidiaries held at least 95%; (2) VAT grouping as a single taxable person under Art. 59 of the VAT Code for financially, economically and organisationally integrated resident entities; (3) election of the 80% IP-box exemption under Art. 50bis LIR; (4) election of a tax year-end other than 31 December; (5) binding rulings with the ACD on business models and transfer pricing; anti-hybrid rules (ATAD) are mandatory, not elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No domestic de minimis turnover threshold: any person carrying on an independent economic activity in Luxembourg is registrable from the first euro. For EU distance sales into Luxembourg, the union-wide threshold is EUR 10,000 per calendar year (Art. 59bis VAT Code), above which the OSS regime applies. For imports of low-value consignments not exceeding EUR 150, the IOSS regime is available.

No real-time fiscal clearance, no dedicated e-invoicing clearance API and no digital stamping obligation for private B2B/B2C transactions. Mandatory e-invoicing applies only to public procurement (PEPPOL/Peppol BIS). Standard EU invoicing rules apply (Art. 75-77 VAT Code), including sequential numbering, audit trail and a 10-year archiving obligation. Physical point-of-sale retail in Luxembourg may be subject to the certified electronic cash register requirement (Law of 19 December 2020), but the pure online DTC flow using Shopify Payments / PayPal does not trigger that POS cash-register obligation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is part of the eurozone and the EU single market; both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible under Articles 63-65 TFEU. There are no exchange control restrictions, no FDI screening, and no profit remittance hurdles for non-resident shareholders beyond standard Luxembourg withholding tax (15% general dividend withholding, reduced to 0% / 5% under participation exemption or tax treaty) and supporting documentation of beneficial ownership. Foreign currency accounts with no position limits are freely permitted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio exists for export proceeds in Luxembourg. Exporters may retain balances in any currency; only statistical reporting thresholds to BCL balance of payments and CSSF AML/CFT reporting apply. No statutory repatriation timeline is imposed by law.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) in coordination with Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) and the Luxembourg Ministry of Finance

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High viability for EU/EEA-regulated electronic money institutions (EMIs) that hold a licence recognized in Luxembourg under PSD2, including local CSSF-licensed EMIs and EEA passporting EMIs. Offshore (non-EEA) EMIs may not provide services to the SARL without a Luxembourg branch or an authorized payment institution representative office; reverse solicitation is applied narrowly and creates a legal risk. For digital commerce, EMIs such as Airwallex, Currencycloud, and multi-currency PSPs are feasible if they maintain segregated safeguarding accounts and full AML/KYC acceptance. Maintain a tier-1 resident bank settlement account as the primary treasury account and use the EMI only for e-commerce collection and disbursement rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Incoming shareholder capital contributions must be sent via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own bank account to the SARL's resident bank account, using ISO 20022 external purpose code 'EQUI' (equity). The remittance must reference the notarial deed of capital increase, the RCS registration number, and the bank's required beneficiary identification. Verification includes the bank's 'notice of inward remittance', the RCS updated capital certificate, and confirmation of proper VAT/CRS/AML checks. For EU-originated payments, SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant is acceptable for amounts under €100k, with purpose code 'BEXP' if not classified as equity; nevertheless, direct capital subscriptions must clearly state 'EQUI'.Outbound supplier payments in euros are executed via SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) or SEPA Instant for settlement; for non-euro currencies, SWIFT MT103 is the primary clean payment rail worldwide. For larger inventory purchases, a commercial letter of credit (sight or deferred) issued through BGL/BCEE may be used, requiring the supplier's draft and complying documents. Shopify/PayPal disbursements settle to the SARL's resident bank account via standard collection rails. All outbound payments must be reconciled against underlying invoices, Incoterms and trade documentation, and respect the anti-money-laundering payment thresholds requiring EU Automated Reporting on cross-border transactions.Outright deliverable FX forward contracts (EUR/USD, EUR/CNY, EUR/GBP) with maturities up to 24 monthsNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for currencies with capital controls or transfer restrictions (e.g., offshore RMB, certain Asian currencies)Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCS/CIRS) aligned with EURIBOR or €STR benchmarksVanilla FX options and zero-premium collars, entered into with board-approved counterparties under EMIR/MiFID II

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 50 million group consolidated turnover and EUR 1 million entity turnover trigger Local File; Master File required if group turnover exceeds EUR 50 million.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg applies Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) for entities engaged in certain activities (e.g., holding, financing, IP). For a retail company, ESR is not directly applicable, but general substance requirements (office, staff, decision-making) must be met to avoid being classified as a shell company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; additional tax surcharges up to 100% of evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for unpaid taxes if they knowingly participated in evasion; civil restitution, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 5 years.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits, reclassification of income, and imposition of 15% withholding tax on deemed distributions; penalties up to EUR 25,000.

Director personal liability: Directors may be liable for fines and restitution; potential criminal charges for fraud if intent is proven.
AML/CFT Violations

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines up to EUR 5 million or 10% of annual turnover; criminal penalties for money laundering up to 5 years imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and imprisoned; travel bans may be imposed during investigation.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income, penalties up to 10% of the adjustment, and interest on underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors may be liable for penalties if they knowingly approved non-compliant pricing; civil liability for damages.
