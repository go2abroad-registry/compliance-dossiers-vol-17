Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Not permissible as a statutory office for a SARL in the strict sense. The registered office (siège statutaire) must be located in Luxembourg and appear on the RCS; however, the concept of a 'registered office service' (cabinet de domiciliation) is permitted under strict statutory conditions: the domiciliation agent must be licensed as a domiciliation agent under the Law of 31 May 1999 on domiciliation of companies (Loi du 31 mai 1999 relative à la domiciliation de sociétés), and the company must maintain real economic link (siège effectif) in Luxembourg, evidenced by meeting minutes, operational records, or administrative management taking place there. Pure 'virtual office' arrangements without any physical substance in Luxembourg are not recognized and will trigger rebuttal of the tax residency presumption and AML compliance deficiencies. For the hydroponics ecommerce business, a registered agent address is acceptable as the back-office only where at least one administrative function (e.g., invoicing, contractual negotiations, or bookkeeping) is performed in Luxembourg; the e-commerce operational head-office may be domiciled offshore but the statutory registered office cannot be purely virtual. Luxembourg Revenue (Administration des contributions directes) requires substance alignment under the ATAD and OECD nexus rules if the company intends to claim treaty benefits.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

The SARL must subscribe a total share capital of at least EUR 12,000; a statutory minimum of 20% (EUR 2,400) must be fully paid up in cash at incorporation and deposited on a blocked capital account ('compte de dépôt bloqué') with a Luxembourg credit institution or the Caisse de Consignation; the blocking certificate is produced at the notarial deed and funds are released only after RCS registration; the remaining subscribed capital must be called within five years.

Funding & unlock path: Compliance with the Law of 17 June 1994 on safety and health at work: ITM workplace notification, personnel register, first-aid provision, risk assessment and periodic electrical installation inspection; fire safety: issue of the fire-safety clearance (avis incendie) by the competent municipal fire brigade for warehouse and office premises, structural load check for storage racks, exit and emergency-lighting controls; the premises must show a valid occupancy certificate ('certificat d'autorisation d'exploitation') before staff deployment.

Paid-in capital rule: The SARL must subscribe a total share capital of at least EUR 12,000; a statutory minimum of 20% (EUR 2,400) must be fully paid up in cash at incorporation and deposited on a blocked capital account ('compte de dépôt bloqué') with a Luxembourg credit institution or the Caisse de Consignation; the blocking certificate is produced at the notarial deed and funds are released only after RCS registration; the remaining subscribed capital must be called within five years.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct tax. Portal: MyGuichet.lu and guichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities not yet established in Luxembourg may obtain a Luxembourg VAT identification number by submitting Form 565 (Demande de numéro d'identification à la TVA) to the AEDT, accompanied by a copy of the foreign registration certificate, articles of association, and evidence of economic activity or planned transactions. If the entity is already registered in the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS), the VAT number is issued automatically or upon simplified declaration through MyGuichet. Non-resident entities are not generally required to have a Luxembourg permanent establishment to obtain a VAT ID where they undertake taxable transactions in Luxembourg, including removal goods into Luxembourg or intra-Community acquisitions.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

The SARL is a capital company within the scope of Luxembourg corporate income tax and cannot be treated as fiscally transparent. No check-the-box election exists under Luxembourg domestic law; however, Luxembourg has implemented ATAD-compliant hybrid mismatch rules (Law of 7 February 2018, as amended) and does offer a written legal status confirmation for hybrid structures. The SARL may, subject to conditions, elect the group income taxation regime (régime d'intégration fiscale) with qualifying Luxembourg subsidiaries, and may elect the exemption for parent companies under the participation exemption if the 10% shareholding threshold or acquisition price threshold is met.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

18%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Domestic annual taxable turnover above EUR 30,000 (exclusive of VAT) triggers mandatory VAT registration under the small enterprise exemption rules. For intra-Community distance sales and OSS (One Stop Shop) purposes, the pan-EU threshold is EUR 10,000; sales in Luxembourg are taxable from the first euro where that threshold is exceeded.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to supplies of goods dispatched or transported outside the European Union. The supplier must retain proof that the goods left the EU within 90 days of the supply or within an extended period accepted by the tax administration. Services related to exported goods may also qualify for zero rating where the recipient is established outside the EU and the services are directly connected with goods destined for export. Intra-Community supplies to VAT-registered EU customers are exempt rather than zero rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD, DAU) with outbound customs office receiving date and signature
  • Bill of Lading, Sea Waybill, airway bill, CMR or other transport document evidencing physical movement to the destination country
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/foreign exchange records evidencing payment received from the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice and, where applicable, Intrastat listings or export loading lists

Luxembourg does not currently mandate real-time B2B e-invoicing or fiscal electronic clearance. Taxable persons may issue e-invoices subject to authenticity and integrity controls (e.g., PDF, EDI or structured XML). Export-oriented e-commerce operators must file Intrastat declarations for goods movements and, when registered in OSS, file periodic electronic VAT returns through the Luxembourg national OSS portal. No digital stamping or government pre-clearance is required for invoices.

Luxembourg VAT authorities may retrospectively deny zero rating and claw back VAT at the standard rate where the exporter fails to provide documentary proof of export or where evidence is fraudulent. Surcharges of 10% apply to belated payments, and monthly interest is charged on under-declared VAT. In cases of deliberate fraud, penalties may reach 100% of the tax evaded, with criminal fines and imprisonment for repeat offenders. All export evidence must be retained for at least six years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg maintains full current and capital account convertibility with no exchange-control authorization, license, or pre-notification requirement. As a Eurozone member, it applies the free movement of capital under Article 63 TFEU; inbound equity, trade settlement, and profit repatriation are unrestricted. The only material friction on profit remittance is the 15% Luxembourg withholding tax on dividends, which is reduced to 0% for qualifying corporate shareholders under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive or under most double tax treaties. The SARL may freely distribute earnings and repay intercompany loans in EUR or any convertible currency without BCL approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory timeline for repatriation and no mandatory conversion ratio. Luxembourg does not require export proceeds to be surrendered, centralized within a prescribed period, or converted into EUR. The SARL may lawfully retain USD, GBP, or other settlement currency balances indefinitely in its treasury accounts. The only administrative duty is the accurate recording of direct investment and trade flows for BCL balance-of-payments statistical reporting where applicable thresholds are met; the functional currency election must also be documented in the annual accounts under Luxembourg GAAP if significant FX balances are held.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating as the Luxembourg member of the Eurosystem, and the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) as national competent authority for prudential and AML/CFT supervision of credit institutions and payment services providers under the Luxembourg Act of 12 November 2004 on the fight against money laundering and terrorist financing, as amended.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium viability as a supplementary collection and FX rail; low viability as a primary treasury backbone. Luxembourg-licensed Electronic Money Institutions are authorised by the CSSF under the Law of 10 August 2018 transposing PSD2; EEA EMIs may passport into Luxembourg. Offshore EMIs without an EEA passport are legally prohibited from holding euro settlement balances or servicing LU entities. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Share capital contribution of EUR 12,000 (or the lower amount permitted under the simplified SARL regime) must arrive by SWIFT MT103 with ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' (Equity) and memo 'Share Capital Subscription - Luxembourg SARL'; the receiving bank issues the blocked-account attestation required by the notary. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required if group revenue exceeds EUR 50 million and related-party transactions exceed EUR 1 million; Master File required if group revenue exceeds EUR 50 million.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg applies Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) primarily to holding, financing, and IP companies. For retail e-commerce, substance is evidenced by physical presence, employees, and decision-making in Luxembourg; no specific ESR filing required for this activity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine up to EUR 250,000 or 10% of evaded tax; criminal prosecution; additional civil penalties and interest.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for unpaid taxes if they knowingly participated in evasion; travel bans and imprisonment up to 5 years.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits, reclassification of income, and imposition of 15% WHT on deemed dividends; fines up to EUR 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors may face personal liability for taxes due, plus penalties; potential criminal charges for abuse of law.
AML (Anti-Money Laundering)

Statutory sanction: Fines up to EUR 1 million for the company; suspension of business license; criminal prosecution of responsible individuals.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined up to EUR 500,000 and imprisoned for up to 5 years; travel bans may be imposed.
