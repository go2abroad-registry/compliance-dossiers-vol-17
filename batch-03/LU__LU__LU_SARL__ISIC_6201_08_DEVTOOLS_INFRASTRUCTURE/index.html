Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum subscribed share capital: EUR 12,000. At incorporation, at least 25% of each share (minimum EUR 3,000) must be fully paid up and deposited in a blocked escrow account opened with a Luxembourg or EU credit institution; the depositary bank must issue a capital blocking certificate (attestation de blocage) prior to RCS registration; the remaining balance must be paid within five years

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD), Luxembourg Direct Tax Administration, 33 rue de Gasperich, L-5826 Luxembourg (portal: impotsdirects.public.lu and eCDF electronic filing); VAT is administered separately by the Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT); company law filings and business registry access are handled via the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) and MyGuichet.lu single portal.

E-government filing portal

Phase A: Incorporate the SARL by notarial deed and register with the RCS (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés), which automatically issues the national business registration number (matricule) via the Central Business Register (RCE); the matricule serves as the corporate TIN. Phase B: Submit VAT registration form 810 (Déclaration d'assujettissement à la TVA) to the AEDT through MyGuichet.lu to obtain the Luxembourg VAT number (prefix 'LU' + 8 digits) within 2-4 weeks. Phase C: Apply separately to the Administration des douanes et accises for an EORI number for customs/export operations. Phase D: Obtain the direct tax reference from the ACD upon first lodgment of the corporate income tax and net wealth tax returns in the eCDF system. Non-EU-incorporated foreign entities without a Luxembourg establishment must appoint a Luxembourg VAT fiscal representative; EU-established suppliers may register directly under Article 62 of the 1979 VAT Law.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Luxembourg has no check-the-box or pass-through election for SARLs; all resident SARLs are mandatorily taxed at entity level (IRC, municipal business tax, net wealth tax). Available statutory elections: (i) fiscal integration (intégration fiscale / Vertikalkonsolidierung) under Article 164bis LIR for a 95% directly/indirectly controlled Luxembourg-resident group, irrevocable for at least 5 years; (ii) VAT grouping election under Article 60 of the VAT Act for closely linked Luxembourg-established entities wishing to be treated as a single taxable person; (iii) election for the small-business VAT exemption under Article 57 where annual turnover is below the threshold; (iv) option for intra-Community distance sales to use the EU VAT One-Stop-Shop. Hybrid mismatch rules under ATAD 2 (Law of 25 March 2020) apply mandatorily with no elective carve-out for hybrid instruments or hybrid payer entities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible. Luxembourg maintains zero exchange-control restrictions on either current-account or capital-account transactions pursuant to Article 63 TFEU and the consolidated BCL circular framework. Dividend and profit remittance are unimpeded by FX approval; the only material frictions are the statutory 15% Luxembourg dividend withholding tax (treaty-reducible) and the obligation to file an annual corporate income tax return before effective clearance of large distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Luxembourg imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation deadline on export proceeds. The SARL may lawfully hold and denominate export receipts in EUR or any foreign currency indefinitely, provided cross-border transactions above EUR 12,500 per item are reported to the BCL for balance-of-payments statistics.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), functioning within the Eurosystem; market conduct, AML and payment institution supervision by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF); no separate foreign-exchange control administration exists for Luxembourg due to euro membership.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium as a secondary disbursement and collections rail, Low as the sole statutory or treasury bank. EU-licensed electronic money institutions (Wise EU, Airwallex EU, Revolut Business) accept Luxembourg SARLs and issue segregated EU IBANs under CSSF e-money passporting rules; however, they cannot serve as the depositary bank for the SARL statutory minimum capital (EUR 12,000) under the amended Law of 10 August 1915, and they do not provide committed credit, letters of credit, or interbank FX forward lines. Mercury ACH is legally unavailable to non-US entities and must be excluded from the design. Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

UBO or intermediate holding entity domiciled in a high-risk, zero-tax, or non-Cooperative Jurisdiction (e.g., offshore layers in the ownership chain).

Root cause: UBO or intermediate holding entity domiciled in a high-risk, zero-tax, or non-Cooperative Jurisdiction (e.g., offshore layers in the ownership chain).

Defense evidence: Complete chain of title from ultimate individuals to the SARL; tax residency certificates (Formulaire 100 / LIR 160 Certificat de Résidence) for every intermediate entity; three years of corporate income tax returns; notarized share register; economic-substance narrative with office lease, payroll records, and board minutes evidencing Luxembourg management substance.
High-frequency, low-value B2B SaaS receivables aggregated from a large base of global micro-merchants via Stripe billing.

Root cause: High-frequency, low-value B2B SaaS receivables aggregated from a large base of global micro-merchants via Stripe billing.

Defense evidence: Executed master subscription agreements with the top-20 customers; platform analytics and transaction-flow summary; SOC 2 Type II report; recognized revenue schedule per IFRS 15; and a reconciliation statement mapping Stripe settlement files to the target bank account to demonstrate a legitimate, auditable revenue stream.
Upstream intercompany shareholder loan with non-arm's-length pricing or absence of a written loan agreement.

Root cause: Upstream intercompany shareholder loan with non-arm's-length pricing or absence of a written loan agreement.

Defense evidence: Notarized loan agreement (acte de prêt) with maturity and interest schedule; OECD transfer pricing comparability analysis and benchmarking study; group intercompany policy memorandum; BCL balance-of-payments declaration for each disbursement exceeding EUR 12,500; and draft Luxembourg CIT return reflecting arm's-length interest.
History of cryptocurrency-derived or ambiguously structured investor funds entering the SARL capital base.

Root cause: History of cryptocurrency-derived or ambiguously structured investor funds entering the SARL capital base.

Defense evidence: Source-of-wealth statement certified by a Luxembourg notary; twelve months of exchange or brokerage statements showing the disposal trail; tax clearance certificate (Avis de taxation) for the investor; a notarized afﬁdavit of lawful origin; and confirmation that no proceeds emanate from sanctioned wallets or mixing services.
Rapid circular movement of funds between EU EMIs and the tier-1 bank account without clear economic purpose codes.

Root cause: Rapid circular movement of funds between EU EMIs and the tier-1 bank account without clear economic purpose codes.

Defense evidence: Treasury policy memorandum approving the payment rails; individually matched purpose codes per transfer (e.g., SUPP – supplier payment, RENT – operating expense); underlying invoices or contracts; monthly EMI-to-bank reconciliation; and a notarized corporate resolution authorizing the treasury routing matrix.
