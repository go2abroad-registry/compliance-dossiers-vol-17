Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (Loi modifiée du 10 août 1915 concernant les sociétés commerciales), Articles 109 to 158 governing the Société à Responsabilité Limitée; Law of 19 December 2002 on the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register.

Formation / constitutional filings

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Statutory minimum share capital is EUR 12,000. At least 25% (EUR 3,000) must be fully paid up and deposited in a Luxembourg bank in a blocked escrow account prior to notarial signature; the account remains frozen until the RCS registration certificate is issued.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Fire and Rescue Administration (Administration des services de secours – CSS) via the communal authority; structural load inspection of shelving and flooring by an approved control office; occupational clearance from the Labour Inspectorate (Inspection du Travail et des Mines – ITM) covering work safety, first-aid equipment, and compliance with the Grand-Ducal Regulation on workplace safety.

Paid-in capital rule: Statutory minimum share capital is EUR 12,000. At least 25% (EUR 3,000) must be fully paid up and deposited in a Luxembourg bank in a blocked escrow account prior to notarial signature; the account remains frozen until the RCS registration certificate is issued.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Grand Duchy of Luxembourg — Indirect/VAT: Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT), VAT returns filed via MyGuichet.lu (e-TVA channel); Direct taxes: Administration des contributions directes (ACD); Customs/export formalities: Administration des douanes et accises (Customs & Excise). Central business registry: Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR / RCS) accessible through MyGuichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

SOP for non-resident owners forming a Luxembourg SARL engaged in ISIC 5229 reverse logistics: (1) reserve corporate name, execute notarial deed of incorporation, and deposit minimum share capital of EUR 12,000 with a Luxembourg credit institution; (2) file incorporation at the RCS/LBR, obtaining the national registration number (matricule); (3) submit the VAT registration application to the AEDT via MyGuichet.lu (form 604 or equivalent electronic declaration); AEDT issues the Luxembourg VAT number with prefix LU + 8 digits; (4) obtain an EORI number from the Administration des douanes et accises before lodging any customs/export declarations; (5) if the SARL is represented by a non-EU principal, appoint an approved Luxembourg VAT fiscal representative who is jointly and severally liable for VAT; (6) complete the beneficial owner declaration in the RBE register. Registration becomes effective from the first taxable transaction with no post-incorporation grace period.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — a resident SARL is a separate taxable person subject to Luxembourg corporate income tax (15% headline CIT plus 7% solidarity surcharge, plus municipal business tax, aggregate approximately 24.94% in Luxembourg City), net wealth tax (0.5% with minimum CIT-based floor), and VAT as a taxable person. Luxembourg does not provide a US-style check-the-box pass-through election for SARLs; no territorial exemption and no tax-exempt zone regime applies — the residence-based system is mitigated by the participation exemption (100% exemption for qualifying dividends and capital gains) and treaty reliefs. — SOP for non-resident owners forming a Luxembourg SARL engaged in ISIC 5229 reverse logistics: (1) reserve corporate name, execute notarial deed of incorporation, and deposit minimum share capital of EUR 12,000 with a Luxembourg credit institution; (2) file incorporation at the RCS/LBR, obtaining the national registration number (matricule); (3) submit the VAT registration application to the AEDT via MyGuichet.lu (form 604 or equivalent electronic declaration); AEDT issues the Luxembourg VAT number with prefix LU + 8 digits; (4) obtain an EORI number from the Administration des douanes et accises before lodging any customs/export declarations; (5) if the SARL is represented by a non-EU principal, appoint an approved Luxembourg VAT fiscal representative who is jointly and severally liable for VAT; (6) complete the beneficial owner declaration in the RBE register. Registration becomes effective from the first taxable transaction with no post-incorporation grace period.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a resident SARL is a separate taxable person subject to Luxembourg corporate income tax (15% headline CIT plus 7% solidarity surcharge, plus municipal business tax, aggregate approximately 24.94% in Luxembourg City), net wealth tax (0.5% with minimum CIT-based floor), and VAT as a taxable person. Luxembourg does not provide a US-style check-the-box pass-through election for SARLs; no territorial exemption and no tax-exempt zone regime applies — the residence-based system is mitigated by the participation exemption (100% exemption for qualifying dividends and capital gains) and treaty reliefs.

Available election forms

Available statutory elections: (a) VAT grouping (assujetti unique) under Article 61 of the Luxembourg VAT Law, enabling the SARL and affiliated companies to be treated as one taxable person upon AEDT approval; (b) option to apply VAT to the let of immovable property under Article 44 of the VAT Law; (c) direct-tax fiscal unity (intégration fiscale) regime, election/application available for qualifying 95%-owned resident or Luxembourg permanent establishments of EEA-resident entities, subject to ACD prior approval for the relevant tax year; (d) optional claim of the VAT small-business exemption where annual turnover does not exceed the statutory threshold. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are mandatory under ATAD II transposition and are not elective; advance tax agreements/rulings with the ACD are accessible to confirm treatment of cross-border reverse-logistics fee arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR — Luxembourg: no VAT registration turnover threshold for foreign/non-established suppliers (registration due from the first euro of taxable activity, with mandatory Luxembourg fiscal representative for non-EU principals); established small enterprises may benefit from the Article 57 VAT Law exemption while annual taxable turnover does not exceed EUR 30,000, provided the status is explicitly claimed and the business is not precluded by the legal form or election.

Luxembourg does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control (CTC), or digital stamping for B2B invoices. Mandatory e-invoicing applies only to B2G transactions via the PEPPOL network and the national public procurement platform; B2B invoices must satisfy Article 5/6 VAT Law content requirements (sequential numbering, issuer VAT number, delivery details, invoice date and issuance deadline). VAT returns are submitted electronically (e-TVA), VIES validation is required for intra-EU customer VAT numbers, and export declarations must be lodged through the EU customs systems (ECS/Export, NCTS) with the Administration des douanes et accises; no revision of invoice flow or separate fiscal receipt stamping is required.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg has no exchange controls. Article 63 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union guarantees free movement of capital to and from third countries. The euro is fully convertible on both current and capital account. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, and shareholder-loan repayment to non-residents are unrestricted subject to the standard Luxembourg dividend withholding tax (15% domestic; 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive with a 12-month holding period) and to statutory BCL/CSSF statistical reporting. No prior authorisation, no FDI screening, and no capital-export restrictions apply to a reverse-logistics SARL (ISIC 5229 / NAICS 488510).

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion rules exist in Luxembourg. Export receipts in foreign currency may be retained or converted at market rates without any time limit. The only compliance duties are (i) flow-by-flow balance-of-payments reporting to the BCL for all cross-border payment transactions, effected via the reporting bank, and (ii) cash-declaration and AML obligations under the amended Law of 12 November 2004 if physical cash or unusual settlement methods are used.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) within the Eurosystem and the European Central Bank (ECB); the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) is the competent credit-institution and AML/CFT supervisor; the Cellule de Renseignement Financier (CRF/Luxembourg FIU) receives suspicious-transaction reports.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

MEDIUM-HIGH for disbursement-only use cases and LOW for primary treasury concentration. A Luxembourg SARL may lawfully use Airwallex or any non-EU/EEA digital EMI only through that EMI's EU/EEA-authorised e-money institution passporting into Luxembourg under the PSD2 and the Law of 10 August 2018 on payment services and e-money (transposing Directive 2009/110/EC). Passport notification to the CSSF by the home-state supervisor is mandatory; the EMI must not breach the Luxembourg banking monopoly (Article 28-1 of the Law of 5 August 1993). Funds safeguarded with an external EMI are not covered by the Luxembourg deposit-guarantee scheme (Fonds de protection des dépôts Luxembourg) and therefore should never replace a tier-1 bank treasury account. Recommended structure: retain BCEE or BNP Paribas as the primary liquidity concentration bank and use Airwallex as a downstream, small-ticket refund rail with capped balances for e-commerce return merchandise.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Root cause: Multi-tier non-resident ownership with opaque ultimate beneficial ownership or UBOs domiciled in jurisdictions with less-developed transparency frameworks.

Defense evidence: Luxembourg UBO register extract (Registre des Bénéficiaires Effectifs), RCSL extract (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés), notarised articles of association, certified passport copies and proof of residential address of all directors and UBOs, a written control-chain narrative, bank references, and, if any tier exceeds three entities, a legal opinion confirming the effective control chain and absence of undisclosed nominees.

Root cause: Unusual payment pattern: high-frequency, low-average-ticket cross-border refunds and return-reimbursement wires without clear linkage to original orders or commercial documents.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: SWIFT MT103 (single UETR) from the shareholder's bank to the LU IBAN account of the SARL, with remittance information in field 70 stating the EU balance-of-payments purpose code 'S001' (direct investment – equity capital), the notarial deed reference, and the share-purchase narration. TARGET2 (T2) real-time settlement is used for immediate funds; MT202/MT202 COVER is acceptable for onward cover. The notary's attestation of capital payment and the shareholder bank statement comprise the verification bundle. All cross-border capital flows must be reported flow-by-flow to the BCL by the receiving bank under the Luxembourg balance-of-payments/IOFA statistics regime.Preferred clearing rails: SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) and SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) for EUR-denominated refunds and supplier payments within the Single Euro Payments Area; SWIFT MT103 with UETR for non-EUR supplier disbursements, referencing invoice numbers and customs declarations in field 70. For large return-merchandise settlements above EUR 50,000, a commercial letter of credit under UCP 600 or documentary collections issued by the Luxembourg bank constitute the standard trade-clearing rail. Local ACH (LuxCSD / SEPA settlement) is used for payroll and fiscal payments, and Airwallex is deployed as the fintech refund rail for small-ticket, high-velocity e-commerce reverse-logistics flows. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

17% (plus 7% solidarity surcharge and municipal business tax; aggregate rate ~24.94% in Luxembourg City)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% (0% on qualifying dividends under EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive if ≥10% holding is maintained for 12 consecutive months)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

31 December of the calendar year following the fiscal year-end (extensions generally available to 30 June of the subsequent year upon request)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File required if the Luxembourg entity's cross-border related-party transactions exceed EUR 1,000,000 (aggregate) and the group's consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 50,000,000; Country-by-Country Reporting threshold is EUR 750,000,000 consolidated turnover.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not apply a British-Offshore-Financial-Centre style ESR regime, but economic substance is required to avoid denial of treaty benefits and participation exemption under the EU ATAD GAAR. Specifically, Luxembourg statutory substance rules for holding, IP, and financing companies require qualified staff, physical offices, and decision-making in Luxembourg. For the IP box regime, the qualifying IP must be developed or improved in Luxembourg.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud penalties: fine up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years; civil interest at 0.6% per month; automatic reassessment of the entire tax shortfall.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil permits criminal prosecution of directors; travel bans may be imposed pending trial; civil restitution includes unpaid tax, interest, and a 10% penalty on the under-declared amount.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing reassessment; penalty of 10% of the additional tax due; daily delay interest at 0.6% per month; administrative fine up to EUR 50,000 for non-cooperation.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign defective returns may be jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall if they acted in bad faith; repeated violations can lead to disqualification from board positions.
Shell Company (Substance Deficiency)

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits and participation exemption; dividend WHT at 25% (rather than 15%) applied; permanent establishment risks; annual fines up to EUR 10,000 for substance legislation non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Civil liability for the entire tax deficiency if the corporate veil is pierced; travel bans imposed by the tax collector; directors may be held criminally for aiding the abuse.
AML Violations in Reverse Logistics

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines up to EUR 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years under the Luxembourg AML law of 12 November 2004 (as amended).

Director personal liability: Directors are individually subject to criminal sanctions for money laundering offenses; repeated violations may trigger a court-imposed ban from managing any regulated activity in the EU.
