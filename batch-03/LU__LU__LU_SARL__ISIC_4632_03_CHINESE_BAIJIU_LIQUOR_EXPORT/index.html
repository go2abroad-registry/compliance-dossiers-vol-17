Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum subscribed and fully paid-up share capital is EUR 12,000 (Article 710-5). Cash contributions must be deposited, before notarization, into a blocked escrow account (compte de blocage) opened in the name of the company in formation with a Luxembourg credit institution; the bank issues an attestation de blocage released only upon presentation of the notarial deed. In-kind contributions require an independent report by an approved statutory auditor (réviseur d'entreprises agréé).

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification by the Corps grand-ducal d'incendie et de secours (CGDIS), structural load inspection certificates for the warehousing and racking system issued by an independent engineering office, and an occupational workplace clearance from the Inspection du travail et des mines (ITM) covering emergency preparedness, first aid, ventilation, and ergonomics under the Labor Code; any premises storing spirits above the flammable-liquid threshold must also satisfy the fire-resistance standards set out in the amended Grand-Ducal Regulation of 23 November 2011.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum subscribed and fully paid-up share capital is EUR 12,000 (Article 710-5). Cash contributions must be deposited, before notarization, into a blocked escrow account (compte de blocage) opened in the name of the company in formation with a Luxembourg credit institution; the bank issues an attestation de blocage released only upon presentation of the notarial deed. In-kind contributions require an independent report by an approved statutory auditor (réviseur d'entreprises agréé).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration de l'Enregistrement, des Domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) — Registration, domains and VAT – and the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) / RCS via the single portal Guichet.lu; direct tax registration with the Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD).

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Luxembourg SARL, the statutory path is: (1) execute the articles of association before a Luxembourg notary and fully subscribe/share up capital; (2) file the incorporation file with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS/Guichet.lu); the RCS registration automatically allocates the national business number (numéro d'identification) and records the entity for corporate tax purposes with the ACD; (3) file Form 1000 (demande d'immatriculation à la TVA) with the AEDT through the 'MyGuichet' e-desk together with the RCS extract, registered office proof, director and shareholder identification, and banking details; (4) obtain the Luxembourg VAT identification number (numéro de TVA LU...) before issuing invoices; (5) obtain an EU EORI number from customs for export declarations and, for alcohol, arrange excise duty suspension/authorised warehouse status or periodic excise reporting under the national alcohol excise system; (6) submit Intrastat/ECS declarations once goods are dispatched.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box system exists; SARL is classified by law as an opaque, taxable entity fully resident in Luxembourg. Relevant elections available: (a) VAT group election (assujetti unique) for financially integrated entities; (b) small-business VAT exemption election (franchise en base) where annual turnover is at or below EUR 35,000; (c) domestic fiscal unity (intégration fiscale) for 95%-plus group shareholdings for CIT/net wealth tax; (d) hybrid-mismatch structuring must be assessed under ATAD implementation, but no generic entity classification election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For non-established foreign suppliers, any taxable supply performed in Luxembourg triggers a Luxembourg VAT registration obligation without a de minimis turnover threshold. For a Luxembourg-established SARL, the domestic small-business exemption (no VAT charge) applies only when annual turnover does not exceed EUR 35,000 (excluding VAT), but a registered export wholesaler will typically be required to register because turnover exceeds that threshold or because supplies are not eligible for the small business exemption; the actual registration threshold is therefore effectively EUR 0 for export-oriented activity requiring VAT identification and zero-rating.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply of Chinese baijiu qualifies for the 0% VAT export exemption if the goods are physically dispatched or transported outside the European Union by the supplier or for the supplier's account, as provided by Article 146(1)(a) of the EU VAT Directive. The SARL must hold VAT registration in Luxembourg, issue an invoice referring to the exemption, and prove through transport/customs evidence that the goods left EU territory. Export to other EU Member States qualifies for intra-Community supply exemption instead of export zero-rating, subject to the buyer's valid EU VAT number, not the export 0% rule.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Déclaration d'Exportation) filed via the Luxembourg ECS/PLDA customs connection
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note demonstrating exit from the EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent proof of export proceeds receipt (SWIFT MT103, bank confirmation, or authenticated letter of credit payment settlement)
  • Commercial invoice with the 0% VAT reference and buyer/vendor VAT/EORI numbers

Luxembourg does not currently impose a real-time CT (continuous tax control) clearance system, invoice API clearance, or digital stamping for private B2B invoices. Commercial invoices must comply with the EU invoicing rules and contain the Luxembourg VAT number and the statement of zero-rating. E-invoicing is mandatory only in the B2G segment, where a PEPPOL BIS 3.0 e-invoice must be channelled through a certified PEPPOL access point. Export procedures require electronic filing of the Standard Export Declaration via the Customs ECS/PLDA portal, with no separate tax-chapter API requirement.

A VAT audit will reassess the export as a standard-rated Luxembourg supply if the 0% deduction is not substantially supported within the statutory records; the resulting VAT, late-payment interest and default surcharges are collected from the supplier. In cases of false or fraudulent zero-rating, the AEDT may apply penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded and the limitation period is extended in fraud cases; criminal tax fraud can also attract fines and imprisonment. Input VAT attributed to export sales is refundable only after documentary verification, and an export refund claim may be clawed back with penalty if the documents are not retained for at least 5 years or the exporter stages a sham export/round-tripping transaction.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible. Luxembourg applies the EU Treaty freedoms under Article 63 TFEU and maintains no general exchange-control approvals for current account or capital account operations. Profit remittance and dividend distributions are subject only to corporate-law formalities, domestic withholding tax at 15% where applicable, and applicable treaty or EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive relief. Foreign-currency accounts are freely permitted, and conversion may be executed through any CSSF-regulated credit institution without prior central-bank authorization. Practical constraints are limited to AML/KYC verification, CSSF conduct-of-business reporting, and BCL balance-of-payments statistics filings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirements and no mandatory conversion ratios in Luxembourg. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency or freely converted into EUR. For external trade and balance-of-payments statistics, banks submit underlying transaction-purpose coding to the BCL. An exporting SARL must retain shipping documents, customs declarations, invoices, and payment records sufficient to support such reporting instruments. No central-bank approval is required for retention, repatriation, or conversion of export earnings.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL); the Luxembourg Ministry of Finance exercises policy oversight, while the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) supervises credit institutions, e-money institutions, and payment services.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Medium for EU/EEA-authorized e-money institutions or payment service providers licensed by the CSSF or passporting under the EU Payment Services Directive 2 (PSD2). Rating: Low for offshore, non-EEA digital EMIs that do not maintain an EU establishment or passport. The SARL may use a regulated EMI for transaction collections and routine supplier disbursements, but an EMI cannot issue or confirm commercial letters of credit, and full trade-finance documentation requires a licensed credit institution. Legal constraints under Luxembourg PSD2 transposition include client-money safeguarding rules, CSSF due-diligence duties, and prohibition against deposit-taking; principal treasury balances should remain with a credit institution and, where possible, benefit from the Luxembourg deposit-guarantee scheme.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions should be remitted by SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer, or MT202 for the interbank leg, using full remitter details and an ECB/BCL balance-of-payments purpose code reflecting equity transactions or direct-investment equity capital. Shareholder loans should be coded as intercompany financing or loans related to direct investment, with the loan agreement referenced in the remittance information. Funds should be routed to the SARL’s euro account at BIL, BCEE, or Société Générale Luxembourg. Before transfer, the bank must receive the board resolution, subscription agreement, updated share-register extract, and complete KYC pack to prevent AML-related suspension. If the contribution is denominated in CNY or USD, conversion to EUR should be carried out through the bank’s FX dealing desk using a spot transaction, or a forward if the inflow timing is uncertain. The capital-escrow account must not be debited for operational expenses without corporate approvals and a clear treasury mandate.For euro payments to counterparties inside the SEPA/EEA zone, the exporter should use SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer. For non-SEPA euro payments or foreign-currency settlements to Chinese or global distributors, SWIFT MT103 messages must include the correct BOP purpose code for goods-wholesale transactions. For high-value, high-risk or Chinese-origin liquor cargo, the preferred clearing rail is a commercial letter of credit confirmed by a Luxembourg bank and governed by the UCP 600 rules. LC settlements require presentation of the commercial invoice, complete bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin, health or phytosanitary certificates, and Chinese export or baijiu-specific approvals. Open-account disbursements against MT103 may be used after established trade history, subject to invoice validation and supply-chain evidence. Legal constraints under Luxembourg PSD2 transposition include client-money safeguarding rules, CSSF due-diligence duties, and prohibition against deposit-taking; principal treasury balances should remain with a credit institution and, where possible, benefit from the Luxembourg deposit-guarantee scheme.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

17% corporate income tax (IRC); plus 7% solidarity surcharge on the IRC, resulting in a headline net rate of 18.19%; municipal business tax (IBP) of approximately 6.75% applies in Luxembourg City, yielding a combined effective headline rate of approximately 24.94%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% Luxembourg dividend withholding tax on outbound dividend distributions; reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive where the corporate parent holds at least 10% of the share capital and the holding period is at least 12 months. Under the China-Luxembourg DTA, a reduced rate generally of 5% may apply for a corporate beneficial owner holding at least 10% of the capital, provided substance and beneficial-ownership conditions are met.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 50,000,000 (consolidated annual group turnover threshold triggering the obligation to prepare and maintain a Master File and Local File; these files must be submitted to the ACD within 30-45 days upon request).

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not apply Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, the tax administration enforces a substance doctrine under anti-BEPS, ATAD, and treaty case law. Strong economic substance (own office, full-time local staff, local board documentation and management decisions in Luxembourg, adequate equity, and genuine operational risk) is mandatory to secure Luxembourg tax residence, treaty benefits, parent-subsidiary relief, and IP box advantages. A deficient substance profile raises a rebuttable presumption of abuse of law and may lead to recharacterisation of income, denial of treaty relief, and exit taxation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (fraude fiscale)

Statutory sanction: Recoverable tax plus late-payment interest of 0.6% per month; administrative increase of up to 100% of the tax evaded in cases of bad faith; criminal fine up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment of up to five years for responsible individuals. The competent Luxembourg prosecutor may additionally seize goods used in the smuggling or export of alcohol.

Director personal liability: Directors and managers are jointly and severally liable for the company's tax debts and penalties where they have personally directed fraudulent arrangements; courts may pierce the corporate veil, order civil restitution of the evaded amounts, issue travel bans, and impose custodial sentences of up to 5 years.
Shell / Letterbox Company Misuse

Statutory sanction: Loss of treaty and EU directive benefits; recharacterisation as hidden dividend distribution and assessment of 15% dividend withholding tax; additional penalties under abus de droit (abuse of law) equal to a maximum of 30% of the tax evaded; the entity may be deemed a tax avoidance vehicle with immediate tax recovery from the parent in the form of deemed exit gains.

Director personal liability: Directors risk personal liability if they signed management statements certifying substance that does not exist; civil restitution may cover the entire tax not withheld; travel restrictions and disqualification of directors may be imposed; in cases of international money laundering or sham activity, imprisonment up to 5 years may follow.
AML / Anti-Money Laundering Breach

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties of up to EUR 1,000,000 or 10% of the annual turnover for administrative violation of the AML law; criminal penalties for laundering offences (up to 5 years imprisonment and/or fines up to EUR 500,000); revocation of the alcohol wholesale licence and prohibition from holding certain commercial functions.

Director personal liability: Directors and AML compliance officers are personally liable for criminal fines in their supervisory capacity; civil liability for restitution of laundered funds and legal costs may attach directly to their personal assets; the power to impose travel bans and temporary serving of commercial prohibition orders further exposes them to administrative revocation of passport rights where a serious laundering scheme is proven.
