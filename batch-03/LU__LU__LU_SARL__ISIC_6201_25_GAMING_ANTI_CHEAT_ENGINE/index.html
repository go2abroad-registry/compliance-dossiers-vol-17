Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum share capital for an S.A.R.L. is EUR 12,000, fully subscribed at incorporation. Each cash quota must be paid up to at least 50% at formation, with the balance payable within five years; in-kind contributions must be fully paid up and described in the notarial deed. For this Stage 2 workflow the entire EUR 12,000 is deposited on a blocked escrow account (compte bloqué) in the name of 'LU SARL — société en formation'; the bank's blocked-funds certificate (attestation de blocage) is delivered to the notary before execution, and the funds are released to the Company upon presentation of the RCS registration certificate.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety certification by the Corps grand-ducal d'incendie et de secours (CGDIS) through the commune's building-occupancy procedure for the registered office, including alarm systems, extinguishers, and evacuation plans; structural load inspection of the floor areas supporting server racks; occupational-safety statement filed with the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) covering ergonomic workstations, ventilation, lighting, and emergency procedures; issuance of the municipal building-occupancy certificate (certificat de conformité) confirming the premises meet fire, safety, and physical-workplace requirements for the software-development operations.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AED) for VAT, registration duties and stamp taxes; Administration des douanes et accises for customs and excise matters. Principal digital portals: MyGuichet.lu (https://www.guichet.lu) for administrative filings, the Luxembourg Business Registers platform (https://www.lbr.lu) for RCS and business number issuance, and the ACD/AED e-filing services at impotsdirects.public.lu and aed.public.lu.

E-government filing portal

For a Luxembourg SARL, the 13-digit national business identification number (matricule / numéro d'immatriculation) issued upon commercial registration is the statutory tax identifier and is obtained as follows: (1) execute the articles of association (statuts) before a Luxembourg notary, with a minimum share capital of EUR 12,500 of which at least 25% (EUR 3,125) must be paid in; foreign corporate shareholders are permitted without nationality restrictions; (2) file the incorporation dossier electronically with the RCS through the notary within one month of signature; (3) obtain the RCS registration and the matricule, which is automatically communicated to the ACD and AED; (4) use the matricule for all corporate income tax, municipal business tax and net wealth tax declarations filed with the ACD via MyGuichet.lu; (5) submit the VAT registration application to the AED obtaining a Luxembourg VAT identification number in the format LU followed by 8 digits; (6) if employing staff, register as an employer with the Centre commun de la sécurité sociale (CCSS). A non-EU taxable person not established in Luxembourg must appoint a fiscal representative approved by the AED, whereas EU taxable persons may register directly without a local representative.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the SARL is a resident corporate taxpayer subject to Luxembourg corporate income tax (impôt sur le revenu des collectivités), municipal business tax (impôt commercial communal) and net wealth tax (impôt sur la fortune). Luxembourg City aggregate statutory charge approximates 22.8% (15% CIT plus 7% solidarity surcharge plus 6.75% municipal business tax before deductibility adjustments). Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income with relief through the participation exemption, double tax treaties and unilateral measures; the passive SPF holding regime is unavailable to an active ISIC 6201 software operating entity, and no territorial pass-through regime applies to SARLs.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Luxembourg; the SARL is per se an entity-level taxpayer and hybrid mismatch outcomes are determined non-electively under ATAD transposition. Available elective mechanisms include: (i) VAT fiscal unity (assujetti unique) under Article 60 of the Luxembourg VAT Law; (ii) optional joint taxation of municipal business tax for Luxembourg group companies with at least 95% direct or indirect ownership; (iii) the IP box election granting an 80% exemption on net qualifying IP income for grandfathered qualifying rights; (iv) the small business VAT exemption election under Article 130 limited to an annual turnover ceiling of EUR 35,000; (v) OSS and IOSS registrations for distance supplies of electronically supplied services and low-value imported goods; (vi) binding tax ruling and advance pricing agreement requests filed with the ACD covering transfer pricing, permanent establishment exposure and hybrid mismatch characterization; and (vii) DAC6 mandatory disclosure of reportable cross-border arrangements without any elective opt-out.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility with zero exchange controls. Luxembourg applies no FX restrictions on either current-account items (royalties, licence fees, outsourcing) or capital-account items (share subscriptions, shareholder loans, dividends) under Article 63 TFEU. Profits may be remitted freely in EUR, USD or any permitted currency without BCL/CSSF authorisation; the only fiscal gate is Luxembourg dividend withholding tax (15% standard; 0% for qualifying corporate shareholders under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive and the domestic participation-exemption regime; treaty-reduced rates otherwise). Bank-level sanctions and embargo screening are the sole operative constraints.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. As a euro-area state, Luxembourg imposes no export-proceeds surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio and no repatriation deadline. The SARL may lawfully retain software-licensing receipts in USD, EUR or any convertible currency in Luxembourg or offshore. Banking practice requires only an economic-purpose narrative for BCL balance-of-payments statistics; no regulator mandates conversion or a holding period.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) is the national central bank and a Eurosystem member. Prudential supervision of banks, payment institutions and e-money institutions is exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF), with significant Luxembourg credit institutions under the ECB Single Supervisory Mechanism. No exchange-control committee or foreign-investment approval authority exists in Luxembourg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-High as a settlement channel, Low-Medium as a sole treasury hub. Luxembourg recognizes CSSF-licensed Payment Institutions and E-Money Institutions, and EEA-passported EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Revolut Business) may serve the SARL without a domestic licence; however, such institutions cannot lend, issue credit or hold funds beyond PSD2 limits on commercial accounts. Recommended deployment: Stripe (acting through an Irish-authorised Payment Institution under EEA passporting) as the card-acquiring collection rail; a global EMI IBAN as a low-cost FX sweep sub-account; and a domestic Tier-1 bank account as the anchor account that holds statutory capital, receives dividends and executes core liquidity clearing.
