Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

The minimum share capital of an SARL is EUR 12,000, fully subscribed and fully paid up in cash at incorporation; the paid-up capital must be deposited in a blocked bank account with a Luxembourg credit institution and released only after RCS registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification by the Grand-Ducal Fire and Rescue Corps and municipal fire-prevention inspection; structural load inspection certificates for tanks, keg racks, and cold-chain storage areas; occupational health and workplace clearance by the Labour and Mines Inspectorate (Inspection du Travail et des Mines) covering forklift operations, pressurized keg handling, and low-temperature work.

Paid-in capital rule: The minimum share capital of an SARL is EUR 12,000, fully subscribed and fully paid up in cash at incorporation; the paid-up capital must be deposited in a blocked bank account with a Luxembourg credit institution and released only after RCS registration.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct and corporate income taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT, registration duties, and excise on beer; centralized business-tax filing portal at MyGuichet.lu; company legal-identity register: Registre de Commerce et des Societes (Luxembourg Business Registers, RCS).

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned SARL in craft-beer distribution: (1) notarized incorporation, deposit of minimum capital with a Luxembourg bank, and filing of the articles with the RCS to obtain the national business registration number (numéro d'immatriculation / 'matricule'), which is the base legal identifier; (2) apply via MyGuichet.lu to the AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT number (LU + 8 digits) using the matricule and the standard VAT registration form; the VAT ID must be obtained before making any Luxembourg taxable supply, before intra-EU acquisitions, and before issuing zero-rated export invoices; (3) a non-EU-established entity must appoint a Luxembourg VAT fiscal representative (représentant fiscal) approved by the AEDT; an EU-established foreign parent generally files directly but may opt for a representative; (4) complete the beneficial-ownership (RBE) register filing and appoint local governance (at least one manager) before the tax ID is activated; the same matricule is used for corporate income tax, municipal business tax, and net wealth tax.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: a Luxembourg SARL is a civil-law corporate entity fully taxable at entity level as a Luxembourg tax resident on worldwide income, plus municipal business tax and net wealth tax; no pass-through or check-the-box transparency is recognized for income tax purposes; a pure distribution SARL without economic substance, premises, staff, and decision-making functions is at risk of being recharacterized as a permanent establishment of the foreign parent or as an artificial arrangement under GAAR/ATAD. — For a foreign-owned SARL in craft-beer distribution: (1) notarized incorporation, deposit of minimum capital with a Luxembourg bank, and filing of the articles with the RCS to obtain the national business registration number (numéro d'immatriculation / 'matricule'), which is the base legal identifier; (2) apply via MyGuichet.lu to the AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT number (LU + 8 digits) using the matricule and the standard VAT registration form; the VAT ID must be obtained before making any Luxembourg taxable supply, before intra-EU acquisitions, and before issuing zero-rated export invoices; (3) a non-EU-established entity must appoint a Luxembourg VAT fiscal representative (représentant fiscal) approved by the AEDT; an EU-established foreign parent generally files directly but may opt for a representative; (4) complete the beneficial-ownership (RBE) register filing and appoint local governance (at least one manager) before the tax ID is activated; the same matricule is used for corporate income tax, municipal business tax, and net wealth tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: a Luxembourg SARL is a civil-law corporate entity fully taxable at entity level as a Luxembourg tax resident on worldwide income, plus municipal business tax and net wealth tax; no pass-through or check-the-box transparency is recognized for income tax purposes; a pure distribution SARL without economic substance, premises, staff, and decision-making functions is at risk of being recharacterized as a permanent establishment of the foreign parent or as an artificial arrangement under GAAR/ATAD.

Available election forms

Luxembourg provides no US-style check-the-box election; entity classification is fixed by civil law. Available statutory elections affecting the SARL include: (i) corporate income-tax group election (régime d'intégration fiscale) for a Luxembourg parent holding at least 95% of sub-subsidiaries, elected for a 5-year term; (ii) participation exemption regime: election/qualification to exempt 100% of qualifying dividend income and capital gains (10% participation held for at least 12 months and subject to substance/compliance conditions); (iii) VAT group election (assujetti unique) pooling VAT registration of related Luxembourg entities; (iv) depreciation and asset revaluation elections under the Luxembourg income tax law (LIR); (v) hybrid-mismatch disclosure and election statements under ATAD to allocate deduction/inclusion outcomes; (vi) no transparency election is available for a SARL, which is always opaque.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 30,000 annual taxable turnover for Luxembourg-established small businesses (small-business exemption under Art. 57 of the VAT law; indexation adjusted to approximately EUR 35,000 from 1 January 2023, current figure to be verified); non-established foreign suppliers have zero registration threshold and must register for any Luxembourg taxable supply; a wholesaler dealing exclusively in zero-rated export supplies must still VAT-register to claim input VAT refunds; intra-EU distance-sale threshold is EUR 10,000 EU-wide, administered through the EU One-Stop-Shop.

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control (CTC), digital stamping, or mandatory B2B e-invoicing exists in Luxembourg for wholesale or export operations as of the current regime; mandatory structured e-invoicing (Peppol BIS 3.0) applies only for B2G supplies to Luxembourg public contracting authorities; all VAT returns, European Sales Lists (recapitulative statements), and Intrastat declarations are filed electronically via MyGuichet.lu; export transactions are controlled through customs AES/SAD records rather than VAT invoice clearance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility. Luxembourg applies Articles 63-66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU), guaranteeing unrestricted movement of capital between EU member states and third countries; no exchange control authorization is required for inbound equity injections, dividend distributions, or trade settlements. Profit remittance is impeded only by the statutory 15% dividend withholding tax, which is reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive (2011/96/EU) and to 5% under the Luxembourg tax treaties with Germany and the United States where the 10% shareholding threshold is met. Eurozone membership eliminates convertibility and transfer risk for euro-denominated treasury positions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg imposes no mandatory surrender, repatriation timeline, or conversion ratio on export proceeds. Export receipts may be retained in any currency in any jurisdiction; the only obligations are the general anti-money-laundering framework (Law of 12 November 2004, as amended) and BCL statistical balance-of-payments reporting for cross-border direct investment flows exceeding EUR 12,500.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating within the Eurosystem alongside the European Central Bank (ECB). Prudential regulation of credit institutions and payment services is jointly discharged by the BCL and the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Luxembourg provides no standalone licensing gateway for offshore digital banks or non-EEA EMI brands; payment institutions must be authorized by the CSSF under the Law of 10 November 2009 on payment services (PSD2 transposition) to passport Luxembourg payment accounts. Offshore EMIs (UK, US, Singapore or Cyprus-licensed entities) cannot obtain a native LU IBAN, fall outside the CSSF resolution framework, and are rejected as a primary treasury rail for a cold-chain wholesaler. Their acceptable role is limited to ancillary collections, never for escrowed capital or tax-critical disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The parent company or subscriber executes an SWIFT MT103 (or SEPA SCT using an ISO 20022 pacs.008 message) to the blocked capital escrow account (compte de séquestre) held at the chosen Luxembourg credit institution in the name of the SARL en formation. The wire must carry SWIFT purpose code 'EQUI' (Equity-linked security/capital contribution) and reference 'Share capital contribution under Article 710-1 et seq. of the amended Law of 10 August 1915'. The notary certifies full subscription and payment of the minimum EUR 12,000 capital before the deed is filed with the Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS). The bank blocks the funds until RCS registration; thereafter the escrow is converted into the operational account. The entity must report the inbound direct investment to the BCL for balance-of-payments statistics whenever the receipt exceeds EUR 12,500. Charge 'SHA' is standard; if the full amount must land in escrow without deductions, charge 'OUR' must be selected explicitly.Primary rail is SWIFT MT103 for both EU and overseas brewery settlements, using charge 'SHA' and trade-related purpose codes ('GDDS' for goods or 'TRDP'). Secondary rail is a confirmed irrevocable documentary letter of credit (UCP 600, issued via SWIFT MT700) for keg shipments exceeding EUR 50,000, with bills of lading, commercial invoices, and ALVA sanitary certificates as presenting documents. For recurring EU supplier collections, the company deploys SEPA Direct Debit Core (scheme creditor identifier registered in Luxembourg) against the target rail 'Local_Direct_Debit'.
