Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

SARL minimum share capital is EUR 12,000, fully subscribed and paid in cash or in kind. Cash capital must be deposited in a blocked escrow account at a Luxembourg credit institution until the RCS registration certificate is issued; the block is lifted automatically upon registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification is required from the Service d'Incendie et de Secours (CGDIS) for the warehouse and logistics premises, including a fire alarm system, escape routes, extinguishers, and emergency lighting. Structural load inspection is required under the Ministry of Labour (ITM) building code to verify the floor load capacity for stacked boxes and pallets. A workplace clearance from ITM confirms compliance with minimum occupational safety levels for lighting, ventilation, ergonomics, and safe handling of material loads.

Paid-in capital rule: SARL minimum share capital is EUR 12,000, fully subscribed and paid in cash or in kind. Cash capital must be deposited in a blocked escrow account at a Luxembourg credit institution until the RCS registration certificate is issued; the block is lifted automatically upon registration.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direct taxes: Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD), 45 Boulevard Roosevelt, L-2420 Luxembourg; e-filing via MyGuichet.lu and the ACD e-space. Indirect taxes: Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT), VAT department; e-filing via MyGuichet.lu. Customs: Administration des Douanes et Accises via the eCDF electronic customs declaration filing system. Commercial register: Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR), RCS Luxembourg.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: execute the notarized deed of incorporation of the SARL and file the registration with the Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS) through MyGuichet.lu. Step 2: the RCS registration automatically issues the 13-digit national identification number (matricule), which is the statutory corporate TIN used by both the ACD and the AEDT. Step 3: file VAT registration form Formulaire 564 with the AEDT via MyGuichet.lu; the AEDT issues the Luxembourg VAT identification number in the format LU + 8 digits. Step 4: register with the Administration des Douanes et Accises to obtain the customs EORI number (format LU + matricule) for export declarations on eCDF. End-to-end statutory procurement typically completes within 2-4 weeks from RCS filing; the SARL may invoice under the matricule pending VAT-number issuance, but any zero-rated invoice should carry the VAT number once attributed.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope. A Luxembourg SARL is a resident, opaque legal entity taxed at entity level on worldwide business income, with treaty relief and the participation exemption for qualifying dividends and capital gains; no pass-through status exists. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope. A Luxembourg SARL is a resident, opaque legal entity taxed at entity level on worldwide business income, with treaty relief and the participation exemption for qualifying dividends and capital gains; no pass-through status exists. Combined headline CIT in Luxembourg City: 17% corporate income tax plus 7% employment-fund surcharge (effective 18.19%) plus 6.75% municipal business tax = 24.94% (municipal rates vary up to about 10.50%); net wealth tax of 0.5% applies to net assets exceeding EUR 500,000, subject to the minimum net-wealth-tax rules. Tax_Exempt_Zone: not applicable; the Luxembourg Freeport grants only customs-duty and VAT suspension within a bonded warehouse, not corporate income-tax exemption.

Available election forms

Luxembourg has no US-style check-the-box regime and no pass-through election for a SARL. Elective mechanisms available: (i) VAT grouping under Article 60 of the VAT Law, allowing the SARL to form a single taxable person with closely connected Luxembourg-established entities; (ii) fiscal unity (integration fiscale) under Article 164bis LIR for CIT and municipal business tax, requiring an uninterrupted 95% direct or indirect holding and prior ACD approval; (iii) election to waive the Article 57 small-business exemption - mandatory where the SARL claims input-VAT recovery or relies on export zero-rating; (iv) application of the participation exemption (effectively elective, subject to the subject-to-tax and minimum-holding conditions); (v) ATAD 1/2 hybrid-mismatch rules constrain any cross-border hybrid structuring, and specific factual analysis is required before relying on hybrid entity or hybrid instrument treatment.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full eurozone capital and current account convertibility. Luxembourg applies Articles 63-66 TFEU, the OECD Capital Movements Liberalisation Code, and the EU Free Movement of Capital framework; there are no licensing requirements or conversion limits for inward or outward capital transfers. Profit remittance by dividend distribution is unrestricted legally but requires adequate distributable reserves, board approval by the shareholders' meeting, and compliance with the SARL's minimum capital maintenance requirement. Dividends paid to foreign shareholders generally incur Luxembourg 15% withholding tax, reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive where the shareholder holds at least 10% of the capital continuously for 12 months, or reduced under an applicable double tax treaty, with the standard treaty rate typically 5% or 0% depending on shareholding. There are no documentary approval hurdles for conversion of EUR to foreign currency, but banks require completion of ECB BOP statistical declarations for transactions above thresholds and adherence to CSSF anti-money-laundering transaction reporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Luxembourg imposes no export proceeds surrender statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio. Exporters may retain proceeds in any freely convertible currency, maintain foreign-currency accounts without central bank authorization, and use receipts for operational or treasury purposes. The only compliance duties are accurate BOP/BNLI statistical reporting by the banking counterparty and anti-money-laundering record-keeping. Cash movements of EUR 10,000 or more crossing the EU external border must comply with EU Regulation (EU) 2018/1672 and be declared to customs.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL); Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) as banking and AML/CFT supervisor; European Central Bank (ECB) for eurozone monetary policy coordination.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High viability is present for EU/EEA electronic money institutions and payment institutions holding a PSD2 authorization that is passported into Luxembourg or licensed by the CSSF. These EMIs can provide SEPA-core accounts, virtual IBANs, commercial cards, API-based treasury controls and faster payments, and are suitable for low-value operational collections, Stripe Billing settlements and Authorize.Net card clearing. Viability is reduced for offshore non-EEA digital EMIs: such institutions generally lack a Luxembourg passport, cannot offer a genuine LU IBAN, are unsuitable for equity capital injections, shareholder loans, corporate tax payments, or bank-intermediated documentary trade, and may create sanctions and AML exposure. If an EMI is used, it must demonstrate client-money safeguarding with a tier-1 credit institution, provide full KYB on the SARL and its UBOs, offer EU payment accounts and SEPA rails, and provide audit access to payment ledger data. A legal opinion from a Luxembourg attorney should be obtained before reliance on any non-bank EMI for mandatory regulatory payments.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Multi-jurisdictional ownership and opaque trust/offshore holding structure

Root cause: Multi-jurisdictional ownership and opaque trust/offshore holding structure

Defense evidence: Luxembourg RCS excerpt, articles of association, full-chain beneficial ownership chart, Luxembourg UBO registry (RBE) filing, notarized certificates for all indirect shareholders, source-of-wealth records for each direct shareholder, consolidated group chart, tax residence certificates, and a written commercial substance explanation for the subscription-box business model.
High recurring cross-border card-not-present revenue from B2C e-commerce

Root cause: High recurring cross-border card-not-present revenue from B2C e-commerce

Defense evidence: PCI-DSS attestation of compliance, merchant acquirer agreements, processed sales ledger, chargeback and refund ratio reports, anti-fraud transaction monitoring policy, CNPD data-protection registration, customer-ticket escalation procedures, and the full payment services agreement with Stripe Billing or Authorize.Net proving settlement directly into the LU SARL bank account.
Sanctions, embargo exposure or high-risk end-customer destinations

Root cause: Sanctions, embargo exposure or high-risk end-customer destinations

Defense evidence: EU/OFAC/UN sanctions screening policy, IP and device geolocation blocking, denied-party list software output, customer-country export classification by HS code, board resolution prohibiting sales to sanctioned jurisdictions, and forward-looking compliance certification with quarterly independent sanctions testing.
Shareholder loan or foreign-currency capital injection

Root cause: Shareholder loan or foreign-currency capital injection

Defense evidence: Notarized loan agreement or share subscription deed, board minutes approving terms, ECB BOP declaration data, source-of-funds evidence from an EU licensed bank, financial projections demonstrating treasury need, and confirmation that the transaction is not a disguised money laundering structure.
Beneficial owner designated as a politically exposed person (PEP)

Root cause: Beneficial owner designated as a politically exposed person (PEP)

Defense evidence: Enhanced due diligence profile, verified source of wealth and source of funds documentation, current professional and business background, previous bank reference, independent external audit letter, legal counsel opinion on the legitimacy of the capital structure, and a commitment to provide risk updates to the Luxembourg AML compliance officer at least semi-annually.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity contributions and shareholder loans should be routed by SEPA Credit Transfer for euro transfers from EEA counterparties, or by SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA remittances. The receiving Luxembourg bank must see the mandated purpose code: 'Direct investment - Equity capital' for share subscription and 'Direct investment - Loans' or 'Other investment - Loans' for shareholder debt. The beneficiary should be the exact legal name 'LU_SARL' with the Luxembourg IBAN. Verification documentation comprises the notarized share subscription deed, updated RCS excerpt, board resolution, bank account confirmation, funding bank statement, UBO declarations, and a capital-payment certificate prepared by a Luxembourg notary or auditor. All receipts above EUR 12,500 are captured in the BCL balance-of-payments and direct investment statistics and may trigger enhanced monitoring where the remitter is located in a non-transparent jurisdiction.Outbound settlements for subscription-box merchandise should use: (i) SEPA Credit Transfer for euro payments within SEPA zone; (ii) SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA suppliers; (iii) documentary letters of credit issued under UCP 600 for high-value or first-transaction imports; (iv) documentary collection for established low-risk trade partners; and (v) supply-chain finance instruments if the bank has granted trade credit limits. All disbursements must reconcile to supplier invoices, HS tariff codes, Incoterms, and the approved treasury cash-flow forecast. The bank's BOP reporting system must code payments as 'International Trade in Goods' or 'Trade Settlement' with corresponding invoice and bill-of-lading references. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% (Luxembourg City: 17% corporate income tax + 6.75% municipal business tax + 7%-of-CIT employment fund contribution); aggregate rate varies with the commune of the SARL's establishment.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% under LIR Art. 151; reduced to 0% where the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive applies (minimum 10% direct holding for 12 months) or where a DTA reduces the rate for a qualifying corporate shareholder.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where the SARL's annual net turnover exceeds EUR 5 million and the group's consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 50 million; Master File mandatory where group consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 750 million; documentation must be contemporaneous and filed with the annual Form 540 annex.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not apply a UAE-style ESR point-scoring regime, but substantive human and physical presence is legally required to access treaty benefits, the Parent-Subsidiary Directive, finance branch exemptions, and favourable circulars. The ACD invokes the GAAR (LIR Art. 6 §1), ATAD/ATAD-II provisions (LIR Arts. 56–56bis), and the OECD MLI Principal Purpose Test to deny benefits to letterbox companies lacking premises, staff, local decision-making, and operative bank accounts.
