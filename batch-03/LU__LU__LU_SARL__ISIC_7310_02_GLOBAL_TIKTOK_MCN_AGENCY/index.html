Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés – RCS), operated through the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) electronic filing platform.

Governing statute

Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended, particularly Articles 1, 9, 100-1 to 100-25, and 710-1 to 710-24 concerning the Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarial deed of incorporation (acte constitutif) together with the single incorporation dossier (dossier unique) filed electronically via the LBR/RCS e-filing portal and published in the Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations (RESA).

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum issued and fully paid-up share capital of EUR 12,000 for a SARL; cash contributions must be deposited into a Luxembourg bank blocked account and certified by a certificate of deposit or blocked-funds attestation before registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification (certificat de sécurité incendie) may be required by the commune for premises open to staff or the public; structural load inspection and building compliance are confirmed by the municipal construction department; occupational health clearance is coordinated by the Service national de la Santé au travail (State Occupational Health Service) when employees are engaged.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD) for direct taxes and Administration de l’Enregistrement, des Domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; centralized digital portal is MyGuichet.lu / guichet.lu and the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) for company registration

E-government filing portal

Foreign entity establishes an SARL via the Luxembourg Business Registers to obtain the RCS matricule (business registration number); then file the incorporation dossier electronically with the AEDT for VAT registration and with the ACD for income tax identification, receiving a Luxembourg TIN / VAT identification number in the format LU00000000

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign entity establishes an SARL via the Luxembourg Business Registers to obtain the RCS matricule (business registration number); then file the incorporation dossier electronically with the AEDT for VAT registration and with the ACD for income tax identification, receiving a Luxembourg TIN / VAT identification number in the format LU00000000

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box exists in Luxembourg; a resident SARL is by default a fully taxable entity. Available elections include forming an intra-group VAT unity (assujetti unique) with related Luxembourg entities, and applying the EU Dioxide/hybrid mismatch rules which may require affirmative filing of information where an optional consolidation or equalization treatment is used; no standalone entity classification election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for Luxembourg-established small enterprises; non-resident suppliers have no threshold and must register for Luxembourg VAT where they make taxable supplies of digital services, distance sales or other activities deemed to be situated in Luxembourg

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported outside the European Union provided the exporter obtains proof of dispatch or transport within 90 days and supplies are made to an EU business customer or an equivalent consignee; services to non-EU business customers (including creator-agency, MCN and advertising services) are generally outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT under the place-of-supply rules, allowing input VAT recovery without applying Luxembourg VAT provided the customer’s non-EU business status is evidenced.

  • Customs Export Declaration (via Luxembourg Customs / EU Single Administrative Document)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR or other transport evidence with signature and date
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/payment confirmation evidencing receipt of consideration

Luxembourg does not impose a real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or continuous transaction controls (CTC) regime for private-sector B2B e-invoicing. B2B e-invoicing is permitted using authenticated formats (e.g. Peppol BIS) and is mandatory for suppliers to Luxembourg public-sector buyers under certain thresholds; all e-invoices must be stored in original format for audit purposes.

Luxembourg tax and customs authorities may retroactively disallow zero-rating and assess Luxembourg VAT plus statutory interest; intentional fraudulent export claims subject to tax penalties (amende fiscale) and criminal sanctions, including potential imprisonment and fines, with joint liability of directors for concealment; mitigation requires full retention of contracts, invoices, customs declarations, transport evidence and incoming payment records.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg applies full capital and current account convertibility pursuant to EU Treaty provisions on free movement of capital and payments (Articles 63-65 TFEU). No exchange controls, no prior authorisation for foreign currency transactions, and no restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distribution, or liquidation proceeds for resident or non-resident shareholders. The euro is fully convertible; non-euro transactions are subject to standard market pricing and EU anti-money-laundering (AML) transparency obligations. Profit remittance hurdles are limited to corporate tax compliance, withholding tax obligations, and documentary proof of economic substance

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg imposes no statutory timeline for export proceeds surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no obligation to repatriate export receipts to a local bank account. Export revenues may be held in foreign currency accounts abroad, subject to general tax transparency and transfer pricing requirements. All settlements must comply with the EU Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA) and international sanctions regimes

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (Central Bank of Luxembourg, BCL) in coordination with the Luxembourg Ministry of Finance and the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for foreign exchange and payments oversight

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-High subject to Luxembourg E-Money Institution (EMI) licensing under the CSSF or EU passporting from another Member State. Cross-border digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Payoneer) may provide ancillary settlement rails but are not recommended as sole treasury counterparties due to restricted KYC acceptance for creator payouts, lower transaction limits, and higher closure risk for high-volume MCN agency flows. A licensed EMI with SEPA and SWIFT capabilities, operating under a valid MiFID/EMD passport, can be used for small-value TikTok creator settlements, but principal operating liquidity should reside with a Tier-1 Luxembourg credit institution to ensure clearing reliability, negative interest rate exemptions, and correspondent banking access

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injection to the Luxembourg SARL must be executed via SWIFT MT103 with clear economic purpose code. Recommended routing: non-resident shareholder's bank sends EUR via TARGET2 or SEPA Instant to the SARL's euro account at a Tier-1 Luxembourg bank. For foreign-currency injections, use SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'TREATY' (EU capital movement free-of-controls) and ensure the beneficiary reference includes 'Capital Subscription SARL' plus the corporate registration number (RCS Luxembourg). For capital increases, file a notarised deed with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register and register the UBO/subscription in the RBE. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% aggregate corporate income tax rate for Luxembourg City (composition: 17% CIT + 7% solidarity surcharge on CIT + 6.75% municipal business tax); municipal business tax rate varies by commune (6.75%–10.5%)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory dividend withholding tax, increased by the 7% solidarity surcharge to an effective 16.05% for non-exempt distributions; 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive, the Luxembourg participation exemption, or applicable DTAs (e.g., 5% treaty rate for a 10% corporate shareholder, 15% otherwise).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing deadline is 31 December of the calendar year following the fiscal year-end (e.g., FY ending 31 December 2025: return due 31 December 2026); extensions may be granted on written request, and administrative penalties apply to late filers.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Full Master File and Local File must be prepared and filed where the Luxembourg entity belongs to a group with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 50,000,000; Country-by-Country Report (Form 800) is mandatory where consolidated group revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750,000,000. Documentation must be delivered to the ACD upon request (typically within 30 days); if not, penalties up to EUR 25,000 and TP re-adjustments apply.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg imposes no filing-based Economic Substance Regulations (BVI/Cayman model); however, substance is enforced through (i) the abuse-of-law doctrine (General Tax Law Article 6), (ii) EU ATAD / GAAR and the OECD MLI Principal Purpose Test, and (iii) transfer pricing substance requirements. An MCN / Agency SARL must demonstrate real activity in Luxembourg (own offices, qualified staff performing creator-representation and management functions, board and manager decisions taken in Luxembourg, adequate capital and operating expenditures) to access treaty benefits, the participation exemption, and the 0% EU dividend withholding regime. Substance deficiencies are recharacterized as artificial arrangements, triggering denial of treaty relief and immediate WHT re-assessments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent False Return

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to EUR 25,000 and imprisonment of eight days to five years (Article 179 of the General Tax Law); mandatory re-assessment plus 10% late-filing or late-payment surcharge and 0.6% monthly interest; loss of the 17-year loss carryforward on abuse.

Director personal liability: The gérant / managing director who signs a false return is personally and jointly liable for the evaded tax, interest and penalties; Luxembourg courts may issue travel bans, seizure of personal assets, and director disqualification for the duration of the criminal proceedings.
Artificial Substance / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization as an abuse of law (Article 6 AO) with reassessment of the full corporate tax at the 24.94% aggregate rate; denial of treaty and EU-Directive relief; 15% WHT plus solidarity surcharge re-levied on distributions made during the relevant limitation window; fines up to EUR 25,000.

Director personal liability: Directors can be declared jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax and WHT under the de facto management doctrine; civil restitution of dividends paid out of artificially tax-exempt profits and personal fines for signing substance statements are imposed.
