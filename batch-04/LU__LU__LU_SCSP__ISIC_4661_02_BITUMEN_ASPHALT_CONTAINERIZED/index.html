Competent corporate registry

Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), Luxembourg, operated by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR); publication in the Recueil Électronique des Sociétés et Associations (RESA).

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended, particularly the provisions on the Special Limited Partnership (Société en Commandite Spéciale) introduced by the Law of 10 August 2016; Law of 19 December 2002 on the Luxembourg Business Registers and the company register; Grand-Ducal Regulation of 22 January 2010 on the RCS filing formalities.

Formation / constitutional filings

Partnership Agreement (Convention de Société en Commandite Spéciale) and the RCS extract of the formation deed (Extrait de l'acte constitutif), lodged electronically via the LBR portal.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 14 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The capital is freely determined in the partnership agreement and no escrow deposit is required for cash contributions to the SCSp.

Funding & unlock path: Not required for an SCSp engaged exclusively in trading and logistics with no employees and no physical premises. If a warehouse or tanking site is subsequently operated, the operator must obtain a fire safety conformity certificate from the fire department, a structural load inspection certificate from a licensed engineering office, and an occupational safety clearance from the Inspection du Travail et des Mines.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The capital is freely determined in the partnership agreement and no escrow deposit is required for cash contributions to the SCSp.

To adopt as the registered office the address evidenced by the landlord’s consent letter and instruct the managers to complete any change notification. 3. Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Ministry of the Economy (Ministère de l'Économie), General Directorate for Industry and Innovation; Administration de l'Environnement for environmental aspects

Category: No operational industrial permit is required for pure containerized wholesale trading of bitumen and asphalt products. A future installation for storage or drumming would require an environmental operating authorization under the Law of 21 June 1976 on classified establishments.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; unified filing portal: MyGuichet.lu (eTVA/impotsdirects.public.lu).

E-government filing portal

Register the SCSp with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés, RCS) through a notary or approved e-filing channel to obtain the 11-digit national business number (numéro de matricule). Separately apply to the AEDT via the MyGuichet/eTVA portal for the 15-character Luxembourg VAT identification number (format LU followed by 8 digits). Non-resident partners may execute powers of attorney; apostilled constitutional documents and proof of registered office in Luxembourg are required. No Luxembourg minimum capital is required for the SCSp.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — the SCSp is fiscally transparent for Luxembourg direct tax purposes (no corporate income tax, municipal business tax, or net wealth tax at entity level; each partner is taxed on its distributive share as it arises). For VAT, the SCSp acts as an independent taxable person in its own name when carrying out economic activity. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the SCSp is fiscally transparent for Luxembourg direct tax purposes (no corporate income tax, municipal business tax, or net wealth tax at entity level; each partner is taxed on its distributive share as it arises). For VAT, the SCSp acts as an independent taxable person in its own name when carrying out economic activity. No territorial tax-exempt zone applies.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Luxembourg law; SCSp pass-through treatment is mandatory for direct tax purposes. Where a partner jurisdiction classifies the SCSp differently, anti-hybrid rules under ATAD 1 and ATAD 2 (Luxembourg law of 27 December 2021) and DAC6 disclosure obligations may apply; taxpayers may only align entity classification with foreign treatment to the extent permitted by Luxembourg VAT law and EU anti-hybrid carve-outs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold for non-resident suppliers or for import/export transactions; the EU-wide EUR 10,000 distance-selling threshold applies to cross-border B2C/remote supplies; resident small businesses may qualify for the annual EUR 35,000 turnover exemption from VAT registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of bitumen/asphalt goods physically dispatched or transported by the seller or for the seller's account from Luxembourg to destinations outside the EU are zero-rated under Article 40 of the Luxembourg VAT law (implementing Article 146 of the VAT Directive), provided the goods leave EU customs territory intact within 90 days of supply and the exporter retains conclusive documentary proof of export. Intra-EU B2B supplies remain taxable at destination via reverse charge or are intra-EU exempt only with the customer's valid EU VAT number.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document SAD/DAU) submitted via the PLDA customs portal
  • Bill of Lading for containerized sea freight (or CMR/airway bill as applicable)
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or equivalent bank FX confirmation evidencing repatriation of export sale proceeds

Mandatory Peppol-BIS B2B e-invoicing is being phased in from 1 January 2026 for large taxpayers and 1 January 2027 for all taxpayers; e-invoices must be transmitted via certified Peppol access points and archived for 10 years; VAT returns are filed electronically through the eTVA/MyGuichet portal. Luxembourg currently applies no real-time fiscal clearance, government API pre-clearance, or digital stamping regime.

The AEDT and the Luxembourg Customs and Excise Administration may audit export evidence within the standard VAT assessment period of three years, extended to five years for irregularities or intentional evasion. Missing, incomplete, or false proof of export triggers reassessment of output VAT at 17%, monthly late interest of 0.6%, administrative fines of up to EUR 15,000 per enforceable claim, clawback of previously credited input VAT on related purchases, and criminal sanctions for willful VAT fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible, and Luxembourg applies no exchange-control statute. Current-account and capital-account operations are liberalised under Articles 63 to 66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, subject only to EU and UN sanctions screening by correspondent banks. The SCSp is a transparent special limited partnership; profit distribution to partners is governed by the limited partnership agreement (LPA) and Luxembourg insolvency rules, not by currency restrictions. No authorisation, registration, or declaration is required for remittance of profits, return of capital, or repayment of partner loans.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender rule, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline applies to export proceeds in Luxembourg. Foreign-currency receipts from wholesale bitumen sales (commonly USD or EUR) may be retained in an onshore foreign-currency account, converted at market rates, or applied directly to offshore trade payables at the entity's discretion. The only operative controls are the EU Wire Transfer Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 information requirements and EU/OFAC sanctions checks applied by clearing banks.

Central bank supervision: Banque Centrale du Luxembourg (BCL); the national central bank of Luxembourg and constituent of the Eurosystem. Foreign-exchange, payment-system, and prudential banking supervision is exercised jointly with the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF), while economic and financial sanctions are implemented by the Luxembourg Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the CSSF.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium as an ancillary payment house; Low as a primary clearing rail. An EEA-authorized electronic money institution (EMI) with a PSD2 passport may lawfully hold funds and execute payments for a Luxembourg SCSp under the Luxembourg Law of 10 November 2009 on payment services, as amended by the Law of 3 July 2020, subject to safeguarding rules. Offshore EMIs (UK, Swiss, Hong Kong, Singapore) cannot passport into Luxembourg and would require a full CSSF branch licence. In practice, EMIs do not provide correspondent USD clearing for commodity trades, will not hold capital escrow, and frequently exclude wholesale petroleum-product traders; their use should be limited to small operating disbursements and employee expense payments.
