Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés – RCS), operated by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR), under the supervision of the Ministry of Justice.

Governing statute

Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (notably by the Law of 19 August 2016 and the Law of 7 April 2021); Articles 710-1 to 710-25 (Société à Responsabilité Limitée); Law of 13 January 2019 on the Register of Beneficial Owners; Grand-Ducal Regulation of 27 November 2017 on RCS electronic filings.

Formation / constitutional filings

Authentic notarial deed of incorporation containing the Articles of Association (statuts constitutifs), e-filed with the RCS and published in the Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations (RESA).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

The SARL is formed with a fully subscribed minimum share capital of EUR 12,000. At incorporation, at least 25% of the nominal value of each share subscribed in cash must be liberated and deposited into a blocked subscription account at a Luxembourg credit institution; the balance is payable on call. Contributions in kind must be fully transferred within five years of incorporation. The bank blocked-funds certificate is annexed to the notarial deed; funds are released only after the notary gives authority in view of RCS registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety conformity: certificate of compliance (certificat de sécurité incendie) issued by the municipal fire-prevention authority for the occupied premises. Structural soundness: landlord's certificate of habitability and load-resistance annexed to the lease. Occupational clearance: registration with the Inspectorate of Labour and Mines (ITM) as an employer, compliance with Labour Code provisions on visual-display-unit workstations, and, if physical archives or server equipment are stored, municipal building clearance under the code of civil safety.

Paid-in capital rule: The SARL is formed with a fully subscribed minimum share capital of EUR 12,000. At incorporation, at least 25% of the nominal value of each share subscribed in cash must be liberated and deposited into a blocked subscription account at a Luxembourg credit institution; the balance is payable on call. Contributions in kind must be fully transferred within five years of incorporation. The bank blocked-funds certificate is annexed to the notarial deed; funds are released only after the notary gives authority in view of RCS registration.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Luxembourg Direct Tax Administration (Administration des Contributions Directes - ACD); Luxembourg Indirect Tax and VAT Administration (Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA - AEDT); corporate registration through Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR / RCS); unified digital entry point: MyGuichet.lu professional eSpace (eTVA portal for VAT).

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must (1) register the LU SARL with the RCS via LBR to obtain the corporate registration number (matricule); (2) obtain the direct-tax NIF automatically aligned to the matricule from the ACD; and (3) obtain the Luxembourg VAT identification number (LU + 8 digits) from the AEDT through the MyGuichet/eTVA portal using the standard VAT registration application, or through the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for intra-EU B2C digital supplies. Non-EU suppliers without a Luxembourg establishment must appoint an AEDT-approved VAT representative unless OSS is used. Required exhibits include apostilled articles of association, proof of substance (office, director mandates), and the Webtoon commercialization contracts with Apple IAP and Google Play Billing.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - an LU SARL is a per se opaque resident company subject to corporate income tax (17%) plus municipal business tax (up to 6.75%) plus the solidarity surcharge (7% of CIT), giving an aggregate rate of about 24.94% (Luxembourg City), taxable on worldwide profits with relief through treaties, the participation exemption, and foreign PE exemption. — Foreign entities must (1) register the LU SARL with the RCS via LBR to obtain the corporate registration number (matricule); (2) obtain the direct-tax NIF automatically aligned to the matricule from the ACD; and (3) obtain the Luxembourg VAT identification number (LU + 8 digits) from the AEDT through the MyGuichet/eTVA portal using the standard VAT registration application, or through the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for intra-EU B2C digital supplies. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - an LU SARL is a per se opaque resident company subject to corporate income tax (17%) plus municipal business tax (up to 6.75%) plus the solidarity surcharge (7% of CIT), giving an aggregate rate of about 24.94% (Luxembourg City), taxable on worldwide profits with relief through treaties, the participation exemption, and foreign PE exemption.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box: SARL tax classification is statutory and cannot be elected. Available statutory elections: (i) VAT group treatment (assujetti unique - Art. 60 VAT Law); (ii) CIT fiscal unity/consolidation (integration fiscale - Art. 164bis ITL) for at least 95%-owned resident subsidiaries, binding for 5 years; (iii) application of the participation-exemption regime (Art. 166 ITL) for qualifying shareholdings; (iv) OSS registration for cross-border B2C digital supplies. Hybrid-mismatch neutralization rules (ATAD 2, in force 1 January 2020) apply automatically and are not elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Zero registration threshold for non-established foreign suppliers - any taxable Luxembourg supply triggers registration. Domestic small-enterprise franchise exemption: EUR 35,000 annual turnover (with option to tax). Pan-EU threshold: EUR 10,000 for intra-EU distance sales and electronically supplied services to consumers, above which OSS registration and the destination-state VAT rate apply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported outside the EU qualify for the 0% rate if the supplier holds proof of departure from the EU within the statutory periods (Art. 55 VAT Law). Digital Webtoon subscriptions are electronically supplied services: B2B supplies are outside Luxembourg VAT by reverse charge; B2C supplies to non-EU consumers are outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT (place of supply is the consumer residence) and may be presented as 0% / no LU VAT; B2C supplies to EU consumers are taxed under OSS at the destination-state rate (Luxembourg rates for domestic consumers: 17% standard, 8% reduced, 3% super-reduced for qualifying e-publications such as digital comics). No Luxembourg statutory FX-repatriation deadline exists; the 180-day benchmark is used for documentary and banking evidence of export proceeds.

  • Customs export declaration (SAD / EU export declaration with MRN) for physical goods
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or CMR consignment note evidencing exit from EU territory
  • Commercial invoice, license and chapter-unlocking statements for the Webtoon content
  • Bank SWIFT / inward remittance certificate evidencing settlement of the export proceeds
  • For digital services: customer residence logs (IP geolocation, billing address), Apple IAP / Google Play Billing payment-rail reports, and OSS recapitulative statements

No mandatory domestic B2B e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance/API stamping is in force as of the dossier reference date. B2G e-invoicing through PEPPOL (EN 16931) is mandatory for public contractors. Luxembourg is transposing the EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) package and has tabled draft legislation to introduce structured e-invoicing for domestic B2B supplies on a phased basis from 2026-2028; until then, optional e-invoicing with ex-post VAT returns filed through the AEDT eTVA/MyGuichet portal applies.

Failure to substantiate zero-rating allows the AEDT to assess the VAT at 17% (or the applicable rate), with default interest of 0.6% per commenced month, administrative fines of EUR 250-7,500 per infraction (Art. 65 VAT Law), and a 10% surcharge on ex officio assessments. Fraudulent claims trigger criminal liability (Criminal Code sanctions) and extend the recovery limitation period from 5 to 10 years. The administration may retrospectively claw back input VAT credits and deny treaty or participation-exemption benefits where the SARL lacks economic substance (employees, premises, bank accounts) in Luxembourg.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is the lawful currency and is fully convertible in Luxembourg for both current transactions and capital movements. Luxembourg maintains no exchange controls under EU law, and both residents and non-residents are free to hold, transfer, and invoice in EUR or any other currency. Capital account transactions, including shareholder contributions, intercompany loans, and purchase of foreign IP, do not require BCL or CSSF authorisation unless the underlying activity is in a regulated financial sector. Current account convertibility is unrestricted for service export payments, including digital webtoon platform royalties and app-store settlements. Profit remittance hurdles are not exchange-based but legal and fiscal: the SARL may distribute dividends only if its net assets exceed the subscribed and paid-up capital plus non-distributable reserves, and Luxembourg branch/tax formalities apply. Dividends paid by a Luxembourg SARL to a non-resident corporate shareholder are generally exempt from Luxembourg dividend withholding tax under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive if a 10% participation or acquisition-cost threshold is met; otherwise a domestic 15% withholding tax arises, often reduced by an applicable double tax treaty. For non-resident individual shareholders, the 15% withholding tax is retained, subject to treaty relief.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Luxembourg imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds, no statutory conversion timeline, and no minimum conversion ratio for foreign currency earnings. A Luxembourg SARL may retain 100% of its export and licensing revenue denominated in EUR, USD, KRW, JPY, or other currencies in domestic or foreign accounts without BCL approval. For balance-of-payments statistics, resident companies are obliged to report certain cross-border services and direct investment flows to BCL under Luxembourg statistical law; digital webtoon publishing proceeds and app-store syndication receipts should be reported as 'other commercial services' when thresholds are met. No customs invoice or foreign exchange certificate is required for electronic delivery of digital comics. The company must keep ordinary commercial, accounting, and VAT documentation to substantiate the export nature of transactions.

Central bank supervision: Banque Centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), acting within the Eurosystem framework, with prudential and market-conduct coordination provided by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) in respect of licensed banks, payment institutions, and electronic money institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-Low for unregulated offshore digital EMIs and Low for using them as the sole treasury rail. A Luxembourg or EEA-passported electronic money institution licensed under PSD2 and supervised by CSSF or another EU authority may legally provide a payment account for the SARL, but its operational viability is constrained by transaction limits, lack of in-person banking services, no credit lines, and potential unwillingness of Luxembourg correspondent banks to accept third-party EMI settlements. Non-EEA, non-passported EMIs are effectively prohibited as primary operating banks because they cannot subscribe to the Luxembourg SEPA Clearing, do not have direct BCL settlement access, create AML/CRS implications, and are rejected by domestic banks that need to know the source of funds. For the webtoon and app-IAP treasury model, an EMI may be used only as a secondary collection node for small platform payouts, with all material balances moved immediately to a tier-1 Luxembourg bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The SARL's initial or subsequent share capital injection must be routed to a dedicated Luxembourg EUR account by SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) from a European shareholder, or by SWIFT MT103 from a non-European shareholder, using the beneficiary’s IBAN and the receiving bank’s BIC. The payment remittance message must contain a transparent reference: 'CAPITAL INCREASE – SHAREHOLDER CONTRIBUTION – [SARL RCS number]' and be accompanied by a copy of the notarized capital increase resolution, the shareholder register, an updated UBO declaration, and a source-of-funds statement. Luxembourg does not require prior exchange-control approval for inbound equity or debt contributions. If the inbound transfer is a subordinated shareholder loan, the loan agreement with interest rate and repayment schedule must be provided to the bank for AML and tax documentation. The bank will apply enhanced scrutiny where the contributing entity is domiciled in a non-EEA jurisdiction without adequate UBO transparency; the SARL must supply proof that the contribution is made from the shareholder’s own bank account and is not remitted through collective investment vehicles or nominee accounts.Outbound treasury disbursements for the webtoon publishing group will be executed over SEPA (for EU vendors and EU creators) and SWIFT MT103 (for non-EU licensors, artists, local subsidiaries, printers, or marketing service providers). Each MT103 must carry a purpose code relevant to digital content royalties, e.g., 'Royalty for syndicated digital comic licensing', 'Licence fee for localisation services', or 'Remuneration to independent author under work-for-hire contract'. Where physical ancillary print merchandise is purchased from the SARL, a Documentary Commercial Letter of Credit (MT700) may be used with specified invoicing and bill-of-lading terms, but for digital intangible deliverables only invoice and electronic delivery certificates are necessary. The SARL treasury will require all outbound transfers to be covered by signed contracts, VAT invoices or pro-formas, and a beneficial-owner tax certificate where preferential withholding rates apply. Luxembourg does not restrict cash outflows, but the bank will impose AML review for payments to tax havens or jurisdictions with opaque corporate registers. A Luxembourg or EEA-passported electronic money institution licensed under PSD2 and supervised by CSSF or another EU authority may legally provide a payment account for the SARL, but its operational viability is constrained by transaction limits, lack of in-person banking services, no credit lines, and potential unwillingness of Luxembourg correspondent banks to accept third-party EMI settlements. Non-EEA, non-passported EMIs are effectively prohibited as primary operating banks because they cannot subscribe to the Luxembourg SEPA Clearing, do not have direct BCL settlement access, create AML/CRS implications, and are rejected by domestic banks that need to know the source of funds. For the webtoon and app-IAP treasury model, an EMI may be used only as a secondary collection node for small platform payouts, with all material balances moved immediately to a tier-1 Luxembourg bank.
