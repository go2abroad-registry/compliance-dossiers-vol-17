Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No legal minimum capital for SCSp; no escrow account required. Capital must be freely determined by the partnership agreement, and each partner's contribution must be fully subscribed as stipulated. General partner liability is unlimited; limited partners' liability is capped at their contribution.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate issued by the Corps grand-ducal d'incendie et de secours (CGDIS) based on a fire-fighting plan, detection and alarm systems, fire-escape routing, and water supply points; structural load clearance certificate from the Administration des bâtiments publics (for permanent installations) and inspection by the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) for workplace safety, including explosion-proof equipment (ATEX zones), confined-space entry procedures, and operator training records. The bunkering vessel and any mobile refueling equipment must receive a separate certificate of conformity from the Ministère de la Mobilité et des Travaux publics (inland navigation authorization under Luxembourg inland waterway regulations).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Luxembourg Inland Revenue (Administration des contributions directes, ACD) for direct taxes; Luxembourg Indirect Tax Authority (Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA, AEDT) for VAT; Luxembourg Customs and Excise Agency (Administration des douanes et accises) for customs and export formalities. Primary taxpayer portal: MyGuichet.lu (business eSpace) and the RCS/RBE single registration system.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident foreign entity obtaining a Luxembourg tax identity must first complete RCS registration (single RBE filing) to obtain the Luxembourg business registration number (matricule). The ACD automatically allocates the national income tax identification number based on the RCS matricule. For VAT purposes, the AEDT requires separate registration via Form 640 before supplies are made; non-established foreign operators must appoint a Luxembourg VAT representative unless an EU-established entity can register directly. Direct tax ID is mandatory for any permanent establishment, partnership, or taxable activity; VAT ID follows the standard LU + 12-digit format.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A non-resident foreign entity obtaining a Luxembourg tax identity must first complete RCS registration (single RBE filing) to obtain the Luxembourg business registration number (matricule). The ACD automatically allocates the national income tax identification number based on the RCS matricule. For VAT purposes, the AEDT requires separate registration via Form 640 before supplies are made; non-established foreign operators must appoint a Luxembourg VAT representative unless an EU-established entity can register directly. Direct tax ID is mandatory for any permanent establishment, partnership, or taxable activity; VAT ID follows the standard LU + 12-digit format.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Default: SCSp is fiscally transparent; all income, gains, deductions, and credits flow through to partners. Under Article 174bis LIR, the SCSp may elect optional full taxation as a corporation if it is a commercial partnership, maintains corporate-style accounts, and all partners are either Luxembourg residents or taxable through a Luxembourg PE, thereby becoming entity-level taxed and enabling the application of double tax treaty benefits. Other elections include the EU fiscal unity regime (integration fiscale) for direct tax under Article 164bis LIR and VAT group election under Article 61ter LVT, plus ATAD hybrid-mismatch filing elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover (small business exemption for Luxembourg-established entities); non-established foreign suppliers performing taxable supplies in Luxembourg must register for VAT from the first euro of turnover, with no threshold exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Marine fuel supplies (bunkers) delivered as stores provisioning to vessels engaged in international traffic qualify for VAT zero-rating (0%) under Luxembourg VAT law implementing EU VAT Directive Article 148; the zero-rated treatment covers supplies of fuel and lubricants for commercial ships operating on international voyages, including transport to/from third countries. The eligibility requires the vessel to be an eligible seagoing or inland-waterway vessel engaged in international navigation, and the bunker supply must be physically delivered at the Luxembourg port or as ship stores for the voyage. Re-supply during a voyage carrying passengers or goods between Luxembourg and another EU/third-country port also qualifies; supplies to yachts or private vessels do not qualify unless used for chartering to third parties with an economic activity.

  • Customs Export Declaration (EX-A/IE-507, AES/ECS confirmation)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document (sea waybill, barge/ship manifests)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or commercial bank wire/SWIFT proof of payment from the shipowner/operator
  • Bunker delivery receipt / marine fuel delivery note (MFDN) endorsed by the vessel's bunkering officer
  • Ship's logbook extract proving the international voyage
  • Charter party agreement or fixture note showing third-country destination
  • IMO number and vessel registration certificate
  • Declaration by the buyer/operator of the vessel's international-trade status

No general real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory API-based e-invoicing clearance currently applies to Luxembourg B2B transactions. Business-to-government e-invoicing must be issued in Peppol-BIS format on the PEPPOL network for public procurement invoicing. VAT returns and recapitulative statements (Intrastat, EU sales lists) must be filed electronically through MyGuichet/ACD-AEDT e-filing modules. For marine bunkering supplies, customs export clearances are processed via Luxembourg's Customs EDP/ECS system (part of EU Customs Decision Management system).

The AEDT and Luxembourg Customs exercise stringent ex-post audit review of export zero-rating claims for bunker supplies. If the zero-rating is not substantiated with the full documentation bundle within the statutory audit timeline, the VAT is retrospectively clawed back with default interest from the due date. Penalties for negligent or intentional VAT evasion range from 10% to 100% of the tax evaded; criminal prosecution can result in fines up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment for repeat fraud. Under Luxembourg tax limitation rules, assessments may be reopened up to five years (ordinary cases), extended to ten years for fraud. Export zero-rating is strictly denied for supplies to fishing vessels, pleasure craft, or inland vessels used exclusively within domestic waterways absent an international-trade test.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full liberalisation of capital and current account transactions applies by virtue of EU law (Articles 63-66 TFEU); Luxembourg imposes no exchange-control authorisation for bunker fuel import/export payments, SCSp profit distributions, or investor capital repatriation. The euro is fully convertible and USD/EUR/other-currency settlement is unrestricted. Remittance hurdles are limited to (i) CSSF/BCL balance-of-payments statistical reporting, (ii) AML verification of beneficial owners under the Law of 12 November 2004 (as amended), and (iii) FATCA/CRS and Luxembourg withholding tax compliance on qualifying interest and dividend payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export proceeds in Luxembourg; bunkering receivables may remain denominated in USD or any freely convertible currency without a repatriation deadline. The only functional conversion arises for Luxembourg statutory annual accounts, which must be kept in EUR (or a documented functional currency) for Commercial Code and tax reporting purposes; this is an accounting translation obligation, not a surrender obligation.

Central bank supervision: Banque Centrale du Luxembourg (BCL); monetary policy and foreign exchange competences are exercised jointly with the European Central Bank (ECB) under the Eurosystem. Luxembourg maintains no separate foreign exchange administration; AML/CFT and financial-sector integrity supervision is carried out by the CSSF (Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier) over credit institutions and e-money institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as the primary treasury rail and MEDIUM only as a satellite operational account. A Luxembourg CSSF-licensed e-money institution (fully authorised under the E-Money Directive 2009/110/EC) can legally host SCSp funds and provide IBANs, but large bunkering settlements (typically EUR 0.5M-10M per stem) require Tier 1 bank-grade SWIFT correspondent access and vessel-counterparty sanctions screening, which most offshore and UK-based digital EMIs cannot support. A passported EEA EMI may handle low-value port expenses such as pilotage, agency fees, and crew wages, but must not receive or disburse physical bunker fuel payments without a full commodity-trade AML programme.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound subscription capital is routed under EU free-movement-of-capital rules with no exchange-control approval. EUR injections use SEPA Credit Transfer on TARGET2 with purpose code EQUI (equity investment); USD and other-currency injections use SWIFT MT103 with a remittance reference describing the equity subscription and the BCL balance-of-payments statistical classification. Each payment must originate from the exact investor account named in the subscription agreement; third-party funding and cash deposits are prohibited. The receiving bank books the funds to a dedicated capital escrow account, performs wire verification against a signed subscription form, and compiles a UBO/source-of-wealth file before converting the funds to operational cash. USD-EUR conversion is executed as an onboarding spot FX trade, with monthly BoP statistical reporting to the BCL.All bunker fuel settlements execute via SWIFT MT103 (single customer credit transfer) for open-account stems and via MT700 documentary letters of credit or MT760 standby letters of credit for secured stems. The clearing rail requires full three-way matching of the commercial invoice, the BDN, and the master's receipt before release; USD payments settle through a Luxembourg Tier-1 bank's New York correspondent via CHIPS or Fedwire, and EUR payments settle through TARGET2/SEPA. SBLCs issued by the SCSp's bank in favour of physical suppliers must contain a clean, on-demand payment undertaking governed by Luxembourg law; the issuance is recorded as a commitment in the SCSp's annual accounts, requiring an authorised credit line and demonstrable economic substance.Deliverable EUR/USD FX forwards (plain vanilla) with a CSSF-licensed bank under the ISDA 1998 FX definitionsEUR/USD FX options (European-style calls, puts, collars, and risk reversals) for hedging volatile USD bunker payablesCross-currency swaps (EUR/USD) to match long-term instalments or term-loan repayments of the SCSpFX swaps (tom-next and spot-next) for short-term funding and liquidity conversionNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permissible only for restricted currencies with no deliverable market (e.g., certain Asian or petro-state exotic FX pairs); EUR/USD NDFs are not permissible because the EUR/USD market is fully convertible and deliverable
