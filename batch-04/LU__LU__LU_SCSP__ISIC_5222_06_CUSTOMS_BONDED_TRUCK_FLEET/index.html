Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital for an SCSp; contributions are freely fixed in the partnership agreement and no escrow account is required at incorporation. For customs-bonded trucking, the Customs and Excise Agency requires a comprehensive guarantee or cash deposit (typically EUR 20,000–50,000) as security for customs debt before bonded operations commence.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate (certificat de sécurité incendie) issued by the Inspection générale de la Protection civile (CGDIS); structural stability attestation for the truck depot, canopies and loading docks issued by a qualified structural engineer; occupational health and safety clearance from the Inspection du travail et des mines (ITM); communal site-operation approval under the building permit for the depot and vehicle parking area.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital for an SCSp; contributions are freely fixed in the partnership agreement and no escrow account is required at incorporation. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct income taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT registration, filing, and recovery; unified digital access via MyGuichet.lu and the guichet.lu official portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign SCSp with Luxembourg economic nexus must: (1) file incorporation and register with the Luxembourg Business Registers (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés) via MyGuichet to obtain the 10-digit national business registration number (matricule national); (2) appoint a Luxembourg-established VAT agent or approved tax representative if the partnership has no fixed local establishment; (3) submit the AEDT VAT registration application (form 640/A or equivalent electronic immatriculation dossier) declaring intended commencement date, turnover projections, and activity codes (ISIC 5222 road feeder / customs bonded transport); (4) receive the Luxembourg VAT identification number in the format LU + 8 digits; and (5) obtain separate national natural-person identification numbers for the partners, managers, and any Luxembourg permanent establishment staff registered with ACD.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — Luxembourg SCSp is fiscally transparent by default and is not itself subject to Luxembourg corporate income tax (CIT), municipal business tax (Uber Gewerbesteuer), or net wealth tax; the partners are taxed on their allocated share of income in accordance with their residence and the territorial nexus rules, and non-resident partners without a Luxembourg permanent establishment are generally outside Luxembourg income tax on capital gains.

Available election forms

Article 2 LIR option: an SCSp may irrevocably elect to be treated as a non-transparent Luxembourg tax-resident corporation for Luxembourg tax purposes if all general partners are Luxembourg-resident corporate entities subject to full CIT; transparency mismatches with the country of a foreign partner are addressed through the hybrid mismatch rules implemented under the law of 20 December 2019 (ATAD I), which deny deductions in cases of double deduction or deduction-without-inclusion outcomes; there is no US-style check-the-box election in Luxembourg, but a tax ruling from the ACD Direct Tax Directorate is available to confirm the default transparent classification and to prevent multiple or inconsistent entity classification in cross-border structures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility; the euro is unconditionally convertible with zero exchange-control restrictions under Articles 63-66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and the EU Capital Movements Directive 88/361/EEC. Profit remittances from a Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp) are not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax (tax-transparent partnership) and face no administrative remittance barrier. Practical hurdles are limited to BCL statistical reporting thresholds, AML/UBO verification, AIFMD classification review, and Luxembourg substance requirements for the feeder-trucking operation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Luxembourg is a full Eurozone member and imposes no statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender obligation on export receipts. FX-denominated income (e.g., USD air-freight receivables) may be held indefinitely in multi-currency current accounts. Statutory obligations are limited to: (i) export declarations via the Single Administrative Document (SAD) and NCTS transit system filed with the Administration des douanes et accises; (ii) physical cash declaration under Regulation (EU) 2018/1672 for movements of EUR 10,000 or more; and (iii) BCL balance-of-payments reporting executed by the resident credit institution on a flow threshold basis.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating in coordination with the European Central Bank (ECB) and the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) as banking supervisor; customs supervision for bonded fleet operations is exercised by the Administration des douanes et accises under the Union Customs Code.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

FEASIBILITY: LOW-MEDIUM. EEA-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) passporting into Luxembourg under the E-Money Directive 2009/110/EC and PSD2 may be used exclusively for ancillary outbound disbursements (small-scale fuel payments, tolls, and courier fees). They cannot serve as the primary treasury vehicle because EMIs are not credit institutions: they cannot extend credit or provide customs guarantee facilities, they offer only incidental FX conversion, and they are structurally unsuitable for capital escrow or bonded-transit guarantee arrangements. A fully licensed credit institution remains mandatory for capital escrow, customs guarantees, and cross-border settlement rails.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required if the SCSp's related-party transactions exceed EUR 1 million (goods) or EUR 500,000 (services) and the group's consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 50 million; Master File required if group revenue exceeds EUR 50 million and the SCSp is part of a group with cross-border transactions

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg applies Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) for entities engaged in certain activities; for a customs bonded truck fleet, substance is evidenced by physical presence, employees, and operational management in Luxembourg; the SCSp must maintain adequate substance to avoid being classified as a shell company

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; additional tax of 10% to 50% of the evaded tax

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for unpaid taxes if they knowingly participated in evasion; civil restitution and travel bans may be imposed
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of tax treaty benefits, reclassification of income, and penalties up to EUR 50,000; potential criminal charges for fraud

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for taxes and penalties if they fail to ensure substance; travel bans and asset freezes possible
AML/CFT Violations

Statutory sanction: Fines up to EUR 1 million and imprisonment up to 5 years; suspension or revocation of operating license

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and imprisoned; civil liability for damages to third parties
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income, penalties up to 10% of the adjustment, and interest on underpaid tax

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for penalties if they knowingly approved non-compliant pricing; potential criminal charges for tax fraud
