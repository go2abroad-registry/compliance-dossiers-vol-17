Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Administration des contributions directes (ACD) — Luxembourg Direct Tax Administration (direct taxes), and Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) — Registration Duties, Estates and VAT Administration (VAT, registration duties); unified e-services portals: MyGuichet.lu, the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) electronic RCS platform, and the eTVA electronic VAT filing platform.

        
E-government filing portal
The LU_SCSp is established by written partnership agreement (notarial deed not required unless real property is contributed) and registered electronically with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) via MyGuichet.lu by a Luxembourg-registered professional; registration automatically generates the 13-digit national identification number (matricule/NIF) issued by the LBR. The SCSp then files Formulaire 640 with the AEDT using LuxTrust/eIDAS credentials and is issued a Luxembourg VAT identification number in LU+8-digit format. Non-resident general partners must provide foreign tax identification certificates and may be required to appoint an AEDT-accepted tax representative; the SCSp must also register as an employer with the Centre Commun de la Sécurité Sociale (CCSS) when it hires personnel for the 3D-printing production facility.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible: The SCSp lacks separate corporate tax personality; it is fiscally transparent for Luxembourg corporate income tax (CIT), municipal business tax (MBT) and net wealth tax (NWT). All income, capital gains, deductions and tax credits are allocated to and taxed at partner level in proportion to partnership interests; a non-resident partner carrying on commercial activity through the SCSp is deemed to maintain a Luxembourg permanent establishment. For VAT purposes the SCSp is an independent taxable person: it must charge output VAT, may deduct input VAT, files periodic VAT returns, and its partners are jointly and severally liable for VAT debts of the partnership. — The LU_SCSp is established by written partnership agreement (notarial deed not required unless real property is contributed) and registered electronically with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) via MyGuichet.lu by a Luxembourg-registered professional; registration automatically generates the 13-digit national identification number (matricule/NIF) issued by the LBR. The SCSp then files Formulaire 640 with the AEDT using LuxTrust/eIDAS credentials and is issued a Luxembourg VAT identification number in LU+8-digit format. Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Pass_Through_Eligible: The SCSp lacks separate corporate tax personality; it is fiscally transparent for Luxembourg corporate income tax (CIT), municipal business tax (MBT) and net wealth tax (NWT). All income, capital gains, deductions and tax credits are allocated to and taxed at partner level in proportion to partnership interests; a non-resident partner carrying on commercial activity through the SCSp is deemed to maintain a Luxembourg permanent establishment. For VAT purposes the SCSp is an independent taxable person: it must charge output VAT, may deduct input VAT, files periodic VAT returns, and its partners are jointly and severally liable for VAT debts of the partnership.

        
Available election forms
No US-style check-the-box election exists. Luxembourg law permits an election under Article 175bis LIR to treat a transparent SCSp as a resident taxpayer subject to CIT, MBT and NWT, binding for at least five years. Hybrid mismatch rules under LIR Article 168quater (ATAD I/II) require partner-by-partner tax classification evidence, including foreign classification certificates (e.g., US Form W-8BEN-E, German Feststellungserklärung), before distributions are made. VAT elections available include waiver of the small-business franchise, VAT grouping (unité de TVA), OSS/Non-Union OSS registration for distance sales, and any statutory periodic option for the export or inward processing regime as applicable.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
17%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
€35,000 annual taxable turnover for Luxembourg-established businesses under the VAT small-business franchise (franchise de taxe); this threshold does not apply to non-established suppliers, who must register from the first taxable euro. For distance sales of titanium orthopedic implants to non-taxable EU consumers, the EU-wide cross-border threshold of €10,000 applies and may be covered by OSS registration rather than local full registration.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Zero-rated (0%) treatment applies under Article 54 of the Luxembourg VAT Law to supplies of titanium orthopedic implants physically dispatched or transported outside the European Union by the supplier, the customer or a third party acting on their behalf, provided the exempt status of the customer is evidenced and the goods leave the EU within the legally required period. Ancillary services (international freight, insurance, loading and handling) may be zero-rated under Articles 55–56; intra-Community dispatches to EU hospitals or distributors holding a valid EU VAT number are zero-rated under Article 53, subject to inclusion in the EC Sales List. Input VAT embedded in export costs is refundable through the ordinary VAT refund mechanism, including the 8th Directive and 13th Directive procedures for non-established EU/non-EU businesses.

No general real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime applies to B2B invoicing. The AEDT verifies export zero-rating by cross-matching the IE599 customs exit record against the 180-day payment benchmark, transport and insurance documents, and partner-level capital-flow reconciliation. Any unexplained gap between shipment date and FX repatriation triggers VAT reassessment at 17% plus late-payment interest at 0.8% per commenced month and administrative fines. The ordinary limitation period is five years, extended to ten years in cases of fraud or false documentation. Criminal sanctions for fraudulent zero-rating claims include fines up to €250,000 and imprisonment from eight days to five years under Article 61 of the Luxembourg VAT Law, with proportional fines up to twice the tax evaded, joint and several liability of the SCSp general partner, and exclusion from the taxpayer good-faith compliance programme.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Luxembourg applies full current-account and capital-account convertibility under Articles 63 and 65 TFEU. No exchange-control authorization is required for foreign direct investment, partnership capital contributions, trade settlements, or profit distributions by an SCSp. The SCSp is fiscally transparent and distributions to non-resident partners are not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax. Practical constraints are limited to the resident bank’s AML/CFT obligations under the law of 12 November 2004 and CSSF Regulation 12-02, EU and OFAC sanctions screening in USD clearing, Eurosystem balance-of-payments statistical reporting, and substance expectations for a pass-through manufacturing vehicle. Export proceeds can be retained indefinitely in EUR or foreign currency without any conversion mandate.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
The Grand Duchy imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio. Neither the BCL nor the CSSF requires repatriation, liquidation, or conversion of export receivables into euro. The only reporting layer is the resident credit institution’s obligation to transmit balance-of-payments and direct-investment statistics to the BCL under Regulation (EU) No 184/2005, as amended. The SCSp must supply the paying bank with the commercial invoice, transport documents, and the correct ISO purpose code for each inward receipt. Documentary retention of at least 10 years under Luxembourg commercial law is recommended, and physical cash movements in excess of EUR 10,000 crossing EU borders must be declared under Regulation (EU) 2018/1672.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) operating within the Eurosystem and the European System of Central Banks; foreign-exchange policy competence is vested in the European Central Bank. Prudential and AML/CFT supervision of credit institutions, payment institutions and electronic money institutions is exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
LOW-MEDIUM feasibility as a primary treasury rail. Luxembourg EMIs licensed under the law of 17 July 2020 implementing EMD2 and PSD2 may hold client funds, execute SEPA and SWIFT transfers, and issue e-money, but they are prohibited from extending credit, opening or confirming documentary credits under UCP 600, and they lack the global correspondent network required for USD trade settlements. An EMI can serve only as a support account for instant SEPA and corporate expense cards; the core cross-border exposure must remain with a full-service credit institution such as BCEE, BIL or Société Générale Luxembourg.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        SCSp registered as a partnership without legal personality creates uncertainty on contractual capacity and bank account signatory authority.
        
Root cause: SCSp registered as a partnership without legal personality creates uncertainty on contractual capacity and bank account signatory authority.

        
Defense evidence: Certified copy of the partnership agreement (contrat de société), RCS registration extract of the SCSp, extract from the Luxembourg Register of Beneficial Owners (RBE), power of attorney granted to the authorized signatory, and a Luxembourg legal opinion confirming that the general partner validly binds the SCSp and may open and operate bank accounts in its name.

      

      
      

        Intra-group non-EU capital injection exceeding EUR 250,000 prompts enhanced due diligence on source of funds and source of wealth.
        
Root cause: Intra-group non-EU capital injection exceeding EUR 250,000 prompts enhanced due diligence on source of funds and source of wealth.

        
Defense evidence: Audited IFRS or local GAAP financial statements of the contributing partner, SCSp capital account ledger, contributing partner board resolution, bank reference letter from the payer’s regulated institution, complete payment chain documentation, and the underlying direct-investment rationale with proof of Luxembourg substance.

      

      
      

        Pass-through partnership perceived as a shell or brass-plate structure lacking economic substance in Luxembourg.
        
Root cause: Pass-through partnership perceived as a shell or brass-plate structure lacking economic substance in Luxembourg.

        
Defense evidence: Luxembourg substance pack evidencing administrative office or contracted service arrangements, local management decisions and bank instructions issued from the general partner’s Luxembourg address, payroll or outsourced employment records, audited accounts of the operating manufacturing affiliate, OECD-compliant transfer pricing documentation, and annual RCS filings confirming continuity of the registered office.

      

      
      

        Third-party payment risk in the medical device distribution chain where the payer of invoices differs from the purchaser on the shipping documents.
        
Root cause: Third-party payment risk in the medical device distribution chain where the payer of invoices differs from the purchaser on the shipping documents.

        
Defense evidence: Full contractual chain comprising distribution agreements, end-user certificates, pro forma and final commercial invoices describing titanium orthopedic implants under ISIC 3250, CMR/B/L/AWB transport evidence, EU MDR or FDA conformity declarations, CE marking documentation, and reconciliation of each SWIFT payment to the corresponding transaction reference and invoice.

      

      
      

        Sanctions, adverse-media, or high-risk jurisdiction flags arising from a non-resident ultimate shareholder or limited partner.
        
Root cause: Sanctions, adverse-media, or high-risk jurisdiction flags arising from a non-resident ultimate shareholder or limited partner.

        
Defense evidence: Completed CSSF and Wolfsberg-style beneficial ownership questionnaires, full identification of every natural person in the ownership chain with name, nationality and date of birth, jurisdictional risk rationale tied to EU and US hospital export markets, negative sanctions and media screening report from a reputable provider, and a board compliance attestation confirming no connection to designated persons or embargoed territories.

      

      
    

    

      
Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Compliant inbound capital injections must be effected by SWIFT MT103 or ISO 20022 pacs.008 from the limited partner’s credit institution to the SCSp’s Luxembourg operating account, using the ISO purpose code CAPC and a precise remittance reference to the partner’s capital account. USD-denominated injections clear through the correspondent banking network into the receiving bank’s USD omnibus account; EUR injections settle directly via Target2 or EBA CLEARING. The receiving bank captures transactions above EUR 100,000 for BCL external statistics, but no prior BCL authorization or foreign-exchange control form exists. Contributions in kind, such as titanium powder feedstock or electron-beam melting equipment, must be documented in a notarized contribution report, reflected in the RCS filing, and where appropriate declared for Luxembourg VAT purposes; source-of-funds evidence and beneficial ownership screens accompany all contributions.The treasury hierarchy for outbound trade disbursements is: (1) SWIFT gpi MT103 or pacs.008 with a unique end-to-end transaction reference for routine supplier payments below EUR 1,000,000; (2) commercial documentary credits under UCP 600 for high-value titanium feedstock and production consumables, issued via MT700, amended by MT707, and negotiated by the Luxembourg confirming bank with MT202 cover; (3) documentary collections under URC 522 for recurring lower-risk vendors; and (4) SEPA B2B Direct Debits for EEA-registered service providers. Every instruction must include the purchase order and invoice references, the Luxembourg VAT registration number, and the ISIC 3250 product descriptor. USD disbursements are executed through the bank’s cross-currency desk so that EUR/USD conversion is performed on the trade date with a single matched conversion, eliminating double-FX leakage and reducing settlement risk.Deliverable EUR/USD FX forward executed for bona fide trade and cash-flow hedging purposesEUR/USD FX swap enabling spot-forward rollover for short-term liquidity bridgingCross-currency interest rate swap (EUR/USD XCCY) permissible for a non-financial counterparty below the EMIR clearing thresholdVanilla EUR/USD option, physically or cash-settled, subject to board approval and documented underlying exposureNon-deliverable forward technically permissible under Luxembourg law but economically unnecessary for the fully convertible EUR/USD pair and therefore reserved only for counterparty settlement constraints

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: EUR 50,000,000 group consolidated turnover (non-SME threshold) triggers mandatory Master File and Local File preparation; EUR 750,000,000 group consolidated revenue triggers Country-by-Country Report filing with the Luxembourg AED.

      
Economic substance scope: Luxembourg imposes no standalone ESR statute, but applies the substance-over-form doctrine, the abuse-of-law rule (Art. 6 AStG), ATAD anti-avoidance provisions (interest limitation, CFC, exit taxation), and DAC6 mandatory disclosure. The SCSp must maintain real premises, local decision-making, adequate employees, and proportionate operating expenditures to obtain tax residence certificates, sustain treaty benefits, and preserve advance pricing agreements and tax rulings.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion
        
Statutory sanction: Additional tax up to 50% of evaded tax; late-payment interest at 0.60% per month; criminal fine up to EUR 25,000; imprisonment up to 5 years; publication of the judgment.

        
Director personal liability: Managing directors and the SCSp's general partner face personal civil restitution for the evaded tax, disqualification orders, asset freezes, travel bans under criminal procedure, and imprisonment; the corporate veil is pierced where the SCSp is used as a mere façade.
