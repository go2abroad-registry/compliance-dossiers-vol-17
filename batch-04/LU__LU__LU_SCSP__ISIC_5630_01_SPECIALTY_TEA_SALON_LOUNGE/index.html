Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés, RCS), operated by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR); electronic filings are submitted through the MyGuichet.lu portal.

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on commercial companies, as amended, including the provisions on the société en commandite spéciale (SCSp) introduced by the Law of 23 July 2016; Law of 19 December 2002 on the register of commerce and companies and on accounting and annual accounts of undertakings, as amended.

Formation / constitutional filings

RCS application for registration of an SCSp, accompanied by the deed of constitution (acte constitutif/contrat de société), submitted via the RCS electronic filing system; subsequent mandatory publication in the Electronic Compendium of Companies and Associations (Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations, RESA).

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None. The SCSp has no statutory minimum subscribed or paid-up capital and no requirement to deposit funds in an escrow account. Contributions may be in cash, in kind, or in services, and are governed solely by the partnership agreement.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate issued by the municipal fire brigade and the Corps Grand-Ducal Incendie et de Secours; building occupancy permit from the commune validating public capacity, exit routes, fire retardancy, and structural load for seating and equipment; occupational health and safety inspection by the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) for all staff areas; public liability insurance is a precondition for the issuance of the establishment licence.

Paid-in capital rule: None. The SCSp has no statutory minimum subscribed or paid-up capital and no requirement to deposit funds in an escrow account. Contributions may be in cash, in kind, or in services, and are governed solely by the partnership agreement.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) – Luxembourg Direct Tax Administration; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) – Luxembourg VAT, excise and registration duties. Official portals: guichet.lu and myguichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

For an SCSp without legal personality, the foreign partnership must first obtain a Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS) national identification number (matricule) by filing the incorporation/registration dossier through a certified notary or authorized agent on myguichet.lu. After RCS enrolment, the entity must apply to the AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT registration (LU + 8-digit numeric ID) using the standard electronic registration form when making taxable supplies in Luxembourg or when engaged in intra-Community acquisitions subject to VAT. If the SCSp has no domestic permanent establishment, a Luxembourg VAT representative may be required; foreign partners must also provide their relevant foreign TIN and proof of legal existence. No minimum capital is required, but proof of a registered office in Luxembourg and identity of the general partner is mandatory.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — For an SCSp without legal personality, the foreign partnership must first obtain a Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS) national identification number (matricule) by filing the incorporation/registration dossier through a certified notary or authorized agent on myguichet.lu. After RCS enrolment, the entity must apply to the AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT registration (LU + 8-digit numeric ID) using the standard electronic registration form when making taxable supplies in Luxembourg or when engaged in intra-Community acquisitions subject to VAT. If the SCSp has no domestic permanent establishment, a Luxembourg VAT representative may be required; foreign partners must also provide their relevant foreign TIN and proof of legal existence. No minimum capital is required, but proof of a registered office in Luxembourg and identity of the general partner is mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

SCSp is fiscally transparent by default; the partnership agreement governs profit and loss allocation. Partners may elect to waive the Luxembourg small-enterprise VAT exemption (annual turnover threshold €30,000) and submit regular VAT returns. SCSp may opt into a Luxembourg VAT group (assujetti unique) under the VAT Law. For income tax, no check-the-box mechanism exists; transparency is statutory. Hybrid mismatch elections may be available under ATAD/DAC6 and the Luxembourg tax anti-abuse rules, subject to EU law and preventing double non-taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

16%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€30,000 (annual taxable turnover excluding VAT, for the small-enterprise exemption; registration is compulsory above this threshold or when making intra-EU acquisitions, importing goods, or without EU establishment supplying taxable services liable to Luxembourg VAT, in which case reverse-charge and/or registration obligations may apply)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of goods physically exported from Luxembourg to a destination outside the European Union qualify for 0% VAT under Article 41 of the Luxembourg VAT Law, provided the goods leave EU territory and are accompanied by customs export declarations (EXA/EX1) and transport evidence. Intra-EU zero-rating (exempt with deductibility) applies to supplies to registered VAT customers in another Member State where VIES and EC Sales List requirements are met. Offshore services connected with export transport, handling, and services ancillary to export or import are zero-rated when performed for a customer established outside the EU; the salon's café/cultural service supplies to international visitors remain standard-rated unless dispatched as goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (EXA or EX1, with MRN), Bill of Lading or AIR WAYBILL/CMR, Commercial Invoice (with HS codes and country of origin), Single Administrative Document, Proof of transport/freight movement, Delivery and receipt confirmation, Inward remittance / payment evidence, VIES VAT number validation evidence for intra-EU zero rating

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or mandatory domestic B2B clearance exists in Luxembourg as of the current framework. B2G e-invoicing must be issued via the Peppol network under Luxembourg public procurement rules. All VAT invoices, including those for the tea salon lounge, must maintain authenticity of origin, integrity of content and legibility; XML/EDI electronic formats are accepted and an audit trail must be kept for at least 10 years.

The AEDT may retroactively claw back zero-rated VAT where proof of export is deficient, inaccurate, falsified or not produced within statutory scrutiny periods. Default interest runs from the date VAT became chargeable (currently 0.8% per month or partial month). Administrative fines apply for VAT understatement or intentional evasion, and criminal penalties may be imposed for fraudulent export documentation, including fines up to €50,000 and imprisonment. The taxpayer must maintain all customs bills, transport evidence, payment receipts and counterparty VAT status evidence for at least 10 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a eurozone member with full capital and current account convertibility under EU treaty law and the ECB regulatory framework. There are no statutory exchange controls or restrictions on profit remittance, loan repayments, or liquidation proceeds. Dividend distributions require standard corporate formalities, audited financial statements where applicable, and tax withholding compliance under the Luxembourg domestic anti-abuse rules, but no administrative authorisation or conversion license is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists in Luxembourg. Export receipts, including those in foreign currency from specialty tea and ceramic exports, may be retained, converted, or hedged freely. There is no mandatory conversion ratio or repatriation timeline imposed by the BCL.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL); Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for banking conduct and AML oversight; European Central Bank (ECB) for eurozone monetary policy

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with structural caveats. A Luxembourg-licensed payment institution (PI) or EEA-authorized electronic money institution (EMI) may technically open payment accounts for an SCSp, but many offshore digital EMIs decline partnership structures due to complex KYC, transparency, and regulatory perimeter concerns. Viability is improved when the EMI holds a valid EEA passport, executes full FATCA/CRS reporting, and accepts the SCSp as a non-resident-tax-transparent vehicle under Luxembourg binding tax clearance.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

SCSp is not registered at the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS), creating no public corporate record.

Root cause: SCSp is not registered at the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS), creating no public corporate record.

Defense evidence: Provide the notarised limited partnership agreement (LPA), a legal opinion confirming the non-registration is lawful for SCSp, identification of the general partner and its legal representatives, and notarised power-of-attorney appointing the account signatories.
Limited partners benefit from statutory anonymity, making percentage of ownership and control difficult to verify.

Root cause: Limited partners benefit from statutory anonymity, making percentage of ownership and control difficult to verify.

Defense evidence: Deliver a certified internal register of all limited partners, a CSSF-compliant beneficial ownership declaration (RBE form), an organisational chart showing 25% or greater ownership interests, and a Luxembourg legal opinion interpreting limited partner rights as purely economic and non-controlling.
Initial capital injection from non-EEA or high-risk jurisdictions is classified as possible wealth concealment.

Root cause: Initial capital injection from non-EEA or high-risk jurisdictions is classified as possible wealth concealment.

Defense evidence: Provide six months of bank statements of the contributing investor, source-of-wealth and source-of-funds documentation, warranty deeds, audited financial statements of the investor if applicable, and a certified transaction trail from the originating account to the Luxembourg escrow account.
Tax classification ambiguity of a pass-through SCSp triggers an incorrect FATCA/CRS self-certification.

Root cause: Tax classification ambiguity of a pass-through SCSp triggers an incorrect FATCA/CRS self-certification.

Defense evidence: Submit an advance tax clearance ruling or a letter from the Luxembourg direct tax administration confirming the SCSp is tax transparent, along with completed Form W-9/W-8BEN-E where required and a CRS self-certification identifying the general partner as the reporting entity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions from investors must be wired via SWIFT MT103 using a clear reference to the SCSp name and the LP agreement.
