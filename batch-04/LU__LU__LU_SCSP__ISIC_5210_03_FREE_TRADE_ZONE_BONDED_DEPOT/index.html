Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual/domiciliation-only office is legally acceptable for the SCSp itself as the statutory seat (siège statutaire), provided the registered address is maintained with a licensed fiduciary, support services provider (PFS/SPS), or authorized legal professional in Luxembourg. However, for the licensed bonded warehouse business (ISIC 5210, NAICS 493110), virtual substance is insufficient: customs authorities require a physically occupied, customs-approved storage facility (bonded depot) within a free trade zone in Luxembourg, with identifiable personnel, operational records and customs-controlled access; without such physical premises the customs authorisation under Union Customs Code Article 211 cannot be obtained.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an SCSp; partners freely fix contributions in the partnership agreement. Any contractually agreed capital must be fully paid per the agreement and, where required by the drafting parties, held in a blocked/escrow bank account until RCS registration; execution is by private written deed except for contributions of immovable property or real rights therein, which require a notarial deed.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety certification from CGDIS on fire load, escape routes and extinguishing installations; structural load-bearing inspection certificate for racking and storage platforms; occupational workplace clearance from ITM covering accident prevention, ventilation and workplace ergonomics.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an SCSp; partners freely fix contributions in the partnership agreement. Any contractually agreed capital must be fully paid per the agreement and, where required by the drafting parties, held in a blocked/escrow bank account until RCS registration; execution is by private written deed except for contributions of immovable property or real rights therein, which require a notarial deed.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des Contributions Directes (Direct Tax Authority) and Administration de l'Enregistrement et des Domaines / Administration de la TVA (VAT Authority) — Luxembourg; unified e-portal MyGuichet.lu

E-government filing portal

Non-resident entities without a fixed establishment (including SCSp) must register for VAT via the MyGuichet.lu e-portal (form 645/646) before supplying taxable goods/services in Luxembourg or engaged in intra-EU/direct exports. A national business registration number (numéro national) or, for non-residents, a temporary VAT number is issued on a ‘VAT-on-demand’ basis, normally within 2–5 business days, upon submission of the partnership deed, director/manager identification, and a statement of intended activities. For TIN (income tax) purposes, non-resident partners must file Luxembourg tax returns; the SCSp itself is transparent and does not obtain a separate income tax ID, except for registration for withholding tax procedures.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Non-resident entities without a fixed establishment (including SCSp) must register for VAT via the MyGuichet.lu e-portal (form 645/646) before supplying taxable goods/services in Luxembourg or engaged in intra-EU/direct exports. A national business registration number (numéro national) or, for non-residents, a temporary VAT number is issued on a ‘VAT-on-demand’ basis, normally within 2–5 business days, upon submission of the partnership deed, director/manager identification, and a statement of intended activities. For TIN (income tax) purposes, non-resident partners must file Luxembourg tax returns; the SCSp itself is transparent and does not obtain a separate income tax ID, except for registration for withholding tax procedures.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Luxembourg has no ‘check-the-box’ election. An SCSp is statutorily transparent for corporate income tax and net wealth tax; it may, however, elect to be treated as a separate VAT registered person (assujetti distinct) for value added tax purposes. Under ATAD/anti-hybrid rules, the SCSp may opt to designate its transparent status for Luxembourg purposes; the partners must align classification with treaty and EU hybrid mismatch rules to avoid disallowance or double-deduction outcomes. No other fiscal elections are available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

16%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover (excluding VAT) for Luxembourg-established small enterprises; for non-residents supplying goods/services in Luxembourg or importing, registration is required regardless of threshold, and for intra-Community distance selling the EUR 35,000 threshold applies per country.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAT) applies to: (a) dispatches of goods to destinations outside the European Union, provided the goods are physically exported from Luxembourg or from another EU Member State by the seller or on its behalf; (b) supplies of goods dispatched to another EU Member State (intra-Community supply) if the tax ID of the recipient is valid; (c) services directly related to the export of goods (handling, transport, loading) and certain offshore services. The SCSp, if VAT-registered, may rely on the buyer’s EU VAT number or customs documentation to substantiate the export. Goods stored in a customs free zone/bonded depot qualify for zero-rating upon actual removal to a third country, subject to delivery and documentary evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/Déclaration d'exportation with customs office stamp or AES electronic confirmation)
  • Bill of Lading / Airwaybill / CMR (transport contract evidencing physical movement)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (proof of payment received in foreign currency from the export sale)

No real-time fiscal clearance (CTC) or mandatory digital stamping regime is in force in Luxembourg. Electronic invoicing is permitted under EU Directive 2014/55 and Luxembourg VAT law, subject to authenticity of origin, integrity of content, and readability. B2B EDI or Peppol format (EN 16931) may be used. Cross-border B2B transactions require compliance with the EU CESOP reporting of payment data; from 1 January 2026, domestic B2B e-invoicing obligations will be phased in (Law of 15 July 2021 as amended). Until the mandate, paper or e-bills are equally accepted.

The Luxembourg VAT Administration can retrospectively disallow zero-rating if export evidence is incomplete or forged. Reclaimed VAT or refunds are clawed back with default interest (0.8% per month) and penalties of 10% up to 200% of evaded VAT for negligent or intentional infringement, plus fines under the General Tax Code (Abgabenordnung) of up to EUR 250,000 for documentary fraud. Criminal sanctions may include imprisonment for repeat fraudulent schemes. The standard control period is five years from the invoice due date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions under TFEU Article 63 and Eurosystem statutes. The SCSp may freely receive equity contributions in EUR or foreign currency, hold multi-currency accounts, and remit profits, dividends, or liquidation proceeds without prior exchange-control authorization. The only administrative obligations are (i) BCL balance-of-payments statistical declarations for cross-border flows exceeding EUR 12,500 and (ii) RBE beneficial-ownership registration with the Luxembourg Business Registers. No capital controls, ring-fencing, or minimum onshore retention periods apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, or onshore-repatriation timeline for export receipts. Proceeds from bonded warehouse services and FTZ re-export may be retained indefinitely in the original settlement currency or converted at the operator's discretion.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), acting as a Eurosystem member, with prudential oversight of credit institutions delegated to the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) and customs/fiscal control assigned to the Administration des douanes et accises.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low for principal treasury or clearing functions. Offshore digital EMIs without a full EU/EEA credit-institution licence or PSD2/EMD passporting cannot offer SWIFT MT103/MT700 settlement, T2 euro clearing, or commercial LC issuance. A licensed EEA EMI may serve only as an auxiliary non-bank channel for small-value disbursements and must not replace a CSSF-regulated credit institution for the SCSp's core accounts.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions route by SWIFT MT103 single credit transfer to the beneficiary account IBAN held at the selected tier-1 Luxembourg bank. The payment must identify the SCSp as beneficiary, reference the subscription or capital-injection instrument, and carry the ECB balance-of-payments classification code for partnership equity. EEA investors settle in EUR via Target2 (T2 dedicated cash account); non-EEA investors remit through the receiving bank's USD, CNY, or CHF correspondent (e.g., CHIPS/Fedwire, CIPS) and must append the intermediary BIC on the MT103. Verification requires a copy of the GP resolution, an updated register of contributions, and a simplified source-of-funds certificate for any single injection above EUR 100,000.Outbound treasury settlements use SWIFT MT103 for supplier, storage-fee, and duty payments, and SWIFT MT700 (documentary letter of credit) for procurement or customs-guarantee transactions. EUR-denominated SEPA Credit Transfers (SCT) settle same day in Target2; non-EUR disbursements clear via the correspondent banking network (USD via CHIPS/Fedwire, GBP via CHAPS, CNY via CIPS with Bank of China Luxembourg as intermediary). The SCSp should maintain a dedicated T2 liquidity buffer covering at least one month of customs-duty peaks and should, where trade volumes justify, open a CIPS/RMB clearing channel to support bonded re-export flows to Asia.Vanilla EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, and EUR/CHF FX forwards (standard 1M-12M tenor, extendable to 24M) executed under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with an investment-grade Luxembourg or EU bank counterparty; EMIR trade-reporting obligations apply.EUR/USD cross-currency basis swaps for hedging long-term USD-denominated warehouse assets or committed capital injections from non-EEA partners.FX swaps (swap de change) for T+2 or intraday rolling conversion of large USD/CHF receipts into EUR to support customs-guarantee top-ups.NDFs permissible only against restricted, non-convertible counterparty currencies (e.g., CNY, INR, BRL) arising from bonded re-export transactions, and only when documented as hedge instruments for commercially committed trade flows; NDFs on EUR are not market standard given full euro convertibility.
