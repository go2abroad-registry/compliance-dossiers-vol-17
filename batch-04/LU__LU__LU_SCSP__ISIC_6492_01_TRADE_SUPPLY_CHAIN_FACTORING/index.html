Not permissible as the sole substance for a Luxembourg SCSp engaging in accounts receivable factoring and reverse factoring. The SCSp must have a real registered office address in Luxembourg under Article 1167(3) of the 1915 Law, which cannot be a post-office box alone. Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés — RCS), operated by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) via the online portal www.lbr.lu.

Governing statute

Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (Loi modifiée du 10 août 1915 concernant les sociétés commerciales), in particular Articles 1, 2, 13-15 and 308-1 to 308-14 as introduced by the Law of 12 July 2013 creating the Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp); supplemented by the Law of 19 December 2002 on the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) and annual accounts.

Formation / constitutional filings

Registration declaration (déclaration de dépôt) lodged with the RCS, accompanied by the executed limited partnership agreement (acte de société) in notarial or private deed form; the SCSp has no separate legal personality, and RCS registration is required to render the partnership opposable to third parties.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 33 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for the SCSp; the general partner and limited partners freely determine capital and contributions in the partnership deed. No escrow capital account is legally mandated at formation, but client-factoring proceeds and any third-party funds must be segregated in a dedicated escrow account held with a Luxembourg credit institution under the AML/CFT framework of the amended Law of 12 November 2004.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety verification (certificat de sécurité incendie) issued by the Grand-Ducal Fire and Rescue Corps (Corps grand-ducal d'incendie et de secours — CGDIS); occupational health and workplace clearance by the Labour and Mines Inspectorate (Inspection du travail et des mines — ITM), including first-aid and emergency-evacuation compliance under Articles L.411-1 et seq. of the Luxembourg Labour Code; structural load acceptance certificate for the leased office premises; and accessibility verification under the Luxembourg construction regulations.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for the SCSp; the general partner and limited partners freely determine capital and contributions in the partnership deed. No escrow capital account is legally mandated at formation, but client-factoring proceeds and any third-party funds must be segregated in a dedicated escrow account held with a Luxembourg credit institution under the AML/CFT framework of the amended Law of 12 November 2004.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT, registration duties and indirect taxation; Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for income and corporate tax administration. Main portals: guichet.lu / MyGuichet, eTVA (AEDT VAT e-filing) and impotsdirects.public.lu.

E-government filing portal

For a Luxembourg SCSp: (i) draft the partnership agreement and file the vehicle with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) through a legal representative, obtaining the RCS matricule which functions as the primary national business/TIN registration number; (ii) lodge VAT registration with the AEDT through the eTVA portal if the SCSp or a non-resident partner suing through the SCSp supplies Luxembourg-taxable services or goods, using the standard Luxembourg VAT registration form and, where applicable, Form 102 for non-established taxable persons; (iii) appoint a Luxembourg VAT agent if the SCSp or foreign partner has no Luxembourg establishment and is established outside the EU; (iv) obtain an LU-format VAT identification number; direct-tax registration of the SCSp is for administrative purposes only because the SCSp is transparent and not itself a corporate income taxpayer.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — A Luxembourg SCSp is fiscally transparent by default. It is not subject to entity-level corporate income tax, municipal business tax or net wealth tax. Luxembourg-resident partners are taxed directly on their allocable share of SCSp income; non-resident partners are taxed only on Luxembourg-source income, subject to the applicable tax treaty and national anti-abuse rules.

Available election forms

Luxembourg does not operate a US-style check-the-box or elective entity classification regime. An SCSp is mandatorily fiscally transparent unless a specific anti-hybrid or ATAD-based rule recharacterises the vehicle. For VAT, however, Article 57 of the Luxembourg VAT Code permits an option to tax certain exempt financial transactions, including debt purchase and factoring/receivables assignment, where the customer is a taxable person and both parties agree, thereby allowing input VAT recovery. Luxembourg's anti-hybrid mismatch rules are mandatory and may deny deductions or require recharacterisation where the SCSp or a partner produces double deduction or deduction/income mismatches.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for domestically established small taxpayers; nil threshold for foreign suppliers making taxable supplies in Luxembourg; EUR 10,000 annual cross-border distance-selling/OSS threshold for intra-EU sales.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods physically leaving the EU customs territory may be zero-rated at 0% Luxembourg VAT provided the supplier holds valid proof of export and the goods are dispatched or transported out of the EU within the statutory time limits. Cross-border factoring and reverse-factoring services are not zero-rated as exports; they are either exempt financial services or, in the B2B export-related context, treated as supplied at the customer's place of business and therefore outside Luxembourg VAT or subject to reverse charge in the customer's jurisdiction. Export zero-rating is restricted to the underlying goods and directly connected ancillary export logistics services, not to the separate financing or factoring fee.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic Union Customs Code declaration with Movement Reference Number – MRN)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR transport document evidencing physical removal of the goods from the EU
  • Commercial invoice and export sales contract together with an Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT payment confirmation evidencing collection/remittance of the export proceeds

Luxembourg does not currently impose general B2B real-time e-invoicing or digital clearance/stamping. B2G e-invoicing is mandatory via PEPPOL. VAT returns and VIES recapitulative statements are filed electronically through the AEDT eTVA portal. A transition to European ViDA-reform digital reporting and real-time transaction controls is expected but is not effective as of the current compliance assessment date.

Unsupported or fraudulent export zero-rating gives rise to retrospective VAT assessment at 17%, statutory late interest running from the original due date, administrative fines, denial/clawback of VAT refunds, and criminal penalties in cases of deliberate tax evasion. Partners, managing parties and any professional intermediaries who knowingly participate in false export documentation may be held jointly and severally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg maintains full currency convertibility as a eurozone member. There are no exchange controls under the EU Treaty provisions on free movement of capital; both current and capital account transactions are unrestricted. Dividends, interest, royalty, and profit remittances from a Luxembourg Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp) to non-resident partners do not require exchange-control approval or a prior clearance certificate. However, the SCSp is fiscally transparent, and the competent tax authorities review substance and anti-abuse rules; profit distributions must comply with domestic partnership law and any applicable withholding tax on non-resident partner income if the SCSp were recharacterized as opaque, and with the anti-hybrid rules under ATAD. Capital repatriation of contributions and advances is freely permitted, but banks require proof of the underlying transaction and the beneficial owner's due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Luxembourg imposes no mandatory conversion, surrender, or repatriation timeline for export proceeds. Export receipts may be retained in any currency in settlement accounts. For statistical and balance-of-payments purposes, residents must report external financial transactions and asset-liability positions to the BCL, generally on a monthly basis when individual transaction amounts exceed EUR 12,500 or asset/liability thresholds are met. There is no statutory conversion ratio, and no requirement to convert trade receipts into euro within a specified number of days.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating within the eurosystem and under the European Central Bank's monetary framework; banking and financial conduct regulation is jointly administered by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for AML/CFT, prudential rules, and financial market supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Medium, subject to substantial legal constraints. A digital electronic-money institution (EMI) can legally serve the SCSp only if it holds a valid e-money licence in an EU/EEA member state and is passported to Luxembourg under the revised Payment Services Directive (PSD2), or holds a restricted licence from the CSSF. Offshore non-EEA digital EMIs cannot open accounts or provide payment facilities for the SCSp without a Luxembourg branch or agent with CSSF authorization. For the particular business of accounts-receivable factoring and reverse factoring, an EMI is generally unsuitable as the primary treasury and clearing counterparty because factoring involves credit extension, risk retention, and potentially regulated financial services. An EMI may provide only ancillary payment collection and disbursement functions within its e-money perimeter, provided there is no grant of credit or commercial lending. The SCSp should maintain its main treasury fortress corridors with Luxembourg commercial banks and use EMIs only for operational sub-account convenience in low-risk EEA collection corridors.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions to the SCSp should be routed by SWIFT MT103 or SEPA Credit Transfer using EU/Eurosystem-compliant protocols, referencing the SCSp's LEI and a clearly defined economic purpose code. For BCL balance-of-payments reporting, capital contributions are generally classified as direct investment with purpose code 'E020' (Direct investment in capital) or, for pure loan contributions, 'E031' or the corresponding debt-purpose code. The remittance instruction must specify the payee as the SCSp, include the correct settlement account name and IBAN, and carry a full cross-referencing narrative indicating 'capital contribution under partnership agreement dated [date]'. Prior to remittance, the bank will request the capital contribution notice, partnership agreement, proof of source of funds, and confirmation that the UBO files are up to date. For intra-EU contributions, SEPA mandates IBAN and no purpose code is mandatory, but Luxembourg banks will require a BCL declaration if the amount exceeds the reporting threshold. Immediate verification includes reconciliation of SWIFT/SEPA advice, matching to legal ledger entries, and retention of the bank's audit trail.For trade supply-chain factoring and reverse-factoring settlement, the primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for international wire transfers; MT202/202COV may be used for covering instructions in non-euro routes. For euro-denominated trade payments, SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) via STEP2/EBA Clearing is settlement-optimized; SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) may be used for small-value receivables. Trade settlements against documents may be executed through commercial letters of credit, where the issuing bank transmits an MT700 (issue LC) / MT707 (amendment) and the confirming bank effects payment under MT103. The SCSp should operate a separate trade-settlement account for outbound funding of purchased invoices and a collection account for the receipts. Each disbursement must be accompanied by the underlying assignment notice, invoice reference, purchase agreement/purchase order and clearance from sanctions/KYC.
