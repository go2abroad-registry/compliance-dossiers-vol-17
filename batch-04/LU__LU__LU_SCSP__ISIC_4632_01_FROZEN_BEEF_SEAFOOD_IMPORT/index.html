Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct tax identity; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT registration and compliance; unified portal at guichet.lu and MyGuichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

Prepare the SCSp partnership deed and register the SCSp in the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) through the RCS electronic portal; the RCS registration generates a corporate matricule number. The ACD subsequently allocates the national identification number (matricule d'identification) for income-tax purposes. Before any taxable import, apply to the AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT identification number using the RCS matricule and, if required, obtain an EORI number from Luxembourg Customs for the declaration of imports.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No election is available under Luxembourg law; an SCSp is statutorily transparent and cannot choose opaque corporate taxation. Foreign anti-hybrid rules may force a divergent classification, triggering Luxembourg ATAD and national anti-hybrid provisions, but that does not affect the SCSp's domestic pass-through status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Luxembourg imposes mandatory VAT registration for importers regardless of turnover. The general domestic VAT exemption for small enterprises applies only where annual taxable turnover remains below EUR 35,000; however, importation of goods and traders established outside Luxembourg cannot invoke this exemption and must register and declare import VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating is granted when a VAT taxable person supplies goods for dispatch or transport outside the European Union, the risk and ownership pass to the foreign principal, and the export is evidenced by competent documentation. For frozen beef and seafood products, zero rating requires the commodities entered for export to match the invoice and customs declaration exactly, and the export must be completed within the prescribed transport period. Among tied services, international freight and commission services can be zero-rated if covered by the same evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/EX) bearing the exportation roster or customs stamp
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent multimodal waybill demonstrating physical receipt in a third-country port
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT payment confirmation proving repatriation of the export proceeds

Luxembourg does not mandate real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital VAT stamping for businesses. E-invoicing is accepted if the invoice's authenticity and integrity are assured. VAT returns and recapitulative statements must be filed electronically through MyGuichet and ACD, while import VAT is assessed and paid via the EU computerised customs declaration system using the SAD format.

If the export evidence is not received or is found inadequate at audit, the tax authority retrospectively cancels the zero rating and substitutes the standard 17% VAT as at the supply date. Late-payment interest and tax penalties apply, ranging under the Luxembourg VAT law from administrative fines to double the undeclared tax in cases of intentional misstatement; customs law additionally imposes fines and criminal sanctions for false export declarations. The ordinary limitation period for reassessments is five years, extended in the event of fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The EUR is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. Luxembourg imposes no exchange controls and no prior-authorisation requirement on inbound or outbound transfers. For the tax-transparent SCSp, profit allocations to non-resident partners and liquidation proceeds can be remitted freely; no Luxembourg withholding tax arises unless a Luxembourg permanent establishment or deemed commercial activity exists. Only standard BCL statistical balance-of-payments reporting (via resident credit institutions) applies to external flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion regime exists in Luxembourg. Receipts in foreign currency (USD, GBP, NOK, BRL, ARS) may be held indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts. As this entity is an importer, no conversion obligation attaches; however, the bank must report each external conversion or transfer above the BCL de minimis threshold through the Eurosystem's external transaction statistics framework.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating in the Eurosystem under the European Central Bank (ECB); prudential banking and AML/CFT supervision delegated to the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW for the primary treasury and LC function. Luxembourg-licensed EMIs (CSSF, Law of 10 November 2009) can issue e-money and provide account-to-account transfers, and EEA-passported EMIs can operate cross-border, but none can issue documentary credits or provide pre-shipment commodity trade financing. For this frozen beef/seafood importer, an EMI is limited to ancillary EUR/USD spot conversions and small disbursements after the commercial LC has been settled; it cannot replace a tier-1 bank.
