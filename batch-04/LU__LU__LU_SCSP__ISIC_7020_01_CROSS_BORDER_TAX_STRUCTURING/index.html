Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
By unanimous written resolution of the board of managers of the SCSp and with the consent of the general partner, the following resolutions are adopted. First, the partnership agreement is approved as the constitutional instrument of the SCSp, and the object as set out therein, concerning cross-border tax structuring and transfer pricing advisory, is ratified. Second, subscriptions of the limited partners, including any contributions in kind, are accepted and recorded. Third, the registered office is fixed at the address stated in the agreement, within Luxembourg. Fourth, the general partner is confirmed as sole managing entity and its authorized signatories are appointed. Fifth, the authorized signatories are instructed to complete RCS registration, publication, and all applicable regulatory filings.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Luxembourg Inland Revenue (Administration des contributions directes - ACD) for direct taxes; Registration Duties, Estates and VAT Authority (Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA - AEDT) for VAT and indirect taxes. Digital services are accessible through the Guichet.lu portal and MyGuichet electronic filing system.

E-government filing portal

Entities incorporated or registered with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) automatically obtain a Luxembourg national identification number (NIF). Foreign entities establishing a permanent establishment or carrying on taxable activities must apply to the AEDT for a VAT identification number and to the ACD for income tax registration, generally using Luxembourg standard forms or MyGuichet. Non-resident entities may need to appoint a Luxembourg tax representative for VAT purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Entities incorporated or registered with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) automatically obtain a Luxembourg national identification number (NIF). Foreign entities establishing a permanent establishment or carrying on taxable activities must apply to the AEDT for a VAT identification number and to the ACD for income tax registration, generally using Luxembourg standard forms or MyGuichet. Non-resident entities may need to appoint a Luxembourg tax representative for VAT purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

The SCSp is fiscally transparent by law and cannot be classified as opaque for Luxembourg direct tax purposes except as explicitly provided under bilateral tax treaties or EU law. There is no U.S.-style check-the-box election. The SCSp may, however, elect to be part of a Luxembourg VAT group where all members satisfy the control and integration requirements. Partners may also make joint protective filings under EU hybrid mismatch rules to align the tax treatment of the partnership in cross-border structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for Luxembourg-established taxable persons after which VAT registration becomes mandatory; non-resident suppliers have no registration threshold and must register on their first taxable supply in Luxembourg.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The supply of goods is zero-rated for Luxembourg VAT when the goods are physically exported to a destination outside the European Union, provided the seller retains sufficient evidence of export within a statutory time limit. Services supplied to non-EU business customers are generally outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT where the place of supply is the customer's jurisdiction; services supplied to non-EU non-taxable persons may be zero-rated in defined circumstances. The exporter must keep proof of export and the foreign status of the customer.

  • Customs Export Declaration (exit summary declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or evidence of payment from abroad

Luxembourg does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping for VAT invoices. B2B and B2C e-invoicing is permitted provided the authenticity of origin and integrity of content are guaranteed. Electronic invoicing is mandatory for B2G supplies, with PEPPOL-compliant formats accepted. No country-wide API-based clearance system or digital fiscal stamping requirement is in force.

Export transactions are subject to enhanced documentary audit review. If zero-rating is claimed without genuine physical exports or corroborative documentation, the Luxembourg tax authorities may retrospectively claw back the input VAT deducted and issue an assessment for the unpaid VAT plus default interest at the statutory monthly rate of 0.6%. Wilful fraud may trigger administrative penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded, and criminal tax fraud sanctions may be imposed, including fines up to EUR 25,000 and/or imprisonment in aggravated cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg observes full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions under the EU Treaty provisions on free movement of capital and payments. Profit remittance by an SCSp is not subject to exchange-control approval or conversion requirements. Practical hurdles are limited to EU sanctions screening, AML/KYC documentation, economic substance disclosure, and accurate balance-of-payments statistical reporting; no distinct capital-control licensing regime applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rule or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Luxembourg. Export receipts may be retained in any freely convertible currency and used directly for cross-border disbursements. Reporting obligations arise only under the European System of Central Banks statistical framework and EU customs/BOP data collection rules; no central bank approval is required for repatriation or conversion.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (Central Bank of Luxembourg; BCL), acting as a member of the Eurosystem and as the competent authority for monetary policy and external statistics; the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) supervises banking conduct, AML/CFT compliance, and fintech/EMI licensing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-moderate with legal constraints. An EU-licensed electronic money institution (EMI) may passport into Luxembourg and offer payment accounts, but an offshore or non-EU EMI is generally not acceptable for institutional capital flows. CSSF-regulated banks require an SCSp to hold its core treasury accounts at a fully licensed credit institution in Luxembourg or in the EEA; EMIs can be used only for ancillary payment execution with segregated funds safeguarding.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Beneficial ownership opacity because SCSp is a limited partnership without legal personality and has limited-partner confidentiality.

Root cause: Beneficial ownership opacity because SCSp is a limited partnership without legal personality and has limited-partner confidentiality.

Defense evidence: Certified partnership agreement, register of partners, declaration of beneficial ownership filed with the Luxembourg Business Registers (RBE), capital contribution proof, and clear identification of all direct and indirect UBOs holding more than 25%.
Cross-border tax structuring consultancy revenue pattern triggers source-of-funds review, especially with credit-card receipts and intercompany fee flows.

Root cause: Cross-border tax structuring consultancy revenue pattern triggers source-of-funds review, especially with credit-card receipts and intercompany fee flows.

Defense evidence: Signed engagement letters, detailed invoices, timesheets, fee calculation reports, VAT returns, tax residence certificate, professional indemnity insurance, and clean source-of-wealth statements from foreign shareholders.
Perceived lack of economic substance due to no physical office or employees in Luxembourg.

Root cause: Perceived lack of economic substance due to no physical office or employees in Luxembourg.

Defense evidence: Lease agreement for a registered office or managed office, employment contracts, payroll filings with the Centre Commun de la Sécurité Sociale (CCSS), statutory board meeting minutes, and an operational schedule evidencing management decisions and filing obligations performed in Luxembourg.
Sanctions exposure arising from non-resident partners or complex offshore investor chains.

Root cause: Sanctions exposure arising from non-resident partners or complex offshore investor chains.

Defense evidence: Group structure chart, UBO passports, sanctions screening results against EU/UN/OFSI/OFAC lists, negative media checks, corporate registry extracts, and a documented AML/CFT compliance manual.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be routed through regulated bank wire protocols: SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA transfers and SEPA Credit Transfer for euro-denominated transfers from EEA jurisdictions. The remittance instruction must state the precise central-bank statistical purpose code identifying the flow as an equity contribution to partnership capital rather than a service or trade payment. Supporting documents should include the signed capital commitment, the partner subscription certificate, the SCSp partnership agreement, a capital account statement, and beneficial ownership declarations. Third-party funding and cryptocurrency transmission are prohibited.Outbound trade settlement from the Luxembourg SCSp treasury should use SEPA Credit Transfer for euro payments within the EEA and SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA or foreign-currency payments. Commercial letters of credit issued under UCP600 are permissible for goods-related cross-border settlements where the supplier requires documentary security.
