Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) — Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), Tribunal d'arrondissement de Luxembourg (commercial register greffe); official electronic gazette: RESA (Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations).

Governing statute

Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies (Loi modifiée du 10 août 1915 concernant les sociétés commerciales), Articles 944-1 to 944-14 (Société en Commandite Spéciale regime introduced by the Law of 23 July 2016, in force 14 August 2016), read with the general partnership provisions (Articles 688 et seq.); Law of 19 December 2002 on the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) and the Trade Register (RESA) and the Electronic Companies and Associations Gazette, as amended.

Formation / constitutional filings

Partnership agreement / instrument of incorporation ('contrat de société') in private writing or notarial deed, with the RCS standard data-capture form, the appointment and acceptance of the responsible person ('personne responsable') and signed powers of attorney; filed electronically via the e-RCS portal as the initial deposit ('1er dépôt').

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital applies to an SCSp (Article 944-2, Law of 10 August 1915); the partnership agreement freely fixes capital commitments. No mandatory bank escrow or capital blocking attestation is required for incorporation, although cash contributions must be paid on the SCSp's professional bank account to the extent provided in the agreement. The SCSp is not a UCI; CSSF prudential capital rules apply only if an external AIFM is appointed.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT and registration duties; Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for income taxes. Electronic portals: MyGuichet.lu and the Luxembourg Business Registers (lbr.lu) for entity registration; eTVA portal for VAT return filing.

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1 — Register the SCSp with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) through MyGuichet.lu/lbr.lu to obtain the RCSL registration number and the national identification matricule. Step 2 — File the VAT registration application (Formulaire 640) with the AEDT before commencing taxable supplies; the AEDT issues the Luxembourg VAT identification number in the format LU followed by 8 digits (e.g., LU12345678). Step 3 — Submit the RCS certificate, partnership agreement identifying the general partner, proof of Luxembourg seat, and anticipated turnover figures; non-resident partners are permitted and no tax representative is required where a Luxembourg-established general partner exists. Step 4 — Where applicable, register separately for the OSS (non-Union scheme) for EU B2C digital services and obtain an ACD income-tax file number (matricule fiscal) for the transparency reporting of partner allocations.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible; the SCSp is fiscally transparent by default for Luxembourg income tax and net wealth tax purposes — all income, gains, losses, and credits flow through to partners and are taxed at partner level. For VAT purposes, the SCSp is a distinct taxable person (assujetti) insofar as it carries out an economic activity such as helpdesk/SaaS services. Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Pass_Through_Eligible; the SCSp is fiscally transparent by default for Luxembourg income tax and net wealth tax purposes — all income, gains, losses, and credits flow through to partners and are taxed at partner level. For VAT purposes, the SCSp is a distinct taxable person (assujetti) insofar as it carries out an economic activity such as helpdesk/SaaS services. No entity-level territorial income taxation applies in the transparent regime; the place of effective management determines only Luxembourg procedural and filing obligations.

        
Available election forms
Available elections: (i) Article 175bis LIR option — the SCSp may elect, binding for five years, to be taxed as a Luxembourg société de capitaux for corporate income tax, municipal business tax, and net wealth tax, subject to the conditions of Article 175bis LIR and ACD guidance; (ii) VAT fiscal unity — election under Article 61 of the Loi TVA to form an assujetti unique with closely related Luxembourg entities; (iii) small-enterprise exemption waiver under Article 57 of the Loi TVA for turnover below EUR 35,000, unavailable for SaaS/helpdesk B2B supplies; (iv) hybrid mismatch documentation and neutralization elections under the ATAD 1/2 implementing law of 21 December 2018. Luxembourg does not operate a 'check-the-box' regime, but partners may file U.S. Form 8832 elections, which interact with Luxembourg tax transparency under reverse-hybrid rules.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Luxembourg is within the eurozone, and the euro is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. No Luxembourg exchange control regime restricts inward or outward capital movements. Profit remittances and distributions by an SCSp to non-resident partners are not subject to withholding tax, provided the partnership is properly registered and the economic substance and beneficial ownership documentation satisfy CSSF/EU anti-money laundering standards. Payment settlement in freely usable foreign currencies is permitted for trade and operating purposes.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
No statutory export proceeds surrender rule or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Luxembourg. Export or service receipts may be retained in any currency in domestic or foreign bank accounts. All cross-border wire transfers require exact purpose codes and comply with EU Regulation 2015/847 on information accompanying transfers of funds and with CSSF anti-money laundering reporting obligations.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Banque Centrale du Luxembourg (BCL); FX and payment regulation also coordinated with Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) and Ministère des Finances

Stage 06
Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

      
Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

    

    

      
Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)
0% at SCSp level (fiscally transparent partnership); each partner is taxed on its distributive share. If the SCSp exercises the option for corporate taxation, the aggregate Luxembourg CIT headline rate in Luxembourg City is 24.94% (15% IRC + 7% solidarity surcharge + 6.75% municipal business tax).

      
Dividend withholding tax (WHT)
15% statutory Luxembourg dividend withholding tax for corporate distributions. Distributions by a fiscally transparent SCSp are not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax at the partnership level; the 15% rate applies only where the SCSp has opted into corporate taxation or where a Luxembourg corporate partner redistributes dividends. EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive: 0%; LU treaty rates: generally 0%-5%.

    

    

      
Mandatory Return Breakdown

      
      

        
        
Deadline:  · Frequency: 

        
On or before 31 May following the calendar fiscal year-end; extendable to 31 December by the ACD upon request; non-calendar year-ends: within 6 months after year-end. Quarterly CIT advance payments fall due on 10 March, 10 June, 10 September and 10 December.

      

      
      

        
        
Deadline:  · Frequency: 

        
31 May following the tax year for resident individuals; extension to 31 December for taxpayers with commercial or partnership income when filed electronically; non-residents generally file Form 540A or a specific non-resident return with the same 31 May deadline.

      

      
      

        
        
Deadline:  · Frequency: 

        
Filing deadline is 5 years from the end of the calendar year in which the withholding tax was levied or the income became taxable; no refund is granted after the statute of limitations expires.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: EUR 50,000,000 group consolidated turnover triggers the Master File; Local File is mandatory where cross-border related-party transactions exceed EUR 100,000 per category (goods, services, financial transactions); Country-by-Country Report applies to groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750,000,000. A TP declaration must be attached to the annual tax return.

      
Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not apply a standalone Economic Substance Regulation (ESR) comparable to UAE, but enforces substance through Article 56bis LIR (arm's length), the judicial GAAR/abuse-of-law doctrine, the Law of 15 March 2020 and Grand-Ducal Regulation of 17 March 2020 on shell entities, DAC6 reporting, ATAD and MLI provisions. The SCSp must maintain real economic substance (local office, qualified helpdesk staff, board management in Luxembourg, commercial activities, and local decision-making); otherwise the ACD may recharacterise profits, deny treaty benefits, impose fines up to EUR 250,000 and apply shell-entity notification obligations.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax evasion and tax fraud (fraude fiscale)
        
Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties under Luxembourg tax and criminal law: imprisonment of 1 month to 5 years, fines up to 10x the evaded tax (statutory maximum EUR 1,000,000), restitution of evaded taxes, plus administrative surcharges of 10%-20% and 0.7% monthly default interest.

        
Director personal liability: The SCSp's general partner (commandité), jointly and severally liable for partnership debts under Art. 155-1 of the Law of 10 August 1915, may be ordered to pay civil restitution; individual managers risk travel bans, professional disqualification and imprisonment where fraud was ordered or tolerated.

      

      
      

        Shell company / absence of economic substance
        
Statutory sanction: Denial of DTA benefits, reversal of income allocation, application of Luxembourg GAAR, and administrative fines under the 15 March 2020 shell-entity law up to EUR 250,000 for failure to notify or to provide substance information.

        
Director personal liability: The general partner can be compelled to discharge the partnership's tax and social debts; directors knowingly designing shell structures face civil liability for the full tax shortfall and criminal prosecution for fraud.
