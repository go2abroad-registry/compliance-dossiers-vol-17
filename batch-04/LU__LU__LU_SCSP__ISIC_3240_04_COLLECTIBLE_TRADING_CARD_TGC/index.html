Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD) for direct tax; Administration de l'Enregistrement, des Domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; official digital portal: guichet.lu/myguichet.lu, tax filing through pfi.guichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

An SCSp is first registered with the Luxembourg Business Registers obtaining an RCS/matricule national identification number; for VAT, a non-established or EU supplier must file the Luxembourg VAT registration with AEDT via guichet.lu before making taxable supplies, submitting charter/By-Laws, registration certificate from home state, BO documentation, activity/estimated turnover, and appointing a fiscal representative when established outside the EU; VAT ID is assigned as LU + 8 digits and the entity is treated as a separate taxable person for VAT even though income-tax-transparent.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible - fiscally transparent for income tax purposes; partners are subject to tax in their respective jurisdictions; autonomous taxable person for VAT on supplies. — An SCSp is first registered with the Luxembourg Business Registers obtaining an RCS/matricule national identification number; for VAT, a non-established or EU supplier must file the Luxembourg VAT registration with AEDT via guichet.lu before making taxable supplies, submitting charter/By-Laws, registration certificate from home state, BO documentation, activity/estimated turnover, and appointing a fiscal representative when established outside the EU; VAT ID is assigned as LU + 8 digits and the entity is treated as a separate taxable person for VAT even though income-tax-transparent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - fiscally transparent for income tax purposes; partners are subject to tax in their respective jurisdictions; autonomous taxable person for VAT on supplies.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box is available; SCSp is statutorily pass-through. Available elections include: VAT grouping (assujetti unique), small-enterprise VAT exemption waiver, optional advance tax ruling confirming transparency/classification, and elections under local tax law affecting withholding/real-estate transactions; ATAD hybrid-mismatch rules constrain transactions that exploit double non-taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover (exclusive of VAT) for established small enterprises; non-established suppliers have no threshold once any taxable supply occurs in Luxembourg, subject to EU distance-selling/OSS thresholds and reverse-charge rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies where tangible collectible trading cards are sold by a Luxembourg taxable person and dispatched or transported to a destination outside the EU; the supply must be evidenced by a customs export declaration naming the supplier/exporter, and the complete chain of commercial, transport and payment evidence must show removal from the EU; directly connected transport, ancillary and offshore processing services may also qualify if separately supplied and documented.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/electronic export declaration accepted by EU/Luxembourg Customs)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing EU port/airport of departure
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence (SWIFT/MT103) proving export receivable
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and export contract
  • Transport documents (e.g., CMR consignment note) or courier's proof of delivery to non-EU address

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime is in force. Mandatory e-invoicing applies only for B2G/public-procurement invoices (Peppol/EN 16931); B2B e-invoicing is optional; all invoices must satisfy EU invoicing rules and VAT records must be retained for a statutory period.

Luxembourg tax authorities may retrospectively disallow zero-rating and reassess VAT, with default interest, where export evidence is defective or payment is not received within prescribed periods. Deliberate falsification of export documents exposes the taxpayer to administrative fines, criminal sanctions (fines and/or imprisonment) for fraud, extended limitation periods, and possible joint liability of directors/representatives; VAT credits claimed on exports are subject to pre-refund audit.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a founding member of the Eurosystem; the Euro is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts pursuant to Article 63 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. There are no exchange control approvals, no central bank licensing for capital movements, and no deposit or repatriation requirements for inbound or outbound flows. Profit and dividend remittance from a Luxembourg SCSp is unrestricted; because the SCSp is fiscally transparent for Luxembourg corporate income tax purposes, no Luxembourg withholding tax is levied on distributions to non-resident partners, provided substance, partnership deed, and transfer pricing documentation substantiate the distribution. Permanent establishment or taxable presence in other jurisdictions is the only material constraint.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory FX conversion rules apply in Luxembourg or anywhere in the Eurosystem. The printing and exporting entity is free to hold export proceeds in EUR or in any foreign currency in licensed Luxembourg bank accounts without conversion, repatriation, or declaration obligations beyond standard BCL balance-of-payments statistical reporting by the bank. For ISIC 3240 exports, EU customs export declarations (SAD/EXA and exit summary declarations) are required, but these are customs formalities and do not impose FX surrender.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) in coordination with the European Central Bank (ECB); prudential banking supervision is delegated to the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-to-Low as a primary treasury rail. A CSSF-licensed payment institution or e-money institution under PSD2 may open payment accounts, execute SEPA/SWIFT payments, and provide card acquiring settlement for Stripe/Authorize.Net flows, but EMIs are not full credit institutions: they cannot grant credit, do not hold deposit-guarantee coverage, and must safeguard client funds in segregated accounts. Luxembourg EMIs and passporting EU EMIs frequently apply stricter risk appetite to SPV/SCSp structures and collectible goods trading; therefore an EMI is viable only as a non-exclusive collection/disbursement rail with capped balances, while all capital escrow, FX, and settlement liquidity must sit with a Tier-1 credit institution.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Layered SCSp partnership with undisclosed limited partners; the bank cannot identify underlying natural-person UBOs above or below the 25% threshold.

Root cause: Layered SCSp partnership with undisclosed limited partners; the bank cannot identify underlying natural-person UBOs above or below the 25% threshold.

Defense evidence: Full partnership deed (acte constitutif and status), up-to-date extract from the Luxembourg Registre des Bénéficiaires Effectifs (RBE), organisational chart tracing every natural-person UBO, signed UBO attestation on the CSSF standard template, and a statutory declaration by the general partner confirming no bearer shares, no nominee partners, and no undisclosed trusts.
Insufficient economic substance: SCSp is a mailbox vehicle with no local office, no local management, and no real operational activity, triggering CSSF and bank anti-shell red flags.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

No BCL or ministry authorisation is required for inbound capital into a Luxembourg SCSp. The compliant routing is: (i) the contributing partner executes a capital call/injection notice under the partnership deed; (ii) the remitter sends a SWIFT MT103 in EUR (or USD via a correspondent EUR leg) to the Luxembourg IBAN, with field 70 stating 'SCSp capital contribution – [deed reference]' and using the bank's standard purpose code for capital payments (e.g., CAPA/INVS); (iii) the receiving bank performs sanctions screening on the remitter and UBO before provisional credit; (iv) the full KYC/UBO file and source-of-funds evidence are delivered to the compliance desk; (v) after final booking, the SCSp issues a capital contribution receipt, updates the RBE register if any partner holding changes, and records the contribution in the annual accounts. For non-EUR injections, the receiving bank may offer an uncontracted FX conversion into EUR at the prevailing mid-rate; this is optional and does not alter the capital-contribution status.For trade settlements to foil, holographic-material, and printing suppliers: (i) EUR payments inside the EEA use SEPA Credit Transfer (ISO 20022 pain.001) with clearing via TARGET2 and next-day settlement; (ii) non-EEA payments use SWIFT MT103 wires with invoice/PO references in field 70 and correspondent-bank settlement in the beneficiary currency; (iii) large or risky documentary purchases use a commercial Letter of Credit confirmed through a Luxembourg Tier-1 bank, transmitted by SWIFT MT700/MT710, and supplemented by SWIFT MT760 standby guarantees where advance payment is required; (iv) e-commerce collections from Stripe and Authorize.Net are settled to the treasury account via SEPA or SWIFT in periodic payouts, after card acquirer netting; (v) the blueprint segregates partner capital in an escrow sub-account, trade cashflow in an operational account, and FX conversion in a dedicated settlement account to produce a clean audit trail for BCL statistical reporting and annual audit.EUR/USD FX Forwards (plain vanilla, bilateral under ISDA/CSA with a Tier-1 Luxembourg or EU credit institution)EUR/GBP FX Forwards for UK distributor and licensee settlementsFX Swaps (spot-next and tom-next rollovers on revenue conversion accounts to manage settlement timing)Cross-Currency Swaps for long-dated USD receivables, provided EMIR clearing thresholds are not exceeded and collateral arrangements are documentedNDFs for non-convertible or restricted supplier/distributor currencies (e.g., select Asian and Latin American currencies), executed offshore through an institution authorised to offer NDFs to EU corporates and subject to EMIR margining and reporting
