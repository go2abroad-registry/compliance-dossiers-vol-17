Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital; freedom of contribution (cash, in-kind or industry) as set in the partnership deed; paid-up amount is agreed contractually and not lodged in escrow; each limited partner's liability is capped at the amount of its agreed contribution.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance from the Administration des services de secours (CGDIS) for commercial premises, including emergency exits, extinguishers, smoke detection and evacuation plan; occupational health and safety inspection by the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) covering workstations (screens, ergonomic chairs, lighting) and electrical safety verified by an approved controller; structural load check of building floors if heavy render servers are installed; completion of a workplace risk assessment in accordance with Article L.312-1 of the Luxembourg Labour Code.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital; freedom of contribution (cash, in-kind or industry) as set in the partnership deed; paid-up amount is agreed contractually and not lodged in escrow; each limited partner's liability is capped at the amount of its agreed contribution.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; official portals MyGuichet.lu / Guichet.lu and electronic filing platform Edp-tax.lu.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned SCSp engaged in 3D animation, the partnership must first be registered with the Luxembourg Business Registers (RCSL) to obtain its national identification number (matricule); no minimum capital, tax clearance, or prior industry license is required. If the SCSp makes supplies with a Luxembourg VAT place of supply, it must apply separately to the AEDT for a Luxembourg TVA identification number using the standard registration procedure; a non-established taxable person may be required to appoint a Luxembourg tax representative for VAT. The SCSp is fiscally transparent for direct tax, so partners obtain or update their Luxembourg tax files with the ACD and report their distributive shares in their respective tax returns.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — For a foreign-owned SCSp engaged in 3D animation, the partnership must first be registered with the Luxembourg Business Registers (RCSL) to obtain its national identification number (matricule); no minimum capital, tax clearance, or prior industry license is required. If the SCSp makes supplies with a Luxembourg VAT place of supply, it must apply separately to the AEDT for a Luxembourg TVA identification number using the standard registration procedure; a non-established taxable person may be required to appoint a Luxembourg tax representative for VAT. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No domestic 'check-the-box' regime exists in Luxembourg. An SCSp is transparent by default; it may elect VAT grouping ('assujetti unique') under Article 60 of the Luxembourg VAT Law, may waive the EUR 35,000 small-enterprise exemption, and may request an advance tax ruling from the ACD to confirm its transparent treatment. Cross-border hybrid mismatches are governed mandatorily by Luxembourg's ATAD implementation (law of 29 March 2019) and no separate domestic opacity election is available for a standard SCSp.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for established taxable persons under the small-enterprise exemption; non-established suppliers have no domestic turnover threshold and must register from the first taxable supply with a Luxembourg place of supply, subject to OSS and distance-selling special schemes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical exports, the 0% export VAT rate applies only where tangible goods are dispatched or transported outside the EU by the supplier or on its behalf and the supplier holds documentary proof of departure. For commercial 3D animation and VFX services, if the customer is established outside the EU, the place of supply of the B2B service is outside Luxembourg and the supply is not taxable in Luxembourg; B2C digital supplies to non-EU consumers likewise fall outside Luxembourg VAT. The SCSp may recover attributable input VAT to the extent allowed under Luxembourg deduction rules and must retain records evidencing the non-EU customer's identity, establishment, and delivery/receipt of the service.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic Single Administrative Document / EXS) for physical goods; for digital animation deliverables, equivalent service export evidence comprising contract, delivery/IP transfer logs and customer tax registration
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or CMR/other transport document proving departure of goods from the EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or matched SWIFT/bank credit evidence showing payment from the non-EU customer
  • Commercial invoice, purchase order, packing list and non-EU customer VAT/tax identification

Luxembourg does not currently impose real-time fiscal clearance, API-based reporting, or digital stamping for B2B VAT invoices. VAT invoices must be issued no later than the 15th day of the month following the taxable supply and must satisfy the content requirements of the Luxembourg VAT Law; e-invoicing is accepted without prior approval. In the public-procurement context, e-invoices must comply with EN 16931 and may be exchanged through Peppol. The EU VAT in the Digital Age framework is expected to introduce mandatory e-invoicing and digital transaction reporting for Luxembourg taxpayers from 2030.

AEDT and ACD auditors may require production of the complete export evidence set within the ordinary five-year assessment period, extended to ten years in fraud cases. If export evidence is missing, false, or the goods/services are subsequently diverted or used in the EU, the claimed 0% treatment is reversed and Luxembourg VAT becomes payable at 17.0%, together with statutory late interest and administrative penalties. Fraudulent zero-rating may trigger criminal prosecution with fines up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment, and may disqualify the entity from future zero-rating or input VAT recovery.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg maintains full current and capital account convertibility, with no exchange controls or capital movement restrictions other than EU sanctions and Funds Freezing Regulations. Profit remittance is freely permitted without withholding for distributions to eligible EU/EEA and treaty partners, subject to anti-abuse provisions and substance requirements. For a pass-through SCSp, no entity-level tax is imposed; distributions follow the underlying partner fiscal attributes and treaty protections, so no convertibility hurdle exists for legally documented commercial profit transfers in euro or other G10 currencies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion timetable exists under Luxembourg law. Exporters may retain receipts in any eligible currency, hold them freely on non-resident-capable accounts, and settle commercial liabilities exclusively in contractual currency. Only BCL statistical reporting thresholds (balance-of-payments categories, usually monthly reporting if cumulative transactions exceed EUR 250,000 or on declaration request) and EU sanctions screening obligations apply.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) together with Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for payment and banking supervision; exchange of reporting data via the BCL's statistical balance of payments declarations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is Medium-High only when the EMI holds a valid EU/EEA passport under PSD2 and is registered with the CSSF. Luxembourg-established licensed EMI entities such as those under the law of 21 December 2023 on payment institutions and electronic money institutions offer the highest viability for an IP-heavy 3D animation SCSp. Fully offshore EMIs that lack a physical EU establishment, are not wallet-eligible under the local AML law, or cannot support a legal capital escrow arrangement are rated Low viability because they trigger higher concentration risk and may reject service to a partnership structure without an established track record.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound transfers are executed via SWIFT MT103 with the BCL balance-of-payments code 'P' (Foreign direct investment in Luxembourg) when capital is injected in the form of partnership capital. Purpose codes must be stated as 'Capital subscription / increase' or equivalent; the account reference must be the partner's capital account identification and the SCSp's RCS number. Banks require verification of origin of funds via anti-money laundering standard, including the underlying partnership agreement, notarized capital increase minutes, and Luxembourg tax form 570 if a UK or other treaty jurisdiction is involved. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% aggregate Luxembourg City headline rate (CIT 17% + solidarity surcharge 1.19% + municipal business tax 6.75%); fiscally transparent SCSp is exempt from CIT at the partnership level.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% domestic rate; reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive and domestic participation exemption for qualifying holdings (>=10% or >=EUR 1.2M, 12 months). Distributions from a fiscally transparent SCSp are profit allocations, not dividends, and are generally outside Luxembourg WHT.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Statutory deadline: 31 March following the fiscal year-end. Automatic extension to 31 December of the following calendar year when filed electronically via eTVA.

Late filing: administrative fine of EUR 25 to EUR 2,500 per return; default interest of 0.6% per month on underpaid tax; forced assessment (taxation d'office) with a 10% mark-up over the assessed tax; coercive fine up to EUR 25,000 for continued non-compliance after formal notice.
Box 2 (Renseignements sur la société) Legal form (SCSp), partnership agreement date, general partner identity, activity code (ISIC 5911 animation/VFX production), number of employees, substance indicators (premises, payroll, bank account). Non-resident partners must not be listed as corporate taxpayers in this section; the SCSp acts only as reporting intermediary. Late filing: administrative fine of EUR 25 to EUR 2,500 per return; default interest of 0.6% per month on underpaid tax; forced assessment (taxation d'office) with a 10% mark-up over the assessed tax; coercive fine up to EUR 25,000 for continued non-compliance after formal notice.
Box 3 (Bénéfice imposable / Taxable profit) Computation of partnership-level taxable income: gross animation service revenue, direct production costs, salaries, depreciation, amortization of 3D character/IP, and net business result per Luxembourg tax accounting rules. Allocate taxable income to each partner according to the partnership agreement share; income of non-resident limited partners without a Luxembourg PE is generally outside Luxembourg tax jurisdiction. Late filing: administrative fine of EUR 25 to EUR 2,500 per return; default interest of 0.6% per month on underpaid tax; forced assessment (taxation d'office) with a 10% mark-up over the assessed tax; coercive fine up to EUR 25,000 for continued non-compliance after formal notice.
Box 4 (Pertes reportables / Losses carried forward) Carryforward losses of the partnership, capped at 17% of the taxable base exceeding EUR 25,000 per partner per year. Non-resident partners claim the loss allocation in their residence country only; Luxembourg does not grant personal loss relief to non-residents absent a PE. Late filing: administrative fine of EUR 25 to EUR 2,500 per return; default interest of 0.6% per month on underpaid tax; forced assessment (taxation d'office) with a 10% mark-up over the assessed tax; coercive fine up to EUR 25,000 for continued non-compliance after formal notice.
Box 5 (Répartition aux associés / Allocation statement) Annex listing each partner: full name, address, tax residence, profit share, allocated taxable income, and any EU/domestic withholding credit. Attach Form 61 residence certificates of all non-resident partners; reduced treaty rates or exemption are applied on that basis. Late filing: administrative fine of EUR 25 to EUR 2,500 per return; default interest of 0.6% per month on underpaid tax; forced assessment (taxation d'office) with a 10% mark-up over the assessed tax; coercive fine up to EUR 25,000 for continued non-compliance after formal notice.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File and Local File mandatory where the group's consolidated annual turnover exceeds EUR 750,000,000 and intra-group cross-border transactions exceed: EUR 500,000 (financial transactions), EUR 250,000 (intangible/IP transactions), or EUR 1,000,000 (goods and services). Country-by-Country Report threshold: consolidated turnover above EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: No standalone Economic Substance Regulations are enacted in Luxembourg, but substance is decisive: the ATAD-derived General Anti-Abuse Rule (Art. 6a StAnpG), the OECD MLI Principal Purpose Test, and the abuse-of-law doctrine can deny treaty benefits, recharacterize financing, and void arrangements lacking economic substance. An SCSp producing 3D animation must demonstrate real activities (production staff, servers/workstations, management decisions, Luxembourg bank account, executed client contracts) to preserve pass-through treatment and treaty access.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Tax Fraud (Fraude Fiscale)

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to EUR 250,000 (aggravated fraud: up to twice the evaded tax) and imprisonment of 8 days to 5 years; reassessment at the 24.94% aggregate rate; default interest at 0.6% per month; publication of the conviction.

Director personal liability: General partner (gérant) of the SCSp is jointly and severally liable without limit; directors incur personal criminal fines, confiscation orders, travel bans, extradition exposure, and imprisonment up to 5 years.
Failure to File and Administrative Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Coercive fine up to EUR 25,000; forced assessment (taxation d'office) with 10% mark-up; default interest at 0.6% per month; loss of deductions and loss carryforward; denial of foreign tax credits.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner may be held personally liable for the corporate tax debt where the failure results from serious mismanagement (Art. 510-1 LSC); risk of bankruptcy clawback and management prohibition.
Shell Company and Substance Failure (GAAR / Treaty Abuse)

Statutory sanction: Application of Art. 6a StAnpG and OECD PPT: profits recharacterized and taxed at the partner level, treaty benefits denied, additional tax assessments with 0.6% monthly interest and penalty surcharges up to 10%; potential criminal referral for fraud.

Director personal liability: Corporate veil pierced where the SCSp is a sham; limited partners lose limited-liability protection if they participate in management; general partner retains unlimited personal liability; residence-state tax authorities impose secondary adjustments and travel bans.
AML, Beneficial Ownership and Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: RBE non-compliance: administrative fines up to EUR 125,000. Money laundering: imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 1,000,000 or twice the value of the laundered property; freezing orders and confiscation of the 3D animation assets and IP.

Director personal liability: Directors and AML compliance officers personally liable for fines; travel bans, house arrest, and prohibition from serving as directors; personal criminal conviction and extradition under mutual legal assistance.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: ACD TP adjustment reallocating profit to the SCSp; additional CIT assessed at 24.94%; 0.6% monthly late interest; 10% penalty surcharge; adjusted amounts recharacterized as constructive distributions and subject to 15% WHT where applicable.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign inaccurate TP documentation face personal fines for false declarations; general partner exposure extends to the full underpaid tax plus penalties; criminal referral in cases of deliberate documentation forgery.
