Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Luxembourg: Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for income tax and Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; consolidated filings via MyGuichet.lu and eC.A.T.

E-government filing portal

A Luxembourg-registered SCSp obtains a national identification number (NID) automatically upon RCS registration. For VAT, the SCSp must file form 3 (VAT registration) with the AEDT, submit its commercial registry extract and articles of association, and identify its partners/activities. Non-resident partners must provide their foreign VAT/TIN identifiers; the VAT number is issued electronically through eC.A.T within 3-5 weeks.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box system in Luxembourg; SCSp is statutorily fiscally transparent. For VAT, the transparent partnership may elect to be treated as an independent taxable person for activities carried out in its own name. Hybrid mismatch rules require aligned elections with foreign counterparty classifications under EU ATAD 2016/1164 provisions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual turnover for domestic exemption; no threshold for non-resident suppliers or importers to register (mandatory registration regardless of turnover when acquiring intra-EU goods or providing B2C digital services).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods outside the EU under customs procedures, including TIR transit, are zero-rated when ownership transfers to a non-EU customer and transport outside the EU is evidenced. International transport of goods from Luxembourg to non-EU destinations and linked loading/unloading services are zero-rated if covered by a CMR/TIR Carnet and the service is directly linked to the exported goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/EX-A and NCTS TIR electronic declaration)
  • Bill of Lading / CMR Consignment Note
  • Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing foreign-currency proceeds

B2G e-invoicing is mandatory in Peppol/UBL formats; B2B e-invoicing is not yet mandatory. No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping exists. VAT returns and lists, including EC Sales Lists, must be filed via eC.A.T.; e-invoices must be retained for 6 years.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating triggers retroactive VAT collection plus late interest at 5% per year (or higher), fines up to EUR 500,000, administrative sanctions, seizure of goods/vehicles, and criminal prosecution for VAT fraud under articles 51-53 of the Luxembourg VAT law; the tax authorities may apply joint liability to the SCSp and its partners where zero-rating is abused.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible. Luxembourg maintains unrestricted free movement of capital and payments under Articles 63 and 64 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and imposes no foreign-exchange controls, conversion licensing, or central bank approval for current or capital account transactions. Profit remittance is commercially available through normal bank transfers; for an SCSp, distributions are treated as partnership withdrawals rather than taxable dividends because the entity is fiscally transparent, and no Luxembourg withholding tax is levied on such distributions. Remittance is nevertheless subject to standard anti-abuse, documentation, and EU/CFSP sanctions screening requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg has no statutory obligation to surrender, convert, or repatriate export receipts within a mandatory timeline. TIR carnet revenues may be credited directly and in full to the SCSp's euro treasury account. The only relevant customs obligation is the orderly discharge of the TIR carnet within the prescribed transit deadlines through the national guaranteeing chain.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating within the Eurosystem; banking conduct, AML/CFT supervision, and payment institution licensing are supervised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF). Luxembourg does not maintain a separate foreign-exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Low for an unlicensed offshore digital EMI; Medium-to-High for an EEA-authorised e-money institution or payment institution that has completed a PSD2 passport notification with the CSSF. Legal constraints: only a licensed credit institution, e-money institution, or payment institution may issue electronic money, hold commercial payment accounts, or initiate SEPA and SWIFT MT103 transactions. An offshore EMI without an EU passport cannot lawfully provide Luxembourg IBANs or process treasury payments; sub-licensing is prohibited, and any arrangement using unregulated payment intermediaries would fail AML/CFT due diligence.

For non-EUR transfers, execute an explicit FX spot or forward with the receiving bank before remittance. The receiving bank may require the partnership agreement, UBO declarations, and a record of contributing partners to satisfy CSSF AML duties.
