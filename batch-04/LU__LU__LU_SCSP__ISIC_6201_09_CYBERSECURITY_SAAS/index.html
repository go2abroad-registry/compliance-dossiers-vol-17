Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés - RCS), operated by the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR); electronic filings through the eJustice.public.lu portal.

Governing statute

Amended Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies (Loi du 10 août 1915 concernant les sociétés commerciales), as amended by the Law of 12 July 2013 introducing the Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp); applicable provisions Articles 20-1 to 20-8 of the 1915 Law; supplementary RCS filing and publication regime under the amended Law of 19 December 2002 on the Register of Commerce and Companies.

Formation / constitutional filings

SCSp Partnership Agreement / Constitutive Deed (Contrat de société / Acte de constitution), executed either as a notarized deed (acte notarié) or as a private writing (acte sous seing privé) with certified signatures; accompanied by the RCS incorporation e-form.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 25 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No legal minimum capital and no escrow (blocage) account requirement for the SCSp. Contributions are freely fixed in the partnership agreement, may be cash, in-kind, or in industry/services for the general partner, and are payable per the agreed contribution schedule; registration does not require evidence of capital paid.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety conformity certificate for office premises issued by the municipal authority after joint inspection with the Grand-Ducal Fire and Rescue Corps (CGDIS), including escape-route plans and fire-extinguisher maintenance; occupational safety registration with the ITM covering electrical installations, VDU workstation compliance, and, where staff are engaged, a health-and-safety coordination dossier, first-aid provisions, and accident declaration protocols.

Paid-in capital rule: No legal minimum capital and no escrow (blocage) account requirement for the SCSp. Contributions are freely fixed in the partnership agreement, may be cash, in-kind, or in industry/services for the general partner, and are payable per the agreed contribution schedule; registration does not require evidence of capital paid.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable — office-based ISIC 6201 cybersecurity SaaS is not an industrial pursuit requiring an industrial establishment permit. Reference authority for regulated professions (none triggered): Ministry of the Economy — Directorate-General for SMEs and Digitalisation (autorisation d'établissement regime).

Category: No operating license (autorisation d'établissement) is required for cloud vulnerability scanning and penetration-testing SaaS; the profession is not listed as a regulated trade, craft, or retail activity under the amended Law of 2 September 2011 on access to professions.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD), the Luxembourg Direct Tax Administration, for corporate and municipal business tax; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT and indirect tax registration. Primary digital portals: MyGuichet.lu for entity registration and filings, eTVA for VAT returns, and the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR).

E-government filing portal

A Luxembourg SCSp obtains its national business matricule (11-digit statutory TIN) upon incorporation and registration with the Luxembourg Business Registers, effected through a notary or an authorized MyGuichet.lu filer. The AEDT subsequently issues the VAT identification number in the format LU plus 10 digits upon VAT registration or on commencement of taxable activity. Non-resident ownership is unrestricted and no minimum capital is required. A foreign entity not otherwise established in Luxembourg but making taxable supplies there applies directly to the AEDT for a VAT number, with no local turnover threshold; documentation required includes proof of identity of the promoters, description of the planned activity (ISIC/NACE), and evidence of foreign establishment.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A Luxembourg SCSp obtains its national business matricule (11-digit statutory TIN) upon incorporation and registration with the Luxembourg Business Registers, effected through a notary or an authorized MyGuichet.lu filer. The AEDT subsequently issues the VAT identification number in the format LU plus 10 digits upon VAT registration or on commencement of taxable activity. Non-resident ownership is unrestricted and no minimum capital is required. A foreign entity not otherwise established in Luxembourg but making taxable supplies there applies directly to the AEDT for a VAT number, with no local turnover threshold; documentation required includes proof of identity of the promoters, description of the planned activity (ISIC/NACE), and evidence of foreign establishment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Default treatment: the SCSp is fiscally transparent; income, gains, deductions, and credits are allocated directly to partners, and no Luxembourg CIT or municipal business tax is levied at the entity level. Statutory options include (i) an opaque-taxation election under Article 175 LITL, as amended by the Law of 21 December 2017, permitting the partnership to elect to be assessed as a Luxembourg resident company for CIT and municipal business tax purposes, subject to anti-hybrid and ATAD constraints; (ii) an election to acquire legal personality under Articles 944-1 et seq. of the Law of 10 August 1915, converting the SCSp into a Société en commandite simple and thereby changing tax classification and treaty entitlement analysis; and (iii) the domestic Luxembourg anti-hybrid mismatch rules, which condition or deny double-dip outcomes in cross-border structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 in annual taxable turnover for Luxembourg-established suppliers under the small-business exemption in Article 57 Loi TVA; registration is compulsory once the threshold is exceeded or upon first taxable transaction. No registration threshold applies to non-established foreign suppliers, although supplies to Luxembourg VAT-registered customers may fall under the reverse-charge mechanism, and cross-border B2C digital services may be declared through the Union or non-Union OSS rather than through full local VAT registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of cybersecurity SaaS services (cloud vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, and compliance software) by a Luxembourg supplier to a taxable customer established outside Luxembourg are subject to 0% Luxembourg VAT because the place of supply is the customer's country under Article 44 of Directive 2006/112/EC and Article 58 Loi TVA. Eligibility requires the supplier to obtain and validate the customer's EU VAT identification number for intra-Community B2B supplies, or to preserve written and commercial evidence of the customer's overseas establishment for non-EU customers; contracts, delivery reports, system/IP logs, payment routing, and service-use data must demonstrate that the services are not effectively enjoyed in Luxembourg. B2C supplies of digital services to non-resident consumers are taxable at the consumer's location via the OSS route, not in Luxembourg. Physical exports of goods would separately require customs export clearance; this does not apply to the pure SaaS service line.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, government API telemetry, or digital stamping requirement currently applies to B2B or B2C VAT in Luxembourg; paper and PDF invoices remain valid subject to the authenticity and integrity requirements of Articles 71 et seq. Loi TVA. B2G e-invoicing is mandatory for Luxembourg public procurement under the Law of 2 March 2022 transposing Directive 2014/55/EU, using the PEPPOL/EN 16931 format. Cross-border EU data exchange runs through the VIES framework; the EU ViDA initiative will progressively introduce standardized e-invoicing and digital reporting, but no national pre-clearance mandate exists today.

An unjustified claim of the 0% rate is recharacterized as a Luxembourg taxable supply at 17% VAT. The AEDT may reassess within the statutory limitation period, which is extended in cases of fraud or intent, with statutory late-payment interest, forfeiture of input VAT credits, administrative fines under the Loi TVA penalty scale, and criminal prosecution, including fines and imprisonment, for willful evasion. The taxpayer bears the burden of proof; missing, contradictory, or untraceable substantiation, whether customs declarations, bills of lading, remittance certificates, or contractual and technical delivery evidence, invalidates the zero-rating and triggers retrospective clawback of VAT together with accumulated interest and penalties.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg maintains full convertibility on both current and capital account transactions. The euro is a freely floating convertible currency. No exchange control restrictions or prior approval requirements apply to outbound profit remittance, dividends, interest, or royalties, subject to EU/UN financial sanctions and the general anti-abuse rules. Non-resident shareholders may freely receive distributions from a Luxembourg SCSp, and applicable withholding taxes are mitigated by the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive and tax treaties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory repatriation requirement exists under Luxembourg law. Exporters may retain foreign currency balances indefinitely and convert them as needed at market rates.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) — the Luxembourg central bank, operating within the Eurosystem and under the Single Supervisory Mechanism (SSM) for credit institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium for strategic treasury use. EEA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and Payment Institutions can passport into Luxembourg under PSD2 and may provide an operational payment account for a SCSp. However, offshore digital EMIs outside the EU/EEA cannot passport, and UK-licensed EMIs have lost EU passporting rights post-Brexit. Legal constraints include limited credit/lending powers, lack of deposit-guarantee protection, and restrictiveness for capital escrow, FX hedging, and high-value trade settlement. Such EMIs are acceptable only for low-value subscription collection and non-critical disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing is executed via SWIFT MT103 or SEPA Credit Transfer using an appropriate EBA BOP purpose code such as 'D020' for capital contributions or 'S001' for general working capital. The ordering party's name, address, account number, and, where applicable, LEI must be transmitted per Regulation (EU) 2015/847 on information accompanying transfers. The beneficiary SCSp must provide the bank with the partnership agreement, inward capital confirmation, UBO declaration, and a board/general-partner resolution to allow credit into the Capital Escrow Account. Any conversion from foreign currency is conducted at prevailing market rates.Outbound settlements are cleared through SWIFT MT103 for direct vendor payments and through Commercial Letters of Credit (ICC UCP 600) with SWIFT MT700/MT799 for high-value trade-backed procurements. For euro-area counterparties, SEPA Credit Transfer with mandatory structured remittance data is the default low-cost rail. All outbound wires require tax-compliant Beneficial Ownership declarations (due diligence), invoices, and proof-of-delivery where needed. Luxembourg does not impose surrender or conversion requirements on foreign-currency disbursements, allowing the SCSp to time FX execution freely.Plain vanilla OTC FX forwards in major convertible pairs (EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, EUR/CHF)Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for restricted or illiquid emerging-market currenciesCross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) under an ISDA Master Agreement, subject to EMIR clearing and margining thresholdsEuropean-style FX options on major pairs, permitted for risk management of subscription revenue and capex commitments
