Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) - Luxembourg Inland Revenue (portal: MyGuichet.lu and ACD 'eSpace'); Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AED) - competent for VAT and indirect duty registration (portal: MyGuichet.lu); Trade and Companies Register (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés - RCS) as the institutional front door for entity identification.

E-government filing portal

A foreign sponsor executes the SCSp partnership agreement (private deed suffices; notarial deed optional) and files it for RCS registration; the Luxembourg Business Registers assigns the statutory 13-digit business identification number (matricule). The SCSp then files VAT application Form 1043 with the AED via MyGuichet.lu and receives the Luxembourg VAT number (TVA LU00000000). Direct-tax registration is completed through the ACD upon first lodgement of the annual transparency declaration (Form 500); no minimum capital is required and non-resident ownership is unrestricted. Non-resident partners are not separately registered for Luxembourg VAT solely because the partnership carries on taxable activities; the partnership registers and acts as a distinct taxable person for indirect tax purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible - the SCSp is fiscally transparent for Luxembourg direct tax purposes: it is not subject to corporate income tax (CIT), municipal business tax (MBT), or net wealth tax (NWT); each partner is taxed directly on its pro-rata share of Luxembourg-source income. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the SCSp is fiscally transparent for Luxembourg direct tax purposes: it is not subject to corporate income tax (CIT), municipal business tax (MBT), or net wealth tax (NWT); each partner is taxed directly on its pro-rata share of Luxembourg-source income. For VAT, however, the SCSp is a separate taxable person in its own name and must charge, collect, and remit Luxembourg VAT on taxable supplies.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists; SCSp fiscal transparency is mandatory under Articles 104(4) and 175bis of the Luxembourg Income Tax Law (LIR) and cannot be waived. Available elections: (i) VAT grouping election (assujetti unique) under Article 60 of the 1979 VAT Law, effective 1 January 2024, allowing the SCSp and its general partner to form one taxable person; (ii) optional partnership tax return (Form 500) to formalise profit allocation for non-resident partners and foreign tax credit claims; (iii) ATAD hybrid-mismatch adjustment election under the implementing Law of 20 December 2019, requiring alignment of the SCSp treatment within a 24-month window to prevent recharacterisation by the partner's tax jurisdiction; (iv) no CFC or territorial-opt-in election is available for a manufacturing SCSp.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Eurozone member state: the euro is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts. There are no exchange control approvals, no restrictions on inward direct investment, and no barriers to profit or dividend repatriation under the EU free movement of capital (Article 63 TFEU). The tax-transparent SCSp may receive capital contributions from non-resident partners and remit profits freely; non-resident limited partners are not subject to Luxembourg income tax on distributions except where profits are attributable to a Luxembourg permanent establishment under the Luxembourg Income Tax Law (LIR). Practical hurdles are limited to AML/KYC documentation, CRS/FATCA registration, and BCL balance-of-payments statistical reporting by the resident bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg imposes no statutory surrender, forced conversion, or holding-period obligation on export proceeds. Exports of ICU mechanical ventilators are subject to EU medical-device regulation (EU 2017/745) and CE marking; export receipts in any currency may be retained onshore or offshore in any bank account. Banks apply standard transaction monitoring and may require underlying sale contracts, customs declarations, and purchase orders to evidence the commercial nature of incoming funds.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) operating within the Eurosystem; competences shared with the European Central Bank (ECB) for monetary policy and with the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for banking supervision and AML/CFT enforcement.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for primary treasury function. Luxembourg-licensed electronic money institutions under PSD2/CSSF supervision may open accounts for an SCSp, but they cannot provide trade finance, government LC confirmation, or correspondent-banking rails for LC-backed ventilator tenders. Offshore digital EMIs are generally rejected for this structure because they lack access to TARGET2, cannot settle BCL-reportable capital flows, and most Tier-1 Luxembourg banks refuse to recognize them as banking counterparties under CSSF AML/CFT guidance. Feasibility: Low; at most an ancillary collection account for customer receivables forwarded to a fully licensed domestic bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

17% CIT on taxable profits exceeding EUR 175,000 (15% on the first EUR 175,000), plus a 7% solidarity surcharge and municipal business tax, producing an aggregate Luxembourg City headline rate of up to 24.94% for taxable entities; an SCSp is ordinarily fiscally transparent and is not itself subject to CIT unless the structure is recharacterised as commercially taxable.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Luxembourg resident taxable company; an SCSp is fiscally transparent, so ordinary profit distributions to partners are generally not treated as Luxembourg-source dividends at the partnership level. Where a taxable Luxembourg underlying company pays dividends, the 15% rate may be reduced to 0% or 5% under the Parent-Subsidiary Directive or an applicable DTA.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File and Country-by-Country Report: group consolidated revenue above EUR 750,000,000. Local File: local net turnover above EUR 50,000,000 or a Luxembourg entity engaged in material related-party transactions with non-residents; prudent contemporaneous documentation is required where related-party intercompany flows exceed EUR 500,000 in aggregate, and always for the ventilator distribution/royalty chains.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg has no formal Economic Substance Regulations statute analogous to offshore regimes, but EU ATAD GAAR, the Luxembourg General Tax Law and case-law require genuine economic substance for the SCSp to be recognised as transparent and to access treaty benefits. Acceptable substance evidence includes local registered office, active and qualified management, local bank account, board minutes, risk control, controlled manufacturing or procurement activity, payroll and operational expenditure in Luxembourg.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Luxembourg General Tax Law penalties for intentional tax evasion may reach three times the evaded tax; criminal fines may reach EUR 500,000 and imprisonment up to five years for accountable managers.

Director personal liability: Directors and SCSp managers who sign false declarations or knowingly implement sham invoicing can be jointly and severally liable for the entity's CIT, MBT, NWT, interest and penalties; courts may impose travel bans, passport seizures, asset freezes and dismissal disqualification.
