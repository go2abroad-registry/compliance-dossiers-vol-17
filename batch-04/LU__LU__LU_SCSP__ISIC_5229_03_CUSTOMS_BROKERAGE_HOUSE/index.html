Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Luxembourg operates a functionally split national tax administration: (1) Administration des contributions directes (ACD, Luxembourg Inland Revenue) - competent for corporate income tax (impôt sur le revenu des collectivités), municipal business tax (impôt commercial communal), net wealth tax (impôt sur la fortune) and all direct tax registration, with e-filing channelled through the national portal MyGuichet.lu and the ACD digital filing systems. (2) Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) - competent for the TVA (VAT), registration duties and stamp duties, operating the VAT registration and electronic VAT return portal. (3) Administration des douanes et accises (ADA) - Luxembourg Customs and Excise - competent for customs law, export/import clearance, AEO decisions and the licensing of customs brokers under the Union Customs Code.

E-government filing portal

For a Luxembourg-established SCSp the procedure is centralized: (1) Formation and RCS registration - execute the notarized partnership agreement and publish it in the electronic register of the Luxembourg Business Registers (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés); the RCS assigns the matricule (national business registration number), which functions as the entity's statutory identifier for all Luxembourg tax files. (2) Direct tax file - upon RCS publication, the ACD automatically opens the direct tax dossier for the SCSp; the transparent partnership does not itself bear corporate income tax, but ACD practice requires an informative filing so that resident partners may integrate their allocable shares in their own tax returns. (3) VAT registration - the SCSp applies to the AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT identification number (format LU + 8 digits) using the standard registration application accompanied by the RCS excerpt, a summary of the partnership agreement and proof of the customs-broker licence; registration is effective from the date of first taxable supply. (4) Foreign taxable persons without an RCS seat - a non-resident supplier making Luxembourg-taxable supplies not subject to the reverse charge must register directly with the AEDT; if the only Luxembourg supplies are reverse-charged B2B services, registration is generally not required, although the customer must be notified of the reverse charge. (5) Customs licence - the brokerage house must hold an ADA-issued customs broker authorization before acting as an indirect customs representative; the ADA application must evidence professional reliability, solvency, customs-law knowledge and an EORI registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - statutory tax transparency without election: the SCSp is a contractual special limited partnership without legal personality. For direct taxes (corporate income tax, municipal business tax and net wealth tax), the SCSp itself is not the taxpayer; each partner (associé commandité and associé commanditaire) is treated as deriving its allocable share of the SCSp's income, gains, deductions and net wealth directly. Resident partners are taxed in Luxembourg on their allocable shares; non-resident partners are taxable in Luxembourg only to the extent the SCSp derives Luxembourg-source income (e.g., Luxembourg real estate or a Luxembourg permanent establishment of a trade or business). For VAT purposes, however, the SCSp is itself a taxable person and must hold a VAT number in its own name for the customs-brokerage activity, notwithstanding its direct-tax transparency.

Available election forms

Luxembourg has no check-the-box classification; the SCSp's fiscal transparency arises by operation of law. The following statutory options and elections interact with the SCSp and its owners: (1) Fiscal unity (Art. 164bis LIR) - available to Luxembourg-resident corporate taxpayers holding directly or indirectly at least 95% of a subsidiary for a qualifying holding period; it is unavailable to the transparent SCSp itself but usable by a corporate partner consolidating its own Luxembourg subsidiaries. (2) VAT elections - the small-enterprise franchise exemption below EUR 35,000 may be waived; the Union/Non-Union One-Stop-Shop (OSS) may be elected for distance sales of goods and certain B2C services; the reverse-charge mechanism governs the brokerage's cross-border B2B supplies. (3) Hybrid mismatch alignment - the ATAD 2 anti-hybrid provisions may recharacterise the transparency of the SCSp where a partner jurisdiction classifies it as fiscally opaque; the partnership agreement must allocate profits in a manner that avoids hybrid mismatches. (4) Customs authorisations - the broker may elect for AEO status (AEO-C/AEO-S), simplified declaration procedures and the 24-hour release channel; these are administrative authorisations, not tax elections. (5) Tax consolidation is not available to partnerships.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible on both current and capital account and Luxembourg satisfies IMF Article VIII obligations. There are no exchange controls on profit remittances, dividend distributions, or capital account repatriations; an SCSp engaged in customs brokerage may transfer operating profits and fee income to non-resident LPs without BCL authorization. The only compliance gate is the banking AML/KYC process, including source-of-funds verification for outward payments. As a euro-area member, Luxembourg applies no convertibility licensing or approval regime for trade-related payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg has no statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender requirement for export proceeds or customs brokerage receivables. The SCSp may freely retain earnings in EUR or any foreign currency and may convert at market rates. For any cross-border remittance exceeding EUR 12,500, the reporting bank files a BCL balance-of-payments statistical declaration; this is a data-reporting obligation, not a repatriation or surrender rule. If counterparties in third countries apply their own FX-rationing, the SCSp must contractually require settlement in euros or freely transferable currencies to remain fully compliant.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating within the Eurosystem of the European Central Bank (ECB); prudential and AML/CFT banking supervision is exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) under the Law of 5 April 1993 and CSSF Regulation 12-02.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. A CSSF-licensed electronic money institution established in Luxembourg (e.g., Mangopay SA) can provide an EUR IBAN for fee collection and cross-border payouts, but cannot serve as the primary treasury rail because the Administration des douanes et accises requires a statutory bank-issued guarantee and a SEPA Direct Debit mandate under the deferred customs payment scheme (paiement différé), which EMIs are generally not structured to support. Passported EU EMIs under PSD2 are viable only as annex collection accounts with monthly volume below EUR 100,000 and cannot execute the documentary letter of credit (MT700) or standby guarantee (MT760) rails, nor provide the BCL balance-of-payments reporting on behalf of the SCSp.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

LP capital contributions are routed to the capital escrow account at BGL BNP Paribas or Banque de Luxembourg. EUR subscriptions settle by SEPA Credit Transfer with purpose code indicating equity injection into the SCSp; non-EUR subscriptions settle by SWIFT MT103 with the narrative including the SCSp RCS number and VAT matricule. The bank executes CSSF 12-02 due diligence, matches remitters to the RBE registry, and automatically files the BCL balance-of-payments statistical declaration for every cross-border remittance exceeding EUR 12,500. For non-EU LP transfers, the MT103 must identify the underlying LP entity, and funds originating from virtual-asset channels are not admissible. A blocked-account release condition is set for the notarial certificate evidencing the formation of the SCSp.Customs duties and import VAT are collected by the Administration des douanes et accises through a SEPA Core Direct Debit mandate on the BIL operational account under the deferred payment scheme (paiement différé); this constitutes the Luxembourg automated broker interface, the counterpart of a customs ACH. Intra-EU trade settlements use instant SEPA or SWIFT MT103; non-EU counterparty disbursements use SWIFT MT103 and MT202 COV with accurate Union Customs Code narrative codes. High-value bonded freight purchases are settled by documentary letters of credit under UCP 600 (MT700/MT710), and the customs transit guarantee is provided by a standby Letter of Credit (MT760) or a comprehensive sûreté douanière pledged by the relationship bank. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory dividend withholding tax for Luxembourg corporate issuers. An SCSp is fiscally transparent, so profit allocations to partners are not treated as Luxembourg dividends at entity level and no 15% withholding tax applies to such allocations. A Luxembourg corporate partner may qualify for the 0% participation exemption / EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive rate on distributions made by a corporate underlying entity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 0 - no statutory de minimis threshold below which transfer pricing documentation is waived for the SCSp; the ACD may demand Master File, Local File and benchmarking documentation on request. Country-by-country reporting applies at the group level where consolidated turnover reaches EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: No UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations exist in Luxembourg, but the Luxembourg anti-abuse doctrine, LIR anti-abuse provisions, and ATAD principles require genuine economic substance. A customs brokerage SCSp must maintain real managerial presence, employees, business premises, bank accounts, and risk-taking functions in Luxembourg; otherwise the ACD may disregard the SCSp, deny treaty benefits or the tax residence certificate, and impose joint liability on the managing partner.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud penalties, administrative default reassessment, monthly late interest of 0.6%, monetary fines up to EUR 250,000, and confiscation of the illegal economic advantage.

Director personal liability: The managing partner and any board-equivalent persons may be personally prosecuted; aggravated tax fraud can result in imprisonment, travel bans, confiscation orders and civil restitution of the unpaid taxes.
Shell company / artificial arrangement

Statutory sanction: Denial of tax residence certificate, refusal of treaty benefits, recharacterisation of the structure under the Luxembourg anti-abuse doctrine, reassessment of diverted profits, and administrative penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors and managers may be jointly liable for the tax liability where the corporate veil is pierced; criminal proceedings may also impose civil restitution, travel bans and imprisonment in cases of fraudulent use of the structure.
AML / customs brokerage integrity failure

Statutory sanction: Regulatory fines up to 10% of annual turnover under Luxembourg AML legislation, temporary or permanent revocation of the customs broker licence, and confiscation of proceeds of money laundering.

Director personal liability: AML responsible officers and directors face personal criminal liability, including imprisonment, travel bans, director disqualification, and orders for civil restitution of laundered amounts.
Transfer pricing mispricing and concealed quasi-distributions

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment, additional corporate income tax, monthly interest, administrative penalties, and recharacterisation of excessive payments as constructive distributions subject to Luxembourg withholding tax at 15%.

Director personal liability: Directors and managing partners may be held personally liable for withheld taxes and civil damages; deliberate or grossly negligent conduct can lead to criminal sanctions, travel bans and imprisonment.
