Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), administered by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR), 12, avenue de la Gare, L-1611 Luxembourg.

Governing statute

Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended by the Law of 23 July 2016 (SCSp regime), specifically Articles 102-139 and Article 102-1; related provisions of the amended Law of 19 December 2002 on the Register of Commerce and Companies and on the Annual Accounts.

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Association (statuts) executed as a private deed or notarial deed, together with RCS incorporation form (Form B) and the declaration of beneficial owner; filed via the LBR e-incorporation portal or in person at the RCS registry.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 27 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital for a Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp); capital is freely determined by the partnership agreement. No escrow or bank blockage rule applies to cash contributions under Luxembourg law, provided each contribution is fully paid up at the time contributions are recorded and listed in the deed.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification requires an inspection and approval letter from the Administration des Services de Secours (Luxembourg Fire and Rescue Corps) for any warehouse or storage area exceeding 1,000 m² or holding substantial combustible materials. A structural load clearance certificate from the municipal building authority (certificat de charge structurale) is required for warehouse racking and mezzanine floors. Occupational health and workplace safety clearance is issued by the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM), covering emergency exit plans, fire extinguisher compliance, electrical installation checks, and mandatory workplace risk assessments under the Labour Code (Articles L.311-1 et seq.).

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital for a Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp); capital is freely determined by the partnership agreement. No escrow or bank blockage rule applies to cash contributions under Luxembourg law, provided each contribution is fully paid up at the time contributions are recorded and listed in the deed.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT and registration duties; Administration des douanes et accises (Luxembourg Customs) for export controls. Unified business e-administration portal: MyGuichet.lu; VAT e-filing: eTVA portal; company registry: RCSL e-Registre.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporate the SCSp and file with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCSL) to obtain the matricule and national business number. Step 2 - File Form 640 for VAT identification with the AEDT Bureau d'identification TVA via MyGuichet.lu; a non-established foreign partner supplying through the SCSp must register in its own name and, if a non-EU resident, appoint a Luxembourg VAT representative (Article 61 VAT Law). Step 3 - Obtain the Luxembourg VAT number in the format 'LU + 8 digits + check digit'. Step 4 - Obtain an EORI number from Luxembourg Customs before any export declaration. Step 5 - Register for the Union OSS / IOSS schemes on the Luxembourg portal if the SCSp makes EU consumer distance sales. Step 6 - Foreign founders without Luxembourg residence must appoint a local tax representative for direct tax notices.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible. The SCSp has no legal personality and is fiscally transparent by law: it is not subject to corporate income tax (IRC), municipal business tax (ICB) or net wealth tax (IFI). Income, gains, and net assets are attributed directly to partners under the partnership agreement and taxed at partner level (corporate partner: up to 24.94% combined in Luxembourg City; individual partner: progressive personal income tax). For VAT, the SCSp is similarly transparent and supplies are deemed made by the partners unless the SCSp has an independent fixed establishment and is separately registered as a taxable person. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. The SCSp has no legal personality and is fiscally transparent by law: it is not subject to corporate income tax (IRC), municipal business tax (ICB) or net wealth tax (IFI). Income, gains, and net assets are attributed directly to partners under the partnership agreement and taxed at partner level (corporate partner: up to 24.94% combined in Luxembourg City; individual partner: progressive personal income tax). For VAT, the SCSp is similarly transparent and supplies are deemed made by the partners unless the SCSp has an independent fixed establishment and is separately registered as a taxable person. Territorial scope: unitary national, no sub-national VAT or sales tax.

Available election forms

No check-the-box entity classification exists; SCSp transparency is mandatory. Available elections: (i) voluntary VAT registration waiving the EUR 35,000 small-business exemption (Article 57 VAT Law); (ii) election to form a VAT group ('assujetti unique') with Luxembourg-established related persons (Article 60 VAT Law); (iii) election of the OSS/IOSS one-stop-shop schemes for cross-border consumer sales; (iv) ATAD hybrid-mismatch grandfathering election for pre-2019 hybrid instruments (Law of 21 December 2018; hybrid mismatch reporting form 708); (v) election to register as a regulated SIF/SICAR fund (CSSF authorization) with separate tax regimes. No election can render the SCSp opaque for corporate income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for the small-enterprise exemption (Article 57 VAT Law). Below this threshold registration remains voluntary; mandatory registration applies from the first euro for EU distance sales when OSS/IOSS is elected, for intra-EU acquisitions above the EUR 10,000 distance-sales threshold, and for non-established suppliers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Maternity and baby goods physically dispatched from Luxembourg to non-EU destinations qualify for the 0% export exemption (Article 40 VAT Law) on proof of departure from the EU obtained within 90 days of supply (Article 74 VAT Law). Intra-Community supplies to EU VAT-registered traders are exempt under Article 43 with a valid EU VAT number and EC Sales List. Distance sales to EU consumers are not zero-rated: they are taxable at destination via Union OSS (goods above EUR 150) or IOSS (goods not above EUR 150). The SCSp must retain PSP settlement records (Stripe/PayPal) as payment evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) with exit confirmation from Luxembourg Customs and the Export Accompanying Document (EAD)
  • Bill of Lading (or alternative transport document: air waybill / CMR) evidencing dispatch from Luxembourg to the non-EU destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate: bank credit advice or PSP settlement report (Stripe/PayPal merchant statement) matching the export invoice
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the maternity/baby goods and their value
  • EORI number of the exporting SCSp and, where applicable, customs representation / power of attorney letter
  • VAT registration proof and, for intra-EU supplies, the customer's valid EU VAT number with EC Sales List reference
  • Proof of export timing (entry into transport registry or customs log within 90 days of the supply)

No general real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or invoice-authorization regime. Mandatory B2G electronic invoicing to Luxembourg public entities via PEPPOL (PEPPOL BIS 3.0). VAT returns and OSS/IOSS statements are filed electronically via eTVA and MyGuichet.lu. No continuous transaction-level API clearance, no SAF-T, and no QR/fiscal-code invoice clearance applies to e-commerce merchants; paper, PDF, and structured e-invoices are equally acceptable.

The AEDT cross-checks eTVA returns, OSS statements, and customs exit data. If proof of export is not produced within 90 days, or the goods did not leave the EU, the 0% rate is clawed back and Luxembourg VAT up to 17% is assessed with late-payment interest (0.6% per month, Article 31 VAT Law). Administrative fines range up to 100% of evaded tax (Article 65 VAT Law); obstruction penalties follow Article 66; criminal tax fraud under Sections 396-398 of the General Tax Law (Abgabenordnung) carries imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 250,000. The 'abus de droit' doctrine permits recharacterization of artificial export routing. SCSp partners may be held jointly and severally liable for clawback, interest, and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Luxembourg franc has been replaced by the euro; the euro is fully convertible. Luxembourg operates an open market economy with no exchange controls on either the current or capital account. Repatriation of profits, dividends, and redemption proceeds from a SCSp is not subject to Luxembourg exchange control approval. The principal regulatory hurdles are documentary: adequate proof of underlying economic activity, compliance with EU anti-money laundering directives, and statistical reporting of cross-border payments under BCL regulations. For tax-transparent SCSp, profit remittance to foreign partners is not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax, however domestic tax law may require proof of the partnership's tax-transparent classification to avoid significant deemed withholding or administrative scrutiny.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists in Luxembourg for the surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds into EUR. The euro is the sole legal tender and the free movement of capital is enshrined in EU law. Export receipts may be retained in any currency of the exporter's choice, subject only to standard banking due diligence and BCL balance-of-payments statistical reporting thresholds. The mandatory conversion ratio is therefore 0%, and no timeline applies.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), in coordination with the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for banking prudential supervision and foreign exchange reporting compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) without a Luxembourg passport or physical branch face significant legal constraints in providing main transaction accounts to a Luxembourg SCSp. Under PSD2, an EMI must be authorized or passported through the CSSF; cross-border remote onboarding is generally permitted only after CSSF notification and compliance with Luxembourg KYC/AML obligations. For a non-resident managed SCSp operating cross-border e-commerce, a CSSF-authorized EMI or an EU-passported EMI with Luxembourg market notification is usable for operational liquidity, but high-volume clearing, multi-currency needs, and merchant settlement resilience are better supported by a tier-1 bank. Regtech-forward EMIs that partner with a Luxembourg credit institution remain viable for payment acceptance and disbursement flow, subject to the SCSp's ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) and source-of-funds documentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing is via SWIFT MT103 from the investor's or parent's bank to the SCSp operational account at a Luxembourg credit institution. The wire must state a clear and accurate purpose code, typically 'BOP_G_OTHER_INVESTMENT' or 'S12' depending on the BCL reporting manual, and clearly identify the sender and beneficiary with the SCSp's IBAN. Verification requires the bank to obtain the underlying capital contribution deed, proof of the initiating party's identity, and evidence that the injection does not violate any EU sanctions or counter-terrorism financing rules. Payment messages should explicitly use 'BEN' or 'OUR' reimbursement per bilateral bank instructions and include an awareness statement if the value exceeds EUR 10,000 equivalent for automatic Currency Transaction Reporting.Use SWIFT MT103 for high-value supplier payments and for final settlements under documentary credits. For confirmed purchase transactions in baby and maternity supplies, a confirmed irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) issued by the Luxembourg SCSp's bank in favour of the overseas supplier is recommended, with Incoterms, inspection certificates, and transport documents attached. The SCSp must ensure that the LC is compliant with UCP 600, contains a precise Goods Description matching ISIC 4791, and is covered by the bank's funds release criteria. Payments to freight forwarders, consolidators, and customs brokers should be executed via SEPA Wire, MT103, or cross-border ACH where the counterparty is in the EU, while non-EU suppliers require correspondent banking rails via SWIFT.
