Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permitted subject to the Law of 31 May 1999 on the domiciliation of commercial companies. For an SCSp, the registered office may be fixed at the address of its general partner or at a licensed domiciliation agent in Luxembourg. However, the domiciliation agent must provide real substance (mail handling, availability during business hours, and access to records). Virtual office-only arrangements without a physical reception of mail and documents are not considered compliant by the RCS, the Luxembourg tax authorities, and the CSSF. Given the manufacturing/toy IP-holding character of the vehicle, a domiciliation address combined with the general partner's EU substance is recommended; industrial premises are not required for the SCSp itself because the operating production entity will hold the classified establishment permit.

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) - Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), electronic filing through the e-RCS portal (lbr.lu / e-RCS.lu); RESA publication (Recueil Électronique des Sociétés et Associations) is performed by the LBR.

Governing statute

Amended Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, Title II (Sociétés en Commandite), Articles 101-1 et seq. as introduced by the Law of 12 July 2013 on the Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp); Law of 19 December 2002 on the Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS), as amended; Law of 13 January 2019 on the Register of Beneficial Owners (RBE); Article 185bis of the Luxembourg Income Tax Law (LIR) confirming fiscal transparency of partnerships.

Formation / constitutional filings

Contrat de société (partnership agreement) of the SCSp, executed by all partners by notarial deed or private writing, filed via the e-RCS portal with the RCS application form and published in the RESA.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum subscribed or paid-up capital applies to an SCSp; the partnership agreement freely determines capital contributions and payment schedules. No escrow account is required by Luxembourg law for partnerships. In AML and banking practice, a Luxembourg bank will require a meaningful opening deposit (commonly EUR 10,000-12,500) and evidence of the contributions per the capital schedule before issuing the account confirmation used in the RCS dossier.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD) for direct corporate/income taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT. Centralized filings via the MyGuichet.lu portal (www.myguichet.lu); official portal www.impotsdirects.public.lu.

E-government filing portal

A Luxembourg SCSp is registered with the Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS) through a notarized deed, obtaining a business registration number (BRC). After RCS registration, the entity automatically receives a Luxembourg national tax number (matricule). For VAT, the SCSp must apply to the AEDT, in writing via MyGuichet.lu, providing the RCS extract, identification of the intended activity (ISIC 3240), and expected turnover. VAT registration becomes mandatory if the annual taxable turnover exceeds EUR 35,000 or for the operation of an intra-Community acquisition/distance sale threshold. Foreign entities may obtain a direct VAT ID with a local appointed agent where the foreign entity is not established but executes taxable supplies in Luxembourg.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

SCSp is fiscally transparent by default. Under Article 175bis LIR, the SCSp may elect to be treated as a taxable entity subject to Luxembourg corporate income tax (opting towards entity-level taxation) provided all general partners are themselves corporate taxable persons and the SCSp is governed by Luxembourg partnership law. This election is irrevocable for the initial tax year and only reversible upon the consent of the tax administration. No US-style Check-the-Box election exists; instead, the transparent/opaque status flows from the partnership deed and the 175bis election. European anti-hybrid rules (ATAD) may override the tax classification if mismatches arise with third-country jurisdictions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 (annual taxable turnover; for small enterprises established in Luxembourg, below this threshold the taxable person may benefit from the VAT exemption under Article 57 LVAT; VAT registration is optional but becomes mandatory when the threshold is exceeded. Non-resident suppliers performing taxable B2C remote sales or goods imports must register from the first euro unless an EU One-Stop-Shop scheme applies)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For export of goods to a destination outside the EU, the supply is subject to 0% VAT, provided the goods are physically dispatched or transported to a non-EU destination within 90 days of the supply and the exporter retains specified evidence. For intra-Community supplies of goods to a business customer registered with a valid EU VAT number, a VAT-exempt (zero-rated) transfer can apply if the seller holds the customer's VAT ID and the goods are transported to another EU member state. For services related to tangible movable goods (e.g., tolling/assembly), the place of supply is generally where the customer is established; if the customer is an overseas taxable person, the service falls outside Luxembourg VAT entirely.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital sealing regime is in force. B2B e-invoicing is permitted under Council Directive 2014/55/EU and national rules. B2G e-invoicing is mandatory through the PEPPOL network. Standard XML formats (e.g., UBL 2.x, Factur-X) are accepted. Regular e-invoicing is required to maintain audit trails, but no pre-clearance is required.

Luxembourg tax authorities may retrospectively cancel the zero-rating and assess VAT at 17% if export evidence is incomplete, inaccurate, or the export declaration and transport means are not consistent. For intentionally fraudulent claims, the penalty regime under Article 11 LSP/P.G. applies: fines of up to 50% of the evaded tax, late interest at 5% per annum, and possible criminal sanctions. If export proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days, the Zero-Rated Export Declaration and method of payment may be scrutinized for economic substance; where repatriation failure is deemed a sham operation, the VAT zero-rating is clawed back from the supplier.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible on both current and capital account in Luxembourg, with unrestricted accumulation of balances by residents and non-residents. Free movement of capital applies under Articles 63-66 TFEU; only protective measures based on public policy, AML/CFT, or EU sanctions may restrict flows. An SCSp is a transparent partnership, so capital contributions and profit distributions to non-resident partners are generally free of Luxembourg foreign-exchange control and, in the absence of domestic nexus, free of withholding. Remittance of export receipts to Luxembourg partners requires no prior license or exchange-control declaration. The only institutional hurdle is the need to evidence commercial substance and beneficiary identities to the banking counterparty.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Luxembourg maintains no compulsory surrender, no prescribed repatriation timeline, and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Exporters may retain balances in foreign currency in resident accounts. The only binding regime is the BCL balance-of-payments statistical reporting: resident companies must report cross-border transactions above the applicable reporting threshold through the BCL e-line portal, normally on a monthly or quarterly aggregate basis. No approval or surrender certificate is required before using export proceeds to fund trade disbursements.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), acting in coordination with the European Central Bank (ECB) and the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF). The BCL administers monetary policy implementation, payments infrastructure, and external statistics; the CSSF supervises credit institutions and payment/e-money institutions. The Ministry of Finance and the CSSF jointly administer any remaining exchange or capital-movement restrictions permitted under EU law.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low as a primary treasury account; Medium as a secondary collection rail. Luxembourg-licensed e-money institutions (EMIs) under the EMD2 regime and EU-licensed payment institutions can legally hold safeguarding accounts, disburse payouts, and support marketplace settlement, but they do not offer credit, deposit protection under the Luxembourg deposit-guarantee scheme, or the range of documentary trade services (LC, guarantees) needed by a goods-importing SCSp. The government of the SCSp may open one EMI account for Amazon Pay/Stripe collections and one bank account at a tier-1 bank as the principal treasury account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from non-resident partners are routed via SWIFT MT103 with a clear beneficiary reference to the SCSp and the purpose code reflecting a direct equity participation or capital contribution (ECB BOP classification 'Direct investment - equity capital'). No Luxembourg foreign-exchange approval is required; the receiving bank will request a board or partner resolution authorising the contribution, a copy of the incorporation deed, and the contribution ledger entry showing that the injected funds are credited to the partnership's capital account and not to the general partner personally. For partner contributions disbursed by European banks, an SEPA Credit Transfer may be used, but non-EU remittance must arrive via SWIFT MT103, and the bank will verify that the ordering customer matches the listed partner.Trade settlements for the wooden-block manufacturing and inventory purchases will run through a two-rail structure: (1) direct supplier payments via SWIFT MT103 (single customer credit transfer) for open-account purchases, using appropriate trade purpose codes and full commercial invoice references; (2) documentary confirmed letters of credit issued via SWIFT MT700 for larger, structured purchase orders, allowing the beneficiary's bank to negotiate against transport and quality documents (EN-71 certificates, sanitary/phytosanitary statements, packing lists). For intra-EU suppliers, SEPA Credit Transfer is the standard low-cost clearing rail.
