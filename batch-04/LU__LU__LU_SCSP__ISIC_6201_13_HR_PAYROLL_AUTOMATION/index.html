Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital and no mandatory escrow account: the SCSp has no minimum paid-up capital requirement; the capital is freely fixed by the partnership agreement, must be fully subscribed, and the contribution schedule is contractually agreed; a notarial deed is required only if immovable property is contributed

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital and no mandatory escrow account: the SCSp has no minimum paid-up capital requirement; the capital is freely fixed by the partnership agreement, must be fully subscribed, and the contribution schedule is contractually agreed; a notarial deed is required only if immovable property is contributed

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) - Luxembourg Inland Revenue for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT. Central electronic portal: https://guichet.lu / MyGuichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity not established in Luxembourg but carrying out taxable activities must appoint a fiscal agent or Luxembourg VAT representative if required. For a payroll SaaS platform with no Luxembourg fixed establishment, VAT registration can be made directly to the AEDT using the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for intra-EU supplies to consumer recipients; a Luxembourg national identification number (matricule) is obtained from the ACD once the entity has a Luxembourg presence for tax filing or payroll reporting; the statutory corporate income tax registration is triggered only upon having a taxable permanent establishment (PE). In practice, obtain a Luxembourg business registration number through the Registry of Commerce (RCS) via MyGuichet.lu, then request a VAT number from the AEDT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

SCSp is fiscally transparent by statutory default under Luxembourg law, treated as a pure pass-through for corporate income tax and net wealth tax. No 'check-the-box' election exists as in the US. An SCSp may nonetheless be treated as taxable in another jurisdiction if hybrid mismatch rules apply; Luxembourg has transposed ATAD anti-hybrid rules that can reclassify a transparent entity for Luxembourg tax purposes when payments trigger double-non-taxation or double-deduction outcomes, subject to a Luxembourg effective-tax-residency or place-of-effective-management analysis.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover (excluding VAT) for Luxembourg-established businesses. Non-resident suppliers of digital services (B2C and B2B without local PE) are required to register from the first euro; alternatively use the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for EUR 10,000 or less in cross-border distance sales into Luxembourg. No VAT registration threshold applies to non-established e-commerce suppliers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods removed from Luxembourg to a destination outside the EU qualify for 0% VAT provided export formalities are completed and transport evidence is held. Services supplied to a non-resident customer (B2B outside the EU) are outside Luxembourg VAT scope, equivalent to zero-rating, where the place of supply is outside the EU. For offshore payroll SaaS services to non-EU business customers, the supply is zero-rated/outside scope upon documentation of the customer's non-EU establishment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Union manifest / customs approved EEA export declaration)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR transport document
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank evidence of payment received in Luxembourg)
  • Commercial invoice with Incoterms 2020 terms
  • Proof of recipient's non-EU establishment (e.g., VAT registration certificate, company registry extract)

From 1 January 2025, Luxembourg mandates structured electronic invoicing (Peppol-compatible formats) for all domestic B2B transactions between Luxembourg taxable persons. No tax authority pre-clearance or real-time fiscal stalping exists; invoices must be exchanged through certified access points or interoperable Peppol networks. B2G invoices already require PEPPOL. No government API stamping requirement is currently in force, but the AEDT utilises e-audit and tax reporting frameworks to verify VAT compliance.

The AEDT can conduct retrospective VAT audits for up to five years (ten years in case of fraud). If zero-rating is claimed without adequate documentary proof of export or non-EU place of supply, the AEDT may issue an assessment for the unpaid VAT, plus monthly interest of 0.9% and penalties up to EUR 25,000 per invoice; intentional fraud can lead to criminal fines and imprisonment of up to five years. Non-established taxpayers may be asked to provide financial guarantees. The clawback will include VAT previously zero-rated where physical export or inbound payment evidence does not match the customs declaration within the deadline.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current and capital account convertibility under Article 63 TFEU; the euro is a reserve currency with no exchange controls applicable to Luxembourg SCSp treasury operations. Profit remittance and capital distributions to partners are free from any Luxembourg foreign-exchange authorization, subject only to standard AML/KYC documentation, EU sanctions compliance, and tax-transparent reporting rules applicable to the SCSp. Non-resident partners' distributive shares are not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax under the pass-through regime absent a Luxembourg commercial establishment or PE, though treaty-based reduced rates may apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline applies to export receipts in Luxembourg or the euro area. Receipts in any currency may be retained in full, transferred at the commercial exchange rate, or used directly for cross-border payables without mandatory conversion to EUR. For the payroll SaaS platform, invoicing of cross-border clients is treated as an EU services export and is not subject to any FX remittance or surrender obligation under BCL regulations.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Luxembourg (Banque centrale du Luxembourg, BCL) and the European Central Bank (ECB) as monetary authority for euro-area policy; financial-sector prudential supervision is delegated to the Luxembourg Financial Sector Supervisory Commission (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium-High as an ancillary payment/FX routing tool, but legally unsuitable as the sole or primary treasury vessel. Luxembourg-licensed or EU-authorised e-money institutions (EMIs) such as Wirecard successors, Payoneer, Wise, or Revolut Business can provide multi-currency IBAN accounts, onward SWIFT/SEPA transfer, and competitive FX, but they cannot hold client funds as deposits, provide capital-guaranteed escrow accounts, or act as settlement banks under CSSF rules. For all corporate treasury, payroll clearing, and inbound capital escrow, a fully regulated credit institution with a Luxembourg or EU passport is mandatory; EMIs may not support statutory capital contributions or partner capital accounts in a legally clear manner.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For non-resident equity or capital contributions into the SCSp, the preferred rail is SWIFT MT103 (customer credit transfer) from the investor's bank to the Luxembourg SCSp operational account, accompanied by a purpose reference 'SCSp capital contribution under LSC Art. 208-1' and the SCSp RCS number. For intra-EU inflows, use SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) with the SCSp's IBAN and a clear code 'CAP_CONTRIBUTION'. BCL statistical balance-of-payments reporting thresholds may require the resident entity to declare the direct investment/capital-account transaction to BCL under the BCL Direct Investment Reporting regime; the underlying purpose code should reflect 'Direct investment — equity capital (D.2/D.3)' as appropriate. The bank must verify the economic substance of the payment by collecting the underlying capital-account entry and the partner resolution.For payroll settlements and software-related disbursements within SEPA, use SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst). For non-EU counterparties, use SWIFT MT103 / MT202 COV with correct commercial purpose codes.
