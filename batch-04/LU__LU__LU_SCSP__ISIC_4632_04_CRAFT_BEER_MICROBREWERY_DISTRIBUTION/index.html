Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital; SCSp may be established with zero capital. Contributions may be cash or in kind, without statutory minimum, and if contributed must be placed in an escrow or partnership corporate bank account subject to AML/KYC verification.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification: obtained from the Administration de la protection contre l'incendie et les secours (APCIS) following inspection of the cold-storage and distribution warehouse; must include evacuation plan, extinguishing systems, and addressable fire-alarm. Structural load inspection: certificate from a state-accredited structural engineer for racking systems and cold-storage floors. Occupational clearance: workplace safety assessment and first-aid training verification issued by the Labour and Mines Inspectorate (Inspection du Travail et des Mines).

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital; SCSp may be established with zero capital. Contributions may be cash or in kind, without statutory minimum, and if contributed must be placed in an escrow or partnership corporate bank account subject to AML/KYC verification.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD; Luxembourg Inland Revenue) and Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT); filings and registrations accessible through the MyGuichet.lu portal and Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS/LBR).

E-government filing portal

Register the SCSp in the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) through a notary to obtain the business registration number; request a national tax identification number (matricule) from ACD; apply separately to AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT number using Form 1000/1020, attaching the RCS extract, constitutional documents, proof of registered office, partner details, and estimated turnover. Non-resident partners may require a Luxembourg VAT number if importing or engaging in VAT-liable transactions; the SCSp may optionally register for VAT even below the EUR 35,000 threshold.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Register the SCSp in the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) through a notary to obtain the business registration number; request a national tax identification number (matricule) from ACD; apply separately to AEDT for a Luxembourg VAT number using Form 1000/1020, attaching the RCS extract, constitutional documents, proof of registered office, partner details, and estimated turnover. Non-resident partners may require a Luxembourg VAT number if importing or engaging in VAT-liable transactions; the SCSp may optionally register for VAT even below the EUR 35,000 threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

SCSp is fiscally transparent under default rules; a partnership may elect or be treated as a taxable corporate entity under Article 175 LIR if it carries on a commercial activity in Luxembourg. Anti-hybrid rules under ATAD (implemented by Luxembourg law of 23 December 2016) restrict hybrid mismatch elections. A check-the-box-style election is not generally available; transparency is automatic unless a valid corporate-taxation election or commercial-activity trigger applies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover (small enterprise exemption); mandatory registration for non-established suppliers and for intra-EU distance sales exceeding EUR 10,000 cross-border threshold under the OSS; no threshold for importation or reverse-charge acquisitions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to direct exports of goods from Luxembourg to non-EU destinations where the exporter retains proof that the goods left the EU within 90 days of supply, including for craft beer shipped under EXW/FOB/CIF terms; ancillary transport services and certain B2B services to non-EU customers may be zero-rated or outside scope provided business status and actual performance are documented.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD) with Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / CMR consignment note evidencing physical departure from the EU
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List conforming to customs valuation requirements
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Transport documentation and insurance certificate supporting the export supply chain

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping; e-invoicing is mandatory for B2G (central government, PEPPOL BIS 3.0 format); B2B e-invoicing remains voluntary; invoice data must be retained for 6 years; no API-based fiscal clearance requirement exists.

Luxembourg VAT authorities may scrutinize zero-rating claims within the 5-year statute of limitation (10 years in cases of fraud); unsubstantiated or fraudulent export claims trigger immediate reversal of zero-rating, VAT collection at 17% plus default interest at 0.8% per month, administrative fines up to EUR 250,000, and imprisonment up to 5 years for criminal fraud; in an SCSp, the general partner and certain partners may be jointly and severally liable for assessed VAT and penalties.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (plus 7% solidarity surcharge and municipal business tax; aggregate rate in Luxembourg City approx. 29.22%)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory withholding tax on dividends; distributions from a transparent SCSp to partners are generally not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For a calendar year, the return must be filed by 31 December of the following tax year; extensions are administratively available.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1.1 Commercial register number, Luxembourg business tax matricule, and registered office details. Non-resident partners with a Luxembourg permanent establishment must identify the PE and its Luxembourg tax number; otherwise state the SCSp is transparent and no Form 100 is required at entity level. Late filing may trigger a surcharge of up to 10% of the tax due when filed after formal notice, plus monthly late-payment interest of 0.6%; administrative fines up to EUR 1,000 per infringement; criminal penalties apply in cases of wilful late filing.
Box 2.1 Net commercial profit or loss derived from the audited financial statements. Non-residents must allocate to the PE only the profit attributable to the Luxembourg craft-beer distribution activity. Late filing may trigger a surcharge of up to 10% of the tax due when filed after formal notice, plus monthly late-payment interest of 0.6%; administrative fines up to EUR 1,000 per infringement; criminal penalties apply in cases of wilful late filing.
Box 2.2 Reintegration of non-deductible expenses: entertaining, fines, excessive director remuneration, 50% of certain vehicle costs. Same add-backs apply; expenses booked at foreign head office cannot be deducted against Luxembourg PE income. Late filing may trigger a surcharge of up to 10% of the tax due when filed after formal notice, plus monthly late-payment interest of 0.6%; administrative fines up to EUR 1,000 per infringement; criminal penalties apply in cases of wilful late filing.
Box 2.3 Tax losses brought forward from prior years applied against current taxable income. Only Luxembourg-sourced PE losses that were previously assessed may be carried forward; losses of a foreign head office are ineligible. Late filing may trigger a surcharge of up to 10% of the tax due when filed after formal notice, plus monthly late-payment interest of 0.6%; administrative fines up to EUR 1,000 per infringement; criminal penalties apply in cases of wilful late filing.
Box 3.2 Taxable base for municipal business tax (Form 100A) before applying the municipal multiplier. Municipal business tax applies to the Luxembourg PE; foreign partners without a PE have no Luxembourg municipal business tax exposure. Late filing may trigger a surcharge of up to 10% of the tax due when filed after formal notice, plus monthly late-payment interest of 0.6%; administrative fines up to EUR 1,000 per infringement; criminal penalties apply in cases of wilful late filing.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: Luxembourg entity annual net turnover exceeding EUR 10 million with related-party transactions; Master File: group consolidated annual revenue exceeding EUR 50 million; Country-by-Country Report: group consolidated revenue EUR 750 million or more.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not maintain formal Economic Substance Regulations comparable to offshore jurisdictions, but the GAAR, CFC rules and jurisprudence require real economic substance. An SCSp with no Luxembourg office, management, or decision-making substance may be re-characterized as an abusive shell and stripped of treaty/domestic tax benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to EUR 10,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; civil reassessment with penalty up to 10% and 0.6% monthly late interest on the unpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for taxes, penalties and interest; travel bans and imprisonment can be ordered in aggravated cases.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income to the arm's length standard; penalty between 10% and 50% of the reassessed profit; loss of double-tax treaty benefits.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize non-commercial intercompany terms can be held jointly liable for the tax deficiency and exposed to asset seizure or personal garnishment.
Shell Company / Lack of Substance

Statutory sanction: Disregard of the SCSp's legal transparency, retroactive taxation at partner level, denial of participation exemptions, and a 50% penalty for abuse of law.

Director personal liability: General partner directors may be civilly liable for the unpaid tax when the partnership is insolvent, and may face travel restrictions and criminal penalties for fraudulent use of a corporate vehicle.
AML / Cash-Distribution Integrity

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines up to EUR 1 million under the Luxembourg AML law of 2020; statutory liquor distribution license may be revoked; business may be barred from intra-EU keg export.

Director personal liability: Responsible general partner directors face personal fines, mandatory travel controls, and imprisonment in flagrant non-compliance where money laundering is established.
