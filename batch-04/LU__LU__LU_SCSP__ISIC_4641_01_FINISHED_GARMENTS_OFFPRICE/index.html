No minimum share capital is imposed on the SCSp; the paid-up contribution may be zero or nominal, made in cash or in kind, and no escrow or blocked account obligation exists. Contribution and drawdown terms are freely determined in the partnership agreement.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro (EUR) is fully convertible in both current and capital account transactions. Luxembourg imposes no exchange controls, no prior approval requirements for profit repatriation, and no restrictions on capital movements within the EU/EEA framework. For an SCSp (Société en Commandite Spéciale), distributions to partners are freely permitted if they comply with the partnership agreement’s cash-flow waterfall and Luxembourg’s statutory rules on limited partnership liabilities, including creditor protection in respect of the general partner. No convertibility hurdles are assessed for the ordinary import and resale of textiles (ISIC 4641).

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement exists. EUR-denominated proceeds from trade activities may be retained abroad or in a Luxembourg account without mandated conversion into EUR. There is no minimum holding period, no percentage conversion quota, and no obligation to declare export proceeds beyond standard statistical reporting to the Banque centrale du Luxembourg for balance-of-payment purposes when the transaction exceeds EUR 12,500, subject to the CSSF’s record-keeping obligations.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), acting within the European System of Central Banks and under the authority of the European Central Bank (ECB); day-to-day foreign exchange and AML supervision delegated to the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium – Luxembourg licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and Payment Institutions (PIs) are permissible for holding the SCSp’s short-term float and for SEPA/instant credit transfers. Legal constraints: EMIs/PIs cannot offer interest-bearing deposit accounts, cannot perform any lending or investment activity, and must route foreign-currency or high-value wire settlements through a correspondent bank that enforces beneficial-ownership transparency. SCSp accounts can be opened with CSSF-regulated fintech operators, but cross-border garment trade flows (especially from non-EU buyers) may be rejected by the fintech’s internal risk policy unless the SCSp provides a clean three-years trade history and a receipt of its anti-money-laundering profile.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% at SCSp level in the default fiscally-transparent regime; if the SCSp is opted-in or viewed as a taxable corporate entity, the Luxembourg combined headline rate is 24.94% (Luxembourg City basis: 17% CIT + 7% solidarity surcharge + 6.75% municipal business tax).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% standard Luxembourg withholding tax on dividends paid from a taxable resident company to non-resident shareholders; 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive once the parent holds directly at least 10% for 12 months, and under most Luxembourg DTAs at 5% or 0% depending on ownership. For a transparent SCSp, no entity-level dividend WHT is imposed on distributions to partners.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing due by 31 May of the calendar year following the tax year-end for entities with a 31 December year-end; extension requests are routinely accepted until 15 September or later upon justified application to the Administration des contributions directes.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Country-by-Country Reporting threshold: EUR 750,000,000 consolidated group revenue triggers mandatory CbC report, Master File and Local File for a Luxembourg MNE group member. In all other cases the ACD may request a complete OECD TP file once related-party transactions in a year exceed EUR 250,000; an SCSp engaged in inventory liquidation must maintain intercompany purchase and sale documentation to defend the arm's length margin.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg applies the OECD BEPS and ATAD substance-over-form rules, including the Principal Purpose Test in tax treaties; an SCSp used for apparel liquidation trade must show real local decision-making, inventory control, a local bank account and adequate administration, otherwise the ACD may disregard the structure, deny treaty benefits and recharacterize profit in Luxembourg.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Incorrect Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of hidden profits plus 10% late-payment interest; administrative fine up to EUR 5,000 per false return; criminal penalty up to EUR 25,000 and imprisonment up to five years under the Luxembourg Abgabenordnung for wilful tax evasion.

Director personal liability: The managing/general partner of the SCSp is jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax when the evasion was ordered or signed by that person; courts may impose civil restitution, travel bans and personal confiscation orders in egregious cases.
Economic Substance Deficit / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty relief and Luxembourg participation exemption; the structure is disregarded as an abusive arrangement under ATAD/PPT; profits are recomputed on a net-worth basis and assessed at 100% of the under-deducted amount; ACD may inform foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: The general partner of an SCSp bears unlimited personal civil liability for partnership debts; directors participating in the shell arrangement face commercial disqualification, and after criminal conviction, travel bans and prison sentences.
Transfer Pricing / Artificial Margin Split

Statutory sanction: ACD transfer pricing adjustment to an arm's length resale price margin; additional CIT and net-wealth tax at the partner level; an intentional mispricing penalty of 30-50% of the corrected tax may be applied.

Director personal liability: Luxembourg secondary adjustment mechanisms allow recovery from the related foreign party if the Luxembourg partner cannot pay; directors who intentionally instruct below-market prices are personally liable for the penal surcharge if the adjustment is linked to their act.
AML/CFT and False Documentation
