Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum statutory capital EUR 12,000, fully subscribed and paid in cash on formation; the amount must be deposited and blocked in a Luxembourg-licensed bank account pending registration (escrow release occurs only upon RCS certificate issuance).

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance for any physical office premises must be obtained from the Fire and Rescue Service (Service d'incendie et de secours - SIS) via the commune's building permit certificate; structural load confirmation for the office floor is issued by the municipal 'Commune' urbanism department following a simplified technical review. Workplace safety clearance is issued by the Luxembourg Inspectorate of Labour and Mines (ITM) after verification that the registered office space meets basic health and safety requirements for seated logistical/administrative staff, including emergency exits, electrical safety, and first-aid provisions.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum statutory capital EUR 12,000, fully subscribed and paid in cash on formation; the amount must be deposited and blocked in a Luxembourg-licensed bank account pending registration (escrow release occurs only upon RCS certificate issuance).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct and corporate income taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; unified business portal: Guichet.lu / MyGuichet.lu

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity engaging in taxable activities in Luxembourg must appoint a fiscal representative (unless established within the EU) and submit the Luxembourg VAT registration application via MyGuichet.lu to the AEDT, providing proof of EU establishment, entity constitutive documents, and expected turnover. Where Luxembourg-source income other than VAT arises, the foreign entity must also register with the ACD using the standard non-resident business registration procedure and obtain a Luxembourg national identification number (numéro d'identification).

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; a Luxembourg SARL is a resident company taxed at corporate income tax, municipal business tax, and net wealth tax on worldwide income, subject to treaty and territorial relief; no tax-exempt zone or pass-through classification applies to a standard commercial SARL. — A foreign entity engaging in taxable activities in Luxembourg must appoint a fiscal representative (unless established within the EU) and submit the Luxembourg VAT registration application via MyGuichet.lu to the AEDT, providing proof of EU establishment, entity constitutive documents, and expected turnover. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a Luxembourg SARL is a resident company taxed at corporate income tax, municipal business tax, and net wealth tax on worldwide income, subject to treaty and territorial relief; no tax-exempt zone or pass-through classification applies to a standard commercial SARL.

Available election forms

Luxembourg does not operate a check-the-box or entity-classification election regime for direct tax. A SARL may elect VAT grouping under Article 61 of the Luxembourg VAT law with related Luxembourg entities, may apply for advance tax agreements with the ACD on transfer pricing or hybrid transactions, and must implement domestic and EU rules on hybrid mismatch arrangements (ATAD) with no elective opt-out for mismatches.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No annual turnover threshold for non-resident suppliers; any taxable supply in Luxembourg triggers registration. A Luxembourg resident small enterprise may qualify for the annual VAT exemption of €35,000 (excluding VAT) under Article 57 of the VAT law, except for certain businesses and supplies. Intra-Community distance sales of goods benefit from the €10,000 pan-EU distance-selling threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% VAT applies to supplies exported outside the EU, provided the exporter obtains a customs export declaration and evidence of physical removal from the EU within 90 days. Services: market research and comparable services supplied to a taxable person established outside Luxembourg and outside the EU are outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT (reverse-charge jurisdiction in the customer's country). B2B services to EU taxable customers are taxable where the customer is established; B2C services may be zero-rated where effective use and enjoyment lies outside the EU and the customer is outside the EU, otherwise the standard Luxembourg rate applies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document)
  • Bill of Lading / CMR / Air Waybill
  • Proof of physical removal from EU (certified export document)
  • Commercial invoice and contract
  • Bank or payment system records showing cross-border receipt (e.g., Inward Remittance Certificate)

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based reporting, or digital stamping is currently imposed by the AEDT. Public-sector (B2G) electronic invoicing is mandatory via PEPPOL BIS 3.0; B2B e-invoicing is voluntary and is to follow EN 16931, with future mandatory requirements planned under EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA).

The AEDT may retrospectively disallow zero-rating where the exporter fails to retain the entire export dossier. Late VAT or wrongly claimed zero-rating attracts legal interest at 0.6% per month plus administrative fines; deliberate or fraudulent misuse of zero-rating may lead to criminal sanctions and full clawback of unreported VAT. Records supporting export zero-rating must be preserved for a minimum of 10 years.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a euro-area member state; the euro is fully convertible and no exchange-control restrictions apply to current or capital account transactions under Articles 63-65 TFEU. Profit remittance is unrestricted, subject to the standard 15% dividend withholding tax, which is reduced to 0% where the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive or the Luxembourg participation exemption conditions (minimum 10% shareholding or EUR 1.2 million acquisition price, 12-month holding period) are satisfied. No ministerial authorization or prior BCL approval is required for dividend distribution, loan repayment, or capital repatriation, provided statutory corporate-law solvency tests are met.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory obligation exists in Luxembourg to surrender, convert, or maintain export proceeds in any mandated currency. Export receipts may be retained indefinitely in EUR or foreign currency, and no minimum conversion ratio applies. The only compliance duty is statistical: cross-border transactions exceeding EUR 12,500 per transaction must be declared to the BCL for balance-of-payments reporting purposes, and the entity must retain underlying commercial documentation supporting each declaration.

Central bank supervision: Banque Centrale du Luxembourg (BCL); banking and financial-sector supervision additionally performed by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility is High for low-value, high-volume payout rails, subject to PSD2 legal constraints. A non-EEA EMI (e.g., FCA-licensed Wise) may provide payment services in Luxembourg only through a valid EEA/EEA-passported entity such as Wise Payments Europe S.A. or another CSSF-recognised EMI. The target rails, PayPal Mass Payout and Wise, are operationally viable for micro-payouts to consumer panelists, but a Tier-1 Luxembourg bank account remains mandatory as the primary treasury nester for subscribed capital, dividend capacity, and statutory reserve maintenance; the EMI route is supplementary, not structural.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% combined headline in Luxembourg City for FY2025: 17% national corporate income tax (CIT), plus a 7% unemployment fund contribution calculated on the CIT, plus 6.75% municipal business tax (communal multiplier of 225% applied to the 3% base rate).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory Luxembourg withholding tax on dividends distributed by a resident société de capitaux (SARL) to non-resident shareholders. The rate is reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive where the shareholder holds at least 10% of the SARL's capital for an uninterrupted 12-month period, or to 0%/5%/15% under applicable double-tax treaties depending on the holding structure; US- and UK-treaty investors typically secure 5% for qualifying 10%+ holdings.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be filed no later than 31 December of the year following the tax year (e.g., FY2025 return due 31 December 2026). The Luxembourg direct tax administration (Administration des contributions directes, AED) may send a reminder or an official tax-form package; failure to receive the package does not relieve the filing obligation. Quarterly prepayments of income tax are due on 10 March, 10 June, 10 September, and 10 December.

Deadline: · Frequency:

No fixed calendar deadline for submission, but the documentation must exist at the time of the tax return and be produced within 30 days of the AED request. Late production triggers the documentation penalty of up to EUR 25,000.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR. Local File and Master File obligations arise where the Luxembourg SARL concludes aggregate related-party transactions exceeding EUR 1,000,000 in a fiscal year; the Master File is mandatory where the consolidated group revenue of the ultimate parent exceeds EUR 750,000,000. Country-by-Country Reporting applies at group level where the consolidated revenue reaches EUR 750,000,000, with automatic exchange through the OECD framework. Documentation must be delivered to the AED within 30 days of request; non-production triggers a fine of up to EUR 25,000.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not apply an offshore-style Economic Substance Regulations statute (as in UAE or similar regimes); the equivalent substance requirements flow from the GAAR in Article 6 of the Steueranpassungsgesetz (abuse of forms), the 2019 Luxembourg transfer-pricing framework, the EU ATAD minimum substance standards, the proposed EU Unshell (ATAD 3) directive, and the beneficial-ownership jurisprudence of the CJEU. For this consumer-panel survey platform, the operational reality of premises, employees, the DEMPE function over the survey software, and the local decision-making of the gérant will satisfy the doctrine; a letterbox SARL without staff, office, or local contracts would be denied treaty benefits, have its income reattributed to the foreign shareholder, and face a 100% recapture of tax incentives.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraud (fraude fiscale)

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under Article 396 of the Steueranpassungsgesetz and the Luxembourg anti-fraud law of 6 July 2016: imprisonment from 1 to 5 years and a fine up to EUR 250,000 for individuals; legal persons are additionally liable to fines up to EUR 1,000,000. The tax administration reassesses the suppressed income, applies the 10% majoration, charges monthly interest of 0.6%, and the fraudulent documents can be used to extend the statute of limitations to 10 years.

Director personal liability: The gérant and any de facto director who signed or directed the false return are personally subject to the criminal fines and imprisonment; courts routinely issue travel bans, asset freezes, and confiscation orders. Directors may also be held civilly liable in solidum for the company's tax shortfall where the evasion results from their intentional wrongful act.
