Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDTV) for VAT and indirect taxes; online portal: myguichet.lu and guichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

For a Luxembourg SCSp, the entity first obtains legal existence and a national registration number (matricule national) by filing its articles of association and managerial information with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce (RCS) via Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR). After RCS registration, the SCSp files VAT Form 640 (registration as taxable person) with the AEDTV through MyGuichet or by paper, attaching the RCS excerpt, partnership agreement, proof of registered office in Luxembourg, identification of all partners and the manager (whether a resident individual or corporate entity), and a description of the taxable activity. The AEDTV issues a VAT identification number (format LU + 8 digits). For corporate income tax, the SCSp itself is fiscally transparent; however, the competent director opens a tax file on the partnership for reporting purposes and to allocate income to partners under their respective residence tax authorities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — The SCSp is a contractual partnership that is fiscally transparent for corporate income tax and net wealth tax; it is not a separate taxable person. Each partner is individually taxed on its share of the partnership's income in accordance with Luxembourg law. For VAT purposes, the SCSp is treated as a separate taxable person where it carries out an economic activity in its own name. The registered office and place of effective management in Luxembourg determine territorial fiscal presence, but because the SCSp is transparent, direct tax exposure arises at the partner level.

Available election forms

Luxembourg does not provide a 'check-the-box' election comparable to the United States. The SCSp is mandatorily fiscally transparent; the partnership agreement cannot elect to convert the vehicle into a corporation for local tax purposes. Within the transparent framework, the partners may contractually allocate profits, losses, and distributions, and may elect to apply the provisions of the Luxembourg law implementing ATAD and hybrid mismatch rules. For VAT, an SCSp may voluntarily register for VAT upon conducting a taxable activity and may elect the small-business exemption conditions where annual turnover is below the statutory threshold; no analogous election exists for income tax classification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg applies full currency convertibility under EU Treaty capital and payments provisions. Both current and capital account transactions are freely permitted for residents and non-residents. Profit remittance from a Luxembourg SCSp is treated as a distributive payment to partners and may be made without exchange control approval, subject to CRS, FATCA, AML/KYC verification, and tax withholding review where applicable. No Luxembourg exchange control licence is required for inbound or outbound transfers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion timeline, or forced repatriation ratio for export receipts in Luxembourg. Exporters of dental zirconia blocks and other goods may retain foreign currency in bank accounts or receive EUR directly. Balance-of-payments statistical reporting to the BCL may apply above set thresholds, but no conversion obligation exists.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating within the Eurosystem; financial supervision is exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for banks, payment institutions, and e-money institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium for SCSp capital subscriptions and escrow purposes; more viable for day-to-day operational FX conversion within the EEA. CSSF-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) with PSD2 passporting rights can open payment accounts and execute FX conversion for euro-denominated flows, but they cannot grant credit, hold notarial escrow balances, or perform the full banking functions expected for capital injection confirmation. Offshore digital EMIs without an EEA passport generally cannot lawfully provide payment services to Luxembourg entities.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

17% standard Luxembourg corporate income tax (CIT) rate for taxable profits exceeding EUR 175,000; 15% up to EUR 175,000; plus 7% solidarity surcharge and municipal business tax. An LU SCSp is a fiscally transparent partnership and is not itself subject to CIT; taxable profit is allocated to partners and taxed in their hands.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory Luxembourg withholding tax on dividends paid by a resident fully taxable company. A transparent SCSp distribution is not a dividend for Luxembourg WHT purposes; the partners are taxed directly. If the SCSp opts/ is treated as a taxable corporation, a subsequent distribution to partners may be subject to 15% WHT, with treaty relief where available.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 / Revenu de référence Taxable result of the SCSp or corporate partner, reconciled to audited commercial accounts. A non-resident partner does not file this box unless it carries on a Luxembourg permanent establishment; enter the PE-share attributable to the Luxembourg activity. Late filing may lead to a default assessment; late-filing penalty up to 10% of the tax due and late-payment interest at 0.80% per month (9.60% p.a.). Fraudulent omission carries criminal penalties.
Box 2 / Frais professionnels Deductible business expenses, including production costs, depreciation, R&D and service fees. Only expenses economically connected with the Luxembourg PE or with the partner’s taxable Luxembourg-source income are deductible. Late filing may lead to a default assessment; late-filing penalty up to 10% of the tax due and late-payment interest at 0.80% per month (9.60% p.a.). Fraudulent omission carries criminal penalties.
Box 3 / Réintégrations Non-deductible items, hidden profit distributions, taxes and other add-backs. Non-resident partners must add back items that are non-deductible under Luxembourg rules even if deductible in the home country. Late filing may lead to a default assessment; late-filing penalty up to 10% of the tax due and late-payment interest at 0.80% per month (9.60% p.a.). Fraudulent omission carries criminal penalties.
Box 4 / Perte reportable Tax loss carryforward claimed in the current year. Transparent SCSp losses are allocated annually to partners; the partner claims loss relief under its own tax rules and with applicable anti-abuse limitations. Late filing may lead to a default assessment; late-filing penalty up to 10% of the tax due and late-payment interest at 0.80% per month (9.60% p.a.). Fraudulent omission carries criminal penalties.
Box 5 / Réductions (IP Box, NID) Qualifying IP income deduction (80% exemption) and notional interest deduction. Claim requires a Luxembourg economic-substance/use assessment at partner level; supporting documentation must be contemporaneous. Late filing may lead to a default assessment; late-filing penalty up to 10% of the tax due and late-payment interest at 0.80% per month (9.60% p.a.). Fraudulent omission carries criminal penalties.
Box 6 / Répartition du résultat entre associés Partnership profit/loss allocation schedule to general and limited partners. Non-resident limited partners with no Luxembourg PE generally have limited or no Luxembourg tax exposure; residence certificate is necessary if treaty protection is claimed. Late filing may lead to a default assessment; late-filing penalty up to 10% of the tax due and late-payment interest at 0.80% per month (9.60% p.a.). Fraudulent omission carries criminal penalties.
Box 7 / Signature et qualité Representative declaration, email and reference to the tax adviser. Non-resident partners with a Luxembourg filing obligation must appoint a Luxembourg tax representative with power of attorney if so required by the ACD. Late filing may lead to a default assessment; late-filing penalty up to 10% of the tax due and late-payment interest at 0.80% per month (9.60% p.a.). Fraudulent omission carries criminal penalties.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Luxembourg CbC reporting threshold: consolidated group revenue of EUR 750,000,000. Master File/Local File contemporaneous documentation is required when related-party transactions exceed EUR 500,000 in aggregate (administrative practice); the ACD may also require documentation for any quantum under Article 56bis L.I.R.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not maintain a Gulf-Cooperation-Council-style ESR statute, but substance is the touchstone of Luxembourg anti-abuse, treaty-benefit, IP-regime and ATAD GAAR analysis. An SCSp holding/operating intellectual property, invoicing dental blocks, or booking intercompany services must show real decision-making, qualified staff, operational premises, appropriate expenditure, and economic risk in Luxembourg; a letterbox SCSp is exposed to recharacterization at partner level.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and fraudulent misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Additional tax assessments, 0.80% monthly late-payment interest, criminal fines up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment for up to five years. The SCSp’s tax advantages are voided retroactively.

Director personal liability: Directors/managers of the general partner may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and civil restitution; travel bans and imprisonment can be imposed in aggravated fraud cases.
Failure to file, late filing and non-cooperation with ACD

Statutory sanction: Default tax assessment for the SCSp/partners; late-filing penalty up to 10% of assessed tax; monthly late-payment interest; administrative fines for defective filing; possible publication of tax-default facts.

Director personal liability: The general partner’s directors can be held personally liable for penalties if the default results from their wilful misconduct or gross negligence.
Shell company / abusive treaty shopping / GAAR (ATAD)

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of exemption/deduction, recharacterization as taxable dividend/royalty/interest, additional tax and interest, potential exit-tax adjustments on migrated IP. Cross-border arrangements may be targeted by the ACD and foreign anti-abuse rules.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign artificial tax shelter documents may face civil liability for the lost tax, fines and travel restrictions under criminal tax-fraud procedure.
Transfer pricing documentation failure and non-arm’s length pricing

Statutory sanction: Primary transfer-pricing adjustment, secondary adjustments on deemed dividends, penalties up to 5% of the cross-border adjustment, and interest. No Luxembourg safe harbor protects excessive intercompany margins without functional/substance analysis.

Director personal liability: Personal liability may be asserted against directors who approved intentionally mispriced related-party contracts or destroyed contemporaneous evidence.
AML / beneficial-ownership register failures

Statutory sanction: Criminal sanctions under Luxembourg AML laws, including fines of up to EUR 1,250,000 for legal persons and imprisonment for natural persons; administrative proceedings may suspend partnership activities and deregister the SCSp.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner can be named in AML investigations, blocked from travel, fined personally and subject to imprisonment where laundering is proved.
