Restricted acceptance. Luxembourg law (Law of 13 August 2021 on commercial domiciliation and Article 2 of the Law of 19 December 2002) requires the registered office (siège social statutaire) to be physically located in the Grand Duchy. A fully virtual office arrangement — i.e., no lease, no personnel, no local books — is categorically inadmissible.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg maintains full liberalisation of both capital and current account transactions under Articles 63-65 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU); the euro is unconditionally convertible and freely transferable in and out of the territory. There are no exchange controls, no prior-approval thresholds, and no profit-remittance restrictions applicable to a resident SCSp. Distributions of partnership profits, liquidation proceeds, interest, and royalties may be settled directly from the entity's bank accounts without any BCL or CSSF clearance. The SCSp's tax-pass-through status has no bearing on convertibility; the only practical requirements are standard AML sanctions screening, source-of-funds evidence, and, where third-currency (USD/CNY) flows are involved, the availability of a corresponding nostro account at the settlement bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Non-applicable. Luxembourg statutes impose no mandatory repatriation timeline, no surrender quota, and no forced conversion ratio for export or commercial proceeds. Receipts from TikTok Shop retail and other e-commerce sales into foreign currencies may be retained in USD, CNY, EUR, or any other freely usable currency, and may be credited to external accounts without central bank approval. Where a bank receives third-currency export proceeds, it will apply its internal transaction monitoring and may request the relevant commercial invoice, marketplace settlement report, or platform payout statement for AML file completion; this documentation requirement is evidentiary only and not a regulatory quota.

Central bank supervision: Banque Centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating within the Eurosystem and under the ultimate monetary authority of the European Central Bank (ECB); prudential supervision of credit institutions and payment/e-money institutions is delegated to the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium feasibility with clearly bounded legal scope. Luxembourg-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and Payment Institutions (PIs), each authorised by the CSSF under the Law of 10 November 2009 and the Law of 10 October 2020, may be used as operationally efficient payout channels for the TikTok Shop settlement stream, including the targeted Wise_B2B rail. Wise B2B is viable as a correspondent or ancillary disbursement channel for merchant balances and low-value supplier payouts, subject to Wise's own entity-level underwriting. Legal constraint: EMIs cannot accept commercial deposits, pay interest, extend credit, or provide letter-of-credit facilities; they are not a substitute for a Tier-1 bank as the exclusive treasury settlement venue. Because the SCSp is an optically transparent partnership with no statutory capital requirement, fintech counterparties may classify the entity as elevated-risk and will demand full RBE beneficial-ownership disclosure, a complete payment-flow diagram, and a demonstrated transaction history before activating the rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant SWIFT/SEPA protocol: (i) EU-resident investors subscribe by SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) to the SCSp's EUR IBAN using ISO 20022 Purpose Code 'SUBS' (subscription) or 'INVE' (investment), the exact code to be confirmed with the receiving bank at account opening; (ii) non-EU investors remit by SWIFT MT103 to the SCSp settlement account, with Field 70 including 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION - [investor legal name] - [SCSp name]' and Purpose Code 'INVE'; USD-denominated subscriptions settle through the bank's nostro-clearing network and are converted to EUR at the commercial spot rate, unless the account is opened multi-currency. The bank will require the executed subscription agreement, a capital-account entry certificate, and UBO confirmation before the injection is cleared through the compliance queue. No BCL or CSSF licensing approval is required for capital inflows into a Luxembourg SCSp; however, the CSSF's economic-substance review may request treasury records during a supervisory visit, so the SCSp should retain a complete funding-generation audit trail comprising investor instruction letters, SWIFT confirmations, and board or general-partner resolutions.The SCSp will operate a two-rail disbursement architecture: (1) SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) for EU suppliers and intra-EU fulfilment costs, cleared through the Tier-1 bank's TIPS-platform connectivity; (2) SWIFT MT103 transfers with ISO 20022 Purpose Code 'TRAD' or 'TRCP' for non-EU suppliers, each message carrying the purchase-order number, commercial invoice reference, and TikTok Shop order identifier in Field 70 to facilitate beneficiary-bank compliance screening and reduce return-risk. For higher-value inventory procurement tranches, a standby Commercial Letter of Credit may be issued by Société Générale Luxembourg in favour of established Asian suppliers when documentary security is required; however, given the Low_Risk_Standard banking tier of this ISIC 4791 social-commerce model, rolling open-account wire payments are expected to dominate, with the LC used only as a selective risk-control overlay for new or concentrated counterparties. Inbound TikTok_Shop_Payout receipts arrive by platform-initiated bank wire into the SCSp's Tier-1 account, and Wise B2B is deployed as a programmed sub-rail for commissions, refunds, and low-value disbursements, all reconciled daily to the general ledger and subject to monthly independent reconciliation review.Outright deliverable FX Forwards (EUR/USD, EUR/CNY) executed under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement and a Luxembourg-law Credit Support Annex, restricted to hedging forecast TikTok Shop settlement exposure with no speculative open positions permitted in excess of one quarter's projected net receipts.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) permitted solely where the underlying currency is non-convertible or where the settlement bank's correspondent network cannot deliver the currency in Luxembourg; NDFs must be cash-settled in EUR, referenced to a recognised benchmark rate, and backed by a documented trade receivable.Cross-Currency Swaps (XCCY / CCS) for matched asset-liability management when the SCSp holds USD-denominated platform reserves or term inventory finance, enabling period-end rebalancing without speculative intent.Plain-vanilla purchased FX Options (including zero-cost collars) as a conservative hedge overlay; writing naked options is expressly prohibited under the SCSp treasury policy without written authorisation from the general partner and the settlement bank's derivatives desk.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

17% CIT + 7% solidarity surcharge = 18.19% effective corporate income tax for taxable corporate partners; SCSp itself is fiscally transparent and subject to 0% at entity level. Municipal business tax (communal trade tax) applies to corporate partners with a Luxembourg permanent establishment (Luxembourg City rate 6.75%), producing a combined headline burden of approximately 24.94%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on distributions made by an SCSp to its partners: Luxembourg treats profit distributions of a fiscally transparent SCSp as non-dividend allocations, so no Luxembourg dividend withholding tax is imposed at entity level. For reference, dividends paid by a Luxembourg resident stock company are subject to a 15% domestic WHT, reduced to 0% under the participation exemption or an applicable DTA.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File: required where the consolidated group turnover equals or exceeds EUR 50 million. Local File: required where the total related-party transactions of the Luxembourg entity exceed EUR 1 million (operational/goods/services) or EUR 250,000 (intra-group financial transactions). Country-by-Country Report: mandatory for groups with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million (if Luxembourg is the ultimate parent or the SCSp is designated as surrogate filer).

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not operate a BVI-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime. However, substantive economic presence is enforced through: (i) the domestic general anti-abuse rule (Art. 6 Abgabenordnung), (ii) the OECD/G20 BEPS Principal Purpose Test under the MLI as implemented in Luxembourg DTAs, (iii) the Luxembourg tax circulars (LIR 14/2 on holding companies and LIR 56/1 on transfer pricing documentation) requiring adequate premises, staff, management decisions, and risk control, and (iv) ATAD interest-limitation and CFC rules. An SCSp deriving e-commerce income from TikTok Shop retail must demonstrate real substance (physical office, employees, local governance, and business records) to maintain pass-through status and treaty benefits; absence of substance may trigger recharacterisation, denial of treaty advantages, or classification as a tax-abusive shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Failure to File (Fraude Fiscale)

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud under Articles 203-207 of the General Tax Law: fine of up to EUR 25,000 and imprisonment of 8 days to 5 years; additionally, civil reassessment of the full taxable base, default interest of 0.6% per month, and a 10% negligence surcharge on reassessed tax.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner (commandité) face joint and several personal liability for the SCSp's unpaid taxes if they directed or tolerated the concealment; criminal convictions can trigger bans from managing companies in Luxembourg, travel restrictions pending prosecution, and personal seizure of assets.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance (Abus de Droit)

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation of the entire arrangement by the ACD under Article 6 Abgabenordnung; denial of DTA benefits under the Principal Purpose Test; recomputation of taxable income at a deemed arm's-length level; administrative penalties of up to EUR 25,000; reassessment statute of limitations extended from 5 to 10 years for abuse cases.

Director personal liability: General partners have unlimited liability for the SCSp's obligations; limited partners (commanditaires) who engage in management or exercise internal control forfeit limited liability (Art. 24-1, Law of 10 August 1915). Directors of the corporate general partner can be held personally liable for the tax deficiency where the abuse was a wrongful act (Art. 441-15, Law of 10 August 1915).
AML and UBO Register Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Law of 12 November 2004 and the RBE Law of 13 January 2019: criminal fines up to EUR 250,000 for entities and imprisonment up to 5 years for individuals in serious money-laundering cases; CSSF administrative sanctions may include warnings, injunctions, and fines up to EUR 10 million for supervised professionals; the RBE register breach alone attracts fines of EUR 500 to EUR 25,000 with doubling for repeat offences.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal liability for money-laundering offences (Criminal Code Arts. 506-1 to 506-8), including imprisonment of 1 to 5 years; travel bans and asset freezes can be ordered during investigation; deliberate AML failures also trigger civil liability for resulting losses of the partnership's creditors.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Shifting (Artificial Profit Allocation)

Statutory sanction: Upward arm's-length adjustment of the SCSp's taxable income by the ACD; recharacterisation of a financing instrument as equity (dangerous debt); disallowance of excessive interest under the ATAD interest limitation rule (30% EBITDA cap); default interest at 0.6% per month; administrative fine up to EUR 25,000; reassessment within the extended 10-year period for international profit shifting.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approved or orchestrated sham pricing become jointly and severally liable for the additional tax and interest; in cross-border aggravated cases, criminal conviction for tax evasion (Art. 207 AO) can result in imprisonment up to 5 years and an order prohibiting the director from operating a business in any Luxembourg company.
