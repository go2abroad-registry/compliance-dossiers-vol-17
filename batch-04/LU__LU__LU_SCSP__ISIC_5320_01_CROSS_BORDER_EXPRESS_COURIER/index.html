Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) - Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), Luxembourg, under the supervision of the Ministry of Justice.

Governing statute

Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended, in particular Articles 101-1 et seq. on the Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp); Grand-Ducal Regulation on the Register of Commerce and Companies.

Formation / constitutional filings

Demande d'inscription (registration request) submitted through the LBR eSpace portal, together with the acte de société (partnership agreement) or a certified extract thereof.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 21 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to the SCSp. The partnership agreement determines the value and timing of partners' contributions; no escrow account is required. In-kind contributions must be described and valued in the partnership agreement.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety approval from the municipal fire prevention authority and the Corps Grand-Ducal d'Incendie et de Secours (CGDIS); structural load inspection certificate by a registered engineer for mezzanine floors and racking; occupational health and safety clearance from the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM), including first-aid equipment, ventilation, and emergency evacuation plans.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to the SCSp. The partnership agreement determines the value and timing of partners' contributions; no escrow account is required. In-kind contributions must be described and valued in the partnership agreement.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD) for direct taxes; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT and registration duties; Administration des Douanes et Accises for customs and excise. Principal public portal: www.guichet.lu; e-filing portals: ACD eSpace and MyGuichet.

E-government filing portal

A foreign SCSp operator must first complete Luxembourg RCS registration through the Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) to obtain the 21-digit business registration number and the national tax identification number. For VAT, file VAT Form 640/1 (non-established taxable person) or standard Form 640 with the AEDT before carrying out taxable supplies; there is no minimum capital requirement for the SCSp. For customs/export operations, obtain an EORI number from the Administration des Douanes et Accises. Non-established operators making B2B reverse-charge supplies are generally required to register only where Luxembourg VAT is due; however, a physical express-courier branch, warehouse or regular local pickup/delivery activity creates a fixed establishment and mandates full VAT registration, invoicing and periodic returns.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Luxembourg has no elective check-the-box regime. An SCSp is mandatorily fiscally transparent for direct-tax purposes: all income, gains, deductions and credits are allocated to partners in accordance with the partnership agreement. If a corporate partner is a general partner, that partner is separately subject to Luxembourg corporate income tax on its allocable share; the SCSp itself does not pay CIT. Luxembourg applies ATAD anti-hybrid election/disregard rules, which may re-characterize an SCSp that is transparent in Luxembourg but non-transparent or ignored in the partner's jurisdiction, and may deny double-deduction or deduction-without-inclusion outcomes. For VAT only, the SCSp can be registered as an independent taxable person despite lacking legal personality, and it may enter a Luxembourg VAT group under applicable conditions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Non-resident/non-established express courier operators: no VAT registration threshold and no de minimis; registration is due from the first Luxembourg-taxable supply. Resident/established small businesses may benefit from the EUR 35,000 annual-turnover exemption, but this exemption does not cover export/intra-EU supplies, reverse-charge services, or courier services requiring a fixed establishment; the intra-EU distance-sales threshold in Luxembourg is EUR 100,000. Express courier services physically performed in Luxembourg generally exceed any threshold because the operator has a local establishment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports to non-EU destinations qualify for 0% Luxembourg VAT where (i) title passes to the customer, (ii) the goods are physically dispatched/transported out of the EU, and (iii) the exporter holds the customs export evidence accepted by the AEDT. Express courier consignments are zero-rated when the operator is the exporter or the transport provider and a valid Union export declaration (SAD/EX) confirms exit. Ancillary transport services (freight forwarding, loading, handling, warehousing incidental to export) supplied directly in connection with third-country exports/imports are zero-rated regardless of the service provider's location if invoiced to the exporter/importer/consignee/freight forwarder. Intra-EU despatches are exempt under Article 138 EU VAT Directive if the recipient has a valid EU VAT ID and the goods are transported to another Member State; EC Sales Lists and quarterly recapitulative statements are mandatory.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Union Customs Code SAD/EX lodged in AES/ECS, with export licence/declaration and movement reference number)
  • Bill of Lading or Master/HAWB Air Waybill evidencing dispatch from Luxembourg and exit from the EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or equivalent SWIFT/MT103 bank credit evidence) demonstrating collection/repatriation of export proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital seal mandate is currently in force in Luxembourg for express-courier invoicing. Taxpayers may issue electronic invoices subject to authenticity, integrity and legibility standards. Luxembourg has announced a phased Peppol-based mandatory B2B e-invoicing reform for established taxable persons, to be implemented prospectively; EDI or Peppol BIS formats must be preserved for audit. Customs exports must be filed electronically via the Union Customs Code's AES/ECS using a SAD/EX declaration, and operators must retain the LRN/export confirmation and ICS records.

The AEDT and customs authorities apply heightened scrutiny to high-volume cross-border courier zero-rating. Missing or inconsistent SAD/EX, transport or payment documentation will cause a reassessment at 17% VAT on the courier charge and/or the underlying goods, plus late-payment interest and administrative surcharges. Deliberate false customs declarations or manipulated export evidence attract criminal tax-fraud penalties including imprisonment, forfeiture and fines, as well as retrospective clawbacks up to the full VAT not collected, with the limitation periods extended in fraud cases. Failure to evidence receipt/repatriation of export proceeds within the 180-day documentary window, absent a valid commercial justification, similarly converts the zero-rating to taxable supply and triggers corrective VAT filings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible. Luxembourg applies the EU free movement of capital regime (TFEU Articles 63-66) and maintains no isolated current-account or capital-account exchange-control regime. A Luxembourg SCSp is unrestricted in receiving equity, debt, and revenue from non-resident partners, provided payments are routed through a supervised credit institution. The practical obstacles are not exchange controls but documentary proof of economic substance and of source of funds required by banks. Profit remittance to non-resident partners is legally unencumbered, with no Luxembourg withholding tax on partnership profit distributions, and only standard withholding considerations apply to Luxembourg-source interest or royalties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion requirement exists in Luxembourg. Foreign-currency courier receipts (USD, GBP, CNY) may be retained in foreign-currency accounts, switched into EUR, or used directly for outbound trade settlements at the SCSp's discretion. The only applicable obligations are standard bank anti-money-laundering verification, ECB/BCL statistical balance-of-payments reporting on cross-border payment thresholds, and corporate tax booking under Luxembourg GAAP.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), in coordination with the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for supervised banking conduct and the Ministry of Finance for exchange-control policy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is Medium, subject to EEA passporting restrictions. An offshore digital EMI (e-money institution authorised in another EEA state) may onboard the SCSp only for payment services under PSD2; it cannot accept deposits or extend credit. Legal constraints include mandatory safeguarding of client funds, prohibition of interest-bearing operational balances, the absence of an overdraft facility, and heightened due diligence because the SCSp has no legal personality and must be contractually represented by its general partner.

Unusual transaction patterns with unrelated courier counterparties, including high-volume small-amount ACH/POS settlements

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from non-resident parents or partners must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 to the SCSp's designated Luxembourg account using the ECB/BCL balance-of-payments purpose code for Direct Investment (D) or Financial Credit (F). Documentary verification includes the capital subscription deed, partners' resolution, loan agreement where applicable, and a bank-required source-of-funds declaration. Payments above regulatory thresholds are reported monthly to the BCL for monetary and statistical purposes; no exchange-control permission is required.Outbound settlements use a tiered clearing structure: for EEA and SEPA-zone EUR obligations, SEPA Credit Transfers (SCT) are preferred; for third-country courier and trade settlements, SWIFT MT103 (customer transfer) and MT202 (bank-to-bank) instructions are used; for high-value or supply-chain transactions, MT700 commercial letters of credit are issued with negotiating banks; customer refunds and franchise disbursements are executed through corporate cards and ACH/direct debit rails where contractually permitted.
