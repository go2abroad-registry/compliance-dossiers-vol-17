Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct tax; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; RCS business registration via guichet.lu and GeRCS; e-filing portal pclux.lu

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity with no Luxembourg fixed establishment must register for VAT with the AEDT through guichet.lu, generally by appointing a Luxembourg tax representative, before making taxable supplies. The entity must file an application for a Luxembourg corporate tax registration number with the ACD and register with the RCS if it has a Luxembourg establishment; for an SCSp, the partnership itself is registered and obtains a Luxembourg national business number, while the tax classification as fiscally transparent applies at entity level.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign entity with no Luxembourg fixed establishment must register for VAT with the AEDT through guichet.lu, generally by appointing a Luxembourg tax representative, before making taxable supplies. The entity must file an application for a Luxembourg corporate tax registration number with the ACD and register with the RCS if it has a Luxembourg establishment; for an SCSp, the partnership itself is registered and obtains a Luxembourg national business number, while the tax classification as fiscally transparent applies at entity level.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

The SCSp is recognised as a Société en Commandite Spéciale and is fiscally transparent under Luxembourg law by default; Luxembourg does not provide domestic check-the-box elections. Taxpayers may apply to the Commission des décisions fiscales for a binding tax ruling confirming transparency or addressing hybrid mismatch treatment under ATAD, but no general entity-level election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold for non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies in Luxembourg; domestic small enterprise franchise applies below EUR 30,000 annual turnover

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The supply of goods dispatched or transported outside the European Union qualifies for the 0% VAT rate if the exporter conclusively proves that the goods left EU territory; for intra-EU supplies, zero rating applies where the purchaser's valid EU VAT number is provided and transport documentation shows delivery to another Member State. For bulk dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper exports, the SCSp must obtain and retain export declarations and transport evidence within the statutory retention window.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime is currently in force for B2B supplies; mandatory PEPPOL-based e-invoicing applies to B2G public contracts. B2B e-invoicing is voluntary and interoperable formats are accepted for VAT deduction and audit retention. Luxembourg intends to introduce mandatory B2B e-invoicing in line with the EU VAT in the Digital Age initiative once the relevant EU directives are transposed, currently expected after 2027.

If zero-rating is challenged, the AEDT re-characterises the supply as taxable and issues a VAT assessment with default interest from the original due date; administrative fines may reach 50% of the tax evaded and increase up to 100% for fraudulent conduct. Deliberately false documentation triggers criminal tax fraud penalties and a mandatory clawback of any refunded VAT, with no limitation on interest accrual during audit proceedings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies. The euro is freely convertible on both current account and capital account, and Luxembourg imposes no exchange-control licensing, no prior authorisation, and no convertibility restrictions for trade-related flows or capital movements. For an SCSp engaged in goods trading, capital contributions, operational funding, and profit remittances are unrestricted; under the Law of 12 July 2012 the SCSp is fiscally transparent, and distributions to non-resident limited partners do not attract Luxembourg dividend withholding tax. The only practical constraints are AML/KYC verification, CSSF regulatory reporting obligations, and standard bank credit approval thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation exists in Luxembourg or the Eurozone. There is no mandatory conversion ratio, and 100% of export receipts may be retained in original currency, converted freely at market rates, or held as euro deposits. The only regulatory duty is statistical: resident traders must classify cross-border transactions under BCL balance-of-payments reporting requirements for monetary statistics, with no administrative restriction on the subsequent use of proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), Luxembourg City, as the Eurosystem monetary authority; exchange-control and financial-sector regulatory authority is shared with the Luxembourg Ministry of Finance (Ministère des Finances) and the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF). Luxembourg applies TFEU Articles 63-66 on the free movement of capital and EU Regulation (EC) No 184/2005 for balance-of-payments statistics.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating is Low-to-Medium with a strong legal-constraint warning. Luxembourg permits treasury flows through PSD2-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) that hold a valid EU/EEA passport accepted by the CSSF, but an unlicensed non-EU EMI cannot lawfully service the SCSp because it lacks a Luxembourg BIC, an appointed CSSF-recognised agent, and would be rejected at the correspondent-bank layer. A physical-goods trading partnership is not an eligible client of virtual-asset-heavy EMIs, and omnibus aggregator settlement models create audit-chain and beneficial-ownership opacity that fails CSSF CDD requirements. The viable use case is limited to incidental low-value euro operational disbursements through an EU-licensed EMI with full SWIFT transparency; the primary liquidity rail must remain a Tier-1 Luxembourg bank account.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

SCSp is a contractual partnership without legal personality, creating ambiguity about the management authority of the general partner and about the sufficiency of the partnership agreement.

Root cause: SCSp is a contractual partnership without legal personality, creating ambiguity about the management authority of the general partner and about the sufficiency of the partnership agreement.

Defense evidence: Notarised or written partnership agreement (contrat de société) expressly conferring on the general partner the powers to open accounts, execute FX orders, and instruct disbursements; current RCS registration extract (extrait du registre) evidencing the SCSp legal form and registration number; certified constitutional documents of the general partner; power of attorney and a resolution of the general partner authorising account opening and treasury transactions; and a formal Luxembourg legal opinion confirming that the SCSp may hold bank accounts and be represented by its general partner.
Non-resident or opaque ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs), including limited partners resident in FATF-monitored or sanction-sensitive jurisdictions, with gaps in source-of-wealth and source-of-funds substantiation.

Root cause: Non-resident or opaque ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs), including limited partners resident in FATF-monitored or sanction-sensitive jurisdictions, with gaps in source-of-wealth and source-of-funds substantiation.

Defense evidence: CSSF-aligned UBO declaration covering the full indirect ownership chain, including every individual controlling more than 25% of the interests; passport and proof-of-address copies of each such individual; source-of-wealth and source-of-funds file (tax returns, inheritance deeds, corporate sale completion statements); certificates of tax residence for all non-resident partners; clean sanctions, PEP, and adverse-media screening results (World-Check or equivalent); and a professional corroboration letter from an EEA-regulated legal, tax, or fiduciary adviser.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and shareholder loans must be routed by SWIFT MT103/MT202 wire transfer to the SCSp Capital Escrow Account at BIL or SGBT, with the BCL balance-of-payments purpose code classified as Direct Investment in a resident enterprise and with SWIFT field 70 referencing the SCSp RCS number, the capital-call notice number, and the remitting investor name. The remitting bank or its correspondent must appear on the receiving bank's approved correspondent list. The receiving bank requires a remitter KYC pack, the capital-call certificate, and an attestation that funds are not derived from third-party pass-through structures or crypto-fiat conversions. Escrow release is conditional upon a signed general partner instruction and a procurement liquidity plan for dehydrated vegetables and spices.USD and EUR trade payments are executed primarily through corporate SWIFT MT103 wires from the operational account, using TARGET2 and SEPA clearing for euro-zone creditors and CHIPS or Fedwire correspondent legs for USD outside the EU. For high-value or new-buyer transactions, commercial documentary letters of credit (sight or usance, SWIFT MT700/MT720) issued by the buyer's bank and advised or confirmed by the Luxembourg bank under UCP 600 provide the most secure clearing rail; standby letters of credit under ISP98 may support open-account terms. Every disbursement must carry the correct ISO 20022 pain.001 classification, the commercial invoice number, the customs export reference, and a BOP trade-in-goods code so that the payment is visibly mapped to a real-economy goods transaction, and must avoid unregulated correspondent or virtual-account aggregator routing.Deliverable spot EUR/USD trades for same-day settlement of trade proceeds under the bank's approved FX credit line.Outright deliverable FX forward contracts with maturities up to 12 months, matched to the exact receivable or payable date of the spice export contracts, documented under a Luxembourg-law ISDA Master Agreement or a bank standard master forward template.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in currencies of buyer jurisdictions with transferability restrictions, such as USD/CNY or USD/INR, permitted only where a verified trade exposure exists and physical delivery of the currency is legally impossible or impracticable in the counterparty's market.FX swap (rollover) transactions for converting residual export receipts into euro funding while preserving a matched future currency obligation for inventory purchases.OTC European-style currency options, being deliverable EUR/USD puts or calls, purchased or written within a covered hedge strategy, with premium and notional amounts capped by the bank's credit limits for a single-purpose trading vehicle.
