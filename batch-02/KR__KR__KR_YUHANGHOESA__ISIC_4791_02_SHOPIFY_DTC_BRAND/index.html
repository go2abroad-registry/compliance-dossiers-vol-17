Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

National Tax Service (NTS) of the Republic of Korea; electronic filing via the HomeTax portal (hometax.go.kr). Export customs clearance is administered by the Korea Customs Service through the UNI-PASS electronic declaration system (unipass.customs.go.kr).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: determine Korean tax nexus; a foreign non-resident DTC operator without a fixed place of business, personnel, warehouse, or permanent establishment in Korea will be treated as a non-resident business operator for VAT and Corporate Income Tax, and must appoint a Korean tax representative. Step 2: execute a power of attorney in favour of a tax representative (individual resident in Korea or a Korean corporation) and obtain the representative's consent. Step 3: file the Business Registration Application (사업자등록신청서) through HomeTax or through the authorised tax representative using the NTS non-resident registration channel, attaching the foreign entity's certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, board resolution on registration, tax representative's power of attorney and identity evidence, and any underlying foreign tax registration. Step 4: NTS validates the application and issues a 10-digit Business Registration Number (BRN; 사업자등록번호), which simultaneously constitutes the VAT identification number and corporate taxpayer file for domestic tax administration. Step 5: complete the VAT registrant file, e-tax invoice enrolment, and list any Korean logistics providers; for a remote Shopify DTC operation, the BRN is issued without a Korean warehousing address unless the cross-border flow creates a physical import, distribution or fulfilment PE. Step 6: maintain continuous tax filing through HomeTax; any change in tax representative, Korean place of business, or address must be filed with the competent district tax office within 15 days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. South Korea does not operate a check-the-box or elective pass-through classification regime. A Yuhan Hoesa with its head office or place of effective management in Korea is legally classified as a resident corporation and taxed at the corporate income tax rate schedule on worldwide income; hybrid mismatch recharacterisation rules are mandatory anti-abuse provisions and not taxpayer elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no general turnover exemption for a domestic Korean DTC entity: every entrepreneur must register for VAT with NTS within 20 days of business commencement, and the same 10-digit BRN serves as the VAT ID. Small businesses with annual VAT-exclusive taxable supplies not exceeding KRW 80 million may elect the simplified taxable person regime, but cannot avoid registration. For a foreign non-resident supplier without a local PE, VAT registration is mandatory when the prior calendar year's VAT-exclusive supply of electronic/digital services to unregistered Korean recipients exceeds KRW 30 million; the NTS commissioner may also require registration to protect the Korean tax base.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from the Republic of Korea are zero-rated under Article 19 of the Value-Added Tax Act, regardless of the DTC sales channel, provided the Korean VAT-registered exporter clears the goods through Korea Customs Service, ships them outside Korea under a foreign transport contract, and obtains foreign currency proceeds from a non-resident buyer. For offshore digital services and intangible rights supplied to a non-resident customer (including game content, digital licences, and certain online services), zero-rating is possible when the supply is made to a foreign recipient under a foreign sales contract, payment is received in foreign currency, and the consumption or use occurs outside Korea; digital services consumed in Korea by Korean recipient require 10% VAT.

  • Korea Customs Service export declaration (수출신고필증) filed through UNI-PASS
  • Commercial invoice issued from the Korean seller to the overseas buyer
  • Packing list describing the goods, quantities and export shipment details
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, or a courier proof-of-despatch scan for international small parcel DTC shipments
  • Foreign exchange bank export settlement certificate (export proceeds purchase/sale certificate) or equivalent inward remittance record showing receipt of the foreign currency amount by the Korean entity
  • Order records, Shopify transfer order and payment gateway transaction history demonstrating the offshore buyer, payment card or alternative payment rail, and absence of Korean delivery address

Korea mandates electronic tax invoices (e-tax invoices) for VAT-registered businesses issuing taxable sales invoices. The e-tax invoice must be transmitted to the NTS via the HomeTax e-tax invoice service, which accepts XML through the official HomeTax API/EDI/web upload channel. There is no pre-issuance fiscal authorisation stamp or real-time clearance gate: the NTS validates the transmission automatically, returns an acceptance result for the invoice to remain compliant, and cross-matches seller-issued and buyer-received invoices in near real time. Anti-fraud functions include automated reconciliation against customs declarations, foreign currency settlement records, and VAT returns; non-transmission, late transmission, or false e-tax invoices attract separate additional tax penalties and loss of input VAT deduction.

The NTS and Korea Customs Service will disallow the 0% rate if the export paperwork is incomplete, contradictory, or not retained for the statutory period, and will reclassify the supply as domestic taxable at 10% VAT with interest from the original filing date. False or unsupported zero-rating exposes the exporter to additional tax penalties under the Value-Added Tax Act, including an extra tax of up to 40% of the under-reported taxable sales in cases of fraudulent overclaim, and to criminal liability under the Punishment of Tax Evaders Act (조세범처벌법) punishable by imprisonment or fines up to three times the tax evaded. The clawback period is generally five years from the original tax return, extended to ten years in fraudulent or undisclosed cases, and the responsible tax representative may also be held secondarily liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

South Korea operates a liberalized but regulated convertibility system. The Korean won is freely convertible for genuine current-account transactions such as export proceeds, service fees, ecommerce settlements, and ordinary operating disbursements; however, capital-account flows -- nonresident equity injections, external loans, overseas investments, and financial derivatives -- are subject to ex-ante notification, post-reporting, or approval supervision under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act. Nonresidents and Korean entities are expected to comply with real-demand documentation at authorized foreign exchange banks. There is no blanket surrender requirement for export receipts, but incoming foreign exchange must be cleared through an onshore foreign exchange bank, and the corporate treasury may retain foreign currency only in a registered foreign-currency deposit account. Profit remittances are generally permissible once audited accounts and tax clearance are evidenced, but the bank will require the shareholder resolution, dividend payment proof, withholding tax certificates, and a foreign-exchange declaration to verify the capital and profit transaction. Domestic fintech/EMI wallets are not substitutes for licensed foreign-exchange banks for corporate treasury, because the FX notification and real-name financial transaction framework requires a regulated nexus.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be settled within one year from the date of exportation unless the authorized foreign exchange bank approves an extension for delays caused by default, dispute, or exchange controls in the buyer's country. There is no fixed statutory percentage requiring mandatory conversion to KRW; exporters may maintain foreign currency balances in onshore foreign-currency deposit accounts, but any conversion or utilization requires an underlying contract and foreign-exchange bank reporting if above the reporting threshold.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Korea (The Central Bank of the Republic of Korea), administered in cooperation with the Ministry of Economy and Finance as the foreign-exchange policy authority under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI platforms that are not licensed under Korea's Electronic Financial Transactions Act and are not authorized foreign-exchange banks have low corporate treasury viability for a Korean-resident Yuhan Hoesa. They may be used indirectly as settlement processors for ecommerce sales, but they cannot legally open the primary KRW corporate operating account, cannot clear inbound FDI capital, and cannot provide the foreign-exchange declaration needed by customs and the central bank. Any offshore EMI payout must ultimately be routed through a domestic licensed bank or payment gateway, otherwise the entity will face FX violation risk and blocked remittances.

Inbound equity capital for the Yuhan Hoesa should be structured as foreign direct investment under the Foreign Investment Promotion Act, with the non-resident shareholder filing a prior notification at KOTRA or through an authorized foreign exchange bank. The capital remittance is transmitted by SWIFT MT103 to the company's dedicated foreign currency escrow/current account at a Korean Tier-1 bank, with remittance information identifying the investor, the Korean company, the acquisition of shares/equity, and the FDI foreign-exchange purpose code. After receipt, the bank issues the foreign exchange declaration confirmation, the entity completes the paid-in capital increase registration with the court registrar, and an external accountant verifies the capital in the statutory capital verification certificate.Operational disbursements for suppliers, digital advertising, and logistics should be cleared via SWIFT MT103 from the Korean bank account to the beneficiary's bank, supported by the underlying invoice and a foreign-exchange payment declaration. For formal B2B purchases and high-value consignments, documentary trade instruments remain permissible: commercial letters of credit issued by the bank (SWIFT MT700/MT710) under ICC UCP600 rules, and documentary collections (D/P and D/A) using shipping documents through correspondent banks. Ecommerce refunds and chargebacks are channeled through the PSP settlement system. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMI platforms that are not licensed under Korea's Electronic Financial Transactions Act and are not authorized foreign-exchange banks have low corporate treasury viability for a Korean-resident Yuhan Hoesa. They may be used indirectly as settlement processors for ecommerce sales, but they cannot legally open the primary KRW corporate operating account, cannot clear inbound FDI capital, and cannot provide the foreign-exchange declaration needed by customs and the central bank. Any offshore EMI payout must ultimately be routed through a domestic licensed bank or payment gateway, otherwise the entity will face FX violation risk and blocked remittances.
