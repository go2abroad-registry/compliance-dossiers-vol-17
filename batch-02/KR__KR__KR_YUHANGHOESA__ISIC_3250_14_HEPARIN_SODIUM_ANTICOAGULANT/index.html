Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. A Korean Yuhan Hoesa is treated as a domestic corporation under the Korean Corporate Tax Act and is taxable at entity level on its worldwide income. It is not eligible for pass-through treatment and is not located in a special tax-exempt zone.

Available election forms

None. Korean domestic tax law does not provide a check-the-box election for domestic limited liability companies, and no elective hybrid-mismatch classification regime exists. The Korean International Tax Adjustment Act applies mandatory matching and controlling provisions to hybrid entities and hybrid instruments, but such rules are not elective.

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover exemption threshold. Every Korean business operator must register with the NTS before commencing business, regardless of expected turnover. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under the Value-Added Tax Act and its Enforcement Decree to tangible goods physically exported from Korea when the Korean exporter of record files an accepted export declaration with the Korea Customs Service, title and risk pass to a non-resident buyer, the goods are delivered outside Korea or into a recognized export route, and the export consideration is earned and settled in foreign currency. The Yuhan Hoesa low-molecular-weight heparin sodium anticoagulant crude fractionation products qualify as exported goods when shipped under a valid pharmaceutical export licence and covered by a customs-cleared export declaration naming the Korean LLC as exporter.

  • Accepted Korea Customs Service Export Declaration (Export Clearance Certificate) issued through UNI-PASS
  • Commercial invoice and packing list corresponding to the customs export declaration
  • Purchase order, sales contract, or binding letter of credit evidencing the overseas buyer and terms of sale
  • Original or authenticated Bill of Lading for ocean freight, or Air Waybill for air freight, naming the Korean LLC as shipper or exporter
  • Foreign exchange receipt settlement confirmation or Certificate of Receipt of Export Proceeds issued by a Korean foreign exchange bank evidencing repatriation of the export proceeds
  • Complete API batch record and certificate of analysis for the heparin sodium anticoagulant product, required for customs and pharmaceutical export substantiation in the ISIC 3250 active pharmaceutical extraction business

All VAT-registered business operators that issue tax invoices must issue electronic tax invoices through the NTS HomeTax electronic tax invoice service or through an NTS-integrated ERP/API channel. The electronic tax invoice must be transmitted to the NTS no later than the day following the supply date, or the invoice issuance date if later, and the NTS performs real-time schema validation on itemised fields including supplier registration number, recipient registration number, supply value, VAT amount, and goods or service description, and then assigns an electronic transmission number. Korea does not use a separate Latin American-style anti-fraud digital stamp; instead, the NTS operates continuous cross-validation between electronic tax invoices, cash receipts for qualifying B2C retail supplies, and Korea Customs Service UNI-PASS export clearance records.

The NTS automatically cross-matches the VAT return zero-rated export line items with Korea Customs Service export declarations and with Korean foreign exchange bank settlement reports. If the exporter cannot substantiate physical export or fails to repatriate foreign currency within the statutory 180-day settlement period, the NTS may reverse the zero-rating, assess VAT at 10% on the invoiced amount, and impose late-payment additional tax, negligence penalties, and other charges under the Framework Act on National Taxes. Fraudulent, false, or deliberately unsubstantiated zero-rating claims may subject the taxpayer to criminal penalties under the Punishment of Tax Evaders Act, including imprisonment and fines, and the NTS may apply retrospective clawback assessments covering prior statutory periods where the export documentation is found to be forged or materially misleading.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

South Korea maintains a generally liberalised but tightly monitored convertibility regime. Current account transactions, including trade in goods and services, are freely convertible and may be settled in KRW or foreign currency through licensed domestic banks. Capital account transactions are also nominally liberalised, yet every inward or outward foreign exchange transaction above the reporting threshold of USD 5,000 or the KRW equivalent must be reported through the Foreign Exchange Information System unless specifically exempted. Dividend remittances and capital repatriation by foreign-invested companies are permitted subject to submission of tax payment certificates, audited financial statements, foreign exchange bank confirmation, and compliance with the Foreign Investment Promotion Act. Despite the absence of an ordinary surrender requirement, export proceeds must be collected before the prescribed settlement deadline and either deposited into a foreign currency account maintained with a Korean foreign exchange bank or repatriated into Korea; conversion into KRW is at the exporter's commercial discretion. The practical convertibility obstacles are therefore documentary and regulatory rather than prohibitive. Banks undertake enhanced due diligence on the ultimate beneficial owner, source of funds, intended transaction profile, and genuine commercial substance of the underlying trade. For a heparin sodium extraction company, audit trails covering the purchase of crude heparin, pharmaceutical toll conversion, and export logistics materially support both convertibility and remittance clearances. No central bank approval is required for routine FX conversion, but the entity must maintain its settlement accounts with a bank authorised as a foreign exchange bank under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act and related regulations, export proceeds must be received within six months from the date of export declaration and must be either repatriated to Korea or deposited in a foreign currency deposit account held with a licensed Korean foreign exchange bank. There is no statutory percentage requiring conversion to KRW; the company may keep export earnings in foreign currency. Any extension beyond six months must be justified and approved by the responsible foreign exchange bank or the relevant foreign exchange authority, and the documentary evidence should include the customs export declaration, commercial invoices, and the corresponding collection file.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Korea, acting together with the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Foreign Exchange Information System under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Thin viability. Korean law does not grant fintech electronic money institutions the status of a foreign exchange bank under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, so offshore EMIs cannot independently clear KRW, open onshore won deposit accounts, register foreign investment, or issue Korean commercial letters of credit. Digital EMI platforms such as cross-border payment processors may be used only as secondary collection or pre-funding conduits that push funds through SWIFT into the entity's domestic corporate account. Any attempt to use an unauthorised EMI as the primary account for customer collections, vendor disbursements, or capital injections would create incomplete transaction-purpose information and expose the company to enhanced AML/CFT scrutiny. The compliant treasury architecture is therefore domestic Korean bank accounts as the regulated settlement rail, with EMIs limited to customer-facing collection conveniences and never serving as KRW clearing infrastructure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The non-resident investor must first deliver the inward foreign direct investment report to the chosen foreign exchange bank or through the Foreign Exchange Information System and obtain a report reference number. The remittance should be sent by SWIFT MT103 to the bank-designated foreign currency investment account, with the payment narration clearly citing the FDI report number, the Korean LLC name, and the purpose of share capital acquisition. On receipt, the bank will verify the identity of remitter and beneficiary, confirm the remittance matches the reported amount, register the foreign investment, and issue a Foreign Investment Registration Certificate. After capital verification, funds may be converted at the bank's prevailing market rate and transferred into the basic KRW operating account. Retain the original MT103, bank advice, FDI report confirmation, board resolution, and certificate of foreign investment registration for future dividend remittance or capital repatriation audits.Outbound trade settlements for the purchase of crude heparin and processing materials will operate over SWIFT MT103 direct wires for small and medium value payments, commercial letters of credit issued or confirmed by a Korean tier-1 bank for larger documented supply contracts, and documentary collections on D/P or D/A terms for repeat low-risk suppliers and buyers. All outward payments above the reporting threshold require FEIS filing and supporting documents such as the import or export contract, customs declaration, bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of analysis. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Thin viability. Korean law does not grant fintech electronic money institutions the status of a foreign exchange bank under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, so offshore EMIs cannot independently clear KRW, open onshore won deposit accounts, register foreign investment, or issue Korean commercial letters of credit. Digital EMI platforms such as cross-border payment processors may be used only as secondary collection or pre-funding conduits that push funds through SWIFT into the entity's domestic corporate account. Any attempt to use an unauthorised EMI as the primary account for customer collections, vendor disbursements, or capital injections would create incomplete transaction-purpose information and expose the company to enhanced AML/CFT scrutiny. The compliant treasury architecture is therefore domestic Korean bank accounts as the regulated settlement rail, with EMIs limited to customer-facing collection conveniences and never serving as KRW clearing infrastructure.

9%-24% progressive: KRT 9% on tax base up to KRW 200M; 19% on KRW 200M-20B; 21% on KRW 20B-300B; 24% above KRW 300B. Local income surtax of 10% on the national corporate tax is also separately imposed.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: KRW 5 billion aggregate cross-border related-party transactions during the fiscal year, or if the global consolidated group revenue exceeds KRW 100 billion and the Korean entity has any reportable cross-border transaction with a related party; both cases require preparation of the Local File, Master File, and annual transfer price report.

Economic substance scope: Korea has no general Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) equal to Jersey/BBO regimes, but the Framework Act on National Taxes and the Law for Coordination of International Tax Affairs (LICTAA) require real substance over another legal formalisation. Korean tax authorities may re-characterise contracts and re-allocate profits to the jurisdiction where the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA), the sourcing personnel, risk control, and strategic decisions are actually exercised. The heparin fractionation plant in Korea must prove ownership of the raw heparin inventory, factory laborers, quality control staff, and procurement decision-making; otherwise the NTS will pierce the Shanghai-Tokyo-Korea contractual veil and allocate at least the local factory return to Korea.
