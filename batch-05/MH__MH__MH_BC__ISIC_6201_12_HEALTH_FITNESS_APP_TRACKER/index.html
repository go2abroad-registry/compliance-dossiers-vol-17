Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; corporate registration and annual fee administration is handled by the Office of the Registrar of Corporations. No integrated national online tax filing portal is operative for non-resident business corporations.

E-government filing portal

The Marshall Islands does not operate a general income tax or VAT system for non-resident business corporations, and no separate TIN or VAT ID is required for a Marshall Islands Business Corporation (Non-Resident). The foreign entity obtains its corporate identity through the Registrar of Corporations by filing Articles of Incorporation and receiving a Registration Certificate. If a tax identification-style reference is required by a foreign bank or intermediary, the corporation may request a Certificate of Good Standing / Tax Status from the Revenue and Taxation Division, supported by its Registration Certificate and registered agent details.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — a non-resident Business Corporation is expressly exempt from Marshall Islands income, withholding, gross revenue, and other national taxes under the Business Corporations Act and applicable non-resident corporation legislation. — The Marshall Islands does not operate a general income tax or VAT system for non-resident business corporations, and no separate TIN or VAT ID is required for a Marshall Islands Business Corporation (Non-Resident). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — a non-resident Business Corporation is expressly exempt from Marshall Islands income, withholding, gross revenue, and other national taxes under the Business Corporations Act and applicable non-resident corporation legislation.

Available election forms

None available. The Marshall Islands has no check-the-box entity classification election and no hybrid mismatch election mechanism. The tax status of a non-resident Business Corporation is fixed by statute and is not elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — the Republic of the Marshall Islands does not impose VAT, GST, sales and use tax, or any comparable general indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Because RMI uses the United States Dollar as legal tender, any analogous customs importer licensing threshold would be denominated in USD, but no indirect tax registration obligation is triggered by supply turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because the Marshall Islands has no VAT/GST levy. Exports of goods and offshore services, including digital health and fitness app tracker services, are outside the scope of RMI indirect taxation; there is therefore no 0% rated export supply and no statutory export refund claim mechanism.

  • No VAT export zero-rating refund bundle is prescribed or required in the Marshall Islands.
  • Customs export declaration/outward manifest retained where tangible goods are physically exported from the RMI.
  • Bill of lading or air waybill retained where applicable for physical export movements.
  • Commercial invoice or service contract for the digital health fitness application services.
  • Inward remittance evidence maintained for corporate record-keeping purposes, not as a condition for any indirect tax refund.

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government clearance API mandate is imposed by the Marshall Islands tax administration. Standard commercial invoicing is sufficient.

As there is no zero-rating or refund regime, no fraudulent export zero-rating clawback can arise. However, false or fraudulent customs declarations are subject to penalties under Marshall Islands customs regulations, including monetary fines, seizure, and forfeiture. Knowingly providing false information to the Registrar of Corporations or the Revenue and Taxation Division may also result in imposition of penalties, cancellation of benefits, or loss of the non-resident tax exempt status.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account. RMI imposes no exchange controls, no advance approval requirements, and no reporting gates on inward or outward USD remittances. A non-resident Business Corporation (MH_BC) deriving no RMI-source income is tax-exempt under the territorial Income Tax Act and faces no RMI withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or management fees. Dividend and profit repatriation is unrestricted in law; practical friction arises only from foreign correspondent bank compliance perception and the completeness of the entity's documentary evidence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rule, holding period, or mandatory conversion ratio applies. Digital export proceeds from Apple IAP, Google Play Billing, and other app-store settlements may be retained indefinitely in USD, converted into any foreign currency, or transferred offshore at treasury discretion without RMI central bank approval. Invoices and payment evidence must be retained under general Banking Act record-keeping obligations but no conversion timeline is prescribed.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) does not maintain a domestic central bank or separate foreign-exchange administration. Monetary authority for the US Dollar, which is the sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association, effectively rests with the United States Federal Reserve. Banking prudential regulation is administered by the Banking Commissioner within the Ministry of Finance under the Banking Act 1980; AML/CFT supervision is exercised by the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU). Corporate registry and Business Corporations Act functions sit with the Registrar of Corporations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low to Medium. RMI law contains no electronic-money-institution or fintech charter, and no non-USD domestic clearing system exists because the US Dollar is the exclusive settlement currency. Offshore EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, or Currencycloud may technically onboard an MH_BC, but they classify an RMI-registered entity in a higher perceived-risk cohort and frequently impose enhanced due diligence, low transaction caps, restricted virtual-account functionality, and termination risk. Recommended architecture: activate the primary operating and reserve accounts at an RMI-licensed bank with a strong US correspondent, or at a US bank accepting the RMI entity under FATCA NFFE status; use an EMI only as a secondary collection rail for app-store proceeds and as a SEPA disbursement channel after the primary account is operational.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder equity is injected in USD by SWIFT MT103 or through a US correspondent Fedwire transfer. The remittance must identify the legal name MH_BC, the RMI Business Corporation registration number, and the governing subscription agreement, with the SWIFT remittance-information field citing the ISO purpose code CAPL (capital payment). Funds clear at the New York correspondent into a segregated Capital Escrow Account and are reclassified to the Operational Account only after the bank's compliance release. The bank concurrently requires a FATCA Form W-8BEN-E evidencing NFFE status, confirmation of the Registered Agent, the entity ID MH_BC, and a board resolution naming treasury signatories. No RMI exchange-control approval is triggered because the USD is legal tender and no conversion step occurs. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - the Marshall Islands has no domestic transfer pricing legislation, no arm's length codification, and no Local File / Master File thresholds. The functional currency is the US dollar. For the health fitness app, arm's length pricing of the app IP and intercompany subscription flows remains relevant only to the tax authorities of the beneficial owner's residence jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: The RMI economic substance regime (aligned to OECD/EU standards) applies to a BC carrying on a relevant activity as a tax resident entity. IP holding and exploitation is an in-scope category, but a non-resident BC that files the non-resident declaration, is tax resident in another jurisdiction, and has no RMI management, employees, premises or expenditure is outside the local substance test. If the entity is characterized as RMI tax resident, it must satisfy the directed-and-managed test and maintain adequate staff and spending in the Marshall Islands, which is commercially unviable for a digital health app; the clean structure therefore preserves non-resident status and avoids the substance burden.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the Section 108 exemption, retroactive assessment of Business Privilege Tax at the general gross-receipts rate with surcharges, monetary fines under the Business Privilege Tax Act and the Criminal Code, and administrative strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers signing a false declaration are joint and severally liable for the assessed tax and penalties; deliberate misrepresentation is a criminal offense carrying imprisonment and personal asset recovery.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Fixed and daily default penalties under the economic substance legislation, mandatory exchange of information with foreign tax authorities, and deregistration from the corporate register.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines for failure to file substance declarations and may be subjected to travel bans and imprisonment for non-compliance with enforcement orders.
Anti-Money Laundering / CFT Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Fines and asset forfeiture under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2012; revocation of intermediation approvals for the registered agent; freezing orders issued by the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and knowing beneficiaries are exposed to criminal money-laundering prosecution and custodial sentences where the corporate entity is used as an instrument of crime.
Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Per-offence monetary penalties plus continuing daily penalties, suspension of filing rights, and possible administrative strike-off.

Director personal liability: Officers and the registered agent may be held personally liable for a false or missing BO filing; deliberate concealment is classified as a criminal offence carrying imprisonment.
Unlawful Distribution / Director Fiduciary Breach

Statutory sanction: The distribution is recoverable under the Business Corporations Act; the court may wind up the corporation on just and equitable grounds and impose fines for false solvency statements.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable to restore the full amount of the unlawful distribution; evidence of fraud leads to civil restitution, travel bans, and imprisonment.
