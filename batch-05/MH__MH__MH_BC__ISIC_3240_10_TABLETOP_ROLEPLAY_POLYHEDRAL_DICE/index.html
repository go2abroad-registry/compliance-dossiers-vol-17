Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Office (RTO), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; corporate registration and annual-report filings are administered by the Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Ministry of Finance; official government portal: https://rmigovernment.org

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — Incorporate a domestic Business Corporation under the Business Corporations Act 1990 through a licensed RMI registered agent by filing Articles of Incorporation (no minimum capital requirement); the Registrar of Corporations issues the Certificate of Incorporation. Step 2 — File the annual corporate report and pay the statutory annual fee (currently USD 250 for a non-resident domestic corporation); a Certificate of Good Standing is issued upon compliance. Step 3 — No automatic tax identification is generated at incorporation; an RTO TIN is required only if the corporation derives income from sources within the RMI, employs RMI-resident workers (triggering income-tax withholding and RMI Social Security registration), or lodges customs entries as importer/exporter. Step 4 — A non-resident offshore BC engaged exclusively in foreign e-commerce sales (Kickstarter, Stripe, PayPal) does not apply for an RTO TIN; its tax identity is evidenced by the Certificate of Incorporation together with the Section 253 non-residence declaration. Step 5 — A Foreign Investment Business License under the Foreign Investment Business Act 1988 is required only for commercial operations physically conducted within RMI territory, which is not contemplated for this offshore tabletop-gaming accessories model.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Non-resident domestic Business Corporation (Section 253, Business Corporations Act 1990) is exempt from all Marshall Islands income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, and distribution taxes on income derived outside the Republic; RMI taxation is territorial/source-based, so sales of polyhedral dice, game accessories, and IP licensing revenue to overseas consumers fall outside the RMI tax net; the only compulsory state charge is the annual corporate registration fee.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime exists under RMI law. Available statutory mechanisms: (a) Section 253 election — for status as a non-resident domestic corporation, effected by filing a sworn non-residence declaration with the Registrar of Corporations; (b) Part V continuation (redomestication) election to migrate a foreign corporation into an RMI Business Corporation; (c) election to operate as a resident domestic corporation, which remains taxable on RMI-source income unless the non-resident exemption applies. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the RMI BC is a per se corporation defaulting to C corporation treatment; a Form 8832 check-the-box election is unavailable, and no OECD BEPS Action 2 hybrid-mismatch election is applicable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Value Added Tax, Goods and Services Tax, or Sales and Use Tax regime is in force in the Republic of the Marshall Islands. Indirect taxation is confined to customs import duties under the RMI Tariff Act and excise duties on alcohol, tobacco, and petroleum fuels, administered by the RMI Customs Service, Ministry of Finance.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST exists, and therefore no turnover threshold triggers indirect-tax registration in the Republic of the Marshall Islands. A general business license and foreign-investment business license are required only for commercial operations physically conducted within the RMI; the offshore non-resident BC selling tabletop RPG dice exclusively to foreign customers owes no indirect-tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Structural non-applicability: the RMI levies no VAT/GST, so no zero-rating claim, output-tax charge, or input-VAT refund mechanism exists for exported goods (hand-cast polyhedral dice and tabletop gaming accessories) or offshore services (IP licensing and digital fulfillment). Exports leaving RMI customs territory are automatically export-duty-free; duty-free status is presumptive and requires only ordinary customs supervision plus documentary proof of physical departure from the Republic.

  • Customs Export Declaration (RMI Customs Service export entry)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List describing the polyhedral dice castings
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from the Marshall Islands
  • Bank SWIFT credit advice / Inward Remittance Certificate, or payment-platform settlement report (Stripe, PayPal/Braintree payout record) evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin (optional, where preferential tariff treatment is claimed in the destination market)

None — the RMI has not implemented real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory e-invoicing APIs, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls. Customs declarations are lodged on standard paper or electronic formats with the RMI Customs Service; the taxpayer must retain books, records, invoices, and export documentation for a minimum of five years.

No VAT zero-rating refund exists to claw back; the RMI imposes no exchange-control regime and no statutory FX repatriation deadline, the 180-day figure being retained as the documentary standard applied by banking counterparties for export-proceeds substantiation. Enforcement exposures comprise: (i) customs misdeclaration and smuggling penalties (seizure, forfeiture, and fines of up to twice the duty evaded) under RMI customs regulations; (ii) retroactive revocation of the Section 253 tax exemption where the Registrar or RTO determines that the corporation carried on business in, or held real property within, the RMI, thereby exposing previously exempt foreign-source income to RMI income tax assessment plus interest and penalties; and (iii) corporate delinquency sanctions, including administrative strike-off, for failure to file annual reports within the statutory filing window.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender of the Marshall Islands; there are no exchange controls restricting current or capital account transactions. Both capital inflows and outflows are fully convertible and unconditional. Profit and dividend remittances to non-resident shareholders are unrestricted and are not subject to any withholding tax, exchange permit, or central bank approval. The only practical hurdle is standard bank-level compliance with international correspondent banking due-diligence requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export-proceeds surrender rules, mandatory conversion ratios, or repatriation timelines in the Marshall Islands. A non-resident Business Corporation may retain 100% of its export receipts in any jurisdiction, in any currency, without obligation to remit or convert into local currency. This is consistent with the RMI's full dollarization and absence of an exchange-control regime.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands does not maintain a central bank. The official financial-sector regulator is the Banking Commissioner of the RMI Ministry of Finance, exercising powers under the Banking Act 1991 and the Financial Institutions (Licensing and Supervision) Act 2000.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. The RMI has no standalone electronic-money institution or payment-institution licensing framework. Non-bank fintech and offshore digital EMI providers cannot directly access the RMI's national clearing infrastructure. Any cross-border fintech solution must be contractually structured through a licensed RMI commercial bank or a U.S.-chartered bank with a correspondent relationship. Offshore digital EMIs are generally rejected for onboarding into the RMI banking market because of the FATF increased-monitoring listing and the absence of a domestic EMI authorisation path.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

40% of net taxable income for resident corporations; non-resident corporations are taxed only on Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI)-source income at 3% of gross income (or 40% of net income from an RMI trade or business); a non-resident domestic corporation (NRDC) deriving no income from RMI sources and conducting no business in the RMI has zero taxable income and a 0% effective rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. The RMI Income Tax Act imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders; distributions by an NRDC are characterized as foreign-source and escape RMI tax entirely.

The income tax return is due on or before the 15th day of the fourth month following the close of the taxable year (April 15 for a calendar-year corporation); the Annual Franchise Tax Report and ESR declaration are due on or before the anniversary of incorporation of the NRDC; tax must be paid in full with the return, and no extension of time to pay is available.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not prescribed. The RMI has no codified transfer pricing documentation regime and no Local File or Master File thresholds in USD. As defensive market practice, an OECD-aligned Local File threshold of USD 10 million consolidated group revenue is adopted where the NRDC intercompany-transacts; a de minimis NRDC with nil RMI-source income files no transfer pricing documentation.

Economic substance scope: The RMI Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), in force since 1 January 2019, apply to all licensed entities carrying on a relevant activity. Handmade polyhedral dice casting (ISIC 3240 manufacturing) is not a listed relevant activity; however, the NRDC must still file the annual ESR declaration and must not inadvertently conduct a distribution and service centre or headquarters activity from the RMI (for example, an RMI-based director managing global distribution logistics would trigger the ESR test). The actual production, personnel, and decision-making should remain in the jurisdiction of real manufacturing to satisfy foreign economic substance rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Willful Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax plus interest accruing at 2% per month; criminal conviction carries a fine of up to USD 10,000 and imprisonment for up to five years; the corporate charter may be revoked.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax deficiency and penalties; individuals face criminal prosecution, travel bans, and extradition exposure in the jurisdiction of actual business conduct.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR sanctions include monetary penalties of up to USD 30,000 per failure and strike-off or dissolution; the entity may be listed on EU and OECD non-cooperative jurisdiction registers, triggering defensive measures by counterparties and denial of banking, Stripe, PayPal, and Kickstarter services.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined for ESR declaration failures; continued operation of a sham entity exposes directors to foreign anti-avoidance assessments and fraud liability in the shareholders' residence jurisdictions.
AML / Fraudulent Use of Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Under RMI anti-money laundering law, legal persons face criminal fines of up to USD 5 million and responsible individuals face imprisonment; civil asset forfeiture and Suspicious Transaction Report investigations by the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit apply; Stripe and PayPal impose permanent bans and fund freezes.

Director personal liability: The sole director is in practice the beneficial owner; personal criminal liability, travel bans, and restraint orders attach, and directors of NRDCs are exposed to UBO-registry disclosure penalties.
Commmingling of Funds / Piercing the Corporate Veil

Statutory sanction: Equitable subordination and disregard of limited liability: creditors and tax authorities may pierce the veil under RMI courts applying Delaware-based Business Corporations Act jurisprudence; retroactive corporate tax is assessed on the de facto business and dissolution proceedings may be commenced.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for civil restitution of diverted amounts plus punitive damages; in flag-of-convenience jurisdictions the director may face personal bankruptcy declarations and travel-ban orders.
