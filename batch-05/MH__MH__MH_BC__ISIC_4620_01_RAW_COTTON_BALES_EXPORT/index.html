Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Administration (RTA), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; official e-services portal: https://eservices.mof.gov.mh (limited online functions; paper filing remains standard for non-resident entities)

E-government filing portal

The Marshall Islands imposes no national income tax on non-resident domestic corporations and has no VAT/GST; therefore no statutory TIN or VAT ID is required. A foreign-owned non-resident Business Corporation (MH_BC) must simply: (i) maintain registration and good standing with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act; (ii) file the annual corporate report; and (iii) obtain an annual business license only if physically carrying on business within the Marshall Islands. No tax identification number is issued absent an active local trading or customs activity.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — The Marshall Islands imposes no national income tax on non-resident domestic corporations and has no VAT/GST; therefore no statutory TIN or VAT ID is required. A foreign-owned non-resident Business Corporation (MH_BC) must simply: (i) maintain registration and good standing with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act; (ii) file the annual corporate report; and (iii) obtain an annual business license only if physically carrying on business within the Marshall Islands. No tax identification number is issued absent an active local trading or customs activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists. Non-resident domestic corporations are statutorily excluded from Marshall Islands income tax under the Business Corporations Act and the Income Tax Act exemptions, and no entity-level or pass-through election can alter this status. Hybrid mismatch elections are not recognized because there is no corporate income tax or equivalent creditable tax regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; only element-specific import duties and limited excise-type levies apply at customs clearance)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — the Marshall Islands levies no turnover-based indirect tax; registration for VAT/GST does not exist. Customs import duties apply transaction-by-transaction at the port of entry and are not triggered by any turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — since no VAT/GST is imposed, there is no export VAT to zero-rate and no refund mechanism. Exports of raw cotton bales from the Marshall Islands are entirely outside any consumption tax net; customs duties are generally not levied on exported goods.

  • Bill of Lading (issued by the ocean carrier for the cotton bale consignment)
  • Commercial Invoice describing ISIC 4620 raw ginned cotton bales
  • Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence for proceeds from the foreign buyer

None — there is no real-time fiscal clearance system, API-based e-invoicing mandate, digital stamping, or fiscal data reporting requirement. Paper-based commercial invoicing and standard export/import documentation satisfy legal record-keeping requirements.

No VAT-related zero-rating clawback exists because no VAT is chargeable. However, false or fraudulent export declarations, misdescription of goods, or under-valuation for customs statistics or shipping registry purposes may attract penalties, seizure of goods, and monetary fines under the Marshall Islands Customs Act and related regulations. A non-resident business corporation engaging in sham substantive activities in the Marshall Islands risks loss of its non-resident tax-exempt classification upon administrative review, and any associated documentary fraud is subject to general anti-fraud criminal sanctions.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility. The RMI maintains no exchange-control regime, capital-administration framework, or repatriation licensing. Capital and current account transactions (equity injections, dividends, management fees, profit remittance, capital repatriation) are freely transferable without central-bank approval or conversion rationing. The only practical hurdles are clearing-side: US correspondent-bank de-risking, OFAC/UN sanctions screening, and FATCA/CRS disclosures. Because the BC is a non-resident tax-exempt corporation, profit remittances are not subject to RMI withholding or income tax.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion requirements exist. There is no mandated timeline for repatriating export receipts, no minimum conversion ratio, and no obligation to sell foreign currency to any RMI instrumentality. Receipts from LC and documentary-collection drawings on raw cotton bale sales may be retained in USD offshore or transferred freely, subject to RMI AML/CFT record-keeping (5 years) and suspicious transaction reporting to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commission of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (Ministry of Finance), the prudential supervisor under the Marshall Islands Banking Act 2002 (as amended). The RMI has no conventional central bank because the US Dollar is the sole legal tender; monetary, clearing and settlement conditions are therefore set de facto by the US Federal Reserve system.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. RMI has no domestic electronic-money-institution (EMI) licensing framework, and the Banking Commission does not supervise foreign EMIs. Commercial offshore EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) may technically hold valid home-jurisdiction licenses, but most decline RMI-domiciled offshore Business Corporations under de-risking policies. Use of such rails is permissible only as a pass-through settlement layer for niche corridors, with an executed EMI client-exit/consent letter, full FATCA/CRS self-certification and a documented decision that custody with an unlicensed RMI entity is avoided.
