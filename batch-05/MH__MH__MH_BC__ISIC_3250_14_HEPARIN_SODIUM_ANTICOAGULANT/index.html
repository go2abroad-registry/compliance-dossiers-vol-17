Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Ministry of Finance, Banking and Postal Services – Revenue and Taxation Division (RMI Tax and Revenue Office), Majuro. Corporate registry: Office of the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act 1990, fileable only through a statutorily licensed registered agent; no consolidated public tax e-portal exists.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The foreign shareholder appoints a licensed RMI registered agent and files the Articles of Incorporation; the Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation (corporate number) as the primary entity identifier. Step 2: No statutory TIN is required for a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation because its foreign-source heparin fractionation and export sales are outside the RMI tax base. Step 3: If the BC elects resident status or employs RMI-resident staff, it files Form TR-1 with the Revenue and Taxation Division to obtain a TIN and employer registration. Step 4: For physical shipments, the BC obtains an annual Importer/Exporter License from RMI Customs. Step 5: For letter-of-credit and SWIFT wire rails, the BC presents the Certificate of Incorporation, certificate of good standing, and beneficial-ownership extract to the collecting bank.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-Resident Domestic Corporation. Taxation is strictly territorial; income derived outside the RMI, including the extraction, fractionation, and export of heparin sodium crude, is wholly exempt from corporate income, capital gains, dividend, and withholding taxes. No minimum tax and no capital duty.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in RMI law. Available statutory elections: (i) status election between Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (default, fully tax-exempt) and Resident Domestic Corporation (taxable at 3% on RMI-source business income), made by charter provision and notified to the Tax and Revenue Division; (ii) continuance/redomiciliation election under Part IX of the Business Corporations Act, tax-neutral in the RMI; (iii) branch-versus-subsidiary election for foreign holdings, immaterial because the RMI has no CFC, Subpart-F, or participation-exemption rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or Sales/Use Tax. The RMI levies only Customs Import Duties and selected commodity excises under the Customs Tariff Act 2006; export goods are duty-exempt by statute.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST threshold exists. Customs registration via an annual Importer/Exporter License (USD 100–500) is required for physical consignments; crude heparin sodium as a biologic/pharmaceutical input may also require a Ministry of Health import/export permit at the RMI port of call.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable under a VAT framework: the RMI levies no VAT, and exports are outside the scope of RMI indirect taxation by the territorial rule rather than by a zero-rating mechanism. Export of heparin sodium crude (ISIC 3250) bears a 0% RMI duty/tax rate; no refund mechanism exists because no input VAT is incurred. Duty-free import of machinery or chemical feedstocks, if used in RMI territory, would require a Customs Duty Exemption Certificate for manufacturing inputs. No statutory FX-control or repatriation deadline applies (USD is legal tender); the 180-day benchmark is a banking documentary expectation for LC/SWIFT collections.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMI has no separate national currency and imposes no exchange control regime. Capital and current account transactions are freely convertible as the U.S. dollar is both the domestic and settlement currency. Profit remittance and dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders are not subject to statutory approval or conversion hurdles; however, all wire transfers must satisfy the AML/CFT reporting thresholds under the RMI Banking Act 1991, as amended, and the Financial Transactions Reporting Act 2019.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender rules, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no repatriation deadlines for export receipts. Exporters may retain U.S. dollar proceeds in any licensed RMI bank or offshore account, subject only to record-keeping and suspicious transaction reporting requirements.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). No central bank is established; the U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender, and the U.S. Federal Reserve's monetary policy effectively governs the currency supply.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. The RMI does not maintain a dedicated fintech or electronic money institution (EMI) licensing framework. Offshore digital payment platforms may only hold cleared funds through a licensed RMI bank or a recognized U.S. correspondent, and domestic banks generally reject client relationships with unlicensed EMIs due to correspondent banking de-risking pressures. Any cross-border EMI arrangement would require a written sponsorship agreement with an RMI-licensed bank and full look-through access to the EMI's transaction monitoring logs.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% effective for a non-resident domestic Business Corporation (BC) on foreign-source income. The nominal corporate schedule of the RMI Income Tax Act (Title 54 MIRC) reaches a maximum of 32%, but the charge attaches only to income derived from sources within the Marshall Islands, which a non-resident BC conducting zero RMI business does not derive.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the RMI imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions to foreign shareholders of a non-resident domestic BC.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before 30 April of each calendar year for calendar-year filers; for corporations with a non-calendar fiscal year, the report is due within six months after the end of the fiscal year. Filing is mandatory irrespective of income level; the franchise tax is a flat pre-payment, not an income tax.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return is due on the 15th day of the fourth month following the close of the taxable year (calendar-year taxpayers: 15 April). Quarterly instalments are required only where the corporation derives RMI-source income. A non-resident domestic BC deriving no RMI-source income has no filing obligation and should not file a nil return absent a specific demand from the Revenue and Taxation Officer.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — the RMI has no transfer-pricing legislation, no Local File or Master File requirements, and no statutory threshold in USD or any other currency. The operative documentation obligation belongs to the foreign parent in its home jurisdiction: where the RMI BC is part of an OECD-aligned group, the parent must prepare Local File and Master File covering the RMI entity (typically triggered at EUR 1,000,000 or the local-law equivalent of related-party turnover) and, where applicable, a Country-by-Country Report at the EUR 750,000,000 consolidated revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: The RMI has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations comparable to those of the BVI or Cayman Islands. However, the RMI maintains commitments on transparency and exchange of information under its TIEA network and the OECD Harmful Tax Practices Framework. The board of the non-resident BC should nevertheless maintain credible substance: a valid RMI registered agent and office address, written minutes of board meetings, proper books and records, a bank account held in the entity's own name, and a clear commercial rationale for the RMI situs of the heparin-fractionation trading vehicle. The failure to maintain these elements is not an RMI statutory violation but will invite a foreign court to apply substance-over-form and recharacterize the BC as a sham.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

RMI Corporate Registry Default

Statutory sanction: Late-payment surcharge, suspension of the Certificate of Good Standing, administrative strike-off or revocation of the charter, and restoration only upon payment of all arrears, penalties, and reinstatement fees (31 MIRC, Business Corporations Act).

Director personal liability: Directors are personally named in restoration proceedings; contracts concluded after strike-off are voidable and directors may be held personally liable in contract or tort for trading through a defunct entity. Travel bans are not an RMI registry remedy but may be issued by the foreign forum enforcing a judgment.
Shell Company / Substance-Sham Doctrine

Statutory sanction: RMI-side: strike-off for failure to maintain a registered agent. Foreign-side: a court may disregard the entity under substance-over-form or piercing doctrines, reallocate all income to the beneficial owner, and impose interest and punitive sanctions on the reallocated amounts.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution of reallocated income, disgorgement of profits, punitive damages, and personal liability for the BC's debts where the entity is found to be a mere instrumentality of the directors or shareholders.
Foreign Tax Evasion and False Residency Claims

Statutory sanction: RMI-side: making a false statement to an RMI public officer is a criminal offence. Foreign-side: civil fraud penalties up to 75% of the understated tax (e.g., US IRC Section 6663), accuracy penalties of 20%-40%, criminal evasion fines, and incarceration for the responsible individuals.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations or direct the concealment face personal fines equal to the tax evaded, civil restitution, travel bans, extradition to the prosecuting State, and imprisonment (e.g., up to five years under US IRC Section 7201; analogous sanctions in EU Member States).
Money Laundering and AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act 2018: for a natural person, a fine of up to USD 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 15 years; freezing and forfeiture of accounts; revocation of the registered agent's license for compliance failures.

Director personal liability: Directors acting as signatories or AML compliance officers are individually criminally liable for willful blindness to suspicious transactions; travel bans and passport seizure are available in extradition and mutual legal assistance proceedings.
Unlicensed Pharmaceutical or Financial Activity

Statutory sanction: RMI Business License Act: fines and imprisonment for unlicensed business activity carried on in the RMI; foreign regulators may impose import bans, product detention, recalls, and civil money penalties for unauthorized APIs.

Director personal liability: Directors of a non-compliant pharmaceutical supply chain face personal prosecution in the importing jurisdiction, including imprisonment of up to 20 years for adulteration or misbranding with intent to defraud (US FDCA analogues), plus civil restitution and debarment from government procurement.
