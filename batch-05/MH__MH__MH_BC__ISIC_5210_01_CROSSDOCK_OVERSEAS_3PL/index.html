Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance and Treasury; online portal via the National Taxpayer Access System at https://rmitax.com (official practical portal for filings and business license tax)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must first obtain a Marshall Islands corporate charter from the Registrar of Corporations under the Registrar of Corporations Act. Thereafter, the entity files the completed application for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) with the Revenue and Taxation Division, together with the registered agent appointment and a copy of the certificate of incorporation. If the entity is a non-resident business corporation, a Foreign Investment Business License under the Foreign Investment Act is also required; the Revenue and Taxation Division then issues the statutory TIN and, where applicable, a Business License Tax permit. Non-resident entities without economic substance in the Marshall Islands are classified as tax-exempt business corporations and are not subject to local income tax or business license tax.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity must first obtain a Marshall Islands corporate charter from the Registrar of Corporations under the Registrar of Corporations Act. Thereafter, the entity files the completed application for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) with the Revenue and Taxation Division, together with the registered agent appointment and a copy of the certificate of incorporation. If the entity is a non-resident business corporation, a Foreign Investment Business License under the Foreign Investment Act is also required; the Revenue and Taxation Division then issues the statutory TIN and, where applicable, a Business License Tax permit. Non-resident entities without economic substance in the Marshall Islands are classified as tax-exempt business corporations and are not subject to local income tax or business license tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available because the Marshall Islands does not impose an entity-level income tax. The Business Corporation Act provides for a non-resident election: by designating the corporation as non-resident, the entity becomes exempt from Marshall Islands income tax and business license tax. No income tax treaty or hybrid-electronic classification regime applies. The entity may elect to be treated as a foreign sales or holding vehicle, but this has no income tax consequence. There is no local equivalent of the U.S. check-the-box regulations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT or GST; the Marshall Islands imposes only customs import and excise duties and a business license tax on resident commercial activities.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — the Marshall Islands has no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax registration threshold. No turnover threshold triggers indirect tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because the Marshall Islands does not levy VAT or GST, the concept of export zero-rating is not statutorily recognized. Goods exported from the Marshall Islands are outside the scope of any national consumption tax and therefore no VAT is charged, regardless of origin or destination. No VAT refund claim mechanism exists. For customs purposes, the export must be made in accordance with the Customs Revenue Act and all applicable commodity export regulations.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Marshall Islands Customs and Trade Service form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence demonstrating receipt of foreign currency for the exported goods

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping requirement exists under Marshall Islands law. Businesses are not required to transmit invoice data to the tax authority; however, all invoices must be retained as part of the business records required for Business License Tax and customs compliance.

As no VAT/GST zero-rating scheme exists, no fraudulent zero-rating clawback regime applies. However, false export declarations submitted to the Customs and Trade Service, or misdeclaration of goods or value to obtain any customs duty relief or export evidence, are subject to administrative penalties under the Customs Revenue Act, including monetary fines, forfeiture of goods, seizure of the cargo, and suspension or revocation of the entity's business license. Misrepresentation in any export document may also constitute an offense under the Criminal Code and carry imprisonment for individuals. Any customs duty relief previously granted may be immediately reassessed and recovered from the declarant with interest and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender and domestic currency. RMI maintains no exchange-control apparatus; both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible. There is no statutory approval requirement for cross-border remittances and no withholding, QI reporting, or profit-repatriation tax imposed by RMI on non-resident Business Corporations. The only practical hurdles are U.S. correspondent banking compliance, OFAC sanctions screening, and the receiving bank's KYC/AML policies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. RMI imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation for non-resident Business Corporations. There is no prescribed statutory timeline for repatriation, and the mandatory conversion ratio is zero because the U.S. dollar is the local currency. Export receipts may be retained in any USD account at home or abroad without central bank approval.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) — no central bank exists; the United States Federal Reserve is the issuer of the circulating currency under the Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rated High for ancillary cross-border collection and disbursement, particularly Airwallex, which is consistent with the target payment rails. A foreign fintech EMI is not within the RMI Banking Act if it maintains customer accounts abroad and has no physical deposit-taking branch in RMI. There is no RMI regulatory licence for pure EMI activities, so onboarding is executed under a standard commercial contract, not a banking licence. The legal constraint is that such EMI cannot lawfully hold escrow or regulated deposits in RMI; all funds must remain in safeguarded accounts with licensed partner banks. Correspondent U.S. banks will impose enhanced due diligence on any RMI non-resident corporate that uses an EMI as a primary treasury rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injections must be routed through a licensed commercial bank using SWIFT MT103 or, for U.S.-dollar domestic transfers, Fedwire/ACH. The receiving bank is the Bank of Marshall Islands or Bank of Guam – Majuro Branch with a short narrative in Field 70 such as 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION / CAPITAL INJECTION – NON-RESIDENT BC'. There is no RMI central bank filing or exchange-control code; the verifiable purpose code is the BOP/BPM6 'Direct Investment – Equity' classification. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a non-resident Business Corporation deriving exclusively foreign-source income; the 3% Gross Revenue Tax (GRT) applies only to gross revenue from a trade or business actually conducted within the Marshall Islands, which is excluded under the target cross-dock overseas-3PL structure

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the Marshall Islands imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions by a non-resident BC to any shareholder, whether resident or non-resident, and there is no branch remittance tax

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No RMI Local File, Master File, or CbC reporting threshold exists under domestic law (the RMI is not in the OECD Inclusive Framework as a mandatory CbC exchange jurisdiction for non-financial MNEs). Nevertheless, where the BC is consolidated into a foreign group with revenue above the parent jurisdiction's CbC threshold (e.g., USD 850 million), the parent must file CbC in its tax-resident country; a contemporaneous Local File benchmark is commercially advisable when the BC invoices related-party fulfilment services, because the overseas-3PL jurisdiction may apply separate documentation thresholds.

Economic substance scope: The RMI has no general Economic Substance Regulations comparable to UAE ESR, but an anti-avoidance substance doctrine operates through the Income Tax Act source rules and the Business Corporations Act maintenance obligations: a foreign BC with no RMI office, employees, books, or management activity is treated as a pure exemption vehicle. The OECD Inclusive Framework membership (through the RMI's commitment to Base Erosion and Profit Shifting) and CRS/FATCA reporting obligations require the BC to maintain a registered office, a registered agent, share registers, director registers, and accounting records sufficient to reconstruct income. Critically, the economic substance of the cross-dock overseas-3PL is located in the warehouse's host jurisdiction; the RMI BC must not claim RMI substance for those activities or the host state may recharacterize the BC as a shell and deem a permanent establishment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Foreign Tax Evasion / PE Concealment

Statutory sanction: Destination-country penalties of up to 200% of underpaid corporate income tax, interest compounding, and criminal fines; in the RMI, BCA s.117 false annual report penalties and general criminal fines apply; the destination tax authority may levy VAT/GST fraud penalties of up to 100% of the non-paid output tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who directed the concealment face civil restitution orders, personal liability for the unpaid foreign tax under veil-piercing or director-statute theories, travel bans from the destination jurisdiction, extradition risk, and imprisonment (typically 5-10 years) for tax fraud.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative revocation of the Certificate of Incorporation, regulatory strike-off, and prohibition of the Registered Agent from continuing to represent the entity; the RMI Registrar may recommend criminal investigation; foreign courts may disregard the entity entirely and assess all profits at the true operating company.

Director personal liability: Directors incur personal liability for contracts signed during the post-revocation trading window, joint and several liability for fraudulent conveyance, and a 10-year ban from serving as director of any RMI entity; the RMI court may order assets tracing and freezing.
AML / Financial Integrity (PAICMA and FIU)

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Prevention of Money Laundering Act, fines up to USD 100,000 per violation, asset forfeiture, and revocation of any registered-agent license; administrative abrogation of the corporate charter; inclusion on international AML blacklists affecting correspondent banking.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face up to 15 years' imprisonment for AML violations, personal confiscation orders equal to the funds laundered, travel bans, and removal from office; officers who knowingly signed false FIU responses are individually prosecutable.
