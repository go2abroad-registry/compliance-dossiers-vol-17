Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax and Revenue Division, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). The official administrative portal for tax, business license, and customs data exchange is the RMI government financial services portal operated by the Ministry of Finance; the corporate registry is administered by the Registrar of Corporations.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Business Corporation does not obtain a VAT ID because the RMI has no VAT. It obtains an RMI Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) only when a statutory local contact point exists (customs account, business license, payroll, real estate, or financial account). The SOP is: (1) file the certificate of incorporation and registered-office/registered-agent records; (2) file an RMI Taxpayer Registration application with the Tax and Revenue Division describing non-resident status; (3) if any goods will enter RMI territory, obtain a Customs importer/exporter code; (4) where a business is actually carried on in RMI, file the Business License Registration and obtain the annual license-tax stamp; and (5) receive the TIN certificate as the statutory identifier for customs, banking, and tax filings.

It obtains an RMI Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) only when a statutory local contact point exists (customs account, business license, payroll, real estate, or financial account). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The Marshall Islands income tax system does not administer U.S.-style check-the-box elections or OECD hybrid-mismatch elections. The operative statutory election is the entry into Non-Resident Domestic Corporation status under the Business Corporations Act, which confirms exclusion from RMI net-income tax on non-RMI-source income and exemption from Business License Tax for offshore gross revenue. An additional election may be made for a project-specific Tax Exemption Certificate under the RMI development-incentive legislation. No election exists to convert the entity into a pass-through vehicle for RMI tax purposes; RMI law respects entity-level classification as stated in the BCA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax is levied in the RMI. The indirect tax architecture consists of Customs Import Duty and ad valorem tariffs under the Customs Tariff Act, plus the annual Business License Tax, which is imposed on categories of gross revenue rather than as a transaction-level VAT/GST.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for a VAT/GST registration threshold because the RMI does not impose VAT/GST. Customs registration is compulsory for any person importing commercial goods. Business License Tax registration is required for a business carried on in RMI; a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation with no RMI-source gross revenue is outside that regime. The RMI uses the United States Dollar as legal tender, so all local-currency references are in USD.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because no VAT/GST applies in the RMI, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim. A Non-Resident Domestic Corporation operating online secondhand-luxury resale is treated as having no RMI indirect tax exposure where sales, payment processing, and delivery are performed outside RMI. For physical goods exported from RMI that had previously been imported, the relevant relief is customs duty drawback or re-export exemption under the Customs Tariff Act, available where the goods are identifiable, exported within the statutory period, and supported by customs entry documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration with electronic customs release
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill as evidence of international transportation
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the vintage/luxury goods, quantities, and export value
  • Customs import entry and duty receipt if a duty drawback or re-export exemption is claimed
  • Inward remittance statement or bank payment records evidencing the foreign sales proceeds
  • Powers of attorney or consignment manifests where applicable

The RMI does not operate a real-time fiscal e-invoicing API, digital stamping, or transaction clearance mandate. Customs entries are processed through the electronic customs clearance system maintained by RMI Customs; the system issues an electronic release/clearance for each declaration. Private commercial invoices are not subject to prior government clearance outside customs.

The Tax and Revenue Division and RMI Customs may audit export documentation, customs duty drawback claims, and non-resident gross-revenue declarations. Fraudulent export or drawback documentation attracts forfeiture or seizure of goods, retrospective cancellation of the exemption, assessment of unpaid duty/tax with interest, monetary penalties (up to USD 10,000 per violation and additional penalty of up to 250% of the duty/tax assessed), and criminal liability for willful false declarations. The non-resident exemption may be reopened within the general six-year limitation period if revenue was mischaracterized as non-RMI source, with a 50% civil fraud penalty and loss of exemption status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Marshall Islands uses the U.S. dollar as its official legal tender; accordingly, there are no separate domestic capital controls or current account convertibility restrictions. Profit remittance fees and statutory withholding taxes (e.g., nonresident withholding on dividends and royalties) apply, but no exchange control approval is required for capital flows because the economy is fully dollarized. Businesses with nonresident bank accounts may freely receive and disburse USD without central bank conversion mandates, subject to suspicious transaction reporting and corresponding U.S. OFAC sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts in the Marshall Islands due to the absence of a domestic currency and the absence of exchange control legislation. Receipts may be held in U.S. dollars indefinitely in any licensed commercial bank. However, reporting thresholds under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act (as amended) require banks to file currency transaction reports for cash transactions exceeding USD 10,000 and suspicious activity reports, and to maintain full underlying trade documentation for audit and lender compliance purposes.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Marshall Islands

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with significant legal constraints. Offshore digital EMI providers are not licensed or regulated by the Marshall Islands Financial Institutions Commission and cannot provide domestic clearing or local account numbers; however, a non-resident Business Corporation may maintain a USD settlement account with a U.S.-chartered fintech or an EMI operating under a U.S. state money transmitter license, provided the entity passes KYC and the EMI offers banking-as-a-service that complies with U.S. sanctions and anti-money laundering regulations. Such accounts are treated as foreign accounts and do not satisfy local documentary requirements for escrow or corporate bank references.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 (or Fedwire for domestic U.S. transfers) from the beneficial owner's personal or corporate account to the Marshall Islands entity's USD operational account. Standard U.S. purpose codes (e.g., 'EQUITY' or 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' under the Federal Reserve's Purpose Code for ACH/Fedwire) are used; for SWIFT transfers, the field 70 (Remittance Information) should reference 'SHARE CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION' or 'CAPITAL INJECTION'. For escrow-based online resale, funds are cleared through the escrow provider's domestic U.S. settlement rails (ACH or wire) and remitted to the company's operating account. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable; no RMI transfer-pricing statute. Currency: USD. Prudent benchmark: prepare Local File when annual related-party transactions exceed USD 1,000,000; Master File when group consolidated revenue exceeds USD 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: RMI Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to non-resident companies carrying on relevant activities in the Republic. ISIC 4791 online luxury consignment is generally not a relevant activity unless it operates as a distribution/service center for connected persons; a nil ESR notification and annual return must still be filed. Failure to comply triggers USD 10,000 penalty; repeat failure USD 50,000 and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Misdeclaration of RMI Source

Statutory sanction: Under Title 60 of the Marshall Islands Revised Code: felony, imprisonment up to 5 years, individual fine up to USD 5,000, corporate fine up to USD 25,000, plus restitution and revocation of charter.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize the false declaration are jointly and severally liable; corporate veil pierced, civil restitution, travel bans, and possible extradition.
Shell Company / ESR Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalties: USD 10,000 first failure, USD 50,000 subsequent failures; deregistration and name transmitted to EU/OECD as non-compliant shell.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for unpaid penalties; disqualification from RMI directorships up to 10 years; travel restrictions and contempt sanctions.
AML/CFT Violation in Luxury Resale

Statutory sanction: RMI AML Act: fines up to USD 1,000,000, asset seizure, business suspension, and imprisonment up to 15 years for controlling individuals.

Director personal liability: Willful blindness to red flags triggers personal criminal liability; disgorgement, freezing orders, travel bans, and extradition under mutual legal assistance.
Unlicensed Payment or Escrow Activity

Statutory sanction: Administrative cease-and-desist, forced restitution, fine of USD 25,000 per day of violation, and criminal prosecution for customer fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors deemed trustees of customer funds; misuse results in personal liability for full restitution, bank account freezing, travel bans, and imprisonment.
