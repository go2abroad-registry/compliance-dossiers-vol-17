Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance (MoF) of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI, ISO 3166-1 alpha-3: MHL) - Revenue and Taxation Office, Majuro; corporate registration and tax-exempt status administration: Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Ministry of Finance, acting exclusively through licensed Marshall Islands registered agents via the Registrar's e-registry platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Appoint a licensed RMI registered agent and maintain a registered office in Majuro. Step 2: File Articles of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act 1990 (31 MIRC) with the Registrar of Corporations and pay the initial filing fee (USD 250); no minimum capital is required. Step 3: Obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; the entity is automatically classified as a non-resident domestic corporation and, under BCA section 52, is exempt from all RMI taxes, fees, and charges for 25 years from incorporation, except the registration and annual fees. Step 4: No TIN or VAT ID is issued to non-resident BCs; the exemption is evidenced by the statute, the Certificate of Incorporation, and (on request) a Certificate of Good Standing from the Registrar. Step 5: If the BC carries on any business inside the RMI or employs RMI-resident staff, it must register with the Revenue and Taxation Office for income tax and withholding (obtain a TIN), register with the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration (MISSA), and obtain a business license under the Business Licensing Act - such activity would compromise the non-resident exemption. Step 6: Annual compliance: pay the annual registration fee based on authorized share capital and, if engaged in a relevant activity (e.g., IP business or headquarters), file the annual Economic Substance Return with the Registrar within the statutory deadline.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - Non-resident domestic Business Corporation within the statutory 25-year Marshall Islands tax-exemption zone (BCA section 52); revenue from offshore gaming anti-cheat and integrity middleware is not RMI-source income and is exempt from RMI income, withholding, and capital taxes.

Available election forms

No RMI check-the-box election and no hybrid-mismatch legislation exist. Available elections: (i) designation as a non-resident domestic corporation at incorporation (BCA section 52); (ii) a deemed election to resident taxable status upon commencing business within the RMI; (iii) statutory conversion and re-domestication among Business Corporation, LLC, and Limited Partnership forms; (iv) for US federal tax purposes only, eligible owners may file IRS Form 8832 (check-the-box), which has no effect on RMI classification. The RMI has not enacted domestic Pillar Two (GloBE) top-up tax or anti-hybrid rules as of this stage.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - the RMI levies no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs import duty, excise-type duties, and the departure green tax, none of which apply to exported digital anti-cheat services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold or regime exists in the RMI. Importers of physical goods must register with Customs, and any entity carrying on a taxable business within the RMI requires a business license; neither obligation arises for the offshore export of anti-cheat middleware services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - with no VAT/GST in force, there is no export zero-rating mechanism or refund claim procedure to invoke. License and subscription income from the video-game kernel-level anti-cheat and integrity middleware supplied to overseas operators is sourced outside the RMI (services performed outside the Republic for non-resident customers) and is excluded from the RMI income tax base; the BCA section 52 exemption confirms that no RMI tax of any kind is leviable on such export income for 25 years from incorporation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility on both current and capital accounts. The RMI imposes no exchange controls, no approval requirements for inward or outward transfers, and no differentiated treatment between resident and non-resident accounts. Capital repatriation, dividend remittance, and liquidation distributions are free from licensing, surrender, or waiting periods. The absence of a domestic central bank eliminates both convertibility risk and sovereign FX intervention risks; the only material friction is correspondent-bank compliance screening at the US clearing level.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory timeline for surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no obligation to repatriate export proceeds to a local account. Export receipts may be held in any jurisdiction and currency; because USD is the domestic currency, export invoices in USD require no conversion for local disbursement. For non-USD receipts, free conversion is available through offshore correspondent channels without any central bank filing requirement.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands — the RMI maintains no national central bank; currency issuance and monetary policy are delegated de facto to the US Federal Reserve System under the Compact of Free Association, with US Dollar (USD) as sole legal tender and unlimited legal-tender status.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility. The RMI has no local Electronic Money Institution licensing regime; the Sovereign Currency Act 2018 authorizes the digital currency SOV through the Sovereign Currency Foundation but does not create an EMI charter for corporate treasuries. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., US-chartered neobanks, UK/EU EMIs, Hong Kong licensed virtual banks) are legally permissible counterparties, but each will independently KYC the RMI Business Corporation under FATF-style standards; the RMI's inclusion on certain correspondent-banking watchlists materially reduces onboarding acceptance rates at US clearing banks. Practical routing: open a US-based arranging account via a permitted correspondent or a Singapore/Hong Kong multi-currency EMI, with BOMI serving as fallback for USD local clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

US correspondent clearing will engage Fedwire/CHIPS processing; the remitter must include a concise reference formula: 'MH_BC — Shareholder Capital Contribution — [Shareholder Reference]'. Verification bundle comprises the executed board resolution, subscription agreement, RMI registered agent confirmation of issued shares, and source-of-funds evidence from the remitting bank. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - no domestic corporate income tax or transfer pricing rules exist in the Marshall Islands; no Local File or Master File threshold is triggered.

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply only to entities undertaking 'relevant activities' defined by the International Tax Cooperation Act, including banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, and distribution/service centers. A gaming anti-cheat middleware engine does not fall within these categories; therefore no ESR report is required. The entity must, however, maintain a registered agent, registered office, and adequate records in the Marshall Islands to preserve good standing.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Unauthorized Domestic Activity

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of income under the Marshall Islands Income Tax Act at statutory rates, plus interest and a penalty of 25% of the tax due; deregistration of the Business Corporation.

Director personal liability: Director(s) may be held jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and fines; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed by the RMI court system.
Economic Substance Avoidance / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Loss of tax-exempt status, monetary penalties up to USD 10,000 per failure in the relevant activity, and compulsory strike-off from the Registrar of Corporations.

Director personal liability: Directors may be subject to civil restitution orders and disqualification from acting as directors of any Marshall Islands entity for up to five years.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines of up to USD 50,000 per violation; the registered office provider may be compelled to resign, causing administrative dissolution.
