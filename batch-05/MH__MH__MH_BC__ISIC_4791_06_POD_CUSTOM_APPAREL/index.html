Competent corporate registry

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance, Majuro; no statutory national e-filing portal for foreign non-resident entities, filings are submitted on paper to the Revenue Office

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation (MH_BC) is not required to obtain a Marshall Islands TIN unless it begins deriving RMI-source gross revenue. If registration becomes necessary, file the corporate registration certificate, certificate of good standing from the RMI Registrar, and completed RMI Gross Revenue Tax registration form with the Revenue and Taxation Division, obtain a GRT number, and then file quarterly returns; there is no check-the-box or advance tax ruling step

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident Business Corporation (MH_BC) is not required to obtain a Marshall Islands TIN unless it begins deriving RMI-source gross revenue. If registration becomes necessary, file the corporate registration certificate, certificate of good standing from the RMI Registrar, and completed RMI Gross Revenue Tax registration form with the Revenue and Taxation Division, obtain a GRT number, and then file quarterly returns; there is no check-the-box or advance tax ruling step

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. The RMI has no check-the-box entity classification election, no hybrid mismatch election, and no branch-equivalent election; the tax status is determined solely by residence and RMI-source revenue activity

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; RMI has a Gross Revenue Tax (GRT) on RMI-source business gross revenue

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST. Under the RMI Gross Revenue Tax, a business holding a valid business license must file and remit GRT where gross revenue exceeds the periodically set small-exemption threshold; a non-resident POD apparel seller with no physical operating presence in RMI has no VAT registration threshold because no VAT regime exists

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable under a VAT system because the RMI does not impose VAT/GST on exports. For GRT purposes, sales of custom-printed and POD merchandise to customers located outside the Marshall Islands are treated as non-RMI-source gross revenue and are outside the GRT base if the seller establishes that the goods were shipped directly abroad (or supplied as offshore services) from an RMI trade or business; no zero-rating claim or refund mechanism exists because there is no output tax against which input tax can be credited

  • Customs Export Declaration (Marshall Islands Customs) evidencing direct foreign shipment
  • POD order record, shipping manifest, and Bill of Lading or last-mile carrier proof of export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or payment processor settlement record identifying the foreign customer and foreign currency remittance

None. There is no real-time fiscal clearance, no API integration mandate, no digital stamping obligation, and no certified electronic invoicing requirement for business-to-consumer POD sales; ordinary commercial invoices and payment processor receipts satisfy documentary requirements

Although no VAT zero-rating clawback exists, a false GRT exclusion for purported export sales is treated as underreporting taxable gross revenue. Penalties include a minimum late-payment and underpayment penalty of 10% of the unpaid tax, interest at the statutory annual rate, criminal fines for fraudulent returns, and retroactive assessment of the full GRT plus penalty for a period of up to six years from the original return due date

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de jure convertibility in both current and capital accounts. The RMI maintains no foreign-exchange-control statute, no capital-transaction licensing, no profit-remittance restriction, and no domestic currency; all payments are USD-denominated. A non-resident Business Corporation, which is tax-exempt under the RMI Income Tax Act, may freely pay dividends, repay shareholder loans, and transfer trading proceeds abroad without CBMI authorization. Current-account items (trade payments, platform settlements, supplier disbursements) and capital-account items (equity injections, intercompany loans, acquisition proceeds) are equally unrestricted. Practical hurdles arise only at the correspondent-bank layer: USD wires clear through U.S. banks that apply OFAC screening, source-of-funds review, and UBO validation; therefore the operative condition for convertibility and profit remittance is a complete, current treasury KYC file comprising registry extracts, a beneficial-ownership schedule, a no-bearer-shares declaration, and platform-derived transaction evidence. Withholding taxes on outbound remittances are nil for the non-resident exempt BC, but the paying bank may request the RMI tax-exempt certification and a board resolution evidencing the distribution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. The RMI imposes no statutory surrender ratio, no repatriation timeline, and no mandatory conversion obligation on export receipts; POD e-commerce proceeds in USD are freely held in the RMI or offshore. In practice, banks expect USD inflows from Stripe/PayPal settlement or card acquirers to be traceable to genuine merchandise sales: retain platform payout reports, daily settlement advices, invoices, and POD fulfilment records for a rolling five-year period to withstand enhanced due-diligence triggered by high-frequency small-value credits.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Marshall Islands (CBMI), established under the Central Bank Act 2018 and operational since 2019 as the RMI banking-licensing and AML/CFT supervisory authority, succeeding the former Banking Commission and the banking supervision unit of the Ministry of Finance. The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender under the RMI Constitution and the Compact of Free Association; no RMI currency is issued, no independent monetary policy exists, and the de facto monetary anchor is the U.S. Federal Reserve System. CBMI administers the Banking Act 1991 (as amended) and does not operate any exchange-control or foreign-exchange-approval regime. There is no separate foreign-exchange administration in the RMI.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No filing obligation arises for a non-resident BC without RMI-source income. A non-resident BC that derives RMI-source gross income must register and file within 120 days after the 31 December year-end; extensions require prior Revenue approval.
