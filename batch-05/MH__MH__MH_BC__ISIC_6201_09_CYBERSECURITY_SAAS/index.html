Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A non-resident Business Corporation must continuously maintain both a registered office and a licensed Registered Agent in the Marshall Islands. The statutory registered office is the physical address of the Registered Agent in Majuro; the corporation itself may conduct all operations remotely with no physical premises in RMI, and virtual-office substitutes that replace the statutory Registered Agent are not permissible. For this cloud cybersecurity SaaS business, the virtual service address arrangement through the Registered Agent is fully compliant.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax and Revenue Division, Ministry of Finance, Banking and Postal Services, Republic of the Marshall Islands (official portal: https://finance.gov.mh; corporate registration is administered by the RMI Registrar of Corporations through licensed registered agents, accessible via the Corporations Registry online entity search system)

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate a Business Corporation under the Business Corporations Act 1990 through a licensed RMI registered agent; the Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation and a Corporate Registration Number (CRN), which is the primary entity identifier for all tax, banking, and compliance purposes. Step 2: If the corporation files the Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC) election and conducts no business within the Republic, no Marshall Islands income tax or gross revenue tax TIN is required; the CRN together with the NRDC certificate constitutes the full statutory tax identity. Step 3: If the corporation later hires RMI-resident employees, register with the RMI Social Security Administration for an employer contribution account and with the Tax and Revenue Division for wage-tax withholding; a payroll-based employer TIN will then be assigned. Step 4: Pay the annual corporate franchise tax of USD 250 (base amount, tiered by authorized capital) and maintain the registered agent's annual renewal filing to keep the tax-exempt NRDC status in good standing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Marshall Islands non-resident domestic corporation deriving no Marshall Islands-source revenue and conducting no business within the Republic is exempt from RMI income tax and the 3% gross revenue tax; the RMI applies a territorial/source-based tax system and imposes no VAT, no capital gains tax, no net worth tax, and no withholding tax on non-residents)

Available election forms

The only relevant RMI statutory election is the Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC) designation under the Business Corporations Act 1990, filed by the registered agent at incorporation or by later amendment; it conclusively places the entity outside the RMI tax base. RMI law has no check-the-box regime, no CFC election, and no hybrid mismatch election. A U.S. federal check-the-box election (IRS Form 8832) may be filed if the corporation becomes a U.S. tax payer or derives U.S.-source income, but that election is purely a U.S. federal tax classification and is not recognized by RMI tax law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None -- the Republic of the Marshall Islands does not operate a VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. The indirect revenue instruments in force are limited to customs import duties (Customs Tariff Act 1995), fuel tax, and the Green Tax departure levy. Cloud vulnerability scanning and penetration-testing SaaS exports are intangible services and have no Marshall Islands indirect tax nexus.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in RMI law (the official currency is the United States dollar). A customs importer licence or excise registration is required only for physical importation of tangible goods into the Republic; SaaS services are delivered digitally from outside RMI customs territory and do not trigger any registration threshold. A non-resident domestic corporation has no indirect tax registration duty.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable -- because the RMI imposes no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating certificate, no export VAT refund, and no claim procedure. The cross-border SaaS supply (cloud vulnerability scanning and penetration testing) constitutes foreign-source revenue outside the RMI gross revenue tax base and outside the customs framework. Documentation that the customer is located abroad and that no performance occurs in the RMI is nevertheless recommended to preserve the NRDC exemption and to satisfy the registered agent's AML/KYC duties.

  • Corporate Registration Number and NRDC certificate issued by the RMI Registrar of Corporations (primary tax identity evidence)
  • Commercial master services agreement and penetration-testing scope/deliverable documents evidencing offshore service delivery
  • Invoice and payment evidence (Stripe payout reports or wire transfer advices) with inward remittance records maintained under the registered agent's AML/KYC file

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or tax-authority API mandate exists in the Marshall Islands for SaaS or other services. Physical-goods customs declarations are processed electronically through UNCTAD ASYCUDA World; intangible service invoices require only ordinary commercial invoice standards. No government seal, hash code, or fiscal watermark is required.

The RMI has no VAT export-fraud clawback regime because no VAT exists. However, abusive use of the NRDC status (e.g., conducting RMI-source business while claiming non-resident exemption) triggers reassessment under the Gross Revenue Tax Act and the Income Tax Act, with penalties of up to 25% for non-fraudulent underpayment and up to 50% plus imprisonment exposure for fraudulent evasion, together with revocation of the corporate licence by the Registrar and director liability under the Business Corporations Act. If the SaaS exporter claims zero-rating in a foreign VAT jurisdiction (such as the EU, Australia, or U.S. states), any fraudulent claim remains subject to that foreign jurisdiction's interest, penalty, and clawback rules.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United States Dollar is the sole legal tender in the Marshall Islands, and the RMI does not operate exchange controls or require approvals for current or capital account transactions. Profit remittances and dividends may be paid freely in USD through RMI chartered banks, but originating banks may require standard source-of-funds documentation, beneficial ownership disclosure, and, where the payor is a domestic RMI bank account holder, a tax clearance certificate or representation from the business corporation confirming no outstanding RMI statutory liabilities.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender requirements under the laws of the Marshall Islands. A non-resident business corporation may retain 100% of export or SaaS subscription receipts in USD in any licensed bank account, without mandatory conversion, waiting period, foreign exchange authorization, or surrender certification.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (the RMI has no domestic central bank; the official currency is the United States Dollar and monetary policy is effectively determined by the U.S. Federal Reserve)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium with legal constraints. The RMI does not have a domestic electronic money institution or EMI licensing framework. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g. Stripe Treasury-like platforms, Wise Business, or licensed EU/UK/SG payment institutions) may be used as auxiliary collection or disbursement rails for SaaS subscription revenue, provided the EMI holds a valid license in its home jurisdiction and meets RMI AML/KYC expectations. Offshore EMIs cannot act as the primary or sole RMI treasury account, and funds held with an unlicensed EMI are not bank deposits protected under RMI banking law or the U.S. Federal Deposit Insurance regime.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing threshold exists in the RMI. The RMI Income Tax Act and Business Corporations Act do not mandate Local File or Master File documentation. As a defensive international compliance measure, contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation is recommended only when the consolidated group turnover exceeds US$50,000,000 or the group operates in jurisdictions applying OECD BEPS Action 13 rules. The same file can substantiate arm's length pricing if a foreign tax authority recharacterizes the RMI entity.

Economic substance scope: The RMI has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations comparable to the BVI or the Cayman Islands. No statutory economic substance test is imposed on a Non-Resident Business Corporation. However, because the entity is a zero-tax cybersecurity SaaS provider, foreign revenue authorities may examine the arrangement under CFC rules, controlled-foreign-company legislation, or substance-over-form doctrines (e.g., U.S. Section 482, U.K. CFC rules, OECD Pillar Two income inclusion). The BC should maintain demonstrable commercial reality: board minutes documenting strategic decisions, executed client contracts, actual billing flows through USD accounts, and evidence of security-service delivery capability outside the RMI. Statutory sanction: The Registrar may dissolve the corporation for failure to maintain a valid registered agent or for filing false Annual Reports.
