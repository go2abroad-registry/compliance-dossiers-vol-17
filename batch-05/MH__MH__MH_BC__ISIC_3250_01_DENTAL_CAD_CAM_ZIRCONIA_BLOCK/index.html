Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Government of the Republic of the Marshall Islands – Revenue and Tax Division; online portal: https://rmi-finance.org (limited e-filing, physical filings accepted through registered agents)

E-government filing portal

Non-resident business corporations are not automatically issued an RMI Taxpayer Identification Number. A TIN is required only when the entity undertakes taxable RMI activities such as employer registration, customs importer registration, or local business licensing. The foreign entity must submit a completed TIN registration application, certified certificate of incorporation, bylaws, certificate of good standing issued by the Registrar of Corporations, and proof of a registered agent in RMI. The Revenue and Tax Division processes applications within 5-10 business days. No VAT identification number is issued because the Marshall Islands imposes no value-added tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No domestic check-the-box or entity classification election exists under Marshall Islands law. Non-resident business corporations are statutorily classified as non-resident entities exempt from RMI income tax. Hybrid mismatch elections are not recognized for RMI domestic tax purposes, although the RMI has participated in BEPS-related dialogue. Separate US federal check-the-box elections, such as IRS Form 8832, may be used by US taxpayers but have no effect on RMI tax status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No National VAT/GST – Limited Import Excise Tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – RMI has no VAT/GST registration threshold. The official currency is the US Dollar (USD). Importers must obtain an RMI business license and customs clearance before goods are landed, regardless of value, subject only to limited de minimis allowances for personal effects.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in RMI as there is no VAT to zero-rate. Exports of dental zirconia blocks and milling discs manufactured in the Marshall Islands are outside the scope of Marshall Islands consumption taxation and are not subject to import excise tax. A certificate of origin and export documentation are nonetheless required to evidence that the goods departed RMI and to support buyer-side duty relief in the destination jurisdiction.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List

No national e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API reporting mandate exists. Customs import declarations are processed electronically through the RMI Customs Division platform (ASYCUDA World). Clearance is based on commercial invoices, bills of lading, packing lists, and duty assessment. No invoice clearance API is imposed on exporters.

Because RMI has no VAT system, there is no export-VAT refund mechanism and no fraudulent zero-rating clawback regime. Export and customs fraud is nevertheless penalized under the RMI Customs Act: false declarations, undervaluation, or misdescription of exported or imported goods may result in forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties up to three times the value of the goods, suspension or revocation of customs and excise licenses, and criminal fines or imprisonment for responsible officers. The RMI Revenue and Tax Division may audit records for up to six years and impose administrative penalties for inaccurate documentation supporting any tax exemption claim.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility exists by design because the U.S. dollar is the circulating legal tender; RMI imposes no exchange controls and no separate capital or current account restrictions on equity injections, trade settlements, dividend remittances, or profit repatriation. The operative constraints are bank-level AML/CFT reporting and record-keeping for cash and wire transactions of USD 10,000 or above, plus U.S. OFAC screening in the correspondent clearing chain. No central bank permit, no exchange-rate approval, and no conversion license is required for any transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI statutes impose no export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline for export receipts. Dental zirconia block export proceeds may be retained in USD or any foreign currency at the exporter's choice; the only practical obligation is retention of underlying trade documentation (invoices, bills of lading, contracts) for AML and tax-treaty transparency purposes.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) has no central bank. Prudential regulation and licensing of financial institutions are administered by the Banking Commissioner of the Ministry of Finance under the Banking Act 2005 (as amended). The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender by law, and the Financial Intelligence Unit under the Currency and Transactions Reporting Act and the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act 2012 supervises AML/CFT compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. The RMI Banking Act 2005 restricts deposit-taking, payment, and foreign-exchange clearing services to licensed banks; no offshore digital EMI (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) is licensed to operate as a settlement institution in the RMI. A fintech EMI may be used only as a pre-funded sponsor account cleared through a U.S. or E.U. partner bank, which adds OFAC and de-risking friction and provides no account protection or local regulatory remedy. This rail is acceptable for low-value outbound operating expenses (e.g., software subscriptions), but is not viable for inbound statutory capital, export trade settlement, or documentary trade finance.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable (reporting currency: USD). Economic substance scope: The RMI Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019 and its Economic Substance Regulations impose a substance test on RMI-registered entities carrying on 'relevant activities': banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, distribution/service centres, and IP holding. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Invoicing

Statutory sanction: In the RMI: deregistration of the BC, revocation of the Certificate of Good Standing, and loss of any medical-device manufacturing license. In counterpart jurisdictions: recharacterization of the RMI entity under CFC, GAAR, and substance-over-form rules; assessment of back taxes at statutory rates (typically 20–35%), interest, and penalties up to 100–150% of tax assessed; criminal tax-fraud prosecution in the manufacturing destination country.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil on a finding of fraud, rendering directors who approved the sham transactions jointly and severally liable for full civil restitution of the evaded taxes; foreign arrest warrants can trigger RMI extradition cooperation, resulting in travel bans, seizure of personal assets, and custodial sentences in high-value fraud cases.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off under BCCA for non-filing, denial of the ORC Certificate of Good Standing, and inclusion on restrictive tax-haven watchlists; under foreign law, withholding agents impose the maximum non-treaty rate (e.g., 30% U.S. withholding) plus penalties; OECD/EU listings can subject every inbound wire transfer to enhanced due-diligence levies.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue to trade knowing the BC lacks substance face liability for fraudulent misrepresentation to banks and trade counterparties; in EU/UK enforcement actions, director disqualification, travel bans, and worldwide asset freezes are routinely sought.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the RMI AML/CFT framework and Financial Action Team directives include monetary fines for non-registration, suspension or revocation of corporate registration, and freezing of correspondent banking access; counterpart banks may terminate USD clearing relationships and file Suspicious Activity Reports.

Director personal liability: Officers are personally exposed to criminal fines and imprisonment for knowingly filing false beneficial-ownership particulars or laundering the proceeds of healthcare supply fraud; civil suits by affected dental clinics may attach the directors' non-RMI assets.
Trading While Dissolved / Annual Return Default

Statutory sanction: BCCA invalidation of corporate capacity: contracts concluded after dissolution are legally ineffective; the ORC may deny reinstatement and the BC forfeits the limited-liability shield; foreign buyers may reject deliveries on the ground that the seller lacks legal personality.

Director personal liability: All debts and liabilities arising after dissolution become the personal liability of the directors and officers who authorized the transactions; this strict statutory liability cannot be waived and coexists with negligence claims from trade creditors and dental laboratory clients.
Misrepresentation of DTA / Withholding Status

Statutory sanction: The uncollected 30% U.S. withholding is restored with interest; the U.S. IRS may assert perjury-related civil fraud penalties of 75% of the underpaid tax and refer the BC's principals for criminal prosecution under U.S. false-statement provisions.
