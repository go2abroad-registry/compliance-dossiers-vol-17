Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands, Ministry of Finance, Revenue and Taxation Division (official government portal: https://rmigovernment.org/)

E-government filing portal

The Marshall Islands does not impose national income tax and therefore does not issue income-tax TINs. Foreign-owned entities incorporating as non-resident Business Corporations obtain a Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Corporations, together with a business license number for local administrative purposes. No VAT ID or national tax identifier is required. If the company employs residents, it must separately register with the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration and obtain an employer social security number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, partnership, or hybrid-mismatch fiscal elections exist under Marshall Islands law. A properly formed non-resident Business Corporation is automatically excluded from RMI-source taxation and enjoys a statutory tax-exempt status. The only entity-level registration obligations are corporate registration and business licensing, not tax elections.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Because the Marshall Islands levies no VAT, GST, or sales tax on any supply of goods or services, there is no zero-rating mechanism to claim and no export refund regime. Exports and offshore transactions are outside any indirect tax scope by default.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are unrestricted, with no exchange controls, no foreign-exchange licensing, and no prescribed profit-remittance approval. A non-resident Business Corporation (MH_BC) may remit dividends, return of capital, interest, or royalties offshore if the distribution is authorized by a board resolution and recorded. Practical hurdles are limited to AML/CFT source-of-funds verification by the RMI bank and compliance with the Distribution Act on any gross distributions, including issuance of a tax clearance letter by the RMI Revenue and Taxation Division where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. RMI imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation for export receipts. Proceeds may be retained in USD or freely convertible currency. As an operational matter, RMI banks apply FATF-aligned due diligence to unexplained large inbound remittances; exporters should retain underlying invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, and certificates of origin to preempt compliance holds.

Central bank supervision: No dedicated central bank exists. The U.S. dollar is legal tender under the Compact of Free Association, making the U.S. Federal Reserve the de facto monetary anchor. Prudential supervision and bank licensing are exercised by the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, under the Banking Act 2011; the Financial Intelligence Unit enforces AML/CFT under the Anti-Money Laundering Act 2002; and the National Banking Board administers government-held banking assets.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low feasibility. Major offshore digital EMIs (Mercury, Airwallex, Wise Business, Payoneer) generally decline RMI-registered non-resident corporations due to the jurisdiction's historical FATF grey-list exposure, the absence of a local EMI licensing regime, and U.S. de-risking policies against shell entities. RMI's DAO-friendly Business Corporations Act and digital-asset pilot laws do not confer passporting rights on foreign fintechs. PayPal Business is available to an RMI-registered corporation, but PayPal is not a full treasury provider and must be linked to a BMI or Bank of Guam operational account for periodic sweeps.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable. The Marshall Islands imposes no corporate income tax and has no local transfer-pricing documentation threshold, no Master File, and no Local File obligation. The national currency is the United States Dollar, so no local-currency threshold exists. Foreign parent jurisdictions may impose transfer-pricing documentation requirements under their own OECD-aligned rules, but those are not RMI filings.

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands Economic Substance Regulation (ESR) applies to non-resident business corporations conducting relevant activities, including holding of intellectual property, distribution, and financing, among others. A toy and mecha model kit manufacturer with actual high-precision injection-molding operations, full-time staff, physical premises, and decision-making in the Marshall Islands will satisfy the directed-and-managed-in-RMI test and the adequate physical presence and payroll tests. A letterbox entity without those substance attributes is exposed to ESR penalties, strike-off, and adverse reporting to foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Filing Fraud

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine, restitution orders, cancellation of corporate registration, and prosecution under the RMI Criminal Code and Business Corporations Act. The Registrar may strike the company from the register and prohibit the procurement of certificates of good standing.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize fraudulent filings or cause the corporation to evade foreign tax may be held personally liable for restitution, subjected to travel bans or surrender of passports, and sentenced to imprisonment; the corporate veil may be pierced where fraud or misrepresentation is proven.
Shell Company and Abusive Economic Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Failure to satisfy the Economic Substance Regulation results in financial penalties, a default penalty of up to USD 20,000 per statute as applied by the RMI Registrar, possible filing of a deregistration notice, and mandatory information exchange with the foreign tax authority where the ultimate parent is resident.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign economic substance declarations knowing there is no genuine RMI presence may incur administrative fines and disqualification from serving as directors; if the lack of substance is used to defraud creditors, courts may pierce the veil and hold directors personally liable.
Money Laundering and AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Marshall Islands Anti-Money Laundering Act and related Regulations, sanctions include monetary penalties, forfeiture of assets, suspension or revocation of any registration or license, and criminal prosecution; AML failure may also trigger FATCA/CRS reporting penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly permit unrecorded beneficial ownership or payment laundering face personal criminal liability, civil fines, travel restrictions, seizure of proceeds, and imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Profit Stripping and Cross-Border Misreporting

Statutory sanction: While the Marshall Islands itself has no corporate income tax, foreign tax authorities may impose primary transfer-pricing adjustments, penalties up to 100% of the underpaid tax in some jurisdictions, interest, and secondary adjustments; the RMI ESR regime may additionally penalize the letterbox entity receiving the shifted profit.

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrate abusive profit shifting through an RMI conduit may be named in foreign tax assessments, face piercing of the corporate veil for fraudulent transfer or sham-transaction findings, and may be subject to civil restitution proceedings, travel bans, and extradition in aggravated fraud cases.
