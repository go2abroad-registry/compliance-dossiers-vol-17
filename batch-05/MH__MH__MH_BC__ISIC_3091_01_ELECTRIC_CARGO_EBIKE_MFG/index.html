Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A purely virtual (non-physical) office is not recognized in the Marshall Islands. Every business corporation must maintain a physical registered office and a licensed registered agent within the Republic, and the registered office must accept service of process during ordinary business hours. For a non-resident micro-mobility OEM holding company, the licensed agent's physical address satisfies the statutory registered-office requirement; no domestic premises, employees, warehouse, or factory footprint is required. Virtual directorship and remote shareholding are permissible provided executed consents and UBO declarations are held by the registered agent.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Administration (RTA), Ministry of Finance, Government of the Republic of the Marshall Islands; official portal: rta.mh (Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Administration online services).

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident Business Corporation (BC), the statutory TIN-equivalent is not required because such entities are exempt from domestic income tax. Procedure: (1) Incorporate the BC with the Office of the Registrar of Corporations under the Associations Law of 1990, filing Articles of Incorporation and a registered agent appointment; (2) file the Non-Resident Domestic Corporation status election with the Registrar, designating the entity as a foreign-owned non-resident BC; (3) obtain the Certificate of Exemption issued by the RTA confirming immunity from Marshall Islands income and withholding taxes for 20 years; (4) maintain annual government franchise fee payments and registered agent renewal filings. No RTA tax identification number is issued or required unless the BC later derives Marshall Islands-source income, employs residents in-country, or engages in regulated local activity.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a non-resident Business Corporation (BC), the statutory TIN-equivalent is not required because such entities are exempt from domestic income tax. Procedure: (1) Incorporate the BC with the Office of the Registrar of Corporations under the Associations Law of 1990, filing Articles of Incorporation and a registered agent appointment; (2) file the Non-Resident Domestic Corporation status election with the Registrar, designating the entity as a foreign-owned non-resident BC; (3) obtain the Certificate of Exemption issued by the RTA confirming immunity from Marshall Islands income and withholding taxes for 20 years; (4) maintain annual government franchise fee payments and registered agent renewal filings. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or entity classification election exists. A BC may elect Non-Resident Domestic Corporation status under the Associations Law to obtain the statutory 20-year tax exemption certificate. No hybrid mismatch elections are available; the Marshall Islands has no comprehensive income tax treaty network and does not apply hybrid mismatch rules. An entity could alternatively elect to be taxed as an ordinary resident domestic corporation under the Income Tax Act 1989, but this is not beneficial and is rarely exercised.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT, GST, or Sales and Use Tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs import duties levied under the Customs and Excise Act.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; there is no VAT/GST/sales tax regime and therefore no turnover-based registration threshold. Customs import duty is applied transactionally per-consignment on the c.i.f. value of goods entering Majuro or Kwajalein ports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: there is no VAT system in force, so no export zero-rating or VAT refund mechanism exists. Export sales of electric cargo e-bikes by a non-resident BC are wholly outside the Marshall Islands tax net; the exemption certificate removes all local taxation on offshore manufacturing, trading, and export revenue. The exporter should nevertheless maintain documented evidence of foreign destination to confirm non-resident status and avoid any Marshall Islands-source characterization.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Marshall Islands Customs Service export form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing foreign consignee and non-resident destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API reporting, or digital stamping mandate exists in the Marshall Islands. Commercial invoices are issued in free-format PDF/paper and are not pre-cleared by the RTA; customs declarations are submitted to the Marshall Islands Customs Service on standard export/import forms.

Although no VAT clawback exists, the RTA and Customs Service apply anti-abuse measures: fraudulent export declarations or misrepresentation of the non-resident status can result in revocation of the tax exemption certificate, retroactive income tax assessment for up to five years, monetary penalties up to 10% per year on underpaid amounts, customs fines up to three times the evaded duty, seizure of goods, and criminal liability for officers in cases of intentional customs fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The USD is legal tender, and the RMI, as an IMF Article VIII member, maintains no exchange restrictions on current or capital international transactions. There is no statutory approval process for capital inflows, profit repatriation, dividend distribution, interest payments, or liquidation proceeds to non-residents. The capital account is fully open, and the RMI tax code imposes no income, withholding, or remittance taxes on the non-resident Business Corporation (tax-exempt statutory status). The only practical friction is documentary: US correspondent banks and RMI licensed banks will apply OFAC screening, FATF-style beneficial ownership verification, and a substantive 'economic purpose' review of each credit/debit flow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None imposed. There is no statutory timeline for surrender or repatriation of export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no obligation to deposit receivables with an RMI domestic bank. However, under the RMI AML/CFT framework (including the 2018 Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act), the BC must retain contemporaneous documentary evidence attaching each inbound trade credit to a genuine underlying sale: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, ISIC 3091 / NAICS 336991 alignment, and the executed purchase order from the foreign distributor or OEM client.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) has no central bank and no dedicated foreign exchange administration. Prudential licensing and supervision of banks is vested in the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, pursuant to the Banking Act 1987 (as amended). The Marshall Islands Financial Services Commission (FISC), established under the Financial Services Commission Act 2020, supervises non-bank financial service providers and trust/company agents. The de facto monetary anchor is the United States Federal Reserve, as the US Dollar is the sole official legal tender of the RMI under the Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Mainstream digital EMI platforms (Wise, Airwallex, Mercury, Revolut Business, Payoneer) typically reject RMI Business Corporations at automated onboarding due to the jurisdiction being tagged as an offshore/financial-secrecy jurisdiction and the absence of an RMI central bank to satisfy EMIs' 'regulated financial institution' checks. A viable alternative is a USD account with Bank of Guam (access to the US domestic ACH/fedwire system) followed by a Payoneer or Bloomberg-backed receiving-only arrangement where the fintech is not the primary settlement rail. Legal constraint: any EMI that onboards an RMI BC must itself demonstrate correspondent AML equivalence; the RMI's non-participation in the US state money-transmitter licensing framework makes this commercially rare.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) pass an MH_BC board resolution approving equity subscription and expressly designating the receiving account (Bank of Guam Majuro USD account, or Bank of Marshall Islands USD account); (2) remit in USD via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's homebank directly to the RMI bank's correspondent — for Bank of Guam use its US ABA routing number and the beneficiary account with RMI company name and reference: 'EQ SUB MH_BC [date]'; (3) if the shareholder is not US-based, use a CHIPS-participant intermediary with a full 20-digit beneficiary account number and the RMI company's registered address to prevent OpRisk fails; (4) the payment purpose code should align with BPM6 category 'equity investment' / capital contribution; RMI requires no exchange-control permit or prior approval; (5) corroborate the booking with the bank's credit advice and deposit a signed subscription statement and updated share register into the corporate treasury file for future AML reviews.Primary rail: SWIFT MT103 (T/T) for component supplier settlements (battery packs, motors, controllers, frame parts, tyres) directly from the USD account; each MT103 must include full beneficiary legal name, account, BIC/IBAN, and, for Chinese beneficiaries, the 11-digit CNAPS code. Secondary rail: Commercial Irrevocable Letter of Credit (at-sight) opened by Bank of Guam or BMI in favour of the supplier's bank for high-value OEM batch purchases (typically above USD 2,000,000); documents must include commercial invoice, full-set clean on-board bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin and, where required, HS 8711.60 declaration. For suppliers requiring accelerated payment, use TT 30% advance / 70% against documents. Prohibited practice: accumulating CNY or EUR balances onshore in the RMI entity — always settle in USD and let the beneficiary's bank perform the cross-currency conversion.USD/CNY Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) traded offshore (e.g., Singapore/Hong Kong level-1 bank treasury desk) — permissible because the RMI imposes no derivatives licensing or exchange-control restrictions on a non-resident BC; documented under English-law ISDA 2002.Deliverable FX forwards (USD/EUR, USD/TWD, USD/KRW) with Bank of Guam or the maintaining bank's treasury desk under an approved FX line — permitted as bona fide hedging of known trade payables/receivables and excluded from speculative dealing by the BC's investment policy.Cross-currency swaps (USD/CNY, USD/VND) under an ISDA 2002 master agreement with a CSA segregated account at Bank of Guam — permissible for hedging long-term OEM supplier financing; the swap must be designated as a cash-flow hedge under IFRS 9 with contemporaneous hedge documentation to preserve auditability.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% applicable corporate income tax rate for a non-resident Business Corporation; the Marshall Islands Income Tax Act (26 MIRC Ch.1) taxes only income derived from sources within the Republic, and a non-resident corporation without RMI-source income owes no corporate income tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the RMI imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions by a domestic business corporation to non-resident shareholders; dividends paid to a non-resident owner are foreign-source income exempt under 26 MIRC sec. 121.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold and no Local File or Master File requirement - the RMI Income Tax Act does not codify transfer-pricing documentation rules, and the non-resident manufacturer sits outside the RMI tax base. Best practice for bank financing and group consolidation: prepare a one-page intercompany pricing memorandum evidencing an arm's length OEM margin (e.g., TNMM operating margin benchmark of 5-10%) although no RMI filing obligation is triggered.

Economic substance scope: The RMI Economic Substance Regulations Act 2019 applies to non-resident domestic corporations conducting 'relevant activities' - banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, pure holding, distribution and service centre, and intellectual property holding. Manufacture of electric cargo bicycles and step-through scooters (ISIC 3091) is not a listed relevant activity and therefore falls outside the annual ESR notification and substance-testing regime. However, if the RMI entity additionally performs group distribution or procurement agency functions for parts and finished e-bikes, the distribution-and-service-centre category may be triggered, requiring local physical presence or outsourced substance and an annual ESR notification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax Exemption Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under 26 MIRC: a 20% delinquency penalty plus monthly interest on underpaid tax, increasing to 50% for fraudulent intent; the Tax Division may issue a jeopardy assessment and freeze bank accounts; a criminal conviction for wilful evasion carries a fine of up to USD 10,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: RMI courts pierce the corporate veil where the exemption declaration is a sham; directors who sign a false report are personally liable for unpaid corporate tax and penalties, may face criminal prosecution, and can be barred from leaving the jurisdiction pending a tax investigation.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the BCA 1990: administrative dissolution, revocation of the certificate of good standing, a USD 200 late-report penalty, and a deregistration flag in the RMI corporate registry; under the ESR regime: a fixed civil penalty of up to USD 10,000 plus USD 5,000 per month for continuing non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue trading while the company is struck off are jointly and severally liable for all new debts; the court may order civil restitution, impose a travel ban, and disqualify a director from serving on the board of any RMI-registered entity for up to 3 years.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under RMI anti-money laundering and counter-financing of terrorism legislation: fines up to USD 25,000 per infraction, forfeiture of the transaction value, and imprisonment of up to 5 years for natural persons; the Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze corporate bank accounts pending investigation.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or remain wilfully blind to anomalous trade invoices face personal criminal money-laundering charges, asset forfeiture, referral to the US Office of Foreign Assets Control for USD-denominated transactions, and a court-issued travel ban pending extradition or confiscation proceedings.
Sanctions Violation / Export Control Breach

Statutory sanction: Under RMI customs and sanctions enforcement: seizure and forfeiture of the cargo, administrative fines up to USD 500,000, suspension of the corporate trading certificate, and referral to OFAC for USD-settlement accounts; repeat offences trigger cancellation of the company's registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers can be held personally and criminally liable for export-control breaches, subjected to arrest and extradition from the Pacific region, personal asset freezing, and a travel ban issued by the RMI High Court pending trial.
