Formation / constitutional filings

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 24 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital or paid-up capital; contributions freely set by the partnership agreement; no escrow account requirement.

Funding & unlock path: The SCSp must obtain a fire safety certificate (Certificat de sécurité incendie) from the Luxembourg Fire and Rescue Service (CGDIS) for any office premises open to employees or visitors. The Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) must clear the workplace with respect to minimum safety, ergonomics, and health standards under the Luxembourg Labour Code. No additional industrial clearance applies.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital or paid-up capital; contributions freely set by the partnership agreement; no escrow account requirement.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Luxembourg - Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes and Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AED) for VAT and registration duties; official electronic access via MyGuichet.lu, guichet.lu, and the Luxembourg Business Registers (RCS) portal at lbr.lu

E-government filing portal

1) Execute the SCSp constitutive deed by notarial act or private instrument and register with the RCS; the RCS issues the national business registration number (matricule) which serves as the master identifier. 2) Submit VAT registration form (Formulaire 430) to the AED, including the constitutive documents, proof of economic activity, and the foreign tax residence certificate where a non-resident partner is established; the AED assigns a 10-character VAT identification number with the LU prefix. 3) Obtain the direct tax dossier number (numéro de matricule fiscal) from the ACD; since the introduction of the single business identifier, the direct tax matricule is synchronized with the RCS number, and the partnership remains fiscally transparent so no entity-level corporate income tax file is opened unless a corporate-law conversion applies.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — 1) Execute the SCSp constitutive deed by notarial act or private instrument and register with the RCS; the RCS issues the national business registration number (matricule) which serves as the master identifier. 2) Submit VAT registration form (Formulaire 430) to the AED, including the constitutive documents, proof of economic activity, and the foreign tax residence certificate where a non-resident partner is established; the AED assigns a 10-character VAT identification number with the LU prefix. 3) Obtain the direct tax dossier number (numéro de matricule fiscal) from the ACD; since the introduction of the single business identifier, the direct tax matricule is synchronized with the RCS number, and the partnership remains fiscally transparent so no entity-level corporate income tax file is opened unless a corporate-law conversion applies.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

The SCSp is statutorily transparent under Article 182bis of the Luxembourg Income Tax Law (LIR): it lacks legal personality and is not included in the closed list of corporate taxpayers in Article 175 LIR. Luxembourg does not provide a US-style check-the-box election; a partner's home-jurisdiction classification is respected for tax treaty and EU law purposes, and hybrid or reverse-hybrid mismatches are neutralised under the ATAD implementation in Articles 168bis-168ter LIR. No entity-level fiscal option is available without converting the SCSp into an opaque vehicle such as an S.à r.l. or SECA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 - annual taxable turnover exclusive of VAT under Article 57 of the Luxembourg VAT law (LTV); the threshold is applied pro rata temporis for a newly established SCSp, small businesses may opt for normal taxation, and voluntary registration is available to recover input VAT on B2B remote services

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For a data-labeling BPO service supplied to a non-EU business customer, the place of supply is outside Luxembourg under Article 44 LTV, so the supply is outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT and is invoiced without VAT (no Luxembourg VAT applies). Where the customer is established in the EU, the reverse-charge mechanism applies at the customer's location. The actual 0% zero-rating rate applies to goods physically exported outside the EU under Articles 50 and 58 LTV and to services directly linked to exported goods such as transport, handling, and freight services. The SCSp must retain the service agreement, the customer's foreign VAT/GST registration evidence, the tax invoice, the bank credit advice, and any transport or customs documents proving the goods left the EU where physical shipment occurs.

  • Customs Export Declaration (the union customs declaration evidencing exit of the goods from EU customs territory)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (transport document proving physical departure and destination outside the EU)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (SWIFT MT103 or MT202 credit advice evidencing receipt of the settlement proceeds within the compliance window)
  • Executed services agreement / statement of work with the counterparty's tax identification number and non-EU billing address (substantiates the outside-scope treatment where the supply is a pure digital BPO service without physical export)

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime is currently imposed for domestic B2B transactions in Luxembourg; B2G e-invoicing via PEPPOL is operational for supplies to public entities. Luxembourg is preparing to align with the EU VAT in the Digital Age package, which will make e-invoicing mandatory for cross-border B2B supplies by 2030. Current compliance is satisfied by periodic VAT returns, EC Sales Lists and Intrastat declarations submitted electronically through the AED filing service and MyGuichet.lu, with standard invoices accepted in electronic or paper form.

Luxembourg does not impose statutory exchange controls binding the repatriation deadline; the 180-day window is maintained as an internal compliance benchmark consistent with transfer-pricing and documentation practice. Prolonged deferral of export proceeds may be treated as a constructive shareholder loan or trigger a transfer-pricing adjustment by the ACD. Where the zero-rated or outside-scope treatment is not documented, the AED may retroactively assess Luxembourg VAT at 17% together with statutory default interest from the due date and impose administrative fines under Article 61 LTV; fraudulent arrangements such as ghost exports, missing-trader chains, or sham non-EU counterparties expose the taxpayer to criminal tax-fraud sanctions, including imprisonment for up to five years and substantial monetary penalties. The standard assessment limitation period is five years, extended to ten years where fraud or deliberate concealment is evidenced.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a full Eurozone member (single currency EUR). Under Article 63 TFEU and Directive 88/361/EEC, capital and current account movements are fully liberalized; no exchange-control approval, quantitative limits, or surrender mechanisms apply to resident or non-resident SCSp partners. The SCSp is tax-transparent, so profit distributions to foreign limited partners are not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax and can be remitted freely in EUR or any convertible currency. Convertibility is complete for trade settlements, repatriation of invested capital, and service fees; no practical hurdles exist for AI data-labeling BPO revenue.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The Eurosystem and Luxembourg law impose no statutory timeline for repatriation, no mandatory conversion quota, and no surrender obligation for export proceeds or services revenue. Export receipts in EUR or foreign currency may be retained indefinitely in the SCSp's bank accounts. The sole compliance burden is indirect: resident entities must report cross-border flows to the BCL external statistics (balance of payments) when aggregate transaction volumes exceed the published BCL reporting thresholds.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) acting solely within the Eurosystem framework of the European Central Bank (ECB); prudential supervision of banks and payment institutions is exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF), not by the BCL.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High for both specified rails. PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. & Cie, S.C.A. is a Luxembourg-incorporated, CSSF-supervised credit institution, making PayPal Business accounts fully Eurozone-compliant for mass payouts to AI labelers and fully aligned with the target payment rails; account opening requires RCS certificate, partnership deed, UBO register, and confirmation of signatory authority. Wise (licensed as an Electronic Money Institution under the EU E-Money Directive 2009/110/EC, passported into Luxembourg) is highly viable for API-driven mass disbursements in local currencies; onboarding of an SCSp is permitted but slower than for standard corporates because the partnership lacks legal personality and the EMI must verify the contractual general partner. Legal constraint: neither PayPal nor Wise provides credit, deposit guarantee coverage, or treasury-grade FX execution; they must be used exclusively as disbursement rails underneath the primary bank account, never as the main treasury repository.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound partner capital subscriptions and subordinated shareholder loans must arrive via SWIFT MT103 with the SCSp's registered name and IBAN in field 59, a remitter matching the partner documentation, and a narrative clause referencing the partnership deed article and the purpose 'equity contribution / shareholder current account'. For BCL external statistics, the transaction must be declared under the direct-investment purpose category (equity or investor loan), not as trade payment, and filed in the BCL balance-of-payments statistical return whenever the monthly aggregation threshold is crossed. For intra-Eurozone injections from EU partners, SEPA Credit Transfer (with or without Instant) is acceptable, but the BCL declaration obligation still applies; the bank must be given a capital introduction letter, source-of-funds evidence, and a copy of the partner register to avoid misclassification. No exchange-control pre-approval, license, or visa-dependent capital import mechanism applies in Luxembourg.For EU/EEA contractor payments and subcontractor invoices use SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) or SEPA Instant, referencing the invoice number and the data-labeling work order; this rail is cost-efficient and same-day for most EU counterparties. For non-SEPA disbursements (US, UK, India, Philippines, Southeast Asia) use SWIFT MT103 for high-value settlements and the Wise API or PayPal MassPay for micro-payouts to individual AI labelers; every outbound instruction must contain a clear reference to the underlying BPO contract and an internal treasury file matching the beneficiary to the invoice. Documentary collection and commercial letters of credit are not required for this BPO trade flow and are structurally disproportionate; the dominant clearing mechanisms are open-account SWIFT/SEPA transfers plus fintech bulk payout rails. The bank must receive the procured client acceptance certificates and monthly invoicing summaries to maintain the SCSp's transparent, low-risk transaction profile.Plain-vanilla deliverable EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, and EUR/CHF FX forwards (tenor up to 12 months) executed under an ISDA 2002 or bilateral master agreement with a Luxembourg/Eurozone credit institution, fully documented against the SCSp's foreign-currency service receivables and contractor disbursement forecast.FX swaps (spot-buy/forward-sell combinations) to manage short-term liquidity mismatches between EUR treasury balance and USD/GBP payroll obligations arising from monthly AI data-labeling disbursements.Cross-currency swaps for longer-dated USD or GBP receivable exposures if the BPO contract is multi-annual and the unhedged currency mismatch exceeds the SCSp's internal risk tolerance.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing USD/EUR or USD/local-bank fixings for residual currency exposure in restricted or illiquid local currencies (e.g., PHP, INR, IDR, VND) where the underlying cash flows are disbursed to contractors through local payout agents and deliverable forward markets are unavailable.
