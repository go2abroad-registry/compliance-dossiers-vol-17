Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-in capital is imposed for non-resident business corporations; at least one share must be issued and consideration therefor must be paid. No escrow account or capital deposit is required under Marshall Islands law.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance from the Majuro Fire Department and compliance with the RMI Fire and Life Safety Code; structural load inspection and certificate of occupancy from the Majuro Planning and Development Office; occupational workplace clearance from the Ministry of Health including radiation shielding verification, worker dose monitoring under ALARA principles, and emergency response certification before commercial production begins.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Customs (MIRC), Ministry of Finance, Government of the Republic of the Marshall Islands; corporate registration through the Registrar of Corporations and official government portal via the Ministry of Finance.

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities are not required to obtain an income tax TIN because non-resident business corporations deriving no Marshall Islands source income fall outside the domestic income tax system. For customs operations, the entity must register with MIRC as an importer/exporter to obtain a Customs Reference Number. For corporate existence, a Certificate of Incorporation must be obtained from the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act. A non-resident status confirmation letter may be requested from the Ministry of Finance to evidence exemption from tax on foreign-source income.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Foreign entities are not required to obtain an income tax TIN because non-resident business corporations deriving no Marshall Islands source income fall outside the domestic income tax system. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch tax elections are available under Marshall Islands law. A non-resident business corporation may elect non-resident domestic status under the Business Corporations Act, which is a corporate legal status election rather than a tax classification election. No direct income tax election is required because foreign-source income of a non-resident corporation is outside the tax base.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax regime; only customs import duty on specified imported goods

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable; the Marshall Islands does not impose VAT, GST, or sales tax on exports. All goods exported from the Marshall Islands, including ISIC 3250 dental/digital X-ray flat panel detectors, fall outside indirect taxation and require no zero-rating claim. Export documentation must support customs exit clearance and comply with any foreign recipient's substantive requirements.

  • Marshall Islands Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill evidencing physical departure
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank wire/LC documentation substantiating receipt of export proceeds

Not applicable - no real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime is implemented in the Marshall Islands.

No VAT clawback exposure exists because no indirect tax applies to exports. Customs audits may still scrutinize export declarations for accuracy. Misdeclaration or fraudulent export documentation is punishable under Marshall Islands Customs Regulations through monetary penalties, forfeiture of goods, and potential suspension/revocation of customs registration privileges.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility; no exchange control regime exists. Capital-account and current-account transactions are unrestricted, including profit remittances, capital injections, and dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders. No surrender, repatriation, or prior-approval requirements apply. The absence of local currency eliminates currency convertibility risk.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Export proceeds may be held indefinitely in USD-denominated accounts, either at licensed domestic banks or via compliant offshore correspondent arrangements.

Central bank supervision: Marshall Islands Banking Commission (Ministry of Finance); the Marshall Islands has no formal central bank, and the U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender and functional currency.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for primary treasury rail. No domestically licensed electronic money institution or specialized digital banking charter exists. Offshore digital EMIs are not recognized by the Marshall Islands Banking Commission for deposit-taking or clearing purposes; they may only be used as auxiliary collection channels if the underlying settlement is ultimately routed through a licensed U.S. correspondent bank. Legal constraints include prohibition on accepting USD deposits from Marshall Islands-licensed entities without a banking license.

High-risk jurisdiction exposure in inbound wire transfers from certain source countries

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injections are executed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 messages from the parent's or investor's bank to the Marshall Islands entity's USD account. Since the U.S. dollar is local legal tender, the transaction routes through US correspondent banks over Fedwire or CHIPS. No exchange-control notification is required. Remittance fields must reference 'Equity Capital Contribution' or 'Paid-in Capital' and include the corporate resolution number and appropriate purpose code (if the remitting jurisdiction requires one, e.g., 'CASHINC' or 'L101'). Supporting documents, including board resolutions, share subscription agreements, and issued share certificates, must be maintained for AML/KYC audit trail.Outbound trade settlements for procurement of amorphous silicon panels, cesium iodide scintillator materials, and flat-panel detector components are processed through SWIFT MT103 for direct payments to vendors and through commercial letters of credit (SWIFT MT700/MT707) for larger or contractual deliveries. Settlement is in USD via correspondent banks, with standard Incoterms (typically FOB or CIF) governing transfer of title.
