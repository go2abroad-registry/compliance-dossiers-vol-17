UBO Ultimate Beneficial Owner Screening Rules Official Registry Only

>=25% ownership / control

Applicable AML statute: Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act (Title 24, Marshall Islands Revised Code), as amended, including beneficial ownership record-keeping duties imposed on licensed Registered Agents; Financial Transactions Reporting Act 2000 and its AML/CFT Regulations (establishing the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)); Counter-Terrorism and Transnational Organized Crime Act 2002; Proliferation Financing (Prohibition) Act 2015. The RMI is a member of the Asia/Pacific Group on Money Laundering (APG). UBO information is maintained by the licensed Registered Agent and is accessible to the FIU and the Registrar of Corporations upon lawful demand; no public UBO register exists.
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permissible at RMI statutory level. A non-resident Business Corporation must continuously maintain a registered office at the address of its licensed Registered Agent; the agent's office serves as the legal seat for service of process and no physical staff presence in the Marshall Islands is required. Caveat: commercial counterparty substance thresholds (Amazon seller verification, Stripe, Airwallex, and downstream bank KYC) require a demonstrable operating nexus such as a stable corporate email, transaction records, and auditable financial trail, but these do not constitute an RMI taxable presence.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Ministry of Finance — Revenue and Taxation Division (RMI Revenue & Taxation), Majuro; online tax portal: https://www.mirevenue.org; customs administration: Customs Division, Ministry of Finance, Majuro; corporate registry: Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Ministry of Foreign Affairs & Trade, Majuro (access via licensed registered agents).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Business Corporation through a licensed registered agent by filing Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations, Majuro, and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation bearing the unique corporate registration number (the de facto statutory entity identifier). Step 2: File the Non-Resident Corporation Declaration and appoint a resident agent to perfect non-resident exempt status; no RMI TIN, VAT ID, or employer number is issued or required unless the company later employs a resident workforce (triggering Social Security Administration registration) or undertakes physical resident business with gross revenue above USD 50,000 (triggering the domestic 3% Gross Revenue Tax registration). Step 3: For banking, Amazon Seller Central, Stripe, and Airwallex KYC, request a Certificate of Good Standing and an optional Tax Clearance Certificate from the Revenue and Taxation Division confirming the absence of RMI tax liability; the corporate registration number is used wherever a jurisdictional tax reference must be inserted in foreign tax-ID fields.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — non-resident Business Corporation: excluded from the RMI taxing jurisdiction under the Marshall Islands Income Tax Act and Revenue and Taxation Act; exempt from national income tax, gross revenue tax, capital gains tax, and withholding tax; extraterritorial Amazon FBA marketplace turnover is not sourced to the RMI.

Available election forms

None under RMI domestic law — no check-the-box, S-corporation, or hybrid-mismatch election exists. For U.S. federal classification (e.g., FATCA / Section 7701), a Marshall Islands Business Corporation is a per se foreign corporation (Treas. Reg. § 301.7701-2(b)(8)) and cannot make a Form 8832 check-the-box election; default entity-level C-corporation treatment applies for U.S. persons owning the entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No RMI VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use Tax regime. Indirect levies are border-only: Import Duty (8% standard ad valorem) plus specific excise duties on alcohol, tobacco, and petroleum, collected by the RMI Customs Division, Ministry of Finance.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — the RMI has no VAT/GST registration concept. The only analogous trigger is the USD 50,000 annual gross-revenue threshold for the domestic 3% Gross Revenue Tax, which applies exclusively to resident businesses and never to a non-resident Business Corporation. The RMI uses the United States dollar (USD) as its official currency; no separate local-currency threshold exists.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — the RMI levies no VAT/GST and therefore has no export zero-rating or refund mechanism. A non-resident BC's Amazon FBA sales are extraterritorial: inventory is consigned from a third-country origin (e.g., China) directly to foreign fulfillment centers (United States, Europe) and never enters RMI customs territory; no RMI output tax arises, no export claim can be made, and no refund machinery exists.

  • RMI Certificate of Incorporation and Non-Resident Corporation Declaration (Registrar of Corporations, Majuro)
  • Certificate of Good Standing and optional Tax Clearance Certificate from RMI Revenue & Taxation Division
  • Commercial invoice / Amazon FBA shipment plan identifying consignor, consignee, and non-RMI fulfillment-center destination
  • Ocean or airway bill of lading evidencing that the goods never transited RMI customs territory
  • Destination-country customs entry documentation (e.g., U.S. CBP entry summary (CBP Form 3461/7501) or EU customs import declaration) showing the RMI entity as seller of record
  • Inward remittance certificate and Amazon disbursement/bank statements substantiating the cross-border proceeds flow (for bank due-diligence only; the RMI imposes no exchange control)

None — the RMI mandates no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, government API reporting, or digital stamping. Non-resident e-commerce entities may issue standard commercial invoices following Amazon FBA / marketplace seller practice; no RMI fiscal authorization or clearance is required.

There is no RMI VAT zero-rating or refund regime to claw back. However, false statements made to secure or preserve non-resident exempt status are punishable under the Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Act and Business Corporations Act by fines of up to USD 10,000, imprisonment, revocation of the tax-exempt classification, and deregistration by the Registrar of Corporations. Separately, incorrect export/import documentation filed for U.S. or EU customs purposes triggers liability under 19 U.S.C. § 1592 (negligence/fraud: penalties up to the dutiable value of merchandise, plus interest and liquidated damages) and may lead to Amazon Seller Central / FBA account termination.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

USD is the sole legal tender and domestic currency; full convertibility on both current and capital accounts with zero exchange controls, approvals, or licensing requirements; profit remittance and capital repatriation by non-resident Business Corporations are unrestricted; no domestic withholding tax on dividends, interest, or royalties; no RMI tax filing or foreign exchange declaration applies to a non-resident BC

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable; there is no statutory export proceeds surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio because the local currency is already USD and the RMI imposes no exchange controls; Amazon FBA settlement proceeds received in USD require no conversion, deposit into an RMI or offshore USD account.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commission of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (banking supervision under the Banking Act 1997); no formal central bank exists; US Federal Reserve System is the de facto monetary anchor because the US dollar is legal tender under the Compact of Free Association

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A - the Marshall Islands levies no corporate income tax and has enacted no transfer pricing documentation statute or Local File/Master File threshold. If a foreign jurisdiction (e.g., IRS or OECD CbCR) requires documentation, the general triggering thresholds are USD 12.5 million gross revenue for a Local File and USD 850 million consolidated group revenue for a Country-by-Country Report.

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands has not enacted a standalone Economic Substance Regulations regime analogous to BVI/Guernsey/Isle of Man. Nevertheless, the Business Corporation must demonstrate it is not conducting 'business' within the Republic, construed as business activity within RMI territory. For a remote Amazon FBA operation with no physical RMI presence, the entity may remain tax-exempt but must maintain: (i) a licensed registered agent; (ii) a local registered office; and (iii) a complete set of corporate records. Foreign tax authorities may independently recharacterize substance based on actual management and control locations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax-Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: Imposition of back taxes under the gross revenue tax regime at the applicable retail rate (up to 7% or other statutory rate) for the relevant years; penalty of 50% of the unpaid tax for fraudulent omission; interest accrual; revocation of the certificate of good standing.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign a false tax-exemption declaration are personally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; criminal conviction for perjury carries imprisonment up to 5 years; foreign directors may face an enforceable judgment through the High Court of the Marshall Islands.
Shell / Conduit Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Business Corporations Act the Attorney General may apply for judicial dissolution; the court may order forfeiture of share capital or disgorgement of profits; the Registrar may impose regulatory fines for failure to maintain adequate records in the RMI.

Director personal liability: If the corporate veil is pierced, directors and shareholders may be jointly and severally liable for company debts and for false representations to payment service providers; directors may be barred from acting as officers for 5 years and, in severe cases, may be imprisoned for fraud under the RMI Criminal Code.
Money Laundering / Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: The Marshall Islands AML/CFT Act criminalizes money laundering with corporate penalties of up to USD 1,000,000 and individual imprisonment up to 15 years; the Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze bank accounts; payment gateways may terminate the merchant agreement and report the seller as high risk.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly or negligently allow the platform to be used for money laundering are subject to criminal prosecution, travel bans, extradition risk, and seizure of personal assets; bank indemnity provisions may hold directors personally liable for chargeback fraud or illegal transactions.
Transfer Pricing Mismatch / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: While the RMI imposes no statutory transfer pricing penalty, foreign revenue authorities (e.g., IRS IRC 482, China SAT, HMRC) may assess upward adjustments, impose 20-40% transfer pricing penalties on the underpaid tax, and disallow the RMI entity's claimed exemption; the entity may also be blacklisted under the OECD's harmful tax practices review.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign manipulated intercompany transfer pricing contracts may be personally liable for restitution if the arrangement is found to be a sham, may face civil asset freezing orders, and in aggravated cases may be subject to extradition for tax fraud; professional advisors may face professional disqualification.
