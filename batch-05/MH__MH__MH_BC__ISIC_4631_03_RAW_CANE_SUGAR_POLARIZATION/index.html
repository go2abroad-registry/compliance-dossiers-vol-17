Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General, Republic of the Marshall Islands, Majuro, MH (filings administered through licensed RMI registered agents).

Governing statute

Business Corporations Act of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI), RMI Revised Code Title 51, as amended; applicable Sections 31–37 (incorporation), Section 33 (capitalization), Section 53 (registered office and registered agent), and Sections 60–80 (directors and officers); Foreign Investment Business Act of 2016 for foreign ownership registration if on-island activity is later conducted.

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation (Certificate of Incorporation) filed under RMI Business Corporations Act Section 36.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 10 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under RMI BCA Section 33; capital may be fixed by the board, and shares may be issued for lawful consideration not less than par value; no escrow or capital deposit requirement applicable to a non-resident Business Corporation.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable — the corporation maintains no physical industrial premises or employees in the RMI; registered office only. Should warehousing or office space be established later, the Majuro Fire Department building safety inspection, National Building Code certificate of occupancy, and workplace health and safety clearance would be required prior to commencement of on-island operations.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital under RMI BCA Section 33; capital may be fixed by the board, and shares may be issued for lawful consideration not less than par value; no escrow or capital deposit requirement applicable to a non-resident Business Corporation.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Division (RTD), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI); corporate registry and taxpayer administration are coordinated through licensed registered agents, the RMI Registrar of Corporations (www.rmicorporation.org), and the RTD taxpayer portal (www.rmi-tax.gov.mh).

E-government filing portal

Foreign incorporators appoint a licensed RMI registered agent; file Articles of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act 1990 (BCA) to obtain a Certificate of Incorporation; the agent then files a non-resident domestic corporation declaration (Business License Act exemption form) with the Registrar of Corporations and applies to the RTD for a Tax Identification Number (TIN). The RTD issues the TIN within 5-10 business days. No VAT ID exists in the RMI because no VAT system is in force. A non-resident corporation with zero RMI-source gross revenue is not required to maintain RMI payroll or transfer pricing files and files only a nil BPT return (or zero-liability declaration) annually.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone. A non-resident domestic Business Corporation is an entity-level taxable person for Business Privilege Tax (BPT) purposes only if it engages in business within the RMI; where wholesale raw cane sugar trading and vessel shipment occur entirely offshore, the BC is statutorily tax-exempt: zero income tax, zero BPT, zero withholding tax, zero gross receipts tax, and zero capital gains tax. — Foreign incorporators appoint a licensed RMI registered agent; file Articles of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act 1990 (BCA) to obtain a Certificate of Incorporation; the agent then files a non-resident domestic corporation declaration (Business License Act exemption form) with the Registrar of Corporations and applies to the RTD for a Tax Identification Number (TIN). The RTD issues the TIN within 5-10 business days. No VAT ID exists in the RMI because no VAT system is in force. A non-resident corporation with zero RMI-source gross revenue is not required to maintain RMI payroll or transfer pricing files and files only a nil BPT return (or zero-liability declaration) annually.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. A non-resident domestic Business Corporation is an entity-level taxable person for Business Privilege Tax (BPT) purposes only if it engages in business within the RMI; where wholesale raw cane sugar trading and vessel shipment occur entirely offshore, the BC is statutorily tax-exempt: zero income tax, zero BPT, zero withholding tax, zero gross receipts tax, and zero capital gains tax.

Available election forms

Under BCA sections 103-104, a Business Corporation may elect 'non-resident domestic' status by filing a certified declaration with the Registrar of Corporations and the RTD through its registered agent, confirming that no business is transacted in the RMI and no income is derived from RMI sources. This binary election (resident vs. non-resident) is the exclusive classification mechanism; no US-style check-the-box, no hybrid mismatch elections, no treaty-based branch elections, and no flow-through elections are recognized, and the RMI maintains no income tax treaty network.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use Tax. The only indirect levies are the Business Privilege Tax (gross receipts tax on RMI-source activity) and the Traders' Tax (customs import duty and services gross receipts tax); both are inapplicable to offshore sugar commodity arbitrage executed by a non-resident domestic BC.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD 0; there is no de minimis turnover threshold. Any gross revenue derived from within the RMI triggers BPT registration and taxation (statutory BPT rates: 3% on the first USD 500,000 of RMI-source gross revenue, 5% on the next USD 500,000, and 7% above USD 1,000,000). A non-resident domestic BC engaged exclusively in offshore sugar shipments crosses no threshold and holds tax-exempt zero-liability status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT concept: the RMI does not impose VAT, so there is no 0% export VAT rate, no export VAT refund, and no input-credit mechanism. Instead, export and offshore revenue is categorically excluded from the BPT base for a non-resident domestic BC under the BCA and BPT Act source rules. Eligibility for the zero-liability classification requires: (i) a valid non-resident domestic declaration on file; (ii) no physical office, employees, or inventory in the RMI; (iii) the sugar cargo never enters RMI customs territory; and (iv) title and risk pass on FOB/CIF/CIF-FO terms outside RMI waters under a revolving letter of credit.

  • RMI Certificate of Incorporation and non-resident domestic corporation declaration (BCA sections 103-104)
  • Negotiable Ocean Bill of Lading issued to the order of the LC issuing bank evidencing shipment on an RMI-flagged or chartered vessel
  • Carrier Cargo Manifest and the origin country's Customs Export Declaration (e.g., Brazil SISCOMEX or Thailand e-Customs)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List for VHP raw cane centrifugal sugar (INCOTERMS FOB/CIF)
  • Certificate of Origin issued by the origin country chamber of commerce
  • SGS or CIQ-licensed Certificate of Quality, Weight, and Polarization (ICUMSA-polarization assay)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or LC settlement advice from the negotiating bank evidencing USD inflow

None. The RMI imposes no electronic invoicing mandate, no real-time fiscal clearance, no government API clearance, and no digital stamping regime. BPT taxpayers may retain paper or electronic records for at least three years. Export transactions are evidenced by the origin country's customs electronic declarations (e.g., Brazil SISCOMEX or Thailand e-Customs) and commercial shipping documents, not by RMI fiscal invoices.

RMI audit exposure is minimal for a properly classified non-resident BC. However, the RTD may audit nil BPT filings where misclassification is suspected (e.g., substantive board meetings in Majuro, RMI office used for decision-making, or shipping documentation showing discharge into RMI ports). Consequences of wrongful zero-liability filing include: (i) retroactive BPT assessment at 3%-7% of recharacterized RMI-source gross revenue; (ii) statutory interest plus a 10% per month delinquency penalty on unreported amounts; (iii) revocation of the non-resident domestic declaration and tax-exempt status; (iv) suspension or revocation of the Business License and corporate certificate by the Registrar of Corporations; (v) director liability and fines for false declarations under the BPT Act; and (vi) no separate export-zero-rating clawback regime applies because the RMI administers no export VAT refund or input credit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility with no exchange control legislation. Capital account and current account transactions are unrestricted. The U.S. dollar circulates domestically, so inward capital injections, trade settlements, dividend distributions, shareholder loan repayments, and liquidation proceeds can be remitted freely without central bank approval. Non-resident Business Corporations are generally exempt from RMI income and withholding taxes, and there are no statutory profit remittance hurdles. Practical hurdles are limited to commercial bank compliance, OFAC/sanctions screening, source-of-funds documentation, and correspondent bank due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirements, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no prescribed repatriation timeline. Export proceeds of raw centrifugal cane sugar may be retained in USD or foreign-currency accounts at the exporter's discretion. If receipts are denominated in a non-USD currency, the exporter may convert freely or hold the funds offshore. The RMI imposes no foreign exchange registration requirement for export contracts.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). The U.S. dollar is the legal tender. Banking and money transmission licensing/supervision is administered by the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low / constrained. The RMI has no digital e-money institution licensing regime. A foreign fintech or EMI cannot independently obtain a banking license or direct RMI clearing membership. Such an entity may only operate through a partnership or sponsor arrangement with a licensed RMI commercial bank or a registered money transmitter, and all fund movements remain subject to the Money Laundering Prevention Act and the correspondent banking compliance standards of the U.S. clearing banks. Offshore EMIs face significant onboarding and de-risking challenges in this jurisdiction.
