Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Authority (RTA), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; corporate and beneficial-ownership registry: Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General (Majuro). Filing is predominantly paper-based; no public e-filing portal exists. The Registrar of Corporations is operationally supported by International Registries, Inc. (IRI) under a long-term administrative services agreement.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation (BC) formed under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act (BCA) is not required to register with the RTA merely to hold foreign-source revenue. SOP: (1) file Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation (no minimum capital); (2) appoint a Registered Agent in Majuro and file a Beneficial Ownership Registration; (3) if no RMI-source revenue is derived, no income tax or Business Gross Revenue Tax (BGRT) registration arises; (4) optionally, a General Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) may be obtained from the RTA by submitting the Certificate of Incorporation, Registered Agent confirmation, and a non-resident business declaration; (5) no VAT ID exists because the RMI does not operate a VAT system. The BC's only recurring obligation is the annual corporate report and franchise fee paid to the Registrar.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (territorial sourcing): a non-resident BC deriving exclusively foreign-source income from the production and global distribution of vertical micro-dramas is exempt from RMI income tax, BGRT, withholding taxes, and capital-based taxes. No permanent-establishment threshold applies, but the exemption lapses if the BC derives income from sources inside the Marshall Islands or carries on business within the RMI (e.g., local screenings, local advertisers, or physical production in Majuro). — A non-resident Business Corporation (BC) formed under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act (BCA) is not required to register with the RTA merely to hold foreign-source revenue. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (territorial sourcing): a non-resident BC deriving exclusively foreign-source income from the production and global distribution of vertical micro-dramas is exempt from RMI income tax, BGRT, withholding taxes, and capital-based taxes. No permanent-establishment threshold applies, but the exemption lapses if the BC derives income from sources inside the Marshall Islands or carries on business within the RMI (e.g., local screenings, local advertisers, or physical production in Majuro).

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid-mismatch, or participation-exemption election exists under RMI law. Tax-exempt status arises by operation of law for non-resident BCs. The BCA permits a formal election to designate the entity as a 'non-resident domestic corporation' for governance and registry purposes. Separately, for U.S. federal tax classification, an RMI Business Corporation is generally treated as a per se corporation under U.S. Treasury Regulations and therefore is not eligible for U.S. check-the-box classification; that is a U.S. tax matter, not an RMI statutory election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or general sales/use tax in the Marshall Islands. Indirect taxation is limited to (a) import duty on physical goods under RMI customs law and (b) Business Gross Revenue Tax (BGRT) of 3% on the gross revenue of any person carrying on business within the RMI. Digital app-store sales of micro-drama content to overseas end users are outside the scope of both taxes. The BC should nevertheless note that Apple, Google, and Stripe may collect foreign digital-services VAT/GST (e.g., EU, Australia, Singapore, Korea) on behalf of the seller in customer jurisdictions; those foreign taxes are not RMI taxes.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT threshold exists because no VAT regime operates. For BGRT, registration is required from the first dollar of RMI-source gross revenue for any person carrying on business within the RMI; a non-resident BC with no RMI activity has no nexus and no registration threshold. An importer code is required only if physical goods are imported into the RMI, which is not the case for pure digital distribution.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: the RMI operates no VAT/GST regime, and therefore no zero-rating, export-refund, or input-credit mechanism exists. The non-resident BC's revenues from the global distribution of vertical micro-dramas through Apple IAP, Google Play Billing, and Stripe are foreign-source and constitutionally outside the RMI indirect tax net; no claim, election, or refund procedure is required, and no RMI output tax is charged to end users.

  • Not applicable for digital exports: no Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, or Inward Remittance Certificate is required for streaming-based micro-drama distribution because no physical goods cross the RMI border. If the BC were to export physical media (e.g., promotional merchandise or pre-loaded sd cards), the RMI Customs Division would require a Customs Export Declaration and transport document; however, no export VAT certificate exists in the RMI.

None. The RMI has no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control (CTC), API-based tax reporting, or digital stamping mandate. Invoicing and records follow general commercial standards; no government data-exchange format is required.

No export zero-rating fraud regime exists because no zero-rating mechanism exists. General enforcement: the RTA may reassess income tax and BGRT (3%) plus statutory interest on any revenue proven to be sourced in the RMI, such as local theatrical screenings, Majuro-based advertisers, physical production conducted in the RMI, or management functions performed from the RMI. The Income Tax Act provides civil penalties and administrative fines for failure to file or for false returns when RMI-source income is at issue, and the RMI business license regime imposes separate penalties for operating locally without a license. The RMI has no currency controls or FX repatriation deadline, hence the 0-day figure; however, the RMI is a member of the OECD BEPS Inclusive Framework and exchanges beneficial-ownership and CRS information. The BC should contemporaneously retain app-store payout reports (Apple App Store Connect, Google Play Console), Stripe settlement statements, bank records, management accounts, and subscriber geolocation data to substantiate the absence of RMI-source revenue in the event of an RTA information request or challenge.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Marshall Islands operates a fully dollarized monetary system, meaning there is no distinction between local currency and hard reserve currency. Both current and capital account transactions are freely convertible and unrestricted. No exchange control legislation, no central bank licensing of foreign exchange transactions, and no restriction on profit repatriation, dividends, or capital returns exist for non-resident Business Corporations. The only formal hurdles relate to Corporate Registry filing fees, annual license franchise tax, and anti-money laundering recordkeeping obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are absolutely no statutory export proceeds surrender rules, no mandatory repatriation timelines, and no minimum conversion ratios. A Marshall Islands Business Corporation may retain export receipts in any jurisdiction, in any currency, and utilize such funds without central bank approval. This position is confirmed by the absence of exchange control regulations under the Marshall Islands Revised Code and the Currency and Valuation Act practice.

Central bank supervision: No domestic central bank exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands; the official legal tender is the United States dollar. Monetary supervision is performed indirectly by the U.S. Federal Reserve System, with prudential regulation and licensing of domestic depository institutions carried out by the Marshall Islands Banking Commissioner under the Banking Act of 1991 and the Financial Institutions Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as the primary treasury clearing venue; Medium as a supplementary collection and payout channel. The Marshall Islands has no digital bank, electronic money institution, or fintech licensing regime; therefore local EMIs cannot be formed or regulated. Offshore digital EMIs may be engaged as payment aggregators or custodial wallet providers for micro-drama app store receipts, but they do not provide secure wire transfer rails, cannot issue bank guarantees, and often lack access to USD correspondent banking. Legal constraints also include US OFAC sanctions screening expectations, FATF membership expectations, and the inability of EMIs to provide corporate credit products.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For compliant inbound capital injection, the standard routing is a SWIFT MT103 wire denominated in USD through the shareholder's banking institution to the Bank of Marshall Islands or Bank of Guam correspondent account in the United States, using advice purpose code (e.g., 'INVT' or 'LOAN' as applicable under ISO 20022), with the beneficiary account being a USD treasury account owned by the Marshall Islands Business Corporation. The sending bank must include the corporate legal name and ultimate beneficiary owner reference. Since the Marshall Islands has no foreign exchange controls, a foreign investment registration certificate is not required; however, the domestic bank will request an internal memorandum mapping the wire to a board-approved capital plan, a signed source-of-funds declaration, and a valid certificate of good standing to satisfy internal OFAC and AML verification.Outbound trade disbursements for production services, post-production, royalty splits, and marketing agency fees will be executed over the SWIFT network, predominantly MT103 customer credit transfers and, for interbank liquidity settlement, MT202 COV messages. Where the arrangement requires documentary credit, a Commercial Letter of Credit (MT700/MT710) may be issued through the domestic bank with a confirming bank based in the U.S. or Hong Kong. Ultimate clearing of all USD payments will occur through the U.S. Federal Reserve Bank wire network or CHIPS, using the domestic bank's U.S. correspondent relationship. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer-pricing threshold in the Republic of the Marshall Islands; RMI has no enacted transfer-pricing law. As a best-practice safeguard, prepare USD-denominated intercompany agreements and a Local File when annual intercompany transactions exceed USD 2,000,000, though no filing is statutorily mandated.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2021 and the Economic Substance Regulations 2021 require an RMI registered entity carrying on relevant activities to demonstrate adequate economic presence in RMI. Vertical micro-drama production and distribution may be classified as IP-holding, distribution, or service-centre activity; the company must document RMI board meetings, local agent oversight, bank accounts, and payroll or expenditure proportional to value creation. A purely non-resident exempt entity with no relevant activity files an annual ESR notification or nil declaration through the Registrar's electronic portal. Non-compliance penalties: initial fine up to USD 10,000, continuing daily default fine of USD 100, and automatic exchange of information with foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Underreporting

Statutory sanction: Civil fraud penalty of 25% of the understated gross revenue tax, if RMI-source, plus 1.5% monthly interest; criminal referral under the RMI Criminal Code for tax fraud carries a fine of up to USD 25,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years per count.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for corporate penalties; RMI courts may pierce the corporate veil and order restitution of diverted funds; travel bans and surrender of passports may be imposed for flight risk; conviction results in disqualification from serving as a director for 5 years.
Shell Company / Abusive Structuring

Statutory sanction: Violation of the Economic Substance Regulations 2021: fine of up to USD 10,000 per offence plus daily default fine of USD 100; the Registrar may strike off the company; foreign tax authorities may re-characterise the entire revenue stream as taxable in the end-market under GAAR or look-through rules, triggering full corporate income tax, interest, and penalties in the source country.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed by foreign revenue authorities under controlled-foreign-company and economic-substance rules; RMI courts may award restitution for misrepresentation to the Registrar; foreign travel bans and extradition requests are enforceable under RMI law; disqualification from holding RMI fiduciary roles for up to 10 years may apply.
AML / Economic Sanctions Failure

Statutory sanction: Breach of the RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act 2005 (as amended): civil penalty of up to USD 1,000,000 for the corporate entity and up to USD 250,000 per responsible individual; wilful breach is a criminal offence punishable by fine and imprisonment of up to 10 years; US OFAC secondary sanctions exposure may attach through dollar-clearing banking.

Director personal liability: Directors sign the AML compliance statement and are presumed to have knowledge where books are absent; civil clawback of the value of payments processed in breach; criminal travel bans, asset freeze orders by the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit, and extradition to the end-market jurisdiction for money laundering where the predicate offence is proven.
Unlicensed Payment Business / Consumer Fraud

Statutory sanction: The RMI Banking Act 1991 treats unlicensed payment intermediation as a criminal offence: fine of up to USD 100,000 and imprisonment of up to 7 years; the RMI Trade Commission may revoke the export licence, impose consumer restitution orders, and platform-level sanctions may include permanent de-listing from Apple and Google with withholding of receivable balances.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct the commingling of user funds face personal restitution orders, disqualification as officers of the Business Corporation, and civil fraud liability to aggrieved users; flight risk triggers RMI court-ordered travel bans and attachment of personal assets; extradition may follow under bilateral arrangements with the United States for wire-fraud predicates.
