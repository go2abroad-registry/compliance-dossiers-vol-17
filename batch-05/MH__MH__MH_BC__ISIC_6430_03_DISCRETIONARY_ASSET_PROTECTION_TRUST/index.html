Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual or hybrid corporate-office model is accepted for the Business Corporation shell provided a licensed registered agent in Majuro maintains the registered office, accepts service of process, and retains the statutory records. However, the licensed trust company activity (fiduciary administration of irrevocable offshore spendthrift trusts) requires substantive commercial substance: a physical office in the RMI or in a jurisdiction expressly approved by the Banking Commissioner, a designated resident or reachable compliance officer, documented AML/CFT procedures, audit-accessible trust records, and board oversight meetings; a purely virtual operation with no substance is not permissible for the Trust Company license.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Customs Administration, Ministry of Finance and Banking (Majuro, Republic of the Marshall Islands); corporate registry maintained by the Office of the Registrar of Corporations

E-government filing portal

Non-resident Business Corporations (BC) are statutorily tax-exempt and generally do not obtain a Marshall Islands TIN or VAT ID. The formation procedure is: (i) file Articles of Incorporation and an Exempt Company application with the Registrar of Corporations; (ii) pay the annual government service fee; (iii) obtain a Certificate of Good Standing. No further tax registration is mandatory for offshore-exempt activities. If the BC will import goods or be subject to customs transactions, a customs importer/exporter registration with the Revenue and Customs Administration may be required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-resident Business Corporations (BC) are statutorily tax-exempt and generally do not obtain a Marshall Islands TIN or VAT ID. The formation procedure is: (i) file Articles of Incorporation and an Exempt Company application with the Registrar of Corporations; (ii) pay the annual government service fee; (iii) obtain a Certificate of Good Standing. No further tax registration is mandatory for offshore-exempt activities. If the BC will import goods or be subject to customs transactions, a customs importer/exporter registration with the Revenue and Customs Administration may be required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or domestic classification elections exist under Marshall Islands law. A non-resident BC is automatically exempt from all national income, withholding, capital gains and estate taxes under the Business Corporations Act. Foreign tax classification (e.g., CFC, foreign partnership) is determined solely by the beneficial owner's home country tax law. Hybrid mismatch elections are not recognized domestically.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST (Customs Import Duties Only)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Inapplicable — the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT/GST. There is no 0% export VAT rate or VAT refund regime. Exports of goods may be relieved of import/customs duties when properly declared, but no value-added tax zero-rating claim can be made.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Form CED-1)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential treatment is claimed)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (where proof of foreign payment is required)

Not applicable — no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime is in force. Physical import/export clearances are handled through the RMI Customs Services using paper or pilot electronic submissions.

No VAT or income tax clawback applies in the absence of a VAT regime. However, fraudulent customs declarations, misdescription of goods, or failure to comply with RMI Customs Act 1990 may result in seizure and forfeiture of goods, non-criminal fines up to USD 10,000, criminal prosecution, and revocation of the company's exempt status by the Registrar of Corporations for serious non-compliance.
