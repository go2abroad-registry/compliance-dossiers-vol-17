Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance (Treasury and Tax Division), Republic of the Marshall Islands; corporate registry administered by the Office of the Registrar of Corporations. No operational online tax filing portal exists; all statutory submissions are made by licensed registered agents, and no electronic TIN issuance platform is available.

E-government filing portal

No statutory TIN or VAT ID is required for a non-resident Business Corporation (BC) because the entity is exempt from Marshall Islands income tax and no indirect tax regime exists. Procedure: (1) engage a licensed registered agent; (2) file Articles of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act 1990; (3) obtain the Certificate of Incorporation bearing the corporate registration number, which serves as the de facto identifier; (4) confirm non-resident domestic corporation status; no separate tax registration step applies.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (non-resident BC exempt from income tax on all non-RMI-source income; territorial scope limited to income arising in or derived from within the Marshall Islands; no capital gains, withholding, payroll, or property tax at national level) — No statutory TIN or VAT ID is required for a non-resident Business Corporation (BC) because the entity is exempt from Marshall Islands income tax and no indirect tax regime exists. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (non-resident BC exempt from income tax on all non-RMI-source income; territorial scope limited to income arising in or derived from within the Marshall Islands; no capital gains, withholding, payroll, or property tax at national level)

Available election forms

None. The RMI does not provide check-the-box, partnership classification, or hybrid mismatch elections under domestic law. The only relevant election is the designation of non-resident status under the Business Corporations Act 1990, which exempts the BC from RMI income tax provided it does not carry on business in the RMI and does not derive income from RMI sources.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax in the Marshall Islands)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no indirect tax registration threshold exists. Customs import duties and a 2% import service charge apply solely to goods entering RMI customs territory and do not affect offshore FPV drone retail sales exported from or transshipped through the RMI.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable; the RMI has no VAT/GST system and therefore no export zero-rating mechanism. Exports of carbon-fiber FPV racing drones, brushless motors, and spare components by a non-resident BC fall entirely outside RMI taxation; no export duty, excise, turnover tax, exchange control, or FX repatriation deadline applies.

  • Not applicable - no VAT zero-rating claim is possible; retain commercial invoice, packing list, and air waybill as underlying trade evidence
  • Certificate of Origin (only if demanded by the destination country's customs regime)
  • Bill of lading or air waybill and freight forwarding records as evidence of physical export movement

Not applicable; there is no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping mandate in the RMI. Import customs declarations are processed through the RMI Customs Service, and no fiscal clearance or prior approval is required for export transactions.

No VAT clawback or zero-rating fraud regime exists because no indirect tax is levied. RMI Income Tax Act penalties apply only if a BC loses non-resident status and derives RMI-source income: 10% monthly penalty on unpaid tax plus statutory interest, and willful evasion is punishable by fine up to USD 5,000 and/or up to one year imprisonment. Under the Business Corporations Act 1990, knowingly false statements to the Registrar are subject to monetary penalties and potential administrative dissolution of the corporation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMI has no foreign-exchange control law. Both current and capital account transactions are notionally fully convertible because the domestic currency is the U.S. dollar. There are no statutory exchange permits, profit-remittance approvals, or repatriation limits for non-resident business corporations. Practical convertibility is nevertheless constrained by correspondent-bank AML/KYC requirements, OFAC sanctions screening, and the requirement that funds flow through a licensed RMI banking institution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI imposes no mandatory surrender, conversion ratio, or statutory timeline for export proceeds. Export receipts from FPV racing drone sales may be held indefinitely in U.S. dollars or in foreign-currency accounts, subject to the commercial bank's internal transaction-monitoring and documentary trade-support requirements.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance and Treasury, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI does not maintain a central bank; the United States dollar is the sole legal tender)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI and e-money institutions do not hold an RMI banking license and cannot directly access RMI clearing infrastructure. They may be used only as non-regulated collection front-ends for PayPal/Stripe-style retail receipts, but local banks will ordinarily reject inbound transfers sourced from unlicensed EMIs without full underlying customer and trade documentation. Legal viability is therefore conditional on the EMI maintaining a U.S. or EU bank license and a transparent corresponding-bank trail.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing threshold exists under RMI law; a prudent USD 500,000 annual intercompany transaction threshold is recommended for maintaining arm's-length documentation and economic substance support

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands does not maintain standalone Economic Substance Regulations applicable to qualified non-resident Business Corporations. The non-resident exemption derives from statutory status. However, the Business Corporations Act requires a registered office, registered agent, and adequate corporate records in the Republic. If the entity is managed or controlled from the RMI or carries on business in the Republic, it may be reclassified as resident and become subject to the 3% gross revenue tax and full economic substance scrutiny

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Gross Revenue Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of 50% of the tax evaded plus monthly interest of 1.5%; criminal conviction carries a fine up to USD 10,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false exemption declarations or direct the evasion are personally liable for tax and penalties; the corporate veil is pierced and directors may face travel bans, extradition, and custodial sentences
Shell Company / Sham Entity Without Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative dissolution by the Registrar of Corporations, reclassification as resident with retroactive assessment of 3% gross revenue tax, and fines up to USD 5,000

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the veil where the shell misleads regulators, creditors, or tax authorities; directors are jointly and severally liable for corporate debts and may be disqualified and imprisoned for fraud
AML / KYC Breach

Statutory sanction: Civil fines up to USD 100,000 for compliance failures, suspension or revocation of the company's registration, and referral to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit for criminal proceedings

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are subject to personal fines up to USD 25,000; in laundering cases, personal asset forfeiture and imprisonment apply, and travel bans are routinely imposed during investigation
False Representation to Registered Agent / Filing Fraud

Statutory sanction: Striking off the company, cancellation of non-resident status, criminal fine up to USD 5,000, and imprisonment up to 2 years

Director personal liability: Directors who execute false filings are personally liable for resulting liabilities; criminal falsification enables veil-piercing and restitution orders against personal assets
Unlicensed Payment Activity / Money Transmission

Statutory sanction: Cease-and-desist orders, fines up to USD 50,000 per violation, asset freezes, and overseas regulatory enforcement

Director personal liability: Directors face civil restitution proceedings in the US, EU, and Asia-Pacific jurisdictions; persistent violations trigger extradition, travel bans, and imprisonment equivalent to wire-fraud charges
