Competent corporate registry

The Registrar of Corporations, Commercial Registry, Republic of the Marshall Islands (Majuro), acting through licensed Marshall Islands resident registered agents.

Governing statute

Business Corporations Act 1990 (as amended), Marshall Islands Revised Code (MIRC) Title 34, Chapter 1; in particular § 204 (Articles of Incorporation), § 205 (Certificate of Incorporation), § 226 (Registered Office and Registered Agent), and § 227 (By-Laws); read together with the Income Tax Act 1989 (MIRC Title 13) for the Non-Resident Domestic Corporation tax-exempt regime.

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation (BC Form 1) executed by the incorporator and lodged with the Registrar of Corporations; issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation constitutes completion of incorporation.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital: the Articles of Incorporation need only state the authorized share capital (e.g., US$1.00 or one no-par share). No escrow or trust account is mandated by the Business Corporations Act; the Registrar's US$275 incorporation fee and US$100 prepaid first-year franchise tax are remitted through the licensed registered agent's client account.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the RMI Fire Marshal's Office (fire extinguisher zoning, commercial kitchen hood suppression system, and egress plan); structural load inspection and occupancy certificate from the Ministry of Public Works; workplace food-safety clearance and food-handler certifications from the Ministry of Health and Human Services (Environmental Health), including occupational hygiene clearance for heat, grease, ventilation, and segregated raw/ready-to-eat food zones.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities are not required to obtain a Marshall Islands Tax Identification Number (TIN) unless deriving Marshall Islands-source business income subject to Gross Revenue Tax; Business Corporations without RMI-source income are tax-exempt and no registration is required. For entities requiring a GRT account, file a registration application with the Revenue and Taxation Division, providing a certified copy of the certificate of incorporation and applicable business license.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Foreign entities are not required to obtain a Marshall Islands Tax Identification Number (TIN) unless deriving Marshall Islands-source business income subject to Gross Revenue Tax; Business Corporations without RMI-source income are tax-exempt and no registration is required. For entities requiring a GRT account, file a registration application with the Revenue and Taxation Division, providing a certified copy of the certificate of incorporation and applicable business license.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None available; RMI does not offer check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. A Business Corporation may elect or maintain status as a non-resident entity under the Business Corporations Act, thereby remaining exempt from RMI gross revenue tax on foreign-source income.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no VAT/GST/sales and use tax imposed at national level; customs import duty applies separately)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because RMI does not impose VAT/GST. Exports of goods or offshore services are not subject to an indirect tax, and therefore no zero-rating claim or refund mechanism exists under domestic law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Marshall Islands has no domestic currency; the US dollar is the sole legal tender. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible and free from exchange controls, with no restrictions on profit, dividend, or distribution remittances to non-resident shareholders. A Non-Resident Business Corporation claiming statutory tax exemption under the Business Corporations Act must nonetheless show that no local business is conducted in the Marshall Islands, and in practice its treasury operations clear through offshore USD correspondent accounts because on-island branches require local substance for sustained mid-market banking activity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Marshall Islands law imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export or service receipts. Revenues of the ghost kitchen entity are held and settled directly in US dollars without central bank intervention; no exchange control approval is required to retain funds abroad.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands; banking supervision and non-bank deposit licensing are administered by the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, with the US dollar as sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium with material caveats. Regulated offshore digital EMIs and licensed payment institutions can accept a Marshall Islands Non-Resident Business Corporation only after enhanced due diligence, and several Tier-1 platforms (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) classify RMI tax-exempt structures as higher-risk due to the absence of central bank supervision. Onboarding is contingent on delivery-platform agreements proving ISIC 5610 operational substance, but EMIs remain unsuitable as the sole treasury rail because they cannot issue commercial letters of credit, hold escrowed capital, or provide committed credit facilities.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions are executed in US dollars by SWIFT MT103 from the overseas shareholder's bank to the BC's USD clearing account at a licensed commercial bank or US correspondent. The wire must reference Shareholder Capital Contribution and use purpose code EQUI (equity injection) or BCT for business commercial transfer, accompanied by the signed subscription agreement, board resolution allotting shares, and share-certificate ledger. Because the Marshall Islands operates a dollarized economy with no exchange controls, no central-bank approval, FX license, or capital-import permit is required; the receiving bank will verify the non-resident status of the BC and may require the tax-exemption certificate to avoid withholding documentation.Trade settlements run through SWIFT MT103 for supplier payments, aggregator payouts, and intercompany disbursements, with MT202 for bank-to-bank nostro transfers when needed.
