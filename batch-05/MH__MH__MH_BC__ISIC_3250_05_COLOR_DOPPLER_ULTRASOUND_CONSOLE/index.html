Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required for a non-resident Business Corporation under the Business Corporations Act 1990. The Corporation may commence business upon the subscription of one or more shares as stated in the Articles; a paid-up capital escrow account is not mandated by Marshall Islands law.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Majuro Fire and Rescue Services confirming compliance with NFPA 101 Life Safety Code and NFPA 10 portable fire extinguisher requirements; structural load inspection certificate issued by the Majuro Atoll Local Government building authority for the leased manufacturing premises; and workplace clearance from the occupational safety authority confirming compliance with electrical safety (IEC 60601-1), cleanroom, and ergonomic workstation requirements for medical device assembly operations.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Customs Administration (RICA) of the Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; official tax portal: https://www.mirevenue.org

E-government filing portal

Non-resident Business Corporations (MH_BC) are exempt from RMI income tax and are not required to obtain a domestic TIN or VAT ID. The company obtains its Marshall Islands Corporate Registration Number from the Registrar of Corporations at formation, which is used for the filing of annual franchise tax reports. If a tax identification number is required by an overseas regulator or counterparty, the company may apply on a voluntary basis to RICA for a Non-Resident Identification Number (NRID) using Form RTD-TIN-01 and a certificate of good standing; however, no recurring income tax filing obligation attaches to such an NRID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No RMI domestic check-the-box, anti-hybrid, or other tax classification election is available; a non-resident business corporation is automatically treated as a tax-exempt foreign entity under RMI law. For U.S. federal income tax purposes, the entity may be separately classified under U.S. Treasury Regulations Section 301.7701-3 (e.g., a single-owner RMI BC may elect to be treated as a disregarded entity), but this election has no domestic RMI effect.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; customs import duty is the only indirect levy, and neither imports nor exports are subject to VAT/GST

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no VAT/GST/sales tax registration threshold exists. Customs duty registration and bond requirements apply only to importers licensed under RMI Customs and Tariff regulations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT, GST, or sales tax. There is no statutory 0% export VAT rate to claim, and no export tax, offshore services tax, or consumption tax is levied on non-resident business corporations exporting medical imaging equipment.

  • Marshall Islands Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World trade processing system) evidencing physical departure of the goods from RMI customs territory
  • Bill of Lading, Sea Waybill, or Air Waybill consigned to the foreign purchaser
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the Color Doppler Ultrasound Console, harmonized system code, serial numbers, and export value
  • Inward remittance evidence, including SWIFT MT103 confirmation or bank credit statement, supporting receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency

Not applicable; no real-time fiscal clearance mandate, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping requirement. Customs export/import filings are processed electronically through ASYCUDA World, but no invoice clearance or tax-stamping applies to exports.

As zero-rating does not exist, there are no export VAT refunds or clawback penalties. However, RICA and RMI Customs may audit export declarations for customs/franchise tax compliance; false declarations, forged shipping documents, or misclassified medical device exports may attract fines up to USD 50,000, penalty assessments up to 200% of undeclared value, forfeiture of goods, and criminal liability for willful fraud. Additionally, export proceeds received after the 180-day best-practice deadline may trigger exchange-control or regulatory reporting scrutiny, though RMI maintains no formal foreign exchange control regime.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de jure and de facto convertibility in both capital and current accounts. The RMI imposes no exchange controls, no foreign-investment approval, no surrender requirements, and no repatriation restrictions on dividends, loan repayments, trade proceeds, or capital injections. Profit remittance is subject only to RMI income-tax compliance: the ordinary corporate tax rate is 3% on RMI-source income, while a non-resident Business Corporation (BC) earning no RMI-source income qualifies for statutory exemption; no central-bank permit or tax clearance is required. Practical convertibility, however, is constrained by the absence of a local interbank FX market and by correspondent-bank de-risking pressure arising from RMI's status on the FATF list of jurisdictions under increased monitoring (grey list) and its inclusion on the EU Code of Conduct Group non-cooperative list. Funds are effectively cleared in USD through U.S. correspondents, and non-USD settlements require offshore banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The RMI imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to surrender export proceeds to the RMI government or any authorized dealer. Export earnings may be retained offshore in any currency; for an ISIC 3250 medical-device exporter with USD functional currency, receipts are typically held in USD. The only obligations are documentary retention: commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, LC documents, and related bank records must be preserved for not less than five years for tax and AML audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance & Banking, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). The RMI maintains no central bank, no currency-issuing authority, and no domestic monetary policy framework. The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association, making the U.S. Federal Reserve and the U.S. correspondent banking network the de facto monetary anchor. Licensing, AML/CFT supervision, and financial-market conduct regulation are administered by the Banking Commissioner and the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) under the Banking Act 1991 and the AML/CFT Regulations 2004, as amended.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Reputable offshore digital EMIs (Airwallex, Mercury, Wise Business, Payoneer, Currencycloud) routinely decline RMI-registered non-resident Business Corporations because RMI is on the FATF grey list and the EU non-cooperative tax list. The lack of a local fit-and-proper licensing nexus, the entity's tax-exempt shell profile, and the zero-minimum-capital structure compound the risk. EMIs that accept RMI entities impose strict conditions: a second operational license or branch in Singapore/Hong Kong/UK, a minimum of 12 months of audited trading history, and a U.S.-bank reference. The greenfield entity cannot satisfy these conditions; reliance should be placed on the two domestic banks with U.S. correspondent clearance for at least the first 18 months of operation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Because the RMI has no inward FDI approval, no exchange-control license, and no capital-account registration requirement, equity injection is executed as a plain USD wire from the shareholder's reputable banking provider directly to the RMI operating account via SWIFT MT103. Optimal routing: the sending bank should be a U.S., Singapore, or Hong Kong licensed institution (e.g., HSBC, Standard Chartered, JPMorgan, or Bank of Hawaii) and the wire must contain (i) tag 50K with shareholder legal name and account number; (ii) tag 59 with the full MH_BC corporate name; and (iii) tag 70/reference field reading 'Equity subscription per Business Corporations Act 1990 - MH_BC share issue' to avoid mischaracterization as trade credit or loan. The receiving RMI bank will require the notarized board resolution authorizing the allotment, the subscription agreement, and the register of members. Inbound amounts above USD 250,000 trigger a 5 to 10 business-day enhanced due-diligence hold pending UBO identity verification. Funds must arrive exclusively through banking channels; RMI banks reject deposits originating from unlicensed money transmitters, casinos, or virtual-currency exchanges.Two compliant clearing rails are available for the color-Doppler ultrasound console supply chain: (1) Direct wire - SWIFT MT103 for component purchases under USD 50,000, settled in USD same-day via U.S. correspondent banks (FEDWIRE/CHIPS), with the invoice number and Incoterms 2020 (EXW or FOB) referenced in tag 70. (2) Documentary credit - an irrevocable UCP600-compliant commercial letter of credit issued by the RMI bank's U.S. correspondent in favor of the Asian component supplier, with the MH_BC as applicant. Given the thin correspondent network, the LC tenor should be limited to 30 to 60 days sight or deferred payment, with the document package comprising the original commercial invoice, full set of bills of lading, packing list, inspection certificate, and any required export registration. Export sales may be settled by LC advised or confirmed through the buyer's bank (commonly HSBC, BNP Paribas, or MUFG) and will credit the RMI account only after U.S. correspondent clearing. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory local file or master file threshold. The Marshall Islands imposes no corporate income tax and accordingly has no domestic transfer pricing documentation rules. Transactions with foreign related parties may be examined by a foreign tax authority, and the Marshall Islands entity should maintain contemporaneous records to support arm's length pricing if used in a foreign group's filing. Recommendation: prepare TP documentation when group revenue exceeds USD 10,000,000 to satisfy foreign parent reporting.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (as amended) apply to corporations carrying on 'relevant activities': banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, holding company, intellectual property, distribution and service centres. Manufacturing of portable phased-array medical diagnostic scanners (ISIC 3250) is not a relevant activity. However, if the company holds intellectual property assets or acts as a pure equity holding entity, it must satisfy the economic substance test by maintaining management and control, staff, and physical presence in the Marshall Islands, and file an annual economic substance notification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / false declaration of foreign-source income

Statutory sanction: Under the Marshall Islands Criminal Code and the Business Corporations Act: fines up to USD 25,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years, plus civil penalties equal to understated amounts if applied by a foreign jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise false filings may be held personally liable for restitution and subject to travel bans and custodial sentences; the corporate veil may be pierced for fraud.
Shell company failure (economic substance non-compliance)

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of USD 10,000 for the initial non-compliance year and USD 100,000 for each subsequent year; the Registrar may also require striking-off or dissolution of the company.

Director personal liability: Directors of a non-compliant entity may be fined USD 5,000 individually and disqualified from acting as a director in the Marshall Islands for up to 5 years.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Marshall Islands Proceeds of Crime Act: fines up to USD 200,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years; assets traceable to the offence are subject to confiscation.

Director personal liability: Directors who are involved in or wilfully blind to money laundering face criminal liability, personal financial restitution orders, and surrender/revocation of passports (travel bans).
Failure to maintain beneficial ownership information

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine of USD 5,000 per offence; continued non-compliance can result in criminal penalties up to USD 20,000 and 2 years' imprisonment for the signatory.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for the administrative fine unless they can prove reliance on a licensed agent; aggravated instances lead to personal liability for criminal penalties.
