Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

No statutory TIN or VAT ID is issued to non-resident Business Corporations. A foreign parent forms the BC under the Business Corporation Act (BCA), receives a corporate registration number from the Registrar of Corporations, and may file the BCA Section 51 application for a certificate of exemption from local taxation for all income derived outside RMI. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Available election forms

The only relevant election is the BCA Section 51 tax-exemption certificate for non-resident corporations, granting elective exemption from all RMI taxation; alternatively, a corporation carrying on RMI business may elect to hold a Business License and pay the fixed or gross-receipts Business License Tax instead of remaining exempt.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — the RMI levies no VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax. Indirect-type charges are limited to customs import duties on physical goods entering the RMI and the local Business License Tax, which applies only to persons carrying on business within the RMI.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists. The Business License Tax is triggered by the existence of RMI business activities rather than a turnover threshold; non-resident offshore voice-streaming revenues are outside the regime. Local currency: USD — applicable threshold: N/A.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — there is no VAT in the RMI, so no export VAT zero-rating is available or required. Export of digital services is instead tax-neutral under the territorial rule: a non-resident Business Corporation performing no RMI-source activity is exempt from all RMI taxation under BCA Section 51, regardless of customer location or place of consumption.

  • Customs Export Declaration (RMI Customs Service) — relevant only if tangible goods were physically exported from RMI territory.

Consequences include revocation of the exemption certificate, back-assessment of any Business License Tax found due for RMI-source activities, monetary penalties under the Business License Act, corporate dissolution or penalties for non-filed annual reports, and criminal liability for fraud under the RMI Criminal Code. Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility, given the RMI's official currency is the USD. Both current and capital account transactions are free from exchange controls. There are no administrative barriers to profit remittance, dividend distribution, or capital repatriation for non-resident or foreign-owned Business Corporations. The absence of a domestic central bank and exchange control law means no formal approval is required for inward or outward transfers. The only applicable constraints are anti-money laundering (AML) reporting requirements and correspondent bank compliance policies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory timeline exists for the surrender of export proceeds. RMI law imposes no obligation on exporters to convert, repatriate, or remit export receipts to local bank accounts. All export proceeds may be retained offshore or in any currency, as the USD is already the settlement vehicle. Mandatory conversion ratios are zero.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists; the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) has adopted the United States dollar as its sole legal tender. Reserve management and monetary policy are de facto delegated to the U.S. Federal Reserve. The banking and financial services sector is regulated by the Registrar of Corporations and the Banking Commissioner within the Ministry of Finance, acting under the Banking Act 1988 and the Business Corporations Act 1990.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. RMI does not have a fintech licensing regime or electronic money institution (EMI) framework. Offshore digital EMIs are not recognized as authorized deposit-taking institutions and cannot obtain direct access to RMI domestic payment infrastructure. A non-resident Marshall Islands Business Corporation may maintain an account with a foreign-licensed EMI (e.g., an EU or UK EMI), but such an account is not a substitute for a domestic bank account and may trigger AML concerns due to lack of RMI regulatory oversight. The enhanced due diligence burden on downstream correspondent banks makes this route operationally fragile for a voice streaming app.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions from shareholders or parent companies are routed through the U.S. Federal Reserve System via correspondent bank wires. The recommended protocol is a SWIFT MT103 wire from the investing entity's bank to the RMI corporate account at Bank of the Marshall Islands, using the corporate account number and BOMI's U.S. correspondent bank routing number. Because the USD is the RMI domestic currency, no foreign exchange conversion occurs. The purpose code should accurately reflect the transaction as 'contribution to capital of a legally incorporated entity' or 'additional paid-in capital' — the standard code used by U.S. correspondent banks is 'C005' (Capital transfer) under IMF Balance of Payments classification. The shareholder must submit a board resolution authorizing the injection, the subscription agreement or share allotment certificate, and a source-of-wealth statement to satisfy the financial institution's AML checks.Outbound trade and supplier settlements are executed in USD through the clearing infrastructure of the U.S. Federal Reserve. For payments exceeding the bank's local clearing threshold or to non-U.S. beneficiaries, bank remittances use the SWIFT MT103 network. For commercial letter of credit (LC) transactions, the RMI business corporation may use an issuing bank's SWIFT MT700 (LC issuance) followed by MT103/MT202 transfers for reimbursement. If the beneficiary holds a USD account at a U.S. bank, a domestic ACH/wire via the correspondent bank is also permissible. There is no RMI domestic clearing house, hence all cross-border disbursements are processed through international correspondent banks subject to OFAC sanctions screening. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Marshall Islands statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies to non-resident tax-exempt Business Corporations. As an OECD-compliant best practice, Local File and Master File should be prepared when the entity's OECD-reportable group turnover exceeds USD 5 million, and the entity must not create an RMI permanent establishment.

Economic substance scope: The RMI Economic Substance Regulations require only entities conducting geographically mobile 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing, leasing, headquarters, distribution or service centers, and intellectual property holding) to demonstrate adequate substance in RMI. A voice-streaming social app monetized through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing does not constitute a relevant activity if it does not hold exploitable intellectual property for the purpose of earning royalty income; however, if the BC books royalty or licence income from the app's copyrighted technology, IP-related economic substance rules may be triggered. Best practice: maintain local board minutes, accounting records, and present substantive management presence in a DTA-compliant jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Facilitation of Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Criminal Code and Tax Act, substantial monetary penalties up to USD 500,000 for each offense and up to 10 years imprisonment for responsible persons; also criminal forfeiture of the digital platform revenues.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly sign false declarations or direct the misreporting of the corporation's controlled foreign income are personally liable for civil restitution and are subject to extradition to the shareholder's tax jurisdiction under a mutual legal assistance treaty request.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Economic Substance Regulations and Business Corporations Act, the Registrar may impose a penalty of USD 50,000, strike the company off the register, and order the disclosure of beneficial owners; repeated non-compliance triggers criminal liability.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally disqualified from acting as a director or corporate officer in RMI for up to 10 years, and may incur civil damages for misrepresentation to third-party creditors and app-platform partners.
Money Laundering / AML Breach via Digital Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Violations of the RMI Money Laundering Prevention Act carry fines up to USD 100,000 and 20 years imprisonment; the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze corporate bank accounts and seize digital payout receivable balances.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are jointly and severally liable for money laundering penalties, travel bans are routinely imposed until account restitution is made, and non-resident directors are subject to arrest under regional RMI-US extradition arrangements.
Failure to File Annual Report / Franchise Tax

Statutory sanction: An administrative dissolution order, loss of limited liability protection, recovery of all unpaid fees plus penalties, and inability to enforce app-store contracts.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue to transact business after dissolution become personally and jointly liable for all debts and obligations incurred post-dissolution, including app-payable refunds and supplier claims.
