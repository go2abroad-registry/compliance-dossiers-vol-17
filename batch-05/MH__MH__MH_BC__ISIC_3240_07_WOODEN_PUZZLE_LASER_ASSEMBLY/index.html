Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI); official government portal: https://rmi.gov.mh (no dedicated tax e-filing portal; filings are accepted in paper form at the Majuro office)

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation (BC) incorporated under the Business Corporations Act has no obligation to obtain a domestic income tax TIN because it derives no income from RMI sources and is expressly exempt from RMI income tax. If the BC nevertheless requires an identification number for banking or customs purposes, it must obtain a certificate of good standing from the Registrar of Corporations. If, in a non-default scenario, the BC engages in any business activity within the geographical limits of the RMI, it must register with the Revenue and Taxation Division on Form R&T-1 within 30 days of commencement, file a Business License application, and obtain a TIN. Foreign entities with no RMI physical presence or RMI-source income complete only the corporate registration; no income tax registration is triggered.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The RMI has no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election system. Under Section 51 of the Business Corporations Act, a BC designated as 'non-resident' is wholly exempt from RMI income tax on all income derived outside the RMI. A BC that carries on business in the RMI may elect or be classified as a 'resident' corporation, becoming subject to the Marshalls Income Tax Act only on RMI-source income; no further elective classifications, tax-transparent pass-through regimes, or hybrid instrument elections are available under RMI law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax; indirect taxation limited to customs import duty and excise under the Customs Act 2000 and applicable tariff regulations

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - the RMI levies no VAT, GST, or general sales tax, and there is no turnover-based indirect tax registration threshold. A customs registration (ASYCUDA World importer/exporter code) is required solely for the operational clearance of goods at the border, with no minimum economic threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the strict VAT sense, as the RMI does not impose a value-added tax. All goods exported from the RMI are outside the indirect tax net: customs duty is chargeable on importation only, and no export duty is assessed. A non-resident Business Corporation deriving income exclusively from offshore sales of precision CO2 laser-cut wooden puzzles is likewise outside the RMI income tax net. To establish zero RMI tax exposure, the BC should evidence that (i) goods are shipped to an address outside the RMI, (ii) title transfers abroad, and (iii) no RMI-source services are rendered.

  • Customs Export Declaration processed through ASYCUDA World
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from the RMI
  • Commercial Invoice and detailed packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank receipt of export proceeds (where required to evidence offshore receipt; RMI generally imposes no exchange controls)
  • Certificate of Origin (only if claiming preferential tariff treatment under a trade agreement)

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate exists. The RMI Customs Service utilises ASYCUDA World for electronic processing of export and import declarations; commercial invoices are accepted in PDF/paper form and do not require tax authority pre-approval. There is no government API clearance mandate applicable to private-issued invoices, and no continuous transaction control (CTC) regime.

There is no VAT or indirect tax refund mechanism to claw back, because no such tax is levied. However, false export declarations, undervaluation, misdescription of goods, or the diversion of goods to the domestic market are prosecutable under Part XI of the Customs Act 2000 and the RMI Criminal Code. Penalties include fines up to USD 500,000 per offence, imprisonment, forfeiture of goods and conveyances, and retrospective reassessment of any import duties circumvented. If a BC mischaracterises RMI-source gross revenue as offshore export income to escape the Marshalls Income Tax Act, the Revenue and Taxation Division may assert tax on the challenged amount plus a penalty of up to 50% of the underpaid tax and statutory interest, determined on audit under the audit powers of the Income Tax Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility with zero statutory exchange-control restrictions. USD legal-tender status eliminates domestic convertibility risk; there are no RMI exchange-control regulations, no capital-repatriation ceilings, and no withholding tax on dividends or profit remittances payable to non-resident shareholders. Dividend distributions require only a valid board resolution, updated share register, and confirmation from the RMI Treasury of the entity's non-resident tax-exempt status.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI imposes no statutory timeline for surrender of export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no requirement to repatriate or hold foreign-currency earnings onshore. USD export receipts for the laser-assembled wooden puzzle products clear directly through the domestic banking system without conversion or regulatory approval.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) maintains no domestic central bank; the United States Dollar is the sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association. Banking and money-services regulation is administered by the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, pursuant to the Financial Institutions Act 2015 (as amended), with AML/CFT oversight shared by the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit. The de facto monetary authority for USD issuance and monetary policy is the United States Federal Reserve System.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — the RMI has no domestic transfer-pricing statute, no Local File/Master File thresholds, no country-by-country reporting notification for a non-resident BC, and no USD-denominated documentation triggers. Counterparty tax administrations (China, US, EU) may independently impose TP documentation and proof-of-substance obligations on transactions with this entity.

Economic substance scope: The RMI does not operate BVI/Cayman-style Economic Substance Regulations for a standard non-resident BC. However, the RMI participates in the OECD Inclusive Framework and the EU fair-taxation screening, under which a zero-tax entity must demonstrate genuine economic substance in its claimed jurisdiction of management. For this structure, retained substance markers should include: a physical management office in the operating jurisdiction, employee and payroll records, board minutes authorizing the Amazon Pay and Stripe accounts, an executed laser-cutting and assembly subcontract with the Chinese factory, and an independent commercial decision trail. A bare shell with no substance anywhere invites IRS re-characterization (US trade or business via FBA inventory; Form 1120-F exposure) or Chinese SAT place-of-effective-management assertions (25% CIT and 10% dividend WHT).

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — deliberate under-declaration of RMI-source income

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 1989, willful attempted evasion of tax is a felony punishable by a fine of up to USD 10,000, imprisonment of up to 2 years, or both, together with interest and civil penalties on the understated tax; the Registrar may additionally revoke the company's good-standing certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are subject to criminal prosecution, imprisonment, and criminal fines; the corporate veil is pierced for the fraudulent filings, exposing personal assets to the RMI tax authority, the Registrar, and civil restitution claims.
Shell Company / extraterritorial economic substance failure

Statutory sanction: US exposure: the IRS may assert that the RMI entity is engaged in a US trade or business through FBA inventory, taxing effectively connected income at 21% via Form 1120-F, imposing the 30% branch profits tax, accuracy penalties under IRC §6662 (20%-40%), and a 75% civil fraud penalty. China exposure: place-of-effective-management re-characterization exposes the Chinese manufacturing profit to 25% CIT and 10% dividend withholding on distributions.

Director personal liability: Directors controlling funds may face the US trust-fund recovery penalty (IRC §6672) equal to 100% of unpaid withheld amounts; travel bans and extradition may be sought in tax-fraud proceedings; directors face personal civil restitution in US state and federal actions.
AML / beneficial-ownership concealment

Statutory sanction: RMI AML/CFT supervision through the Financial Intelligence Unit authorizes administrative fines, deregistration of the company, and referral for criminal prosecution; payment processors (Stripe, Amazon Pay) and correspondent banks may freeze balances, terminate accounts, and file SARs, triggering global de-risking.

Director personal liability: Officers face criminal prosecution for money-laundering offenses, asset freezing and confiscation orders, imprisonment, and permanent exclusion from international banking; directors may be held personally liable for civil restitution of laundered proceeds.
Annual report default / administrative dissolution

Statutory sanction: The Registrar strikes off and dissolves the company; it loses legal capacity to sue or defend, the Amazon seller account and Stripe balance are frozen, and the corporate bank account is blocked; restoration requires court proceedings plus payment of all arrears, surcharges, and reinstatement fees.

Director personal liability: Directors incurring obligations after dissolution are jointly and severally liable for the company's debts; post-dissolution contracts bind the directors personally, and the corporate veil cannot be invoked to shield them from creditor claims.
