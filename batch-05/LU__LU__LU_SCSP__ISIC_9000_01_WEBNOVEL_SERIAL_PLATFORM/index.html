Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR) / Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS) – MyLBR e-filing portal; Guichet.lu for related formalities.

Governing statute

Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended, notably Articles 101-1 to 101-8 concerning the Société en Commandite Spéciale (SCSp), inserted by the Law of 12 July 2013; amended Law of 19 December 2002 on the Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS); RESA publication rules.

Formation / constitutional filings

Déclaration de constitution / RCS incorporation statement filed electronically via MyLBR, followed by publication in the Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations (RESA).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 27 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxation; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; registration and filings via MyGuichet.lu (https://myguichet.lu) and the eTVA portal.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the SCSp by notarial deed and register with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) via the Luxembourg Business Registers portal (eJustice). 2) Obtain the 13-digit RCS number (matricule) which serves as the company TIN. 3) If a foreign entity established outside Luxembourg, register for VAT with the AEDT by filing Form 1 (or Form 645 for non-established businesses) before commencing taxable activities; a VAT number (LU + 8 digits) is issued. 4) For non-EU entities, appoint a local VAT agent in Luxembourg. 5) Where direct tax obligations arise (e.g., due to a permanent establishment), file a separate ACD TIN application. Registration must occur within 15 days before the first transaction; no minimum capital requirement for SCSp.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — 1) Incorporate the SCSp by notarial deed and register with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (RCS) via the Luxembourg Business Registers portal (eJustice). 2) Obtain the 13-digit RCS number (matricule) which serves as the company TIN. 3) If a foreign entity established outside Luxembourg, register for VAT with the AEDT by filing Form 1 (or Form 645 for non-established businesses) before commencing taxable activities; a VAT number (LU + 8 digits) is issued. 4) For non-EU entities, appoint a local VAT agent in Luxembourg. 5) Where direct tax obligations arise (e.g., due to a permanent establishment), file a separate ACD TIN application. Registration must occur within 15 days before the first transaction; no minimum capital requirement for SCSp.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

SCSp is statutorily tax-transparent; no domestic check-the-box election exists. Luxembourg's ATAD anti-hybrid rules may override treatment where mismatches arise. A foreign classification election (e.g., US LLC check-the-box) has no direct domestic effect; the partnership remains transparent unless an actual entity-level tax is borne abroad.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

16%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual turnover (excluding VAT) for the small business exemption; for Luxembourg-established companies registration is mandatory from the first taxable supply unless the exemption applies. Non-established digital suppliers must register under the OSS when intra-EU B2C distance sales exceed EUR 10,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported to non-EU destinations qualify for 0% VAT where transport is initiated/terminated in Luxembourg and is evidenced. Electronically supplied services (webnovel subscriptions) sold to non-EU business recipients are outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT; sales to non-EU consumers are also outside Luxembourg VAT if the effective use/consumption occurs outside Luxembourg. Intra-EU B2B digital exports may be taxable at the customer's VAT number with no Luxembourg VAT (supply under reverse charge).

  • Customs export declaration (only for physical taxation of tangible goods; not applicable to pure digital serial platform)
  • Bill of lading / airway bill (for physical incidental exports)
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank FX receipt
  • Subscriber registration log with IP geolocation and billing address proving non-Luxembourg destination

No national certification/clearance of e-invoices in Luxembourg; B2B-bilateral e-invoicing is permitted. B2G e-invoicing is mandatory via PEPPOL. VAT returns and listings (e.g., Form 5, VIES) must be filed electronically through the eTVA application on MyGuichet.lu. Digital archiving of invoices is required for 10 years.

Retrospective audit period of 6 years (general statute of limitations) from the year the VAT return was filed. Where export zero-rating is improperly claimed, AEDT will additionally assess VAT at 16% on the previously zero-rated amount, plus late interest (0.6% per annum, capitalised), and an administrative fine of 10% if the VAT is not reported or is understated; serious fraud attracts criminal penalties, including up to 5 years' imprisonment and/or a fine under Article 895 of the General Tax Code.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a fully liberalised euro-area jurisdiction under Article 63 TFEU. The euro is freely convertible for current-account and capital-account operations. There is no exchange-control approval requirement, no restriction on inward or outward payment flows, and no impediment to profit or partnership distribution remittance by an SCSp to non-resident partners. The SCSp, as a transparent partnership, may receive and disburse EUR or foreign currency without central-bank consent, subject only to BCL statistical reporting obligations arising from Regulation (EU) 2015/534 and to CSSF AML/KYC requirements imposed on the banking counterparty.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio. Export and digital-service receipts, including App Store/Google Play payouts and subscription royalties, may be retained in EUR or any foreign currency, freely reinvested, or converted at commercial market rates without requirement to sell foreign currency to the BCL or the domestic banking system.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), together with the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for banking and AML market-conduct supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for EU/EEA-regulated Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) passported into Luxembourg under PSD2 or licensed by the CSSF; Low for offshore non-EEA digital EMIs. Legal constraints: a non-bank EMI cannot accept deposits or provide full treasury services; it can only safeguard e-money funds. For this WebNovel platform, an EU-licensed EMI could support operational collection of subscription and app-store payouts, but a Luxembourg commercial bank remains the appropriate clearing and escrow counterparty. Offshore EMIs without a Luxembourg passport are generally not viable because they lack the local establishment and supervisory co-operation required by CSSF AML rules.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For euro-denominated partner contributions within the EU/EEA, use a SEPA Credit Transfer with an ISO 20022 pain.001 payment instruction and, where requested by the bank, purpose code CAPI (capital payments). For non-EUR or non-EEA injections, use a SWIFT MT103 with full remitter details, the SCSp account as beneficiary, and clear remittance information stating: capital contribution, SCSp legal name, and underlying transaction reference. Luxembourg does not require a statutory purpose code, but the bank will require an internal source-of-funds declaration, partnership deed excerpts, UBO confirmation, CRS/FATCA classification as a Passive Non-Financial Entity or equivalent, and sanctions/embargo screening. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% (Luxembourg City composite corporate income tax rate: 17% CIT + 7% solidarity surcharge + 6.75% municipal business tax; municipal rate varies by commune up to 10.5%)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory Luxembourg withholding tax on dividends paid by a Luxembourg resident company; however, a transparent SCSp does not pay dividends, and profit allocations to partners are not subject to Luxembourg dividend WHT. If the SCSp is recharacterised as taxable, distributions become constructive dividends at 15%, reducible to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive or an applicable DTA where the partner holds at least 10% for 12 months.

For a fiscal year ending on 31 December, the return must be filed by 31 May of the following calendar year; for a non-calendar fiscal year, within five months after the accounting year-end. The Luxembourg tax administration may grant extensions until 31 December of the relevant year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The transparency annex must be filed by 31 May following the fiscal year-end or within five months of the accounting year-end, even if the SCSp itself has no taxable corporate result.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR: Local File is mandatory when annual related-party transactions in aggregate exceed EUR 500,000 or individual service transactions exceed EUR 100,000. Master File is required for groups with consolidated net revenue of EUR 50,000,000 or more; CbC Report is triggered when the consolidated group revenue reaches EUR 750,000,000. All intercompany transactions with the webnovel platform, including IP royalties, subscription platform services, app-store fee sharing, and marketing recharge, must be benchmarked on arm's length terms.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg has not enacted a standalone Economic Substance Regulations regime in the Gulf-Cooperation-Council sense, but the General Tax Act business-purpose doctrine, EU ATAD, and treaty anti-abuse rules functionally require economic substance. An SCSp carrying on a webnovel subscription platform must demonstrate local management, a Luxembourg bank account, a substantive corporate general partner, enforceable commercial contracts, office or server presence if relevant, and real decision-making. Absent substance, the tax administration may disregard the entity, deny treaty benefits, and attribute the platform income to the true controlling enterprise.
