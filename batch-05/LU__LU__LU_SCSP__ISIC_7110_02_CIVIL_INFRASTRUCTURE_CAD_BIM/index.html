Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration des contributions directes (Luxembourg Inland Revenue) for direct tax and taxpayer matricule matters; Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (Luxembourg Registration Duties, Estates and VAT Authority) for VAT and indirect tax matters. Official digital portal: guichet.lu and MyGuichet.lu.

E-government filing portal

For a Luxembourg SCSp: (1) conclude the partnership deed and register with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register through a notarised or private deed via MyGuichet; (2) obtain the RCS registration number and the corporate matricule; (3) apply to the Administration des contributions directes for the national taxpayer identification number where a separate matricule is required; (4) submit the VAT registration application to the Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA before commencing taxable supplies using the official AEDT registration form; (5) if the entity is a foreign supplier not established in Luxembourg, register directly for a Luxembourg VAT number and appoint an accredited VAT fiscal representative where the supplier has no EU or EEA establishment and no OSS eligibility; (6) retain supporting evidence of the foreign taxable person status, intended taxable activities, expected turnover and place-of-supply rationale.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — For a Luxembourg SCSp: (1) conclude the partnership deed and register with the Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register through a notarised or private deed via MyGuichet; (2) obtain the RCS registration number and the corporate matricule; (3) apply to the Administration des contributions directes for the national taxpayer identification number where a separate matricule is required; (4) submit the VAT registration application to the Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA before commencing taxable supplies using the official AEDT registration form; (5) if the entity is a foreign supplier not established in Luxembourg, register directly for a Luxembourg VAT number and appoint an accredited VAT fiscal representative where the supplier has no EU or EEA establishment and no OSS eligibility; (6) retain supporting evidence of the foreign taxable person status, intended taxable activities, expected turnover and place-of-supply rationale.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Luxembourg does not have a US-style check-the-box election. An SCSp is fiscally transparent by default for corporate income tax, municipal business tax and net wealth tax purposes. Under Article 175bis LIR, a Luxembourg partnership may validly elect, subject to applicable conditions, to be treated as a fully taxable Luxembourg resident corporation for income tax purposes; the election is binding for at least five taxable periods unless the Luxembourg tax administration accepts earlier termination. Hybrid mismatch and reverse-hybrid rules under Article 168ter LIR are mandatory and cannot be elected away; they apply to recharacterise a transparent SCSp only to the extent necessary to prevent double non-taxation, double deduction or deduction-without-inclusion outcomes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

17%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 35,000 annual taxable turnover for small businesses established in Luxembourg; non-established foreign suppliers have no domestic registration threshold where taxable supplies are made in Luxembourg, unless the supply falls within the EU One-Stop-Shop or Import One-Stop-Shop regimes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported to non-EU destinations are zero-rated if the exporter retains proof that the goods physically left the EU. Engineering, CAD, BIM and structural drafting services supplied to a non-EU B2B customer are generally zero-rated or outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT because the place of supply is where the customer is established. The SCSp must retain sufficient documentary evidence of the customer's non-EU status, the contractual service description, and, where relevant, the actual export movement or foreign recipient's establishment details.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

Luxembourg does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-based clearance, or digital stamping for B2B VAT invoices as of the current regime. Electronic invoicing is accepted subject to authenticity, integrity and readability requirements; PEPPOL BIS 3.0 is accepted in the public procurement environment. VAT returns, recapitulative statements, Annex VIII filings and OSS returns must be filed electronically through MyGuichet or the Luxembourg VAT authority portal; no national mandatory e-invoice clearance repository applies.

The AEDT may recharacterise zero-rated or outside-the-scope supplies as taxable at 17% Luxembourg VAT where the export or non-EU customer evidence is missing, false or insufficient. Associated input VAT is subject to partial or full clawback, unpaid VAT is collected with late-payment interest of 0.6% per month, and administrative fines may range from 10% to 200% of the tax due depending on culpability. Fraudulent zero-rating or VAT evasion may additionally trigger criminal penalties, including imprisonment and fines up to EUR 50,000 or more under Luxembourg fiscal criminal law. The standard assessment limitation period is five years, extended to ten years in cases of fraud or intentional concealment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a full Eurozone member; the euro is completely convertible for current and capital account transactions. No exchange control approval, conversion quota, or beneficiary notification requirement applies to cross-border profit remittances or capital flows, subject only to EU and Luxembourg sanctions regulations and CSSF AML/CTF obligations. Partnership distributions from an SCSp are generally exempt from Luxembourg withholding tax; however, fees, interest, or royalties paid to non-residents may trigger domestic withholding at 15% or a treaty-reduced rate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement to surrender, convert, or repatriate export proceeds within a fixed timeline. Invoices for civil-infrastructure CAD/BIM services may be settled in any currency and retained in foreign-currency accounts. Statistical reporting applies to the BCL Balance of Payments survey for entities meeting turnover and cross-border transaction thresholds; no mandatory conversion ratio exists.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL); banking prudential and conduct supervision delegated to the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) under the Law of 5 April 1993 on the financial sector, as amended.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium for EEA-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) under PSD2 via passporting; a full Luxembourg banking auxiliary is not required. Non-EEA offshore EMIs are not acceptable for local IBAN or TARGET2 settlement and present legal uncertainty for corporate treasury. Wise Business can be used for operational disbursements but should be backed by a CSSF-supervised credit institution for capital escalation and high-value settlements.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Pass-through SCSp with multiple non-resident limited partners obscuring UBO identification

Root cause: Pass-through SCSp with multiple non-resident limited partners obscuring UBO identification

Defense evidence: Organizational chart, partnership deed, any registered-office agreement, notarized UBO declarations per the Law of 13 January 2019, passports and proof of residential address for every partner (or the general partner for dormant limited partners), plus a CSSF-compliant source-of-wealth questionnaire.
Capital escrow contribution exceeding EUR 25,000 without transparent source of funds

Root cause: Capital escrow contribution exceeding EUR 25,000 without transparent source of funds

Defense evidence: Twelve-month bank statements of the contributing partner, signed capital contribution agreement, audited financial statements or corporate tax returns of the funder, and a documented source-of-funds declaration showing commercial viability of the injection.
Cross-border trade disbursements for BIM/CAD services lacking underlying invoice and contract evidence

Root cause: Cross-border trade disbursements for BIM/CAD services lacking underlying invoice and contract evidence

Defense evidence: Master services agreement, itemized invoices, project scoping documents, IP assignment or non-disclosure undertakings, and annual financial statements of the contracting counterparty; also an EU sanctions check against all parties.
Low operational substance in Luxembourg (no employees, no physical office)

Root cause: Low operational substance in Luxembourg (no employees, no physical office)

Defense evidence: Registered office agreement with a Luxembourg service provider, an economic substance file, engagement letter of a local accountant or tax advisor, minutes of partner decisions held in Luxembourg, and evidence of local ultra-vires management mandates.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All injections must be routed via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for bank-to-bank) using an appropriate purpose code such as 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' under the EU Balance of Payments classification. The beneficiary must be the SCSp's account name exactly as registered. For transfers within the EEA, SEPA Instant Credit Transfer is permissible for amounts below EUR 100,000; larger sums are cleared through TARGET2. Each contribution must be booked in the SCSp's statutory ledger with a corresponding partner capital record.Trade settlements are executed through SWIFT MT103 with trade-related purpose codes, e.g., 'Engineering and professional services'. For larger infrastructure procurements, commercial documentary credits (MT700) or standby letters of credit (MT760) may be opened through a Luxembourg credit institution; these are cleared against presentation of conforming documents, usually through the beneficiary's confirming bank. Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: No compulsory statutory audit applies to an SCSp in Luxembourg because it lacks separate legal personality under the Law of 10 August 1915 on commercial companies. The mandatory audit regime of the Law of 19 December 2002 (applicable where two of three size criteria are exceeded: balance-sheet total over EUR 4.4 million, net turnover over EUR 8.8 million, or average headcount over 50) targets legal persons only. An audit becomes compulsory if (i) the limited partnership agreement expressly mandates a réviseur d'entreprises agréé, (ii) the SCSp is regulated by the CSSF as an investment fund or SIF, or (iii) the SCSp is a consolidated undertaking of an EU group subject to group audit thresholds. For an unregulated civil-infrastructure CAD/BIM outsourcing vehicle, statutory audit is elective.
