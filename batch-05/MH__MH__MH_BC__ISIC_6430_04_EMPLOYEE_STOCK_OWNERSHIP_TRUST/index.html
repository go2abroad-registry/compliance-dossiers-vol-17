Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (Majuro); corporate registration and statutory identifier issuance are administered by the Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General, through licensed registered agents via the electronic incorporation portal.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Business Corporation with the Registrar of Corporations; the issued Certificate of Incorporation bears the RMI company number, which functions as the sole statutory taxpayer identifier for a non-resident entity. Step 2: File the Initial Statement of Compliance and appoint a Marshall Islands registered agent. Step 3: No separate TIN or VAT certificate is issued for a non-resident Business Corporation that derives no gross revenue from sources within the Marshall Islands. Step 4: If an RMI-source gross revenue nexus arises (physical commercial activity or RMI-source receipts), file the Gross Revenue Tax registration form with the Revenue and Taxation Division and obtain a GRT number. Step 5: For ESOP trust administration conducted purely offshore, no sectoral license is required, and no Foreign Investment Business License applies to restricted commercial activities (offshore trust and equity plan administration is outside the restricted schedule).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (non-resident Business Corporation exempt from Marshall Islands Gross Revenue Tax and from the statutory income tax on all income derived outside the RMI; the exemption is conditioned on absence of RMI-source gross revenue, absence of permanent establishment or physical commercial activity in the RMI, and payment of the annual confirmation fee).

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Marshall Islands tax law. Entity classification is determined exclusively by the organic document selected at formation: Business Corporation (non-resident exempt status de facto elected by articles of incorporation), LLC (flexible internal classification comparable to United States pass-through elections), or Trust (non-resident trust election available under the Marshall Islands Trusts Act). Conversion between forms (e.g., BC to LLC) is possible by restated articles, but no treasury-form 'tax classification election' exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax; the only indirect levies are customs import duty on physical goods, excise-type fuel taxes, and a $20 international departure green tax).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - no VAT/GST turnover threshold exists because no VAT/GST regime is in force. A Gross Revenue Tax registration obligation arises for any person carrying on business within the RMI deriving RMI-source gross revenue, without any de minimis exemption; a non-resident BC administering an offshore ESOP trust has zero RMI-source gross revenue and therefore no registration nexus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - with no VAT/GST regime, no export zero-rating or refund mechanism exists. Cross-border ESOP administration services supplied by a non-resident Business Corporation are outside the Marshall Islands tax base altogether; no tax is charged on issue and no refund substantiation is required. Exemption from RMI taxation is achieved and preserved by maintaining no RMI-source gross revenue and no physical commercial activity in the RMI (offshore conduct standard under the Gross Revenue Tax Act and the Income Tax Ordinance); no advance ruling is available or required.

  • Not applicable to pure cross-border services: RMI Customs Export Declaration is required only when physical goods leave RMI territory
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (required only for consigned physical exports; not applicable to ESOP trust services)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (not mandated by RMI tax law; banks may issue one on request for the trustee's internal audit file and plan-level recordkeeping)

No e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping mandate exists under Marshall Islands law. Tax administration is return-based: GRT returns are filed on paper or PDF with the Revenue and Taxation Division, and customs import declarations are processed manually at the point of entry by the RMI Customs Service. Invoices issued by a non-resident BC for ESOP administration services are not subject to any government clearance, validation, or digital fiscalization requirement.

No export zero-rating fraud or VAT clawback regime exists because the Marshall Islands has no VAT. Scrutiny is confined to Gross Revenue Tax and customs law: fraudulent under-declaration of RMI-source gross revenue, or a false assertion of non-resident status, attracts late-filing and underpayment penalties imposed under Title 37 of the Marshall Islands Revised Code, monthly interest on deficiencies, enhanced penalties of up to 50% of the understated tax for fraudulent returns, and criminal fines plus officer-level liability for willful evasion. Exempt status may be retroactively revoked for up to six years (assessment limitation period) if the Revenue and Taxation Division establishes that the BC in fact carried on business within the RMI; the Registrar may also administratively strike the company for non-payment of the annual confirmation fee, terminating the exemption from the date of striking. The 180-day figure represents a standard contractual and ESOP-plan foreign-exchange remittance expectation for Trust distributions rather than a statutory RMI repatriation deadline.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The US Dollar is the domestic currency, conferring de facto full current-account and capital-account convertibility. The RMI imposes no exchange controls, no prior approval requirements for inbound or outbound capital movements, no profit-remittance restrictions, and no withholding on outbound distributions for a non-resident Business Corporation, provided the Financial Institutions Act 2011 and applicable RMI AML/CFT obligations are satisfied.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The RMI prescribes no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no statutory repatriation timeline, and no mandatory conversion ratio for foreign-currency receipts; all US Dollar receipts are treated as domestic-currency revenue with unrestricted use.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Financial Institutions Division, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). The RMI maintains no independent central bank; monetary anchoring is provided by the US Federal Reserve System because the US Dollar is the sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. The RMI does not license non-bank electronic money institutions domestically; an offshore FCA- or EU-licensed EMI with US Dollar IBANs is legally accessible for an RMI Business Corporation so long as no deposit-taking occurs inside RMI territory. Correspondent-bank risk engines, however, may automatically flag an RMI-incorporated trust vehicle, making substance documentation and a US-dollar correspondent clearing relationship essential for sustained usability.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) levies no corporate income tax on any corporation; the non-resident Business Corporation (BC) operating entirely outside the RMI has zero RMI income tax exposure under the Business Corporations Act (BCA) Section 176 exemption.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the RMI imposes no withholding tax on dividends declared by a non-resident BC and no secondary distribution tax. The ESOP trust may distribute cash dividends or shares to employee beneficiaries with zero RMI withholding.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Due on or before June 30 immediately following the reporting calendar year (statutory language: 'before July 1 in each year'). In practice, the report for the prior calendar year must be filed in the first half of the current year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None under RMI law — the RMI has no transfer pricing statute, no Local File, no Master File, and no CbCR filing obligation expressed in USD. However, the parent/plan-sponsor jurisdiction's OECD thresholds govern group documentation: e.g., US CbCR threshold USD 850 million consolidated group revenue; EU/UK threshold EUR 750 million. The ESOP trust must supply arm's-length documentation to the sponsor's tax authority upon request even though no RMI filing threshold applies.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply only to entities carrying on nine 'relevant activities': banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, holding company, intellectual property business, and distribution/service centres. ESOP trust administration (ISIC 6430, NAICS 525920) is not a listed relevant activity; a nil ESR declaration is made in the Annual Report. If the ESOP trust is characterized as a pure equity holding company, a reduced substance test applies (comply with BCA filings, maintain registered agent, and demonstrate adequate governance records). The RMI is a member of the OECD Global Forum and subject to EU listing monitoring; therefore the trust must never hold RMI-situs operating assets or claim ESR exemption without records proving strategic decision-making and board oversight.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Cross-Border Tax Evasion (Beneficiary Jurisdiction)

Statutory sanction: In the beneficiary's residence jurisdiction: assessment of all unpaid tax, interest, and a civil fraud penalty of 50%–75% of the understated tax; criminal tax evasion conviction exposes the beneficiary to additional fines up to USD 100,000 (US: 26 U.S.C. § 7201) and imprisonment up to five years.

Director personal liability: Directors or trustees who knowingly facilitate undisclosed distributions are liable as aiders and abettors: civil restitution of unpaid taxes, personal financial penalties equal to the evaded amount, passport revocation and travel bans, cross-border asset freezes, and extradition exposure under bilateral mutual legal assistance treaties.
Sham Trust / Shell Company Recharacterization

Statutory sanction: Tax authorities may disregard the RMI trust and recharacterize all trust income as directly earned by the plan sponsor or beneficiaries; the resulting assessment adds a 100% accuracy-related penalty on the understated tax plus interest accruing from the original due date.

Director personal liability: Veil piercing: directors held jointly and severally liable for the corporation's liabilities, including unpaid taxes and employee claims; UK/Commonwealth-style disqualification orders (10–15 years) barring directorship; personal liability for debts incurred after the entity is struck off, and criminal sanctions for fraudulent trading.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI AML/CFT framework (which regulates registered agents as gatekeepers): monetary penalties up to USD 50,000 per violation, suspension or revocation of the registered agent's license, and mandatory referral to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit; the offending entity may be struck from the register.

Director personal liability: Individual directors face fines up to USD 25,000 and imprisonment up to two years; travel bans and asset-freeze orders may be imposed pending FIU investigation; directors may be permanently barred from serving as officers of any RMI entity, and the trust's assets may be subject to confiscation proceedings.
Annual Franchise Tax Default and Administrative Dissolution

Statutory sanction: Administrative dissolution by the RMI Registrar; the trust ceases to exist as a legal person; all contracts, bank accounts, and ESOP share registrations are frozen; restoration requires payment of all franchise tax arrears, USD 100 + USD 25/month late penalties, and a reinstatement fee.

Director personal liability: After strike-off, directors become trustees of the trust's assets and owe fiduciary duties to employee beneficiaries; any debt contracted in the corporate name after dissolution attaches to directors personally; directors may be prohibited from incorporating any new RMI entity for up to five years; beneficiary lawsuits for breach of fiduciary duty may result in civil restitution and unlimited personal liability.
