Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed by the Business Corporations Act. Share subscriptions may be satisfied through cash, services, or promissory notes credited to paid-in surplus. However, before bonded warehouse operations may commence, the Customs authority shall require a security bond of not less than USD 50,000 or 30% of projected annual excise duty, whichever is higher. No escrow account is mandated.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate issued after inspection under NFPA 1 Life Safety Code including automatic sprinkler coverage, ventilated cellar storage, and compliance with flammable liquids chapter 66; structural clearance by the Ministry of Public Works confirming loading capacity of the racking system and concrete slab; occupation health clearance under the Workplace Health and Safety Act confirming alcohol vapor monitoring, spill kit placement, emergency eyewash, fire wardens, and daily work environment inspection records.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance (Majuro); corporate registration and licensing coordinated through the Registrar of Corporations and the RMI Business Licenses portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity incorporating as a Business Corporation under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act files incorporation documents with the Registrar of Corporations, obtains a certificate of incorporation, and then registers for a Business License with the Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Division. The Business License number serves as the de facto tax identification number; no separate income-tax TIN is issued because the Marshall Islands does not impose income, capital gains, or withholding taxes on non-resident corporations.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign non-resident entity incorporating as a Business Corporation under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act files incorporation documents with the Registrar of Corporations, obtains a certificate of incorporation, and then registers for a Business License with the Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Division. The Business License number serves as the de facto tax identification number; no separate income-tax TIN is issued because the Marshall Islands does not impose income, capital gains, or withholding taxes on non-resident corporations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, fiduciary-transparent, or hybrid-mismatch tax elections exist under RMI law. A non-resident Business Corporation is statutorily exempt from Marshall Islands income taxation with respect to all income derived outside the Marshall Islands and all transactions conducted outside the Republic; there is no domestic revenue election because the direct tax base is nil.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax system. The Marshall Islands levies customs and excise duties on imported goods under the Customs and Excise Act; excise duties apply to imported wine and spirits, but there is no general consumption tax applicable to domestic turnover.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no VAT/GST registration threshold because no VAT/GST system exists; customs-duty liability attaches to physical importation rather than economic turnover, and bonded-warehouse licenses are granted under the Customs and Excise Act rather than through tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Because RMI imposes no VAT/GST, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim and no VAT refund mechanism. Exports and re-exports from bonded warehouses are outside the customs duty base, and import duty is ordinarily suspended while goods remain in bond and extinguished upon lawful exportation.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

None. The Marshall Islands does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, electronic digital stamping, or API-based e-invoicing. Commercial invoices and contract documents are used for customs declaration and audit purposes, and bonded-warehouse movements are recorded in customs-approved stock records.

Not applicable in the VAT sense. However, false or fraudulent customs declarations, undervaluation, or misdescription of bonded goods intended for export may trigger penalties under the Customs and Excise Act, including monetary fines, forfeiture of goods, revocation of the bonded-warehouse license, and retrospective reassessment requiring payment of all suspended duties and excise taxes.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility applies because the U.S. dollar is the domestic currency and there are no exchange controls, surrender requirements, or restrictions on foreign currency accounts. Profit remittance may be executed freely in USD via correspondent banking; no central bank approval or tax clearance certificate is statutorily required for dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders under the Business Corporations Act.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory export proceeds surrender. There is no statutory timeline requiring repatriation or conversion of export receipts. Bonded trade proceeds may be retained in domestic or foreign accounts, subject only to bank-level AML documentation for SWIFT wires and underlying trade invoices.

Central bank supervision: No domestic central bank. The Republic of the Marshall Islands is dollarized, using the United States dollar as legal tender; monetary policy and currency issuance are effectively exercised by the United States Federal Reserve. Banking supervision is vested in the Marshall Islands Banking Commissioner, operating within the Ministry of Finance, which licenses and regulates depository institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Offshore fintech electronic money institutions (EMIs) are not recognized as depository institutions under the Marshall Islands Banking Act and cannot provide banking services to licensed bonded trade entities. The use of non-licensed digital EMI accounts for operational treasury is exposed to regulatory rejection, restricted access to commercial credit, and elevated AML-banking correspondence risk. Only licensed depository banks with physical or agent presence in Majuro may lawfully operate current accounts for duty-suspension bonded wholesalers.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from non-resident shareholders are routed via SWIFT MT103 using the U.S. dollar clearing system (Fedwire or CHIPS). The payment field must contain the corporate registration number and indicate the transaction purpose code as 'Cash inflow into a non-resident business corporation.' For Marshall Islands bank-account reporting, the transfer should be annotated as 'equity contribution under Business Corporations Act.' No exchange-control filing is required. Funds must be sourced from a bank account in the shareholder's name, and the bank may request the share subscription agreement and board resolution authorizing the issuance of shares.Trade disbursements for bonded Scotch and Bordeaux wine settlements use (1) SWIFT MT103 direct wires for payment against invoice, (2) commercial letters of credit under UCP600 with a confirming bank for large aged-cellar purchases, and (3) compliant sight drafts under documentary collections for recurring bonded transfers. Each disbursement requires the underlying invoice, transport document, and customs bond entry. For bonded warehouse-to-warehouse transfers, payment may be made via MT103 with the reference 'customs bonded transfer' and the destination warehouse permit number.
