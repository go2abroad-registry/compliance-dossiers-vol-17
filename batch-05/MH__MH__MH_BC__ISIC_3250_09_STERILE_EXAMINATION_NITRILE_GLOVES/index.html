Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands Ministry of Finance – Revenue and Taxation Division, Majuro; no separately branded national online tax portal is generally available to foreign entities.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident Business Corporation is not required to obtain a VAT ID because the RMI has no VAT system. If it carries on any business within the Republic, it must file a gross revenue tax registration application with the Revenue and Taxation Division, attaching the certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing, particulars of the registered agent, and the RMI business license; a Tax Identification Number is then issued. A non-resident exempt Business Corporation operates solely outside the RMI and typically does not obtain a local TIN, but must maintain its registration number with the Registrar of Corporations.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign non-resident Business Corporation is not required to obtain a VAT ID because the RMI has no VAT system. If it carries on any business within the Republic, it must file a gross revenue tax registration application with the Revenue and Taxation Division, attaching the certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing, particulars of the registered agent, and the RMI business license; a Tax Identification Number is then issued. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

RMI law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. A non-resident Business Corporation maintains its exempt status by ensuring it does not carry on business within the Republic; there is no election to be treated as a pass-through entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax (RMI levies customs import duties and a 3% gross revenue tax on business carried on within the Republic)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD – no VAT/GST registration threshold exists; a person carrying on a business in the Republic must register for gross revenue tax from the first dollar of local source gross revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT zero-rating mechanism exists in the RMI. Exports of sterile examination nitrile gloves are outside the scope of any consumption tax; therefore no zero-rating claim may be made. For gross revenue tax purposes, export revenue derived by a non-resident Business Corporation from customers outside the Republic is non-RMI source income and is not subject to gross revenue tax, provided the entity does not carry on business within the RMI.

  • RMI Customs Export Declaration or equivalent export entry document
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing dispatch from the port of export
  • Inward remittance certificate or commercial bank SWIFT documentation evidencing receipt of foreign currency export proceeds

No e-invoicing, continuous transaction controls, fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping mandate is in force. Import and export declarations are processed through the RMI Customs and Trade Administration, using customs declaration forms and available national electronic customs platforms.

The RMI has no export VAT zero-rating refunds to claw back. False declarations or fraudulent misrepresentations in customs and gross revenue matters may result in forfeiture of goods, reassessment of duties and taxes, civil penalties up to 50% of the underpaid amount, statutory interest, and criminal fines. A non-resident entity that misrepresents its exempt status can lose tax-exempt treatment and become liable for gross revenue tax retrospectively.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Total currency convertibility, both de jure and de facto. Since no domestic currency exists, all payments are denominated in U.S. dollars and neither current-account nor capital-account transactions are subject to RMI exchange-control approval, conversion quotas, profit-remittance taxes, or repatriation licensing. Dividends, management fees, and capital reductions may be remitted freely once board approval and solvency conditions under the Business Corporation Act are satisfied. The only practical hurdles to profit remittance are commercial: correspondent-bank de-risking in the Pacific corridor, FATF-oriented documentation, and the receiving bank's own compliance policies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. RMI law imposes no statutory deadline, no mandatory conversion percentage, and no surrender or repatriation duty for export receipts. Export proceeds may be held in USD onshore or offshore indefinitely without filing any foreign-exchange declaration with the Marshall Islands Government.

Central bank supervision: Not applicable. The Republic of the Marshall Islands maintains no central bank and no foreign-exchange administration. The U.S. dollar is sole legal tender, so monetary authority is effectively exercised by the U.S. Federal Reserve; prudential licensing and supervision of banks are vested in the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, pursuant to the Banking Act 1990 (as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. The RMI has no dedicated e-money-institution or payment-services licensing regime; an offshore digital EMI holding or disbursing treasury funds is not a recognized 'bank' under the Banking Act 1990 and may create shell-institution/unauthorized-deposit-taking concerns for RMI-facing compliance. The route is workable only as an ancillary, non-primary disbursement channel, and only where the EMI independently holds a valid license in its home jurisdiction (e.g., UK FCA, Singapore MAS, HKMA) and the full UBO/trade-documentation bundle is available.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% corporate income tax rate for non-resident Marshall Islands Business Corporations not engaged in trade or business in the Republic of the Marshall Islands. A 3% Gross Revenue Tax applies only if the corporation derives RMI-source gross revenue through an actual RMI business nexus.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Business Corporation to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual Report is due on or before the anniversary of the date of incorporation in each calendar year. The deadline is not aligned to a fiscal year-end for income tax purposes because no corporate income tax is imposed on a non-resident BC.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 0 — no statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold or Local File/Master File regime is codified in RMI law. A non-resident BC engaged in a multinational nitrile-glove supply chain should nevertheless maintain transactional documents and an economic rationale to avoid adverse recharacterization by the shareholder's home tax authority.

Economic substance scope: RMI Economic Substance Regulations apply to RMI-tax-resident entities carrying on relevant activities, including or covering distribution and services, financing/leasing, headquarters, holding companies, and IP holding. For this non-resident Medical PPE business, RMI substance is not required if the entity is tax-resident outside RMI and files the necessary foreign tax-residency declaration; however, if the entity is treated as RMI-resident or performs any relevant activity in RMI, it must maintain physical employees, premises, and adequate RMI operating expenditure in proportion to income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Immediate reassessment of the 3% Gross Revenue Tax on all unreported RMI-source receipts; late payment penalties and interest; substantial civil fraud penalties; criminal fines and imprisonment for wilful false filing and concealment; revocation of registration.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations or knowingly divert RMI-source receipts outside the BC can be assessed personally for restitution, penalties, and interest; courts will pierce the corporate veil; travel bans and surrender of passports may be imposed in criminal tax proceedings.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Breach

Statutory sanction: Registrar administrative dissolution, financial penalties under the Economic Substances Regulations, revocation of the Certificate of Good Standing, adverse exchange-of-information referral to the home tax authority, and possible treatment of the BC as tax-resident in the customer's jurisdiction with assessment under controlled-foreign-company rules.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for unlawful distributions and false declarations; the corporate veil may be pierced when the shell entity is merely an instrument of fraud, exposing directors to civil restitution, personal bankruptcy, entry bans, and imprisonment.
AML / Sanctions Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Referral to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit; criminal anti-money laundering penalties including imprisonment and corporate fines; confiscation and freezing orders over PPE inventory, bank accounts, and letters-of-credit proceeds; revocation of business registration; exclusion from correspondent banking.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who authorize or fail to identify suspicious transactions face personal criminal liability, fines, imprisonment, travel bans, asset seizure, and disqualification from serving as directors of any RMI entity.
Fraudulent Trade / Veil Piercing by Creditors

Statutory sanction: Civil restitution of misappropriated assets, compensatory and punitive damages, court-ordered freezing of bank accounts and LC proceeds, administrative liquidation, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent trading.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced; directors and controlling shareholders are jointly and severally liable for the BC's debts, with personal exposure to asset attachment, travel bans, seizure of passports, disqualification orders, and imprisonment for intentional fraud.
