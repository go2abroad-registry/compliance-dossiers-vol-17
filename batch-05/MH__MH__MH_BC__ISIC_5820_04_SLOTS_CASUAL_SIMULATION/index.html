Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Bureau (RTB) — official national tax authority; tax administration portal accessible via https://rtb.mh (foreign investor guidance issued by the Ministry of Finance and the Registrar of Corporations).

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Business Corporations incorporated under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act are not automatically registered for income tax because their offshore-derived income is exempt. A foreign entity seeking a statutory TIN obtains it only when it generates Marshall Islands domestic-source income or elects to be treated as a domestic taxpayer. Procedure: file Form RTB-TIN-1 with the Revenue and Taxation Bureau at the Office of the Registrar, attach the Certificate of Good Standing from the Registrar of Corporations, provide a copy of the Articles of Incorporation, a notarized statement of non-resident status, and a Declaration of No Local Source Income; the RTB issues a TIN within 5 business days for administrative identification purposes but with a tax exemption certificate under the Income Tax Act (Chapter 20) if the entity confirms it will derive no Marshall Islands source income. For export-focused gaming software publishers, no active TIN is required by default; the non-resident corporation may operate solely on the basis of a Clean Exemption Certificate.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The Business Corporations Act (BCA) permits a foreign-owned corporation to elect in its Articles or by board resolution to be classified as a Non-Resident Business Corporation (NRBC), which is automatically outside the scope of Marshall Islands income tax. There exists no check-the-box classification equivalent to the U.S. or a hybrid mismatch election under Marshall Islands law, but the entity may further elect under Section 84 of the BCA to restrict its activities to offshore commercial operations, thereby rendering all income, profits, and transactions entirely tax-neutral. Where the gaming company holds an election to remain a domestic taxable corporation, it may later revoke that election to revert to non-resident status; this is not a hybrid mismatch instrument but a statutory exemption election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / No GST / No General Sales and Use Tax — Marshall Islands levies no broad-based indirect tax at the national level

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no indirect tax registration threshold exists because no indirect tax is imposed; the de facto threshold is zero and no fiscal registration for indirect tax purposes is triggered by economic turnover in the Marshall Islands

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in a VAT/GST context: the Marshall Islands imposes no indirect tax and therefore provides no VAT zero-rating mechanism for exports or offshore services. For direct tax purposes, a Non-Resident Business Corporation deriving income from publishing and licensing social casino software to foreign consumers is wholly outside the Marshall Islands income tax net. The concept of '0% export rating' is replaced by the statutory exemption of foreign-source income under the Income Tax Act, Section 4(1)(a) — provided that the contract for sale or license of the software is executed outside the Marshall Islands, payment is received through a foreign banking channel, and no office or permanent establishment in the Marshall Islands is maintained.

  • Board Resolution declaring all income to be foreign-source and not arising from the Republic of the Marshall Islands
  • Digital software delivery manifest or electronic export record (where applicable, no physical goods enter customs)
  • Foreign bank account statement showing SWIFT/ACH inward remittance from the overseas counterparty
  • Signed software license agreement or service agreement evidencing offshore place of performance
  • Certificate of Exemption issued by the Revenue and Taxation Bureau (RTB Form EX-105)

None — the Marshall Islands does not mandate e-invoicing, fiscal clearance APIs, or digital stamping; record-keeping requirements are confined to general corporate books under the Business Corporations Act (minimum 5 years of storage, no digital fiscal integration requirement)

Marshall Islands tax law imposes no explicit VAT export zero-rating clawback because no VAT exists. However, where an exempt non-resident entity mischaracterizes domestic-source income (e.g., revenue from a Marshall Islands resident customer or from a PE in Majuro) as foreign-source income, the Revenue and Taxation Bureau may retroactively assess income tax at the standard rate of 3% for corporations (or 11% under the alternative minimum regime if applicable), add a 25% civil fraud penalty, and calculate statutory interest at 1% per month from the original due date. In flagrant cases, the Registrar may petition the High Court to revoke the Non-Resident status, resulting in full retroactive taxation of all accumulated undistributed profits. The RTB also retains criminal enforcement powers under the Income Tax Act (Amendment) 2021 for deliberately falsified exemption certificates, with fines up to $50,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years for responsible officers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The officially adopted currency is the U.S. dollar (USD). There are no foreign exchange controls in the RMI. Current and capital account transactions are fully convertible; profit remittances, dividend distributions, and liquidation proceeds may be freely repatriated without exchange control approval, subject only to normal banking due diligence, AML verification, and OFAC sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists for the surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds. Businesses may retain receipts in USD or convert to other currencies freely. The RMI imposes no exchange control regime, no centralized reporting of current account receipts, and no repatriation timeline beyond standard commercial practices.

Central bank supervision: Office of the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI); no domestic central bank exists, and the U.S. Federal Reserve serves as the de facto monetary authority under the Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is low. The RMI has no fintech or electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime. Offshore digital EMIs cannot directly provide local account facilities; they may only offer virtual IBAN backing via partner banks, but settlement ultimately requires a U.S. correspondent clearing relationship. Legal constraints arise from the absence of a central bank, limited banking sector infrastructure, and FATF-styled AML supervision.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — non-resident Business Corporations are exempt from all Marshall Islands income tax; the 3% Gross Revenue Tax applies only to revenue derived from business conducted within the Republic and must be avoided by a compliant offshore BC.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the RMI imposes no withholding tax, no dividend tax, and no undistributed-profits tax on distributions by a BC to non-resident shareholders; social casino profits may be repatriated gross without RMI formalities.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — the RMI has no OECD-aligned transfer pricing statute, no Local File or Master File obligation, and no related-party threshold denominated in local currency (USD). Nonetheless, contemporaneous OECD TP documentation is recommended where the BC licenses gaming software or charges service fees to related parties in CFC jurisdictions and cross-border intercompany flows exceed USD 3,000,000 per annum, because foreign tax authorities will apply arm's-length tests to the group's economics.

Economic substance scope: No domestic Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) are codified in the RMI; the BCA exemption is available without a local substance test. De facto substance requirements arise extraterritorially: the OECD BEPS Inclusive Framework reviews the RMI's no-or-nominal-tax regime, EU and UK anti-avoidance directives impose CFC rules, and US Subpart F/GILTI attributes income to US shareholders of the RMI BC. A letterbox operator must demonstrate real operational substance — game development staff, IP ownership, procurement and user-acquisition decision-making — in its actual management jurisdiction to prevent recharacterization by foreign courts.
