E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate under the Business Corporations Act 1990 by filing Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations (Office of the Associate Registrar) and appointing a registered agent; obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and corporate registration number. (2) Submit a Business License application to the Business License Office under the Business License Act 2006 for activity ISIC 3240, pay the annual license fee, and obtain the Business License number, which functions as the principal regulatory identifier. (4) If physical goods are exported from Marshall Islands territory, register with the Marshall Islands Customs Service and file export declarations under Title 21 of the Marshall Islands Revised Code.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMI has no domestic non-convertible currency; the USD is fully convertible and freely transferable. Both current account and capital account transactions are unrestricted. Profit remittance, capital repatriation, dividend distribution, and shareholder loan repayments are not subject to exchange control approval or withholding by the RMI. The only practical hurdles are bank-level OFAC/AML screening, sanctions filter review, and the requirement to document the economic substance and beneficial ownership of the non-resident Business Corporation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rules exist. There is no mandated timeline for repatriation, no compulsory conversion ratio, no central pool requirement, and no requirement to convert export receipts into any local currency. Export proceeds denominated in USD may be retained in overseas correspondent accounts, held in the RMI bank account, or reinvested without filing with the Banking Commissioner.

Central bank supervision: Not applicable - the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) has no central bank. The competent banking, AML, and foreign exchange supervisory authority is the Office of the Banking Commissioner within the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of the Marshall Islands. The United States dollar is the sole legal tender, with the U.S. Federal Reserve serving as the de facto currency issuer.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for ancillary collection and disbursement channels; Low as a primary treasury or settlement account. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business and Airwallex are not licensed under the RMI Banking Act 2011 and do not provide Marshall Islands-based deposit accounts, domestic ACH access, or local account numbers. They may be used to collect e-commerce receivables from Stripe/Shopify Payments and to make selected vendor payouts, but all statutory capital injections and substantive trade settlements should be cleared through an RMI-licensed commercial bank and its USD correspondent network. Correspondent banks may apply enhanced scrutiny to EMIs if the EMI is used as the sole treasury route.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for wholly foreign-source business income; the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or undistributed profits tax. The Income Tax Act (54 MIRC) purports to tax only gross income derived from sources within the RMI; an NRDC engaged in offshore cosplay apparel manufacturing has no RMI-source income and therefore no tax liability.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the RMI does not impose withholding tax on dividends (or any other distribution), and no approval, clearance, or stamp duty is required before a non-resident shareholder receives a dividend.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None - the RMI has not enacted transfer pricing, Local File, Master File, or country-by-country reporting legislation, and no monetary threshold exists in RMI law (USD is the official currency). Multinational groups must nonetheless apply the parent jurisdiction's TP rules (e.g., US IRC 482/6662(e), OECD TP Guidelines) and should keep contemporaneous arm's length documentation for the NRDC's intercompany sales, royalty, and cost-sharing arrangements.

Economic substance scope: The ESR 2019 applies to every 'relevant entity' (including NRDCs) carrying on 'relevant activities'. Pure offshore manufacturing of cosplay apparel/wigs (ISIC 3240) is not a relevant activity, but a company that also (i) distributes goods from inventories, (ii) operates as a service center, (iii) provides financing/leasing to affiliates, or (iv) owns and licenses IP rights in its costume designs must demonstrate adequate employees, premises, expenditure, and CIGA management in the RMI, or prove tax residence in a qualifying foreign jurisdiction. Annual ESR self-assessment returns are mandatory even where no relevant activity is identified.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Registry Default / Unauthorized Trading After Strike-Off

Statutory sanction: 10% statutory surcharge on the unpaid fee; dissolution by proclamation after three months of arrears; contracts of the dissolved company are void; the Registrar's Certificate of Dissolution freezes the merchant accounts and banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders transacting in the name of a dissolved company are jointly and severally liable for all obligations so incurred; the High Court may order civil restitution, asset tracing, and freezing orders against directors who continue operations.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Evasion

Statutory sanction: ESR 2019 penalties: up to USD 10,000 for failing the economic substance test; up to USD 20,000 for false or misleading information; the Registrar may strike off the company, revoke the NRDC exemption, and exchange the corporate records with foreign tax authorities under the TIEA.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from serving on any RMI company; if the structure is used to mislead foreign revenue authorities, directors face civil restitution, travel bans, and extradition on tax-fraud charges at the request of treaty partners.
AML-CFT / Money Laundering and Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the RMI AML/CFT Act 2022 and the Crimes Act 2012; penalties include substantial criminal fines, asset-freezing orders by the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit, confiscation of the corporate bank balance, and forced deregistration; card-acquirer termination notices are customary.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers knowingly benefiting from tainted receipts are subject to criminal prosecution, civil forfeiture of personal assets, imprisonment (including custodial sentences prescribed by the RMI Criminal Code), travel bans, and extradition to the requesting state.
Tax Evasion Through Foreign Misreporting

Statutory sanction: The RMI imposes no domestic income tax, so the evasion charge is prosecuted in the market jurisdiction (e.g., US IRC 7201/7206, UK Criminal Finances Act, EU DAC-6 penalties); the RMI TIEA commitment authorizes exchange of corporate records, and the Registrar may void the structure as contrary to public policy.

Director personal liability: Directors who design or sign the offending arrangements may be pursued as enablers and liable for disgorgement of profits, foreign criminal fines and imprisonment, permanent director disqualification, and enforcement of foreign restitution orders through the RMI High Court.
