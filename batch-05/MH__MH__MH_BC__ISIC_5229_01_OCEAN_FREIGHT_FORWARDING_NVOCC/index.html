Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance; official government portal: https://www.rmi.gov.mh (no dedicated e-filing portal for non-resident tax-exempt corporations)

E-government filing portal

Non-resident Business Corporations (BCs) are not issued a Marshall Islands TIN or VAT ID. Upon incorporation, the BC obtains a Certificate of Incorporation and a registration number from the Office of the Registrar of Corporations. If the BC engages in any local conduct (e.g., employing residents), it must acquire an employer registration number from the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration. For customs purposes (import/export of physical goods), the entity must register as an importer/exporter with the Marshall Islands Customs Service and obtain a customs client code in the ASYCUDA World system. No VAT ID exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-resident Business Corporations (BCs) are not issued a Marshall Islands TIN or VAT ID. Upon incorporation, the BC obtains a Certificate of Incorporation and a registration number from the Office of the Registrar of Corporations. If the BC engages in any local conduct (e.g., employing residents), it must acquire an employer registration number from the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration. For customs purposes (import/export of physical goods), the entity must register as an importer/exporter with the Marshall Islands Customs Service and obtain a customs client code in the ASYCUDA World system. No VAT ID exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Not applicable in the U.S. check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch sense. Under the Business Corporations Act, a non-resident domestic corporation elects its exempt status by filing a declaration with the Registrar of Corporations confirming it will not carry on business within the Marshall Islands and designating a registered agent. Once that election is maintained through annual filings and government license fees, the corporation is exempt from all Marshall Islands income, withholding, capital gains, and gross-receipts taxation. No hybrid mismatch or pass-through election is available under RMI domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST or general sales and use tax; the RMI levies selective customs duties and excise taxes on certain imported goods (tobacco, alcohol, fuel, vehicles), plus municipal business license fees for on-island activities.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Nil — there is no VAT/GST registration threshold. A non-resident NVOCC with no physical local activity is not required to obtain any indirect tax registration. Municipal business license requirements arise only if a business maintains a place of business or personnel in the Marshall Islands.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — no VAT/GST exists, therefore there is no zero-rating mechanism. Exported goods and offshore services, including NVOCC ocean freight brokerage services rendered outside the Marshall Islands by a non-resident BC, fall entirely outside the RMI indirect tax net. No output tax is charged, and no refund claim is necessary.

None. The Marshall Islands does not mandate e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API connectivity, or digital stamping. Customs declarations are filed electronically through ASYCUDA World for import/export physical cargo clearance, but no tax clearance API or pre-shipment tax stamping regime applies to freight forwarding services or non-resident exporters.

No export zero-rating or clawback regime exists because no indirect tax is imposed on exports. However, fraudulent customs export declarations are subject to the RMI Customs and Excise Act penalties: civil fines, forfeiture and seizure of goods, monetary penalties up to ten times the evaded duty, and criminal sanctions including imprisonment. Additionally, a non-resident corporation that misrepresents its activities to the Registrar to obtain tax-exempt status may lose its exemption and be liable for all unpaid municipal license fees and penalties, without any VAT or export VAT clawback exposure.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility applies because the U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender of the Marshall Islands. There is no foreign exchange control statute, no requirement for exchange control approval, and no restriction on the repatriation of profits, capital returns, or intercompany settlements. Convertibility hurdles arise only from bank-level compliance: OFAC sanctions screening, anti-money laundering due diligence, and confirmation that the underlying transaction does not involve controlled goods or a sanctioned jurisdiction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rule exists in the Marshall Islands. Export and service receipts, including freight forwarding fees, may be retained in U.S. dollars offshore or freely transferred. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0% and there is no statutory timeline for surrender or reinvestment.

Central bank supervision: Ministry of Finance of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (Banking Commissioner) – the RMI maintains no domestic central bank; the U.S. Federal Reserve System acts as the de facto monetary authority for the U.S. dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. The Republic of the Marshall Islands does not administer a stand-alone electronic money institution licensing regime. Offshore digital EMIs are not eligible to take deposits or to offer deposit-taking treasury services to an RMI-incorporated business corporation. A U.S.-licensed money transmitter with correspondent access may be considered for incidental collections, but the primary operating and escrow accounts must sit with an RMI-licensed commercial bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory RMI threshold applies. For global tax-risk management, the recommended benchmark is USD 1,000,000 of aggregate intercompany charges before preparing a local-file-grade transfer pricing package for audits in revenue-hungry jurisdictions.

Economic substance scope: The RMI does not apply EU-style Economic Substance Regulations to non-resident business corporations. However, the NVOCC must retain genuine strategic management and operational substance outside the RMI to avoid adverse tax residency and CFC attacks in the shareholders' home jurisdictions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Gross Revenue Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Restitution of the avoided gross revenue tax, civil penalties up to 100% of the underpaid amount, criminal fines, and disqualification from operating a licensed NVOCC by the RMI Ministry of Finance.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve false filings face personal liability for unpaid taxes, asset freezes, travel bans, and imprisonment under the RMI Criminal Code.
Shell Company / Corporate Veil Piercing

Statutory sanction: Administrative dissolution by the Registrar; foreign courts may disregard the RMI corporation and reattribute freight income and liabilities to the parent or the ultimate beneficial owner.

Director personal liability: Shareholders and directors become personally liable for cargo claims, unpaid shipping charges, customs penalties, and freight-forwarding tariff liabilities once the corporate veil is pierced.
AML / Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: RMI AML/CFT enforcement actions, financial penalties per violation under the applicable Money Laundering Control Act, and international de-risking leading to loss of all correspondent banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Directors may be criminally prosecuted for willful AML/sanctions violations, face personal financial penalties, travel restrictions, and imprisonment, and be disqualified from holding global shipping industry offices.
Unlicensed Ocean Transportation Intermediary Activity

Statutory sanction: U.S. Federal Maritime Commission civil penalties of up to USD 30,000 per violation, per shipment or per day for continuing bond failures, plus CBP liquidated-damages claims against the surety.

Director personal liability: Under U.S. shipping statutes, principals and directors are jointly and severally liable for reparations to shippers and penalties, and the corporate veil is unavailable as a shield.
