Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance, Banking and Postal Services (no public e-filing portal; filings are coordinated through registered agents and the RMI Registrar of Corporations)

E-government filing portal

Non-resident Business Corporations in the Marshall Islands are generally exempt from domestic income tax and are not required to obtain a tax identification number for income tax purposes. If a company creates a domestic taxable nexus through local-source activities or engages RMI employees, it must apply to the Revenue and Taxation Division for a TIN using Form TIN-01, attaching the certificate of incorporation, registered agent confirmation, and beneficial ownership declaration. Because the RMI has no VAT, there is no separate VAT identifier.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Entity classification is fixed by the Associations Law at formation; the RMI does not operate a check-the-box regime or recognize hybrid mismatch elections. Non-resident Business Corporations are automatically tax-exempt unless they carry on trade or business within the Republic of the Marshall Islands, in which case statutory corporate income tax applies by operation of law rather than by election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (No VAT/GST/Retail Sales Tax; customs import duty and excise taxes apply at the border)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Imports are subject to customs duty; an importer must be registered with the RMI Customs Service under its customs business identifier, regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. The Marshall Islands imposes no VAT, so there is no 0% export VAT rate, no input VAT credit, and no VAT refund mechanism. Goods and offshore services supplied by a non-resident Business Corporation are outside the scope of any RMI indirect tax, provided they do not constitute imports into the RMI customs territory.

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime exists in the Marshall Islands. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de jure and de facto convertibility prevails. Because the RMI is fully dollarized, the distinction between capital-account and current-account convertibility is legally moot: there is no national currency, no central-bank reserve constraint, and no statutory exchange-control mechanism requiring approval of cross-border transfers or profit remittances. Dividends, royalties, license fees, and loan-service payments may be remitted offshore without prior licensing or conversion restrictions. Practical frictions are confined to non-statutory factors, principally correspondent-bank de-risking, OFAC screening, FIU threshold reporting, and investor-funds segregation compliance. The entity should therefore maintain all treasury liquidity in USD, hold a valid Migratory or Non-Resident Corporation Certificate of Good Standing, and structure upstream receivables into USD to avoid jurisdiction-level withholding at the payor source.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory obligations: nil. The RMI imposes no surrender, conversion, repatriation-timeline, or concentration-ratio requirements for export proceeds, subscription proceeds, or any other foreign receipts. Foreign-currency account balances may be retained indefinitely with no forced conversion. The only applicable duties are ordinary record-keeping, FIU suspicious-transaction reporting at statutory thresholds, and the general prohibition against structuring transactions to evade reporting. For the crowdfunding portal, all escrow receipts must be attributable to identified investors with documented source-of-funds trails; however, no mandatory conversion timeline applies.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) does not maintain an indigenous central bank and has adopted the United States dollar as its sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association and the RMI Monetary System Act. The sovereign monetary and currency administration function is therefore exercised de facto by the U.S. Federal Reserve System, while domestic banking licensing, prudential oversight, and foreign-exchange-related regulatory conduct are administered by the Marshall Islands Banking Commission, located within the Ministry of Finance, Banking and Postal Services, with AML/CFT surveillance carried out by the Marshall Islands Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU). No separate central-bank exchange-control department exists in the RMI.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium with restrictive legal conditions. The RMI does not issue electronic-money-institution (EMI) licenses; an offshore digital EMI chartered under FCA, EU/EEA, or Canadian MSB laws cannot itself function as the primary escrow vault for a non-resident RMI crowdfunding portal. Such an EMI may serve only as a pass-through settlement mechanism when paired with an RMI-compliant licensed bank or a U.S. state-licensed money transmitter or chartered trust company holding bankruptcy-remote escrow. Legal constraints include: the absence of reciprocal RMI recognition of foreign EMI authorisations; the inability of an offshore EMI to establish a U.S. Federal Reserve master account without a U.S. physical nexus and Employer Identification Number; and the high likelihood that EMIs will decline the Crowdfunding_Portal AML classification or impose low balances, tight daily caps, and strict velocity limits.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Investor subscriptions flow through one of two compliant corridors: (a) domestic ACH collection into a licensed U.S. trust-company escrow account followed by a bank-wire sweep to the RMI entity's BOMI capital escrow account; or (b) direct SWIFT MT103 wires from investor banks in the investor jurisdiction. All instructions must state: beneficiary bank as Bank of Marshall Islands, Majuro, with its U.S. correspondent clearings; purpose code 'IP — Investment Purposes / Equity Subscription'; and a beneficiary sub-account reference containing the portal offering code in field 59 narrative to enable automated reconciliation. The entity must respond to any correspondent request letter with the board resolution, the licence certificate, the escrow agreement, and the investor attestation bundle. Cash, bearer instruments, and third-party funding are prohibited except upon express contractual novation. All funds above USD 10,000 trigger FIU-equivalent threshold reporting on the RMI-compliant basis.Operating disbursements, refunds, regulatory fees, and portfolio-advance settlement run on SWIFT MT103 via the BOMI U.S. correspondent network, or on Fedwire for same-day finality when required. Every outbound debit must carry an OFAC/UN/EU sanctions screening token, the underlying investor or offering reference, and a pre-authorised positive-pay instruction. Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Item 1 - Registered Name Exact corporate name as recorded in the Articles of Incorporation; must not be confusingly similar to a name reserved for a licensed registered agent or a regulated financial institution. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None in the RMI: domestic law imposes no Local File, Master File, or Country-by-Country Reporting filing thresholds (USD or otherwise) on non-resident BCs. An MNE group exceeding the EUR 750 million BEPS consolidated-revenue threshold is reportable only through the RMI-facilitated automatic exchange of information, not through any local filing obligation. Transfer pricing on the portal's intercompany service fees, escrow margins, and technology royalty flows is therefore governed by the operating jurisdiction where the crowdfunding business is tax resident.

Economic substance scope: The RMI has not enacted BVI/Cayman-style Economic Substance Regulations for non-resident BCs. Practical substance standards follow OECD Inclusive Framework expectations: an exempt RMI BC must maintain the statutory non-resident indicia — no RMI business, no RMI property, and no RMI-resident management or board meetings. For a high-risk ISIC 6499 equity crowdfunding business, the operating license, physical staff, board decision-making, escrow governance, KYC/AML controls, and audit trail must reside in the regulated market jurisdiction. An RMI BC retaining only a registered agent and no demonstrable activity is presumptively a shell and is the primary target of foreign anti-avoidance recharacterization.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Offshore Abusive Structure

Statutory sanction: Revocation of exempt status, retrospective tax assessment, criminal fines and confiscation orders under RMI financial-crime provisions, referral to the RMI Banking Commissioner and Financial Intelligence Unit, and in the operating jurisdiction tax-fraud penalties of up to 75% of the understated tax with criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the corporate veil for sham nominee structures and impose joint and several liability for taxes, interest, and penalties on each director and controlling shareholder; asset-freeze orders, travel bans, and custodial sentences of up to 10 years apply for fraud and false declaration.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative strike-off by the Registrar for annual-report default, judicial dissolution, and loss of legal personality; foreign tax authorities may disregard the entity entirely and impose entity-level taxation on all portal revenue, or treat the portal as a permanent establishment of the foreign parent.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal exposure for unauthorized securities intermediation, civil restraint orders over personal assets, travel bans pending regulatory inquiry, and foreign director-disqualification orders for operating an unlicensed portal.
AML/CFT Breach and Unlicensed Crowdfunding Activity

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines per violation and revocation of the registered agent's license under the RMI Banking Act and AML/CFT Regulations, criminal conviction for unlicensed securities dealing, mandatory referral to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit, and FATF-linked countermeasures that may terminate correspondent banking access.

Director personal liability: Directors and the designated AML compliance officer are personally and criminally liable for willful breaches; sanctions include imprisonment up to 5 years per offense, extradition under mutual legal assistance treaties, travel bans, personal fines, and permanent disqualification from holding directorships in licensed entities.
False Filings and Fraud on the Registrar

Statutory sanction: Corporation-level penalty of up to USD 5,000 plus daily default fines under the Business Corporations Act; the Registrar may revoke the certificate, refuse reinstatement, void statutory filings, and remove the entity from good-standing registers relied on by financial counterparties and regulators.

Director personal liability: Each director or officer who signed or authorized the false report is jointly and severally liable for the penalty; the RMI High Court may impose imprisonment of up to 2 years for perjury or false declaration and issue travel bans pending full restitution to affected investors or creditors.
