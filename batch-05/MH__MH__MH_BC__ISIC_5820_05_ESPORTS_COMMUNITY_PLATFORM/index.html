Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands Tax and Revenue Office, Ministry of Finance (administrative paper-based process; no public e-filing portal)

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation with no Marshall Islands-source business activity is not required to obtain a tax identification number. Should the entity derive gross revenue from sources within the Republic, it must submit a Business License Application (Form BL-1) to the Tax and Revenue Office along with the Certificate of Incorporation, registered agent details, and the applicable annual business license fee. Upon approval, the Office issues a Business License Number which also serves as the taxpayer identification number. Foreign entities may not self-register online; filings are processed through a licensed registered agent in Majuro.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The U.S. dollar is legal tender and the RMI maintains no domestic currency, making current and capital account transactions freely convertible in law. There are no exchange-control decrees, no revenue repatriation restrictions, and no approval requirement for the remittance of profit, dividends, liquidating proceeds, or invested capital. Remaining frictions are not FX-control related but rather arise from bank-level AML/CFT due diligence, FATCA/CRS reporting, and correspondent-bank expectations; an esports platform can hold, convert, and remit funds globally without prior official approval from the RMI Government.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The RMI imposes no statutory timelines, mandatory conversion ratios, or surrender requirements on export proceeds. Because the esports platform's receipts are non-tangible exports (matchmaking fees, tournament entry fees, sponsorship and in-platform digital goods), all receipts in USD or other convertible currencies may be retained freely, net of service fees paid to payment processors such as Stripe and PayPal, without reference to any RMI central bank or FX-administered quota.

Central bank supervision: No central bank is established in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI); banking and foreign-exchange-license supervision is conducted by the Office of the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, with United States dollar as the sole legal tender and de facto monetary policy anchored to the U.S. Federal Reserve.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Business Corporations Act 1991, Part XIII, Section 14 exempts qualifying non-resident domestic corporations from all income taxes; Income Tax Act 1989 imposes no tax on foreign-source income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the Marshall Islands imposes no withholding tax on dividends, distributions, or liquidation proceeds paid by a domestic business corporation to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing window opens 30 days before the anniversary of incorporation and closes 90 days after the anniversary date; late fees accrue after the close of the window. Recommended filing before the anniversary to avoid penalty.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable in USD — the Marshall Islands has not enacted mandatory Local File or Master File transfer pricing documentation rules. Voluntary OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation is recommended for any RMI entity belonging to a multinational enterprise group with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million, given the RMI's commitment to the OECD Inclusive Framework / Global Minimum Tax (Pillar Two) and the expected enactment of a Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT).

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations comparable to BVI/Cayman ESR; however, OECD Inclusive Framework membership and the Global Minimum Tax framework require genuine economic substance for entities claiming zero-tax status. For an esports platform, the registered agent, local directors, decision-making records, board minutes, and an operating bank account must be demonstrably maintained, and the platform's IP, contracting staff, and tournament operations should not be artificially relocated merely to avoid foreign tax. Banks and payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) will independently apply economic substance and AML due diligence and may terminate the merchant account without warning if substance is not verifiable.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Non-Residence Declaration

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of the full 3% gross revenue tax on all RMI-source revenue; statutory interest at 18% per annum; civil penalty of up to 10% of the tax deficiency; criminal conviction for willful evasion carries a fine of up to USD 10,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are jointly and severally liable for the tax deficiency; RMI courts may pierce the corporate veil upon a finding of alter ego or fraud, ordering civil restitution, asset freezes, and a court-imposed bar on leaving the jurisdiction pending settlement.
Unlicensed Prize Tournaments / Illegal Gambling

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of tournament proceeds; a criminal fine of up to USD 25,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years under the RMI Penal Code gambling provisions; gaming contracts and prize payouts may be declared void and unenforceable.

Director personal liability: All directors engaged in the operation are criminally liable as principals; the corporate veil is disregarded to prevent limited liability from shielding individual operators; restitution to losing participants may be ordered against directors personally.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: At the domestic level, the Registrar may administratively dissolve the corporation for non-filing and impose reinstatement penalties; at the international level, OECD Inclusive Framework rules, if applicable, subject the group to a 15% Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT) as RMI implementing legislation advances; foreign jurisdictions may deny treaty benefits and impose secondary adjustments.

Director personal liability: Foreign courts and tax authorities may recharacterize the RMI entity as a conduit and attribute its income to the ultimate shareholders; directors and beneficial owners face CFC attribution, substantial interest penalties, and inclusion on payment-provider and correspondent-bank blacklists, which terminate the ability of the entity to use Stripe, PayPal, or any global payment rail.
AML / Counter-Financing of Terrorism Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to USD 200,000 per violation; regulatory order to cease payment processing; asset freezing orders; revocation of the corporation's ability to maintain RMI bank accounts; referral to criminal prosecution for laundering the proceeds of crime.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers knowingly engaged in laundering are subject to fines of up to USD 500,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years, confiscation of personal assets, disqualification from holding directorships, and, under the RMI extradition arrangements, travel bans and extradition to foreign prosecuting jurisdictions.
Failure to File Annual Registry Report / Administrative Dissolution

Statutory sanction: Administrative dissolution under Section 110 of the Business Corporations Act; the dissolved corporation loses the capacity to enter contracts, hold bank accounts, process payments, or defend proceedings; reinstatement requires all arrearages, penalties, and reinstatement fees, plus a 10% surcharge on unpaid franchise tax.
