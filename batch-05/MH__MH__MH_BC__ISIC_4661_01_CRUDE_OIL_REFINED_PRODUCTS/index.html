Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Customs Division, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; official portal: RMI Revenue Online Services (e-service for taxpayer registration and customs declaration).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Business Corporation (BC) must first file articles of incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act (BCA), then file a non-resident domestic corporation (NRDC) status election and obtain a Marshall Islands Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) from the Revenue and Customs Division by submitting the incorporation certificate, directors/shareholders register, and declaration of non-resident status.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Business Corporation (BC) must first file articles of incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act (BCA), then file a non-resident domestic corporation (NRDC) status election and obtain a Marshall Islands Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) from the Revenue and Customs Division by submitting the incorporation certificate, directors/shareholders register, and declaration of non-resident status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Election under BCA to become a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC), conditioned on 100% non-resident ownership, no local-source income, and no RMI business activity, resulting in statutory exemption from Marshall Islands income and stamp taxes; no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under RMI law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No domestic VAT or GST; Marshall Islands import duty and fuel tax regime applies to physical importation and licensed fuel supply

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST threshold; any trader physically importing into the Marshall Islands must obtain an importer registration and customs TIN, regardless of turnover

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is not applicable because the Marshall Islands does not impose VAT/GST. Bunker fuel and distilled products sold from non-resident balance-of-plant or ship-to-ship operations are outside RMI tax jurisdiction if title passes outside RMI territorial waters and no importation into RMI occurs.

  • Customs Export Declaration (where physical export from RMI territory occurs)
  • Bill of Lading or Mate's Receipt
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Bunker Delivery Note (BDN)
  • Cargo/quantity survey report and independent inspection certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate exists; customs clearance is conducted through the customs declaration platform on the RMI Revenue Online Services portal, with paper or PDF invoices retained for audit.

Misdeclaration, false export documentation, or fraudulent use of tax-exempt status triggers customs and revenue penalties, including fines up to US$100,000, revocation or suspension of the fuel wholesale license, seizure/detention of vessels or cargo, and potential criminal prosecution; retrospective tax and duty clawbacks may be assessed for up to 6 years.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Unrestricted full convertibility on both current and capital account. The RMI maintains no Foreign Exchange Control Act, no exchange control approval mechanism, and no capital account restrictions. Trade settlements, brokerage commissions, equity injections, shareholder loans, and profit remittances may be freely effected in USD without central bank clearance or licensing. RMI income tax law (30 MIRC) imposes no withholding tax on dividends, interest, or royalties paid by a non-resident Business Corporation to non-resident shareholders, and there are no limitations on the repatriation of invested capital or accumulated trading profits. The only practical control is bank-level AML/CFT due diligence at the account-holding institution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI legislation imposes no surrender requirement, statutory repatriation timeline, or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds or brokerage receivables. Receipts from physical wholesale brokerage of marine fuel oil and distillates may be retained offshore in USD or any freely convertible currency, with no obligation to remit funds into the RMI domestic banking system. The sole requirement is documentary evidence of the underlying transaction (commercial invoice, bill of lading, and brokerage agreement) for the account-holding bank's audit file.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Banking Commission, operating within the Ministry of Finance under the Banking Act 2011 (24 MIRC). The RMI has no statutory central bank; the United States Federal Reserve System functions as the de facto monetary authority because the US Dollar is the sole legal tender under the RMI Constitution and 24 MIRC §101, pursuant to the Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility with binding legal constraints. Foreign-licensed electronic money institutions (e.g., UK FCA, MAS Singapore, or Lithuania) may lawfully provide auxiliary payment services to the MH_BC so long as they do not solicit business from or maintain a place of business in the RMI, which would trigger the licensing requirement of the Banking Act 2011. However, no RMI-licensed EMI exists, and global correspondent-bank de-risking policies materially restrict EMIs from serving physical commodity trading clients. An EMI is therefore viable only as a secondary disbursement rail for specific bunkering hubs (e.g., Singapore or Fujairah), never as the primary treasury platform, and must not hold Capital Escrow Account funds or perform FX conversion against USD for the BC.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity subscriptions and shareholder loans are effected via SWIFT MT103 from the investor's money-center bank (Singapore, Hong Kong, or United States) to the MH_BC's USD operational account at Bank of Marshall Islands (USD correspondent: Bank of Hawaii, Honolulu) or Bank of Guam (U.S. head-office correspondent), settling through Fedwire or CHIPS. The MT103 must reference remittance field 20 (e.g., 'EQY_INJ_MH_BC_001'), field 72/77B purpose code 'GBC' (General Business Current) or 'GDS' (Goods and Services), and the bank's compliance file must hold the board resolution authorizing the injection and the share allotment certificate. No RMI regulatory filing, exchange-control approval, or capital verification certificate exists; only standard source-of-funds verification applies (remitter bank statements, source-of-wealth declaration, and certified constitutional documents). Where the account is designated a Capital Escrow Account, the bank must additionally hold an executed Escrow Agreement with defined disbursement conditions and dual-signature authority for any release of funds.USD clearing is executed exclusively via Fedwire and CHIPS through the RMI bank's U.S. correspondent network. 0% for a qualifying Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC); 2% Business Privilege Tax (BPT) on gross revenue sourced within the Republic of the Marshall Islands (inapplicable to a pure offshore broker transacting no RMI-source business)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions by a Business Corporation to resident or non-resident shareholders; no advance tax, no branch remittance tax, and no secondary withholding on distribution in specie

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No RMI statutory transfer-pricing regime and therefore no Local File or Master File threshold in RMI law. International best-practice triggers: prepare contemporaneous local transfer-pricing documentation where related-party brokerage turnover exceeds USD 10,000,000, and prepare a group Master File and Country-by-Country Report where consolidated group revenue exceeds USD 850,000,000. Functional documentation should support a limited-risk agency profile with a Berry-ratio operating margin of 3% to 10% on commission income

Economic substance scope: The RMI has not enacted BVI-style Economic Substance Regulations, but substance requirements arise indirectly from the Non-Resident Domestic Corporation Act's condition of no domestic activity, the registered-agent AML/KYC regime requiring termination of shell relationships, and OECD/EU review pressure on zero-tax jurisdictions. For a high-risk fuel broker the substantive profile should include: a board that meets with minutes, negotiation and contracting functions exercised at a real business office, a separate bank account with discretionary control by the beneficial owner, payroll records for staff, audited financial statements evidencing trading risk borne, and executed trade confirmations signed by the principal rather than by a nominee

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Entity Recharacterization

Statutory sanction: In the residence jurisdiction: recharacterization of the RMI entity as a sham or conduit, assessment of tax at the owner's marginal rate, accuracy penalties of up to 75% of the understated tax, criminal fines, and imprisonment terms of five to ten years; in the RMI: administrative dissolution, termination by the registered agent, and referral into the CRS automatic-exchange framework with full disclosure of beneficial ownership to the residence country

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where the RMI entity is a mere instrumentality; directors and signatories become personally answerable for the entire tax liability with interest, freezing orders extend to personal assets, travel bans are issued against principals, and nominee directors face criminal exposure for signing false statutory declarations
Shell Company Abuse / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative strike-off and loss of the limited-liability shield; deregulation of the registered agent for deficient enhanced due diligence; inclusion in negative OECD/EU listings triggers automatic de-risking by U.S. correspondent banks, closure of the USD cash-management account, and Suspicious Activity Report filings to FinCEN; reinstatement requires payment of arrears and penalties of up to USD 5,000

Director personal liability: Directors trading after strike-off become personally liable on all contracts by operation of law; the registered agent may claim an indemnity against the directors; foreign courts disregard the dissolved entity and enforce judgments directly against the beneficial owner's assets, with civil restitution and punitive costs awarded
