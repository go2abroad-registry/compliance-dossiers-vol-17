Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Ministry of Finance, Revenue and Taxation Division; corporate registration under the Business Corporations Act is administered by the Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Majuro. No income-tax filing portal exists because the RMI imposes no corporate income tax; limited fee-based filings are made directly with the Registrar.

E-government filing portal

The RMI does not issue TINs or VAT IDs. A foreign-owned non-resident Business Corporation obtains its corporate registration number from the Registrar of Corporations after presenting articles of incorporation and a declaration of non-resident status. Importers subsequently obtain a customs importer/owner code from RMI Customs Service for duty assessment. No direct tax registration is required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone - non-resident Business Corporation is exempt from RMI income, withholding, capital gains, and indirect taxation; its only RMI fiscal exposures are customs import duties on physical goods entering the RMI and annual government registration fees. — The RMI does not issue TINs or VAT IDs. Importers subsequently obtain a customs importer/owner code from RMI Customs Service for duty assessment. No direct tax registration is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - non-resident Business Corporation is exempt from RMI income, withholding, capital gains, and indirect taxation; its only RMI fiscal exposures are customs import duties on physical goods entering the RMI and annual government registration fees.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections are available because the RMI has no income tax or CFC regime. An entity may elect exempt-company status under the Business Corporations Act by filing articles of exemption and paying the annual government fee; this is a corporate-status election, not a tax classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act 2001, the RMI's only material transaction-based revenue instrument.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. All persons importing goods into the RMI are subject to customs duty; no minimum economic-turnover registration trigger applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. The RMI has no VAT/GST regime, so no 0% export VAT rating or refund mechanism exists. Exported designer toys and collectibles leave the customs territory untaxed; export transactions are outside the scope of RMI customs duty and there is no notional input-tax credit to recover.

  • Customs export declaration or outward manifest (where goods physically depart the RMI)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Customs importer/owner code and any applicable export permits
  • Not required: VAT refund claim files or inward remittance certificates (no VAT refund system exists)

No mandate for e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping. Import entries are processed by RMI Customs at the point of clearance with electronic manifest filing supported; exporter and invoice records are not required to be fiscally pre-cleared.

No VAT clawback regime exists. Misdeclaration or smuggling under the RMI Customs Act attracts seizure, forfeiture, fines up to double the duty, and imprisonment; false statements to the Registrar attract registration and penalty provisions under the Business Corporations Act. The US dollar is legal tender and no statutory repatriation deadline applies; the 180-day parameter reflects prudent banking and AML documentation practice for export proceeds.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMI is a fully dollarized jurisdiction with no independent currency and no exchange-control statute. Capital account injections, current account trade settlements, and profit remittances are freely convertible without RMI government approval, withholding, or foreign-investment permit. Residual frictions are confined to U.S. correspondent-bank due diligence and the absence of a domestic interbank FX market, which requires offshore derivative and clearing channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, repatriation, or conversion regime applies in RMI: export receipts from designer toy pre-sales, wholesale invoices, and e-commerce settlements may be retained indefinitely in USD with any RMI-licensed or foreign bank, subject only to the account-holding bank's internal AML/CFT monitoring.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Ministry of Finance - Office of the Banking Commissioner; RMI maintains no standing central bank, and the United States Federal Reserve serves as the de facto monetary authority because the U.S. Dollar is the sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Non-Resident Domestic Business Corporation: no Marshall Islands income tax, gross revenue tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax is levied on offshore business activities conducted entirely outside the Republic of the Marshall Islands.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the Marshall Islands imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a non-resident exempt Business Corporation to its non-resident shareholders; distributions of after-tax offshore profits may be made freely and without RMI statutory withholding.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Annual Report must be filed on or before the anniversary of incorporation in each year; the USD 300 franchise tax for a Business Corporation is remitted with the report to maintain good standing.

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before the last day of the third month following the close of the taxable year (31 March for calendar-year taxpayers). A non-resident tax-exempt Business Corporation has no filing obligation unless it unexpectedly derives RMI-source income.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — the Marshall Islands has not enacted transfer pricing legislation and prescribes no Local File, Master File, or country-by-country reporting threshold. An arm's length standard would be relevant only if the BC unexpectedly derived RMI-source income and became taxable; in that event, documentation would follow OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, but the non-resident exempt BC has no compliance obligation.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply to registered entities that are tax resident in the Marshall Islands and carry on 'relevant activities', which include holding business, intellectual property business, distribution and service center business, and headquarters business. A non-resident BC that is tax resident in another jurisdiction may file a Non-Resident Entity Declaration supported by foreign tax residence evidence to be exempt from the substance test. The designer blind-box toy business (ISIC 3240: rotocasting injection, airbrush finishing, brand IP, and global merchandising) would constitute an intellectual property business if the BC purported to be RMI tax resident; without RMI premises, employees, and core income-generating activities in the RMI, substance cannot be satisfied. The entity must therefore remain non-resident and file the corresponding declaration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — False Non-Resident Exemption

Statutory sanction: Retroactive revocation of the tax-exempt offshore regime, reassessment of RMI corporate income tax and the 2% gross revenue tax on unreported RMI-source revenue, statutory default interest, penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded, criminal fines, and imprisonment under the RMI Income Tax Act 1989 and the RMI Criminal Code.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are personally exposed to civil restitution of the evaded tax, criminal prosecution, travel bans, asset freezes, and forfeiture orders; the corporate veil is pierced for fraud, and shareholders may be joined to the assessment.
Abusive Shell Company / Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations 2019, the RMI Registrar may impose escalating financial penalties, require corrected filings, apply to strike off or dissolve the company, and share information with foreign tax authorities under the TIEA; the tax exemption may be voided retroactively.

Director personal liability: Directors and the licensed registered agent incur personal liability for repeated substance compliance failures; RMI courts may disqualify directors, order restitution of avoided taxes, impose travel bans, and compel dissolution of the shell.
AML/CFT Breach — Concealment and Payment Rail Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act 2010, the entity and its registered agent face financial penalties of up to USD 500,000, suspension or revocation of the agent's license, asset forfeiture, and criminal prosecution for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners face personal fines, imprisonment, travel bans, and clawback of laundered proceeds; the limited liability shield is removed where the BC is used as an instrument of fraud or financial crime.
Fraudulent Dividend / Insolvent Distribution

Statutory sanction: Under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act, unlawful distributions must be restored to the company; the court may order liquidation, clawback of distributions, and voidance of related transactions.

Director personal liability: Directors who assent to a wrongful distribution are jointly and severally liable for the full amount distributed; for fraud the court will pierce the corporate veil, and enforcement may include civil restitution, travel bans, and seizure of personal assets.
Sanctions and Export Control Violations

Statutory sanction: RMI sanctions enforcement, international embargo penalties, customs forfeiture, loss of RMI registry privileges, detention of cargo or vessels, and listing on sanctions databases; significant financial penalties may be imposed by the RMI and by foreign regulators with jurisdiction over the payment rails.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly facilitate sanctioned transactions face criminal prosecution, personal fines, travel bans, and seizure of overseas assets; the corporate form does not shield wilful misconduct or intentional violation of sanctions laws.
