Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Luxembourg Trade and Companies Register (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés, RCS), operated by Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR), 1-3 Boulevard de la Foire, L-1528 Luxembourg.

Governing statute

Luxembourg Law of 10 August 1915 on Commercial Companies, as amended (Loi modifiée du 10 août 1915 concernant les sociétés commerciales), Articles 101-1 to 101-11 (Société en Commandite Spéciale), together with Articles 18 to 22 on partnerships and Article 1 on the general corporate regime.

Formation / constitutional filings

Constitution Deed and Articles of Association (Acte de constitution et statuts) - executed by private deed or before a Luxembourg notary, filed with the RCS, and published in the RESA (Recueil électronique des sociétés et associations).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 26 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The SCSp has no legal minimum capital pursuant to Article 101-4 of the 1915 Law; the deed freely fixes the amount and nature of contributions. Cash contributions do not legally require a blocked escrow account, though a Luxembourg bank confirmation of contribution receipt (attestation de blocage) is customary for third-party and financing purposes.

Funding & unlock path: For a standard Luxembourg office (up to 1,000 m2 and fewer than 50 employees): communal fire safety certificate and CGDIS inspection of the premises; occupational safety clearance from the Inspection du Travail et des Mines (ITM) covering workstation ergonomics, electrical safety, and building access. Where the registered office is provided by a licensed domiciliation agent, the agent's building safety compliance certificate and communal fire certificate at fit-out are sufficient evidence.

Paid-in capital rule: None. The SCSp has no legal minimum capital pursuant to Article 101-4 of the 1915 Law; the deed freely fixes the amount and nature of contributions. Cash contributions do not legally require a blocked escrow account, though a Luxembourg bank confirmation of contribution receipt (attestation de blocage) is customary for third-party and financing purposes.

Not applicable for pure digital webtoon publishing. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a euro-area jurisdiction with full convertibility on both current and capital account transactions by virtue of Articles 63-65 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. There is no exchange-control licensing, prior-authorisation, quota or investment-screening requirement for ordinary commercial or treasury flows of an SCSp. Profit remittances to non-resident partners are legally unrestricted, and distributions of an SCSp are not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax when addressed to corporate or qualifying partnership partners, subject to substance, anti-abuse and beneficial-ownership conditions. Residual hurdles are limited to AML/KYC verification of beneficial owners, CRS/FATCA reporting, and the BCL's statistical balance-of-payments reporting on cross-border transactions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Luxembourg imposes no statutory surrender, conversion, retention or repatriation timeline on export proceeds. Webtoon subscription and licensing revenue received in USD, EUR, KRW or JPY may be freely kept in foreign currency, converted into euro, or used directly for outbound royalty settlements. The only mandatory obligations are the BCL's monthly/quarterly balance-of-payments statistical declarations triggered by cross-border payment activity above the de-minimis reporting threshold (generally EUR 12.5 million aggregate annual turnover per resident reporting entity) and standard tax/CRS disclosures; no mandatory conversion ratio exists.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL) in conjunction with the Luxembourg Ministry of Finance (Direction de la Trésorerie) and the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF) for payment, foreign-exchange intermediation and financial-market supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low for primary treasury use. EEA-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and Payment Institutions passportable into Luxembourg under PSD2 (e.g., Adyen, Stripe, Payoneer, CurrencyCloud) can legitimately execute SEPA, SEPA Instant and cross-border collections for Apple IAP and Google Play Billing settlement, as well as automated payouts to webtoon creators and translators. However, an EMI cannot provide a depositary, credit, capital-escrow or investment-custody relationship; its safeguarding obligations require client funds to be segregated in a shell account held with a Luxembourg/EEA credit institution or central bank, effectively making the domestic bank the ultimate custodian of the SCSp's cash pool. Offshore EMIs headquartered outside the EEA (UK FCA-authorised, US FinCEN MSB, Singapore MAS-licensed) do not enjoy EU passporting rights and require local CSSF/BCL authorisation or engagement via a white-label Luxembourg partner; therefore they must be confined to payment-processor functionality and never treated as treasury counterparties.

Defense evidence: Fund-flow narrative; 12 months of underlying bank statements; tax residence certificates; notarised corporate registry extracts or trust deeds; source-of-wealth report; CV of the UBO; signed declaration of the general partner accepting liability for the accuracy of the dossier; Luxembourg substance records (lease agreement, payroll, board minutes).

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partner capital is wired via SWIFT MT103 (or SEPA Transfer within EUR) to the SCSp's dedicated capital-contribution escrow account at the Luxembourg bank. The remittance must carry the SCSp's LEI, the partnership act date, and the BCL balance-of-payments statistical purpose code falling under 'direct investment' / 'equity capital' in the BCL nomenclature; the unformatted remittance field must explicitly state 'contribution to partnership capital - SCSp - no consideration'. The bank additionally requires the subscribed capital schedule of the partnership agreement and, before funds can be released to the operating account, a compliance clearance confirming the inbound funds are unrelated to third-party receivables; where the capital injection is a treasury transfer from an affiliated company, an intercompany loan or capital-contribution resolution and CRS/FATCA self-certifications of the transferring company must accompany the instruction.For syndication royalties, creator advances and licensing fees, the primary clearing rail is the SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer (or ISO 20022 pain.001 within SEPA), with BCL balance-of-payments category 'royalties and licence fees' entered in the purpose code field and structured creditor reference (RF/SCOR) identifying the generating contract. For high-value physical comic-book distribution to bookstores and wholesalers, an irrevocable documentary credit under UCP 600 can be established: the Luxembourg bank issues/advises an MT700 LC with presentation of commercial invoices, transport documents, and copies of distribution licences. MT202 COV alongside MT103 messages is used for intercompany funding between the SCSp and its foreign sub-entities, always accompanied by a tax-valid intercompany agreement to satisfy Luxembourg substance and transfer-pricing expectations.Outright deliverable FX forwards (EUR-USD, EUR-KRW, EUR-JPY) under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement and the applicable Credit Support Annex or local bank master agreementNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for restricted-settlement currencies such as KRW or INR (offshore NDF tenors up to 12 months) with EU/UK authorised swap counterparties, subject to EMIR reporting/clearing thresholds and board-approved notional limitsCross-currency swaps (XCCY) and short-dated FX swaps for duration-matched conversion of USD-denominated webtoon subscription cash flows into EUR operating liquidity, governed by ISDA CSA collateral methodologies and the EU Margin Rules (VM/IM) where applicable

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

24.94% combined corporate income tax headline for Luxembourg collective entities (15% corporate income tax plus solidarity surcharge and municipal business tax); 0% tax at SCSp entity level because the SCSp is fiscally transparent and profits are attributed to partners.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% Luxembourg dividend withholding tax on dividend payments by Luxembourg collective tax entities; 0% withholding tax on ordinary profit distributions by a transparent SCSp, because such distributions are characterized as partnership profit allocations rather than dividends.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File/CbCR documentation is triggered where the multinational group's consolidated annual turnover exceeds EUR 750 million; Local File retention is recommended for any related-party transaction exceeding EUR 5 million in annual aggregate controlled turnover, and is mandatory on demand where the SCSp or its partners engage in controlled cross-border digital-content, royalty, financing, or management-fee transactions.

Economic substance scope: Luxembourg does not impose formal GCC-style Economic Substance Regulations, but substance-over-form, ATAD general anti-abuse rules, beneficial ownership principles, and tax ruling requirements apply in full. An SCSp engaged in webtoon syndication must demonstrate genuine commercial purpose, effective management and decision-making in Luxembourg, physical presence, personnel, and real economic activity to preserve treaty network access and avoid recharacterization as an artificial conduit.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Misdeclaration of Digital Revenue

Statutory sanction: Additional tax assessment, monthly late-payment interest at 0.6%, administrative penalties up to 10% of the understated tax, and in criminal cases fines up to EUR 250,000 and imprisonment for up to five years; directors or managers involved in the false declaration may be jointly and severally liable.

Director personal liability: Veil-piercing is available where the SCSp is used as a sham; directors, the general partner, and authorized tax representatives may be required to pay unpaid tax personally, face asset seizure, travel restrictions, and imprisonment.
Abusive Shell / Conduit Structure

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits and Luxembourg tax residence advantages, application of GAAR/ATAD anti-abuse measures, retroactive reassessment, refusal of deductions, and possible nullity of the arrangement for tax purposes.

Director personal liability: Directors who establish or maintain a hollow SCSp may be held civilly liable for the unpaid tax consequences, including clawback of royalties and interest, and may face director disqualification and travel bans.
AML / Financial Crime / Partnership Misuse

Statutory sanction: Anti-money-laundering penalties of up to EUR 5 million for the legal entity and up to EUR 250,000 for individuals, imprisonment up to five years, freezing of app-store payout accounts, and removal of professional authorizations; false UBO declarations carry separate fines.

Director personal liability: The managing general partner and compliance officers can be personally fined and imprisoned; directors may be barred from serving in Luxembourg companies and may have personal assets confiscated.
Transfer Pricing Misalignment / Unapproved Margin Architecture

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustments to the Luxembourg taxable base, disallowance of excessive deductions, penalties of 10% to 30% of the adjusted underpayment, late-payment interest, and in intentional cases tax-fraud criminal liability.

Director personal liability: Directors and tax representatives who certify false transfer-pricing documentation face joint and several liability for the resulting tax deficiencies; civil fraud actions may pierce the corporate veil and reach personal assets.
