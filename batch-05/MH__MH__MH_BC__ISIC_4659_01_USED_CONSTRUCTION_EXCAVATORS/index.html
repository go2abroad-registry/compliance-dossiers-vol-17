Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance and Banking, Republic of the Marshall Islands, administered jointly with the Office of the Registrar of Corporations (RMI Registrar); corporate registration and maintenance filings are submitted through licensed registered agents under the Business Corporations Act (BCA) rather than a public e-filing portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign business corporation (non-resident) obtains its statutory entity registration number by filing Articles of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act through a licensed RMI registered agent. Because the RMI imposes no income tax, capital gains tax, VAT, or sales tax on non-resident entities, no separate TIN or VAT ID is issued. The BCA registration number is used for banking, customs formalities, and corporate records; a customs/port user account may be obtained separately for physical export shipments.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign business corporation (non-resident) obtains its statutory entity registration number by filing Articles of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act through a licensed RMI registered agent. Because the RMI imposes no income tax, capital gains tax, VAT, or sales tax on non-resident entities, no separate TIN or VAT ID is issued. The BCA registration number is used for banking, customs formalities, and corporate records; a customs/port user account may be obtained separately for physical export shipments.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election, hybrid-mismatch election, or entity-classification election exists under RMI domestic law. Classification is purely statutory under the BCA: the entity has separate legal personality and is 0% income-taxed in the RMI. If the shareholder is a US person, the US check-the-box election under IRS Form 8832 or US hybrid-mismatch rules may apply for US federal tax purposes, but such an election has no RMI tax consequence.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax in Force; Imports Only Subject to RMI Customs and Excise Tariff

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no indirect-tax turnover registration threshold exists; the RMI levies no VAT/GST/sales tax, and therefore the economic activity threshold is USD 0.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - the RMI imposes no VAT/GST/sales tax. Exported used construction excavators (ISIC 4659) depart the RMI tax-neutral; there is no 0% VAT claim, no output VAT on exports, and no input VAT refund mechanism to substantiate.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility with zero exchange controls. RMI statutes impose no restrictions on current-account or capital-account flows, no central-bank pre-approvals for dividend remittances, loan servicing or liquidation proceeds, and no limitations on foreign-currency account holdings, although the formal banking sector clears exclusively in USD. Profit and capital repatriation by a non-resident Business Corporation is executed via a standard SWIFT MT103 to the shareholder's offshore or US account. The only practical friction arises from FATF-aligned and OFAC-driven enhanced due diligence at the vesting bank and its US correspondent, not from RMI law. RMI's historic presence on the FATF increased-monitoring (grey) list compels robust documentation of source of funds and trade purpose.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. RMI imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio. Because all export receipts for used construction excavators and rollers are collected in USD, no conversion obligation arises. The exporting BC must nevertheless retain a complete audit trail of the full commercial value in each transaction — bills of sale, bills of lading, customs declarations and bank credits — to satisfy the receiving bank's trade-based money laundering controls and to demonstrate that no over- or under-invoicing has occurred.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands ('RMI') has no domestic central bank or monetary authority. Pursuant to Section 105 of the Amended Compact of Free Association, the US Dollar is the sole legal tender and monetary policy is effectively delegated to the US Federal Reserve. Prudential regulation of deposit-taking institutions is administered by the Banking Commissioner of the RMI Ministry of Finance under the Banking Act 1991 (MI-RC Title 24); AML/CTF supervision is exercised by the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing (Amendment) Act 2014. OFAC sanctions obligations apply de facto through US correspondent clearing channels.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. RMI law does not license e-money institutions or payment service providers; only banks licensed under the Banking Act 1991 may lawfully hold deposits or operate payment accounts in RMI. Foreign digital EMIs (FCA- or EU-licensed) lack any RMI presence and, due to the RMI's offshore-registry risk profile and FATF increased-monitoring status, most regulated EMIs will refuse onboarding of a non-resident BC trading used heavy machinery. At most, a foreign EMI may be used as a low-value collection point (single transactions under USD 25,000) with automatic sweeps to the primary BOMI account; all trade settlements and LC operations must execute on licensed bank rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections are executed by SWIFT MT103 in USD from the shareholder's own account to the BC's BOMI operating account. Field 70 must state the purpose as 'INCREASE PAID-IN CAPITAL' and reference the corporate resolution number. Pre-filed with the receiving bank: signed capital contribution resolution, subscription agreement, extracted shareholders' register, and a source-of-funds attestation letter from the remitting bank. No RMI central-bank or ministry approval is required for capital import; however, for amounts above USD 1,000,000, the bank may escalate a 'receipt of funds' inquiry to the FIU and will require a director-certified resolution and a bank reference letter. For time-sensitive closings, use same-day Fedwire settlement through the US correspondent; otherwise the standard SWIFT MT103 with two-day value suffices.Supplier payments for used excavators settle via SWIFT MT103 with field 70 carrying the invoice number, bill-of-lading reference, and equipment serial numbers. For recurring or high-value purchase programs (above USD 250,000 per transaction), use an irrevocable documentary Letter of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700 and confirmed by a first-class US or Asian bank, with 90% payable against shipping documents and 10% against final inspection certificate. A standby LC (SWIFT MT760) may be added as a performance bond to secure volume allocations. All USD clearing runs through the US correspondent network via CHIPS or Fedwire; same-day settlement is available for instructions received before 11:00 AM Majuro time (UTC+12). 0% for Non-Resident Domestic Business Corporations with a valid Tax Exemption Certificate; the general RMI corporate income tax rate is 11.5% and applies exclusively to RMI-source business income of non-exempt entities.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — The Republic of the Marshall Islands does not impose any withholding tax on dividends distributed by a Business Corporation to its shareholders, whether resident or non-resident, nor any secondary corporate tax on distributions.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual income tax nil return is due on the last day of the third calendar month following the close of the fiscal year (calendar-year companies: 31 March). The BCA Annual Corporate Report and registration fee are due on the anniversary date of incorporation; a grace period of 60 days applies before penalty accrual.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File or Master File thresholds have been enacted in the Marshall Islands. The RMI Income Tax Act applies the arm's length principle for related-party dealings, but this is inoperative where the non-resident BC is tax-exempt and derives no RMI-source income. A best-practice threshold of USD 2,000,000 aggregate intercompany turnover is applied by international banks and foreign tax authorities; for the used excavator remarketing business, the RMI entity typically acts as a limited-risk distributor and should retain contemporaneous documentation — including intercompany agreements, pricing matrix, and a TNMM benchmarking study demonstrating a routine return below 0.2% of gross resale value — in order to satisfy the source-country audit requirements of the markets where the heavy machinery is physically deployed.

Economic substance scope: The RMI Economic Substance Regulations Act 2021 applies exclusively to companies that are RMI tax residents — i.e., entities incorporated in RMI, except those that have established tax residency in a foreign jurisdiction and submit an annual declaration to that effect. A Non-Resident Business Corporation engaged in offshore remarketing of used construction excavators is outside the ESR scope because (a) it is not an RMI tax resident for income tax purposes, and (b) it does not conduct any relevant activity within the territory of RMI. The applicable classified activity would be 'distribution and service center' if conducted in the RMI, but physical operation is entirely offshore. Economic substance obligations therefore rest with the principal trading entity in the foreign jurisdiction where management, employees, and physical storage for the excavators are located. The RMI BC must nonetheless file a signed Annual ESR Non-Resident Declaration with the Registrar to maintain its exempt status; failure to file this declaration triggers automatic ESR review, statutory penalties up to USD 20,000, and possible director disqualification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through False Exemption Certification

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the Tax Exemption Certificate retroactively to the start of the fiscal year; full reassessment of all RMI-source income at the progressive corporate rate culminating in 11.5%; a 25% negligence penalty and a 50% fraud penalty on the underpayment; monthly interest accrual at 1.5% on outstanding principal; immediate deregistration of the Business Corporation and publication of the dissolution in the RMI gazette; the Revenue and Taxation Department may preserve an in-rem lien over all corporate assets held in the RMI, including receivables due from local dealers.

Director personal liability: Any director who signs a false nil return or exemption declaration is jointly and severally liable for the assessed tax, penalties, and interest without the benefit of the corporate shield; the director additionally faces criminal prosecution under Section 12 of the RMI Income Tax Act and the Criminal Code with fines up to USD 25,000, imprisonment for up to five years, a travel ban preventing departure from the RMI pending full settlement, and a lifetime prohibition on serving as an officer or director of any RMI-registered entity.
Beneficial Ownership Concealment and Shell Company Abuse (AML)

Statutory sanction: Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) administrative penalties of up to USD 200,000 per incidence; mandatory suspicious activity reporting failures exposed; freezing orders over bank accounts and tradeable assets under the RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2016 (as amended); deregistration of the corporation; referral to the US Department of Justice or foreign authorities through the Mutual Legal Assistance framework; reinstatement of FATF greylist countermeasures may subject the entity to enhanced foreign scrutiny and asset blocking.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and beneficial owners failing to disclose UBO information are personally liable for fines up to USD 100,000 and imprisonment up to ten years; the FIU may apply to the High Court for a travel ban and passport surrender; proceeds generated through the concealed shell arrangement become recoverable via criminal asset forfeiture, and directors who knowingly facilitated sanctions evasion face extradition risk and personal foreign civil judgments for conversion and fraud.
Persistent Default on Annual Registration Reporting

Statutory sanction: Late filing penalty of USD 250 plus a monthly surcharge of USD 50; automatic administrative dissolution after 90 days of default; dissolution extinguishes the legal personality of the corporation, voiding the validity of all contracts, wire transfers, bills of lading, and letters of credit executed after the dissolution date; contracts entered in the name of a dissolved corporation are unenforceable, and all proceeds remitted after dissolution are considered funds received without legal capacity.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue to trade during a period of involuntary dissolution are personally liable in contract and tort for all obligations incurred during the void period; the veil is pierced as a matter of the RMI Business Corporations Act; directors may be required to make restitution to counterparties, face a High Court injunction preventing their departure from RMI, and be stripped of their directorships in any domestic or international entity registered in the RMI for a period of five years.
Base Erosion and Artificial Substance Abuse (BEPS Countermeasures)

Statutory sanction: The RMI itself imposes no new tax, but the foreign revenue authorities will apply their CFC rules, controlled-foreign-corporation regimes, and transfer-pricing recharacterization to repudiate the interposition; underpayment assessments at 10% to 30% in the source market, plus interest and penalties; the RMI entity may be placed on the public EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions if structural abuse becomes systemic, triggering defensive withholding regimes in every EU member state; adverse FATF ratings may follow, leading to enhanced due-diligence rejection by all correspondent banks and a functional inability to open or maintain the corporate bank accounts required for cross-border wire transfers and SWIFT letters of credit.

Director personal liability: Directors who architect or consent to the interposition without valid economic substance face civil restitution claims in the foreign revenue jurisdiction, personal assessment orders under the foreign CFC legislation, and fraudulent conveyance actions brought by creditors; foreign courts may issue travel bans pursuant to international tax-judgment recognition protocols, and extradition proceedings are available under the RMI extradition legislation for indictable tax offences; professional license revocation applies to attorneys or corporate service providers who knowingly facilitate the artificial arrangement.
