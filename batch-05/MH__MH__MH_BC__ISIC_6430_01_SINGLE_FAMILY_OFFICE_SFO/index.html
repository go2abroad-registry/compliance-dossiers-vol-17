Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Corporations, Republic of the Marshall Islands (Office of the Attorney General, Majuro), acting through licensed resident registered agents.

Governing statute

Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act (BCA 1990, as amended), codified at Title 27 of the Marshall Islands Revised Code (27 MIRC Ch. 1); governing articles on certificate of incorporation, registered office and agent, and board organization (BCA ss. 44-45, 23/27, 46-48).

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation (Certificate of Incorporation) filed on the official registrar-endorsed form.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 14 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. Shares may be subscribed at any board-approved consideration; there is no capital escrow, deposit, or capitalization certificate requirement for a standard non-resident business corporation.

Funding & unlock path: No premises in the RMI — clearance is not mandated. If a representative office is established: fire-safety certificate per the Majuro Building and Fire Code (Occupancy Class B), structural load check of the leased unit, and workplace clearance under the RMI Safety at Work Regulations; a local business license from the Majuro Atoll Local Government would also apply.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. Shares may be subscribed at any board-approved consideration; there is no capital escrow, deposit, or capitalization certificate requirement for a standard non-resident business corporation.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Ministry of Finance, Revenue and Taxation Division; official portal: no centralized online e-portal currently operational - formal correspondence is accepted through the Ministry of Finance, Majuro, and the Office of the Registrar of Corporations.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned non-resident Business Corporation is not required to obtain an income tax TIN because the RMI imposes no general income tax on foreign-source income of non-resident corporations. For identification purposes, the Registrar of Corporations issues a certificate of incorporation with a unique registration number. If the entity employs RMI-resident individuals, it must register as an employer with the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration and obtain a six-digit employer number. For any entity that derives RMI-source gross revenue, registration with the Revenue and Taxation Division for the 3% gross receipts tax may be necessary; this is rare for a non-resident single-family office holding passive assets outside the RMI.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election is available under Marshall Islands law. The Business Corporations Act, as amended, permits an entity to elect non-resident domestic status by incorporating with non-resident designation and appointing a registered resident agent. The entity remains opaque for RMI income tax purposes; no pass-through transparency election exists under the Income Tax Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax in the Marshall Islands; indirect taxation is limited to import customs duties and certain regulatory/administrative fees)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration obligation exists. Local business license requirements may apply only to persons actively carrying on business inside the RMI; a non-resident offshore Business Corporation without RMI-source activity is outside this regime.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. The Marshall Islands does not operate a VAT or GST system, and therefore there is no zero-rating mechanism for exported goods or offshore services. Export transactions are simply outside the scope of indirect taxation; no refund claim or zero-rating election is required.

  • Not required for VAT zero-rating purposes due to absence of VAT in RMI; however, exporters may be required to file a Customs Export Declaration for statistical and customs control purposes if goods depart from RMI territory.

No real-time fiscal clearance, electronic invoice API, or digital tax stamping mandate exists. RMI Customs operations utilise ASYCUDA-based or paper declarations solely for import duties, not for VAT or sales tax purposes.

No VAT zero-rating clawback applies because no VAT is collected on exports. Misuse or falsification of customs export documentation is subject to penalties under the Customs Act, including monetary fines, forfeiture of goods, restoration or compensation orders, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent activity. Deliberate non-compliance with any RMI fiscal reporting obligation may additionally attract administrative sanctions and interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility de facto: because the U.S. dollar is domestic legal tender and no exchange-control legislation constrains current or capital account transactions, inbound capital injections, dividend and profit remittances, and portfolio repatriation may flow freely. The SFO faces no RMI exchange-rate risk, no capital-account approval requirement, and no profit-remittance tax because the Business Corporation is treaty-exempt under the RMI income-tax regime. The only practical constraints are OFAC and correspondent-bank sanctions screening in the home jurisdiction of the underlying bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The RMI imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export or other external receipts. U.S.-dollar receipts are domestic currency and may be freely held on deposit domestically or offshore without central-bank reporting.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). Banking and financial-sector supervision is administered by the Marshall Islands Banking Commission under the Banking Act 2012; the U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender (pursuant to the Compact of Free Association). There is no dedicated foreign-exchange control authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium (limited). The RMI Banking Act 2012 does not recognise a standalone electronic-money-institution (EMI) licence; offshore digital EMIs are not authorised deposit-takers and may not hold client funds in RMI. An offshore EMI may be employed only as a payment wrapper or disbursement conduit through a licensed bank partner, provided no RMI-resident wallet or custodial balance is established. Core treasury balances must remain with a licensed commercial bank or a reputable offshore bank in a reciprocal supervisory jurisdiction.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not codified - the RMI has no statutory transfer-pricing documentation rules and no mandatory Local File or Master File threshold in USD; an SFO with related-party investment, custody, or management transactions in excess of USD 1,000,000 annually should voluntarily prepare a functional analysis and arm's-length benchmark to demonstrate compliance with international best practice

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2019 applies to companies registered under the Business Corporations Act that derive geographically mobile income. A Single Family Office structured as a pure equity-holding company is classified as a 'pure equity-holding company' and is exempt from the full economic substance test, but must file an annual Economic Substance Return and satisfy reduced substance requirements: maintain at least one director, a registered agent, adequate records, and comply with statutory corporate filings; failure to file the Economic Substance Return attracts a default penalty of USD 10,000 and potential revocation

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Exemption Declaration

Statutory sanction: Immediate assessment of corporate income tax on all RMI-source income; delinquency penalty of 10%; interest at 12% per annum; criminal fines up to USD 50,000; revocation of exempt status and corporate charter

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or cause the filing of a knowingly false exemption declaration are jointly and severally liable for the assessed tax, subject to fines of USD 50,000 and imprisonment of up to three years; travel restrictions may be imposed pending resolution
Shell Company / Illegitimate Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: Administrative dissolution by the Registrar of Corporations; mandatory de-registration; referral to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit; inclusion in adverse international transparency listings; potential blacklisting of the registered agent

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face permanent disqualification from serving as officers of any RMI entity; civil restitution orders to reverse diverted assets; in aggravated cross-border cases, cooperation with foreign tax authorities may result in extradition proceedings
AML/CTF and Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties under the RMI AML/CFT framework of up to USD 100,000 per violation; suspension of the entity's banking relationship; cancellation of the annual renewal certificate; default penalty of USD 10,000 for ESR filing failures

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined, subjected to civil forfeiture of undisclosed assets, prohibited from leaving the Marshall Islands pending investigation, and, for knowing money laundering, imprisoned for up to ten years despite corporate limited liability protections
