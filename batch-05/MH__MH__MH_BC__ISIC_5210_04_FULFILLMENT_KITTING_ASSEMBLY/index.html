Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Tax and Customs Administration (TCA), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; taxpayer portal: https://www.finance.gov.mh

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation files its articles of incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations, then registers as a non-resident domestic corporation and applies to the Tax and Customs Administration for a Tax Exemption Certificate. No VAT/TIN is issued because the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT/GST; the unique taxpayer reference is the Tax Exemption Certificate number, aligned with the corporate registration number.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident Business Corporation files its articles of incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations, then registers as a non-resident domestic corporation and applies to the Tax and Customs Administration for a Tax Exemption Certificate. No VAT/TIN is issued because the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT/GST; the unique taxpayer reference is the Tax Exemption Certificate number, aligned with the corporate registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Election under the Business Corporations Act to operate as a non-resident tax-exempt corporation; no check-the-box pass-through election or hybrid mismatch election is available because the Marshall Islands imposes no corporate income tax. Tax-exempt status is evidenced by an administrative Certificate of Tax Exemption issued by the Minister of Finance.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax; indirect revenue is collected through customs import duties and excise-style levies only.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no indirect tax registration threshold exists. A customs import license or bond is nevertheless required for physical importation, and duty is assessed per the RMI Customs Tariff as applicable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable for VAT, as the Marshall Islands levies no VAT/GST. Exported goods and offshore services fall outside the scope of RMI indirect taxation; exporters of physical goods may, where applicable, claim a customs duty drawback for duty-paid imported inputs incorporated into exported products and re-exported within the statutory period.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping mandate exists. Import declarations are processed through the customs electronic declaration system; e-invoicing is not required.

Fraudulent misdeclaration in export or duty drawback filings is investigated by the Customs and Tax Administration; penalties may include forfeiture of goods, retrospective duty clawback and refund recovery, monetary fines up to USD 25,000 per offense, and imprisonment of responsible individuals for up to three years under the Customs Act and Revenue Administration provisions.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility in both current and capital accounts because the jurisdiction circulates the US dollar. There are no exchange controls, no surrender requirements, and no approval barriers to profit or dividend remittances by non-resident business corporations. Any practical friction is limited to bank-level AML/KYC verification of the underlying trade documentary trail.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The Marshall Islands imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation for export proceeds or service receipts. USD-denominated receivables may be retained, swept, or remitted offshore without approval from any monetary authority.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands has no true central bank; the US dollar is the sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association. Banking supervision is carried out by the Office of the Banking Commissioner (Banking Commission) under the Banking Act 1987, while treasury policy and financial administration reside with the Ministry of Finance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. The Marshall Islands has no fintech EMI or payments-institution licensing category; the Banking Act 1987 limits deposit-taking to licensed banks. Offshore digital EMIs such as Airwallex or Wise cannot open domestic settlement accounts in the RMI and may only serve as non-resident, multi-currency clearing adjuncts for outbound trade disbursements. The primary operational account must be held with a licensed domestic bank.

Parent or shareholder routes USD via SWIFT MT103 through US correspondent clearing (Fedwire/CHIPS) to the RMI operational account. The beneficiary field must show the entity registration number in the Marshall Islands; the MT103 field 70 must carry a clear purpose code such as GIFT/INVCAP for paid-in capital, LOAN for intercompany debt, or PURCHASE for trade funding. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% effective rate for a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC); the Marshall Islands imposes no corporate income tax on foreign-source income. The general statutory rate of 3% applies only to income derived from sources within the Marshall Islands; a fully offshore fulfillment, kitting, and assembly operation derives zero RMI-source income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. The Marshall Islands does not impose any withholding tax on dividends paid by a domestic corporation to a non-resident shareholder, irrespective of shareholding percentage.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Annual Report is due on the anniversary date of incorporation each year. There is no statutory grace period; a late surcharge is imposed immediately upon failure to file by the anniversary date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the last day of the third month following the close of the tax year (31 March for calendar-year taxpayers).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None. The Marshall Islands has no statutory transfer pricing rules, no Local File, no Master File, and no country-by-country (CbC) reporting thresholds in local currency (USD). Multinational groups must, however, assess CbC and Master File obligations under the parent jurisdiction's laws (e.g., US, EU) and may prepare OECD-aligned TP documentation defensively for foreign tax audits. There is no RMI penalty for failing to prepare contemporaneous TP documentation.

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019 (as amended) and associated Economic Substance Regulations impose an economic substance test on resident entities carrying on 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, distribution and service centre, and IP business). The subject fulfillment/kitting/assembly business (ISIC 5210) falls within 'distribution and service centre' only where the buying, selling, or provision of services is carried on with related parties. Pure third-party 3PL logistics for unrelated customers does not constitute a relevant activity. The entity must nonetheless file an annual economic substance notification through its registered agent, confirm its non-resident exempt status, and maintain documentary evidence that management, personnel, premises, and core income-generating assets are located outside the RMI.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Wilful Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Income Tax Act, criminal fines up to USD 10,000 per offense and/or imprisonment up to three years; civil penalty of 50% of the understated tax; immediate revocation of the NRDC tax-exempt status with re-taxation of all prior exempt profits.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and the registered agent who knowingly participate in the false filing may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, civil penalties, and criminal fines; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed pending prosecution.
Economic Substance Non-Compliance / Shell Company Presumption

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Economic Substance (Amendment) Act, administrative fines of up to USD 10,000 for initial non-compliance and further daily penalties for continuing default; the Registrar may strike off the entity; information will be exchanged spontaneously with the parent jurisdiction's tax authority under OECD/EUR exchange-of-information mechanisms.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from acting as directors of any RMI entity for up to five years; foreign tax authorities may disregard the RMI entity as a sham, attributing its profit to the offshore parent and imposing recharacterization penalties on the directors who signed the intercompany documents.
Unlicensed Local Business Activities

Statutory sanction: Daily fines for unlicensed operation, closure of the unauthorized business, forfeiture of profits derived from the unlicensed activity, and a ban on future FIBL issuance.

Director personal liability: Shareholders and directors knowingly conducting unlicensed business are personally liable for all resulting debts and statutory penalties; the corporate veil is pierced to the extent of the unlawful profit.
AML/CFT Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism legislation, monetary penalties of up to USD 100,000 per violation for a body corporate, suspension or revocation of the registered agent's license, and reporting to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers and directors face criminal liability for wilful AML failures, including imprisonment up to five years and personal fines; directors of an intermediary payment rail may also be pursued by foreign regulators for sanctions-busting.
Director Breach of Fiduciary Duty / Fraudulent Trading

Statutory sanction: Under the Business Corporations Act and Marshall Islands common law, directors may be ordered to repay unlawful distributions, pay equitable compensation, and be disqualified from serving as directors of any RMI entity.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced: directors face civil restitution, court-imposed travel bans, asset freezes, and criminal imprisonment for fraudulent trading; shareholders who knowingly receive tainted distributions must disgorge them with interest.
