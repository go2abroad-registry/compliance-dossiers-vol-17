Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation (Form BC-1), accompanied by written consent of the registered agent.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Ministry of Finance, Revenue and Taxation Division (Majuro). Corporate registration infrastructure: Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Majuro, operating through licensed RMI Registered Agents; customs administration: RMI Customs Service using the ASYCUDA electronic single-window platform.

E-government filing portal

No statutory TIN or VAT ID is issued to a non-resident Business Corporation (BC) because the RMI does not tax non-resident domestic corporations. Procedure: (1) appoint a licensed RMI Registered Agent and registered office address; (2) file Articles of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act (BCCA) designating non-resident domestic corporation status; (3) pay the government filing fee; (4) obtain the Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Corporations. The registry number and Certificate of Good Standing serve as the de facto tax identifiers. A Foreign Investment Business License and RMI tax registration become necessary only if the entity commences business activity with RMI-source income.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-Resident Domestic Corporation: a BC designated as non-resident in its articles is statutorily exempt from RMI income tax on all income derived outside the Republic, and is not subject to VAT/GST, capital gains tax, or import duty on outbound supplies, provided it has no RMI-source income and does not carry on business in the RMI; only annual registry/government fee obligations apply.

Available election forms

None analogous to US check-the-box; RMI has no corporate income tax for non-resident BCs and no hybrid-mismatch rules. Available status elections under the BCCA: (i) resident vs. non-resident domestic corporation designation in the articles; (ii) continuation into/out of the RMI (re-domestication); (iii) conversion between BC, LLC, and partnership forms; (iv) issuance of Certificate of Good Standing. Improper designation or RMI-source activity forfeits the exemption and triggers standard RMI taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax in force. Sole indirect fiscal regime: import duty under the Customs Tariff Act 1990; exports are duty-free. Electronic B2B MRO supplies (ISIC 4791) invoiced abroad attract no RMI indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. RMI official currency is the US dollar (USD); an economic turnover threshold of USD 0 is deemed inapplicable because no indirect tax is imposed on outbound supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — absence of a VAT/GST system means no 0% export VAT rate and no VAT refund mechanism. Goods exported from the RMI are free of export duty; industrial MRO inputs imported into the RMI and subsequently re-exported may qualify for customs duty drawback or duty suspension upon documentary proof of import, storage, and export.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed through the ASYCUDA platform (required only when goods physically depart RMI customs territory)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing outbound shipment
  • Commercial invoice and packing list (inward remittance certificate not required — RMI imposes no exchange controls and uses the US dollar)

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API-stamping, or digital control mandate. Where goods physically enter or leave RMI customs territory, declarations are filed electronically through ASYCUDA; ordinary commercial invoices, packing lists, and transport documents are accepted.

No statutory FX repatriation deadline and no VAT zero-rating clawback can arise in the RMI. However, false declarations, drawback fraud, smuggling, or evasion of customs duty under the Customs Tariff Act 1990 subject the exporter to forfeiture, fines, and criminal liability, with the Registrar able to strike off an offending BC for non-compliance; resumption of RMI-source activity without proper FIBL and tax registration exposes the entity to income tax, penalties, and interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMI operates a fully dollarized economy with zero exchange-control restrictions. Current-account transactions (trade settlement, shipping and logistics for MRO goods) and capital-account transactions (equity injection, shareholder loans and dividend repatriation) are freely convertible and require no central bank approval. Profit remittance from a non-resident Business Corporation is unrestricted once beneficial-ownership filings are current, the account passes AML/KYC screening, and no unpaid RMI income-tax liability exists for any economic activity conducted within RMI territory.

Export proceeds surrender rules

RMI imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio and no repatriation deadline for export receipts. All MRO electronic-catalog proceeds may be credited and retained indefinitely in USD demand or time deposits held at an RMI-licensed bank, with unrestricted availability for operational disbursements.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) has no statutory central bank; the Banking Commissioner within the Ministry of Finance exercises all banking licensing, prudential supervision and foreign-exchange monitoring powers, while the U.S. Federal Reserve System serves as the de facto monetary anchor through the Compact of Free Association. The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender under RMI law.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to Medium. The RMI Banking Act confines deposit-taking to domestically licensed banks and no RMI electronic-money-institution (EMI) licence exists; therefore a U.S., U.K. or EU-licensed EMI may be used solely as a secondary collection rail for card-not-present MRO payments, with legal settlement forced to an RMI-licensed bank account. Foreign EMIs cannot provide the principal account, cash escrow, or trade-finance clearing structure required by the blueprint.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a non-resident Business Corporation with tax-exempt status; no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no branch profits tax, and no payroll tax are imposed by the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) on income derived from sources outside the RMI. A 3% Gross Revenue Tax applies solely to gross revenue derived from trade or business carried on within the Marshall Islands; offshore catalog sales of industrial MRO supplies fall outside the charge.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the Marshall Islands imposes no withholding tax on dividends distributed by a Business Corporation to non-resident shareholders, regardless of share class or amount.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the 30th day of the calendar month following the anniversary date of incorporation. A late-filed report attracts penalties, and continued non-filing for two consecutive years subjects the corporation to administrative dissolution by the Registrar.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual return due on or before April 30 following the close of the tax year; quarterly installments due by the 10th day of the month following each calendar quarter. Non-resident zero-filers must still submit the annual return to preserve eligibility for the business license exemption.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No domestic transfer-pricing legislation or Local File / Master File regime exists in the RMI. As a compliance benchmark, mandatory documentation would only be contemplated where local-source gross revenue exceeds USD 1,000,000 (gross revenue tax threshold) and related-party transactions affect the RMI tax base; for a tax-exempt non-resident BC with no RMI permanent establishment, the effective documentation threshold is nil and no transfer-pricing return is filed.

Economic substance scope: The RMI has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations comparable to BVI or Cayman, but it participates in OECD/G20 inclusive framework transparency reviews and maintains a FATF-consistent AML regime. A non-resident BC operating a B2B MRO catalog must demonstrate real economic substance — independent board meetings, actual procurement and distribution functions, a funded bank account, and retention of commercial risk — to prevent recharacterization of the RMI entity as a sham in the shareholder's jurisdiction and to preserve access to foreign tax credits under CFC, PFIC, and GAAR rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Gross Revenue Tax Act and the Business Corporations Act: 20% penalty on tax underpaid plus interest of 1% per month; criminal fines of USD 5,000 to USD 25,000 per false filing; revocation of tax-exempt status; retroactive assessment for three years; administrative dissolution of the corporation.

Director personal liability: Each signing director is jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, penalties, and restitution; conviction carries imprisonment of up to 5 years; the court may issue travel bans and asset-freeze orders; the corporate veil is pierced upon proof that the declaration was made with knowledge of falsity.
Shell Company / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: The RMI does not levy a domestic penalty for lack of substance, but adverse OECD/FATF peer-review results and EU listing produce defensive withholding and de-risking: correspondent banks close accounts, and foreign tax authorities recharacterize the structure, imposing 30% penalties and interest on the economic owner.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors knowingly facilitating the sham are exposed to civil claims for damages in RMI courts; managing directors can be disqualified from acting as directors for up to 10 years; foreign criminal courts may issue arrest warrants, making travel outside the RMI high-risk.
Anti-Money Laundering / AML-CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Entity fines of up to USD 100,000 per violation; failure to file a suspicious transaction report attracts an additional USD 50,000 fine; continued non-compliance compels the registered agent to withdraw representation and the Registrar to strike the corporation from the register.

Director personal liability: Willful blindness is treated as aiding and abetting: directors face imprisonment of up to 10 years, personal confiscation orders, disqualification orders, and travel bans pending extradition; the corporate veil is pierced for fraud, and directors bear joint liability for laundered amounts.
Facilitation of Foreign Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: FATCA 30% withholding on U.S.-source payments; RMI financial institutions breach compliance obligations and may be fined; listing in the EU non-cooperative jurisdiction annex triggers 25% defensive withholding on EU payments to RMI entities.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly assist foreign tax evasion face extradition and criminal liability in the residence state, asset-freezing orders, and confiscation of personal assets; the absence of RMI domestic income tax does not shield a director from foreign civil fraud claims.
