Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AEDT) for VAT; Administration des contributions directes (ACD) for direct taxes; integrated digital portal: MyGuichet.lu / guichet.lu

E-government filing portal

A foreign SCSp must register its identity with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) via the MyGuichet single point, obtaining a 13-digit national identification number (numéro d'identification). If taxable supplies are made in Luxembourg, the partnership must file VAT Form 1 with the AEDT, provide proof of its registered seat/activities, and, unless established in the EU or in a country with a tax administrative-assistance agreement, appoint an approved Luxembourg fiscal representative. The VAT number takes the form 'LU + 8 digits'. No minimum capital or local substance is required for the SCSp itself, but a domestic tax representative contact is mandatory for non-EU investors without a permanent establishment.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign SCSp must register its identity with the Luxembourg Register of Commerce and Companies (RCS) via the MyGuichet single point, obtaining a 13-digit national identification number (numéro d'identification). If taxable supplies are made in Luxembourg, the partnership must file VAT Form 1 with the AEDT, provide proof of its registered seat/activities, and, unless established in the EU or in a country with a tax administrative-assistance agreement, appoint an approved Luxembourg fiscal representative. The VAT number takes the form 'LU + 8 digits'. No minimum capital or local substance is required for the SCSp itself, but a domestic tax representative contact is mandatory for non-EU investors without a permanent establishment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Luxembourg does not apply a 'check-the-box' election for the SCSp, which is by law fiscally transparent for income tax, municipal business tax and net wealth tax. No corporate tax election is available; however, the SCSp may opt for VAT grouping (assujetti unique) with its qualifying subsidiaries if certain control conditions are met, and the partnership remains subject to hybrid-mismatch rules under ATAD, e.g., by electing to align the tax treatment of a partner under a foreign partnership analogy.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

16%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general registration threshold for domestic taxable supplies, but a small-business exemption applies up to EUR 30,000 annual turnover; non-established businesses have no threshold and must register for any taxable supply; intra-EU distance sales of goods trigger Luxembourg VAT registration when total cross-border distance sales to Luxembourg consumers exceed EUR 10,000 per calendar year.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported outside the EU qualify for 0% VAT provided they physically leave the EU by a prescribed deadline and proof of export is obtained within 90 days. For cross-border B2B testing services (dangerous goods UN38.3 drop testing and CE-RED electromagnetic lab), the place of supply is the customer's country; if the customer is a taxable person established outside the EU, the supply is outside the scope of Luxembourg VAT, effectively zero-rated, conditional on obtaining adequate commercial evidence of the customer's business establishment, e.g., a tax registration certificate or foreign VAT ID. Supplies to EU taxable customers are subject to reverse charge in the customer's EU Member State and require the customer's valid EU VAT number.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD – Single Administrative Document)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note evidencing physical departure of goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank/currency receipt evidencing receipt of foreign payment
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • UN38.3 test report and CE-RED conformity file referencing the export order

Mandatory structured e-invoicing is only imposed for B2G invoices issued to central government entities, which must be sent through the PEPPOL network; there is no real-time fiscal clearance, no continuous transaction controls, and no digital stamping requirement for B2B or B2C invoices.

The Luxembourg tax authorities may conduct post-export audit checks and require production of all listed documentation. If export zero-rating is denied or proven fraudulent, the VAT becomes immediately collectable with statutory default interest; late payment penalties and corrected assessments up to 20% of the amount due may be imposed; the standard statute of limitation is three years, extended to five years in the case of misdeclaration and up to ten years for criminal tax fraud. Serious abuse may trigger criminal sanctions, joint liability of the fiscal representative, and withdrawal of zero-rating benefits for previously filed returns.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Luxembourg is a euro-area member with full EUR convertibility on both current and capital accounts pursuant to Article 63 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU) and the OECD Code of Liberalisation of Capital Movements. There are no exchange controls, no restrictions on cross-border payments, and no differentiated regime for non-resident investors. Profit remittance from an SCSp is freely permitted; distributions by the fiscally transparent SCSp are not subject to Luxembourg withholding tax at the partnership level. The only practical constraints are bank-level AML/KYC due diligence and the monthly BCL balance-of-payments statistical declaration required for each cross-border transaction above EUR 12,500.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Luxembourg imposes no surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory timeline for export proceeds. Receipts from UN38.3 battery-safety testing and CE-RED electromagnetic laboratory services may be retained in EUR or any freely convertible currency with no central-bank approval. The sole overlay is statistical: the credit institution must report cross-border flows to the BCL under the balance-of-payments framework, using the appropriate transaction purpose code, for amounts equal to or exceeding EUR 12,500.

Central bank supervision: Banque centrale du Luxembourg (BCL), operating within the Eurosystem under the European Central Bank framework; prudential and AML/CFT supervisory functions are exercised by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF). The BCL manages the national TARGET2 settlement accounts, oversees market infrastructure, and compiles balance-of-payments statistics reported by Luxembourg credit institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium for operational collection and foreign-currency clearing of low-value test-fee receivables; Low for capital escrow or capital-injection functions. Wise Europe SA (the 'Wise' product) holds a Belgian Electronic Money Institution licence passported into Luxembourg under PSD2/EMD2 and can provide a multi-currency business wallet (EUR, USD, GBP, CNH) to the SCSp. Legal constraints: (i) an EMI cannot take deposits, grant credit, or operate a classical escrow/segregated account; (ii) customer funds are safeguarded but not protected by the EU Deposit Guarantee Scheme; (iii) practical transaction velocity limits (EUR 10,000-50,000 per transaction) render the rail unsuitable for capital calls or equipment disbursements; (iv) the EMI must still verify ultimate beneficial owners against the Luxembourg RBE register. Recommended architecture: use Wise for sub-EUR 20,000 invoice collection from non-EU battery producers with same-day conversion, then sweep the balance to the BIL/BGL escrow or operational account.
