Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Office (RTO), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (Majuro); corporate registration administered by the Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General. No centralized national e-filing portal exists; registration is processed through licensed registered agents and the RTO business-license desk.

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident Business Corporation: (1) engage a Marshall Islands licensed registered agent; (2) file articles of incorporation under the Business Corporations Act (BCA) with the Registrar of Corporations and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (3) apply for an annual Business License (foreign business license, as applicable) with the Business License Office/RTO, which is based on a statutory fee schedule rather than income; (4) if physically importing or exporting goods, obtain a Customs Dealer's License; (5) note that the Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no corporate income tax and maintains no VAT or income-tax TIN for non-resident corporations, so the Business License number and registered-agent file number serve as the de facto tax and customs identifiers.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (Marshall Islands territorial fiscal regime; a non-resident Business Corporation is outside the RMI income-tax net and enjoys fiscal neutrality on foreign-source income, with no domestic corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on non-resident shareholders). — For a non-resident Business Corporation: (1) engage a Marshall Islands licensed registered agent; (2) file articles of incorporation under the Business Corporations Act (BCA) with the Registrar of Corporations and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (3) apply for an annual Business License (foreign business license, as applicable) with the Business License Office/RTO, which is based on a statutory fee schedule rather than income; (4) if physically importing or exporting goods, obtain a Customs Dealer's License; (5) note that the Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no corporate income tax and maintains no VAT or income-tax TIN for non-resident corporations, so the Business License number and registered-agent file number serve as the de facto tax and customs identifiers.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Marshall Islands territorial fiscal regime; a non-resident Business Corporation is outside the RMI income-tax net and enjoys fiscal neutrality on foreign-source income, with no domestic corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on non-resident shareholders).

Available election forms

None. The Marshall Islands has no check-the-box election, no hybrid-mismatch election, and no formal tax-status election regime. The BCA corporation's tax classification is fixed by statute as an exempt offshore-type entity for non-resident owners; no ministerial election forms or procedures exist.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — no VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax exists. The RMI levies customs import duties (Import Tax Act) and limited excises on specific goods (e.g., fuel, alcohol, tobacco); exports fall outside the indirect-tax base.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Under the import-tax regime, a customs dealer registration is mandatory for any physical importation of goods into the RMI, irrespective of turnover or value.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — the RMI has no VAT/GST system, and therefore no zero-rating election exists. For customs-duty purposes, goods exported from the RMI are outside the indirect-tax base; imported inputs may qualify for duty suspension or drawback only under RMI customs warehousing or re-export rules, provided the inputs are demonstrably incorporated into qualifying exported goods such as titanium orthopedic implants.

  • Customs Export Declaration (outward manifest or customs database entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (proof of physical export from the Marshall Islands)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list (value, quantity, and description of the exported titanium orthopedic implants)
  • Certificate of Origin / Export Certificate (where US Compact of Free Association trade preferences are claimed)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (SWIFT wire or letter-of-credit banking evidence where export proceeds are repatriated through an RMI licensed bank)

None. The Marshall Islands does not mandate e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping. Customs clearance remains documentary: the importer/exporter files an import entry/export declaration supported by commercial invoice, packing list, and transport document; processing is paper-based or simplified automated system depending on the port of entry.

As no VAT refund mechanism exists in the RMI, the relevant scrutiny is customs-law enforcement: duty-free treatment claimed for imported inputs that are diverted to domestic use, false export declarations, undervaluation, or misdescription of exported goods. Penalties include forfeiture of goods, monetary fines up to 100% of the duty evaded, suspension or revocation of the Customs Dealer's License, and criminal prosecution; any customs-duty benefit granted on the strength of an export claim is retrospectively clawed back with interest where the export documentation is falsified, goods are re-imported, or goods are diverted into the RMI domestic market.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and free convertibility. RMI maintains no exchange controls, no capital-account restrictions, and no foreign investment screening. The fully dollarized economy permits unconditional conversion for both current-account and capital-account transactions. Profit remittance and dividend distributions by a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC) are not subject to RMI withholding, exchange approval, or remittance hurdles, provided the entity is in Good Standing. Practical diligence constraints arise only at the U.S. correspondent clearing level, where OFAC and sanctions screening apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory timeline for receipt repatriation. Export proceeds from titanium orthopedic implant sales may be retained in USD or any other currency in any permitted bank. The only binding obligation is AML record-keeping: source-of-funds evidence (commercial invoice, bill of lading, buyer identification, and wire advice) must be preserved for at least five years under the AML/CFT Act 2012.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI); no conventional central bank exists. The U.S. dollar (USD) is the sole legal tender, and USD currency management is effectively undertaken by the U.S. Federal Reserve. The Banking Commissioner licenses and supervises the two licensed commercial banks under the Banking Act 1987, while the Marshall Islands Financial Intelligence Unit executes AML/CFT oversight under the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act 2012.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium for ancillary use only. The Banking (Special Purpose Dealers) Amendment Act 2018 permits RMI-licensed Special Purpose Dealers (SPD/stablecoin issuers), but no SPD currently provides SWIFT membership, commercial LC issuance, or institutional-grade clearing. Legal constraints: an SPD license requires an RMI registered agent and office, imposes transaction caps, and obligates independent OFAC/U.S. dollar clearing compliance. An offshore digital EMI may serve only as a non-core concentration account capped at 10% of monthly treasury flows and must be disclosed separately to the primary bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a qualifying Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC) under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act; a corporation that carries on business in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) and derives RMI-source revenue is instead subject to the RMI gross revenue tax of 3% on that gross revenue.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on distributions made by an exempt non-resident BC to non-resident shareholders. The RMI imposes no dividend withholding tax, branch remittance tax, or undistributed profits tax on such entities.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual report and franchise tax statement must be filed within 30 days after the anniversary date of incorporation; the deadline is not based on the accounting fiscal year-end.

Item 6: Non-Resident Exemption Declaration Declaration that the corporation derived no income from sources within the RMI and carried on no business in the RMI during the reporting period. This declaration must be supported by board minutes and transaction records; a false declaration is treated as tax evasion and can expose directors to personal liability. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory RMI Local File or Master File threshold exists for an exempt NRDC. As a best practice, prepare and retain transfer pricing documentation whenever related-party cross-border transactions for the titanium implant business exceed USD 1,000,000 in a reporting period, and always when intercompany charges exceed 1% of gross revenue or when LC trade flows are routed through a group purchasing hub.

Economic substance scope: The RMI expects registered entities engaged in geographically mobile activities to satisfy economic substance requirements to the extent the Economic Substance Regulations apply. A non-resident BC manufacturing and distributing porous titanium orthopedic implants must be managed and controlled outside the RMI, hold real board meetings, maintain comprehensive contracts and ledgers outside the RMI, and derive no RMI-source income. An NRDC with no commercial substance or used only as an LC conduit will be treated as a shell, lose its tax exemption, and face strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false non-resident declaration

Statutory sanction: Reclassification from NRDC to resident corporation; retroactive 3% gross revenue tax assessment on all RMI-source revenue; late-payment penalties and interest assessed by the RMI Tax Administrator; cancellation of good standing; administrative dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who signed or directed the false declaration can be made personally liable for the assessed tax and penalties. Marshall Islands courts may disregard the corporate veil when the BC is a sham; travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment may follow upon criminal tax conviction.
Shell company and absence of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Denial or revocation of the non-resident tax exemption; economic substance monetary penalties; registered-agent termination; administrative strike-off; issuance of a deficient good-standing certificate for foreign bank onboarding.

Director personal liability: Nominee and shadow directors are jointly answerable for fraudulent trading. Courts can order restitution of assets moved through the BC, prohibit service as director, impose travel bans during investigation, and attach personal assets if the shell was used to mislead creditors or regulators.
AML/CFT failure and trade-based money laundering

Statutory sanction: Penalties under RMI AML/CFT legislation; court-ordered freezing of Swift wire and letter-of-credit proceeds; civil forfeiture; criminal fines and imprisonment; deregistration of the entity by the Registrar.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal fines and imprisonment for wilful blindness. Travel bans are routinely imposed pending mutual legal assistance, and the corporate veil can be pierced to recover funds where the entity was used as a vehicle for trade-based money laundering.
Unlawful dividend and capital stripping

Statutory sanction: The dividend is voidable and must be restored; directors are jointly and severally liable to the BC and its creditors under the BCCA; the Registrar can revoke good standing for persistent default.

Director personal liability: Directors who vote an insolvent dividend or a capital-stripping shareholder loan are personally liable for restitution. Fraudulent-conveyance proceedings may lead to travel bans, disqualification orders, and civil seizure of transferred assets.
Unlicensed and embargoed medical-device trade

Statutory sanction: Goods may be seized and recalled in destination markets; import and export licences may be revoked; monetary fines may be imposed by regulators; the RMI registered agent may refuse to represent the entity; criminal export-control penalties can apply.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally exposed to product-liability, recall, and export-control proceedings. Deliberate shipment to sanctioned end users can result in imprisonment, debarment, travel restrictions, and personal asset freezes; the corporate form will not shield intentional violations.
