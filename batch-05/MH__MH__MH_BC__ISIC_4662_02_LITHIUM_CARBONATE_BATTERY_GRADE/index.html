Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance – Tax and Customs Administration (TCA), Majuro; no dedicated public e-filing portal; corporate registration processed by the Registrar of Corporations under the Office of the Attorney General.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation that is tax-exempt is not required to obtain a Marshall Islands TIN or VAT ID. If the entity engages in taxable local business or customs transactions, it must register with TCA by filing Form BPT-1 and the customs importer/exporter registration form, together with certified articles of incorporation, registered agent details, and a business license obtained from the local administration; no VAT ID is issued because the RMI does not operate a value-added tax system.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident Business Corporation that is tax-exempt is not required to obtain a Marshall Islands TIN or VAT ID. If the entity engages in taxable local business or customs transactions, it must register with TCA by filing Form BPT-1 and the customs importer/exporter registration form, together with certified articles of incorporation, registered agent details, and a business license obtained from the local administration; no VAT ID is issued because the RMI does not operate a value-added tax system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections exist in Marshall Islands law. A foreign-owned Business Corporation is automatically exempt from the 3% Business Profits Tax if it does not derive income from sources within the Republic of the Marshall Islands; this is a passive statutory status rather than an elective classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; indirect taxation limited to customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no indirect tax registration threshold exists because there is no VAT/GST/sales tax; customs duty applies on each import shipment, and Business Profits Tax registration is triggered only by commencing any RMI-source commercial activity.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT/GST on exports, and no 0% rate claim or VAT refund regime exists. Exports from a non-resident exempt Business Corporation are outside the scope of RMI taxation; an export customs declaration is nevertheless required to validate the physical and legal exit of the goods from RMI where applicable.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, digital stamping, or e-invoicing is mandated by the Marshall Islands tax administration; invoice integrity is governed by general commercial law, not fiscal electronic clearance rules.

The RMI imposes no VAT that could be clawed back on exports; however, false customs declarations, misdescription of export status, or fraudulent use of tax-exempt status are subject to enforcement under the Customs Tariff Act, including monetary penalties, forfeiture of cargo, denial of exemption benefits, and criminal liability for wilful false statements. For any hypothetical BPT refund, TCA may audit records and impose interest and penalties on amounts extracted from the RMI without proper substantiation.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility in both current and capital accounts is statutory in practice because the RMI has no domestic currency of its own and imposes no exchange control law, no repatriation permit regime, and no profit remittance restrictions. Dividends, loan repayments, and liquidation proceeds may be paid freely to non-resident shareholders without central bank approval. Practical hurdles are confined to commercial bank level compliance, including source-of-wealth verification, OFAC and FATF-related sanctions screening, and correspondent bank due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. RMI imposes no statutory timeline for repatriation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation for export proceeds. A trading entity engaged in cross-border procurement may retain sale proceeds offshore indefinitely. However, where trade finance facilities are secured from a domestic bank, the lender may contractually require that export receivables be routed through and settled into the borrower's account at that bank as a condition of the credit facility.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) does not maintain a central bank or monetary authority. The U.S. Dollar is the sole legal tender, and monetary policy is effectively set by the U.S. Federal Reserve. Prudential supervision of licensed banks is vested in the Office of the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, under the Banking Act 1990 and the Financial Institutions (Amendment) Act 2018.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to Medium. RMI has no electronic money institution licensing regime; any entity taking deposits or providing payment services to RMI residents requires a banking license under the Banking Act 1990. Offshore digital EMIs and non-resident payment firms generally cannot onboard RMI-domiciled business corporations without a locally licensed bank intermediary. A practical structure is to open a USD account at a licensed foreign EMI with U.S. partner bank rails, but such EMIs typically decline RMI-registered entities unless the company provides a substantive nexus justification, audited financials, and a clean UBO profile. The RMI's DAO and Sovereign (SOV) legislation does not create an EMI passport and does not lower the banking licensing bar.

Recommended narrative field: 'Capital subscription per board resolution dated [date] MH__MH__MH_BC'. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD; no statutory Local File, Master File, or CbCR threshold exists under RMI law; transfer pricing documentation is not statutorily mandated, but arm's-length margin evidence must be retained for Economic Substance (Distribution and Service Centre) compliance, including intercompany purchase orders, resale invoices, and commission/warehouse agreements

Economic substance scope: RMI Economic Substance (Exemptions) Regulations 2019 apply to Relevant Entities conducting Relevant Activities, which expressly include Distribution and Service Centre operations such as cross-border lithium carbonate procurement and arbitrage resale. CIGA (core income-generating activities) must be managed and directed in RMI, with adequate premises, full-time employees, and operating expenditure proportional to income. An NRDC may claim exclusion if it is tax resident in a qualifying foreign jurisdiction and subject to income tax there. Annual ESR-1 declaration required; non-compliance attracts penalties up to US$10,000 plus daily default surcharges.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil fraud penalty of 50% of the tax deficiency plus monthly underpayment interest at 1.5%; late-filing and non-payment surcharges up to 25% of the assessed tax; criminal prosecution under Title 54 RMC with conviction leading to fines of US$10,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years

Director personal liability: Directors signing false declarations are jointly and severally liable for tax restitution and civil penalties; criminal conviction triggers travel bans, exit restrictions, and compulsory surrender of passports
Shell Company / Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty of US$10,000 plus US$500 per day for continuing breach; administrative strike-off or dissolution by the Registrar; forfeiture of the corporate name; referral to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit

Director personal liability: Knowing participation exposes directors to personal civil liability for unpaid penalties, disqualification orders prohibiting board service, and imprisonment of up to 12 months for ESR filing evasion
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering
