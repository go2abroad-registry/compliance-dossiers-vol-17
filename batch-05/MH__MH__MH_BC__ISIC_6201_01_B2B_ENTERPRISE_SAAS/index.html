Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Registrar, Majuro, Republic of the Marshall Islands; for non-resident corporations, filing administration is channeled through International Registries, Inc. (IRI) as the official worldwide agency of the Marshall Islands Corporate Registry.

Governing statute

Business Corporations Act, 1990 (as amended), Title 51 of the Marshall Islands Revised Statutes, Division 1; principal sections include 51 MIRC §14 (Articles of Incorporation), §15 (Corporate name requirements), §16 (By-laws), §20 (Issuance of shares) and §25 (Registered agent and registered office).

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation for a Non-Resident Business Corporation, together with the registered agent's acceptance of appointment.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 13 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is required for a Non-Resident Business Corporation; shares may be authorized with or without par value, and no escrow account, bank attestation, or capital deposit obligation applies at formation.

Funding & unlock path: Not triggered for a non-resident entity with no physical premises in the Marshall Islands. If a local office were opened in Majuro, the company would be required to obtain fire safety certification under the RMI Fire Prevention Code, structural load inspection and occupancy clearance from the Building Inspectorate, and workplace registration under the Occupational Health and Safety Act prior to occupation; none of these clearances applies to the currently contemplated virtual, non-resident B2B SaaS operation.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital is required for a Non-Resident Business Corporation; shares may be authorized with or without par value, and no escrow account, bank attestation, or capital deposit obligation applies at formation.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Customs Administration (RCA), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; official government portal: https://rmi.gov.mh

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Non-Resident Business Corporation: (1) file Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General, including a declaration that the corporation will not carry on business in the Marshall Islands; (2) obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (3) if the corporation later derives Marshall Islands-source income, apply to the RCA on Form TIN-01 with the certificate, registered agent details and local business license; (4) no VAT ID is available because the RMI has no VAT/GST system.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a foreign-owned Non-Resident Business Corporation: (1) file Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General, including a declaration that the corporation will not carry on business in the Marshall Islands; (2) obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (3) if the corporation later derives Marshall Islands-source income, apply to the RCA on Form TIN-01 with the certificate, registered agent details and local business license; (4) no VAT ID is available because the RMI has no VAT/GST system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Non-resident status is elected at incorporation under the Business Corporations Act; a corporation making this election is exempt from income tax and gross revenue tax on offshore income. The RMI has no check-the-box classification and no hybrid mismatch election; no US-style entity classification election is recognised.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (RMI levies no VAT/GST or sales and use tax; customs duties apply only to imported physical goods)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in the RMI (economy uses USD)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the VAT sense because the RMI has no VAT/GST. Cross-border B2B enterprise SaaS supplied by a Non-Resident Business Corporation to foreign customers falls outside the RMI territorial tax net. To maintain the exemption the corporation must not (i) carry on business in the RMI, (ii) derive Marshall Islands-source income, or (iii) hold real property in the RMI. No export registration or zero-rating approval is required.

  • No mandatory customs documentation bundle exists for offshore SaaS exports; electronic software delivery involves no customs export declaration. If physical media is ever shipped, an RMI Customs Export Declaration and bill of lading may be required, but no VAT refund will arise.

None. The RMI does not mandate e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API-based transmission to the RCA, or digital stamping for indirect tax purposes.

There is no export VAT zero-rating or refund regime in the RMI, therefore no clawback of VAT refunds is possible. Nonetheless, abusive claims of non-resident exemption where the corporation in fact earns Marshall Islands-source income will trigger RCA reassessment, interest at 1.5% per month, late payment penalties of up to 10% per month (capped at 50% of the tax due), and criminal fines of up to USD 10,000 or imprisonment under the Income Tax Act. False customs export declarations may attract forfeiture and fines up to three times the goods' value under the Customs Act.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender, giving the RMI a fully dollarized, fully convertible monetary system. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible, with no foreign-exchange controls, no repatriation permits, and no profit-remittance taxation or FX surcharge. Practical hurdles are limited to documentary compliance by the paying and receiving banks, including U.S. OFAC sanctions screening and internal AML/KYC policies. A non-resident Business Corporation may hold funds, pay dividends, and remit liquidation proceeds in USD without central-bank approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The RMI imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no export- proceeds surrender obligation because exports and all commercial receipts are denominated in U.S. dollars, the domestic currency. Foreign-currency export proceeds, if any, may be held or converted freely without obligation to the RMI government or to any monetary authority.

Central bank supervision: No standalone RMI central bank or foreign exchange administration exists. Banking supervision and licensing are administered by the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands. The de facto monetary authority is the U.S. Federal Reserve because the U.S. dollar is legal tender under the Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low–Medium as a business matter, and legally constrained. The RMI Banking Act 1991 and the Financial Institutions (Financial Obligations) Regulations 2001 require any deposit-taking or custodial banking activity to be licensed by the Banking Commissioner; there is no standalone e-money institution (EMI) or digital-bank licensing regime. Offshore digital EMIs, including Wise B2B and Stripe Billing, cannot lawfully open virtual accounts in the RMI as principal bank substitutes. However, they may be engaged lawfully as payment facilitators that route funds to and from a licensed RMI bank account, provided the underlying customer account is held at Bank of Marshall Islands, Inc. or Bank of Guam (Marshall Islands Branch). The RMI entity must maintain a licensed-USD bank account as the liquidity anchor; otherwise, external payment processors will reject onboarding or treat the RMI entity as unbankable.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound USD capital injections for a non-resident Business Corporation are cleared through the U.S. banking network, primarily via SWIFT MT103 or Fedwire. The injector wires USD to the RMI bank's U.S. correspondent, which posts the funds to the beneficiary account at Bank of Marshall Islands, Inc. or Bank of Guam (Marshall Islands Branch). The RMI has no central-bank purpose-code regime; banks instead rely on the wire's 'remittance information' field. Compliant instructions must include: a) beneficiary bank SWIFT/BIC; b) full beneficiary name as per incorporation; c) RMI account number; d) an explicit purpose line such as 'Equity contribution – Business Corporation capital'; and e) OFAC-compliant originator details. Verification requires submission of the capitalization board resolution, share issuance calculation, source-of-funds evidence, and a completed UBO declaration before unconditional crediting. Capital escrow accounts may be used temporarily to allow bank counsel to verify the injection before transfer to the operating account.Outbound disbursements for trade settlements use SWIFT MT103 for general vendor payments, SaaS licensing fees, subcontractor payments, and operational expenditures. For high-value procurement of hardware, managed infrastructure, or services requiring documentary credit, a Commercial Letter of Credit via SWIFT MT700 is permissible. Because the RMI does not operate a domestic RTGS system, USD payments are cleared through the U.S. correspondent network, CHIPS, Fedwire, or ACH/RTP for lower-value items. Every outbound MT103 must include a valid BIC, the beneficiary bank's account, and an appropriate U.S.-destination clearing path; all wires are parsed against OFAC sanctions. For recurring SaaS payouts and processor settlements, standard daily or weekly crypto-like settlement is not available; the RMI account receives aggregated USD settlement directly from Stripe/Wise, then authorizes MT103 payments to the operating vendor set.Plain-vanilla currency forwards (USD/EUR, USD/GBP, USD/AUD, USD/JPY) executed with a licensed international bank or correspondent bankNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for illiquid or restricted emerging-market currencies where physical delivery is impracticalCross-currency swaps (legally covered by ISDA) for long-term balance-sheet hedging of non-USD SaaS revenue or vendor liabilitiesPlain-vanilla FX options and collars purchased from a licensed bank for cash-flow protection without leverage

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: No compulsory statutory audit applies to a non-resident domestic Business Corporation under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act 1990. No revenue, asset, share-capital, or employee threshold triggers a mandatory audit where the entity is privately held, not listed on a recognized securities exchange, and derives no income from sources within the Marshall Islands. An audit becomes mandatory only if the corporation is admitted to listing on a securities exchange or if its articles of incorporation, by-laws, or a binding shareholder agreement expressly imposes an independent audit requirement.
