Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Office (Ministry of Finance, Government of the Republic of the Marshall Islands); filings are administered at the central office in Majuro and through licensed registered agents. No separate nationwide electronic tax filing portal is in active commercial operation.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation must first be incorporated by a licensed RMI registered agent at the Marshall Islands Registry of Corporations, which issues a statutory corporate registration number. Because the entity is classified as a Tax_Exempt_Zone non-resident corporation and does not derive RMI-source income, no tax identification number or VAT identification number is required at formation. If a future RMI tax liability arises, for example through hiring RMI-resident employees or earning RMI-source income, the company must submit its Certificate of Incorporation, corporate register details, and registration forms to the Revenue and Taxation Office to obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number for income tax, social security, and health fund purposes. No VAT ID exists in the RMI.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident Business Corporation must first be incorporated by a licensed RMI registered agent at the Marshall Islands Registry of Corporations, which issues a statutory corporate registration number. No VAT ID exists in the RMI.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

RMI law does not provide a U.S.-style check-the-box election, an elective pass-through classification, or hybrid-mismatch election rules. A non-resident domestic Business Corporation is automatically tax-exempt with respect to RMI income tax while it performs no activities in the RMI and derives no RMI-source income. No affirmative election or annual tax filing is required to preserve this status. Shareholders may voluntarily obtain a tax-residency certificate for banking or treaty purposes, but such a certificate does not alter the entity's statutory Tax_Exempt_Zone classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales/use tax. The RMI's indirect fiscal regime consists exclusively of customs import duty under MIRC Title 44 and limited municipal licensing charges.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold applies, as the RMI does not operate a turnover-based indirect tax system. The official currency is the United States dollar (USD). Cross-border express courier operators acting as importers of record or bonded carriers must register with the Marshall Islands Customs Service for customs entry and clearance purposes, but this registration is based on activity type rather than turnover and has no de minimis threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable, as no VAT or GST is chargeable in the RMI. Exports of physical goods and cross-border express courier services originating from the RMI fall outside the scope of RMI indirect taxation; there is no 0% VAT rate to claim and no VAT refund mechanism. Express courier operators may nevertheless use customs duty suspension or relief regimes for imported operational equipment, aircraft waybills, packaging materials, and sorting machinery; those measures are customs-duty concessions and are not export zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration / outward cargo manifest filed with the Marshall Islands Customs Service
  • Commercial invoice or consolidated parcel-value manifest
  • Packing list, including consolidated dimensional weight manifest
  • Master Air Waybill / House Air Waybill, and Bill of Lading or multimodal transport document when sea carriage is used
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by an RMI banking counterpart where documentary proof of non-RMI-source service revenue is required by banks or foreign tax authorities
  • Operator license and customs bond reference in the name of the licensed express carrier

None. The Republic of the Marshall Islands has not enacted any national electronic invoicing mandate, real-time fiscal clearance requirement, continuous transaction control platform, API-connectivity obligation, or digital stamping system. Shipment manifests and export declarations are submitted in paper or electronic format for customs control without any additional fiscal digital certification.

The RMI does not impose VAT clawback because no export zero-rating regime exists. However, any false export declaration, inaccurate cargo manifest, or misuse of customs duty relief may subject the operator to Marshall Islands Customs Act enforcement, including administrative penalties, forfeiture of goods and carrier equipment, revocation of the express courier license and customs bond, and criminal sanctions for smuggling or fraud comprising fines and imprisonment according to the severity of the offence. Duty-free import concessions used in courier operations may also be retrospectively clawed back, with statutory interest, where the Commissioner of Revenue determines disqualifying use.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

De jure and de facto full convertibility: the RMI maintains zero exchange-control requirements on current-account or capital-account transactions, no profit-remittance licensing, and no separate domestic FX market. A non-resident Business Corporation may freely repatriate dividends, repay shareholder loans, and distribute liquidation proceeds in USD; practical frictions are limited to US OFAC/FinCEN screening, FATCA/CRS reporting, and jurisdiction-risk pricing arising from the RMI's listing on the EU Annex I list of non-cooperative jurisdictions. The treasury model is therefore USD-native, with no conversion gate at the RMI bank layer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI statute imposes no surrender deadline, no fixed conversion ratio, and no export-receipts escrow for service income. Cross-border express courier proceeds collected via credit-card POS settlement, ACH Direct sweeps, or SWIFT MT103 credits may be retained indefinitely in the operational USD account; the only administrative obligations are accurate books and the annual registry fee filings applicable to non-resident domestic corporations under the Business Corporations Act.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Ministry of Finance, Banking Commission — the statutory banking supervisor acting through the Banking Commissioner under the Banking Act 1991 (as amended). The RMI has no domestic central bank, discount window, or foreign-exchange authority; because the US dollar is the sole official legal tender under the Compact of Free Association, the US Federal Reserve's clearing and monetary-policy framework operates as the de facto central-bank environment for all USD-denominated treasury flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% flat corporate income tax (CIT) under the Income Tax Act 1989, as amended, on taxable income of corporations outside the non-resident exemption; 0% effective tax rate for a qualifying Non-Resident Business Corporation (BC) exempt under Section 107 of the Business Corporations Act 1990 (BCA).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. The Marshall Islands imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Business Corporation, whether distributed as an interim dividend, final dividend, or liquidation distribution, and regardless of the residence or nationality of the shareholder.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on the anniversary date of incorporation in each calendar year; no extension is available. Payment of the annual franchise fee is a precondition to the filing being accepted.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing is due within the period prescribed by the RMI Tax Office for the tax year, generally within four months after the close of the corporation's fiscal year-end; the RMI Tax Office may prescribe a uniform calendar-year deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable: the RMI Income Tax Act contains no transfer pricing documentation legislation, and no Local File or Master File threshold is imposed on a non-resident tax-exempt BC. The operative threshold is territorial: all income of the BC derived outside the RMI is outside the CIT net. For practical multi-jurisdiction compliance, the group must satisfy OECD transfer pricing master file and local file obligations in the operating markets where courier services are performed, with intercompany agreements and functional analyses prepared to the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines standard, particularly where last-mile delivery fees, hub handling charges, and technology royalties are settled among affiliates.

Economic substance scope: The RMI does not yet apply a BVI-style Economic Substance Act to non-resident BCs; however, the BCA Section 107 exemption is structured as a territorial tax-exempt zone and requires two substantive conditions: (1) the corporation derives no income from RMI sources, and (2) the commercial substance of the enterprise is located outside the RMI. The mandatory Annual Corporate Report requires a signed declaration of economic presence and commercial domicile. Under OECD Global Forum and EU Code of Conduct Group peer review, an RMI BC acting as a pure booking vehicle for cross-border courier profit, with no employees, premises, or management functions in the operating territory, is rebuttably presumed to be a shell; such presumption triggers foreign CFC, GILTI, and substance-over-form recharacterizations, loss of the RMI exemption, and potential blacklisting consequences for the jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion: Conducting RMI-Source Domestic Courier Business While Claiming the Non-Resident Exemption

Statutory sanction: Retroactive revocation of the non-resident exemption for the offending tax years; full assessment of corporate income tax at 20% on all undeclared RMI-source income; default interest at 2% per month; an evasion penalty of up to 100% of the tax deficiency; forfeiture of the right to transact business in the RMI; and reference to criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Act 1989.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and the registered agent who executed or approved the false declaration are jointly and severally liable for the assessed tax, interest, and penalties under corporate veil-piercing principles; the RMI may issue a departure/travel ban against the directors; on criminal conviction, directors face imprisonment and monetary fines.
Shell Company Abuse and Sham Substance Structure

Statutory sanction: At the RMI level: administrative dissolution by the Registrar for sham particulars, rejection of the Annual Corporate Report, and withdrawal of the certificate of good standing. At the foreign level: recharacterization of the BC as a resident or permanent establishment, mandatory CFC/GILTI inclusions, transfer pricing adjustments, denial of treaty and foreign tax credit protections, and anti-avoidance penalties of up to 100% of the tax recomputed in the operating jurisdictions.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal civil restitution claims in foreign courts and in liquidation proceedings for fraudulent transfer and wrongful trading; promoters of the sham structure are exposed to conspiracy and tax-shelter promotion prosecutions; international travel restrictions and extradition requests may be pursued in egregious cross-border abuse cases.
AML/CFT Failures on Enhanced-Risk Courier Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Enforcement under the RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act 2020 and the National Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Risk Assessment framework: administrative monetary penalties against the BC of up to USD 1,000,000 per violation; license suspension or revocation of the statutory courier operating license; seizure of assets under mutual legal assistance; and blacklisting from correspondent banking settlement rails.

Director personal liability: Directors, beneficial owners, compliance officers, and Money Laundering Reporting Officers face personal criminal prosecution, fines of up to USD 500,000, and imprisonment of up to 15 years; additionally, the RMI Registrar may disqualify the directors from serving on any RMI-incorporated entity, their personal assets may be frozen, and re-entry to the RMI is barred.
False Statements to the Registrar and Fraudulent Exemption Claims

Statutory sanction: Cancellation of the corporate registration and certificate of good standing; administrative strike-off and dissolution without the benefit of winding-up protections; imposition of reinstatement penalties and a fresh incorporation requirement; criminal liability for false corporate filings under the Business Corporations Act; and revocation of the tax-exempt status ab initio.
