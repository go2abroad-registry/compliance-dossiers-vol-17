Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance, Banking and International Trade, Republic of the Marshall Islands, Majuro; taxpayer services are conducted in person or through a licensed resident agent, with no publicly accessible electronic filing portal.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation (BC) is not mandatorily registered for Marshall Islands income tax or VAT. If a TIN is required for banking, contracting, or foreign tax purposes, the BC must submit an application through its appointed resident agent using the prescribed government form, together with the certificate of incorporation, registered agent acceptance, directors' and shareholders' particulars, and a declaration that no RMI-source business activity is conducted. The Revenue Commissioner issues a tax identification number; no VAT identifier exists because the RMI levies no value added tax.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully convertible, unrestricted jurisdiction. The U.S. Dollar (USD) is the exclusive legal tender; RMI issues no national currency, so there exist no convertibility restrictions, no capital account controls, no exchange-rate spread, and no mandatory surrender regime. The RMI is an IMF Article VIII member, guaranteeing that payments and transfers for current international transactions are free from restrictions. Capital contributions, profit remittances, dividend distributions, and shareholder loan repayments from a non-resident Business Corporation are neither subject to RMI exchange approval nor to any repatriation tax or withholding tax. The only practical frictions are bank-level AML/KYC verification, OFAC/FATF screening via U.S. correspondent rails, and the requirement that the entity remain in Good Standing with the RMI Registrar. Because the Steam and PlayStation publishing revenue cycle is naturally USD-denominated, conversion needs are limited to optional outsourcing of development services in EUR, GBP, JPY, or KRW, which may be settled freely at negotiated bank rates.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI statutes impose no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no settlement time limit. Digital game export proceeds received from Valve Corporation (Steam) and Sony Interactive Entertainment (PlayStation Store) may be retained indefinitely in USD or in any freely convertible currency. The only formalities are (i) designation of a licensed RMI commercial bank account or U.S. correspondent account, (ii) maintenance of a clean invoice/contract trail under the Steam Works and PlayStation Partner agreements, and (iii) submission of beneficial-ownership attestations for wires exceeding the bank's internal reporting threshold. No central-bank filing or exchange-control declaration is required.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) does not maintain a domestic central bank or a separate foreign-exchange administration. Prudential supervision and AML/CFT oversight of deposit-taking institutions are exercised by the Banking Commissioner, appointed under the Banking Act 1991 and operating within the RMI Ministry of Finance. Given that the U.S. Dollar is the sole legal tender pursuant to RMI constitutional law and the Compact of Free Association, the U.S. Federal Reserve (San Francisco) functions as the de facto monetary and payments authority, while local dollar clearing is intermediated through U.S. correspondent banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low viability. The RMI has no e-money or EMI licensing regime, so no local EMI authorization exists, and offshore digital EMI providers (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Revolut Business) generally reject or restrict RMI-registered entities because of jurisdictional risk policy and the absence of an EMI passporting arrangement. A non-resident BC should therefore maintain its statutory USD transaction account with an RMI-licensed commercial bank (BOMI or Bank of Guam) or a U.S. correspondent bank. If an offshore EMI is used solely as a segregated pay-in collection vehicle for Steam/PlayStation balances, the EMI must (i) expressly permit RMI corporate clients, (ii) be limited to B2B platform settlements rather than consumer deposit-taking, and (iii) be swept daily to the licensed RMI/USD account. The entity's commercial risk tier is 'Low_Risk_Standard' under the pipeline classification, which supports this two-tier structure, but the EMI route remains legally fragile and operationally secondary.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant route: inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital is injected by SWIFT MT103 (or Fedwire for U.S.-based remitters) in USD to the non-resident BC's account at BOMI or Bank of Guam. Because RMI imposes no capital controls, no central-bank approval, no filing, and no conversion requirement is triggered. Protocol: (i) the BC board of directors adopts a capital injection resolution recorded with the registered agent; (ii) the remitting parent/shareholder issues wire instructions using the bank's internal purpose code for 'equity injection' or 'intercompany loan' (SWIFT transaction type field 50F/56A fully populated for OFAC screening); (iii) for injections above USD 100,000, the bank's compliance team receives a copy of the transfer instruction, a source-of-funds statement, and the remitter's bank statement evidencing lawful derivation; (iv) the BC records the funds as paid-in capital or shareholder loan in its corporate books; and (v) if a Capital Escrow Account is used, funds are held in a segregated sub-account subject to milestone release conditions defined in the board resolution.Digital publishing outbound flows comprise (i) royalty and license payments to developer studios (SWIFT MT103, USD or cross-currency), (ii) platform fees to Valve/Sony, which in practice are net-settled by the store before payout to the entity, (iii) vendor payments for marketing and localization services (Commercial LC under UCP600 or SWIFT MT103), and (iv) dividend and shareholder loan repayments (SWIFT MT103 with appropriate payment details). Clearing rails: U.S. correspondent banks provide Fedwire/CHIPS clearing for USD-denominated flows; SWIFT MT103 is used for cross-border payments involving EUR, GBP, JPY, KRW, and other currencies; Commercial Letters of Credit are reserved for milestone-based co-publishing agreements and any physical-goods procurement (e.g., localized console packaging or promotional merchandise). The absence of a domestic RTGS system means all clearing is intermediated via the U.S. clearing system through BOMI and Bank of Guam correspondent relationships. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Marshall Islands transfer-pricing statute fixes any Local File or Master File submission threshold; the zero-tax regime produces no transfer-pricing liability. Nevertheless, the ESR Annual Notification and substantiation obligations apply irrespective of revenue thresholds to any relevant entity, including non-resident business corporations holding IP or performing distribution/service functions for digital video-game publishing; the operator must maintain contemporaneous substance and accounting evidence (local board minutes, bank records, and expenditure) in the Marshall Islands.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (as amended) require every relevant entity carrying on relevant activity - expressly capturing IP ownership, distribution and service centers, and headquarters - to file an economic substance notification and an annual substantiation report with the Registrar. Although the MH_BC remains tax-exempt, core income-generating activities must be managed and directed in the Marshall Islands, with adequate premises, full-time employees, and operating expenditure proportionate to gaming publishing revenue; mobile game/digital-settlement functions cannot be designated to a post-box.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Filing

Statutory sanction: Under the Business Corporations Act and the ESR Act: fine up to USD 10,000 per false filing, administrative dissolution, forfeiture of the certificate of good standing, and court-ordered disgorgement of misdeclared profits for the relevant years.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly sign false declarations are personally liable for restitution; criminal conviction carries imprisonment up to 5 years, with asset freeze and travel ban issued by the Attorney-General pending trial.
Shell Company Structure and ESR Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: ESR enforcement: monetary penalty for the entity of up to USD 10,000 plus a daily surcharge for continued non-compliance; potential strike-off and adverse listing with the Registrar that blocks the company from executing digital-store agreements.

Director personal liability: Directors continuing trade after strike-off become personally liable for the company's debts; the court may pierce the veil, allow creditor claims against personal estates, and impose imprisonment up to 1 year for operating an unauthorized entity; travel restrictions can be applied in cross-border insolvency assistance.
Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: Under the Marshall Islands AML/CFT Regulations and Banks Act: administrative fines up to USD 50,000 per violation, revocation of any money-services license, freezing of transaction accounts, and disgorgement of unlawful proceeds.

Director personal liability: Responsible officers face civil forfeiture of assets, personal financial penalties, travel bans under mutual legal assistance requests, and imprisonment up to 10 years for money-laundering predicates; the corporate veil is pierced for knowing participation.
Mischaracterized Cross-Border Distribution and Unauthorized Money Transmission

Statutory sanction: Business Licensing Regulations: fine up to USD 5,000, license revocation, and refusal of Registrar authentication; tax authorities in the payer jurisdiction may re-characterize the arrangement and impose gross-basis or branch-profit taxes.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve deceptive invoicing are jointly and severally liable for claw-back claims and stuck interest at the statutory judgment rate; foreign judgments are enforceable against their personal assets, and nationals/non-resident directors can be stopped at the border upon issuance of a criminal complaint.
