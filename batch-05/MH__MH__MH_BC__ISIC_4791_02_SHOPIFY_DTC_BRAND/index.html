Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Business Registry Division, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, Majuro Atoll; filings are presented directly or through a duly licensed Marshall Islands registered agent.

Governing statute

Business Corporations Act of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (B.C.A., modelled on the Delaware General Corporation Law; codified under the Marshall Islands Revised Code), applicable sections 29 (Articles of Incorporation; contents and execution), 33 (registered office and registered agent), 39 (annual report and franchise tax) and the Registrar's published fee schedule.

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation (Section 29 B.C.A.), together with the registered agent's consent; the Registrar issues the official Certificate of Incorporation evidencing the creation of the legal entity.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 12 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow account requirement: the Corporation may register with authorized capital of 5,000 shares without par value, and subscriber consideration may consist of cash, services, or property. Capital is fully paid and nonassessable upon the board's declaration.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable: the webstore has no physical premises, employees, or stored inventory within the Republic, so no fire safety certification, structural load inspection, or workplace clearance is required. If the Corporation opens an office or warehouse in Majuro, it must obtain a fire code certificate from the Majuro emergency/fire authority, a structural completion inspection from the relevant buildings and land-use authority, and workplace health-and-safety clearance under domestic labor law before occupancy.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow account requirement: the Corporation may register with authorized capital of 5,000 shares without par value, and subscriber consideration may consist of cash, services, or property. Capital is fully paid and nonassessable upon the board's declaration.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable: the statutory business licensing regime of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (Business License Act, Ministry of Finance; Foreign Investment Business License Act for foreign-controlled entities) applies only where a business is carried on within the territorial jurisdiction. The Shopify DTC webstore conducts all sales, logistics, and payment processing abroad and therefore triggers no domestic business license.

Category: Not required for the non-resident webstore. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Office (RTO), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; official correspondence via email and physical filing to the RTO, with no fully automated national tax filing portal.

E-government filing portal

Non-Resident Business Corporations are exempt from Marshall Islands income tax and are not statutorily required to obtain a TIN. If a TIN is desired for banking, customs brokerage, or contracting counterparty verification, the foreign entity (through its registered agent) must submit to the RTO a TIN application form together with a certified copy of the Articles of Incorporation, the Non-Resident Corporation certificate, and evidence of the registered agent appointment. The RTO issues a TIN without creating any tax filing obligation or domestic tax nexus.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-Resident Business Corporations are exempt from Marshall Islands income tax and are not statutorily required to obtain a TIN. If a TIN is desired for banking, customs brokerage, or contracting counterparty verification, the foreign entity (through its registered agent) must submit to the RTO a TIN application form together with a certified copy of the Articles of Incorporation, the Non-Resident Corporation certificate, and evidence of the registered agent appointment. The RTO issues a TIN without creating any tax filing obligation or domestic tax nexus.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act, a foreign-owned corporation may elect to operate as a Non-Resident Business Corporation by expressly stating its non-resident status in the Articles of Incorporation and by refraining from carrying on business within the Marshall Islands. This election is recorded with the Registrar of Corporations and confers a comprehensive exemption from Marshall Islands income tax, gross revenue tax, franchise tax, and all other territorial taxes. The jurisdiction does not provide a check-the-box classification regime similar to the United States, and hybrid mismatch elections are not recognized under Marshall Islands tax law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST or general sales tax; the Marshall Islands imposes a Gross Revenue Tax on businesses engaged in domestic business activity, but non-resident exempt corporations are outside its scope.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because no such tax exists. For the limited Gross Revenue Tax, any person engaged in business within the Marshall Islands must register with the RTO irrespective of turnover; no threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — the Marshall Islands levies no VAT, GST, or sales tax, and therefore contains no zero-rating mechanism for exports. Export of goods or services by a non-resident Business Corporation is tax-exempt and outside the scope of domestic gross revenue tax; no refund procedure is necessary.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API-based reporting, or digital stamping requirement exists in the Marshall Islands. The RTO does not operate an e-invoicing or clearance platform.

No VAT zero-rating clawback regime exists. If a non-resident corporation is found to have engaged in domestic business within the Marshall Islands without proper registration, the RTO may assess Gross Revenue Tax, interest, and penalties. Knowingly submitting false statements to the RTO or Customs may attract monetary fines and, in egregious cases, criminal sanctions. There is no statutory export-credit VAT clawback mechanism.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Because the U.S. dollar is the sole circulating and legal currency, there is no domestic currency to convert. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully open and are not subject to exchange-control approval. Profit remittance, dividend payments, and capital repatriation may proceed without central-bank consent or FX licensing. The only practical hurdles are bank-level AML/KYC documentary safeguards, board resolutions, and source-of-funds verification required by correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender rule, mandatory repatriation timeline, or compulsory conversion ratio in the Marshall Islands. Export proceeds may remain in U.S. dollars in any licensed domestic bank account or in offshore accounts of the business corporation. No RMI Government exchange-control filing is required.

Central bank supervision: None - the Republic of the Marshall Islands does not maintain a central bank; the U.S. dollar is legal tender, monetary policy is set by the U.S. Federal Reserve, and the Marshall Islands Commissioner of Banking and Ministry of Finance supervise licensed financial institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital payment institutions such as Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, and Stripe Treasury are commercially available to an RMI non-resident Business Corporation and may be used for customer collections or supplier disbursements, but they do not constitute RMI banking licenses. RMI AML/CFT law treats unauthorized deposit-taking as restricted; therefore a licensed domestic settlement bank remains necessary for capital injection, statutory records, and corporate treasury integrity. Foreign EMIs must be fully licensed in their home jurisdiction, must comply with U.S. OFAC and UN sanctions screening, and cannot offer a domestic RMI bank account or clear USD through the domestic banking system.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For an RMI non-resident Business Corporation, the recommended rail is a U.S. dollar transfer from the shareholder's or ultimate beneficial owner's bank to the RMI bank's USD correspondent account via SWIFT MT103 or U.S. Fedwire/CHIPS. RMI law imposes no foreign-exchange approval obligation and no statutory purpose-code regime. The bank will, however, require accurate remittance information in field 70, including the words 'Equity Capital Contribution' or 'Foreign Direct Investment', the borrower or issuer reference, the Corporate ID or account number, and a cross-reference to the subscription agreement and board resolution. The full source-of-wealth and corporate KYC dossier must accompany the first injection, and the same correspondent bank will conduct OFAC, UN, and interdict screening. Upon completion of the screening and compliance sign-off, funds are released to the Capital Escrow Account, then re-classified to the Operational Account after the share issuance is recorded.Outbound trade disbursements for inventory, logistics, and supplier payments should use SWIFT MT103 for single supplier invoices, MT202 for bank-to-bank transfers, MT700 for confirmed commercial letters of credit, or U.S. Fedwire for same-day USD settlement. Commercial letters of credit are appropriate for high-value goods with shipping documentation, using required documents such as invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, certificate of origin, and any inspection certificate. The RMI bank will rely on New York or Los Angeles correspondent/US clearing intermediaries; therefore payment finality is governed by U.S. clearing house rules. Where the beneficiary is located in a jurisdiction with currency controls, the USD rail remains valid and settlement is in USD without RMI FX conversion. All disbursements must reference the underlying commercial invoice and be supported by the transaction dossier to avoid a trade-pass-through AML trigger.USD/USD treasury instruments are not required because the RMI has no domestic currency and the operating currency is USD.Offshore deliverable foreign-exchange forwards for EUR, GBP, and AUD expense exposures, contracted through a foreign licensed bank with the RMI corporation as principal.Cross-currency swaps and participating FX swaps for multi-year non-USD procurement contracts, executed outside RMI jurisdiction under common-law contract capacity.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for volatile customer-facing currencies such as IDR, INR, and PHP, settled in USD, provided the counterparty is a licensed offshore bank and the hedge is documented by board resolution.Vanilla FX options on non-USD exposures, approved by the board and recorded in the corporate treasury register.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (the Republic of the Marshall Islands levies no general corporate income tax on non-resident Business Corporations; the RMI tax system is strictly territorial and an exempt BC's foreign-source income falls outside the tax base)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (the Republic of the Marshall Islands does not impose dividend withholding tax or any other tax on distributions paid by a Business Corporation to its shareholders; outbound profit remittances from the Shopify DTC brand are free of RMI withholding tax)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For an exempt Business Corporation, no income tax return is due. The annual renewal and exemption statement must be filed no later than the anniversary date of incorporation each year. If RMI-source taxable income were unintentionally triggered, the income tax return is due within four months after the end of the tax year, i.e. by 30 April.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable (USD): the Marshall Islands Income Tax Act does not adopt transfer-pricing documentation rules and does not impose mandatory Local File/Master File thresholds. As a defensive governance standard, management should maintain an internal pricing memorandum for intercompany transactions equal to or exceeding USD 10 million in consolidated turnover to substantiate arm's-length pricing before foreign tax authorities and CFC regimes.

Economic substance scope: The Republic of the Marshall Islands does not apply EU/Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) to ordinary non-resident Business Corporations. Nevertheless, foreign jurisdictions applying controlled-foreign-company, anti-havens or sham-transaction doctrines will expect genuine economic substance: a physical registered agent in Majuro, current board and accounting records, a transactional bank account, real e-commerce contracts, and evidence that commercial decisions are made outside the RMI. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false foreign-source declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act (Cap. 24) and the Criminal Code of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, tax fraud is a statutory offence punishable by a fine of up to USD 10,000, treble assessment of the evaded tax, statutory negligence and wilful penalties (50% late-payment penalty, 100% negligence penalty, 200% wilful penalty), and imprisonment of up to one year. The Registrar may also suspend, revoke or strike off the certificate of good standing.

Director personal liability: Directors, registered agents and beneficial owners who sign or approve a false exemption declaration are subject to civil restitution for the evaded tax, personal assessment, travel bans, asset seizure, criminal prosecution and imprisonment where fraud is proven. The corporate veil is pierced because the fraudulent act is outside the legitimate corporate functions.
Shell company misuse and lack of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Business Corporation Act, failure to maintain a registered office, registered agent, annual accounts, and exemption records exposes the BC to monetary fines of up to USD 5,000, administrative strike-off, loss of limited liability, and judicial dissolution. Foreign tax authorities may reclassify the full DTC profit as attributable to the market jurisdiction and impose source-country income tax, VAT, penalties, and interest.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who authorize the continued operation of a defunct or sham corporation are jointly and severally liable for the company's debts, consumer refunds, and vendor claims. Civil restitution orders may reach personal assets of directors and shareholders where corporate formalities were abandoned or the entity was used as an instrument of fraud.
AML and beneficial-ownership evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism Act 2011, as amended, money laundering is punishable by a fine of up to USD 500,000 or imprisonment up to 20 years, or both, plus forfeiture of criminal proceeds. Non-compliance with RMI beneficial-ownership disclosure requirements attracts fines up to USD 10,000, deregistration of the Business Corporation, and reporting to regional FATF-style bodies.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners engaged in AML evasion face personal criminal liability, asset forfeiture, travel bans, and imprisonment. Courts may pierce the corporate veil and order full personal restitution against foreign shareholder-principals where the company was used as a vehicle for money laundering or payment fraud.
CRS/AEOI information deficiency

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Tax Information Exchange Agreement and Automatic Exchange of Information framework, penalties of USD 1,000 per omitted report and daily penalties of USD 100 per day of continued non-compliance apply. Repeated violations lead to revocation of the BC registration and referral to the OECD Global Forum for punitive listing.

Director personal liability: The RMI Registrar and Financial Intelligence Unit may impose fines directly on the registered agent and directors. Persistent non-cooperation triggers contempt proceedings, civil restitution, travel restrictions, seizure of RMI-located assets, and imprisonment of up to 12 months for responsible officers.
