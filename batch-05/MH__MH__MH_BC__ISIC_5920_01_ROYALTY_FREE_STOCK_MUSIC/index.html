Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Administration (RTA), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands, with the Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General for the corporate register. There is no dedicated taxpayer e-portal; RTA administers the Income Tax Act, the Business License Act, and tax-clearance certificates via paper and email filings (rmi government portal: https://rmigovernment.org).

E-government filing portal

Non-resident foreign-owned Business Corporations are not issued a TIN. The statutory procedure for an exempt non-resident BC is: (1) file Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act (Delaware-derived); (2) pay the annual registration/franchise fee to the Registrar; (3) submit a Business License Application to RTA electing the Non-Resident Exempt category; (4) pay the fixed annual business license fee; and (5) obtain the license and, upon request, a tax clearance certificate confirming no RMI-source income. While the entity derives no Marshall Islands-source income, no income tax registration, no income tax returns, and no TIN are required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-resident foreign-owned Business Corporations are not issued a TIN. The statutory procedure for an exempt non-resident BC is: (1) file Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act (Delaware-derived); (2) pay the annual registration/franchise fee to the Registrar; (3) submit a Business License Application to RTA electing the Non-Resident Exempt category; (4) pay the fixed annual business license fee; and (5) obtain the license and, upon request, a tax clearance certificate confirming no RMI-source income. While the entity derives no Marshall Islands-source income, no income tax registration, no income tax returns, and no TIN are required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid-mismatch, or entity-classification elections exist under Marshall Islands law. A Non-Resident Business Corporation is presumptively tax-exempt when all income is foreign-sourced. The only operative election is the statutory choice to apply for, and maintain, the non-resident exempt business license; engaging in local trade or business without that election would convert the entity into a partially taxable RMI-source filer. No partnership or disregarded-entity elections are recognized for RMI tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax is imposed; the only indirect levy is customs import duty on physical goods under the Customs and Excise Act)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A (USD) - the Marshall Islands has no VAT/GST/sales tax, therefore no economic turnover threshold exists for indirect tax registration; any local business activity, regardless of revenue, merely requires a business license under the Business License Act 1981 and does not trigger any consumption tax liability.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because the Republic of the Marshall Islands does not levy VAT or GST, the concept of a 0% export VAT rate and export VAT refund is inapplicable. Instead, all revenue from royalty-free stock music licensing and commercial soundscape audio subscriptions qualifies for categorical exemption from RMI tax when the income is derived from sources outside the Marshall Islands, the licensee is non-resident, and the entity holds a valid Non-Resident Exempt business license; no output tax is chargeable and no input tax refund mechanism exists or is needed.

  • No customs export declaration or bill of lading is required for intangible audio-licensing exports, as the Customs and Excise Act applies only to physical goods
  • Executed stock music license agreements and end-user license agreements (ISIC 5920 / NAICS 512240 records)
  • Digital distribution platform payout reports and Stripe/PayPal account statements evidencing foreign-source consideration
  • RTA Business License (Non-Resident Exempt) and Registrar-issued certificate of good standing
  • Board resolutions and ownership records confirming non-resident status and absence of RMI-source revenue

No electronic invoicing, continuous transaction control, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime exists. Issuance of conventional sequentially-numbered commercial invoices or PDF/Stripe-generated receipts is sufficient; no government API integration, pre-clearance approval, or digital tax stamp is required for royalty-free stock music licensing or audio sample subscriptions.

There is no export zero-rating clawback regime to enforce because no VAT/GST is levied. The analogous risk is RTA audit recharacterization of claimed foreign-source licensing income as Marshall Islands-source. Penalties include: revocation of the exempt business license; assessment of back corporate income tax at the 3% rate applicable to RMI-source income of a domestic corporation; statutory interest accrual on unpaid assessments; late-filing and negligence penalties up to 25% of the tax due; and, in cases of intentional misrepresentation, monetary fines and recovery of underpaid license fees. Documentary substantiation must therefore be retained for at least six years.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Marshall Islands has no exchange-control law and no separate domestic currency against which conversion could be restricted; current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible because the circulating medium is USD itself. The RMI imposes no income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or profit remittances paid by a non-resident Business Corporation, and no advance-approval, repatriation, or utilization requirement applies to foreign-source licensing revenue. Practical hurdles are limited to bank-level AML/KYC evidence of source of funds and FATCA/CRS classification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The RMI maintains no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, retention-period, or licensing requirement for export proceeds or service-export receipts. Cross-border royalty income from the commercial soundscape licensing marketplace may be retained offshore, swept to U.S. clearing nodes, or disbursed directly to foreign producers and licensors without regulatory filing or approval.

Central bank supervision: No indigenous central bank exists. The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender pursuant to the Compact of Free Association, and monetary conditions are therefore governed by the U.S. Federal Reserve. Banking and foreign-exchange-adjacent supervision is vested in the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands, acting under the Banking Act 1980 (as amended); the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) administers AML/CFT supervision.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Share capital and pre-funded working capital are wired in USD via SWIFT MT103 from the beneficial owner's own account at a recognized bank into the escrow/sweep sub-account. Field 50K must show the remitter name exactly matching KYC records; Field 70 must cite 'SHARE CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION PURSUANT TO BOARD RESOLUTION DATED [DATE]' or 'DIRECTOR LOAN UNDER FACILITY AGREEMENT DATED [DATE]'. The bank requires the board resolution and shareholder register in advance and internal-codes the credit as equity or loan to support capital-account reconciliation; injections above USD 50,000 typically trigger enhanced source-of-funds review (tax returns and asset statements) and a two-to-five business day clearing hold.Producer and label royalty payouts: (i) high-value or correspondent-cleared payments are made via SWIFT MT103 through the bank's U.S. correspondent chain; (ii) low-value monthly producer pools are aggregated and remitted via mass-payment APIs (Wise Business, Tipalti, or Payoneer) from the U.S. nexus LLC, or via ACH where counterparties hold U.S. accounts; (iii) commercial letters of credit are not applicable because music-licensing revenue is a service/intangibles flow, not documentary goods trade. All outbound beneficiaries must be pre-screened against OFAC/EU consolidated lists, and a no-third-party-beneficiary policy must be enforced in the payout registry.Deliverable USD/EUR, USD/GBP, and USD/AUD spot-and-forward contracts (1M/3M tenors) executed with a top-tier U.S. or EU counterparty bank to fix producer payment currencies; permitted without RMI regulatory approval because USD is the home currency and RMI law imposes no derivatives restriction.Cross-currency swaps under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement, permissible and prudent only where multi-year producer advance or catalogue-acquisition contracts create material long-dated non-USD liabilities.Natural-hedge multi-currency operating accounts (multi-currency balances via the U.S. nexus or a Wise Business edge) to offset EUR/GBP payables against receivables pockets where contracts permit.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are legally permissible offshore but commercially unnecessary and disfavored given the full deliverability, depth, and liquidity of the USD side of all relevant currency pairs.

Wise, Airwallex, Mercury, and U.S. neo-banks (Brex, Relay, Column) will not onboard the Marshall Islands business corporation directly; legal viability is achieved by interposing a U.S. single-member LLC (check-the-box, disregarded) owned by the MH BC to hold the Stripe/PayPal settlement accounts and a U.S. fintech treasury account, with onward remittance to the MH BC by intercompany transfer. Structural constraint: the U.S. LLC must be documented as an active NFFE under FATCA, must complete U.S. beneficial-ownership (FinCEN BOI) filing, and should be supported by a U.S. tax opinion confirming no unintended U.S. trade-or-business or withholding exposure.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC) exemption: the Marshall Islands imposes no corporate income tax on foreign-source income of a Business Corporation. Incidental RMI-source business income (if any) would be taxable at the 3% standard corporate rate under the Income Tax Act 1989 (as amended).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the Republic of the Marshall Islands levies no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Business Corporation to its shareholders, whether resident or non-resident, and no undistributed-profits tax applies.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 0 / not applicable - the RMI has no domestic transfer-pricing legislation and prescribes no Local File or Master File thresholds. Contemporaneous substance and pricing records are nevertheless mandatory for all licensing revenue under the Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2024; the annual ESR declaration must be filed irrespective of revenue level.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2024, issued under the Business Corporations Act, apply to financial years commencing on or after 26 February 2024. A Business Corporation carrying on the Relevant Activity of Intellectual Property Business - which encompasses the exploitation and licensing of copyrighted audio works (ISIC 5920) - must demonstrate: (i) management and control exercised with adequate physical premises in the RMI; (ii) adequate, proportionate staff with appropriate qualifications; and (iii) core income-generating activities (CIGA) performed, and income earned, in a jurisdiction where the company is subject to tax. Where the licensing activity is actually operated abroad, the company must invoke the 'reasonable alternative jurisdiction' route by proving tax residence and substantive operations in an OECD-compliant jurisdiction; otherwise the registrar will impose ESR penalties and may notify the source-state tax authority.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / NRDC Exemption Misuse (Income Tax Act 1989, as amended)

Statutory sanction: Additions to tax of up to 100% of the amount evaded, fixed penalties of up to USD 25,000 per offense, cancellation of the exemption certificate, and civil recovery proceedings by the Attorney General.

Director personal liability: Joint and several liability for evaded tax and penalties; travel bans; and imprisonment for up to five years for willful evasion.
Shell Company / Sham Substance Evasion (ESR 2024 and BCA 1990)

Statutory sanction: ESR corrective notices; financial penalties up to USD 50,000 per assessment year plus USD 1,000 per month of continuing default; strike-off of the BC and onward notification to the source-state tax authority.

Director personal liability: Personal fines, disqualification orders of up to ten years, civil liability for taxes assessed in the substance jurisdiction, and criminal prosecution of officers who certified false ESR declarations.
AML/CFT Breach (Financial Transactions Reporting Act 2000, as amended)

Statutory sanction: Entity fines up to USD 1,000,000 per violation, freezing and forfeiture of funds, revocation of the registered agent's license or the BC's registration, and exclusion from using RMI banking corridors.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal fines, travel bans, excluded-person orders, and imprisonment of up to ten years in aggravated cases.
Misrepresentation to Foreign Withholding Agents / Tax Information Exchange

Statutory sanction: Foreign penalties: US backup withholding plus a 75% civil fraud penalty on under-withheld amounts; EU GAAR/DAC6 penalties; cancellation of the RMI good-standing certificate and listing on the registrar's non-compliant entities register.

Director personal liability: Cross-border enforcement and extradition exposure in treaty states; personal judgments for the under-withheld amount plus interest where the director signed the false certificate.
Director Misfeasance / Trading While Dissolved (BCA 1990)

Statutory sanction: Directors become personally liable for all debts incurred after dissolution; restoration requires a court order with full arrears and penalties; the court may order restitution and disqualification.

Director personal liability: Personal civil judgments and charging orders on beneficial shareholdings; disqualification from acting as a director of any RMI entity; in fraud cases, custodial sentences.
