Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent corporate registry

Business Registrar, Corporations Registry, Ministry of Finance, Majuro, Republic of the Marshall Islands; filings are effected exclusively through a licensed Marshall Islands Registered Agent.

Governing statute

Business Corporations Act ('BCA'), Marshall Islands Revised Statutes (MIRS), Title 51, Chapter 1, as amended, specifically Sections 51.151 (Articles of Incorporation), 51.701 (Registered Agent), and 51.851 (Organization Meeting / First Board of Directors).

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation (Form BCA-2), submitted with the written Consent of Registered Agent (Form BCA-3), and issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation by the Registrar.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; no escrow or capital-blocking rule applies. Authorized capital may be nominal (e.g., USD 1 paid-in against par value) and consideration may be cash, property, or services under the BCA. Annual franchise tax of USD 400 applies to non-resident corporations.

Funding & unlock path: Not mandatory for the non-resident entity. If a Majuro office is opened, the following clearances apply in the following order: fire safety inspection from the Majuro Fire Department and RMI National Disaster Management Office; workplace registration and occupational health compliance under the RMI Occupational Health and Safety Act 2007, administered by the Ministry of Health and Human Services; and structural load inspection for any server or storage rooms. None of these clearances is required for the offshore, cloud-hosted SaaS operation.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance of the Republic of the Marshall Islands — Revenue and Taxation Division (RTD), Majuro; corporate registry filings: Office of the Registrar of Corporations, RMI Registry (https://www.rmioc.com).

E-government filing portal

Incorporate MH_BC as a non-resident Business Corporation under the Business Corporations Act through the Office of the Registrar of Corporations, appointing a licensed RMI registered agent. Because the corporation derives no Marshall Islands-source income and transacts entirely offshore, no TIN, VAT registration, or income-tax file is required by RTD. Annual compliance consists of the Economic Substance Declaration, the registry renewal fee, and maintenance of a Certificate of Good Standing. A TIN and employer registration with the RMI Social Security Administration are required only if the company later employs RMI-resident workers or establishes a physical office in the Republic.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box classification regime exists under RMI law. A Business Corporation is automatically classified as non-resident and tax-exempt under Section 191 of the Business Corporations Act unless it elects, by board resolution and commencement of RMI-source business activity, to be treated as a resident taxable corporation. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available; RMI applies domestic nexus rules and relies on OECD BEPS-compliant economic substance declarations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in force. The indirect tax architecture comprises customs import duties assessed on the CIF value of physical imports (HTS-based ad valorem rates including a general basic rate of approximately 10%, with higher specific rates on alcohol, tobacco, and fuel) and municipal business license fees. Digital SaaS exports by a non-resident corporation fall outside all RMI indirect taxes.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Customs duty liability arises only when physical goods are imported into RMI territory. A municipal business license fee is triggered only upon establishment of a local place of business. A pure offshore MH_BC is subject solely to the annual non-resident corporation registry fee.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

RMI taxation is territorial: a non-resident Business Corporation is exempt from income tax under Section 191 of the Business Corporations Act, and AI avatar video-generation SaaS supplied to non-RMI customers is outside the RMI tax base, attracting no income tax, VAT, or customs duty. No zero-rating claim or refund is required. Eligibility is evidenced by: non-resident classification; management and control exercised outside the RMI; customers located and services consumed outside the RMI; no RMI fixed place of business; and annual economic substance certification. RMI imposes no exchange-control repatriation mandate; the 180-day window below reflects standard sanctions and treasury due-diligence practice.

  • No RMI customs export declaration is required for intangible AI SaaS supplies (no physical goods cross the RMI border)
  • Executed SaaS Master Subscription Agreement confirming offshore delivery of AI video-generation services to non-RMI customers
  • USD-denominated commercial invoice issued by MH_BC, referencing plan, renewal period, and payment-transaction ID
  • Stripe or Paddle settlement statement evidencing receipt of subscription consideration into an offshore bank account
  • Annual RMI Economic Substance Declaration attesting continued non-resident tax-exempt status

No e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital fiscal-authority approval regime exists. Importers lodge a customs entry and certified manifest electronically with RTD Customs; duty is paid on the declared CIF value before release. Invoices for AI SaaS exports require no government clearance and are substantiated by payment-rail settlement records (Stripe/Paddle).

RTD and the RMI economic substance filing authority may audit the exemption. If management and control are found to have been exercised in the Marshall Islands, services are shown to have been supplied to or consumed by RMI parties, or a tax-exempt status is falsely claimed, the exemption is revoked retrospectively, the company is reclassified as a resident taxable corporation, and full Income Tax Act 1989 liability for open years is assessed, plus interest and late-payment penalties up to a 25% statutory cap. False economic substance declarations attract fines of up to USD 10,000 per violation, revocation of the Certificate of Good Standing, deregistration risk, and consequent KYC de-risking by Stripe/Paddle.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility exists for both capital and current account transactions because the U.S. dollar is the domestic currency. There are no exchange controls, no differential treatment between current and capital account flows, and no profit remittance hurdles. Dividends, royalties, loan repayments, and capital repatriation can be freely remitted in USD without any exchange allocation requirement, subject only to standard AML/CTF due diligence and compliance with the RMI Banking Act, the Financial Transactions Reporting Act, and international sanctions obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender requirement on export proceeds. All export receipts may be retained in USD, freely used, and remitted without central bank approval or administrative license.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). Banking regulation and foreign financial oversight are exercised by the Banking Commissioner under the Ministry of Finance, established by the Banking Act 1991. The United States Federal Reserve System acts as the de facto monetary authority through official dollarization, with the U.S. dollar as the sole legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. The RMI has no regulated fintech or Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing framework. Offshore digital EMIs are not authorized to hold bank accounts or provide payment services in RMI unless separately licensed under the Banking Act or registered as a money transmitter. For an AI video SaaS company, the preferred structure is a USD-denominated treasury account at an RMI licensed commercial bank connected to U.S. correspondent banking rails; engaging unlicensed EMIs creates reputational, sanctions, and regulatory blocking risk.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be made in USD via SWIFT MT103 wire transfer to the RMI business corporation's designated account at a licensed RMI bank, using a U.S. correspondent bank and either the Federal Reserve Fedwire or CHIPS clearing system. The payment reference should include the corporate name, bank account number, and a clear purpose description such as "share subscription / capital contribution under Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act". The bank will require a certified board resolution authorizing the capital injection, executed share subscription documents, plus AML/EDD evidence confirming the source of funds. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) imposes no corporate income tax; a Non-Resident Business Corporation (BC) is fully tax-exempt on foreign-source and RMI-source passive income, subject only to the annual franchise/listing fee.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the RMI imposes no withholding tax on dividends at the statutory level; distributions declared by a Non-Resident Business Corporation to foreign shareholders are entirely free of RMI tax and no withholding return or payment is required.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Annual Report is due on or before the last day of the month containing the anniversary of incorporation; the Annual Listing Fee is due concurrently. For any entity momentarily deriving RMI-source gross revenue, the gross revenue tax return and payment deadline is 30 April of the calendar year following the tax year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 0 - no mandatory RMI transfer pricing documentation regime applies to a non-resident BC deriving no RMI-source revenue; a de facto documentation threshold of USD 500,000 in annual related-party transactions is recommended to defend the 0% tax position under OECD alignment, while a 3% gross revenue tax exposure, if triggered, requires arm's-length substantiation for related-party revenue above USD 100,000.

Economic substance scope: The RMI applies OECD-aligned Economic Substance Regulations for entities performing 'relevant activities,' which include IP holding, distribution, and financing or licensing arrangements resembling a SaaS model. An AI Avatar video generation company that licenses or monetizes software intellectual property through the RMI BC must demonstrate: (i) a physical RMI office and premises, (ii) local full-time employees or qualifying expenditure proportionate to revenue, (iii) board meetings and strategic decisions taking place in the RMI, and (iv) adequate operating expenditure and assets maintained in the RMI. Non-compliance results in declaration as a shell entity, monetary penalties, automatic exchange of information with foreign tax authorities, and potential re-attribution of profits to the ultimate beneficial owner's jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Income Tax Act, civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded plus a criminal fine and imprisonment for a term fixed by the court (up to several years); the Registrar may administratively dissolve the corporation and impose compounded late fees and reinstatement charges.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for fraud or false certification: directors and signatories are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax, penalties, and restitution to affected customers and investors; courts may issue travel bans, asset freezes, and custodial sentences against directors personally.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Economic Substance Regulations and OECD BEPS Action 5 exchange framework, the entity is reclassified as a shell; the RMI Tax Office may impose financial penalties of USD 10,000 to USD 100,000 per year of non-compliance, exchange information with foreign tax authorities, and the Registrar may strike the company for default.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize a shell structure without substance are personally exposed to penalty assessments in the shareholder's home jurisdiction through CFC and exit-tax rules, and to mismanagement claims under the Business Corporations Act; foreign courts may issue director travel bans and worldwide freezing orders in aid of enforcement.
Money Laundering / AML Violations (AI-Generated Deepfake Fraud)

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Money Laundering (Prevention and Control) Act, as amended, laundering criminal proceeds attracts a monetary penalty of up to USD 1,000,000 or twice the value of the property laundered, whichever is greater, and/or imprisonment; the Supreme Court may order forfeiture of assets, and payment processors may permanently terminate the merchant account and file suspicious activity reports with foreign financial intelligence units.

Director personal liability: Director-level liability attaches where directors knowingly participate in or benefit from the laundering scheme; the corporate veil is pierced, directors are personally liable to victims for restitution, and courts may issue travel bans, arrest warrants, and extradition requests under RMI mutual legal assistance procedures.
Fraudulent Trading / Corporate Veil Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Business Corporations Act and Penal Code, fraudulent trading is a criminal offense: the court may order compulsory winding-up, claw back assets, impose equitable compensation equal to the loss caused, freeze bank accounts, and fine the company up to the value of the assets misappropriated.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed misleading statements or approved wrongful distributions bear unlimited personal liability for the company's debts, must make full civil restitution to creditors and customers, face termination of the RMI resident-agent relationship, and are subject to international asset tracing, travel restrictions, and possible extradition where assets are moved abroad.
