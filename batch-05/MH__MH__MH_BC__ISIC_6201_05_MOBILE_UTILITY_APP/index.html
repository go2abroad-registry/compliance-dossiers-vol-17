Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permissible for general corporate compliance: the BCA requires only a registered office and a licensed registered agent in the Republic of the Marshall Islands, and the registered agent's professional address may serve as the registered office; no physical local office, employees, or equipment are statutorily required for a non-resident Business Corporation. However, under the RMI Economic Substance Regulations (2019), if the company is treated as carrying on an 'Intellectual Property Business' or 'Distribution and Service Centre' activity involving connected foreign parties (relevant to an app monetizing software/IP), it must satisfy economic substance tests requiring RMI management, employees, expenditure, and physical assets. Freemium app-store sales directly to end users are generally treated as ordinary active business income rather than passive IP exploitation where the entity does not merely hold or license IP for an affiliate; a licensing or cost-sharing structure should be reviewed at incorporation to avoid creating a substance obligation that cannot be physically satisfied in the RMI.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (Majuro); corporate registration administered by the Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General; official government portal: gov.rm (RMIGOV).

E-government filing portal

Foreign persons incorporate a non-resident Business Corporation under the RMI Business Corporations Act (Title 53) through a licensed registered agent. No RMI tax identification number or Business Privilege Tax (BPT) certificate is required for a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation that derives no gross revenue from business conducted within the Republic of the Marshall Islands. Only if RMI-source gross revenue exceeds the USD 10,000 annual exemption must the corporation register with the Revenue and Taxation Division, obtain a BPT account, and file quarterly BPT returns.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone - Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (Business Corporation Act Section 104; Business Privilege Tax Act Section 209). Exempt from Business Privilege Tax, and RMI levies no income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (Business Corporation Act Section 104; Business Privilege Tax Act Section 209). Exempt from Business Privilege Tax, and RMI levies no income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. Territorial scope: only business transacted within the Republic of the Marshall Islands could attract BPT; offshore app-store revenue is outside RMI nexus.

Available election forms

No income tax code exists, so there are no check-the-box, partnership, or hybrid-mismatch elections. The only operative statutory election is the Non-Resident Domestic Corporation status declaration: an annual sworn statement that no shares are beneficially owned by RMI residents or citizens and that no business is transacted within RMI. A corporation that ceases to satisfy either condition becomes electively taxable as a resident BPT taxpayer at a 3% gross-receipts rate.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales tax. The sole turnover-based levy is the Business Privilege Tax (a direct gross-receipts tax imposed at source), which does not apply to Non-Resident Domestic Corporations and is not an indirect or consumption tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD 10,000 annual gross revenue from business transacted within RMI is the BPT exemption and registration threshold; the non-resident corporation is outside BPT entirely regardless of worldwide revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: with no VAT/GST regime, there is no export zero-rating or input-credit refund mechanism. Offshore digital sales of the mobile utility app via Apple IAP and Google Play Billing create no RMI tax nexus, and the non-resident corporation's revenue stream is simply outside RMI taxation under a territorial non-filing position.

  • Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Good Standing from the Registrar of Corporations
  • Sworn Non-Resident Domestic Corporation declaration (Business Privilege Tax Act Section 209)
  • App Store Connect and Google Play Console sales and earnings reports
  • Apple IAP / Google Play Billing payment-processor settlement statements
  • Bank statements evidencing receipt of offshore settlement remittances in USD

None. The Republic of the Marshall Islands has no electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, real-time tax API, or digital stamping mandate. Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console sales reports, together with payment-processor settlement statements, constitute the sole commercial documentation.

Under the Business Privilege Tax Act 2012 (as amended), a false non-resident claim or under-reported RMI-source gross revenue subjects the corporation to reassessment at 3%, a late-filing penalty of 10% per month (capped at 100% of tax due), interest, accuracy-related penalties, and, in cases of fraudulent declaration, criminal fines and revocation of the non-resident exemption. No VAT clawback regime exists because no VAT is levied; no foreign-exchange repatriation deadline applies because the USD is the RMI legal tender and no exchange controls are imposed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility exists because the RMI is dollarized and has no domestic currency. Both current-account items (profits, dividends, royalties, service fees) and capital-account items (equity injections, liquidation proceeds) are freely convertible and remittable without exchange-control approval. Profit remittance from a Non-Resident Business Corporation is tax-exempt under Section 173 of the Business Corporations Act provided no income is sourced in the RMI. Practical impediments are limited to commercial-bank due diligence, correspondent-bank de-risking, and OFAC screening, not statutory currency controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The RMI imposes no surrender obligations, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no statutory timelines for export proceeds. Digital app store proceeds may be retained indefinitely in U.S. dollars in any designated account.

Central bank supervision: No independent central bank in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI); the U.S. dollar is sole legal tender. The Banking Commissioner, operating under the Financial Institutions Act 2017 and the Banking Act within the Ministry of Finance, exercises prudential banking supervision. No separate foreign-exchange administration or reserve-management authority exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for transaction settlement, Low for capital escrow and regulated deposit-taking. The RMI has no EMI licensing framework or passporting regime; an offshore EMI authorised in a recognised jurisdiction (e.g., FCA, EBA-licensed) may onboard the non-resident entity as a payment-services customer, but cannot maintain a local branch, hold RMI escrow capital, or represent itself as a bank in the RMI.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — the Marshall Islands does not impose transfer-pricing documentation requirements. No local currency threshold exists for Master File, Local File, or Country-by-Country Reporting obligations.

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019 imposes substance requirements only on entities carrying on 'relevant activities' enumerated in the Act. Development, distribution, or operation of a mobile utility app is not a relevant activity. Therefore a non-resident business corporation engaged solely in mobile app commerce generally has no Marshall Islands economic substance obligation. If the same entity also carries on a relevant activity such as intellectual property holding or intra-group financing, an economic substance self-assessment and a registered notification would be required.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Exemption Status

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties, reassessment of back taxes plus interest, and criminal fines under the RMI Income Tax Act; repeated or willful misconduct may also trigger administrative dissolution and referral for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorise false statements may be held personally liable for restitution of taxes; courts may pierce the corporate veil and issue travel bans and asset freezing orders in connection with criminal tax proceedings.
Shell Company / Lack of Substance

Statutory sanction: The RMI Registrar may strike off or dissolve the corporation; the entity may be blacklisted as a shell company under FATF recommendations; ancillary fines may be imposed under the RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act.

Director personal liability: Directors may become personally liable if the company is used as a mere instrument for fraud; foreign tax authorities may seek civil enforcement, while RMI courts may lift the veil, impose disqualification orders, and restrict international travel.
AML / Digital Payment Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Non-compliance with the RMI AML/CFT regime can result in fines of up to USD 100,000 per breach, suspension of the entity's registration, inclusion in a high-risk financial activity registry, and enhanced mandatory audits.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may be personally fined, disqualified, and imprisoned for up to five years for knowingly facilitating money laundering or terrorist financing; a travel ban is routinely imposed during the investigation and trial.
Failure to File Annual Report / Veil Piercing

Statutory sanction: Statutory late penalty of USD 100; after two consecutive years of non-filing the Registrar may administratively dissolve the corporation. Dissolved company assets may be treated as bona vacantia and vested in the Registrar.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue trading after dissolution become jointly and severally liable for all debts and obligations incurred in the corporate name; the protection of limited liability is removed and the corporate veil is automatically pierced.
