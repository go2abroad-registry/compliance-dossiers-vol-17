Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue Administration, Ministry of Finance, Planning and Commercial Affairs, Republic of the Marshall Islands (income tax administration); Office of the Registrar of Corporations (corporate registry and formation, administered through licensed Marshall Islands registered agents). No comprehensive tax e-portal exists; statutory income tax filings are submitted on prescribed forms to the Revenue Administration in Majuro, while corporate formation filings are submitted electronically by licensed registered agents.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Business Corporation under the Business Corporations Act 1991 through a licensed registered agent (name availability check, filing of Articles of Incorporation, payment of charter fee); no statutory TIN is issued at formation. Step 2: If the entity derives Marshall Islands-source income, file an income tax registration application with the Revenue Administration within the statutory period and obtain a TIN. Step 3: If the entity employs personnel in the Republic, register simultaneously with the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration. Step 4: A purely non-resident Business Corporation with zero Marshall Islands-source income is not required to hold a TIN or file income tax returns; it must maintain its registered agent and comply with annual corporate franchise/permit requirements. Step 5: No indirect tax registration is possible or required because the Republic imposes no VAT, GST, or general sales tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or comparable pass-through election exists under Marshall Islands law; entity classification is statutory. The Income Tax Act 1989 permits affiliated resident corporations to elect consolidated group filing, and a non-resident corporation with a permanent establishment in the Republic may elect to be taxed as a resident on Marshall Islands-source income. The Republic has not adopted BEPS Action 2 hybrid-mismatch rules, so no hybrid mismatch elections are available; no tax treaty elections exist because the Republic maintains no comprehensive double tax treaties.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None_No_VAT_GST_or_Sales_Use_Tax_Regime

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no indirect tax registration regime or economic turnover threshold exists. Functional currency: United States Dollar (USD).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — the Republic of the Marshall Islands levies no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; consequently, there is no export zero-rating mechanism and no input VAT refund regime to claim. Vertical ERP/CRM cloud software is an intangible service delivered remotely; as a non-resident Business Corporation deriving no Marshall Islands-source income, the entity remains outside the territorial income tax base and requires no zero-rated export documentation.

  • Not applicable — cloud-delivered ERP/CRM software is an intangible cross-border service involving no physical export; no Marshall Islands Customs export declaration, bill of lading, inward remittance certificate, or VAT zero-rating certificate is required.
  • Contingency — if physical goods were exported from the Republic, the Marshall Islands Customs Service would require a formal Export Entry under the Customs Act, commercial invoice, and transport documentation; no VAT-related certificate exists.

None. The Marshall Islands imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing clearinghouse, tax administration API integration, digital stamping, or invoice clearance obligation on software or cloud service providers; invoicing is governed by general commercial law only.

No export zero-rating or VAT refund regime exists, so no indirect-tax clawback or refund-reversal exposure applies. General anti-avoidance authority under the Income Tax Act 1989 permits the Revenue Administrator to disregard artificial transactions designed to create or preserve non-resident exemption; failure to register, file, or provide information attracts penalties of up to USD 1,000 per offence, and false or fraudulent declarations may result in criminal fines and/or imprisonment. The Republic maintains no exchange controls, and with the USD as domestic currency, no FX repatriation compliance timeline or clawback deadline exists.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both capital and current account transactions because the Marshall Islands does not issue an independent currency and does not operate foreign exchange controls. Profit remittance and capital repatriation encounter no exchange-control hurdles but must comply with corporate filing, tax registration, and anti-money laundering verification obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement exists. Export receipts may be held, converted, or remitted freely in US dollars; only standard record-keeping and AML reporting thresholds apply.

Central bank supervision: N/A – No central bank exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands; the US dollar is the sole legal tender. Banking supervision is performed by the Banking Commissioner under the Ministry of Finance, pursuant to the Banking Act 1991.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. The Marshall Islands has no fintech EMI or electronic money institution licensing framework; offshore digital EMIs cannot obtain a local licence and may only facilitate payments through a licensed local bank or a correspondent arrangement. Practical viability is conditional on banking-as-a-service integration with Bank of Marshall Islands or Bank of Guam and on satisfying both US OFAC and local AML requirements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None. The Marshall Islands has no statutory transfer pricing documentation regime, and no Local File or Master File is required for an RMI non-resident BC. The only relevant documentation requirements arise under foreign tax systems in the shareholder or user jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: The RMI has adopted Economic Substance Regulations applicable to registered entities carrying on relevant activities: banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, holding entity, distribution and service centre, and intellectual property business. A vertical ERP/CRM cloud business may fall within the distribution/service or IP holding category if the RMI entity holds exploitable software intangibles or performs distribution functions. Entities in scope must satisfy a RMI-based management, expenditure, employees, and physical presence test and file an annual economic substance declaration. Penalties apply for non-compliance and false declarations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Assessment of unpaid tax plus interest and penalties; criminal conviction carries imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to USD 10,000 under the Marshall Islands Income Tax Act and criminal law.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly cause the misrepresentation are subject to civil restitution and criminal liability. Courts may pierce the corporate veil on proof of fraud, making controlling persons personally liable and subject to asset freezing orders and travel restrictions imposed as bail or court conditions.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties under the RMI Economic Substance Regulations per year of non-compliance, cancellation of registration, and referral to the Registrar for strike-off; false declarations may constitute an offence punishable by fine and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute false declarations may be fined and disqualified from acting as directors. Piercing is available where the shell is used to defeat creditor or tax claims, exposing directors and members to personal liability for dissipated assets.
AML/CFT Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties per violation under RMI anti-money laundering legislation, regulatory orders, freezing orders, and criminal liability for knowing facilitation of money laundering or terrorist financing.

Director personal liability: Senior officers and directors may be prosecuted individually for wilful AML/CFT failures; they may be banned from corporate office, fined, imprisoned, and subjected to court-imposed travel bans and asset freezes pending proceedings.
Unlicensed Financial Activity / Payment Regulatory Contravention

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed financial business is legally unenforceable; regulators may issue cease-and-desist orders, impose monetary penalties, and require either registration as a money services business or documented reliance on a licensed payment processor.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for debts and losses arising from unauthorized financial business, and may face regulatory disqualification, personal fines, and civil claims by injured counterparties.
