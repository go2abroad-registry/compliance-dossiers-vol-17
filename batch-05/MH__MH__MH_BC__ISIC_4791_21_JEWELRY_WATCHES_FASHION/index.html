Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permissible under RMI law: every Business Corporation must maintain a registered office and a licensed registered agent within the Marshall Islands (typically the agent's premises in Majuro), and a purely virtual footprint with no staffed operational premises is lawful for a non-resident, tax-exempt BC. However, the target online jewelry and timepieces business cannot onboard to Stripe or PayPal using only the RMI registered-agent address. Recommended structure: a non-resident RMI BC holding brand and treasury functions, supplemented by a merchant-account-bearing operating entity in a supported jurisdiction (e.g., US LLC or Hong Kong company) for Stripe and PayPal onboarding, with intercompany agreements for the e-commerce order flow.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. A non-resident domestic Business Corporation may be formed without depositing any capital and may authorize shares with or without par value; no escrow or bank-account requirement within the Marshall Islands. An annual government franchise tax is payable to maintain good standing.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Office (RTA), Ministry of Finance, Government of the Republic of the Marshall Islands; principal office in Majuro; no electronic tax-filing portal (paper-based administration); corporate law registry administered separately by the Marshall Islands Registrar of Corporations through licensed registered agents.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation (BC) is not required to obtain a Marshall Islands TIN unless it derives income from sources within the Marshall Islands or employs local personnel. Procedure: (1) incorporate via a licensed Marshall Islands registered agent; (2) obtain the RMI Business Corporation Number and Certificate of Good Standing; (3) if RMI-source income is anticipated, submit a written TIN application to the RTA (no statutory form fee for issuance; entity details and registered agent information required); (4) if no RMI-source income is derived, no TIN filing is made and no income-tax return is required; annual corporate renewal with the Registrar of Corporations remains mandatory.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident Business Corporation (BC) is not required to obtain a Marshall Islands TIN unless it derives income from sources within the Marshall Islands or employs local personnel. Procedure: (1) incorporate via a licensed Marshall Islands registered agent; (2) obtain the RMI Business Corporation Number and Certificate of Good Standing; (3) if RMI-source income is anticipated, submit a written TIN application to the RTA (no statutory form fee for issuance; entity details and registered agent information required); (4) if no RMI-source income is derived, no TIN filing is made and no income-tax return is required; annual corporate renewal with the Registrar of Corporations remains mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Marshall Islands law. The Income Tax Act 1989 applies a strict territorial/source-based regime: a non-resident domestic BC is outside the RMI tax net provided it derives no RMI-source income, maintains no office or employees in the RMI, and conducts no sales activity within the RMI. Exemption is automatic, not elective; an entity may evidence its status by filing a voluntary non-resident declaration or nil-return with the RTA and by maintaining proper registered-agent records; no OECD hybrid rules apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - the Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT, GST, sales and use tax, excise, or any other indirect tax regime at the national or sub-national level.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no indirect tax regime exists and therefore no turnover-based registration threshold, VAT ID, or indirect tax compliance obligation arises for the BC.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - because the Marshall Islands levies no VAT, GST, or sales tax, there is no zero-rating mechanism to claim and no export VAT refund regime. Exports of jewelry, watches, and fashion e-commerce goods from the RMI are subject to no RMI indirect tax charge; customs may apply documentary survey only for statistical and prohibited-goods control purposes.

  • Customs Export Declaration (RMI Customs export entry where goods physically clear Majuro or other RMI ports)
  • Commercial Invoice (seller, buyer, HS Code at 6-digit level, Incoterms, unit value, total value and currency)
  • Packing List (itemized weights and counts)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (ocean/air transport evidence for the outbound movement)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 credit advice evidencing export proceeds receipt
  • Certificate of Origin (only where required by the destination market's preferential tariff scheme)

None - the RMI operates no real-time fiscal clearance system, no e-invoicing API mandate, no digital tax stamping requirement, and no continuous transaction control regime for jewelry, watches, fashion, or any other e-commerce trade.

No indirect-tax clawback or zero-rating fraud penalty exists in the RMI because no VAT regime is in force. Scrutiny focuses on (i) income tax: if the RTA asserts RMI-source income (e.g., sales consummated within the RMI or domestic payroll), late-filing and non-filing penalties accrue monthly (up to 25% of tax due), negligence and fraud penalties may reach 100% of the assessed tax, interest applies, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion is available; (ii) customs: false export declarations attract fines, forfeiture of goods, and suspension of the exporter's customs privileges; (iii) AML/CFT: under the RMI Financial Transactions Reporting Act, failure to document export proceeds or to file suspicious transaction reports can expose the BC to civil penalties and license/registered-agent remedial action; adequate commercial, shipping, and banking records must be retained for at least five years to substantiate the offshore-source character of all revenue.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de jure and de facto convertibility. USD is legal tender; there are zero exchange controls, no capital account restrictions, no prior-approval requirements for capital inflows or profit remittances, and no repatriation taxes or withholding constraints for non-resident Business Corporations. The only practical hurdle is downstream compliance with US OFAC sanctions and correspondent-bank due diligence, as all USD clearing ultimately settles through Fedwire/CHIPS at US correspondent institutions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI law imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline for export proceeds, including online merchandising receipts from jewelry, watches, and fashion goods. Exporters may retain proceeds in USD or any freely convertible currency in any domestic or foreign bank, without reporting obligations to the RMI authorities.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) maintains no central bank and no foreign exchange control authority. Monetary and banking supervision is vested in the Banking Commissioner under the RMI Banking Commission (Ministry of Finance), operating pursuant to the Banking Act 1998 (as amended) and the Financial Institutions Act. The United States Federal Reserve acts as the de facto monetary anchor because the US Dollar (USD) is the sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-High viability with a foreign-license dependency. RMI does not operate a stand-alone EMI or e-money licensing regime; unlicensed money transmission within RMI is unlawful under the Financial Institutions Act. A foreign-licensed Electronic Money Institution (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, or similar) can lawfully serve an RMI Business Corporation as an offshore payment processor provided the EMI holds an active license in a FATF-compliant jurisdiction (FCA-UK, EU-EMI, MAS-Singapore) and the RMI entity is onboarded as a merchant counterparty rather than as a local payment institution. Practical limitation: EMIs generally cannot open USD corporate accounts in RMI domestic banks; they operate as sub-accounts routed via partner banks, which may trigger enhanced due diligence on high-value jewelry/watch transactions.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

3% on the first USD 5,000,000 of Marshall Islands-source taxable income plus 5% on the excess (Income Tax Act 1989, Title 30 MIRC). A Non-Resident Domestic Business Corporation deriving exclusively foreign-source income from the online merchandising of fashion jewelry and fine timepieces has an effective corporate income tax rate of 0%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the Marshall Islands imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Business Corporation to shareholders of any residency; distributions are paid gross

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold applies - the Marshall Islands has not enacted transfer-pricing documentation rules and imposes no Local File or Master File obligation in USD or any other currency. As risk-based best practice for this jewelry and watches e-commerce structure, maintain intercompany pricing and cost allocation records once annual related-party revenue exceeds USD 1,000,000 or where Stripe or PayPal underwriting requires proof of commercial substance

Economic substance scope: Marshall Islands law does not enact EU-style Economic Substance Regulations; a Non-Resident BC has no statutory local substance requirement. However, the Medium_Risk_Enhanced AML tier applied by Stripe, PayPal, and correspondent banks effectively tests substance: maintain board minutes, a share register, the corporate records book, and inventory or procurement contracts demonstrating genuine control over the jewelry and timepiece trade. Absent these records, merchant accounts are exposed to freezing and termination for shell-entity indicators
