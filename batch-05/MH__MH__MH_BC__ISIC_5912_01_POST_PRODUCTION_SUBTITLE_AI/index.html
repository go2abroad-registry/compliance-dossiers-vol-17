Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

No national corporate income tax, VAT, or general sales tax administration exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). The operative statutory body for the non-resident Business Corporation is the Registrar of Corporations, Office of the Attorney General, Majuro, operating under the Business Corporations Act, 1990. Indirect levies on physical imports are administered by the Marshall Islands Customs Service, Ministry of Finance. Wage tax and social security, if local employees are engaged, are administered by the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration. The Foreign Investment Policy Act, 1990 is administered by the Office of the Chief Executive Officer, Marshall Islands Development Fund / FIBL unit.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation is not issued a tax identification number (TIN) because the RMI imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, estate tax, gift tax, VAT, GST, or sales and use tax on non-resident corporations. The statutory identity credentials are: (i) Certificate of Incorporation issued by the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act; (ii) the company registration number appearing on the certificate; and (iii) a Foreign Investment Business License (FIBL) required for any business with non-resident ownership conducting or contemplating business in the RMI under the Foreign Investment Policy Act, 1990. If the entity imports physical equipment or goods into the RMI, it must obtain a Customs importer registration from the Marshall Islands Customs Service. If it hires RMI-based employees, it must register with the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration for wage tax withholding and social security contributions; this is the only situation in which a tax-related filing number is generated. No other national registration is required for the conduct of offshore post-production services.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None applicable. The RMI has no domestic corporate income tax statute and therefore no check-the-box, transparency election, or hybrid mismatch election is available under RMI law. Tax-exempt status is automatic and mandatory for a Business Corporation recognizing itself as non-resident within the meaning of the Business Corporations Act, 1990. The U.S.-RMI income tax treaty does not create taxing jurisdiction over the non-resident corporation and no treaty election is required. The entity may, however, voluntarily elect to file consolidated foreign reporting or maintain internal tax classification for the benefit of foreign parent reporting jurisdictions, but such elections are contractual/administrative and do not affect RMI taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — the Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT, no GST, and no sales and use tax. The only indirect levy is an import duty (customs tariff) applied to tangible goods entering RMI territory; offshore digital services such as automated subtitle transcription and AI voice cloning are outside the indirect tax nexus entirely.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no value-added or general consumption tax is enacted. Import duty registration arises per transaction, not upon a turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a tax concept. Because the RMI has no VAT/GST/sales tax, there is no export zero-rating mechanism and no refundable input tax. Subtitle transcription and AI voice cloning services supplied by the non-resident Business Corporation from outside RMI to foreign customers are entirely outside the RMI indirect tax net, regardless of customer location, supply chain platform, or payment rail. As a policy matter, the entity can invoice customers without any RMI tax add-on, and no export approval is required from the RMI tax authorities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) has adopted the U.S. dollar as its exclusive legal tender and maintains no exchange-control regime on capital or current account transactions. Dividends, profit remittances, and royalty outflows of non-resident Business Corporations are fully convertible and require no central bank approval or repatriation tax. Current account and capital account transactions are unrestricted; commercial banks apply only standard AML/CTF due diligence and correspondent-bank compliance screening, which may require documentary substantiation of source of funds but does not impose convertibility barriers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timelines or mandatory conversion ratios apply because the U.S. dollar is the domestic currency and RMI imposes no foreign-exchange surrender regime. Licensed banks are not required to convert or surrender any portion of export trade proceeds to the government; the entity retains 100% of export receipts, including revenue from AI subtitle transcription and voice-cloning services.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (exercising prudential supervision under the Banking Act 1991; there is no domestic central bank, and the U.S. Federal Reserve serves as the de facto issuer of the U.S. dollar, the sole legal tender).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Moderate, subject to jurisdictional licensing constraints. RMI does not issue electronic money institution (EMI) or e-money licences; digital payment providers such as Stripe and Wise generally onboard RMI-incorporated entities only through U.S., U.K., or E.U. licensed acquiring partners. The low-risk classification of the AI post-production business and the selection of Stripe and Wise as target rails improve viability, but offshore EMIs may decline onboarding where the entity lacks physical substance or regional presence. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - the Marshall Islands (whose official currency is the U.S. dollar) has no statutory transfer pricing regime and enacts no Local File or Master File documentation thresholds; multinational groups operating the subtitle transcription and AI voice cloning studio prepare OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation solely to satisfy the TP statutes of counterpart jurisdictions such as the United States (IRC Section 482).

Economic substance scope: The RMI has adopted Economic Substance Regulations aligned with OECD harmful tax practice standards for geographically mobile activities. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Reclassification as a resident domestic corporation subject to income tax on worldwide income; back-assessment of tax plus interest; a penalty of up to 100% of underpaid tax; and criminal prosecution for tax evasion with statutory fines and a term of imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false exemption declarations are personally liable for the tax deficiency; courts may pierce the corporate veil to order civil restitution, impose travel bans and passport surrender to secure the investigation, and impose imprisonment for evasion and false oath.
Shell Company / Deficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties under the Economic Substance Regulations for non-compliance; administrative strike-off of the corporation; and loss of the non-resident tax exemption if local management is found to exist in the RMI.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false economic substance declarations face fines and imprisonment; nominee and shadow-directorship arrangements expose the actors to personal liability for fraudulent trading and to confiscation of proceeds.
AML / KYC Breach (Beneficial Ownership)

Statutory sanction: Civil fines and administrative penalties under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act; regulatory action against the registered agent; referral to the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit; and termination of correspondent banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for AML fines; repeated breaches attract personal criminal liability, travel restrictions to prevent asset dissipation, and civil forfeiture of the proceeds of the unlawful activity.
Annual Return / Corporate Registry Default

Statutory sanction: Statutory late penalty plus interest; after continued default the Registrar strikes the corporation off the register, the entity is dissolved, and it loses legal capacity, bank accounts and payment rail access.

Director personal liability: Continued trading after strike-off renders directors personally liable for the company's obligations; restoration costs and imprisonment for misrepresentation in registry filings may also be imposed.
