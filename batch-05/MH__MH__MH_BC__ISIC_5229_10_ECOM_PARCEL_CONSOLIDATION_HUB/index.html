VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – exports of goods and offshore services from a non-resident BC are outside the scope of RMI indirect tax because no VAT/GST is levied. No export zero-rating claim is required or available, and there is no input VAT refund mechanism.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMI has no domestic currency and maintains no exchange-control apparatus. USD is fully convertible and freely transferable for both current-account transactions (trade settlements, logistics fees, carrier payments) and capital-account transactions (equity injections, shareholder loans, dividend remittances, liquidation proceeds). There are no statutory approvals, taxes, or waiting periods on profit repatriation; the only practical hurdles are bank-level KYC/AML, correspondent-bank due diligence, and the financial-statement evidence required to distinguish genuine treasury flows from money laundering.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The RMI imposes no mandatory surrender of export receipts, no prescribed conversion ratio, and no statutory timeline for converting foreign currency into USD. Export-related receivables may be retained indefinitely in a licensed bank as USD deposits or freely converted through the banking system; a mandatory conversion ratio of 0% applies.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (no central bank is established; the RMI uses the U.S. dollar as legal tender)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for supplemental disbursement routing; Low as a primary treasury relationship. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Airwallex are not licensed to take deposits or provide payment services inside the RMI Banking Act 1987 framework. They may lawfully be used only as front-end aggregation, FX conversion, and disbursement engines whose underlying settlement is ultimately cleared through a licensed RMI bank or a U.S. correspondent bank; any platform that holds and re-transmits client funds in a manner constituting deposit-taking within RMI could require a banking license. Accordingly, the EMI rail is acceptable for high-velocity small-parcel collections and linehaul injections, but capital escrow and statutory books must remain with a licensed banking institution.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All shareholder capital, director loans, and intercompany funding must be remitted in USD by SWIFT MT103 (or Fedwire/CHIPS through a U.S. correspondent) explicitly naming the RMI corporate account as beneficiary. Recommended message field 70 purpose text is 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' and, where the correspondent bank maps ISO 20022 purpose codes, use 'CAPIT' for a capital investment or 'EQUIT' for equity injection. The receiving bank will verify the remitter against sanctions lists, will require a board resolution and updated register of shareholders to be presented before credit is confirmed, and will normally place funds into the Capital Escrow Account. Any foreign-currency capital must be converted into USD through the receiving bank or its correspondent at the prevailing spot rate; no central-bank confirmation is necessary because the RMI has no exchange-control regime.Trade settlements to suppliers, linehaul carriers, and overseas consolidation partners should be executed via SWIFT MT103 for open-account disbursements and via irrevocable documentary letters of credit (UCP600) where the seller requires bank-assured payment. Documentary LC settlements will be routed through the issuing bank's U.S. correspondent using MT103/MT202 COV, with commercial invoices, packing lists, air waybills, and proof-of-delivery certificates constituting the presentation package. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — The RMI imposes no withholding tax on cash or stock dividends paid by a Non-Resident Business Corporation, irrespective of the shareholder's country of residence.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — the RMI has no domestic transfer-pricing legislation, no OECD-aligned Local File or Master File obligations, no country-by-country reporting threshold, and no arm's-length documentation requirement (threshold legally nil in USD).

Economic substance scope: The RMI has not yet codified a comprehensive Economic Substance Regulations regime comparable to the BVI or Cayman Islands. However, the EU Council has placed the RMI under Annex II (grey-list) monitoring and the RMI has committed at a high political level to introduce substance requirements for non-resident corporations and maritime entities by the agreed reform deadline. For the parcel-consolidation and linehaul business, the substantive standard is that the entity must perform genuine commercial functions: contract negotiation, risk assumption, real billing operations, and complete financial records maintained in a physical office. A letterbox arrangement directly invites EU black-listing, APG/FATF enhanced scrutiny, correspondent-bank de-risking, and denial of transparency-related cooperation benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / unauthorized on-island activity

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 1989: assessment of corporate income tax on RMI-source income, statutory interest on overdue tax, a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax assessed for wilful default, revocation of the registered agent's standing, and administrative strike-off of the company.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the exempt-status declaration while knowingly or recklessly disregarding on-island activity are personally liable for the unpaid corporate tax as responsible officers and may be subjected to civil restitution; conviction for filing a false declaration is punishable by imprisonment up to three years, disqualification from holding RMI directorships, and the RMI High Court may impose a travel ban pending trial.
Shell company / absence of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative dissolution under the Business Corporation Act for failure to maintain a registered agent or to file the Annual Report; placement of the RMI on the EU Annex I black list with defensive tax measures applied by EU Member States; APG/FATF enhanced follow-up; and automatic termination of the corporate bank relationship by AML-constrained correspondent banks.

Director personal liability: Directors cannot shelter behind the corporate veil where the company is an instrumentality of fraud: the RMI High Court will pierce the veil in fraudulent-conveyance or intermingled-assets cases, imposing civil restitution and equitable compensation; directors engaged in sham invoicing may be extradited on fraud or forgery charges with a travel ban and preventive arrest possible.
AML/CFT breaches (Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act 2002)

Statutory sanction: Money laundering is punishable by a fine up to USD 100,000 and imprisonment up to five years; STR failure and tipping-off offences carry statutory maximum fines of USD 10,000 and imprisonment up to two years; the Financial Intelligence Unit may additionally impose administrative monetary penalties and prohibit the entity from maintaining an account in the RMI banking system.

Director personal liability: Senior management and the designated compliance officer are personally criminally liable for AML failures; directors who knowingly move illicit settlement funds face seizure of personal assets, civil forfeiture proceedings, a statutory travel ban pending investigation, and imprisonment up to five years; the corporate veil is displaced to the extent the legal-entity structure was used as a facade.
