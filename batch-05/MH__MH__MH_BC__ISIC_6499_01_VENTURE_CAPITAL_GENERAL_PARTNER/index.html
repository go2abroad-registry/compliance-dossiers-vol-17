Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands Ministry of Finance, Banking and Postal Services, Revenue Management Division (Majuro); corporate registration and annual franchise tax administered by the Office of the Registrar of Corporations (Majuro) under the Business Corporations Act 1990. No national digital tax portal exists; filings are submitted through licensed registered agents in paper or PDF form.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned non-resident Business Corporation (NRDC) files Articles of Incorporation with the Office of the Registrar of Corporations through a licensed registered agent. The Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation bearing the corporate registration number; the RMI does not issue a TIN or VAT ID for offshore exempt entities. The Certificate of Incorporation and subsequent Certificate of Good Standing serve as the authority's proof of tax-exempt status. A TIN and Business License are required only if the entity engages in economic activity within the RMI, in which case it must register with the Revenue Management Division for the 3% gross revenue tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid mismatch, or elective taxation regime exists under Marshall Islands law; the exemption of non-resident domestic corporations from RMI income and gross revenue tax is mandatory and automatic, not elective. A US owner may file US Form 8832 for US federal classification purposes, but such an election has no effect on the entity's RMI tax status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax in force. The RMI indirect revenue regime consists of customs import duties under the Customs Act 1990 and a 3% Gross Revenue Tax under the Income Tax Act 1989, both of which apply only to persons or goods with an RMI territorial nexus.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST legislation exists. For the 3% Gross Revenue Tax, any entity 'carrying on business' inside the RMI is liable from the first US dollar of locally sourced gross revenue; no minimum threshold is provided. A non-resident venture capital GP with no RMI office, employees, or local revenue never triggers registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in a VAT sense because the RMI imposes no VAT/GST on goods or services. Management fees and carried interest derived by a non-resident GP from offshore venture funds are outside the RMI tax net entirely and therefore do not require a zero-rating mechanism. Where physical goods are exported from RMI territory, customs duty exemption or rebate may be claimed under the Customs Act 1990 for temporarily imported or re-exported goods; no VAT refund mechanism exists.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

None. The RMI imposes no electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping mandate. Paper-based invoicing and customs declarations remain the operative standard.

The RMI has no export-VAT refund fraud regime because no VAT is levied. General fraud and misrepresentation provisions in the Business Corporations Act 1990 and the Customs Act 1990 empower the Registrar and customs authorities to impose fines, seize goods, order forfeiture, and dissolve the offending corporation for false statements or duty evasion. A non-resident entity that misrepresents its status or activities to the Registrar to retain automatic tax exemption risks revocation of its registration and loss of separate legal personality, exposing owners to retrospective personal liability.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility in both law and practice. Because the RMI issues no domestic currency and the U.S. dollar is legal tender, the distinction between current-account and capital-account convertibility is effectively immaterial: no Marshall Islands statute restricts the purchase, sale, repatriation, or liquidation of foreign exchange. The RMI is an IMF member state (membership effective 21 May 1992) and its exchange arrangements are consistent with Article VIII obligations, with no restrictions on payments and transfers for current international transactions. A non-resident Business Corporation acting as a venture capital General Partner faces zero statutory profit-remittance hurdles; the only practical impediments arise from U.S. correspondent-bank sanction screening and global AML/CFT de-risking, not from Marshall Islands law.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. The RMI imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender period, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation requirement under the Banking Act 1994 or any exchange-control decree. All export receipts, capital-call inflows, carried interest, and management fee receivables may be freely retained and disposed of in USD without central-bank approval, escrow, or conversion obligation.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commission of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance), established under the Banking Act 1994. The Marshall Islands has no domestic central bank, no monetary policy authority, and no foreign exchange administration; the lawful tender is the United States dollar, distributed through the U.S. Federal Reserve correspondent network.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility with strict legal constraints. Offshore digital EMI accounts are not licensed by the RMI Banking Commissioner and have no supervisory standing in the Marshall Islands. US/UK-licensed EMIs will generally accept RMI-registered corporations only after enhanced due diligence, and several major EMIs systematically decline RMI entities because of the jurisdiction's elevated AML/CFT risk profile and correspondent-bank de-risking. EMIs may serve as auxiliary disbursement rails but cannot substitute for a regulated Tier-1 bank account for capital calls or carried interest.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital calls are settled by SWIFT MT103 wires from each LP's bank into the GP's Capital Escrow Account, with USD clearing occurring through the U.S. correspondent-bank network (Fedwire and CHIPS). Each instruction must populate field 50 with the LP legal name, field 59 with the account beneficiary, and field 70 with the fund identifier, capital call number, and investor reference; where the originating jurisdiction applies standardized economic-purpose codes, the flow is classified as 'S3010 — Investment in equity capital' under the IMF BPM6 purpose-code framework. No RMI exchange-control filing, central-bank approval, or declaration is required. Value release is conditional on positive matching against the capital call schedule, the subscription agreement, and the investor KYC pack, followed by an OFAC/UN/EU sanctions screen at the receiving bank.Portfolio-company investments and drawdown disbursements are executed by SWIFT MT103 corporate credits bearing the investment-agreement reference and the payment purpose 'Equity Investment', 'Convertible Note', or 'Working Capital Facility' in field 70. Irrevocable documentary credits governed by UCP600 are available through the issuing bank for trade-linked transactions but are non-standard for venture-stage technology funding. All outbound disbursements are cleared via the same USD correspondent rails, screened against OFAC, EU, UN, and UK sanctions lists, and recorded against the fund's cash-flow model. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The Marshall Islands does not have a statutory transfer pricing regime and does not impose Local File, Master File, or Country-by-Country reporting thresholds in USD; however, venture capital general partner entities are deemed to require a contemporaneous transfer pricing policy if they are part of a multinational group whose ultimate parent files CbC reports in any OECD/G20 jurisdiction and whose group revenue exceeds the equivalent of the OECD threshold (EUR 750 million / approximately USD 810 million)

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands has adopted a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation economic substance framework aligned with the 2019 EU Code of Conduct Group requirements; in-scope entities (including Pure Equity Holding Companies, finance and leasing companies, and holding companies that generate income from geographically mobile activities such as management or investment services) must satisfy the Economic Substance Regulations and maintain the following evidenced in the Marshall Islands or utility-certified documentation: (a) locally managed meetings by at least one executive, (b) adequate physical presence including a lease or owned premises, (c) a dedicated bank account and books of account, and (d) an annual economic substance return filed with the Registrar within seven months of the fiscal year-end

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Pursuant to the Marshall Islands Economic Substance Regulations, the Registrar may impose administrative fines of up to US$100,000 for failure to file the economic substance return or misrepresentation; equivalent fixed penalties apply for each year of non-compliance; the Registrar may also strike the company from the register, dissolve the entity, and issue a certificate of dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers knowingly causing or permitting the filing of false economic substance declarations face individual fines of up to US$20,000 per offense, civil damages for any resulting tax loss, and disqualification from serving as a director in the Marshall Islands; if continued registration is maintained through false sworn declarations, the corporate veil will be pierced to hold directors jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid franchise taxes, penalties, and restoration costs.
Corporate Veil Piercing / Fraudulent Transfer of Fund Assets

Statutory sanction: Under general principles of Marshall Islands law derived from the Business Corporations Act, courts will disregard limited liability upon a finding of sham or alter ego; penalties include the entry of judgment against shareholders and principals for all amounts paid out of the deficient entity, punitive damages, and disgorgement of fees; the Registrar may likewise dissolve the corporation administratively for failing to maintain a registered agent.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who authorize fraudulent distributions or who are found to be the alter ego of the venture capital fund may be personally and jointly liable to fund investors and general creditors for the full amount of distributions made while insolvent; travel restrictions and cancellation of the entity's certificate of good standing may be imposed to secure restitution; criminal prosecution is possible for willful diversion of investor funds, with imprisonment of up to seven years under the Marshall Islands Criminal Code.
AML / Counter-Financing of Terrorism Violation

Statutory sanction: Adoption of AML obligations will be enforced under the Marshall Islands Criminal Code and the financial Action Task Force-aligned guidance; banks and trust agents are required to report suspicious transactions to the Financial Intelligence Unit; sanctions-involved payments expose the entity to asset freezing, surrender of the proceeds to the government, and administrative fines of up to US$250,000 per unreported transaction.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly process or structure transactions to evade AML reporting are subject to criminal fines of up to US$500,000 and imprisonment of up to 10 years; senior management of the general partner may be personally liable for civil restitution to investors defrauded by an unreported money laundering transaction, and entry to jurisdictions that are part of the Asia-Pacific Group on Money Laundering may be denied due to travel bans imposed by the Registrar or its foreign counterparts in connection with corrupt fund administration.
Franchise Tax Evasion and False Filing

Statutory sanction: Fines of not less than US$1,000 and not more than US$100,000 per false filing, plus 10% compounded penalty on unpaid franchise tax, judicial dissolution, and a 15% civil penalty on any amount of tax avoided; a certificate of good standing will be revoked until restitution is complete.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or authorize a false annual return are liable under the Business Corporations Act for misrepresentation penalties, may be ordered by the High Court of the Marshall Islands to pay the resulting tax, penalties, and costs personally, and may be barred from acting as a director of any Marshall Islands corporation for a period of five years; fraudulent intent elevates the misconduct to tax evasion under the criminal law with a maximum sentence of five years' imprisonment.
