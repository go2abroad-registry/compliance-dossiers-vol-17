Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Under the Business Corporations Act, every RMI corporation must continuously maintain a registered office and a licensed Registered Agent at a physical street address in the Marshall Islands. A virtual mailbox, post-office box, or purely electronic address is not acceptable for the statutory registered office. However, for a non-resident Business Corporation, the physical statutory office may be the office of the licensed Registered Agent, and the company may conduct its trade and cross-border factoring operations entirely remotely without establishing a substantive place of management in the RMI.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement is imposed under the Business Corporations Act; the Company may operate on subscribed capital, and for this factoring undertaking it is recommended to maintain an authorised capital of US$100,000 or 1,000,000 no-par-value shares. Funds are not required to be escrowed unless the registered agent or financing bank imposes a deposit condition, and such escrowed capital may be held in the Company's own account rather than a government escrow.

Funding & unlock path: The physical office must obtain a Fire Safety Certificate from the RMI Department of Fire and Emergency Preparedness, complete an annual evacuation drill, and secure a structural load inspection certificate from the Ministry of Public Works for the premises floor area where safes and record storage are installed. Occupational clearance must be issued by the Ministry of Health, confirming first-aid provision, sanitation, and compliance with workplace safety requirements for the office and any factoring in-floor records vault.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement is imposed under the Business Corporations Act; the Company may operate on subscribed capital, and for this factoring undertaking it is recommended to maintain an authorised capital of US$100,000 or 1,000,000 no-par-value shares. Funds are not required to be escrowed unless the registered agent or financing bank imposes a deposit condition, and such escrowed capital may be held in the Company's own account rather than a government escrow.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax and Revenue Office (TRO), Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (Majuro, RMI); filing is paper-based and there is no operational national e-filing taxpayer portal. The separate corporate registry is the Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Republic of the Marshall Islands.

E-government filing portal

An offshore non-resident Business Corporation is not required to obtain a tax identification number: its sole statutory identifier is the corporate registration number issued by the Registrar of Corporations upon filing articles of incorporation through a licensed Majuro registered agent. If the BC elects to carry on business within RMI, it must first obtain a Foreign Investment Business License, then file the prescribed business license application with the TRO, pay the gross-receipts license fee, and be issued a TIN; thereafter the company files annual license returns and, if it employs staff, registers as a wage and salary tax withholding employer.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone: a Marshall Islands Business Corporation that is beneficially owned by non-residents, transacts no business within RMI, and derives no income from sources within RMI is exempt from all Marshall Islands income, business license, and wage taxes; its only recurrent state imposts are the modest annual franchise fee and registered-agent fee paid to the corporate registry. — An offshore non-resident Business Corporation is not required to obtain a tax identification number: its sole statutory identifier is the corporate registration number issued by the Registrar of Corporations upon filing articles of incorporation through a licensed Majuro registered agent. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: a Marshall Islands Business Corporation that is beneficially owned by non-residents, transacts no business within RMI, and derives no income from sources within RMI is exempt from all Marshall Islands income, business license, and wage taxes; its only recurrent state imposts are the modest annual franchise fee and registered-agent fee paid to the corporate registry.

Available election forms

None equivalent to a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election: RMI tax law contains no entity classification election and no alignment with foreign hybrid rules. The operative practical mechanisms are (i) the incorporators' designation/resolution creating the BC as a non-resident, tax-exempt corporation and (ii) the optional later election to obtain an onshore Business License, which converts the entity from Tax_Exempt_Zone status to full RMI gross-receipts and wage-tax exposure; revocation of the license returns it to exempt status for future periods.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Not applicable: the Republic of the Marshall Islands does not operate a VAT, GST, or general Sales and Use Tax. The indirect fiscal levies are limited to customs import duties on physical imports into RMI and the Business License Tax, a non-creditable gross-receipts turnover tax (headline rate 3%) on persons carrying on business inside RMI.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD 0 — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because there is no VAT/GST regime. Customs duty registration applies to all importers of record without a de minimis value threshold, and Business License Tax liability attaches from the first US dollar of gross revenue derived from business activity carried on in RMI.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: absent a VAT/GST regime, RMI offers no export zero-rating, input-tax-credit, or VAT refund mechanism. Physical exports are exempt from customs duties when covered by an RMI export declaration, while cross-border factoring and reverse-factoring services (ISIC 6492) are sourced and performed outside RMI and therefore fall entirely outside the RMI tax base, requiring no zero-rating claim. RMI imposes no exchange control or statutory FX-repatriation deadline; the 180-day parameter is retained solely as the customary trade-finance settlement benchmark for cross-border receivable remittances.

  • None required for services: offshore factoring, discounting, and reverse-factoring settlements involve no RMI customs or tax documentation; the bank file (SWIFT MT103/MT202, escrow statements, and receivable assignment notices) constitutes the evidentiary record.
  • For physical goods exported through RMI customs territory: an RMI Customs Export Declaration, commercial invoice, and, where applicable, bill of lading or airway bill; the Marshall Islands does not tax exports.
  • Audit preservation bundle: executed factoring agreements, invoice assignment schedules, and inward settlement certificates are retained as anti-money-laundering evidence, given the Medium_Risk_Enhanced classification of the trade-finance activity.

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API reporting, continuous-transaction-control, or digital stamping mandate is in force; RMI customs and tax administration rely on paper declarations and manual records. Invoice copies must be retained by the taxpayer and produced on TRO demand, but no national fiscal database or approved invoicer certification is required.

There is no VAT refund to claw back, so the enforceable exposure is the status audit: if the TRO determines that the BC performed factoring services for RMI obligors or derived RMI-source fees, it will retroactively withdraw the Tax_Exempt_Zone classification and assess unpaid Business License Tax, wage and salary tax, statutory monthly interest from the original due dates, late-filing penalties, and civil fraud penalties. A knowingly false customs export declaration or forged shipment evidence exposes the entity to seizures, smuggling fines, and criminal prosecution; factoring files must therefore prove non-RMI obligor situs and offshore settlement to sustain the exemption.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility with zero statutory exchange controls: capital and current account transactions (including inward equity, factoring advances, and profit remittances) may be freely converted and transferred in USD; no repatriation permit, dividend tax, or remittance surcharge is imposed, the only operative hurdles being correspondent-bank AML/KYC screening and U.S. sanctions enforcement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation exists: export-related receivables and factoring proceeds may be retained offshore or repatriated freely in USD without mandatory conversion ratios or statutory timeline, as the Marshall Islands imposes neither exchange-control surrender rules nor obligatory repatriation windows.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands does not maintain a domestic central bank; the U.S. dollar is sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association, and banking supervision and foreign-exchange administration are exercised by the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Majuro.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High as a non-resident settlement conduit for receivables collections, low as a primary deposit keeper. Legal constraints: the Marshall Islands has no domestic EMI licensing regime, so an offshore digital EMI (EU/UK/Singapore licensed) may lawfully service a non-resident Business Corporation, provided the EMI does not take deposits from domestic residents and the BC maintains at least one licensed bank account as the principal clearing layer for factoring advances.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - RMI law imposes no Local File, Master File, or country-by-country reporting thresholds. The Income Tax Act contains a controlled-transaction allocation rule analogous to IRC Section 482, which applies only where RMI-source income is in issue. In practice, documentation is nonetheless required by the home jurisdiction of the beneficial owner (e.g., US CFC/GILTI, EU CFC/Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive rules) to substantiate arm's-length pricing of factoring discounts and guarantee fees.

Economic substance scope: The RMI has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations comparable to BVI/Cayman. Substance obligations arise from: (1) the Business Corporations Act requiring a registered office, registered agent, and annual reporting; and (2) the RMI AML/CFT legal framework, which treats trade receivables factoring as a financial business requiring licensing/FIU registration with substantive local governance, record-keeping, and risk-management functions. A letter-box BC lacking a licensed factoring operation and genuine management functions is vulnerable to Registrar strike-off, loss of the territorial tax exemption, and adverse treatment under OECD/EU good-governance reviews.
