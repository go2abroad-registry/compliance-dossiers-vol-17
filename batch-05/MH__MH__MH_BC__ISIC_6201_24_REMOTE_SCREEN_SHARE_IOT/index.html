Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is not permissible as the statutory registered office of an RMI Business Corporation. The BC must maintain a physical registered office in the Republic of the Marshall Islands with a licensed registered agent. Operational staff and the remote desktop/IoT software business may be managed remotely from any location, but service of process, corporate records, and the registered office address must remain at a physical RMI location. A virtual mailing address or remote-work location may be used commercially only if it is not designated as the statutory registered office.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required for a non-resident Business Corporation; nominal or no-par-value share capital is permitted, and no escrow deposit or bank-account blocking requirement applies before incorporation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety inspection and occupational workplace clearance are required for the leased office premises; structural load inspections do not apply to non-industrial office use.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required for a non-resident Business Corporation; nominal or no-par-value share capital is permitted, and no escrow deposit or bank-account blocking requirement applies before incorporation.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Treasury, and Customs of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (Revenue Division); corporate registry administered by the Office of the Associate Registrar of Corporations (Marshall Islands Corporate Registry). No national tax filing portal exists because the RMI levies no income tax, VAT, payroll tax, or capital tax on non-resident domestic corporations.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity obtains its sole statutory identifier by incorporating a Business Corporation (BC) under the Business Corporations Act and registering as a non-resident domestic corporation under the Non-Resident Domestic Corporations Act 2001 (NRDCA). No separate TIN or VAT ID is issued; the Registrar's certificate registration number is the sole fiscal identifier for banking, KYC, Stripe/Paddle onboarding, and all compliance purposes. No annual tax return, tax election filing, or iterative tax-ID procurement procedure applies to a tax-exempt non-resident BC.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — a non-resident domestic BC is categorically exempt from Marshall Islands income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, and all indirect taxes; taxation is neither pass-through nor entity-level for RMI purposes, as the BC's non-resident status is the exclusive determinant of tax immunity. — A foreign entity obtains its sole statutory identifier by incorporating a Business Corporation (BC) under the Business Corporations Act and registering as a non-resident domestic corporation under the Non-Resident Domestic Corporations Act 2001 (NRDCA). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — a non-resident domestic BC is categorically exempt from Marshall Islands income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, and all indirect taxes; taxation is neither pass-through nor entity-level for RMI purposes, as the BC's non-resident status is the exclusive determinant of tax immunity.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election and no hybrid-mismatch election exists under RMI law. The operative statutory election is designation of the BC as a non-resident domestic corporation (tax-exempt) versus a resident domestic corporation (technically taxable at nominal rates); foreign owners of remote software and IoT telemetry businesses must file and maintain non-resident status. No elective VAT or GST registration is available because no VAT/GST regime exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax in force. The only indirect-type levies are import customs and excise duties on physical goods, which never attach to remote screen-share or IoT telematic services delivered electronically.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — the Marshall Islands imposes no indirect-tax registration threshold; economic turnover concepts are inoperative for digital services, and a non-resident BC owes no VAT/GST registration or collection obligation regardless of global revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — with no VAT/GST in the Marshall Islands, no zero-rating claim or export VAT refund is required or available for remote screen-share software, telemetry, or IoT services. Income from such offshore digital services is exempt from RMI taxation by operation of the NRDCA, and a foreign-owned non-resident BC cannot incur RMI indirect tax on export receipts. No filing, ruling, or statutory application is required to invoke the exemption.

  • Customs Export Declaration — not applicable: RMI Customs does not process or require an export declaration for intangible software or telemetry services delivered electronically.
  • Bill of Lading — not applicable: no physical movement of goods occurs in remote desktop access services, and no shipping document can or should be generated.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate — not required: the RMI has no foreign-exchange controls and does not mandate repatriation certificates or central-bank approval for USD software subscription receipts.

None — there is no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API transmission, or digital stamping mandate in the RMI. A BC may issue paper or PDF invoices in any commercial format without tax-authority pre-approval; invoicing for remote desktop access and unattended-machine telemetry subscriptions is governed solely by contract law and by the customer's destination jurisdiction (e.g., EU digital-services VAT, US sales tax, or digital-services withholding in the customer's country).

No VAT zero-rating clawback can arise because no VAT is imposed in the RMI. The operative risks are status-based: filing a false non-resident declaration, abusing the non-resident election while deriving RMI-source income, or failing to maintain registered-agent and corporate records. Consequences include registry de-registration and strike-off, monetary penalties under the NRDCA, reinstatement costs, and director or agent liability. The 180-day figure in this dossier is a standardized pipeline placeholder carrying no RMI statutory force; US-source digital receipts remain independently subject to IRS enforcement and applicable US state sales-tax regimes.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The U.S. dollar is fully convertible across both current and capital account transactions. There are no exchange controls, surrender requirements, or central bank approvals. Dividend remittances, capital injections, and profit repatriation are unrestricted for non-resident-owned Business Corporations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies. Export receipts in U.S. dollars may be freely retained, credited to offshore accounts, or disbursed without reference to any central bank conversion requirement.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commission of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (Superintendent of Banking); no domestic central bank exists, and the U.S. dollar is official legal tender under the RMI–U.S. Compact of Free Association.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-to-Low. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., U.K. or E.U. e-money institutions) can provide rapid multi-currency collection points for software subscription revenue, but they do not constitute licensed banks in the Marshall Islands, offer no local guarantee of clearing finality, and are generally unable to support statutory capital escrow structures. Use is legally permissible when supported by contractual disclosure and full AML/KYC documentation, but a domestic or high-grade offshore banking relationship remains mandatory for robust liquidity and inbound wire verification.

Bank's due-diligence concern over IT services and software licensing payment flows

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound non-resident capital contributions should be routed as U.S.-dollar SWIFT MT103 credits to the RMI beneficiary bank or to a named U.S.-dollar clearing account held by the entity. Remittance purpose must state 'Capital contribution for software development and working capital—non-resident Business Corporation; no exchange-control approval required.' The sending institution must provide full beneficiary name, account number, and address, together with source-of-funds supporting documentation; the receiving RMI bank will conduct OFAC, sanctions, and internal large-credit verification before crediting the account.Outbound disbursements should settle through SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers, Fedwire/CHIPS for domestic U.S.-dollar clearing, or a Commercial Letter of Credit issued by the RMI relationship bank and confirmed by a reputable U.S. or E.U. bank for procurement of hardware or dedicated servers.
