Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands, Ministry of Finance – Revenue and Taxation Division (Official government portal: https://www.rmi.gov.mh)

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation operating as a Free Trade Zone Bonded Depot is not required to obtain a domestic Taxpayer Identification Number for ordinary income tax purposes, as it qualifies for the Tax-Exempt Zone classification. The statutory procurement SOP is: (i) apply for a Foreign Corporation Registration Certificate from the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act; (ii) submit an exemption confirmation letter to the Revenue and Taxation Division using Form TAX-EX-01, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, articles of association, good standing certificate, and passport copy of the ultimate beneficial owner; (iii) once the exemption is granted, the Revenue and Taxation Division issues an Exempt Entity Registration Number (EERN) which serves as the functional TIN for customs bonded licensing and banking counterparty verification; (iv) processing takes 5-10 business days and is renewable annually by filing a nil-profit declaration.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident Business Corporation operating as a Free Trade Zone Bonded Depot is not required to obtain a domestic Taxpayer Identification Number for ordinary income tax purposes, as it qualifies for the Tax-Exempt Zone classification. The statutory procurement SOP is: (i) apply for a Foreign Corporation Registration Certificate from the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act; (ii) submit an exemption confirmation letter to the Revenue and Taxation Division using Form TAX-EX-01, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, articles of association, good standing certificate, and passport copy of the ultimate beneficial owner; (iii) once the exemption is granted, the Revenue and Taxation Division issues an Exempt Entity Registration Number (EERN) which serves as the functional TIN for customs bonded licensing and banking counterparty verification; (iv) processing takes 5-10 business days and is renewable annually by filing a nil-profit declaration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the Business Corporations Act and the Fiscal Concessions Act 2001, an eligible non-resident corporation may file a one-time written election to be designated as a Non-Resident Exempt Corporation (referenced as the 'Exempt Company Election'). No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under Marshall Islands domestic law; however, an exempt company may voluntarily elect to be subject to the Gross Revenue Tax (GRT) at a rate of 3% on local-source receipts in order to enter into tax-sparing treaty arrangements or to satisfy counterparty substance requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gross Revenue Tax (GRT; a turnover-based sales tax) with customs duty applying at the border; no VAT/GST regime in force

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable to Tax-Exempt Zone operators. In the general local economy, any business with annual gross revenue exceeding USD 25,000 must register for GRT; however, bonded depot operators within the free trade zone are zero-rated and not subject to GRT registration, regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Eligibility for the 0% export zero-rating treatment is automatic for goods physically entering and leaving the Free Trade Zone Bonded Depot. The operator must (a) hold a valid Free Trade Zone licence issued by the Marshall Islands Development Authority; (b) hold a Customs Bonded Warehouse permit; (c) maintain separate inventory accounts for zone-origin goods; and (d) ensure that all exported goods leave the customs territory within 180 days of admission to the depot. Offshore services supplied by depot operators to non-resident clients may also be zero-rated where the contract is concluded outside the Marshall Islands and consideration is paid directly from a foreign bank account outside the jurisdiction.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Bonded Warehouse Movement Register
  • Free Trade Zone Inventory Control Slip

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate is imposed for exempt-zone transactions. Nonetheless, all customs-related documentation must be transmitted electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs clearance platform. Bonded warehouse movement records must be filed digitally on a quarterly basis to the Marshall Islands Customs Service, and export declarations must carry a digital signature and be matched to the free trade zone e-ledger.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims are subject to retrospective reassessments by the Revenue and Taxation Division for a period of up to six years from the year of export. Civil penalties include a 200% under-declaration surcharge and interest at 1.5% per month. Administrative sanctions include immediate revocation of the bonded depot licence and blacklisting of the responsible customs broker. Wilful evasion is a criminal offence under the Customs Act 1990, carrying imprisonment of up to five years and fines of up to USD 50,000.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Marshall Islands does not issue its own currency and maintains no statutory distinction between current-account and capital-account convertibility. All payments are executed in US dollars, and there are no exchange-control restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distributions, loan repayments, or capital repatriation for non-resident shareholders. Legal and administrative obstacles are minimal, but practical hurdles include stricter correspondent-bank due diligence on Marshall Islands entities due to jurisdiction reputation and the absence of a formal central-bank guarantee facility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory requirements for the surrender, conversion, or mandatory repatriation timelines for export proceeds. Bonded-depot operators may retain export earnings in US-dollar accounts indefinitely. No conversion obligation exists because the US dollar is the domestic currency.

Central bank supervision: There is no central bank or foreign exchange authority in the Republic of the Marshall Islands; the financial sector supervisor is the Banking Commission of the Marshall Islands Ministry of Finance. The US dollar is the sole legal tender and clearance ultimately relies on the US payments system, but the Federal Reserve exercises no direct supervisory authority over Marshall Islands-licensed banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. The Marshall Islands has no dedicated EMI, digital-bank, or e-money licensing framework. Offshore electronic money institutions cannot obtain a passported license locally, and any non-Marshall Islands entity holding client funds in the RMI would likely be deemed unlicensed deposit-taking, prohibited under the Marshall Islands Banking Act and Captive Insurance Act principles. An offshore EMI account may be used only for receiving corporate funds if it maintains a genuine USD correspondent relationship, but local regulated-entity payers and dedicated correspondent banks will typically reject a purely virtual-license EMI due to AML and jurisdiction-exposure concerns. Direct clearing for bonded-depot licensees is therefore not viable through unlicensed EMIs.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections into a Marshall Islands bonded-depot entity are USD-denominated. The compliant routing is a SWIFT MT103 remittance from the ultimate beneficial owner's or shareholder's personal or corporate bank account in the originating jurisdiction to the RMI bank's correspondent account (usually at a US clearing bank), followed by a Fedwire or CHIPS transfer to the RMI bank. The funds are credited to the RMI entity's operational account. The remittance reason code must accurately indicate 'equity injection' or 'shareholder loan' using SWIFT field 70 (Remittance Information). For inward investments, the standard IFM type is 'domestic direct investment' and the beneficiary must provide a board resolution, subscription agreement, or shareholder loan agreement. The RMI bank will require name-match screening of the originating financial institution, proof that the sending entity is not a shell or high-risk entity, and, if the amount exceeds USD 10,000, a Currency Transaction Report. No central-bank approval is needed.Outbound disbursements for bonded-depot trade settlements are executed through: (1) SWIFT MT103 for direct high-value interbank transfers covering payment to foreign suppliers, freight forwarders, or customs duty service providers; (2) MT202 COV for correspondent-bank head-to-head USD clearing when the receiving bank is outside the US; (3) commercial letters of credit (LC) issued by the RMI bank's US dollar correspondent for importing goods into the bonded depot, with the beneficiary's bank receiving an MT700; and (4) local clearing via the Marshall Islands Clearing System (run by the Bank of Marshall Islands) for domestic payments to RMI suppliers, utilities, and the RMI government. Each outgoing payment must show a valid purpose description matching the goods or service category in the bonded-depot license. The bank will perform sanctions screening against OFAC, UN, EU, and RMI consolidated lists. The lawful use of an internal corporate account for cross-border settlement is not permitted without a separate money-transmitter license.No hedging is required for USD/USD exposures; as the RMI is a dollarized jurisdiction, the functional currency for all accounts and contracts is the US dollar.For non-USD receivables (e.g., from APAC or European supply chains), Marshall Islands corporations may enter into outright forwards with a non-RMI international bank that maintains a USD clearing correspondent; the forward is directly settled in USD through a master agreement.Non-deliverable forwards (NDF) are permitted only as an offshore risk-management artifact, not through an RMI licensed bank, because there is no local onshore RMB or other non-USD market; NDF contracts would be cleared through Singapore or Hong Kong and recorded as a hedge of the underlying trade receivable or payable.Cross-currency interest rate swaps are permissible only for a debt facility exceeding USD 5 million, executed under an ISDA master agreement with a high-grade foreign counterparty; local bond and swap clearing infrastructure does not exist.Plain-vanilla leveraged or speculative currency swaps are not permitted for a non-resident Business Corporation unless the transaction is backed by a real commercial flow and the foreign counterparty confirms an exemption from the RMI's lack of securities licensing.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold (USD 0); RMI does not impose a domestic Local File/Master File requirement. If the entity becomes taxable, OECD-aligned documentation must support pricing of intercompany storage, logistics, and IP royalties

Economic substance scope: No separate Economic Substance Regulations filing regime applies to non-resident BCs. However, the FTZ bonded depot licence requires a physical warehouse or locker in the FTZ, a licensed customs bonded operator, a resident agent, a registered office in RMI, and books and records maintained in RMI; substance is evidenced by payroll, warehouse lease, inventory records, and customs filing logs

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Exemption Abuse

Statutory sanction: Re-assessment of unpaid gross revenue tax for the applicable limitation period; late filing penalty of 25%; negligence penalty of 10%; fraud penalty of 50%; monthly interest of 1.5%; revocation of the FTZ licence; criminal fine for wilful evasion of up to USD 50,000

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign false returns or cause fraudulent exemption claims are jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax debt; courts may pierce the corporate veil and order civil restitution, asset seizure, travel bans, and imprisonment for up to one year per count
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Cancellation of the Business Corporation registration and FTZ licence; forfeiture of the customs bond or comparable security; administrative penalties under the RMI Business Corporations Act and Customs Act; deregistration by the Registrar

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for customs duties and taxes when the corporation is treated as an agent or alter ego; travel restrictions and director disqualification orders may be imposed
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Criminal Code and Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act: fines up to USD 500,000 or the value of the laundered property; forfeiture of assets; licence revocation; imprisonment up to 25 years for organised money laundering

Director personal liability: Directors who direct, consent to, or benefit from the scheme may be prosecuted as principals; personal civil restitution equal to the laundered value plus punitive damages; international travel bans and extradition may apply
