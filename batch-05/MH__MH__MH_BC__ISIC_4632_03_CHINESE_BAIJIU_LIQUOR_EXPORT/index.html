Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Marshall Islands Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance, Majuro Atoll; document-based filing accepted at the Majuro office, with no centralized public e-filing portal for indirect tax registration

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Business Corporation deriving no Marshall Islands-source gross revenue is not required to obtain a Marshall Islands Tax Identification Number. If the entity conducts any local business, imports goods into RMI, or maintains a physical presence, it must file the Gross Revenue Tax registration form with the Revenue and Taxation Division, together with: (1) certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation; (2) certificate of good standing from the RMI Registrar; (3) completed Business License application if trading within RMI; (4) executed Resident Agent appointment form. Processing is typically 5 to 10 business days. No VAT ID exists because the RMI does not impose VAT or GST.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident Business Corporation deriving no Marshall Islands-source gross revenue is not required to obtain a Marshall Islands Tax Identification Number. If the entity conducts any local business, imports goods into RMI, or maintains a physical presence, it must file the Gross Revenue Tax registration form with the Revenue and Taxation Division, together with: (1) certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation; (2) certificate of good standing from the RMI Registrar; (3) completed Business License application if trading within RMI; (4) executed Resident Agent appointment form. Processing is typically 5 to 10 business days. No VAT ID exists because the RMI does not impose VAT or GST.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists. The RMI Business Corporations Act permits a foreign corporation to domesticate as an RMI domestic corporation, but that election does not itself create RMI-source taxation absent local business activity. A non-resident Business Corporation automatically qualifies for exemption from RMI income tax and Gross Revenue Tax on offshore export income without filing an election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax; the RMI imposes a Gross Revenue Tax (GRT) on RMI-source business receipts and customs import duty on physical importation

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD 0; no de minimis turnover threshold. Any person or entity deriving gross revenue attributable to sources within the Marshall Islands must register for Gross Revenue Tax. Offshore export sales of Chinese baijiu by a non-resident Business Corporation, concluded and delivered outside RMI, are outside the scope of GRT and trigger no registration requirement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. The Marshall Islands does not operate a VAT/GST system, so there is no export zero-rating mechanism and no output tax to reclaim. For Gross Revenue Tax purposes, an export transaction is excluded from the tax base only where the sale is negotiated, invoiced, and delivered outside the Marshall Islands with no RMI-source service component. The exporter should maintain board resolutions, purchase orders, offshore INCOTERMS, and destination port documents to substantiate the offshore character of the transaction.

  • RMI Customs Service Export Declaration (required for any physical export from a Marshall Islands port)
  • Bill of Lading (original or authenticated electronic copy showing foreign port of discharge)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/Letter of Credit payment confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list referencing the foreign buyer and destination
  • Signed purchase order or distribution agreement demonstrating offshore contract formation

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping regime applies. The Revenue and Taxation Division accepts paper and PDF-based records; taxpayers must retain source invoices, contracts, bills of lading, and payment proofs for at least six years.

The RMI has no VAT-related export refund to claw back. However, falsely classifying RMI-source trading or service receipts as offshore export income, or misrepresenting physical exports in customs declarations, exposes the entity to reassessment of unpaid Gross Revenue Tax and customs duty, statutory interest, civil fraud penalties, and criminal prosecution under RMI revenue and customs law. A material false declaration may also lead to suspension or revocation of the Business Corporation's registration and referral to the Attorney General. The Revenue and Taxation Division may audit supporting export documentation for up to six years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current accounts are fully convertible in USD. The RMI has no exchange-control legislation, and non-resident Business Corporations (BCs) face no statutory approval, conversion ratio, or repatriation restriction on dividends, profit distributions, shareholder loans, royalties, or trade settlements. Practical remittance friction arises exclusively from correspondent-bank de-risking and enhanced AML documentation for alcohol-export proceeds cleared through the US banking system, not from regulatory exchange controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or deposit obligation applicable to export receipts in the RMI. A non-resident BC may retain all export proceeds in any USD or freely convertible foreign-currency account and may apply them directly to supplier settlements, subject only to the record-keeping and wire-transfer reporting thresholds prescribed by the RMI AML/CFT Act 2018.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) maintains no domestic central bank and issues no sovereign currency. Banking supervision is administered by the Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, under the Banking Act 1988, as amended; AML/CFT supervision is exercised by the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act 2018. The US Federal Reserve functions as the de facto monetary authority because the US dollar is the sole legal tender circulating in the RMI by virtue of the Compact of Free Association with the United States.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High feasibility for a non-resident RMI BC with fully remote onboarding, subject to strict industry filtering. US-licensed and non-US licensed fintech EMIs and multi-currency clearing platforms (Mercury, Airwallex, Currenxie, Wise Business-type analogs) generally accept RMI-incorporated entities that supply a certified registry extract, registered-agent certificate, board resolution, full UBO declaration, and biometric video KYC; approval typically completes within 2-5 business days. Legal constraints: no RMI banking licence is required to hold an offshore account, but EMI partner banks may independently decline due to residual RMI AML-risk ratings; alcohol-industry coding triggers automated enhanced due diligence; and EMIs generally cannot issue documentary credits, so the Letter-of-Credit payment rail must be executed through a licensed trade-finance bank.
