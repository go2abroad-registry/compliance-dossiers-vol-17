Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Registrar of Corporations, Republic of the Marshall Islands (portal: www.register-iri.com, administered by International Registries, Inc.); Customs and Revenue Division, Ministry of Finance, Majuro, RMI. The RMI maintains no income-tax filing authority for non-resident business corporations.

E-government filing portal

No statutory TIN or VAT ID is issued by the RMI to non-resident Business Corporations (BCs) deriving exclusively foreign-source ship crew recruitment revenue. A foreign entity obtains: (1) a Certificate of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act from the Registrar of Corporations; (2) a Foreign Investment Business License (FIBL) from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade if any on-island presence exists; and (3) a Business Privilege Tax license only when physical business activity occurs on-island. The standard documentation package comprises a Certificate of Good Standing, a service-company affidavit, and the FIBL where applicable.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (Foreign Income Tax Act non-resident), taxed only on RMI-source income; ship crew recruitment and crew management services performed entirely offshore are outside the tax base. — No statutory TIN or VAT ID is issued by the RMI to non-resident Business Corporations (BCs) deriving exclusively foreign-source ship crew recruitment revenue. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (Foreign Income Tax Act non-resident), taxed only on RMI-source income; ship crew recruitment and crew management services performed entirely offshore are outside the tax base.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under RMI law. The BC's tax status is governed by the non-resident declaration filed with the Registrar under the Business Corporations Act; an RMI LLC may alternatively file an election to be treated as a foreign disregarded investment vehicle, yielding no RMI entity-level taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax regime; the only indirect fiscal charges are RMI import customs duties on physical goods and the 3% Business Privilege Tax on gross receipts of on-island business activity.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in the RMI. The Business Privilege Tax imposes licensing from the first US Dollar (USD 0 threshold) of on-island gross receipts; pure offshore crew recruitment services do not trigger registration. Monetary amounts and records are USD-denominated.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — absent a VAT regime, there is no export VAT zero-rating claim. For vessels under the RMI flag, crew recruitment, payroll, and crew-management services supplied to non-resident shipowners are deemed performed wholly outside the Republic and are outside both the Foreign Income Tax Act and Business Privilege Tax bases; no refund mechanism exists to substantiate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (physical goods only; not applicable to crew recruitment services)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (physical shipments only)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank USD wire credit evidence retained for the RMI Maritime Administrator's crew-payroll verification)

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate exists. Physical import clearance runs through the Customs and Revenue Division's automated customs system (ASYCUDA) for goods only; crew recruitment service invoices remain standard commercial documents without government clearance or cryptographic stamping.

The RMI has no fraudulent zero-rating penalty because no zero-rating mechanism exists. Mischaracterizing on-island Business Privilege Tax-liable revenue as offshore triggers a 3% gross-receipts assessment, 12% annual interest, revocation of non-resident status, and a fine not exceeding USD 10,000 under the Business Privilege Tax Act; repeated false declarations expose the crew agency license to suspension by the RMI Maritime Administrator. The 180-day FX reference reflects the Maritime Administrator's maximum acceptable documentary gap between invoice date and inward remittance credit to the agency's USD account.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility for both capital and current account transactions because the RMI uses the U.S. dollar and imposes no foreign exchange controls. There are no statutory limits on inbound capital subscription or on outbound profit remittance. Non-resident Business Corporations have no RMI income tax liability and RMI law imposes no withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or service fees. The only practical hurdles are not statutory but regulatory and bank-driven, namely U.S. correspondent bank due diligence under the Bank Secrecy Act and OFAC compliance expectations, and the absence of a domestic interbank FX market, which forces all foreign currency conversion to be executed offshore.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The RMI maintains no exchange control regime, no export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory timeline for repatriation. Receipts from international crew recruitment and manning agency services may be retained indefinitely in USD or any freely convertible currency in any jurisdiction without any obligation to convert or surrender funds to a licensed bank. The only controlling obligations are the contractual terms of the underlying banking facility and standard FATF-aligned transaction monitoring, which does not impose surrender but does require transparent documentation of the source and purpose of funds.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commissioner, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI), acting in coordination with the U.S. Federal Reserve System and the U.S. Department of the Treasury. The RMI maintains no domestic central bank; the U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender under the Compact of Free Association, and monetary policy is effectively set by the United States.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Viability: LOW-to-MEDIUM. Legal constraints: (a) The RMI has no e-money or payment-institution licensing framework; deposit-taking is reserved to banks licensed under the RMI Banking Act, so foreign EMIs have no passporting rights into the RMI. (b) A non-resident Marshall Islands Business Corporation is generally treated as a higher-risk customer by offshore digital EMIs, and leading providers (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) typically decline RMI-incorporated entities or condition acceptance on substantive presence in a recognized jurisdiction such as the United States. (c) All USD settlement ultimately clears through the U.S. Federal Reserve network, exposing EMI providers to U.S. correspondent and sanctions compliance risk. (d) The RMI offers no deposit insurance. Recommended work-around: maintain the primary operating and treasury account at a licensed RMI bank with a U.S. correspondent chain, and use offshore EMIs only as secondary disbursement tools, with each EMI position fully mirrored by an RMI bank master account and subject to daily reconciliation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant USD routing is executed exclusively by SWIFT MT103 (customer transfer) or MT202 with cover through a U.S. correspondent bank because the RMI has no RTGS system. A recommended chain uses Bank of Guam (ABA 121405105) or Bank of Hawaii as the receiving correspondent, crediting the MH_BC account at the licensed RMI bank. The remitter name on the SWIFT message must match the shareholder KYC record on file, and field 70 remittance information must state 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - NON-RESIDENT BUSINESS CORPORATION' or similar. The receiving bank will require, before release of funds, a board resolution approving the capital injection, UBO source-of-funds documentation, and evidence of the statutory crew recruitment license. No RMI capital duty, stamp duty, or registration tax applies, and no statutory purpose-code regime exists, so the remittance narrative must follow the receiving bank's own compliance template.Primary clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 for crew payroll runs, principal shipowner settlements, and vendor payments. MT202 or MT202 COV is used only for funding intermediate accounts and interbank positions between the RMI bank and its U.S. or Asian correspondents. Commercial letters of credit may be opened for large manning service contracts where the shipowner requires documentary proof of shipment of crewing services, but routine payroll dispersal is executed through MT103 push payments to beneficiary banks. For multi-currency crew payroll, funds are swept from the USD treasury account to a licensed payout aggregator or the crew member's local bank using local real-time rails: PESONet in the Philippines, BI-FAST in Indonesia, IMPS/RTP in India, SEPA in eurozone jurisdictions, and FedACH/CHIPS in the United States. Every outbound MT103 must carry structured ISO 20022 remittance information including crew name, payroll contract reference, and period, and must pass OFAC, UN, EU, and UK sanctions screening before release. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — the Marshall Islands (RMI) levies no corporate income tax; a non-resident domestic Business Corporation is statutorily exempt from all taxes on income under Section 213 of the RMI Business Corporations Act.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the RMI imposes no dividend withholding tax; distributions declared by a non-resident Business Corporation to shareholders are entirely outside the RMI withholding framework.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — the RMI has no enacted transfer-pricing statute, no Local File/Master File filing obligation, and no statutory threshold in local currency (the functional currency is the U.S. dollar). Nevertheless, the BC must contemporaneously document intercompany commission rates, payroll-funding arrangements, and margin benchmarks, because the crew-buyer's home jurisdiction will apply its own arm's-length rules to the agency fee.

Economic substance scope: The RMI applies an OECD/EU-aligned Economic Substance framework. A non-resident BC carrying on any RMI relevant activity, including crew supply or vessel management connected to RMI-flagged tonnage, must demonstrate locally directed and managed operations, adequate employees and premises, proportionate expenditure, and must file an annual economic substance return. A pure offshore recruitment and payroll agency operating from a foreign head office, with no RMI management or core income-generating activity, is exempt from the charging net but must retain payroll contracts, KYC records, and substance evidence to withstand foreign tax authority review.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Facilitation of Foreign Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the RMI Criminal Code for money laundering and fraudulent dealing: fines up to USD 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 25 years, plus civil forfeiture of wire-transfer proceeds and commission income.

Director personal liability:
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and deregistration by the Registrar for annual-report default; court-ordered restoration with penalties; economic-substance penalties for failure to file the annual substance return; foreign tax authorities may disregard the BC as a sham and reallocate the agency profit to the real operating entity.

Director personal liability:
AML/CFT Breach in Payroll and Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Fines and regulatory sanctions under the RMI AML/CFT framework, suspension or revocation of the crew recruitment license and RMI vessel manning approvals, per-day fines for beneficial-ownership record failures, and naming by the RMI Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability:
Unlicensed Maritime Crew Recruitment

Statutory sanction: Statutory fines and imprisonment under RMI maritime and seafarer protection legislation; the Maritime Administrator may detain the vessel, order crew disembarkation, void the manning agreement, and register unpaid seafarer wages as a maritime lien against the vessel.
