Competent corporate registry

Office of the Registrar of Corporations (RMI Registrar), Majuro, Republic of the Marshall Islands; filings are submitted through licensed RMI Registered Agents.

Governing statute

Business Corporations Act 1990 (as amended), codified in Title 48 of the Marshall Islands Revised Code 2021: sections 31-33 (incorporators and Articles of Incorporation), section 35 (Certificate of Incorporation and correction), section 47 (consideration for shares), sections 55-57 (registered office and registered agent), and section 66 (officers).

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation, executed by the incorporator and acknowledged before a notary public, filed with the Registrar of Corporations; the Registrar issues the Certificate of Incorporation as conclusive evidence of corporate existence.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands Ministry of Finance, Revenue and Tax Division (Tax and Customs Office); official portal: https://www.rmi-tax.com

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a non-resident Business Corporation under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act must file Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations, appoint a resident registered agent, and pay the applicable filing fee. Following incorporation, the entity must obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by submitting Form REG-1 to the Revenue and Tax Division. Non-resident corporations not engaged in business within the Marshall Islands are not required to obtain a business license and are generally not subject to domestic taxation beyond annual government maintenance fees.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Marshall Islands law. The non-resident Business Corporation is automatically treated as a separate taxable person for the limited purposes of the Gross Revenue Tax; however, by statute, gross revenue derived from sources outside the Republic of the Marshall Islands is expressly excluded, effectively conferring tax-exempt status on the offshore activity. No elective pass-through regime is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (No VAT, GST, sales tax, or other general indirect tax is imposed by the Republic of the Marshall Islands)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — the Marshall Islands imposes no value-added tax, goods and services tax, or general sales tax. Therefore no economic turnover threshold exists for indirect tax registration. The only local revenue measures are the Gross Revenue Tax (applicable only to revenue sourced within the Marshall Islands) and the Business License Tax (exempt for non-resident business corporations).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — as the Marshall Islands does not operate a VAT/GST system, there is no export zero-rating mechanism, no output tax to relieve, and no input tax credit or refund scheme. Exporters of die-cast scale automotive models from the Marshall Islands are not subject to any indirect tax on export proceeds. The only customs-relevant obligation is compliance with the RMI Customs and Excise Regulations regarding export declarations and movement of goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (where required under RMI Customs Regulations)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing shipment from the Marshall Islands
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (only if preferential tariff treatment is claimed in the destination jurisdiction)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing export proceeds

Not applicable — the Marshall Islands has no electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping regime for indirect tax purposes. Invoicing for exported die-cast scale models follows ordinary commercial practice, with no government fiscal clearance requirement.

Because there is no export VAT zero-rating, there is no specific VAT clawback or zero-rating fraud penalty. However, false declarations to the RMI Customs Service regarding exported goods are subject to seizure, customs penalties, and potential criminal fines under the Customs and Excise Regulations. Under the Gross Revenue Tax and Business License framework, failure to file accurate returns or maintain adequate export documentation may result in assessment of tax on revenue that the entity claims as non-resident-source, plus interest and penalties of up to 25% of the unpaid amount; fraudulent documentation may attract criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender and the domestic currency of the Marshall Islands. The RMI imposes no exchange-control regime, no distinction between current and capital account categories, and no licensing requirement for foreign exchange transactions. Profit repatriation, dividend distributions, and capital withdrawals are freely permitted in USD. Practical friction is limited to compliance with U.S. OFAC sanctions, anti-money laundering (AML) due diligence, and correspondent-bank restrictions applicable to cross-border transfers routed through the U.S. banking system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirements, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no requirement to repatriate export earnings to a domestic bank account. Exporters may lawfully retain USD receipts in any jurisdictionally admissible bank; however, corporate accounts opened in the RMI may be subject to contractual bank covenants requiring visibility of trade documents for AML purposes.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commission of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (statutory banking and foreign exchange supervisory authority; the RMI is a dollarized economy and maintains no independent central bank)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low viability. The RMI has no bespoke e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime and does not recognize foreign digital EMI e-money accounts as bank accounts for corporate treasury purposes. Non-resident fintech platforms not holding an RMI banking license cannot clear transactions inside the domestic USD banking system. Platforms such as PayPal may be used as receivables collection tools but are not permissible primary clearing rails for trade settlements. A regulated RMI banking relationship is mandatory for liquidity management, capital injection escrow, and documentary trade settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections must be wired in USD via SWIFT-compliant banking channels to the RMI operating or escrow account. The remitter should use the appropriate cross-border purpose codes required by the sending jurisdiction; within the RMI no FX approval is required, but U.S. correspondent banks may require a beneficiary-specific instruction leading with 'Beneficiary: [MH_BC entity full name], Account Number [XX], Bank Reference [XX]' and any applicable OFAC screening reference. A board resolution authorizing the capital contribution, an escrow agreement (if funds are held pending a condition precedent), and a funds-receipt certificate must be retained to evidence lawful source and purpose. For collection of trade receivables from international distributors, e-commerce marketplace settlements, and licensing royalties, the routing preference is SWIFT MT103 to the operational USD account; payment gateways must be configured to sweep balances daily into the bank account to avoid non-bank float risks.Settlement for foundry, tooling, packaging, logistics, and licensing payments should be made through the RMI operational account using SWIFT MT103 wire transfers for direct supplier disbursements. For larger or contractually complex transactions, a commercial letter of credit (irrevocable documentary credit) issued by a tier-1 correspondent bank is the preferred clearing rail, with MT700 and MT799 messages for negotiation and amendment. Standby letters of credit may be used for advance payments. Domestic RMI payments are settled in USD on a real-time gross basis via the bank's internal ledger; all disbursements to non-RMI suppliers must clear through the international SWIFT network and U.S. Fedwire/CHIPS infrastructure. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

3% flat corporate rate on taxable income derived from sources within the Marshall Islands (RMI Income Tax Act 1989); the rate applies only to RMI-source income and is structurally neutralized for a Non-Resident Business Corporation.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the RMI Income Tax Act imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Business Corporation to shareholders resident outside the Marshall Islands; a taxable RMI-source dividend arises only where the corporation derives income from RMI real property or RMI government contracts, which is structurally avoided by the offshore BC.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on the anniversary of the date of incorporation; corporations on the legacy cycle must file by the uniform 30 September registry deadline. No extension is available beyond the statutory anniversary date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be filed by the 15th day of the fourth month following the close of the taxable year (calendar year ending 31 December unless a different fiscal year is approved by the Minister).

A Non-Resident BC may deduct only expenses incurred inside the RMI; foreign manufacturing costs, molds, tampo tooling, and group overhead booked offshore are not deductible against any positive RMI base. Late filing: USD 100 per month, capped at USD 1,000; late payment: 1% monthly interest plus a 10% negligence penalty; willful non-filing is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to USD 5,000 and/or imprisonment for up to one year.
Line 3 Taxable income before exemption (Line 1 minus Line 2). For a compliant offshore BC this line is zero; attach the annual AR-1 receipt as supporting evidence. Late filing: USD 100 per month, capped at USD 1,000; late payment: 1% monthly interest plus a 10% negligence penalty; willful non-filing is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to USD 5,000 and/or imprisonment for up to one year.
Line 4 Income tax computed at 3% on Line 3. Compute only if positive RMI-source taxable income exists; otherwise insert USD 0. Late filing: USD 100 per month, capped at USD 1,000; late payment: 1% monthly interest plus a 10% negligence penalty; willful non-filing is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to USD 5,000 and/or imprisonment for up to one year.

Tick the election and attach a signed declaration confirming that the place of effective management, production facility, and principal hazard of business are located outside the RMI; the effective tax rate is 0% on the full foreign-source net profit. Late filing: USD 100 per month, capped at USD 1,000; late payment: 1% monthly interest plus a 10% negligence penalty; willful non-filing is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to USD 5,000 and/or imprisonment for up to one year.

List transactions above USD 250,000 per counterparty; attach an arm's-length benchmark if a foreign tax authority challenges the transfer price. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory RMI transfer-pricing documentation threshold is codified for Non-Resident Business Corporations. Best-practice trigger levels: prepare a Local File where annual RMI-related-party transactions exceed USD 250,000, and prepare Master File/transfer-pricing policy at the group level where consolidated worldwide revenue exceeds USD 100,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The RMI Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply to RMI tax-resident entities carrying on relevant activities (banking, insurance, shipping, pure equity holding, IP holding, headquarters, financing/leasing, and distribution/service centers). Die-cast manufacturing of physical scale models is not a listed relevant activity, but a trading-heavy distribution model can be recharacterized as a distribution and service center if inventory is held or purchasing decisions are made through an RMI presence. A Non-Resident BC that files the annual ESR notification and proves a foreign place of effective management and foreign tax residence remains exempt from the substance test; failing the ESR notification exposes the entity to the 3% RMI-source tax and penalties up to USD 50,000.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Civil fraud penalty of 50% of the underpaid tax plus monthly interest of 1%; criminal conviction carries a fine of up to USD 10,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 3 years; the Revenue and Taxation Division may reconstruct RMI-source gross income from the last 6 years.

Director personal liability: Under the fraudulent-trading rule of the Business Corporations Act, directors signing or directing the false return are jointly and severally liable for the full corporate tax debt; the High Court may impose a departure-prohibition order and asset freeze against the director pending settlement and may order personal restitution.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty of USD 10,000 per relevant activity, capped at USD 50,000; suspension of the certificate of good standing; a second-year default leads to mandatory striking-off and asset forfeiture to the liquidator under the ESR Enforcement Manual.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for outstanding ESR penalties after dissolution; the High Court may disqualify a director from managing any RMI company for up to 5 years and issue an international travel restriction based on unpaid regulatory penalties.
Anti-Money Laundering and Trade-Based Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Anti-Money Laundering Act and Criminal Code, money laundering is punishable by imprisonment for up to 20 years and a fine of up to USD 500,000; the die-cast inventory, injection molds, tampo-printing equipment, and bank balances are subject to criminal forfeiture; the company faces debarment from the licensed registered-agent network.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced automatically upon a money-laundering conviction; directors, beneficial owners, and the appointed AML compliance officer are personally indictable, subject to bench-warrant travel bans, pre-trial asset seizure, and personal restitution judgments equal to the full laundered amount.
Annual Report Default and Administrative Dissolution

Statutory sanction: Late-payment surcharge of 10% per commenced month (minimum USD 200, maximum USD 2,000); after 12 months of continuous default, the Registrar administratively dissolves the corporation; contracts concluded after dissolution are unenforceable and the corporate name may be re-registered by a third party.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue trading for more than 60 days after service of the dissolution notice are personally liable for all debts contracted during that period; the Minister of Finance may also issue a certificate of tax indebtedness enforceable personally against the directors in the High Court.
