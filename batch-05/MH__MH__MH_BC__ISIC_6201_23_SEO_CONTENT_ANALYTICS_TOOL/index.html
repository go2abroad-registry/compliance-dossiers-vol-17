Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Ministry of Finance — Revenue and Taxation Division, Majuro Atoll, in conjunction with the RMI Office of the Registrar of Corporations (Business Corporations Act registry) administered through licensed registered agents. There is no national online tax filing portal; filings are paper-based or agent-mediated.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate a Business Corporation as a non-resident domestic corporation under the Business Corporations Act (BCCA) through a licensed Registered Agent; the Certificate of Incorporation carries the RMI corporate registration number. Step 2: File the annual corporate renewal and pay the fixed annual government fee to the Registrar of Corporations; this renewal is the operative ongoing compliance identifier. Step 3: No income-tax TIN is statutorily required for a non-resident BC engaged in offshore activity, and no VAT identification number exists because the RMI imposes no VAT or GST. Step 4: Only if an RMI nexus later materializes (RMI-source income, local payroll, or physical importation of goods) must the BC apply to the Revenue and Taxation Division for a TIN and file the corresponding returns, withholding, or customs declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. The RMI does not provide a US-style check-the-box election, transparent-entity election, or hybrid-mismatch election. Exemption is automatic and statutory at incorporation provided the corporation is non-resident-owned, has no RMI-source gross income, and conducts no business within the RMI. A prospective re-designation as a Resident Domestic Corporation is available but would trigger RMI domestic tax exposure to the extent of RMI-source activities. There are no hybrid entity classification regimes under the BCCA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — the RMI levies no VAT, GST, or general sales-and-use tax. The only indirect fiscal levy is the Customs Import Duty on physical goods entering the RMI, which does not apply to exported SaaS or digitally delivered SEO analytics services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT or GST statute exists, so no registration threshold applies; customs import duty obligations arise only upon actual physical importation of goods into the RMI and have no application to offshore digital service revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT zero-rating mechanism because the RMI has no VAT or GST. The BC's cross-border supply of the SEO Content Analytics Tool (ISIC 6201 / NAICS 541511) is entirely outside the RMI taxing jurisdiction while the corporation remains non-resident, maintains no RMI office, staff, or assets, generates no RMI-source income, and delivers services through foreign cloud infrastructure. There is no export VAT charge that could be zero-rated and consequently no refund claim to file.

  • Customs Export Declaration — required only for physical goods departing an RMI port; not applicable to cloud-delivered software subscriptions but retained where data-collection hardware or proxy servers transit the RMI.
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill — applicable only where physical analytics infrastructure moves through an RMI port; otherwise the commercial SaaS subscription agreement and delivery records evidence cross-border service supply.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank FX settlement records — evidencing receipt of subscription fees from non-resident customers through payment rails such as Stripe or Paddle within 180 days of invoice issuance.
  • Non-Resident Status Evidence Bundle — Certificate of Incorporation, annual corporate renewal receipt from the Registrar of Corporations, Registered Agent confirmation, and a board resolution confirming the absence of RMI business activity and RMI-source income.

None — there is no national electronic invoicing mandate, real-time fiscal clearance requirement, tax-authority API integration, or digital stamping regime. Invoices may be issued in any commercially acceptable format. Records must be retained with sufficient detail to substantiate non-resident status and the offshore source of service revenue under BCCA books-and-records requirements; seven-year retention is the prudent market practice for the SaaS export model.

No RMI VAT clawback exists because no export-VAT refund mechanism applies to this structure. Material compliance risks: (i) if the Registrar of Corporations or the Revenue and Taxation Division determines that the BC conducted actual business in the RMI, derived RMI-source income, or used property located in the RMI, the non-resident exemption is lost retrospectively, exposing the entity to RMI tax assessments, statutory non-resident withholding obligations, municipal business license fees, customs import duties, and social-security contributions on any RMI-based personnel; (ii) false certificates, inaccurate annual filings, or failure to maintain records attract administrative fines, deregistration, and reinstatement penalties under the BCCA; (iii) directors and registered agents making fraudulent declarations may face criminal liability; (iv) under RMI tax information exchange agreements, beneficial ownership and revenue data may be exchanged with a foreign tax authority, enabling retrospective foreign tax clawback with interest and penalties where the RMI structure lacks economic substance. No RMI refund remedy exists for taxes never imposed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility - the RMI does not operate any foreign exchange control. Both current account and capital account transactions are unrestricted, and there are no profit remittance hurdles, registration requirements, or official FX approval procedures. Dividends and capital distributions to non-resident shareholders can be paid without local permission; only standard commercial bank AML/CFT and beneficial-ownership verification apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable - RMI imposes no statutory surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline on export proceeds. Export receipts may be retained in USD or any other freely convertible currency; banks must retain transaction records for anti-money-laundering/CTF purposes under RMI AML obligations.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI). The RMI uses the U.S. Dollar (USD) as legal tender; banking supervision is vested in the RMI Banking Commissioner under the Banking Act 1991.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-Medium - Cross-border fintech EMI providers (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Mercury, Payoneer) are not locally licensed in RMI and do not offer native RMI accounts. An RMI Business Corporation may be eligible to open an account with a non-U.S. EMI under the EMI's own foreign-company KYC policy, but mainstream EMIs commonly decline RMI offshore-registered entities because of perceived offshore-banking risk, substance expectations, and tax-facilitation concerns. For a core treasury, a Marshall Islands domestic bank account or a U.S. correspondent account obtained through an introducer is more reliable.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions from shareholder accounts should be wired to the RMI BC's USD account at the receiving bank using SWIFT MT103 (unsecured) or MT202 (if the receiving account is at the correspondent level). The wire should include the receiving bank's BIC/ABA, the named BC ('MH_BC'), and an unambiguous purpose code in the 70 field, such as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - MH_BC - EQUITY INJECTION' or, where the receiving bank accepts it, '/INV/ CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION'. Prior to the wire, the beneficiary bank must hold the account-opening KYC package and a board resolution to verify the source of funds. For funds routed through U.S. correspondents, attach OFAC-compliant wording and ensure the ordering customer is not blocked; no RMI central-bank clearance is required.SaaS platform disbursements (vendor costs, independent contractor payments, merchant settlement returns, dividends) should clear via SWIFT MT103 (customer transfer) using the RMI bank's U.S. correspondent on Fedwire/CHIPS. For merchant settling through Stripe/Paddle, the payment processors aggregate card/ACH payments and remit to the RMI BC's designated USD payout account through their payout banking partner; this route is treated as an operational settlement and must be disclosed to the main bank as a predetermined payment rail. Commercial letters of credit are not a required rail for SaaS/SEO analytics subscriptions; electronic invoices, subscription billing, and SWIFT transfers provide the appropriate clearing mechanism. Tax Evasion / False Non-Resident Declaration

Statutory sanction: Revocation of non-resident tax-exempt status; assessment of RMI gross revenue / business license taxes and applicable import or service taxes; monetary penalties for false declaration under the Business Corporations Act; administrative dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or procure false declarations are personally exposed to civil restitution, regulatory fines, travel bans, and imprisonment for fraud or perjury; courts may pierce the corporate veil where the BC is used as a facade for tax evasion.
Transfer Pricing / Artificial Offshore Profit Booking

Statutory sanction: Foreign tax authorities may recharacterize the RMI BC as a conduit, impose transfer-pricing adjustments, apply penalties up to 40% of the understated tax, add interest, and require CbC/beneficial-ownership disclosure under exchange-of-information mechanisms.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute artificial intercompany transactions can be held personally liable for penalties; travel bans and extradition may be imposed where transfer pricing fraud is treated as tax evasion; corporate veil piercing is available if the BC lacks independent economic reality.
Shell Company / Sham Substance under ESR

Statutory sanction: RMI Economic Substance assessment, fixed and escalating daily penalties, mandatory information exchange, suspension of the entity’s registration, and strike-off; foreign courts may regard the entity as a sham and disregard the separate legal personality.

Director personal liability: Directors and ultimate beneficial owners may be ordered to restore diverted assets, disgorged profits, or tax liabilities personally; travel restrictions may be imposed pending investigation; criminal convictions for fraudulent evasion can lead to imprisonment.
AML/CDD and Beneficial Ownership Irregularities
