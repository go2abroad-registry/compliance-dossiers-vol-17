Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Division, Ministry of Finance and Treasury, Republic of the Marshall Islands; no dedicated national tax e-portal is publicly available for TIN issuance, and registrations are ordinarily submitted through a licensed registered agent or in person at the Revenue and Taxation office in Majuro.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the non-resident Business Corporation under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act through a licensed registered agent; 2) if physical business activity will be conducted in-country, obtain the necessary Foreign Investment Business Act registration or license; 3) file the RMI taxpayer registration form with the Revenue and Taxation Division, together with the certificate of incorporation and registered-agent details, to obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number; 4) no VAT identification number is issued because the RMI does not operate a VAT/GST regime; 5) the non-resident BC remains outside the RMI income tax net with respect to foreign-source income provided no income is derived from sources within the Marshall Islands.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — 1) Incorporate the non-resident Business Corporation under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act through a licensed registered agent; 2) if physical business activity will be conducted in-country, obtain the necessary Foreign Investment Business Act registration or license; 3) file the RMI taxpayer registration form with the Revenue and Taxation Division, together with the certificate of incorporation and registered-agent details, to obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number; 4) no VAT identification number is issued because the RMI does not operate a VAT/GST regime; 5) the non-resident BC remains outside the RMI income tax net with respect to foreign-source income provided no income is derived from sources within the Marshall Islands.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The RMI has no local check-the-box election and no statutory tax classification election. A non-resident Business Corporation is a separate juridical corporation under RMI law. The foreign beneficial owner's jurisdiction may apply its own classification rules, such as U.S. Treasury entity-classification principles, but any foreign election has no effect on the RMI tax classification. The RMI has not enacted domestic hybrid-mismatch rules; hybrid outcomes, if any, arise solely under the owner's home-country tax regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax; RMI levies only customs/import duties at the border under the Customs and Tariff Act.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - there is no VAT/GST turnover registration threshold. A commercial importer or exporter may be required to register with RMI Customs for declaration and duty purposes, but this is not a turnover-based indirect tax registration threshold in local currency.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. The RMI does not impose VAT/GST on exports or on offshore services, and therefore has no 0% export VAT rate, no VAT refund mechanism, and no zero-rating substantiation form. Exports of intraocular acrylic lenses manufactured in the RMI are outside the RMI indirect tax base; provided the income is foreign-source, the non-resident BC is also outside the RMI income tax net.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic filing through RMI Customs/ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill together with commercial invoice and packing list evidencing physical export from the RMI
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds where proceeds are routed through an RMI bank

No real-time fiscal e-invoicing or digital stamping mandate exists. Customs export and import declarations are filed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs system operated by RMI Customs; ordinary commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading and air waybills remain the controlling trade documents.

No VAT zero-rating clawback regime exists because the RMI has no VAT. The RMI also imposes no statutory 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation deadline; the 180-day figure is used by trade-finance providers and certain foreign tax authorities as a customary remittance benchmark. The principal RMI audit risk is misdeclaration of exports to evade customs duties, which may result in customs penalties, forfeiture of goods, and criminal fines. U.S. owners of MH_BC must separately assess U.S. GILTI/Subpart F consequences; the RMI non-resident tax status does not eliminate U.S. tax exposure where U.S. persons are controlling shareholders.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMI currency system is characterized by de facto full current- and capital-account convertibility based on the U.S. dollar as legal tender; no domestic exchange-control law, no prior authorization requirement for capital inflows or outflows, and no restriction on dividend or profit repatriation or on conversion of export earnings into any traded currency exists for non-resident Business Corporations. In practice, the operational hurdles are documentary: legitimate repatriation should be supported by directors' resolutions, audited financial statements, and underlying trade contracts, while any suspicious funds movement remains subject to FIU and bank-level reporting. The non-resident entity is not subject to RMI income tax, and dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders carry no withholding tax.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender rule, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no time limit for holding export receipts. Receipts for intraocular lenses may be retained in USD indefinitely or converted to counterparty currencies at negotiated bank rates. The only applicable obligations are (i) retention of trade documentation (invoices, bills of lading, and customs declarations) for no less than five years to evidence the underlying sale; (ii) FIU reporting of cash or wire transactions meeting the $10,000 threshold rules; and (iii) for LC settlements, full documentary compliance with UCP600 or ISP98 presentation terms to the confirming bank.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands does not maintain a central bank or a statutory exchange-control authority. The U.S. dollar is legal tender under the Compact of Free Association (Title II, Article V), and prudential supervision of licensed banks and foreign-exchange-relevant institutions is performed by the Banking Commissioner of the Ministry of Finance, with AML/CFT oversight executed by the Financial Intelligence Unit under the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Financing of Terrorism Act 2012. The relationship to the U.S. Federal Reserve system and the OFAC and de-risking policies of U.S. correspondent banks therefore constitutes the effective regulatory screen for all USD clearing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. Offshore E-Money Institutions (Wise-class and Mercury-class digital platforms, EU/UK/HK-licensed) are, in strict law, permitted to service a Marshall Islands-domiciled company, but most will decline the relationship because the RMI sits outside SEPA/CHIPS membership and is treated as a higher-risk non-resident entity structure. A viable opening is possible only where (a) the EMI uses a U.S. OCC or Fed correspondent rail, (b) the BC provides the full AML/EDD dossier described below, and (c) the EMI explicitly approves the ISIC 3250 medical-device purpose. Even then, the platform will neither provide an IBAN nor accept documentary credit (LC) flows, so the EMI can function only as a secondary collection or settlement rail for open-account trade, never as the sole treasury account of the entity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital contributions should be wired via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank to the Bank of Marshall Islands in Majuro using the bank's published receiving SWIFT/BIC, quoted in USD, with field 70 containing the purpose descriptor 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - BOARD RESOLUTION NO.
