Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (Customs and Revenue Division), Majuro, with corporate registration administered by the Office of the Registrar of Corporations (RMI Corporate Registry). No dedicated national income-tax administration portal exists because the RMI levies no income, corporate, or VAT/GST tax.

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities are not issued a domestic TIN or VAT ID. The operative statutory tax identifier is the Tax Exemption Certificate (TEC) issued under the Business Corporations Act (BCCA), obtained through a licensed Registered Agent and the Registrar of Corporations, accompanied by a Foreign Investment Business License (FIBL) where foreign ownership engages in local activity. For a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation (NRDC), the TEC is the sole tax identity instrument; no tax filings or returns are required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone / Territorial_Scope: A Non-Resident Domestic Corporation is expressly exempt from all income tax, franchise tax, and other taxes of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, including any tax computed on gross revenue, provided no business is carried on within RMI territory and no local-source income is derived. — Foreign entities are not issued a domestic TIN or VAT ID. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone / Territorial_Scope: A Non-Resident Domestic Corporation is expressly exempt from all income tax, franchise tax, and other taxes of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, including any tax computed on gross revenue, provided no business is carried on within RMI territory and no local-source income is derived.

Available election forms

None applicable: the RMI has no check-the-box entity election system, no hybrid mismatch election, and no regime analogous to U.S. Subchapter S. The only relevant election is the irrevocable statutory selection of non-resident status under BCCA, which fixes the tax-exempt classification for the corporation's existence.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. The RMI imposes only customs import duties (tariffs) on goods entering the territory and municipal business license fees for physical operation within Majuro or other municipalities.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no indirect tax registration threshold exists. Import duty registration arises only when the entity physically imports goods into RMI customs territory; an NRDC engaged solely in cross-border e-commerce and offshore export of nutraceutical supplements has no indirect-tax nexus and no registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: absent a VAT/GST system there is no export zero-rating to claim and no VAT refund mechanism. Exports of dietary supplements and nutraceuticals from the RMI are not subject to any export duty, sales tax, or indirect tax. An NRDC should nevertheless maintain documentary proof that title, risk, and control pass to foreign customers, and that goods never enter RMI customs territory, to preserve the BCCA tax-exempt status and satisfy high-risk acquiring banks.

  • Customs Export Declaration (filed only where goods transit RMI customs territory; otherwise a General Export Certificate or exemption letter)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing shipment from origin to foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or acquiring-bank settlement statement evidencing foreign-source payment flow
  • Commercial Invoice with HS/ISIC code 4791 scope description
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (or supplier's declaration for nutraceutical raw materials)

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, API clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in the RMI. Commercial invoices and pro forma invoices are retained purely as banking and customs evidence; no government-side fiscal clearance of invoices is required for export transactions.

Although no VAT refund clawback is possible in the RMI, false certification of non-resident or export status, or submission of forged customs or banking documents, triggers: (i) revocation of the Tax Exemption Certificate and retroactive assessment of any taxes that would otherwise have been due (limited to municipal fees and import duties); (ii) monetary fines under the Business Corporations Act; and (iii) criminal penalties for fraud, perjury, and money laundering under the Criminal Code and the Anti-Money Laundering (Amendment) Act, including imprisonment. High-Risk_Merchant_Account acquirers additionally reserve contractual clawback rights for chargebacks and documentary non-compliance, which may result in holdback forfeiture and termination of payment processing.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Marshall Islands utilises the U.S. Dollar as sole legal tender. There are no exchange control regulations, no distinction between current and capital account treatment, and no convertibility restrictions. Dividends, profit remittances, capital repatriation and direct investment outflows are fully free without Central Bank approval, subject only to commercial bank AML/CFT due diligence and FATF-aligned sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender rules exist in the Republic of the Marshall Islands. Exporters are not required to convert or repatriate export receipts within any statutory timeline. Export proceeds may be retained in USD offshore or onshore at the exporter's discretion; however, domestic banks may require supporting trade documentation such as invoices, bills of lading, and customs entries for value greater than USD 10,000.

Central bank supervision: Not applicable – the Republic of the Marshall Islands has no central bank. The de facto banking and foreign exchange regulator is the Republic of the Marshall Islands Banking Commission, established under the Banking Act 1991, with supervisory oversight through the Minister of Finance. The official legal tender is the U.S. Dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. The Marshall Islands does not maintain a dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime. Offshore digital EMIs and payment fintechs cannot obtain a direct membership in USD domestic clearing or access the Federal Reserve/CHIPS system. Non-resident fintech charters recognised under Marshall Islands corporate law do not carry banking or clearing-house privileges. For a high-risk cross-border nutraceutical e-commerce operation, engagement with an offshore EMI is legally possible but commercially impracticable due to downstream correspondent bank de-risking and incompatibility with domestic bank KYC expectations.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold or Local File/Master File obligation exists under RMI law. As a best-practice benchmark for a cross-border nutraceutical supply chain, arm's length documentation should be prepared when controlled transactions exceed USD 1,000,000 in annual value.

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands has not enacted OECD-style Economic Substance Regulations and does not apply ESR to a non-resident Business Corporation. Nevertheless, foreign tax authorities may invoke substance-over-form to pierce the structure if management, procurement, payment routing, and customer operations are physically directed from the shareholder's residence country. Board meetings, bank accounts, supplier contracts, and record-keeping should therefore be situated in the jurisdiction where the shareholder asserts tax residence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion in a Foreign Tax Jurisdiction

Statutory sanction: Foreign revenue authorities may recharacterise the arrangement as a sham and impose fraud penalties of 100–200% of the understated tax, interest, and criminal fines up to USD 500,000. The RMI Registrar may additionally strike off the company for filing a false exemption declaration.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false declarations or divert foreign sales proceeds face civil restitution of all concealed profits, personal travel bans while foreign tax warrants are outstanding, and imprisonment of up to seven years under the prosecuting jurisdiction's tax laws.
Shell Company / Abusive Tax Avoidance
