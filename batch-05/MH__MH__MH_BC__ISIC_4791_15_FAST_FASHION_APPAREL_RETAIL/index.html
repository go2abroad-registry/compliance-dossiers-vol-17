Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Revenue and Taxation Office, Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands; corporate registration administered by the Office of the Registrar of Corporations (Association Registry), Majuro; filing and payments are effected through licensed registered agents recognized by the Registrar.

E-government filing portal

Incorporate under the Business Corporations Act 1990 (BCA) through a licensed registered agent; the Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation and a unique entity registration number. A Nonresident Corporation (BCA Section 105) deriving no income from sources within the Republic of the Marshall Islands and maintaining no local office is not required to obtain a separate income-tax TIN or file RMI income tax returns. A voluntary taxpayer identification number may be obtained from the Revenue and Taxation Office solely for FATCA/CRS self-certification or local banking accommodation.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Nonresident Corporation under BCA Section 105; taxation is limited territorially to income derived from sources within the Republic of the Marshall Islands; foreign-source e-commerce income is outside the RMI income tax base and entity-level income tax does not apply. — Incorporate under the Business Corporations Act 1990 (BCA) through a licensed registered agent; the Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation and a unique entity registration number. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Nonresident Corporation under BCA Section 105; taxation is limited territorially to income derived from sources within the Republic of the Marshall Islands; foreign-source e-commerce income is outside the RMI income tax base and entity-level income tax does not apply.

Available election forms

An eligible corporation may elect Nonresident Corporation status by filing a Statement or Articles Amendment with the Registrar of Corporations designating non-resident classification under BCA Section 105; the election is revocable by amendment converting the entity to a resident domestic corporation, which would subject it to the Income Tax Act 1999 on RMI-source income. The Republic of the Marshall Islands does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime and maintains no hybrid mismatch elective rules; its treaty network is negligible, so no hybrid mismatch treaty-based elections are available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_Value_Added_Tax_No_GST — the Republic of the Marshall Islands levies no national VAT, GST, or general sales tax; only customs import duty applies to physical importation of goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST regime exists, therefore no economic turnover threshold triggers indirect tax registration; importers must nonetheless register with RMI Customs for a customs/import duty account to process physical shipments.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT concept — because the Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no VAT/GST, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim and no refund mechanism. Substantive tax protection instead rests on Nonresident Corporation status: sales of fast-fashion apparel and footwear via online platforms to non-RMI customers constitute foreign-source income excluded from the RMI income tax base. Physical goods merely transiting RMI ports or using the RMI flag as a maritime registry do not create RMI-source income.

  • Customs Export Declaration (RMI Customs export entry where goods physically leave RMI territory)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing carriage from the port or airport of loading
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 record evidencing offshore payment collection
  • Registered Agent Certificate attesting Nonresident Corporation status under BCA Section 105

No e-invoicing mandate exists: the Republic of the Marshall Islands has no real-time fiscal clearance system, no B2B/B2C electronic reporting obligation, and no digital stamping requirement. Cross-border fast-fashion e-commerce sales processed through Shopify Payments and Klarna remain outside any RMI fiscal invoicing or clearance net.

Because the Republic of the Marshall Islands has no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating fraud exposure; however, material risks remain. Under the Income Tax Act 1999, if the Revenue and Taxation Office determines that the entity derived RMI-source income, the Nonresident Corporation exemption collapses; tax may be reassessed with interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax deficiency. Under BCA Section 105, a false non-resident certification can result in revocation of registration, monetary penalties imposed by the Registrar, and reinstatement obligations. For physical shipments, customs duty evasion triggers seizure or forfeiture of goods and penalty assessments of up to three times the duty evaded.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full dollarization means absolute convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. There are no foreign-exchange controls, no central bank approval requirements, and no separate exchange-rate regime. Profit remittances, dividends, and distributions to non-resident shareholders are freely payable in USD, subject only to corporate law solvency tests, tax clearance for licensed entities (if any), and anti-money laundering (AML) reporting thresholds. No capital account restrictions exist under RMI law.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender obligations or minimum conversion ratios exist. All receipts are retained in USD; the Banking Commission does not mandate repatriation timelines or conversion. Exporters must, however, maintain records supporting the underlying trade transaction for AML/CFT and tax audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands Banking Commission (within the Ministry of Finance) — the RMI has no central bank; the US dollar is the sole legal tender and the de facto monetary anchor.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Offshore digital EMIs and neobanks not licensed in RMI may provide onboarding convenience, but RMI-regulated banks generally do not treat them as qualified customer-facing financial institutions for treasury operations. Corporate bankers and auditors will expect the operating treasury account to be domiciled at an RMI-licensed or US federally insured bank. For fast-fashion e-commerce flows, a licensed EMI with a US partner bank may be used only for merchant settlement collection (e.g., Shopify Payments) and must be supplemented by a domestic clearing account for statutory disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections from shareholders or related parties must be routed via SWIFT MT103 from an account in the investor's jurisdiction, using standard payment purpose codes. Since RMI has no domestic foreign-exchange code, the bank relies on the remitting bank's purpose classification (e.g., OECD/IMF Balance of Payments code). Recommended narrative: 'Share capital contribution to RMI business corporation' or 'Director's loan — operating working capital'. The bank will require a board resolution, executed subscription agreement, and source-of-funds evidence. No RMI regulatory approval is required; funds become immediately available in USD.Outbound trade settlements for fast-fashion apparel purchases and logistics costs are typically executed via (a) SWIFT MT103 wire transfers for supplier invoices under USD 500,000; (b) Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) through a US correspondent or the bank's trade finance desk for larger bulk fabric or finished-goods shipments; and (c) card or local clearing networks for e-commerce platform fees. All outbound payments require supporting commercial invoices, bill of lading/air waybill, and the relevant stated purpose code. The bank will apply its own OFAC and AML transaction monitoring.USD is the functional currency; no local-currency FX hedging is needed.Offshore USD/EUR or USD/CNY forward contracts through a US or non-RMI counterparty are permitted for hedging non-USD supplier costs where the bank maintains a global market access.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on Asian currencies may be used indirectly via a licensed international bank or fintech treasury provider, subject to the RMI bank's correspondent relationship.Cross-currency swaps on major pairs (EUR/USD, JPY/USD) are permissible for balance-sheet hedging but require a documented operational FX risk policy.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - the Marshall Islands imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no gross receipts tax, and no excess profits tax on a non-resident Business Corporation

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the Marshall Islands imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a non-resident Business Corporation to any shareholder, whether resident or non-resident; dividends are repatriated free of all RMI taxation and may be declared in any freely convertible currency.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - the Marshall Islands has no transfer pricing statute, no Local File or Master File obligation, and no country-by-country reporting regime; no local-currency threshold is codified. Practical benchmark: maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation where related-party cross-border transactions exceed US$1,000,000 equivalent per annum to withstand foreign revenue authority audits.

Economic substance scope: The Marshall Islands has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations; no domestic substance filing, economic substance test, or related compliance obligation applies to non-resident Business Corporations. The RMI was placed on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions (Annex I) on 14 February 2023 on tax-transparency grounds, activating EU defensive measures such as enhanced monitoring, DAC6 disclosure scrutiny, and potential withholding or CFC attribution by EU member states. Foreign tax authorities will apply their own substance doctrines, US Subpart F and PFIC rules, UK CFC rules, and EU ATAD anti-abuse measures to attribute fast-fashion e-commerce profits to the ultimate beneficial owner's residence jurisdiction unless real management, employees, procurement, IT infrastructure, and IP development are genuinely located in the RMI.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: No RMI domestic tax penalty applies because no RMI income tax is levied; however, the foreign residence jurisdiction will impose fraud penalties, e.g., under the US Internal Revenue Code a 75% civil fraud penalty on the underpaid tax plus interest, and criminal tax evasion penalties of up to US$100,000 fine (individual) or US$500,000 (corporation) and imprisonment of up to 5 years. Enforcement is facilitated by the US-RMI Tax Information Exchange Agreement and the FATCA automatic exchange channel.

Director personal liability: Nominee and de facto directors who knowingly execute an evasion scheme face personal civil restitution orders, foreign asset freezes, travel bans under criminal procedure, extradition under the US-RMI extradition treaty (in force), and imprisonment in the residence jurisdiction; the UBO additionally faces money-laundering charges where proceeds are layered through RMI bank accounts.
Shell Company / Sham Corporate Veil

Statutory sanction: Under RMI law, the Registrar of Corporations may administratively dissolve the BC and revoke its certificate for default in annual filing. Foreign tax authorities may apply sham transaction and economic substance doctrines to disregard the entity and reattribute all e-commerce income to the UBO with back taxes, interest, and penalties; EU member states may impose 25% defensive withholding on payments to the entity as an EU-listed jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: RMI courts will pierce the corporate veil where the company is demonstrated to be the alter ego of a dominant shareholder; directors face disqualification orders, civil restitution of diverted funds, and personal liability for contracts entered on behalf of a dissolved company; travel bans and arrest warrants may issue in cross-border insolvency or fraudulent trading proceedings.
Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Financing of Terrorism

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI Criminal Code 2019 and the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act 2019, money laundering is a serious criminal offence carrying substantial custodial sentences and criminal fines; regulatory action may also suspend or revoke the licensed agent's authorization, barring the entity from further operations in the RMI; US OFAC violations separately expose the payment processors and any US nexus to civil penalties per violation.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers with knowledge or willful blindness can be prosecuted personally in the RMI and extradited to partner jurisdictions; personal assets are subject to freezing and forfeiture orders; directors may be banned from serving as officers of any RMI entity and face imprisonment in the jurisdiction where the predicate offence occurred.
Transfer Pricing / CFC Attribution Failure

Statutory sanction: No RMI penalty arises domestically; exposure sits in counterparty jurisdictions: Chinese and Hong Kong tax authorities may reassess outbound service and royalty payments, apply 5-15% withholding on royalties, and levy penalties of up to 5 times the underpaid tax; the US IRS may reallocate income under IRC Section 482 with a 40% valuation misstatement penalty and impose Subpart F inclusions on the RMI entity as a controlled foreign corporation.

Director personal liability: Directors signing intercompany agreements without commercial substance can be joined in foreign tax and insolvency proceedings; backdating or falsifying agreements triggers criminal fraud exposure with custodial sentences in the counterparty jurisdiction and professional disqualification.
