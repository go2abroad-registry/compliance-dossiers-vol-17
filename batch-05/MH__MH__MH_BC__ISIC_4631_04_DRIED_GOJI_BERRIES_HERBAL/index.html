Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Treasury Division – Revenue and Tax Administration; Business License Office of the Ministry of Finance; Registrar of Corporations (Office of the Attorney General). No national taxpayer portal is operated; business license filings and corporation registrations are lodged through the RMI Business License / Registrar pipeline.

E-government filing portal

The Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no income tax, VAT, or GST and issues no statutory TIN or VAT ID. A non-resident Business Corporation obtains legal personality by filing Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act and thereafter obtains a Business License Certificate from the Business License Office solely for administrative and license-fee purposes. Export-only operations conducted wholly offshore require no fiscal registration or tax identification number.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — The Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no income tax, VAT, or GST and issues no statutory TIN or VAT ID. A non-resident Business Corporation obtains legal personality by filing Articles of Incorporation with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act and thereafter obtains a Business License Certificate from the Business License Office solely for administrative and license-fee purposes. Export-only operations conducted wholly offshore require no fiscal registration or tax identification number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The RMI has no check-the-box, hybrid mismatch, or subpart F style election. Non-resident status is established de jure through the entity's certificate of non-residency filed with the Registrar of Corporations. A voluntary election to be treated as a resident business is possible by holding an RMI trading license and carrying on business within the RMI, which subjects the entity to the 3% Gross Revenue Tax on RMI-source gross revenue; no such election is recommended or required for the offshore export mandate.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax regime exists. Indirect fiscal levies are limited to the 3% Gross Revenue Tax (GRT) on RMI-source business gross revenue, customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act, and license-based fees. Export transactions are outside the scope of the GRT.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT registration threshold exists in the RMI. Under the Gross Revenue Tax framework, a non-exempt entity becomes liable to obtain a Business License when it commences taxable business activity within the RMI; a non-resident exporter transacting exclusively offshore has zero RMI-source revenue and remains outside the licensing tax net regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – there is no VAT to zero-rate and no export refund mechanism to claim. A non-resident Business Corporation exporting dried goji berries and herbal botanicals is entirely exempt from indirect taxation provided no sale, delivery, or economic activity occurs within the RMI. The exemption flows from the entity's non-resident status rather than from a zero-rating certificate; goods consigned through RMI free-zone or port transit facilities do not lose this exempt character.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged via ASYCUDA World through the RMI Customs Service
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export movement
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Phytosanitary Certificate issued under the Plant Quarantine Act for dried lycium and herbal botanicals
  • Certificate of Origin (self-certification or RMI Chamber of Commerce issuance)

No real-time fiscal clearance, API interconnectivity, digital stamping, SAF-T filing, or e-invoicing mandate exists. Commercial invoices and packing lists accompany customs export declarations lodged via ASYCUDA World through the RMI Customs Service, but no tax-administration electronic clearance or fiscal approval of invoices is required.

No VAT clawback or fraudulent zero-rating penalty regime exists because the RMI levies no VAT on exports. False declaration of export status or abuse of non-resident tax classification is prosecuted under the Business Corporations Act and the Business License Act, exposing the entity to license revocation, corporate dissolution, fines up to USD 10,000 for corporation-register misconduct, and daily fines up to USD 5,000 for unlicensed RMI trading activity. If non-resident status is fraudulently claimed while trading inside the RMI, the 3% Gross Revenue Tax becomes retroactively assessable on RMI-source gross revenue, with statutory interest and penalty surcharges for underreporting; the RMI does not impose any separate exchange-control repatriation sanction.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current account items are fully convertible. The official currency is the United States dollar, and the Republic of the Marshall Islands imposes no domestic capital controls, no currency rationing, and no restriction on profit remittance. Because no local currency exists, convertibility is effectively the same as USD convertibility in international markets; the only practical frictions arise from bank-level AML/KYC diligence and OFAC/sanctions screening by correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation exists. Export receipts are not subject to mandatory conversion ratios, forced timelines, or repatriation limits. The company may retain proceeds in USD or any freely convertible foreign currency, and may manage settlements through its own treasury without central bank approval.

Central bank supervision: Banking Commission of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (Ministry of Finance), with no central bank or foreign-exchange administration; the U.S. Federal Reserve acts as the de facto monetary authority via official dollarization.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium conditional. Regulated offshore EMIs may accept an RMI Business Corporation only when the corporate secretary is an RMI-licensed registered agent and the company provides transactional evidence such as invoices, bills of lading, and phytosanitary certificates. Major U.S.-centric digital banks generally do not open accounts for RMI-domiciled entities because the RMI is not an EEA or U.S. domestic jurisdiction; global EMIs may escalate due to the herbal/botanical product classification. Legal constraints include sanctions screening, prohibited-business list review, and enhanced beneficial-ownership disclosure.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold (USD) is prescribed for an exempt non-resident BC; no Local File or Master File is required. If the BC unexpectedly becomes taxable under the RMI Gross Revenue Tax, contemporaneous pricing evidence must substantiate the arm's-length allocation of RMI-source gross receipts; no published de minimis threshold applies.

Economic substance scope: The RMI Economic Substance (Implementation) Act imposes substance requirements on RMI tax-resident entities conducting 'relevant activities', including distribution and service centre activities. A dried goji berry wholesale exporter structured as an RMI BC cannot be a mere invoicing shell: if the entity is tax resident in RMI, it must demonstrate CIGA (core income generating activities) in RMI, adequate employees, physical premises, and routine board oversight. If the entity claims non-resident status, it must prove tax residence in another qualifying jurisdiction; otherwise the BC is exposed to economic substance penalties and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by Misuse of Exempt Non-Resident Status

Statutory sanction: RMI civil tax fraud penalties of up to 50% of the underpaid tax plus interest; criminal liability for false statements under the RMI Criminal Code includes a fine and imprisonment for up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve false non-resident declarations and fraudulent invoices are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes; courts may pierce the corporate veil and issue restitution orders, travel bans, and criminal custodial sentences.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Economic substance penalties as prescribed by the RMI Economic Substance (Implementation) Act, including a base financial penalty, daily default penalties for continuing non-compliance, and mandatory disclosure/exchange of information with foreign tax authorities; the Registrar may strike off the entity.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally exposed to disqualification orders and pecuniary penalties; the corporate veil may be disregarded, making directors personally liable for creditors' losses, including unpaid trade suppliers of herbal commodity consignments.
Fraudulent Transfer and Asset Stripping

Statutory sanction: RMI fraudulent conveyance and voidable preference rules allow court-ordered setting aside of the transfers and restoration of assets to the estate; criminal penalties apply for fraudulent disposition of property with intent to defraud creditors.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the value of assets fraudulently conveyed; courts may issue worldwide asset freezing orders, seizure warrants, and travel bans, and may commit directors to imprisonment for contempt.
Anti-Money Laundering / UBO Disclosure Failure

Statutory sanction: Violations of the RMI Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act attract criminal fines, imprisonment, asset confiscation, and regulatory sanctions, including suspension or revocation of the BC's banking facility and corporate registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and AML officers are personally liable for wilful contraventions and may be fined, imprisoned, disqualified from managing corporations, and barred from international travel pending investigation.
Administrative Dissolution and Unauthorized Trading

Statutory sanction: The RMI Registrar may administratively dissolve the BC; after dissolution, corporate capacity and contracts are void or voidable, bank accounts are blocked, and reinstatement requires payment of all arrears, late penalties, and reinstatement fees.

Director personal liability: Directors who trade after administrative dissolution are personally liable for all debts incurred post-dissolution; the corporate veil is pierced as a matter of law, and affected counterparties may proceed directly against directors' personal assets.
