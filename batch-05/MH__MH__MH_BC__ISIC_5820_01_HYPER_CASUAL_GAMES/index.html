Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Republic of the Marshall Islands (Revenue and Taxation Division). The RMI does not maintain a public tax e-filing portal for non-resident tax-exempt business corporations.

E-government filing portal

Not applicable. The RMI does not issue a general income-tax TIN, VAT ID, or income-tax registration to foreign entities. A non-resident Business Corporation obtains its sole corporate identifier from the Registrar of Corporations under the Marshall Islands Business Corporations Act (MIRC Title 51); this identifier is used for corporate filing and annual government fee purposes. No VAT ID is obtainable because the RMI has no value-added tax. If the entity directly employs RMI residents, it must separately register as an employer with the Marshall Islands Social Security Administration and receive an employer registration number; this does not constitute an income-tax TIN.

Available election forms

None. RMI law does not provide a check-the-box, pass-through election, or hybrid-mismatch election. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no VAT, no GST, no general sales-and-use tax). The RMI imposes only customs import duties and excise-type duties at the border.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no indirect-tax registration threshold because no VAT, GST, or sales tax exists. Import duty is triggered by individual imports based on customs value and the applicable duty schedule; no economic-turnover threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Because the RMI has no VAT or GST, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim and no export VAT refund mechanism. Ad-mediated hyper-casual mobile game publishing services are outside the RMI indirect-tax net, and non-resident Business Corporations are not subject to RMI income tax on offshore gaming or advertising revenue. Export zero-rating substantiation is therefore legally irrelevant.

  • If the same entity were to export physical gaming media from the RMI, RMI Customs would require a customs export declaration or entry, commercial invoice, and transport documentation; this relates to customs control, not to any VAT refund.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility. The U.S. dollar is the sole legal tender and local currency, so there are no exchange controls or convertibility restrictions on either the current account or the capital account. Profit remittances are unrestricted and there is no withholding tax for non-resident business corporations. All outward funds transfers are subject only to standard bank-level AML/KYC due diligence, including verification of ultimate beneficial owner and source of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. RMI law imposes no statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds, no prescribed timeline for repatriation, and no mandatory conversion ratio into local currency because the local currency is already the U.S. dollar. Foreign-currency export receipts may be retained, freely converted, or remitted subject to normal banking documentation standards and Financial Transactions Reporting Act compliance.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) does not operate a central bank or a standalone foreign exchange administration; monetary policy is effectively delegated to the United States Federal Reserve through the use of the U.S. dollar as legal tender. The prudential regulator for banking activities is the Banking Commissioner under the RMI Ministry of Finance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Moderate-High. Offshore fintech EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Airwallex, or Mercury) may onboard a non-resident RMI Business Corporation with digital-only KYC, provided the entity delivers a full corporate pack including certificate of good standing, certificate of incumbency, register of directors, registered agent letter, and a business narrative describing the hyper-casual gaming monetization model. RMI has no domestic EMI licensing regime, and offshore EMIs generally treat Pacific jurisdictions as enhanced-risk; therefore, onboarding may require an additional compliance review. Such EMIs are suitable for daily liquidity and ad-network payouts, but a tier-1 SWIFT bank remains necessary for capital escrow and large-value treasury settlements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable; a non-resident Business Corporation is taxed at 0% and falls outside the Marshall Islands income tax base, so no local transfer pricing documentation threshold exists. For parent-jurisdiction compliance, a prudent multinational group should still prepare transfer pricing documentation under the OECD Guidelines, but no Marshall Islands Local File or Master File obligation arises.

Economic substance scope: Applicable selectively under the Marshall Islands Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2019. An entity carrying on a 'relevant activity' (banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, holding company, distribution/service center, or IP holding) must demonstrate economic substance in the Marshall Islands. A non-resident company that holds foreign intellectual property for hyper-casual game publishing and receives passive advertising revenue may qualify as a pure equity/asset holding entity and satisfy the reduced substance requirements. However, IP holding entities must meet specific active business tests and cannot rely on passive royalty arrangements alone.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Exemption Abuse / Domestic Activity Without License

Statutory sanction: The corporation is recharacterized as a taxable domestic entity retroactively. Local business privilege tax or gross revenue tax may be assessed on local revenue (up to 3%), plus a 10% understatement penalty and monthly interest of 1.5%. The business license may be revoked and the entity struck off.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit the corporation to misrepresent its non-resident status are jointly and severally liable for resulting corporate liabilities, may be fined up to USD 50,000, and may be prohibited from serving as directors of any Marshall Islands entity for up to five years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may strike the company off the register, impose a financial penalty of USD 10,000 to USD 50,000, and require disgorgement of any tax or registration benefits. In serious cases the entity may be dissolved and its assets vested in the Republic.

Director personal liability: Officers may be personally fined up to USD 250,000 per offense and imprisoned for up to two years for knowingly filing false economic substance declarations. Directors' personal assets may be reached in civil recovery proceedings for restitution to the government.
AML / Tax Evasion / False Filings

Statutory sanction: Under the Marshall Islands Crimes Act 2016 and the Anti-Money Laundering Regulations, money laundering carries a fine of up to USD 5 million or twice the value of the laundered property, whichever is greater, and imprisonment for up to 25 years. Fraudulent filings attract an additional fine of up to USD 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face criminal liability, including imprisonment of up to 25 years, asset confiscation, travel bans, and exclusion orders. The corporate veil is pierced for fraudulent conduct, and directors may be ordered to make full civil restitution to victims and governmental agencies.
