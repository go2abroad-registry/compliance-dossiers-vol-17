Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Ministry of Finance, Banking and Postal Services – Tax and Customs Office (RMI Revenue & Customs); corporate registration is administered by the RMI Registrar of Corporations within the Office of the Attorney General. The Tax and Customs Office operates prescribed paper/electronic filing channels; no single consolidated national tax portal exists, and foreign entities typically act through licensed registered agents.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Appoint a licensed RMI registered agent. Step 2: File Articles of Incorporation under the Business Corporations Act; provide a registered office address in Majuro and a resident registered agent. Step 3: Obtain the Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Corporations. Step 4: If the corporation will derive RMI-source gross receipts, register with the Tax and Customs Office for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and obtain a business license under the Business License Act. Step 5: A Non-Resident Domestic Corporation that conducts no business within RMI may remain outside active tax registration but must still file annual corporate report documents and pay the Registrar's annual franchise fee. No minimum capital applies and 100% foreign ownership is permitted.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

RMI law provides no check-the-box election equivalent to U.S. Treasury Regulations section 301.7701-3, and no domestic hybrid mismatch election mechanism. Available elections are structural: (i) a Business Corporation may elect status as a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation, confirming that all income is foreign-source and outside RMI taxation; (ii) a BC may be converted or continued as an International Business Corporation under the International Business Corporations Act; (iii) a foreign corporation may be continued into RMI as a domestic BC or IBC. No CFC, anti-deferral, or hybrid-mismatch elections exist in RMI domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax – the Republic of the Marshall Islands does not impose a value-added tax, goods and services tax, or general sales and use tax. The only consumption-side levy is customs import duty under the RMI Customs Act, and the only business turnover-type tax is the 3% Gross Revenue Tax on RMI-source gross receipts, applied at entity level rather than as an invoice-and-credit indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable (USD). No VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no VAT/GST is legislated. A person carrying on a business within RMI must obtain a business license and register for Gross Revenue Tax with the Tax and Customs Office regardless of turnover; however, a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation earning no RMI-source gross receipts has no registration or filing obligation for indirect or turnover taxes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – the Marshall Islands has no VAT/GST regime and therefore no statutory zero-rating mechanism, no export VAT refund, and no claim-based substantive test for 0% export taxation. Offshore wholesale distribution of pharmaceutical APIs by a Non-Resident Domestic Corporation to foreign buyers is simply outside RMI indirect and direct taxation, consistent with the RMI territorial-source principle. If goods physically depart from RMI territory, customs export declaration obligations apply under the Customs Act, but no indirect tax consequence arises.

  • Customs Export Declaration (RMI Customs Act) for physical export from Marshall Islands territory
  • Commercial Invoice / Proforma Invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (only where preferential origin claim is made)
  • Bank documentation evidencing SWIFT/letter-of-credit settlement retained for AML/KYC record-keeping purposes

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping mandate exists in RMI. There is no mandatory electronic invoicing API obligation and no government-certified tax clearance platform. Tax returns and customs declarations are submitted on prescribed forms via paper or RMI Revenue and Customs electronic channels; import and export declarations are processed through the customs administration without algorithmic fiscal stamping.

Not applicable to VAT zero-rating as no zero-rating claim exists. General anti-evasion provisions under the RMI Income Tax Act and Customs Act permit the Tax and Customs Office to audit gross revenue tax, customs declarations, and non-resident exemption claims; false or fraudulent declarations attract monetary penalties, interest, potential clawback of any underpaid tax, and criminal liability including fines and imprisonment. Knowingly filing false statements with the Registrar of Corporations under the Business Corporations Act is independently a criminal offense and may result in loss of non-resident status, administrative penalties, and shareholder/director liability.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility because the U.S. dollar is the domestic currency; no currency controls, foreign exchange licensing, or Central Bank reporting requirements exist. Profit remittances and dividend distributions are freely transferable, subject only to standard corporate documentation and substance filings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts; all proceeds may be held in USD or any freely convertible currency without repatriation or surrender obligations.

Central bank supervision: Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) has no central bank; monetary affairs are handled by the RMI Banking Commissioner under the Ministry of Finance, with the United States dollar serving as the sole legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. The RMI has no digital EMI licensing or passporting regime; offshore fintech EMIs are not permitted to open domestic bank accounts or access local clearing rails without a physical branch and RMI banking license. Operation through a licensed U.S.-based money services business or a U.S. Federal Reserve member bank is the only practical alternative, but such accounts are difficult for a non-resident pharma API wholesaler to obtain.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound equity wiring from the RMI shareholder to the RMI corporate bank account must reference purpose code 'BR' (other business borrowing) or 'ES' (equity capital) on SWIFT MT103, with beneficiary bank name and account number exactly matching the RMI corporate bank certificate. The bank will require the corporate resolution approving capital injection, a notarized certificate of incumbency listing the authorized signatories, and evidence that the remitting entity is the disclosed UBO. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable in the RMI (jurisdictional currency: USD); the RMI statute prescribes no mandatory Local File or Master File threshold. OECD-aligned international practice for the pharmaceutical API wholesale model is to maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation once annual intercompany turnover exceeds USD 1,000,000, or whenever the parent jurisdiction threshold - commonly USD 10,000,000 to USD 25,000,000 - is met. The functional analysis must evidence that the MH_BC controls inventory risk, customer counterparty risk, and procurement decisions, and does not function as a self-billed commissionaire.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The RMI Economic Substance Regulations Act subjects RMI business corporations to economic substance filing and test requirements. A wholesale distributor of active pharmaceutical ingredients constitutes the relevant activity of distribution and service centre. Compliance requires RMI premises adequate for the activity, a proportionate number of full-time employees in the RMI, minimum operating expenditure incurred in the RMI, substantive RMI board meetings, and performance in the RMI of the core income-generating activities: inventory management, contract negotiation, credit and risk management, invoicing, and logistics. A pure equity holding entity enjoys a reduced substance test only where no distribution activity is conducted. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Facilitation of Offshore Tax Abuse

Statutory sanction: In the RMI: fines and forfeiture orders under automatic exchange of financial account information legislation and the Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act. In the foreign jurisdiction: reassessment of the hidden profits, substantial understatement penalties, 30% FATCA withholding on US-source payments, and criminal tax evasion prosecution of the controlling person.

Director personal liability: Courts will pierce the corporate veil where the BC is a mere instrumentality. Directors face civil restitution orders, asset freezing injunctions, court-imposed travel bans, extradition proceedings, and imprisonment for knowingly filing false declarations.
Shell Company - Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations Act: a fine of up to USD 50,000 per contravention, daily penalties for continuing default, strike-off and dissolution for persistent breach, and automatic exchange of information with OECD CRS partner jurisdictions.

Director personal liability: Directors who caused or acquiesced in the failure are jointly and severally liable for ESR monetary penalties. Wilful false ESR filings attract director disqualification orders and custodial sentences, and unpaid restitution may result in court-ordered travel bans.
AML and Sanctions Breach in Cross-Border Pharma Payments

Statutory sanction: Under the RMI money laundering and proceeds of crime regime: imprisonment up to 7 years, unlimited fines, asset forfeiture, freezing orders, regulatory suspension of the mandatory High-Risk Special License for pharmaceutical wholesale, and customs seizure of the API cargo.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for knowledge-based participation: directors individually face criminal prosecution, travel bans, surrender of passports pending restitution, and prohibition from acting in regulated pharmaceutical or financial roles.
Unlicensed Pharmaceutical API Distribution

Statutory sanction: Under the Business Licence Act: licence revocation and fines. Under customs legislation: seizure and forfeiture of the API inventory. Under pharmaceutical licensing legislation: criminal fines of up to USD 5,000 per unlawful sale batch and imprisonment up to 5 years for unauthorised distribution of active pharmaceutical ingredients.

Director personal liability: Directors who arranged the unlawful sales are individually criminally liable.
